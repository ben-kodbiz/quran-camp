# Episode 22 Audio Master Specification: When Dead Earth Quivers
## Surah Al-Hajj (22:5, 22:27)

### 1. Engineering Directives
* **Voice Delivery:** Warm, empathetic tone transitioning into awe and reverence; intimate studio proximity (4–6 inches from condenser mic); resonant warmth without harsh sibilance.
* **Pacing:** 110 WPM contemplative slow-burn; generous 2.0-second pauses following Arabic recitation of Ayah 22:5 (*«Fa-idhā anzalnā 'alayhā al-mā'a ihtazzat wa rabat...»*) and Ayah 22:27 (*«Wa adhdhin fī an-nāsi bil-ḥajj...»*).
* **Zero Music Policy:** 100% halal soundscape. Strictly prohibited: instruments, pads, synth melodies. Permitted: organic nature foley (subtle dry desert wind in Section 1, heavy slow rain impacts blooming into soil in Section 2, mountain canyon breeze in Section 3, quiet courtyard fountain ripples in Section 5).
* **Master Target:** -14 LUFS integrated (YouTube Broadcast Master) / -16 LUFS (Stereo Podcast); True Peak ceiling at -1.0 dBTP; Loudness Range (LRA) 7.0 LU.
* **Foley Beds:** High-pass filtered at 80Hz, mixed at -22 dB relative to voiceover dialogue.
