# Episode 24 Audio Master Specification: The Niche of Divine Light
## Surah An-Nur (24:35, 24:39–41)

### 1. Engineering Directives
* **Voice Delivery:** Calm, contemplative baritone; deep resonant warmth, quiet reverence without theatricality.
* **Pacing:** 108 WPM slow-burn cadence; generous 1.8-second contemplative pauses following Arabic recitations of Ayah 24:40 (*«Aw ka-ẓulumātin fī baḥrin lujjiyyin...»*), Ayah 24:35 (*«Allāhu nūru as-samāwāti wal-arḍ... Mathalu nūrihī ka-mishkātin...»*), and Ayah 24:41 (*«Waṭ-ṭayru ṣāffāt...»*).
* **Zero Music Policy:** 100% halal soundscape. Strictly prohibited: strings, pads, melodies. Permitted: organic nature foley (subtle deep sea oceanic swell in Section 1, desert breeze in Section 2, sacred stone acoustic warmth in Section 3, gentle doves birdsong in Section 4, calm coastal dawn tide in Section 5).
* **Master Target:** -14 LUFS integrated (YouTube Broadcast Master) / -16 LUFS (Stereo Podcast); True Peak ceiling at -1.0 dBTP; Loudness Range (LRA) 7.2 LU.
* **Foley Beds:** High-pass filtered at 80Hz, low-pass at 14kHz, mixed at -24 dB relative to voiceover dialogue.
