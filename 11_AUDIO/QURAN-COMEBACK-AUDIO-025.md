# Episode 25 Audio Master Specification: The Criterion in the Noise
## Surah Al-Furqan (25:48, 25:53, 25:61, 25:63, 25:74)

### 1. Engineering Directives
* **Voice Delivery:** Calm, contemplative baritone; deep resonant warmth, gentle authoritative stillness, zero aggressive rhetoric or theatricality.
* **Pacing:** 105 WPM slow-burn cadence; generous 1.8-second contemplative pauses following Arabic recitations of Ayah 25:48 (*«Wa-huwal-ladhī arsalas-riyāḥa bushram bayna yaday raḥmatih...»*), Ayah 25:53 (*«Wa-huwal-ladhī marajal-baḥrayn...»*), and Ayah 25:63 (*«Wa-‘ibādur-Raḥmānil-ladhīna yamshūna ‘alal-arḍi hawnā...»*).
* **Zero Music Policy:** 100% halal soundscape. Strictly prohibited: strings, synthesizers, melodies. Permitted: organic nature foley (subtle falling raindrops on stone basin in Section 1, gentle coastal mist and tide wash in Section 2, open dawn sanctuary wind in Section 3, peaceful mountain pathway footsteps and soft wind in Section 4, silent sacred alcove in Section 5).
* **Master Target:** -14 LUFS integrated (YouTube Broadcast Master) / -16 LUFS (Stereo Podcast); True Peak ceiling at -1.0 dBTP; Loudness Range (LRA) 7.0 LU.
* **Foley Beds:** High-pass filtered at 80Hz, low-pass at 14kHz, mixed at -24 dB relative to voiceover dialogue.
