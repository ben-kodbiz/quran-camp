# Episode 30 Audio Master Specification: The Living Horizon
## Season 4 Finale — Surah Ar-Rum (30:41, 22, 24, 46, 30)

### 1. Engineering Directives
* **Voice Delivery:** Resonant, majestic baritone; philosophical gravity, cosmic wonder, tender spiritual urgency, deep peace.
* **Pacing:** 105 WPM slow-burn cadence; generous 1.8-second contemplative pauses following Arabic recitations of Ayah 30:41 (*«Zahara al-fasādu fī al-barri wa al-baḥri...»*), Ayah 30:22 (*«Wa min āyātihi khalqu as-samāwāti wa al-arḍ...»*), Ayah 30:24 (*«Wa min āyātihi yurīkumu al-barqa khawfan wa ṭama'ā...»*), Ayah 30:46 (*«Wa min āyātihi an yursila ar-riyāḥa mubashshirāt...»*), and Ayah 30:30 (*«Fa-aqim wajhaka lid-dīni ḥanīfā...»*).
* **Zero Music Policy:** 100% halal soundscape. Strictly prohibited: synthesizers, melodies, instruments. Permitted: organic nature foley (autumn gale and rustling leaves in Section 1, vast cloud wind in Section 2, distant rumbling thunder and rain in Section 3, calm ocean wash against hull in Section 4, silent prayer alcove resonance in Section 5).
* **Master Target:** -14 LUFS integrated (YouTube Broadcast Master) / -16 LUFS (Stereo Podcast); True Peak ceiling at -1.0 dBTP; Loudness Range (LRA) 7.0 LU.
* **Foley Beds:** High-pass filtered at 80Hz, low-pass at 14kHz, mixed at -24 dB relative to voiceover dialogue.
