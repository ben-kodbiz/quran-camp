# Episode 26 Audio Master Specification: The Sound Heart
## Surah Ash-Shu'ara (26:63, 78–82, 88–89, 217)

### 1. Engineering Directives
* **Voice Delivery:** Calm, contemplative baritone; deep resonant warmth, unshakable spiritual quietude, reverent certainty.
* **Pacing:** 105 WPM slow-burn cadence; generous 1.8-second contemplative pauses following Arabic recitations of Ayah 26:63 (*«Kallā! Inna ma'iya rabbī sa-yahdīn»*), Ayah 26:80 (*«Wa idhā mariḍtu fa-huwa yashfīn»*), Ayah 26:89 (*«Illā man atā Allāha bi-qalbin salīm»*), and Ayah 26:217 (*«Wa inna rabbaka la-huwa al-'Azīzu ar-Raḥīm»*).
* **Zero Music Policy:** 100% halal soundscape. Strictly prohibited: synthesizers, melodies, strings. Permitted: organic nature foley (subtle morning shoreline tide wash in Section 1, gentle rain falling on olive leaves in Section 2, mountain breeze rustling stone branches in Section 3, micro-ripples across clear spring water in Section 4, silent reading alcove ambience in Section 5).
* **Master Target:** -14 LUFS integrated (YouTube Broadcast Master) / -16 LUFS (Stereo Podcast); True Peak ceiling at -1.0 dBTP; Loudness Range (LRA) 7.1 LU.
* **Foley Beds:** High-pass filtered at 80Hz, low-pass at 14kHz, mixed at -24 dB relative to voiceover dialogue.
