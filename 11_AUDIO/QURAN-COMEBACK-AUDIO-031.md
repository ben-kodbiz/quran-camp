# Episode 31 Audio Master Specification: The Architecture of Wisdom
## Season 5 Premiere — Surah Luqman (31:16, 27, 29, 17, 19)

### 1. Engineering Directives
* **Voice Delivery:** Gentle, paternal baritone; resonant wisdom, intimate warmth, patient moral gravity, quiet stillness.
* **Pacing:** 105 WPM slow-burn cadence; generous 1.8-second contemplative pauses following Arabic recitations of Ayah 31:16 (*«Yā bunayya innahā in taku mithqāla ḥabbatin min khardal...»*), Ayah 31:27 (*«Wa law annamā fī al-arḍi min shajaratin aqlām...»*), Ayah 31:29 (*«A-lam tara anna Allāha yūliju al-layla fī an-nahār...»*), Ayah 31:17 (*«Yā bunayya aqimi aṣ-ṣalāta wa'mur bil-ma'rūf...»*), and Ayah 31:19 (*«Waqṣid fī mashyika waghḍuḍ min ṣawtik...»*).
* **Zero Music Policy:** 100% halal soundscape. Strictly prohibited: synthesizers, melodies, instruments. Permitted: organic nature foley (high granite mountain silence in Section 1, gentle courtyard fountain bubbling in Section 2, nighttime desert breeze in Section 3, stone prayer alcove resonance in Section 4, olive leaves rustling in soft wind in Section 5).
* **Master Target:** -14 LUFS integrated (YouTube Broadcast Master) / -16 LUFS (Stereo Podcast); True Peak ceiling at -1.0 dBTP; Loudness Range (LRA) 7.0 LU.
* **Foley Beds:** High-pass filtered at 80Hz, low-pass at 14kHz, mixed at -24 dB relative to voiceover dialogue.
