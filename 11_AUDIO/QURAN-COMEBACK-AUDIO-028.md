# Episode 28 Audio Master Specification: The Well of Madyan
## Surah Al-Qasas (28:7, 24, 30, 76–77, 88)

### 1. Engineering Directives
* **Voice Delivery:** Calm, contemplative baritone; deep resonant warmth, tender pastoral empathy, quiet reverent awe.
* **Pacing:** 105 WPM slow-burn cadence; generous 1.8-second contemplative pauses following Arabic recitations of Ayah 28:7 (*«Fa-alqīhi fī al-yammi wa lā takhāfī wa lā taḥzanī...»*), Ayah 28:24 (*«Rabbi innī limā anzalta ilayya min khayrin faqīr»*), Ayah 28:76 (*«Innamā ūtītuhu 'alā 'ilmin 'indī»*), and Ayah 28:88 (*«Kullu shay'in hālikun illā wajhah»*).
* **Zero Music Policy:** 100% halal soundscape. Strictly prohibited: synthesizers, melodies, strings. Permitted: organic nature foley (soft rainfall on stone basin in Section 1, mountain valley wind in Section 2, gentle breeze through olive branches in Section 3, quiet desert wind in Section 4, silent reading alcove resonance in Section 5).
* **Master Target:** -14 LUFS integrated (YouTube Broadcast Master) / -16 LUFS (Stereo Podcast); True Peak ceiling at -1.0 dBTP; Loudness Range (LRA) 7.0 LU.
* **Foley Beds:** High-pass filtered at 80Hz, low-pass at 14kHz, mixed at -24 dB relative to voiceover dialogue.
