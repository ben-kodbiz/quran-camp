# Episode 23 Audio Master Specification: The Architecture of a Quiet Heart
## Surah Al-Mu'minun (23:1–2, 23:20)

### 1. Engineering Directives
* **Voice Delivery:** Calm, grounded baritone; soothing intimacy (4–6 inches from condenser mic); resonant warmth without harsh sibilance.
* **Pacing:** 110 WPM slow-burn cadence; generous 1.8-second contemplative pauses following Arabic recitations of Ayah 23:1–2 (*«Qad aflaḥa al-mu'minūn, alladhīna hum fī ṣalātihim khāshi'ūn»*) and Ayah 23:20 (*«Wa shajaratan takhruju min ṭūri saynā'a...»*).
* **Zero Music Policy:** 100% halal soundscape. Strictly prohibited: strings, synthesizers, melodies. Permitted: organic nature foley (subtle autumn wind in Section 1, mountain breeze and olive foliage rustle in Sections 2–4, quiet dawn courtyard atmosphere in Section 5).
* **Master Target:** -14 LUFS integrated (YouTube Broadcast Master) / -16 LUFS (Stereo Podcast); True Peak ceiling at -1.0 dBTP; Loudness Range (LRA) 7.0 LU.
* **Foley Beds:** High-pass filtered at 80Hz, mixed at -22 dB relative to voiceover dialogue.
