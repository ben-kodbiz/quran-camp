# Episode 20 Audio Master Specification: The Qur'an Was Not Sent to Break You

## 1. Engineering Directives
* **Voice Delivery:** Warm baritone or grounded mid-register; intimate studio proximity (4–6 inches from large diaphragm condenser).
* **Pacing:** 115 WPM average; generous 1.5–2.0 second pauses after quoting Surah Ta-Ha 20:2–3, 14, 25–28, 44.
* **Zero Music Policy:** Absolutely zero musical instruments or synthesized synth pads. Strictly natural acoustic room ambiance and organic field recordings (subtle morning birds, distant wind, rain on stone).
* **Master Target:** -16 LUFS integrated (stereo podcast) / -14 LUFS (YouTube master).
