# Episode 5 Audio Master Narration Specification (`QURAN-COMEBACK-AUDIO-005`)

**Campaign:** Come Back to the Qur'an  
**Episode 5 Title:** *"When Life Becomes Heavy"*  
**Assigned Agent:** `AGENT-11` (Audio Agent)  
**Acoustic Philosophy:** Unhurried, intimate, sincere, quiet natural resonance. Strictly no musical instruments. Natural acoustic atmosphere only.  

---

## 1. Acoustic Sound Design Architecture

* **Vocal Tone & Personality:** Grounded, stabilizing, compassionate, and steady. For someone carrying heavy burnout, the voice must act as an acoustic anchor—slow, measured, relieving physical tension with each phrase.
* **Microphone Technique:** Large-diaphragm condenser (Cardioid), proximity effect calibrated (approx. 12–15 cm from capsule with double-mesh pop filter). High-pass filter engaged at 60 Hz (18 dB/octave). Dry recording in treated wooden space (RT60 ~ 0.35s).
* **Foley & Ambient Layering (Islamic Compliance):**
  - **Bed A (0:00–0:35):** Low, heavy gray overcast wind; deep atmospheric pressure, stillness before dawn.
  - **Bed B (0:35–1:45):** Slow, deliberate mountain stream over smooth river pebbles; steady, unhurried flow.
  - **Bed C (1:45–2:50):** A sudden softening of wind; sun breaking through forest canopy, gentle bird calling in distance.
  - **Bed D (2:50–5:00):** Warm wooden study acoustic; peaceful breath release, tranquil settling.

## 3. Acoustic Post-Production & Mastering Standards

* **Integrated Loudness Target:** -16.0 LUFS (±0.5 LUFS) per EBU R128 / YouTube Spoken Word standard.
* **True Peak Limit:** -1.0 dBTP to prevent inter-sample clipping across mobile DACs.
* **EQ Profile:** Slight dip at 2.8 kHz to soften vocal edges; generous dynamic range allowing the listener to feel deep physical sigh and release..
* **Dynamic Control:** Transparent optical-style compression (max 2.5 dB gain reduction on peaks) to preserve natural human breathing dynamics and emotional vulnerability.
* **Mastering Outputs:**
  1. `WAV_MASTER`: 24-bit / 48 kHz Linear PCM Broadcast WAV.
  2. `PODCAST_DIST`: 320 kbps AAC-LC / MP3 for standalone audio streaming and meditation apps.
