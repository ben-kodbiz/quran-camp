# Episode 27 Audio Master Specification: The Praise of All Creation
## Surah An-Naml (27:16–19, 60, 62, 88, 93)

### 1. Engineering Directives
* **Voice Delivery:** Calm, contemplative baritone; gentle awe, reverent clarity, quiet warmth without rhetorical drama.
* **Pacing:** 105 WPM slow-burn cadence; generous 1.8-second contemplative pauses following Arabic recitations of Ayah 27:19 (*«Rabbi awzi'nī an ashkura ni'mataka...»*), Ayah 27:60 (*«Am-man khalaqa as-samāwāti wal-arḍ...»*), Ayah 27:88 (*«Ṣun'a Allāhi alladhī atqana kulla shay'»*), and Ayah 27:62 (*«Am-man yujību al-muḍṭarra idhā da'āhu...»*).
* **Zero Music Policy:** 100% halal soundscape. Strictly prohibited: synthesizers, melodies, strings. Permitted: organic nature foley (subtle tropical rainforest birdsong in Section 1, stone courtyard fountain bubbling in Section 2, high mountain wind and distant waterfall in Section 3, bubbling river stream in Section 4, calm morning coastal tide in Section 5).
* **Master Target:** -14 LUFS integrated (YouTube Broadcast Master) / -16 LUFS (Stereo Podcast); True Peak ceiling at -1.0 dBTP; Loudness Range (LRA) 7.0 LU.
* **Foley Beds:** High-pass filtered at 80Hz, low-pass at 14kHz, mixed at -24 dB relative to voiceover dialogue.
