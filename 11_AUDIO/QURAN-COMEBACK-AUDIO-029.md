# Episode 29 Audio Master Specification: The Fragile Shelter
## Surah Al-Ankabut (29:2, 41, 60, 69, 45)

### 1. Engineering Directives
* **Voice Delivery:** Calm, grounded baritone; introspective conviction, quiet dignity, tender assurance, resolute spiritual stillness.
* **Pacing:** 105 WPM slow-burn cadence; generous 1.8-second contemplative pauses following Arabic recitations of Ayah 29:2 (*«A-hasiba an-nāsu an yutrakū an yaqūlū āmannā wa hum lā yuftanūn»*), Ayah 29:41 (*«Mathalu alladhīna ittakhadhū min dūnillāhi awliyā'a ka-mathali al-'ankabūti ittakhadhat baytā...»*), Ayah 29:60 (*«Wa ka-ayyin min dābbatin lā tahmilu rizqahā Allāhu yarzuquhā wa iyyākum...»*), Ayah 29:69 (*«Wa-lladhīna jāhadū fīnā la-nahdiyannahum subulanā...»*), and Ayah 29:45 (*«Wa ladhikru Allāhi akbar»*).
* **Zero Music Policy:** 100% halal soundscape. Strictly prohibited: synthesizers, melodies, instruments. Permitted: organic nature foley (high ridge mountain wind in Section 1, vast desert silence and warm breeze in Section 2, deep ocean current hum in Section 3, misty pine forest footsteps and rustling needles in Section 4, marble sanctuary morning acoustic resonance in Section 5).
* **Master Target:** -14 LUFS integrated (YouTube Broadcast Master) / -16 LUFS (Stereo Podcast); True Peak ceiling at -1.0 dBTP; Loudness Range (LRA) 7.0 LU.
* **Foley Beds:** High-pass filtered at 80Hz, low-pass at 14kHz, mixed at -24 dB relative to voiceover dialogue.
