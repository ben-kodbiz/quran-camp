# Audio Direction & Acoustic Engineering Specification
## Master Showcase: Ayat al-Kursi & The Last Two Verses

### 1. Voiceover Acoustic Persona & Pacing
- **Tone Profile:** Grounded, calm, reverent, intellectually mature, and intimate. Speaks not as an aloof academic nor as a breathless influencer, but as an authentic spiritual mentor sitting across the table.
- **Pacing & Speed:** 110–120 words per minute. Deliberate, unhurried cadence allowing listener reflection.
- **Strategic Micro-Pauses:**
  - 1.5-second silence following major theological affirmations (e.g., *"Neither drowsiness nor sleep seizes Him."*).
  - 2.0-second silence preceding the final Huurs motto: *"Read. Reflect. Return."*
- **Acoustic Environment:** Dry, warm vocal booth with zero noticeable artificial reverb. Subtle proximity effect on the microphone to convey warmth and presence.

---

### 2. Music & Sound Design Architecture

#### Track Profile A: The Scale of the Throne (Ayat al-Kursi)
- **Key & Instrumentation:** D Minor / F Major, low-register cello drone, minimalist acoustic piano arpeggios, subtle atmospheric wind texture.
- **Acoustic Transitions:**
  - *Intro (0–12s):* Low cello hum at -18 dB under voiceover.
  - *Build (12–40s):* Gradual entrance of soft, distant piano notes when discussing the scale of the Kursi.
  - *Zenith (40–52s):* Swell of warm string pads when affirming divine preservation.
  - *Outro (52–60s):* Single struck Tibetan or crystal bowl tone resonating softly into pure silence.

#### Track Profile B: The Nightly Fortress (The Last Two Verses)
- **Key & Instrumentation:** C Major / A Minor, gentle nylon-string acoustic guitar, warm bowed upright bass, nocturnal ambient night air.
- **Acoustic Transitions:**
  - *Intro (0–12s):* Subdued, solemn atmosphere with night breeze ambience.
  - *Climactic Shift (25–40s):* Lifting harmonic chord as the divine answer *«La yukallifullah»* is declared.
  - *Outro (52–60s):* Reassuring, warm acoustic guitar resolution.

---

### 3. Audio Delivery Guidelines for Production
- No synthetic voice modulation or artificial pitch shifting.
- Strict avoidance of melodramatic background music that distracts from the spoken Qur'anic meaning.
- Background score must remain side-chained at -16 dB to -20 dB below the primary narration track.
