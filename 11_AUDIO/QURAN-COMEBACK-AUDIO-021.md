# Episode 21 Audio Master Specification: Light in the Deepest Dark
## Surah Al-Anbiya (21:30, 21:87–88)

### 1. Engineering Directives
* **Voice Delivery:** Grounded baritone; contemplative, intimate proximity (4–6 inches from condenser mic); resonant warmth without harsh sibilance.
* **Pacing:** 112 WPM slow-burn cadence; generous 1.8-second contemplative pauses following Arabic recitations of Ayah 21:87 (*«Lā ilāha illā anta subḥānaka...»*) and Ayah 21:30 (*«Wa ja'alnā mina al-mā'i kulla shay'in ḥayy»*).
* **Zero Music Policy:** 100% halal soundscape. Strictly prohibited: strings, synthesizers, melodies. Permitted: organic nature foley (subtle deep ocean swell resonance in Section 1, gentle mountain rain and fresh spring stream water in Sections 2–4).
* **Master Target:** -14 LUFS integrated (YouTube Broadcast Master) / -16 LUFS (Stereo Podcast); True Peak ceiling at -1.0 dBTP; Loudness Range (LRA) 7.0 LU.
* **Foley Beds:** High-pass filtered at 80Hz, mixed at -22 dB relative to voiceover dialogue.
