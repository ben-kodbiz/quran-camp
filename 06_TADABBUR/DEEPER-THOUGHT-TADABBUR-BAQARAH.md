## 2. Deep Experiential Reflection Prompts

### Station 1: The Trap of Selective Obedience
- *Tadabbur Reflection:* The Children of Israel were not destroyed because they lacked knowledge; they fell because they bargained with revelation, prioritizing convenience over fidelity. Where in your life are you saying *"Sami'na wa 'Asayna"* (we hear and disobey) while dressing it up as technical piety?

### Station 2: The Security of Ayat al-Kursi
- *Tadabbur Reflection:* If the One who upholds the heavens and the earth never sleeps or slumbers, why do you lose sleep over tomorrow's sustenance? Rest your anxious heart in His perpetual stewardship.
