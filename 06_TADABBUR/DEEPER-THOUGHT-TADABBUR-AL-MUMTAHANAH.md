# Tadabbur Contemplation Guide: Surah 60 (Al-Mumtahanah)

**Operating Philosophy:** READ. REFLECT. RETURN.

## Structured Contemplative Inquiries

### Contemplation Gate 01: THE CLANDESTINE EPISTLE & REBUKED ALLIANCE
> *Pillars 01 & 02: Do Not Take My Enemy as Ally, Secret Affection Rebuked, Expelling the Messenger & Hatib's Letter*

- **Reflective Focus:** How does PILLAR 01: THE INTERDICTION OF ENEMY ALLIANCES reorient your everyday priorities?
- **Soul Return:** What practical commitment emerges from contemplating PILLAR 02: THE PERIL OF CLANDESTINE TREASON?

### Contemplation Gate 02: THE ABRAHAMIC PARADIGM OF DIVORCE FROM TYRANNY
