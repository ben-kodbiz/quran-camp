# Brand_Campaign.md

## Islamic Digital Publishing & Reflection Brand
### Google Ecosystem Execution Blueprint

**Purpose:**  
This document is the master execution brief for Google Antigravity and its MCP-enabled agents. It defines the brand, campaign system, visual identity, content architecture, product architecture, short-video/image generation workflow, quality controls, and reusable prompt framework.

The goal is to build a coherent Islamic digital publishing brand around:

> **Read. Reflect. Return.**

The brand produces Sunni Islamic digital content spanning Qur'an, tafsir, tadabbur, motivation, hadith, seerah, visual reflections, audio, short video, mind maps, ebooks, and multimodal learning products.

---

# 1. MASTER OBJECTIVE

Build an Islamic digital media + publishing ecosystem rather than a collection of isolated ebooks.

The ecosystem must allow one trusted research artifact to become:

- Tafsir/tadabbur material
- Mind map
- Visual explanation
- Short video
- Long-form video
- Audio reflection
- Image/carousel
- Ebook
- Mini ebook
- Reflection journal
- Social post
- Landing-page copy


# 5. ISLAMIC POSITIONING & SOURCE DISCIPLINE

The project is intended for Sunni Islamic content.

Every educational product must distinguish clearly between:

1. Qur'an
2. Authentic/accepted hadith sources
3. Tafsir
4. Scholarly interpretation
5. Tadabbur/reflection
6. Motivation/application
7. Creative presentation

Do not present AI-generated interpretation as revelation or authoritative tafsir.

For Qur'anic verses and hadith:

- preserve exact source references
- verify quotations
- verify surah/ayah numbers
- verify hadith references
- distinguish direct quotation from paraphrase
- avoid fabricated citations
- preserve scholarly context
- flag uncertainty for human review

### Quality hierarchy

```text
AUTHENTICITY
     ↓
SOURCE VERIFICATION
     ↓
ISLAMIC CONTEXT
     ↓
EDUCATIONAL CLARITY
     ↓
REFLECTION
     ↓
VISUAL BEAUTY
```

Beauty must never outrank authenticity.

# 9. SHORT VIDEO GENERATION SYSTEM

Short video is a primary discovery channel.

Target platforms:

- YouTube Shorts
- TikTok
- Instagram Reels
- Facebook Reels where appropriate

Default duration:

- 20–60 seconds

Occasional:

- 60–120 second educational pieces

## Short-video structure

```text
0–3 sec
HOOK

3–10 sec
VISUAL + QUESTION

10–30 sec
ISLAMIC INSIGHT

30–45 sec
REFLECTION

45–60 sec
QUIET CTA
```

### Hook styles

Use reflective hooks rather than sensational hooks.

Examples:

- "Have you ever wondered why the Qur'an asks us to look at creation?"
- "Sometimes the answer begins with slowing down."
- "Look at the sky for a moment."
- "What does patience actually look like?"
- "When the heart feels tired, where does it return?"
- "There is a difference between knowing an ayah and living with it."

Do not manufacture promises such as:

- "This one ayah will change your life instantly."
- "You won't believe what Allah revealed."
- "Secret meaning scholars don't want you to know."
