# Surah Al-Ma'idah: Classical Sunni Exegetical Monograph
## The Jurisprudence of Covenants, Sacramental Law & The Eschatological Vindication

> **Operating Philosophy:** **READ. REFLECT. RETURN.**  
> **Exegetical Lineage:** Imam al-Tabari (*Jami' al-Bayan*), Imam Abu Bakr al-Jassas (*Ahkam al-Qur'an*), Imam Fakhr al-Din al-Razi (*Mafatih al-Ghayb*), Imam al-Qurtubi (*Al-Jami' li-Ahkam al-Qur'an*), Imam Ibn Kathir (*Tafsir al-Qur'an al-'Adheem*).

---

## 1. Epistemological Foundations: The Meaning of 'Uqud

The opening injunction of Surah Al-Ma'idah represents the broadest legal mandate in the entire Quranic legislative canon:
$$\text{"Ya ayyuhalladheena amanoo awfoo bil-'uqud" (5:1)}$$

### Linguistic & Juristic Precision
* **Linguistic Derivation:** The term *'Aqd* (plural *'Uqud*) literally signifies the firm tying of a knot, an indissoluble binding.
* **Imam Ibn Jarir al-Tabari** records from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, and Qatadah that *Al-'Uqud* encapsulates three concentric spheres of obligation:
  1. **The Divine Covenant (*'Ahd Allah*):** The primordial and revealed obligations of monotheism, the boundaries of halal and haram, and adherence to revelation.
  2. **Mutual Human Compacts:** Commercial contracts, pledges, treaties, alliances, and marriage covenants entered into between individuals or states.
  3. **Personal Oaths & Vows (*Ayman wa Nudhur*):** Binding commitments one imposes upon oneself before God.
* **Imam Abu Bakr al-Jassas** in *Ahkam al-Qur'an* establishes that this verse establishes the foundational legal maxim in Islamic jurisprudence: **"The default in contracts and conditions is validity and binding force"** (*Al-asl fil-'uqud al-wafa'*), invalidating only that which explicitly contravenes divine law.

## 2. The Declaration of Perfection (*Ikmal ad-Deen*)

$$\text{"Al-yawma akmaltu lakum deenakum wa atmamtu 'alaykum ni'mati wa radeetu lakumul-Islama deena" (5:3)}$$

### Historical Occasion & Theological Significance
* **Imam Ibn Kathir** notes that this verse represents the greatest favor bestowed upon this Ummah. Revealing it on the **Day of Arafah**, on a Friday during the Prophet's Farewell Pilgrimage (10 AH), Allah declared the religion structurally and jurisprudentially complete.
* **Imam al-Bukhari** relates that a Jewish scholar said to Umar ibn al-Khattab: *"O Commander of the Faithful! There is a verse in your Book; had it been revealed to us Jews, we would have taken that day as an annual festival ('Eid)."* Umar replied: *"I know the exact day and place it was revealed; it was revealed to the Messenger of Allah standing at Arafah on a Friday."*
* **Theological Deductions:**
  1. **Closure of Legislative Additions:** The fundamentals of creed (*'Aqeedah*), worship (*'Ibadah*), and fundamental ethics (*Akhlaq*) are sealed. No individual, scholar, or council possesses the authority to innovate or abrogate core tenets.
  2. **Self-Sufficiency of Divine Guidance:** Humanity requires no supplementary moral or philosophical framework outside of the revealed guidance.

## 3. Transcendent Justice: Equity Toward Antagonists

$$\text{"Wa la yajrimannakum shana'anu qawmin 'ala alla ta'diloo, i'diloo huwa aqrabu lit-taqwa" (5:8)}$$

### Exegetical Exposition
* **Imam al-Qurtubi** highlights that whereas Surah An-Nisa (4:135) commanded justice even against one's own self and parents, Surah Al-Ma'idah demands justice **even toward sworn enemies and hostile factions**.
* *Shana'an* denotes deep-seated resentment, intense hatred, and historical antagonism.
* The verse commands: Let not your visceral hatred for an enemy community cause you to deviate into injustice, fraudulent testimony, or disproportionate retribution.
* **The Golden Axiom:** *"I'diloo huwa aqrabu lit-taqwa"* (Be just; that is nearer to Taqwa!). In Sunni political jurisprudence, the legitimacy of a judge or state is fundamentally anchored in its ability to execute impartial justice without emotional bias or tribal prejudice.

## 4. The Cosmic Inviolability of Life: Habil and Qabil

### The Exposition of Ayah 32
$$\text{"Man qatala nafsan bi-ghayri nafsin aw fasadin fee al-ardi faka'annama qatala an-nasa jamee'a, wa man ahyaha faka'annama ahya an-nasa jamee'a"}$$

* **Contextual Anchor:** Following the tragic murder of Habil by Qabil—where the righteous brother chose passive restraint rather than retaliatory fratricide (*"If you stretch your hand against me to kill me, I will not stretch my hand against you to kill you"*), Allah decreed this universal standard for humanity.
* **Imam Fakhr al-Din al-Razi** in *Mafatih al-Ghayb* addresses the question: *How can killing one soul be equivalent to killing all of humanity?*
  1. **Assault on the Sanctity of Existence:** The murderer attacks the very principle of the sacredness of human life instituted by the Creator. By arrogating the right to terminate an innocent soul, he has philosophically legitimized the slaughter of all souls.
  2. **Social Terror & Moral Collapse:** One unjust murder unbinds societal restraint; left unchecked, it triggers cycles of vengeance that tear apart civil order.
  3. **The Mirror of Saving Life (*Ihya'*):** Rescuing one soul—whether from drowning, fire, starvation, sickness, or unjust execution—affirms the universal value of human existence, preserving the moral sanity of the entire human species.

## 5. The Hierarchy of Revelation: The Qur'an as *Muhaymin*

$$\text{"Wa anzalna ilaykal-kitaba bil-haqqi musaddiqan lima bayna yadayhi minal-kitabi wa muhayminan 'alayh" (5:48)}$$

### The Meaning of *Muhaymin*
* **Imam al-Tabari** compiles the interpretations of the early authorities:
  - **Ibn Abbas:** *Muhaymin* means *Ameen* (a trustworthy guardian and custodian over all previous scriptures).
  - **Sa'id ibn Jubayr & Mujahid:** It signifies *Shahid* (a witness and arbiter) confirming the original divine truths of the Torah and Gospel while exposing human interpolations, textual distortions (*Tahrif*), and obsolete local dispensations.
  - **Al-Khalil & Classical Lexicographers:** A *Muhaymin* is one who watches over, preserves, and exercises supreme authority over an object.
* **The Dual Nature of Divine Law:**
  - *"Li-kullin ja'alna minkum shir'atan wa minhaja"* (To each of you We prescribed a law and an open pathway).
  - While historical legal ordinances (*Shara'i'*) differed according to time and circumstance, the core epistemic creed (*Tawhid*, moral accountability, resurrection) was identical across all prophets.
  - The coexistence of past communities was intended as a moral arena: *"Fastabiqul-khayrat"* (Compete vigorously in virtuous deeds!).

## 6. Sifatullah: Rebuttal of Cosmic Slander (*Bal Yadahu Mabsutatani*)

$$\text{"Wa qalatil-Yahoodu yadullahi maghloolah, ghullat aydeehim wa lu'inoo bima qaloo, bal yadahu mabsutatani yunfiqu kayfa yasha'" (5:64)}$$

### Orthodox Sunni Doctrine (*Madhab Ahl al-Sunnah*)
* **Imam Ibn Kathir** and **Imam al-Qurtubi** explain that when financial constriction afflicted certain groups in Madinah, some voiced the blasphemy: *"Allah's hand is chained"* (meaning He is stingy or unable to provide).
* **The Divine Reply:**
  - Allah cursed them for this statement.
  - He affirmed: *"Bal Yadahu Mabsutatani"* (Nay, both His hands are widely outstretched; He spends however He wills!).
* **Methodological Safeguard:**
  - Orthodox Sunni theology affirms the attribute of *Yad* (Hand) as Allah affirmed it for Himself—denoting His supreme generosity, bestowal, and sovereign agency.
  - Free from **Tashbih** (resembling creation) and free from **Ta'teel** (emptying the words of their divine meaning).
  - *"Laysa kamithlihi shay'un wa Huwas-Samee'ul-Baseer"* (There is nothing like unto Him, and He is the All-Hearing, All-Seeing).

## 7. The Eschatological Cross-Examination of Isa ibn Maryam

The closing verses of Surah Al-Ma'idah (5:116–120) contain one of the most awe-inspiring dialogues in religious history:

$$\text{"Wa idh qalallahu ya 'Isa-bna Maryama a'anta qulta lin-nasi-ttakhidhoonee wa ummiya ilahayni min doonillah..."}$$

### The Exegetical Drama
1. **The Divine Inquiry:** Allah addresses Isa on the Day of Resurrection before the entire assembly of creation: *"Did you say to people, 'Take me and my mother as two deities besides Allah'?"*
2. **The Sublime Response of Adab (*Subhanaka!*):**
   - Isa begins with absolute glorification: *"Subhanak!"* (Exalted and Transcendent are You!).
   - *"Never could I say that which I had no right to say! If I had said it, You would surely have known it. You know what is within myself, and I do not know what is within Yourself. Indeed, You are the Knower of the unseen!"*
3. **The Fidelity of Prophethood:**
   - Isa clarifies that he uttered only what he was commanded: *"Worship Allah, my Lord and your Lord!"*
   - He was a witness over them while among them, and upon his bodily ascension, Allah alone was the Watcher over them (*Al-Raqeeb*).
4. **The Supreme Intercession Formula:**
   $$\text{"In tu'adhdhibhum fa'innahum 'ibaduk, wa in taghfir lahum fa'innaka antal-'Azeezul-Hakeem" (5:118)}$$
   - *"If You punish them, indeed they are Your servants; and if You forgive them, indeed You are the Exalted in Might, the Wise."*
   - **Prophetic Sunnah:** Imam Ahmad and the Sunan compilers record that the Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him) stood in night prayer reciting this single ayah repeatedly until the dawn broke, weeping and pleading for his Ummah.
5. **The Sovereign Seal:**
   $$\text{"Lillahi mulkus-samawati wal-ardi wa ma feehin, wa Huwa 'ala kulli shay'in qadeer" (5:120)}$$
   - Sealing the Surah by restoring all dominion, kingship, and cosmic authority exclusively to Allah alone.
