# Sunni Exegesis Monograph: Surah As-Saffat
### The Symphony of Angelic Discipline, Prophetic Resolution & Unshakeable Tawhid

---

## Chapter 1: The Angelic Orders & Cosmic Defense (Ayat 1–11)
Imam al-Tabari explains that *As-Saaffat* refers to the angels lining up in prayer and cosmic assignment just as believers line up behind the Imam. The transition from celestial oaths to the core assertion *Inna Ilahakum la-Wahid* establishes that the vast, intimidating machinery of the cosmos serves a single, unpartnered Sovereign.

## Chapter 2: The Inquest and the Tree of Zaqqum (Ayat 12–74)
The terrifying arrest *Waqifoohum innahum mas'ooloon* breaks the arrogance of those who lived without accountability. In Hell, the botanical nightmare of *Zaqqum* serves as retribution for those who consumed ill-gotten wealth and indulged in mocking arrogance. Its bitter clusters burn the throat, followed by boiling brine.

## Chapter 3: The Sacrificial Surrender of Ibrahim & Ismail (Ayat 75–113)
The climax of the prophetic narratives is the intimate dialogue between Ibrahim and his youth Ismail. Notice the pedagogical gentleness: *Fa-unzur madha tara* (See what you think). Faith is not coerced; it is embraced. When both submitted (*Falamma aslama*), the trial was won in the realm of the heart, and the physical sacrifice was replaced by the heavenly ram.

## Chapter 4: The Final Triumph & Sovereign Doxology (Ayat 114–182)
The surah concludes with divine reassurance: *Wa inna jundana lahumu al-ghaliboon*. Truth may face seasons of hardship, but the moral arch of history bends inexorably toward prophetic victory. The final three verses form a golden triad of praise, peace, and monotheistic exaltation.
