# Classical Sunni Exegetical Monograph: Surah An-Naml
### The Dominion of Wisdom: Prophetic Power, The Ant's Compassion & Cosmic Sovereignty

---

## 1. The Opening Illumination & Musa at the Burning Bush

Surah An-Naml opens with the letters *Ta-Seen*, introducing the Qur'an as *Ayatu al-Qur'ani wa Kitabin Mubeen*—signs of the Qur'an and an illuminating Book that guides and brings glad tidings to the righteous who establish prayer, give zakah, and possess unwavering certainty (*Yooqinoon*) in the Hereafter.

The narrative immediately turns to Musa seeking a burning brand for his freezing family in the Sinai desert. Approaching the fire, a celestial voice proclaims:
*Buriqa man fee an-nari wa man hawlaha wa subhana Allahi rabbi al-'alameen*
("Blessed is whoever is at the fire and whoever is around it. And exalted is Allah, Lord of the worlds").

Imam al-Tabari and Imam al-Razi explain that the light Musa witnessed was not a chemical combustion, but a manifestation of divine radiance (*Nur*). Musa casts his staff, which writhes like an agile, darting serpent (*Ka'annaha Jann*), causing him to turn back in instinctual flight. Allah commands him: *"O Musa, do not fear; indeed, the messengers do not fear in My presence."*

He is given the second sign: drawing his hand from his bosom, it emerges radiant white without leprosy or disease (*Bayda'a min ghayri soo'*). Yet when presented to Pharaoh and his ruling council, they reject these signs:
*Wa jahadu biha was-tayqanat-ha anfusuhum zulman wa 'uluwwa*
("And they rejected them, though their souls were convinced of them, out of injustice and haughty pride").
This verse provides the classical Sunni psychological diagnosis of willful disbelief: it was not an intellectual deficit, but moral arrogance and political pride silencing inner cognitive conviction.

---

## 2. The Legacy of Knowledge: Dawud & Sulayman

The surah transitions to the golden age of prophetic dominion granted to Dawud and Sulayman:
*Wa laqad atayna Dawuda wa Sulaymana 'ilma, wa qala al-hamdu lillahi alladhi faddalana 'ala katheerin min 'ibadihi al-mu'mineen*
("And We had certainly given to Dawud and Sulayman knowledge, and they said: 'Praise be to Allah, who has favored us over many of His believing servants'").

Imam al-Qurtubi observes that despite possessing supreme earthly royalty, vast armies, and miraculous gifts, their immediate reaction was not vanity, but *Al-Hamd* (praise) for the gift of *'Ilm* (sacred knowledge). Knowledge is thus crowned as the supreme divine bounty, elevated far above wealth, military prowess, or political empire.

Sulayman inherited from Dawud (*Wa waritha Sulaymanu Dawud*) not physical gold or land—for the Prophet ﷺ stated: *"We prophets do not leave inheritance; whatever we leave behind is charity"* (Sahih al-Bukhari)—but inherited prophethood, wisdom, and the leadership of the believers.

Sulayman proclaimed to his people: *Ya ayyuha an-nasu 'ullimna mantiqa at-tayri wa ooteena min kulli shay'* ("O people, we have been taught the language of birds, and we have been given of all things"). His military divisions assembled in rigorous, disciplined formations: jinn, men, and airborne flocks, held in check so that the ranks marched as a unified body (*Yooza'oon*).

---

## 3. The Valley of the Ants & The Sublime Du'a of Gratitude

As the grand army entered the Valley of the Ants (*Wadi an-Naml*), a small female ant perceived the approaching tremor and called out:
*Ya ayyuha an-namlu udkhulu masakinakum la yahtimannakum Sulaymanu wa junuduhu wa hum la yash'uroon*
("O ants, enter your dwellings that you not be crushed by Sulayman and his soldiers while they perceive not").

Imam al-Qurtubi and Imam Ibn al-Qayyim highlight the extraordinary linguistic and moral precision of this single address:
1. She gave a vocative call (*Ya ayyuha*).
2. She addressed her entire collective (*An-naml*).
3. She issued an urgent command (*Udkhulu*).
4. She directed them to safe shelter (*Masakinakum*).
5. She warned of danger (*La yahtimannakum*).
6. She identified the specific source (*Sulaymanu wa junuduhu*).
7. She provided an ethical excuse for the army (*Wa hum la yash'uroon*—affirming that if they crush the colony, it would be unintentional, completely exonerating the prophet from tyranny or cruelty!).

Overhearing this tiny voice through miraculous linguistic understanding, Sulayman smiled with joyful laughter (*Fa-tabassama dahikan min qawliha*). Immediately, he turned his heart upward in the timeless prayer of righteous consciousness:
*Rabbi awzi'ni an ashkura ni'mataka allati an'amta 'alayya wa 'ala walidayya wa an a'mala salihan tardahu wa adkhilni bi-rahmatika fee 'ibadika as-saliheen*
("My Lord, inspire me to be grateful for Your favor which You have bestowed upon me and upon my parents, and to do righteousness of which You will be pleased, and admit me by Your mercy among Your righteous servants").

---

## 4. The Intelligence of the Hoopoe & The Geopolitics of Sheba

Sulayman conducted a meticulous inspection of his airborne scout divisions (*Wa tafaqqada at-tayr*), discovering the absence of the Hoopoe (*Hudhud*). Demonstrating rigorous military discipline, he demanded an explanation: *"I will surely punish him with a severe punishment or slaughter him unless he brings me a clear justification."*

The Hoopoe returned shortly, presenting strategic reconnaissance:
*Ahat-tu bi-ma lam tuhit bihi wa ji'tuka min Saba'in bi-naba'in yaqeen*
("I have encompassed that which you have not encompassed, and I have come to you from Sheba with certain news").

The Hoopoe reported that Sheba was ruled by a woman endowed with supreme earthly abundance and a magnificent royal throne (*Laha 'arshun 'adheem*), yet culturally crippled by solar idolatry: *Wajadtuha wa qawmaha yasjuduna lish-shamsi min dooni Allah*. The bird expressed astonishment that creatures endowed with intellect could prostrate to a celestial body while ignoring the One who brings forth what is hidden in the heavens and the earth.

### The Imperial Letter
Sulayman tested the bird's credibility by sending an official diplomatic missive:
*Innahu min Sulaymana wa innahu bismillahi ar-rahmani ar-raheem, alla ta'lu 'alayya wa'tooni muslimeen*
("Indeed, it is from Sulayman, and indeed it reads: In the name of Allah, the Entirely Merciful, the Especially Merciful: Be not haughty against me, but come to me in total surrender").

---

## 5. The Wisdom of Bilqis & The Translocation of the Throne

Queen Bilqis convened her parliamentary council (*Al-Mala'*), stating that she never decides an affair without their presence. Her nobles displayed martial readiness: *"We are men of great strength and mighty war, but the command is yours."*

Bilqis revealed profound diplomatic and political acumen:
*Inna al-muluka idha dakhalu qaryatan afsaduha wa ja'alu a'izzata ahliha adhilla, wa kadhalika yaf'aloon*
("Indeed kings, when they enter a city, ruin it and make the most honorable of its inhabitants humiliated; and thus do they do").

She resolved to send diplomatic gifts of gold and jewels to assess whether Sulayman was an ordinary territorial conqueror or a genuine prophet. Sulayman rejected the bribe with utter disdain: *"Do you provide me with wealth? What Allah has given me is far better than what He has given you! Rather, it is you who rejoice in your gifts!"*

### The Miracle of Translocation
Before her royal retinue arrived, Sulayman asked his assembly: *"Which of you will bring me her throne before they come to me in surrender?"*
- A powerful *'Ifrit* from among the jinn offered: *"I will bring it to you before you rise from your council seat, and indeed, I am strong and trustworthy."*
- But the one who possessed knowledge of the Scripture (*Alladhi 'indahu 'ilmun mina al-kitab*—identified in classical tafsir by Ibn 'Abbas, Tabari, and Baghawi as Asif ibn Barkhiya, who knew the Greatest Name of Allah) said:
  *Ana ateeka bihi qabla an yartadda ilayka tarfuk*
  ("I will bring it to you before your gaze returns to you—in the twinkling of an eye!").

Instantly, the massive, jewel-encrusted throne materialized before Sulayman. Rather than gloating, Sulayman proclaimed the quintessential motto of God-consciousness:
*Hadha min fadli rabbi li-yabluwani a-ashkuru am akfur, wa man shakara fa-innama yashkuru li-nafsihi, wa man kafara fa-inna rabbi ghaniyyun kareem*
("This is from the favor of my Lord to test me whether I will be grateful or ungrateful! And whoever is grateful is grateful only for his own soul, and whoever is ungrateful—indeed, my Lord is Free of need and Generous").

---

## 6. The Paved Palace & Bilqis's Conversion

Sulayman ordered her throne to be subtly disguised to test her perceptive intelligence. When Bilqis arrived, she was asked: *"Is your throne like this?"* With brilliant intellectual balance, she replied: *Ka'annahu huwa* ("It is as if it were it!"), neither blindly asserting identity nor foolishly denying resemblance.

She was then invited into the royal pavilion:
*Qeela lahad-khuli as-sarh, fa-lamma ra'at-hu hasibat-hu lujjatan wa kashafat 'an saqayha*
("She was told: 'Enter the palace.' But when she saw it, she thought it was a body of deep water and uncovered her shins").

Sulayman gently clarified: *Innahu sarhun mumarradun min qawareer* ("Indeed, it is a palace paved with smooth crystal glass"). Looking through the transparent crystal, she saw the pristine water flowing beneath with fish gliding unhindered. 

The realization struck her with illuminating force: just as the crystal floor appeared to be water yet was solid glass, the material sun she had worshiped was merely a created object through which divine light operates. Perceiving the sublime transcendence of the Creator, she cast away polytheism and proclaimed:
*Rabbi inni zalamtu nafsi wa aslamtu ma'a Sulaymana lillahi rabbi al-'alameen*
("My Lord, indeed I have wronged myself, and I submit with Sulayman to Allah, Lord of the worlds").

## 7. The Five Cosmic Interrogations: A-Ilahun Ma'allah?

The surah moves from human royalty to the ultimate theater of divine omnipotence, confronting humanity with five undeniable cosmological questions, each followed by the thunderous refrain: **A-ilahun ma'allah?** (Is there any deity with Allah?):

1. **The Architecture of Ecology (Verse 60):** *Who created the heavens and the earth and sent down rain from the sky, causing gardens of breathtaking splendor to sprout, whose trees you could never grow?* Is there a deity with Allah? Nay, they are a people who set up equals!
2. **The Planetary Foundation (Verse 61):** *Who made the earth a stable resting place (*Qararan*), placed rivers in its valleys, set firm mountains to anchor it, and placed an invisible dividing partition (*Hajiz*) between the two seas?* Is there a deity with Allah? Nay, most of them do not know!
3. **The Answer to Human Desperation (Verse 62):** *Am-man yujeebu al-mudtarra idha da'ahu wa yakshifu as-soo' wa yaj'alukum khulafa'a al-ard?* ("Who responds to the desperate soul when he calls upon Him, removes distress, and makes you inheritors of the earth?"). Is there a deity with Allah? Little do you remember!
4. **Celestial Navigation & Harbingers of Mercy (Verse 63):** *Who guides you through the pitch darkness of land and sea via the stars, and sends the atmospheric winds as joyful glad tidings before His rain?* Is there a deity with Allah? Exalted is Allah above what they associate!
5. **Origination, Recreation & Cosmic Provision (Verse 64):** *Who originates the creation of life from nothingness, then recreates it after death, and provides your sustenance from the sky and the earth?* Is there a deity with Allah? Say: Bring your proof, if you are truthful!

## 8. Eschatological Unveiling & The Craftsmanship of Moving Mountains

The surah closes with apocalyptic and geophysical realities:

### The Beast of the Earth (Dabbat al-Ard)
*Wa idha waqa'a al-qawlu 'alayhim akhrajna lahum dabbatan mina al-ardi tukallimuhum*
("And when the decree falls upon them, We will bring forth for them a creature from the earth speaking to them, because humanity was not certain of Our signs").
Imam Ibn Kathir records authentic traditions that this emergence occurs near the end of time, marking the believers with radiant beauty and the deniers with disgrace.

### The Moving Mountains & Divine Engineering (Verse 88)
*Wa tara al-jibala tahsabuha jamidatan wa hiya tamurru marra as-sahab, sun'a Allahi alladhi atqana kulla shay', innahu khabeerun bi-ma taf'aloon*
("And you see the mountains, thinking them rigid and motionless, while they pass like the passing of clouds. It is the craftsmanship of Allah, who perfected all things. Indeed, He is Aware of what you do").

Classical exegetes, including Fakhr al-Din al-Razi, noted that while this verse describes eschatological mechanics when the earth's gravity and structure dissolve, it simultaneously reveals the absolute mastery (*Atqana*) and subtle planetary movement underlying divine creation.

### The Final Commission
The Prophet is instructed to declare his foundational mission:
1. *Innama umirtu an a'buda rabba hadhihi al-baldah* (To worship the Lord of the sacred city of Mecca).
2. *Wa an atluwa al-Qur'an* (To continuously recite the living revelation to mankind).
3. Whoever chooses guidance benefits his own soul; whoever strays, the Prophet is merely a warner.
4. *Wa qul al-hamdu lillahi sa-yureekum ayatihi fa-ta'rifoonaha* ("And say: All praise belongs to Allah. He will show you His signs, and you will recognize them. And your Lord is never heedless of what you do").
