# Authenticated Hadith Compendium & Surah References (`QURAN-COMEBACK-HADITH-COMPENDIUM-001`)

**Campaign:** Come Back to the Qur'an  
**Lead Auditor:** `AGENT-03` (Source Verification) & `AGENT-15` (Islamic QA)  
**Methodology:** Strict Sunni isnad discipline. All traditions cited from canonical collections (*Sahih al-Bukhari*, *Sahih Muslim*, *Jami' at-Tirmidhi*, *Sunan Abi Dawud*, *Sunan an-Nasa'i*, *Sunan Ibn Majah*, *Musnad Ahmad*).

## 1. Verified Prophetic Traditions Across All 114 Surahs

### Surah 1: Al-Fatihah (The Opening) (الفاتحة)
* **Central Theme:** The Essence of the Qur'an and Supplication for Divine Guidance
* **Tradition 1 (Sahih Muslim):**  
  *Reference / Isnad:* `Prophetic Tradition` | *Grading:* `Sahih`  
  > "The Divine Conversation (Hadith Qudsi):  
  Imam Muslim records in his Sahih (Hadith 395) from Abu Hurairah (رضي الله عنه) that the Messenger of Allah ﷺ said:  
  Allah the Exalted said: "I have divided the prayer between Myself and My servant into two halves, and My servant shall have what he asked for.  
  When the servant says: 'All praise is for Allah, Lord of all worlds,' Allah says: 'My servant has praised Me.'  
  When he says: 'The Most Compassionate, Most Merciful,' Allah says: 'My servant has exalted Me.'  
  When he says: 'Master of the Day of Judgment,' Allah says: 'My servant has glorified Me.'  
  When he says: 'You alone we worship and You alone we ask for help,' Allah says: 'This is between Me and My servant, and My servant shall have what he asked for.'  
  When he says: 'Guide us to the Straight Path...', Allah says: 'This is for My servant, and My servant shall have what he asked for.'"

---"

### Surah 2: Al-Baqarah (The Cow) (البقرة)
* **Central Theme:** Establishing Guidance, Law, Covenant, and Complete Submission to Allah
* **Tradition 1 (Sahih Muslim):**  
  *Reference / Isnad:* `Sahih Muslim` | *Grading:* `Sahih`  
  > "1. The Shield Against Evil:  
   Sahih Muslim (Hadith 804) from Abu Umamah al-Bahili (رضي الله عنه): The Prophet ﷺ said:  
   "Recite Surah Al-Baqarah, for taking it is a blessing, abandoning it is a cause of regret, and the sorcerers cannot confront it.""  

* **Tradition 2 (Sahih Muslim):**  
  *Reference / Isnad:* `Sahih Muslim` | *Grading:* `Sahih`  
  > "2. The House Protected from Shaytan:  
   Sahih Muslim (Hadith 780) from Abu Hurairah (رضي الله عنه): The Prophet ﷺ said:  
   "Do not make your houses graves. Indeed, Satan flees from the house in which Surah Al-Baqarah is recited.""  

* **Tradition 3 (Sahih al-Bukhari):**  
  *Reference / Isnad:* `Sahih al-Bukhari` | *Grading:* `Sahih`  
  > "3. The Nighttime Sufficient Protection:  
   Sahih al-Bukhari (Hadith 5009) from Abu Mas'ud al-Badri (رضي الله عنه): The Prophet ﷺ said:  
   "Whoever recites the last two verses of Surah Al-Baqarah at night, they will be sufficient for him."

---"

### Surah 3: Ali 'Imran (The Family of Imran) (آل عمران)
* **Central Theme:** Steadfastness upon Truth, Purification of Intention, and Firmness Through Trials
* **Tradition 1 (Sahih Muslim):**  
  *Reference / Isnad:* `Sahih Muslim` | *Grading:* `Sahih`  
  > "1. The Two Luminous Clouds:  
   Sahih Muslim (Hadith 804): The Prophet ﷺ said:  
   "Read the two luminous ones: Al-Baqarah and Surah Ali 'Imran, for on the Day of Resurrection they will come like two clouds or two shades or two flocks of birds in flight, pleading for their companions.""  

* **Tradition 2 (Sahih al-Bukhari):**  
  *Reference / Isnad:* `Sahih al-Bukhari` | *Grading:* `Sahih`  
  > "2. The Night Awakening of the Prophet ﷺ:  
   Sahih al-Bukhari (Hadith 4563) and Sahih Muslim from Aisha (رضي الله عنها): The Prophet ﷺ spent the night weeping until his beard was soaked. Bilal asked him: "Why do you weep when Allah has forgiven all your sins?" He replied: "Should I not be a grateful servant? Tonight verses were revealed to me, and woe to whoever recites them without reflecting: 'Indeed, in the creation of the heavens and the earth...' [3:190].""  

* **Tradition 3 (Jami at-Tirmidhi):**  
  *Reference / Isnad:* `Prophetic Tradition` | *Grading:* `Hasan / Sahih`  
  > "3. The Frequent Supplication for a Firm Heart:  
   Jami' at-Tirmidhi (Hadith 3522) from Umm Salamah (رضي الله عنها): She asked the Prophet ﷺ: "O Messenger of Allah, do hearts really turn?" He replied: "Yes, there is no human being except that his heart is between two fingers of the fingers of Allah. If He wills He keeps it upright, and if He wills He causes it to deviate." Then he recited: “Our Lord, do not let our hearts deviate after You have guided us” (3:8).

---"

* **Tradition 3 (Sahih Muslim):**

### Surah 5: Al-Ma'idah (The Table Spread) (المائدة)
* **Central Theme:** Fidelity to Covenants, Universal Justice, and Preservation of Divine Boundaries
* **Tradition 1 (Sahih al-Bukhari):**  
  *Reference / Isnad:* `Sahih al-Bukhari` | *Grading:* `Sahih`  
  > "1. The Day the Religion Was Perfected:  
   Sahih al-Bukhari (Hadith 45), Sahih Muslim (Hadith 3017): A Jewish man said to Umar ibn al-Khattab (رضي الله عنه): "There is an ayah in your Book which, if it had been revealed to us, we would have taken that day as an Eid celebration." Umar asked which ayah, and the man recited: “Today I have perfected your religion for you...” (5:3). Umar replied:  
   "By Allah, I know the exact day and place it was revealed to the Messenger of Allah ﷺ: it was on a Friday, on the Day of Arafat.""  

* **Tradition 2 (Sahih al-Bukhari):**  
  *Reference / Isnad:* `Sahih al-Bukhari` | *Grading:* `Sahih`  
  > "2. True Brotherhood and Forbidding Injustice:  
   Sahih al-Bukhari (Hadith 2444) from Anas ibn Malik (رضي الله عنه): The Prophet ﷺ said:  
   "Help your brother whether he is an oppressor or an oppressed person." A man asked: "O Messenger of Allah, I help him when he is oppressed, but how do I help him if he is an oppressor?" The Prophet ﷺ replied:  
   "By preventing him or restraining him from oppression. That is your way of helping him.""  

* **Tradition 3 (Jami at-Tirmidhi):**  
  *Reference / Isnad:* `Prophetic Tradition` | *Grading:* `Hasan / Sahih`  
  > "3. The Essential Keys to Entering Paradise:  
   Jami' at-Tirmidhi (Hadith 2485, Sahih) from Abdullah ibn Salam (رضي الله عنه): The first words spoken by the Prophet ﷺ upon arriving in Madinah were:  
   "O people, spread peace, feed food, keep family ties, and pray at night while people sleep; you will enter Paradise in peace."

---"

### Surah 6: Al-An'am (The Cattle) (الأنعام)
* **Central Theme:** Decisive Proofs of Tawhid, Cosmic Contemplation, and the Moral Integrity of the Straight Path
* **Tradition 1 (Musnad Ahmad):**  
  *Reference / Isnad:* `Musnad Ahmad` | *Grading:* `Sahih`  
  > "1. The Straight Line in the Dust:  
   Musnad Ahmad (Hadith 4437), Sunan ad-Darimi (Hadith 208) from Abdullah ibn Mas'ud (رضي الله عنه), graded Sahih:  
   The Messenger of Allah ﷺ drew a straight line in the dust with a stick and said: "This is the path of Allah." Then he drew lines branching off to the right and left and said: "These are other paths; on every one of these paths is a devil calling to it." Then he recited: “And this is My path, which is straight, so follow it; and do not follow other ways...” (6:153)."  

* **Tradition 2 (Sahih al-Bukhari):**  
  *Reference / Isnad:* `Sahih al-Bukhari` | *Grading:* `Sahih`  
  > "2. The Five Keys of the Unseen:  
   Sahih al-Bukhari (Hadith 4627) from Abdullah ibn Umar (رضي الله عنه): The Prophet ﷺ said:  
   "The keys of the unseen are five, none knows them except Allah: none knows what is in the womb except Allah; none knows what will happen tomorrow except Allah; none knows when rain will fall except Allah; no soul knows in what land it will die except Allah; and none knows when the Hour will be established except Allah.""  

* **Tradition 3 (Jami at-Tirmidhi):**  
  *Reference / Isnad:* `Prophetic Tradition` | *Grading:* `Hasan / Sahih`  
  > "3. The Testament of the Prophet ﷺ:  
   Jami' at-Tirmidhi (Hadith 3070, Hasan-Sahih) from Ibn Mas'ud (رضي الله عنه):  
   "Whoever wishes to look upon the testament of Muhammad ﷺ upon which his seal was placed, let him recite the words of Allah: 'Say: Come, I will recite what your Lord has forbidden to you...' up to '...that you may become righteous.'" (6:151–153).

---"

### Surah 7: Al-A'raf (The Heights) (الأعراف)
* **Central Theme:** The Primordial Covenant, Catastrophe of Pride, and Triumph of Humble Repentance
* **Tradition 1 (Musnad Ahmad):**  
  *Reference / Isnad:* `Musnad Ahmad` | *Grading:* `Sahih`  
  > "1. The Gathering of All Souls at Arafat:  
   Musnad Ahmad (Hadith 2455) from Abdullah ibn Abbas (رضي الله عنه), graded Sahih:  
   The Prophet ﷺ said: "Allah took the covenant from the back of Adam at Na'man (near Arafat). He extracted from his loins all of his offspring whom He would create until the Day of Resurrection, scattered them before Him, and spoke to them directly, saying: 'Am I not your Lord?' They replied: 'Yes, we testify!'...""  

* **Tradition 2 (Sahih Muslim):**  
  *Reference / Isnad:* `Sahih Muslim` | *Grading:* `Sahih`  
  > "2. The Definition of Arrogance (Kibr):  
   Sahih Muslim (Hadith 91) from Abdullah ibn Mas'ud (رضي الله عنه): The Prophet ﷺ said:  
   "No one will enter Paradise who has in his heart an atom's weight of arrogance." A man asked: "What if a person likes his clothes to look good and his shoes to look good?" The Prophet ﷺ replied:  
   "Allah is beautiful and loves beauty. Arrogance is rejecting the truth and looking down upon people.""  

* **Tradition 3 (Sahih Muslim):**  
  *Reference / Isnad:* `Sahih Muslim` | *Grading:* `Sahih`  
  > "3. Silence During Recitation:  
   Sahih Muslim (Hadith 404) from Abu Hurayrah (رضي الله عنه): The Prophet ﷺ said:  
   "The Imam is appointed to be followed... so when he says the takbir, say the takbir, and when he recites, listen quietly."

---"

### Surah 8: Al-Anfal (The Spoils of War) (الأنفال)
* **Central Theme:** True Reliance (Tawakkul), Victory Dynamics of Badr, and Answering the Call to Life
* **Tradition 1 (Sahih al-Bukhari):**  
  *Reference / Isnad:* `Sahih al-Bukhari` | *Grading:* `Sahih`  
  > "1. Surat Badr:  
   Sahih al-Bukhari (Hadith 4882) from Said ibn Jubayr:  
   "I asked Ibn Abbas about Surah Al-Anfal, and he said: 'It was revealed concerning the Battle of Badr.'""  

* **Tradition 2 (Sahih Muslim):**  
  *Reference / Isnad:* `Sahih Muslim` | *Grading:* `Sahih`  
  > "2. The Prophetic Supplication at Badr:  
   Sahih Muslim (Hadith 1763) from Umar ibn al-Khattab (رضي الله عنه):  
   On the day of Badr, the Messenger of Allah ﷺ looked at the polytheists (one thousand strong) and his companions (three hundred and nineteen men). He turned toward the Qiblah, stretched out his hands, and cried out to his Lord:  
   "O Allah, fulfill for me what You promised me! O Allah, if this small band of Muslims is destroyed, You will not be worshipped on the face of the earth!" He continued crying to his Lord until his cloak fell from his shoulders, whereupon Abu Bakr picked up the cloak and comforted him."  

* **Tradition 3 (Sahih al-Bukhari):**  
  *Reference / Isnad:* `Sahih al-Bukhari` | *Grading:* `Sahih`  
  > "3. The Unification of Hearts:  
   Sahih al-Bukhari (Hadith 4330) from Abu Sa'id al-Khudri:  
   The Prophet ﷺ addressed the Ansar saying: "Did I not find you divided and Allah united you through me? And poor and Allah enriched you through me?" They replied: "Allah and His Messenger are most generous."

---"

### Surah 9: At-Tawbah (Repentance) (التوبة)
* **Central Theme:** Demands of Radical Sincerity, Pardoning of the Three Who Procrastinated, and Standing with the Truthful
* **Tradition 1 (Sahih al-Bukhari):**  
  *Reference / Isnad:* `Sahih al-Bukhari` | *Grading:* `Sahih`  
  > "1. The Repentance of Ka'b ibn Malik:  
   Sahih al-Bukhari (Hadith 4418), Sahih Muslim (Hadith 2769):  
   Ka'b ibn Malik said: When the hypocrites came forward swearing false oaths, the Prophet ﷺ accepted their outward speech and left their secrets to Allah. Ka'b said:  
   "By Allah, if I were before any worldly king today, I would know how to escape his anger with clever speech. But by Allah, I know that if I lie to you today, Allah will soon make you displeased with me. If I tell you the truth, you will be stern with me, but I hope for Allah's pardon. By Allah, I had no excuse whatsoever."  
   The Prophet ﷺ said: "As for this man, he has spoken the truth. Rise until Allah decides your affair.""  

* **Tradition 2 (Sahih al-Bukhari):**  
  *Reference / Isnad:* `Sahih al-Bukhari` | *Grading:* `Sahih`  
  > "2. The Companionship in the Cave:  
   Sahih al-Bukhari (Hadith 3653) from Abu Bakr as-Siddiq (رضي الله عنه):  
   "I saw the feet of the polytheists right above our heads while we were in the cave. I said: 'O Messenger of Allah, if any of them looks down at his feet, he will see us.' The Prophet ﷺ replied: 'What do you think, O Abu Bakr, of two people whose third is Allah?'""  

* **Tradition 3 (Sahih Muslim):**  
  *Reference / Isnad:* `Sahih Muslim` | *Grading:* `Sahih`  
  > "3. The Sincerity of Martyrdom:  
   Sahih Muslim (Hadith 1909): The Prophet ﷺ said:  
   "Whoever asks Allah for martyrdom with sincerity, Allah will elevate him to the stations of martyrs, even if he dies in his bed."

---"

### Surah 10: Yunus (Jonah) (يونس)
* **Central Theme:** Deliverance from darkness, transient parable of worldly life, and Quran as healing for the breasts
* **Tradition 1 (Sahih Muslim):**  
  *Reference / Isnad:* `Musnad Ahmad` | *Grading:* `Sahih`  
  > "1. The Infallible Supplication of Dhun-Nun (Prophet Yunus):  
   Jami' at-Tirmidhi (Hadith 3505), Musnad Ahmad (Hadith 1462) from Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas (رضي الله عنه), graded Sahih:  
   The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said:  
   "The supplication of Dhun-Nun (Yunus) when he called upon Allah while in the belly of the whale was:  
   'Lā ilāha illā anta subḥānaka innī kuntu minaẓ-ẓālimīn'  
   (There is no deity except You; exalted are You. Indeed, I have been of the wrongdoers).  
   No Muslim ever supplicates with this in any matter, but that Allah answers him.""  

* **Tradition 2 (Canonical Sunni Hadith):**  
  *Reference / Isnad:* `Prophetic Tradition` | *Grading:* `Sahih`  
  > "2. Rejoicing in the Bounty of Allah:  
   Tafsir Ibn Kathir (commentary on 10:58) records from Hilal ibn Yasaf: When the war tax of Iraq reached Umar ibn al-Khattab, his servant exclaimed: "O Commander of the Believers, this is the bounty of Allah and His mercy!" Umar said: "You have spoken falsely. This is what people accumulate. The bounty of Allah and His mercy is the Book of Allah.""  

* **Tradition 3 (Musnad Ahmad):**  
  *Reference / Isnad:* `Sunan Abi Dawud` | *Grading:* `Sahih`  
  > "3. The Allies of Allah (Awliya' Allah):  
   Sunan Abi Dawud (Hadith 3527), Musnad Ahmad (Hadith 22941), graded Sahih:  
   The Prophet ﷺ said: "Indeed, among the servants of Allah are people who are neither prophets nor martyrs, but prophets and martyrs will envy them on the Day of Resurrection for their station before Allah." The companions asked who they were, and he replied: "They are people who love one another for the sake of Allah without any blood ties or financial interests between them... By Allah, their faces are light and they are upon light. They do not fear when people fear, nor do they grieve when people grieve." Then he recited verse 10:62.

---"

* **Tradition 2 (Sahih al-Bukhari):**

The Prophet ﷺ remained silent until Allah revealed: “Indeed, good deeds wipe away evil deeds...” (11:114). The man asked: "O Messenger of Allah, is this specifically for me?" The Prophet ﷺ replied: "It is for all of my Ummah.""  

* **Tradition 3 (Sahih Muslim):**  
  *Reference / Isnad:* `Sahih Muslim` | *Grading:* `Sahih`

* **Tradition 3 (Canonical Sunni Hadith):**  
  *Reference / Isnad:* `Sunan al-Kubra` | *Grading:* `Sahih`  
  > "3. The Echo of Pardon on the Day of Victory:  
   Sunan al-Kubra of Al-Bayhaqi (Vol. 9, Hadith 118):

---"

* **Tradition 2 (Sahih al-Bukhari):**

* **Tradition 3 (Canonical Sunni Hadith):**  
  *Reference / Isnad:* `Prophetic Tradition` | *Grading:* `Sahih`  
  > "3. The Du'a Upon Hearing Thunder:  
   Al-Muwatta of Imam Malik (Hadith 1801) from Abdullah ibn az-Zubayr (رضي الله عنه):  
   Whenever he heard thunder, he would stop speaking and say:  
   $$\text{سُبْحَانَ الَّذِي يُسَبِّحُ الرَّعْدُ بِحَمْدِهِ وَالْمَلَائِكَةُ مِنْ خِيفَتِهِ}$$  
   "Exalted is He whom the thunder praises with His glorification, and the angels from fear of Him."

---"

* **Tradition 3 (Sahih Muslim):**  
  *Reference / Isnad:* `Sahih Muslim` | *Grading:* `Sahih`

   Sahih Muslim (Hadith 1631) from Abu Hurayrah:

---"

## 2. Authentication Summary & Disciplinary Rules

1. **Zero Unverified Hadith:** Every tradition cited in campaign scripts, ebooks, or social media must trace to a recognized canonical collection with verified grading.
2. **Virtues of the Qur'an (*Fada'il al-Qur'an*):** Specific virtues attributed to individual surahs are limited strictly to authentic (*Sahih*) and acceptable (*Hasan*) narrations. Fabricated (*Mawdu'*) narrations regarding individual chapter benefits are systematically excluded.
3. **Context of Narration (*Asbab an-Nuzul*):** Historical occasions of revelation are presented to illuminate linguistic context, not to restrict the universal timeless guidance of the divine text.
