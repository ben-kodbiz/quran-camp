# Episode 3 Flagship Script (`QURAN-COMEBACK-SCRIPT-003`)

**Campaign:** Come Back to the Qur'an  
**Title:** *"Allah Is Closer Than You Think"*  
**Duration:** ~5 minutes (approx. 680 spoken words)  
**Tone:** Tender, reassuring, intimate, grounded  

---

## PRODUCTION SCRIPT & DIALOGUE

### 00:00 – 00:30 | SECTION 1: THE HOOK
**[VISUAL]**  
*A wide shot of a calm sea at dawn. Gentle fog sits above the water. A person sits alone by the shore, looking at the distant horizon where sky and water merge. Immense negative space above.*

**(VOICEOVER):**  
"Have you ever raised your hands in the middle of the night... and felt like your words were hitting the ceiling?

You whisper your fears. You ask for help. But inside your chest, a quiet doubt whispers:  
*Does Allah even hear me? Am I too sinful for Him to care? Why does He feel so far away?*

If you have ever felt that distance, you need to hear what Allah revealed in Surah Al-Baqarah."

---

### 00:30 – 01:30 | SECTION 2: THE AYAH & THE MISSING WORD
**[VISUAL]**  
*Macro close-up of open Qur'an pages, sunlight tracing across the verse. Text appears in calm, legible serif typography.*

**(VOICEOVER):**  
"In Surah Al-Baqarah, verse 186, Allah reveals:

$$\text{وَإِذَا سَأَلَكَ عِبَادِي عَنِّي فَإِنِّي قَرِيبٌ ۖ أُجِيبُ دَعْوَةَ الدَّاعِ إِذَا دَعَانِ}$$

*‘When My servants ask you ˹O Prophet˺ concerning Me: I am indeed near. I respond to the call of the caller when he calls upon Me.’*

Notice something extraordinary about this verse. Something scholars have pointed out for centuries.

Every time the companions asked the Prophet ﷺ about something in the Qur'an, Allah answered with the word: *Qul*—‘Say to them.’  
*They ask you about the crescent moons? Say to them...*  
*They ask you about spending? Say to them...*

Except here.

When they ask about Allah, Allah doesn’t say, *‘Tell them I am near.’*  
He drops the intermediary completely. He answers directly:  
**Fa-innī Qarīb:** *‘I am near.’*

Between your whisper and the Lord of the Throne, there is no barrier. No appointment. No waiting line. He is already there."

---

### 01:30 – 02:45 | SECTION 3: TAFSIR & THE BEDOUIN'S QUESTION
**[VISUAL]**  
*Atmospheric shot of desert sand dunes at sunset, long shadows stretching across the earth. The wind is completely still.*

**(VOICEOVER):**  
"Ibn Kathir records that a man once came to the Prophet ﷺ and asked:  
*‘O Messenger of Allah, is our Lord near so that we should whisper to Him, or is He far so that we should shout to Him?’*

And this verse descended as the answer.

You do not need to shout. You do not need to construct elaborate, eloquent poetry.  
The Prophet ﷺ said in an authentic hadith recorded in Sahih al-Bukhari and Sahih Muslim:  
*‘You are not calling upon one who is deaf or absent. You are calling upon One Who is All-Hearing, All-Near, and He is with you.’*

He hears the prayer you are too exhausted to say out loud.  
He hears the tear that falls into your pillow before you even understand why you are crying."

---

### 02:45 – 04:00 | SECTION 4: TADABBUR — THE GENEROSITY OF ALLAH
**[VISUAL]**  
*Close-up of hands raised gently in prayer in a quiet room, warm window light catching the silhouettes of the fingers.*

**(VOICEOVER):**  
"And look at how generous your Lord is.

In an authentic narration recorded by Imam at-Tirmidhi, the Prophet ﷺ said:  
*‘Indeed, your Lord is Shy and Generous. When His servant raises his hands to Him, He is shy to return them empty.’*

Think about that. The Creator of the billions of galaxies feels *haya*—divine modesty and generosity—toward the servant who lifts empty hands to Him.

When you raise your hands, Allah does not look at the stack of your past sins and say, *‘You don't deserve an answer.’*  
He looks at your humility. He looks at your need.  
The distance you felt was never from His side. It was your distraction.  
He was near all along."

---

### 04:00 – 05:00 | SECTION 5: PRACTICAL ACTION & QUIET CTA
**[VISUAL]**  
*Hand gently closing the Qur'an, textured cloth ribbon resting on the page. Fades to the calm Huurs brand card.*

**(VOICEOVER):**  
"Tonight, test this verse.

Don't wait until you feel spiritually holy.  
Tonight, before you go to sleep, sit in the quiet. Raise your hands.  
Do not recite a practiced speech. Talk to Allah like you would speak to the One who knows you better than you know yourself.

Tell Him what hurts. Tell Him where you feel weak.  
And remember His promise:  
*I respond to the caller when he calls upon Me.*

Read it. Understand it. Live it.  
And come back tomorrow."
