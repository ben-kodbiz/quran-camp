# Episode 1 Flagship Video Script (`QURAN-COMEBACK-SCRIPT-001`)

**Campaign:** Come Back to the Qur'an  
**Title:** *"You Don't Need Another Islamic Video — You Need the Qur'an"*  
**Target Duration:** ~6 minutes (approx. 780 spoken words at calm, contemplative pacing)  
**Tone:** Calm, sincere, unhurried, emotionally intelligent  
**Visual Pairing:** [QURAN-COMEBACK-VISUAL-001.md](file:///mnt/AI/ag/Campaign/00_BRAND/QURAN-COMEBACK-VISUAL-001.md)

## PRODUCTION TIMELINE & DIALOGUE

### 00:00 – 00:30 | SECTION 1: THE HOOK
**[VISUAL: SH-01 / SH-02]**  
*Static shot of an unopened Qur'an on a high wooden shelf. Cool morning shadows. Cut to a close-up of a glowing smartphone screen scrolling endlessly in a dark room. The screen suddenly turns black. Complete silence.*

**(VOICEOVER / HOST — Calm, quiet, conversational):**  
"What if the answer to that emptiness inside you... isn't another ten-minute lecture?

What if the reason you feel so tired, so spiritually scattered... isn't that you lack Islamic reminders, but that you’ve spent months listening to everyone talk *about* Allah, without ever sitting down to listen to Allah Himself?

Take an honest look at your day. We scroll through hundreds of religious posts. We save motivational reels we never re-watch. But the Book of Allah... sits quietly on a shelf. 

Today, let’s stop scrolling. Let’s come back."

---

### 00:30 – 01:45 | SECTION 2: THE AYAH & TRANSLATION
**[VISUAL: SH-03]**  
*Slow macro camera gliding across the textured cover of the Mushaf. Gentle hands open the page. A shaft of golden morning light falls across the Arabic script. The text appears on screen with immense negative space.*

**(VOICEOVER):**  
"In Surah Muhammad, verse 24, Allah asks a question that cuts straight through our excuses:

$$\text{أَفَلَا يَتَدَبَّرُونَ الْقُرْآنَ أَمْ عَلَىٰ قُلُوبٍ أَقْفَالُهَا}$$

*‘Do they not then reflect upon the Qur'an? Or are there locks upon their hearts?’*

Listen to that question carefully. Allah does not ask, *‘Have they not memorized the entire book?’* He does not ask, *‘Are they scholars of grammar?’* 

He asks: *Do they not reflect?* 

The word used here is *tadabbur*. The classical scholars explain that *tadabbur* comes from a root meaning the end or outcome of an affair. It means: do you not look past the surface of the words into what they mean for your life, your choices, and your return to Allah?"

---

### 01:45 – 03:15 | SECTION 3: TAFSIR & THE UNLOCKING
**[VISUAL: SH-04]**  
*A quiet path winding through an early morning forest. Mist gently rising as dawn illuminates the trees. Spacious, editorial, breathing room.*

**(VOICEOVER):**  
"In his classical commentary, Ibn Kathir explains this verse with striking clarity. He writes that Allah commands contemplation and understanding, and warns that when a heart is filled with heedlessness, it gathers locks. It becomes shut, so that nothing penetrates inside.

Centuries ago, the Prophet ﷺ recited this very ayah to his companions. Standing in the gathering, a young man from Yemen called out: 
*‘Rather, upon the hearts are their locks, until Allah unlocks them or opens them.’* 
Umar ibn al-Khattab was so moved by that young man’s insight that he never forgot it.

Think about that for a moment. The lock on your heart isn't there because Allah rejected you. The lock is formed when we spend months feeding our minds with digital noise, while starving our souls of divine speech. 

And the key... has always been in your hands."

---

### 03:15 – 04:30 | SECTION 4: TADABBUR & THE PROPHETIC TEARS
**[VISUAL: SH-05 / SH-06]**  
*A vast, still ocean horizon at sunrise. Water rippling gently. Cut to a quiet silhouette of a believer sitting in contemplation before an open window.*

**(VOICEOVER):**  
"This is not just academic knowledge. The Qur'an was revealed to be felt.

Imam al-Bukhari and Imam Muslim record that one afternoon, the Prophet ﷺ asked his companion Abdullah ibn Mas'ud: *‘Recite the Qur'an to me.’* 

Ibn Mas'ud was shocked. He said, *‘O Messenger of Allah, shall I recite it to you when it was revealed to you?’* 
And the Prophet ﷺ replied: *‘I love to hear it from someone other than myself.’*

Ibn Mas'ud began reciting Surah An-Nisa. He read slowly, deliberately. When he reached verse 41—describing the Day of Judgment—the Prophet ﷺ quietly whispered: *‘That is enough.’* 

Ibn Mas'ud looked up. And he saw tears flowing from the eyes of the Messenger of Allah.

That is what happens when the speech of Allah enters a heart that is truly listening. It doesn’t create noise. It creates awe. It creates tears of relief. It brings peace."

---

### 04:30 – 05:30 | SECTION 5: PRACTICAL ACTION — THE 5-MINUTE RETURN
**[VISUAL: SH-07]**  
*Close-up of an open Qur'an, warm linen tones. Text card appears cleanly: The 5-Minute Return Protocol.*

**(VOICEOVER):**  
"You don't need to change your entire life overnight. You don't need to read an entire juz today.

All we ask of you is five minutes tonight.

Here is your commitment:
Before you go to bed, put your phone in another room. Sit down with the Qur'an. 

Turn to Surah Al-Baqarah, verse 186. Read it once in Arabic, and read the English translation slowly:
*‘When My servants ask you concerning Me: I am indeed near. I respond to the call of the caller when he calls upon Me.’*

Pause on those three words: *I am near.* 
Ask yourself: *If Allah is this near, why have I been running everywhere else?*

Then raise your hands. Talk to Him in your own words. Ask Him: *‘Ya Allah, unlock my heart for Your Book.’*"

---

### 05:30 – 06:00 | SECTION 6: QUIET END CARD & CTA
**[VISUAL: SH-07 / END CARD]**  
*Hand gently closes the Mushaf, placing a textured ribbon bookmark inside. Soft natural light fades to a warm, elegant slate background.*

**(VOICEOVER):**  
"Don't just watch this video and move to the next.

Close the screen. Open the Book of Allah. 
Read it. Understand it. Live it. 

And come back tomorrow."
