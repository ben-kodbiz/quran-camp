# Episode 6 Flagship Script (`QURAN-COMEBACK-SCRIPT-006`)

**Campaign:** Come Back to the Qur'an  
**Title:** *"The Most Important Dua You Say Every Day"*  
**Duration:** ~5 minutes (approx. 660 spoken words)  
**Tone:** Lucid, revelatory, contemplative, transformative  

---

## PRODUCTION SCRIPT & DIALOGUE

### 00:00 – 00:35 | SECTION 1: THE HOOK
**[VISUAL]**  
*A long, straight stone pathway cutting through a tranquil pine forest at dawn. The path extends toward the horizon where morning light pierces through morning mist. Footsteps walking quietly.*

**(VOICEOVER):**  
"If you pray your five daily prayers, you recite this exact prayer at least seventeen times every single day.

Seventeen times:  
**Ihdināṣ-Ṣirāṭ al-Mustaqīm**—*‘Guide us to the straight path.’*

Have you ever stopped to wonder:  
*If I am already a Muslim... if I am already standing on the prayer rug facing the Ka'bah... why do I have to keep asking for guidance in every single unit of prayer?*

Are we not already guided?

When you understand why Allah made this specific request mandatory for every prayer, the way you pray will change forever."

---

### 00:35 – 01:45 | SECTION 2: GUIDANCE IS A CURRENT, NOT A STATUE
**[VISUAL]**  
*Macro shot of water flowing in a clear river channel. A small wooden leaf floating forward against subtle eddies.*

**(VOICEOVER):**  
"Imam Ibn Kathir and Ibn al-Qayyim explain that guidance is not a diploma you hang on your wall and keep forever.  
Guidance is like breathing. You don’t say, *‘I breathed ten minutes ago, so I don’t need air right now.’*

The moment you stop receiving oxygen, you suffocate.  
And the moment your heart stops receiving divine guidance, it begins to drift.

Life does not stand still.  
Every day presents you with dozens of micro-decisions:  
How you respond to an angry email.  
What your eyes glance at when no one is watching.  
How you spend your money.  
How you speak to your spouse after an exhausting day.

In every single one of those moments, there is a Straight Path, and there is a ditch on either side.  
When you say *Ihdinas-Sirat al-Mustaqim*, you are begging Allah:  
*‘Ya Allah, guide my next step. Do not leave me to my own intellect for even the blink of an eye.’*"

---

### 01:45 – 03:00 | SECTION 3: THE PROPHETIC PARABLE OF THE PATH
**[VISUAL]**  
*Cinematic tracking shot along an ancient stone wall lined with hanging linen curtains. Soft amber lanterns illuminating the straight corridor.*

**(VOICEOVER):**  
"In an extraordinary authentic hadith recorded by Imam at-Tirmidhi, the Prophet ﷺ painted an unforgettable picture of this journey.

He said:  
Imagine a straight, wide road.  
Along both sides of this road are two walls.  
In those walls are open doorways leading into dark rooms, and over each doorway hangs a light curtain.

At the very entrance of the highway stands a caller shouting:  
*‘O people! Enter the straight path together and do not branch off!’*

And high above the highway stands another caller.  
Whenever a traveler is tempted to pull back one of those curtains and peek into an open room, the caller shouts:  
*‘Woe to you! Do not open that curtain! If you open it, you will enter it!’*

The Prophet ﷺ then explained:  
The road is Islam.  
The walls are the limits of Allah.  
The open doors behind the curtains are the things Allah has forbidden.  
The caller at the gate is the Holy Qur'an.  
And the caller shouting from above is the voice of conscience—the admonisher of Allah placed inside the heart of every believer."

---

### 03:00 – 04:00 | SECTION 4: TADABBUR — BETWEEN ANGER AND DRIFTING
**[VISUAL]**  
*Focus on the open verses of Surah Al-Fatihah, illuminated by warm morning light.*

**(VOICEOVER):**  
"Look at how Surah Al-Fatihah concludes:

$$\text{صِرَاطَ الَّذِينَ أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ غَيْرِ الْمَغْضُوبِ عَلَيْهِمْ وَلَا الضَّالِّينَ}$$

*‘The path of those You have blessed, not of those who have evoked Your anger, or of those who are astray.’*

The scholars point out that humans deviate in two distinct ways:  
1. *Al-Maghdub 'alayhim:* Knowing the truth, but refusing to act upon it because of pride or greed.  
2. *Ad-Dallin:* Having good intentions, but lacking authentic knowledge, drifting aimlessly in ignorance.

The Straight Path is the razor-thin line of balance:  
Knowledge married to action. Sincerity married to truth."

---

### 04:00 – 05:00 | SECTION 5: PRACTICAL ACTION & CALL TO PRAYER
**[VISUAL]**  
*A person standing in quiet prayer in a minimalist room, head bowed in humble concentration.*

**(VOICEOVER):**  
"In a few hours, the call to prayer will echo for your next salah.  
You will stand up. You will raise your hands. You will recite Surah Al-Fatihah.

When you reach verse 6, do not let your tongue rush past it out of habit.  
Pause.  
Feel your utter poverty before Allah.  
Feel the weight of the day ahead of you.  
And say it with your entire soul:  
**Ihdināṣ-Ṣirāṭ al-Mustaqīm.**

*‘Guide me today, Ya Allah. Keep my feet firm.’*

Read it. Understand it. Live it.  
And come back tomorrow."
