When Prophet Sulayman was given dominion over kings and armies, his greatest gift was not military might. It was the sensitivity to pause and listen to the speech of creation:

«Rabbi awzi'nī an ashkura ni'mataka allatī an'amta 'alayya wa 'alā wālidayya wa an a'mala ṣāliḥan tarḍāhu...»

'My Lord, inspire and restrain me, that I may be grateful for Your favor which You have bestowed upon me and upon my parents, and that I may do righteous deeds that please You.'

Notice the word: *Awzi'nī*. Gather my scattered thoughts. Restrain my arrogance. Make gratitude my default state."
