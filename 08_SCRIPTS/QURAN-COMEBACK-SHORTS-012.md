### #Scene-1: Short A (The High-Impact Hook)
* **Duration:** 35s  
* **Visual:** Close-up macro of an open Quran turning in soft dawn light.  
* **Audio Voiceover:**

* **On-Screen Text:** STOP SEARCHING EVERYWHERE ELSE.

### #Scene-2: Short B (The Ayah Recitation Focus)
* **Duration:** 40s  
* **Visual:** Peaceful natural footage of morning rain falling on leaves.  
* **Audio Voiceover:**  
  "Listen to Surah Yusuf 12:18, 86, 100: 'He said, 'I only complain of my suffering and my grief to Allah, and I know from Allah that which you do not know.'' What is Allah asking of you right now in this exact chapter of your life?"  
* **On-Screen Text:** Surah Yusuf 12:18, 86, 100  
* **CTA:** Save this reminder for tonight.

### #Scene-3: Short C (The Practical Action Step)
* **Duration:** 45s  
* **Visual:** Minimalist reading desk with notebook and tea.  
* **Audio Voiceover:**  
  "Here is your exact action step today: The Private Grievance Protocol: Today, whatever betrayal or delay is keeping you awake at night, refuse to complain to human beings on social media or group chats. Turn off your screen, make wudu, sit in the dark, and whisper verse 12:86 to Allah alone. Try this for 3 days and feel the change in your chest."  
* **On-Screen Text:** THE 3-MINUTE RETREAT.  
* **CTA:** Share this with someone who needs it.
