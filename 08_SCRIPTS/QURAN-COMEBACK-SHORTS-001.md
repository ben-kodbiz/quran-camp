# Episode 1 Shorts Repurposing Pack (`QURAN-COMEBACK-SHORTS-001`)

**Campaign:** Come Back to the Qur'an  
**Format:** 9:16 Vertical Video (30–50 seconds each)  
**Assigned Agent:** `AGENT-13` (Social Agent)  
**Parent Artifact:** `QURAN-COMEBACK-SCRIPT-001`  

---

## SHORT A: THE EMOTIONAL HOOK (Duration: 30s)
* **Visual:** Close-up of hands laying down a glowing phone on a dark table, turning to a softly illuminated open Qur'an in dawn window light.
* **On-Screen Text (Clean serif):** *"You don't need another video."*

```text
[00:00 - 00:08]
VOICEOVER:
"You don't need another ten-minute lecture. You don't need another motivational video.

[00:08 - 00:20]
VOICEOVER:
What you need... is five quiet minutes with the words of your Creator. 
We spend our entire day listening to people talk ABOUT Allah, but when was the last time you sat down to listen to Allah Himself?

[00:20 - 00:30]
VOICEOVER:
Tonight, close this app. Open the Qur'an. Even if it's just one ayah.
Don't just scroll past. Come back."
```
* **CTA Graphic:** *"Come Back to the Qur'an. Free 7-Day Guide in Bio."*

---

## SHORT B: THE ONE AYAH (Duration: 40s)
* **Visual:** Macro shot of textured Arabic calligraphy of Surah Muhammad 47:24, dust motes drifting across sunlight.
* **On-Screen Text:** Arabic Uthmani text + English translation fading in with generous padding.

```text
[00:00 - 00:10]
VOICEOVER:
"Listen to this question in Surah Muhammad, verse 24:
'Afalā yatadabbarūna al-Qur'āna am 'alā qulūbin aqfāluhā.'

[00:10 - 00:25]
VOICEOVER:
'Do they not then reflect upon the Qur'an? Or are there locks upon their hearts?'
Notice that word: 'Locks'. Ibn Kathir explains that when we become heedless, our hearts gather locks, and nothing penetrates inside.

[00:25 - 00:40]
VOICEOVER:
The lock isn't there to punish you. It's there because you've been feeding your mind with noise.
Ask Allah tonight: 'Ya Allah, unlock my heart for Your speech.'"
```

---

## SHORT C: THE PRACTICAL ACTION (Duration: 35s)
* **Visual:** Top-down aesthetic desk setup: notebook, pencil, closed Qur'an, gentle natural lighting.
* **On-Screen Graphic:** 3-step checklist appearing cleanly on screen.

```text
[00:00 - 00:10]
VOICEOVER:
"If you feel completely distant from the Qur'an, do this tonight before sleeping:

[00:10 - 00:22]
VOICEOVER:
Step 1: Put your phone in another room.
Step 2: Turn to Surah Al-Baqarah, verse 186: 'I am indeed near. I respond to the caller when he calls.'

[00:22 - 00:35]
VOICEOVER:
Step 3: Pause for two minutes. Don't read anything else. Just talk to Him in your own words.
Start your 5-minute return tonight."
```

---

## SHORT D: THE REFLECTION QUESTION (Duration: 30s)
* **Visual:** Wide, calm horizon where gentle sea ripples meet the morning sky.
* **On-Screen Text:** *"When did you last let an ayah sink in?"*

```text
[00:00 - 00:10]
VOICEOVER:
"When was the last time you read an ayah from the Qur'an... and actually paused to ask: What is Allah teaching ME here?

[00:10 - 00:22]
VOICEOVER:
Not what it means for someone else. Not reading just to finish a page. 
The Prophet ﷺ once listened to Surah An-Nisa until tears overflowed from his eyes.

[00:22 - 00:30]
VOICEOVER:
The Qur'an was sent to melt hearts, not to collect dust. Open it today."
```

---

## SHORT E: THE MYTH CORRECTION (Duration: 40s)
* **Visual:** A person holding a pocket translation of the Qur'an by a sunlit window, turning pages gently.
* **On-Screen Text:** *"Myth: The Qur'an is only for scholars."*

```text
[00:00 - 00:12]
VOICEOVER:
"There is a dangerous lie many Muslims believe: 'I don't know classical Arabic, so I shouldn't try to reflect on the Qur'an.'

[00:12 - 00:26]
VOICEOVER:
That is false. While deducing legal rulings is for qualified scholars, reflecting on Allah’s warnings, mercy, and guidance is an obligation for every believer.

[00:26 - 00:40]
VOICEOVER:
Allah asked in Surah Muhammad: 'Do they not reflect?' He spoke to everyone.
Start with a reliable translation and classical tafsir. Don't let hesitation keep you away."
```
