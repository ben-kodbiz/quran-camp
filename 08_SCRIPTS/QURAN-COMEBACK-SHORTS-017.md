# Episode 17 Shorts Repurposing Pack: The Two People Who Deserve Your Gentle Voice
### *5 Vertical 9:16 Scripts for Social Distribution*

### #Scene-1: Short A (The High-Impact Hook)
* **Duration:** 35s  
* **Visual:** Close-up macro of an open Quran turning in soft dawn light.  
* **Audio Voiceover:**  
  "Why is it that we can speak with infinite patience to colleagues and strangers, yet unleash our shortest tempers on the aging parents who raised us? We search everywhere for peace, but we leave the words of our Creator unread on the nightstand. Tonight, give Allah 5 uninterrupted minutes."  
* **On-Screen Text:** STOP SEARCHING EVERYWHERE ELSE.  
* **CTA:** Tap link in bio for the 7-Day Guide.

### #Scene-2: Short B (The Ayah Recitation Focus)
* **Duration:** 40s  
* **Visual:** Peaceful natural footage of morning rain falling on leaves.  
* **Audio Voiceover:**  
  "Listen to Surah Al-Isra 17:23–24, 79, 82: 'And your Lord has decreed that you not worship except Him, and to parents, good treatment. Whether one or both of them reach old age with you, say not to them [so much as], 'uff,' and do not repel them but speak to them a noble word.' What is Allah asking of you right now in this exact chapter of your life?"  
* **On-Screen Text:** Surah Al-Isra 17:23–24, 79, 82  
* **CTA:** Save this reminder for tonight.

### #Scene-3: Short C (The Practical Action Step)
* **Duration:** 45s  
* **Visual:** Minimalist reading desk with notebook and tea.  
* **Audio Voiceover:**  
  "Here is your exact action step today: The Lowered Wing Call: If your parents are alive, call them today with no agenda other than to ask how their day was, speaking with the gentle cadence commanded in 17:24. If they have passed, make a sincere du'a for their forgiveness. Try this for 3 days and feel the change in your chest."  
* **On-Screen Text:** THE 3-MINUTE RETREAT.  
* **CTA:** Share this with someone who needs it.
