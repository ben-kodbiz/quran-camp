## SHORT A: THE EMOTIONAL HOOK (Duration: 30s)
* **Visual Direction:** A turbulent mountain stream crashing against dark rocks, then smoothing into crystal clear still water downstream.
* **On-Screen Graphic Hook:** *"Two Eases, One Hardship"*

```text
[00:00 - 00:08]
VOICEOVER:
"Did you know that in Surah Ash-Sharh, Allah grammatically guarantees that your hardship is outnumbered?

[00:08 - 00:20]
VOICEOVER:
In Arabic grammar, 'Al-'Usr' (hardship) is mentioned with the definite article—meaning one single hardship. 
While 'Yusr' (ease) is indefinite and repeated twice—meaning multiple, endless eases!

[00:20 - 00:30]
VOICEOVER:
The early scholars used to say: 'One hardship can never defeat two eases!'
Take a breath. Relief is already surrounding you."
```

## SHORT B: THE ONE AYAH (Duration: 40s)

* **On-Screen Graphic Hook:** *"Inside the Trial"*

```text
[00:00 - 00:12]
VOICEOVER:
"Look at the prepositions in Surah Ash-Sharh:
'Fa-inna ma'al 'usri yusrā.'
'For indeed, with hardship comes ease.'

[00:12 - 00:26]
VOICEOVER:
Allah did not say 'Ba'da'—after hardship. He said 'Ma'a'—WITH hardship!

It means the seed of your ease is already planted inside the soil of your current difficulty.

[00:26 - 00:40]
VOICEOVER:
The resilience you are developing, the prayers you are making, the character being forged—that IS the ease. 
Trust the Gardener."
```
