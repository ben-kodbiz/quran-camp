# Episode 8 Flagship Script (`QURAN-COMEBACK-SCRIPT-008`)

**Campaign:** Come Back to the Qur'an  
**Title:** *"A Qur'an Routine for Someone Who Has No Time"*  
**Duration:** ~5 minutes (approx. 660 spoken words)  
**Tone:** Practical, encouraging, empathetic, structured, empowering  

---

## PRODUCTION SCRIPT & DIALOGUE

### 00:00 – 00:35 | SECTION 1: THE ALL-OR-NOTHING TRAP
**[VISUAL]**  
*A magnificent peacock standing gracefully in a quiet sunlit courtyard garden, iridescent emerald and sapphire tail feathers catching the morning light as it moves with unhurried dignity.*

**(VOICEOVER):**  
"Here is a cycle you might recognize:

You feel spiritually depleted. You listen to a powerful reminder, and you say:  
*‘Starting tomorrow, I am going to wake up an hour before Fajr and read a full juz of the Qur'an every single day.’*

Day one goes well.  
Day two is harder.  
On day three, the baby cries all night, or you work a twelve-hour shift. You miss your goal.  
Guilt washes over you. You feel like a failure.  
And by day five, you don’t even touch the Qur'an. Three months pass before you open it again.

We call this the *all-or-nothing trap*.  
And it is destroying our relationship with the Book of Allah."

---

### 00:35 – 01:45 | SECTION 2: THE QUR'AN WAS NOT SENT FOR DISTRESS
**[VISUAL]**  
*Lush green terraced mountain ridges of Taif at sunrise, soft morning mist drifting through the valley as warm golden sunlight illuminates ancient stone terraces.*

**(VOICEOVER):**  
"In Surah Ta-Ha, Allah opens the chapter with words that should soothe every exhausted heart:

$$\text{مَا أَنزَلْنَا عَلَيْكَ الْقُرْآنَ لِتَشْقَىٰ}$$

*‘We have not sent down to you the Quran that you should be distressed.’*

And in Surah Al-Muzzammil, Allah acknowledges our reality:  
*He knows that some of you are sick, some of you are traveling seeking His bounty, some of you are exhausted... so recite whatever is easy for you.*

Notice that:  
Allah didn’t command you to burn yourself out.  
Allah doesn’t measure your love by whether you can read for sixty uninterrupted minutes in a chaotic day.

In an authentic hadith in Sahih al-Bukhari, the Prophet ﷺ was asked:  
*‘Which deeds are most beloved to Allah?’*  
He answered:  
**Aḥabbu al-a'māli ilallāhi adwamuhā wa in qall.**  
*‘The most beloved deeds to Allah are those that are most consistent, even if they are small.’*"

---

### 01:45 – 03:15 | SECTION 3: THE 10-MINUTE DAILY METHOD
**[VISUAL]**  
*Macro cinematic shot of golden honeybees moving methodically across natural honeycomb inside a hollow trunk in an oasis, amber drops of pure honey glistening in sunlight.*

**(VOICEOVER):**  
"If you have ten minutes, you have enough time to build an unbreakable relationship with Allah.  
Here is the exact framework we teach in our 7-Day Guide:

**Minute 1 to 3: The Recitation.**  
Pick just three to five verses. Read them slowly. Do not rush. Let your voice be your connection to the divine text.

**Minute 4 to 6: The Meaning.**  
Read the English translation. Don’t guess the meaning; read an authentic, clear translation. Understand what Allah is saying to you in those three verses.

**Minute 7 to 8: The Tadabbur Question.**  
Take out a journal or a piece of paper and answer one question:  
*‘If I had to live by this single verse today at work or with my family, what would I change?’*  
Write one sentence.

**Minute 9 to 10: The Return.**  
Raise your hands. Make a simple, heartfelt dua asking Allah to give you the strength to act upon what you just wrote.

Ten minutes.  
Not forty minutes. Not two hours.  
Ten unbroken minutes every single day."

---

### 03:15 – 04:15 | SECTION 4: TADABBUR — THE DROP OF WATER ON STONE
**[VISUAL]**  
*Macro tracking shot of a single water droplet falling steadily onto a dark ancient stone. Smooth circular indentations carved by centuries of patient droplets.*

**(VOICEOVER):**  
"Think about this:  
If you throw an entire bucket of water onto a boulder at once, the water splashes everywhere and evaporates in minutes. The rock remains unchanged.

But if a single drop of water falls upon that same boulder, every single day, at the exact same spot... over time, that single drop carves straight through the solid stone.

Ten minutes of Qur'an every single day will soften a heart that ten hours of sporadic reading can never penetrate.

Do not let Shaytan convince you that ten minutes is too small.  
Ten minutes with Allah is infinite compared to zero."

---

### 04:15 – 05:00 | SECTION 5: PRACTICAL ACTION & TODAY'S COMMITMENT
**[VISUAL]**  
*Panoramic telephoto shot of the ancient carved sandstone monumental facade of Hegra in Al-Ula standing timeless in the vast golden desert at sunset.*

**(VOICEOVER):**  
"Look at your schedule tomorrow morning.  
Find ten minutes.  
Before you check social media. Before you open your emails.  
Anchor those ten minutes right after your Fajr prayer, or during your lunch break.

Commit to ten minutes for seven straight days.  
Protect it like an appointment with a king.  
Because it is an appointment with the King of Kings.

Read it. Understand it. Live it.  
And come back tomorrow."
