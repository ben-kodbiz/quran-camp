# He Does Not Sleep : The Effortless Preserver
## 60-Second Cinematic Vertical Video Script

### Core Visual Equation
`DEEP FOREST MIST + RAINDROPS ON LEAF + TRANQUIL MOUNTAIN LAKE + WARM CANDLELIGHT`

---

### Shot 1: The Weight of Fatigue (00–12s)
- **Visual:** Slow tracking shot through a dense, mist-shrouded temperate pine forest at twilight. Rain begins to fall gently. Water droplets gather on the tip of a cedar needle, reflecting the gray sky before dropping.
- **Audio:** Gentle pitter-patter of soft rain against foliage. Low cello resonance.
- **Voiceover (Gentle, empathetic, intimate):**  
  *"Every human being eventually reaches a breaking point. We exhaust our energy, our emotions fray, and our eyelids become too heavy to hold open. We must collapse into sleep."*

---

### Shot 2: The Cosmic Machinery (12–25s)
- **Visual:** Majestic cinematic time-lapse of mountain peaks bathed in cloud mist as night falls. The stars revolve overhead in smooth, unbroken mechanical precision. No turbulence, no interruption.
- **Audio:** Swell of atmospheric strings. Wind moving through high mountain pines.
- **Voiceover:**  
  *"And while we sleep defenseless, who is keeping the Earth spinning on its axis? Who commands the tides, circulates the blood in your heart, and guides the flights of birds?"*

---

### Shot 3: The Sleepless Sovereign (25–40s)
- **Visual:** Macro shot of crystal-clear river water flowing serenely over polished river stones. A beam of moonlight pierces the dark canopy, illuminating the effortless current.
- **Audio:** Rushing, pure water tones. A serene low sub-bass hum.
- **Voiceover:**  
  *"In Ayat al-Kursi, the Creator reveals His sovereign reality: 'Neither drowsiness nor sleep seizes Him.' He has never blinked. He has never grown weary. Governing the universe costs Him zero energy."*

---

### Shot 4: The Resting Soul (40–52s)
- **Visual:** Warm indoor atmosphere: A rustic timber desk beside an open window overlooking the quiet rain. An open Qur'an rested on a wooden stand. The soft, warm glow of a candle flame resting steadily in the dark.
- **Audio:** Gentle rain outside the window. Calming solo piano melody.
- **Voiceover:**  
  *"When you lay your head down tonight, release the illusion that you must carry the world alone. The One who protects you never sleeps. Rest in His effortless care."*
