# Episode 14 Flagship Script (`QURAN-COMEBACK-SCRIPT-014`)

**Campaign:** Come Back to the Qur'an  
**Title:** *"The Secret of Never Losing What You Have"*  
**Ayah Reference:** Surah Ibrahim 14:7, 24–25  
**Duration:** ~4 minutes (approx. 520 spoken words)  
**Tone:** Unhurried, deeply contemplative, compassionate, authentic, restorative  
**Visual Living Creation Directive:** 100% Living nature and sacred geography. Ancient rooted olive tree, hovering hummingbird at blossom, honeybees on honeycomb, soaring birds over mountain waterfall, and Masjid an-Nabawi at sunrise. Absolute Zero-Human Mandate. Zero burned-in subtitles.

---

## PRODUCTION SCRIPT & DIALOGUE

### 00:00 – 00:45 | SECTION 1: THE ANXIETY OF SCARCITY
**[VISUAL]**  
*Macro cinematic shot of an ancient gnarled olive tree trunk with massive roots deeply gripping the earth, silver-green leaves gently rustling in warm golden morning sunlight. Stability, endurance, grounded peace.*

**(VOICEOVER):**  
"Have you ever looked at a blessing in your life—your health, your family, your livelihood—and felt a sudden, sharp knot of anxiety that one day it might be stripped away?

We live in a culture driven by scarcity and fear. We hoard our energy, overthink our future, and desperately try to micromanage outcomes we cannot control.

We search for security in bank accounts, insurance policies, and human promises. But we forget that the One who gave the gift established a specific spiritual fortress to protect it."

---

### 00:45 – 01:40 | SECTION 2: THE DIVINE GUARANTEE
**[VISUAL]**  
*An exquisite jewel-toned hummingbird hovering with delicate precision at a vibrant mountain blossom, wings in gentle motion, drinking sweet morning nectar bathed in dawn sunbeams.*

**(VOICEOVER):**  
"In Surah Ibrahim, Allah reveals the ultimate law of spiritual conservation:

'And remember when your Lord proclaimed: If you are grateful, I will surely increase you in favor. But if you are ungrateful, indeed, My punishment is severe.'

Look closely at the certainty in this decree: 'La-azīdannakum' — I will unconditionally increase you.

Look at the hummingbird at the flower. It does not store years of nectar in terror of tomorrow. It arrives each dawn with empty wings, trusting that the blossom has already been prepared. Gratitude is not merely saying thank you; it is recognizing that every breath is an unearned gift from the King of kings."

---

### 01:40 – 02:45 | SECTION 3: WHAT CLASSICAL TAFSIR EXPLAINS
**[VISUAL]**  
*Macro slow-motion of golden honeybees systematically building and nurturing a pristine wild honeycomb inside a hollow tree trunk in warm morning light.*

**(VOICEOVER):**  
"The classical exegete Ibn Kathir recorded that the early scholars described Shukr as two things: the preserver of existing blessings, and the summoner of unseen additions.

When you thank Allah for what you hold in your hands today, you lock that blessing against decay. And when you remain grateful during hardship, you unlock doors of relief you never knew existed.

The Prophet Muhammad, peace be upon him, marveled at this inner state in authentic tradition: 'How wonderful is the affair of the believer! For all his affairs are good. If ease comes to him, he is grateful, and that is good for him. And if hardship strikes him, he is patient, and that is good for him.'

For the grateful soul, there is no such thing as a total loss."

---

### 02:45 – 03:35 | SECTION 4: THE TADABBUR SHIFT & THE GOOD TREE
**[VISUAL]**  
*A flock of pure white birds soaring freely over a breathtaking turquoise mountain waterfall under a pastel morning sky. Expansive negative space and soaring freedom.*

**(VOICEOVER):**  
"In the same chapter, Allah gives the parable of the believer's heart:

'A good word is like a good tree, its root firmly fixed and its branches high in the sky, producing its fruit at all times by permission of its Lord.'

Take a slow, deep breath and reflect on your own roots.

Are you anchoring your peace to fragile circumstances that blow away in the wind, or are you rooting your soul in daily recognition of your Lord? A tree with deep roots does not panic during winter. It knows the sap still runs within its trunk, and fruit will return when the appointed time arrives."

---

### 03:35 – 04:15 | SECTION 5: THE LIVING ACTION & CALL TO RETURN
**[VISUAL]**  
*Sunlight breaking over the white marble colonnades and sacred courtyard arches of Masjid an-Nabawi in Medina. Serene dawn tranquility, golden rays on polished stone.*

**(VOICEOVER):**  
"Do not let this reminder remain mere sentiment. Faith is knowledge accompanied by deliberate action.

Here is your practical anchor for today: The Uncounted Blessing Audit.

Tonight, write down five blessings you currently enjoy that you never once thought to ask for: the rhythm of your beating heart, the clarity of your sight, the quiet air in your lungs, or the unseen harms diverted from your path today. Say 'Alhamdulillah' for each one with conscious presence.

Open Surah Ibrahim tonight. Sit for five unhurried minutes with your Lord.

Read it. Reflect upon it. Return to Him."
