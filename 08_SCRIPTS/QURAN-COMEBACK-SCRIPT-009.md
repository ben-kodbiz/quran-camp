# Episode 9 Flagship Script (`QURAN-COMEBACK-SCRIPT-009`)

**Campaign:** Come Back to the Qur'an  
**Title:** *"The Surah That Explains Why You're Losing"*  
**Duration:** ~5 minutes (approx. 660 spoken words)  
**Tone:** Urgent yet calm, piercing, wake-up call, deeply grounded  

---

## PRODUCTION SCRIPT & DIALOGUE

### 00:00 – 00:35 | SECTION 1: THE DISAPPEARING CLOCK
**[VISUAL]**  
*A solitary ancient stone sundial on an ocean cliff at late afternoon. The shadow crawls silently across the engraved numerals as the golden sun sinks toward a vast, calm ocean horizon.*

**(VOICEOVER):**  
"Have you felt lately like time is moving unnaturally fast?

You wake up on Monday morning, blink your eyes, and suddenly it is Friday night.  
You look back at the last six months, or the last five years, and a terrifying thought crosses your mind:  
*‘Where did it all go? What did I actually build for my soul?’*

We check our screens for hours. We hustle. We buy things. We accumulate credentials.  
And yet, in the quiet moments late at night, a lingering anxiety remains:  
*I am busy, but I am losing.*

Fourteen hundred years ago, in just three short verses, Allah revealed the absolute reality of human loss."

---

### 00:35 – 01:45 | SECTION 2: THE PARABLE OF THE MELTING CAPITAL
**[VISUAL]**  
*Tracking shot across open parchment displaying the short, powerful verses of Surah Al-Asr.*

**(VOICEOVER):**  
"Listen to the oath:

$$\text{وَالْعَصْرِ (1) إِنَّ الْإِنسَانَ لَفِي خُسْرٍ (2)}$$

*‘By time! Indeed, mankind is in loss.’*

Notice the Arabic: Allah does not say mankind *might* lose.  
He says: **Innal-insāna lafī khusr.**  
*‘Indeed, mankind is submerged inside loss.’*

Loss is the default state of every human being.  
The classical scholar Imam Fakhr al-Din ar-Razi once wrote:  
*‘I never truly understood this ayah until I was walking through a scorching summer marketplace, and I heard an ice vendor desperately shouting to the crowds:  
"Have mercy upon a man whose entire capital is melting away!"’*

Think about that.  
If the ice vendor sits there and does nothing, his ice melts into water. He goes bankrupt.  
Your time is that block of ice.  
Every heartbeat, every breath, every second that ticks by is a piece of your life dissolving.  
If you do not trade your seconds for eternity, you are guaranteed to end in ruin."

---

### 01:45 – 03:00 | SECTION 3: THE FOUR CONDITIONS OF SALVATION
**[VISUAL]**  
*A majestic lone cedar tree standing firm on a coastal bluff against ocean winds. Roots digging deep into rocky soil.*

**(VOICEOVER):**  
"Then Allah gives the only exception in verse 3:

$$\text{إِلَّا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا وَعَمِلُوا الصَّالِحَاتِ وَتَوَاصَوْا بِالْحَقِّ وَتَوَاصَوْا بِالصَّبْرِ}$$

*‘Except for those who have believed, and done righteous deeds, and advised each other to truth, and advised each other to patience.’*

Imam ash-Shafi'i famously said:  
*‘If humanity reflected upon this single surah alone, it would be enough for them.’*

Why? Because it outlines the four non-negotiable anchors:  
1. **Iman:** You cannot live on blind autopilot; you must seek authentic knowledge.  
2. **Righteous Action:** Knowledge without practice is hypocrisy.  
3. **Advising to Truth:** You cannot be a private righteous person who ignores the world; you must stand for integrity and remind others.  
4. **Advising to Patience:** Because standing for truth is exhausting, and you need endurance."

---

### 03:00 – 04:00 | SECTION 4: TADABBUR — WHAT COMPANIONS DID BEFORE SAYING GOODBYE
**[VISUAL]**  
*Two travelers in simple linen walking side-by-side on an ancient mountain pathway at sunset, turning toward each other in mutual respect.*

**(VOICEOVER):**  
"In an authentic narration in At-Tabarani, Abu Madinah ad-Darimi tells us:  
Whenever two companions of the Prophet ﷺ met—even for a few minutes—they would never part ways until one of them recited Surah Al-Asr to the other.  
Then they would say *As-salamu alaykum* and walk away.

Why?  
Because they knew how fragile the human mind is.  
They knew how easily conversations drift into gossip, worldly complaints, and empty boasting.  
Surah Al-Asr was their compass.  
It was their way of looking each other in the eye and saying:  
*‘Remember: Our ice is melting. Don’t waste your life.’*"

---

### 04:00 – 05:00 | SECTION 5: PRACTICAL ACTION & TODAY'S RESET
**[VISUAL]**  
*The sun setting over the calm horizon, leaving behind a tranquil twilight sky filled with deep indigo and amber hues.*

**(VOICEOVER):**  
"Before this day closes:

Look at your screen-time report. Look at how many hours went into scrolling through other people’s lives.  
Then look at the Qur'an resting on your table.

Stop the melting.  
Take five minutes right now. Read Surah Al-Asr slowly.  
And text a friend or family member one genuine word of encouragement or truth.

Be the one who reminds others of patience.  
Because the clock is not waiting for anyone.

Read it. Understand it. Live it.  
And come back tomorrow."
