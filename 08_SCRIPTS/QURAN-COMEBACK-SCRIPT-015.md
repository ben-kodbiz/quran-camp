# Episode 15: What to Do When Words Wound Your Soul
### *Come Back to the Qur'an — Season 2 (Surah Al-Hijr)*

**Ayah Reference:** Surah Al-Hijr 15:9, 97–99  
**Tone:** Unhurried, deeply contemplative, compassionate, authentic, restorative  
**Pacing:** 110–125 words per minute (intentional breathing space between ideas)  

---

## 1. The Hook (00:00 – 00:45)
*(Atmospheric natural dawn audio. Visual of Shot F (Dawn stone reflection). Slow push-in.)*

When someone mocks your character, spreads rumors about you, or betrays your trust, what happens inside your chest—and what is the Qur'an's exact antidote?

We run from podcast to podcast, from book to book, seeking a cure for the restlessness in our chests. But we forget that the One who created this heart sent down a specific manual for its healing.

---

## 2. The Ayah (00:45 – 02:00)
*(Recitation in clear, unadorned Tartil. Text appears on screen with reverence.)*

Allah says in Surah Al-Hijr 15:9, 97–99:

> **وَلَقَدْ نَعْلَمُ أَنَّكَ يَضِيقُ صَدْرُكَ بِمَا يَقُولُونَ فَسَبِّحْ بِحَمْدِ رَبِّكَ وَكُن مِّنَ السَّاجِدِينَ**  
> *"And We already know that your chest is constrained by what they say. So glorify [Allah] with praise of your Lord and be among those who prostrate."*

Listen to how personal these words are. Allah is not addressing an abstract crowd—He is speaking directly into your private reality right now.

---

## 3. What Classical Tafsir Explains (02:00 – 04:00)
*(Transition to Shot E (Book on carved rehal). Soft sunlight illuminating natural wood and parchment.)*

When the great commentators of Islam examined this verse, they uncovered profound wisdom:

Ibn Kathir explains that Allah acknowledges the constriction of the heart (Deeq as-Sadr) caused by insults and cruelty. Allah does not tell the Prophet to retaliate with venom; He prescribes the celestial remedy: Tasbeeh (elevating Allah above human flaws) and Sujood (placing your forehead on the earth in vulnerability).

As the Prophet ﷺ affirmed in authentic tradition:
*Abu Dawud 1319 (Hasan): Hudhayfah reported: 'Whenever a matter distressed the Prophet ﷺ, he would immediately hasten to prayer.'*

---

## 4. The Tadabbur Shift (04:00 – 05:30)
*(Visual of Shot B (Mountain pool). Gentle camera motion, zero digital rush.)*

Take a deep breath and reflect on your own life:
* Divine validation of emotional hurt, the eternal preservation of divine truth, and Sajdah as emotional rehabilitation.
* What would change if you stopped trying to carry this burden entirely on your own shoulders?
* What happens when you hand the outcome back to Allah?

---

## 5. The Living Action (05:30 – 06:45)
*(Visual of Shot A (Indigo sky). Calm horizon closing shot.)*

Do not leave this video as mere entertainment. Islam is knowledge accompanied by action:

**The Sajdah of Relief: If anyone made you feel humiliated or hurt this week, do not fire back an angry text. Make two rak'ahs and make your Sujood 3 minutes long. Lay your forehead on the earth and tell Allah exactly where it hurts.**

Turn off your phone tonight. Open your Mushaf to Surah Al-Hijr. Sit for five unhurried minutes with your Lord.

*Read it. Reflect upon it. Return to Him.*
