# Episode 15 Flagship Script (`QURAN-COMEBACK-SCRIPT-015`)

**Campaign:** Come Back to the Qur'an  
**Title:** *"What to Do When Words Wound Your Soul"*  
**Ayah Reference:** Surah Al-Hijr 15:97–99  
**Duration:** ~4 minutes (approx. 510 spoken words)  
**Tone:** Unhurried, deeply contemplative, compassionate, authentic, restorative  
**Visual Living Creation Directive:** 100% Living nature and coastal dawn. Ocean dawn with rolling sea mist, full moon over rippling desert dunes, Taif mountain agricultural terraces, magnificent courtyard peacock, and sunrise over ocean coastline. Absolute Zero-Human Mandate. Zero burned-in subtitles.

---

## PRODUCTION SCRIPT & DIALOGUE

### 00:00 – 00:45 | SECTION 1: THE WOUND OF SHARP WORDS
**[VISUAL]**  
*Cinematic wide shot of an ocean dawn with cool sea mist rolling gently over calm wild waters in early morning light. Solitary atmosphere, emotional weight, expansive negative space.*

**(VOICEOVER):**  
"Have you ever had someone say something so cruel, so dismissive, or so deeply unfair that you felt your entire chest tighten, as if the air was physically being pressed out of your lungs?

We try to brush it off. We tell ourselves that other people's opinions don't matter, or we fire back with defensive arguments on messaging apps. But inside, the sting lingers for days, replaying in our minds late into the night.

We search for ways to protect our fragile egos. But we forget that the One who created human speech gave us the exact divine protocol for an aching chest."

---

### 00:45 – 01:40 | SECTION 2: THE DIVINE VALIDATION
**[VISUAL]**  
*A luminous full moon suspended over pristine, wind-rippled desert sand dunes beneath millions of crystalline stars. Deep cosmic stillness, cool silver luminescence, and peaceful negative space.*

**(VOICEOVER):**  
"In Surah Al-Hijr, Allah speaks directly to the most noble soul that ever lived, acknowledging his silent pain:

'And We already know that your chest is constrained by what they say. So glorify with praise of your Lord and be of those who prostrate. And worship your Lord until there comes unto you the certainty.'

Listen to the tenderness of this opening: 'Wa laqad na'lam' — We already know. Allah did not scold the Prophet for feeling hurt. He did not tell him to suppress his emotions or pretend the insults did not wound him.

He validated the tightness in his chest, and then immediately handed him the celestial cure."

---

### 01:40 – 02:45 | SECTION 3: WHAT CLASSICAL TAFSIR EXPLAINS
**[VISUAL]**  
*Panoramic cinematic sweep across the resilient stone agricultural terraces of the Taif mountains in Saudi Arabia, shrouded in morning mist. Green foliage rooted firmly against rugged mountain peaks.*

**(VOICEOVER):**  
"Classical scholars of tafsir noted that when Allah diagnosed the constriction of the chest, He prescribed two specific remedies: Tasbeeh and Sujood.

Ibn Kathir explains that when human beings insult or betray you, your instinct is to fixate on the creation. Tasbeeh breaks this trap. Saying 'Subhan Allah' elevates your mind above the petty flaws, deceit, and cruelty of people, lifting your gaze to the flawless perfection of your Creator.

The companion Hudhayfah reported in an authentic tradition: 'Whenever a matter distressed the Prophet Muhammad, peace be upon him, he would immediately hasten to prayer.'

Prayer was not his duty after the crisis; it was his sanctuary during the storm."

---

### 02:45 – 03:35 | SECTION 4: THE TADABBUR SHIFT & HEALING IN PROSTRATION
**[VISUAL]**  
*A magnificent peacock walking gracefully across sunlit stone flagstones in a quiet garden, trailing its shimmering emerald and sapphire tail feathers in soft morning light.*

**(VOICEOVER):**  
"Take a slow, deep breath and look at what Sujood truly offers you today.

When you prostrate, you place the highest part of your physical body—the head that holds your pride, your anxieties, your intellectual overthinking—directly onto the lowest earth. You are physically declaring: I am powerless, but my Lord is Supreme.

Look at the peacock in the garden. It steps calmly, carrying its breathtaking beauty without self-doubt, unconcerned with the noise around it.

When you lay your forehead on the carpet in secret, the ground absorbs the weight you were never designed to carry. In Sujood, you whisper your pain into the dust, and it is heard above the seven heavens."

---

### 03:35 – 04:15 | SECTION 5: THE LIVING ACTION & CALL TO RETURN
**[VISUAL]**  
*Golden morning sunlight breaking over an expansive ocean coastline, gentle waves rolling smoothly onto natural sand beneath warm pastel skies. Horizon opening up into boundless peace.*

**(VOICEOVER):**  
"Do not allow the toxicity of other people's words to poison your relationship with Allah.

Here is your practical anchor for today: The Sajdah of Relief.

If someone made you feel hurt, insulted, or unappreciated this week, refuse to send an angry message. Instead, perform two quiet rak'ahs of prayer tonight. When you drop into Sujood, stay there for three full minutes. Tell Allah exactly what was said, how it felt, and ask Him to expand your chest with His peace.

Open Surah Al-Hijr tonight. Sit for five unhurried minutes with your Lord.

Read it. Reflect upon it. Return to Him."
