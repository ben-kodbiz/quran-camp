"There are seasons in life where holding on causes far more damage than letting go.

«Wa awḥaynā ilā ummi Mūsā an arḍi'īh, fa-idhā khifti 'alayhi fa-alqīhi fī al-yammi wa lā takhāfī wa lā taḥzanī...»

Because the river has no power to drown unless the Lord of the river commands it. And the enemy's palace cannot kill what Allah has decreed to preserve.

When your heart feels completely emptied by grief or panic, remember: the One who guarded the cradle upon the Nile is watching over you."

**(VOICEOVER):**  
"Years later, Musa found himself in total exile.

Once a royal prince, he was now a fleeing fugitive: penniless, homeless, starving, with blistered feet after walking eight days across the desert into the land of Madyan.

He arrived at a well and saw two young women waiting patiently while aggressive shepherds pushed their way forward. Despite his overwhelming exhaustion, Musa stepped forward, drew the water for their flock, and expected nothing in return.

He walked into the shade of a tree, sat down in the dust, and whispered:

«Rabbi innī limā anzalta ilayya min khayrin faqīr.»

'My Lord, indeed I am in absolute need of whatever good You send down upon me.'

He did not demand a palace or specify his desires. He declared himself a complete beggar before the Almighty, open to whatever grace Allah chose to send.

And within hours, Allah provided him with shelter, food, honest work, and a family."
