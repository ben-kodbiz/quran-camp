# The Gift Beneath the Throne : The Nightly Sufficiency
## 60-Second Cinematic Vertical Video Script

### Core Visual Equation
`NIGHT HORIZON + CELESTIAL STARDUST + TIME-LAPSE MOON + TRANQUIL INTERIOR`

---

### Shot 1: The Ascent to the Farthest Lote Tree (00–12s)
- **Visual:** Slow, awe-inspiring vertical tilt upward through deep layers of atmospheric midnight clouds into the pristine darkness of space. Silver moonlight illuminates the edges of the cloud banks. No artificial graphics—pure cinematic sky photography.
- **Audio:** Deep, reverberating bell drone and ethereal ambient texture.
- **Voiceover (Reverent, focused, profound):**  
  *"On the night of the Heavenly Ascension, the Prophet Muhammad stood at the boundary of all created existence. And there, he received a gift granted to no prophet before him."*

---

### Shot 2: The Celestial Treasury (12–25s)
- **Visual:** Cinematic wide shot of a lone traveler atop a vast desert plateau beneath a breathtaking starry cosmos. The Milky Way arches majestically overhead in crisp, photorealistic perfection.
- **Audio:** Swelling warm string quartet. Gentle breath of the wind.
- **Voiceover:**  
  *"Revealed directly from a treasury beneath the Divine Throne—without the intermediary of an angel—came the final two verses of Surah Al-Baqarah."*

---

### Shot 3: The Promise of 'Kafataah' (25–40s)
- **Visual:** Macro shot of a hand gently opening an authentic, classically bound Qur'an under soft amber reading light. The pages turn with deliberate reverence. The camera catches the exquisite contrast of black ink and cream paper.
- **Audio:** Subtle sound of crisp paper turning. Low piano chords.
- **Voiceover:**  
  *"The Prophet declared in Sahih al-Bukhari: 'Whoever recites these two verses during the night, they will suffice him.' They suffice you as night prayer. They suffice you as protection from every evil spirit. They suffice you in peace."*

---

### Shot 4: The Closed Eyes & The Fortress (40–52s)
- **Visual:** Serene, shadowy room. A window slightly ajar shows the tranquil crescent moon in the night sky. The peaceful silhouette of a person exhaling slowly into restorative rest.
- **Audio:** A gentle, comforting lullaby tone on acoustic guitar and cello.
- **Voiceover:**  
  *"Two minutes before you sleep. That is all it takes to wrap your soul in the unassailable fortress of God's Throne."*
