# Episode 17 Flagship Script (`QURAN-COMEBACK-SCRIPT-017`)

**Campaign:** Come Back to the Qur'an  
**Title:** *"The Two People Who Deserve Your Gentle Voice"*  
**Ayah Reference:** Surah Al-Isra 17:23–24, 79, 82  
**Duration:** ~3.8 minutes (approx. 495 spoken words)  
**Tone:** Unhurried, deeply contemplative, compassionate, authentic, restorative  
**Visual Living Creation Directive:** 100% Living nature and sacred geography. Calm mountain lake at twilight reflecting stars, infinite spiral galaxy, noble Arabian horse in palm oasis, bird of paradise in emerald canopy, and Masjid an-Nabawi at sunrise. Absolute Zero-Human Mandate. Zero burned-in subtitles.

---

## PRODUCTION SCRIPT & DIALOGUE

### 00:00 – 00:45 | SECTION 1: THE SHARP TONGUE AT HOME
**[VISUAL]**  
*Cinematic wide shot of a calm, solitary mountain lake at twilight reflecting crystalline stars and pre-dawn morning mist. Solitary atmosphere, emotional stillness, expansive negative space.*

**(VOICEOVER):**  
"Why is it that we can speak with infinite patience to colleagues, strangers, and clients on the phone, yet unleash our shortest tempers on the aging parents who raised us?

When an outsider asks for our time, we smile politely. But when a mother asks a simple question for the third time, our tone sharpens. When an aging father struggles with technology, an audible sigh slips from our lips.

We convince ourselves that we are just tired or stressed. But inside, that sharp tone leaves a hollow ache—a quiet guilt that sits in the chest long after the door has closed."

---

### 00:45 – 01:40 | SECTION 2: THE SACRED CHARTER OF PARENTS
**[VISUAL]**  
*Cinematic wide view of an infinite spiral galaxy slowly rotating in deep cosmic space beneath billions of brilliant stars. Vast cosmic order, timeless perspective, profound humility.*

**(VOICEOVER):**  
"In Surah Al-Isra, Allah places kindness to parents directly beside the highest commandment of monotheism:

'And your Lord has decreed that you worship none but Him, and to parents, excellence in treatment. Whether one or both of them reach old age with you, say not to them so much as uff, and do not repel them, but speak to them a noble word.'

Look at the divine pairing. Worship Allah alone, and treat parents with Ihsan.

Allah did not say: treat them well if they were perfect. He said: treat them with excellence when they reach old age—at the very moment they are most frail, most repetitive, and most vulnerable to your neglect."

---

### 01:40 – 02:40 | SECTION 3: THE PROHIBITION OF A SIGH
**[VISUAL]**  
*Dark noble Arabian horse resting peacefully in a dewy palm grove oasis in early pre-dawn light. Dewdrops glisten on grass blades; absolute calmness, strength, and quiet dignity.*

**(VOICEOVER):**  
"Classical scholars noticed something startling about this verse. Allah prohibited the smallest word in the human language: *Uff*—an exasperated sigh.

If Allah forbade the sound of air escaping your lungs in frustration, what does that say about raising your voice, rolling your eyes, or ignoring their messages for days?

The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ warned us with utmost clarity: your paradise lies beneath her feet, and the father is the middle gate of Jannah.

You can accumulate degrees, build businesses, and amass wealth. But if you break the heart of the mother who carried you, you are holding ashes in your hands."

---

### 02:40 – 03:30 | SECTION 4: THE SANCTUARY OF TAHAJJUD & SHIFA'
**[VISUAL]**  
*A rare Bird of Paradise (Cenderawasih) perched gracefully on an emerald rainforest branch in early dawn light. Its exquisite iridescent plumage gleams softly, untroubled by the world.*

**(VOICEOVER):**  
"Later in this same surah, Allah gives the remedy for a burdened soul:

'And from the night, arise from sleep for prayer as an additional devotion for you; perhaps your Lord will resurrect you to a praised station.'

And He reminds us: 'And We send down of the Qur'an that which is healing and mercy for the believers.'

When the house goes quiet and everyone is asleep, stand before Allah in the darkness of Tahajjud. In that solitary hour, ask Allah to soften your tongue, to heal old family wounds, and to grant your parents His boundless mercy."

---

### 03:30 – 04:15 | SECTION 5: THE LOWERED WING CALL
**[VISUAL]**  
*Golden dawn light illuminating the soaring marble colonnades and sacred courtyard arches of the Prophet's sanctuary in Medina. Peaceful morning sky, pure architectural serenity, timeless tranquility.*

**(VOICEOVER):**  
"Here is your practical anchor for today: The Lowered Wing Call.

If your parents are alive, call them today with zero agenda. Do not ask for favors. Just ask how their morning was. Lower the wing of humility to them in your cadence. Speak gently, listen patiently, and let them talk.

And if they have already returned to Allah, perform two quiet rak'ahs tonight and make this Qur'anic du'a: 'My Lord, have mercy upon them as they brought me up when I was small.'

Turn off the noise tonight. Open Surah Al-Isra. Sit for five unhurried minutes with your Lord.

Read it. Reflect upon it. Return to Him."
