# Episode 4 Flagship Script (`QURAN-COMEBACK-SCRIPT-004`)

**Campaign:** Come Back to the Qur'an  
**Title:** *"What If You've Been Away From Allah?"*  
**Duration:** ~5 minutes (approx. 660 spoken words)  
**Tone:** Compassionate, liberating, hopeful, emotionally healing  

---

## PRODUCTION SCRIPT & DIALOGUE

### 00:00 – 00:30 | SECTION 1: THE HOOK
**[VISUAL]**  
*Rain falling steadily against an old glass window pane. Outside, a lush mountain forest breathes in mist. The camera stays inside the quiet room, watching single water droplets trace paths down the glass.*

**(VOICEOVER):**  
"One of the quietest lies we tell ourselves is:  
*‘I’m too far gone. I haven’t prayed in months. I’ve made too many mistakes. Who am I to open the Holy Qur'an?’*

You see the Mushaf sitting on your desk. You want to touch it. But an overwhelming wave of guilt washes over you. You feel like a hypocrite.

If you have ever felt that weight... I want you to hear how Allah speaks to you in Surah Az-Zumar."

---

### 00:30 – 01:30 | SECTION 2: THE AYAH & TRANSLATION
**[VISUAL]**  
*Macro tracking shot of an open page in Surah Az-Zumar. Clean typography fades in over the gentle rain atmosphere.*

**(VOICEOVER):**  
"In verse 53, Allah commands His Messenger:

$$\text{قُلْ يَا عِبَادِيَ الَّذِينَ أَسْرَفُوا عَلَىٰ أَنفُسِهِمْ لَا تَقْنَطُوا مِن رَّحْمَةِ اللَّهِ ۚ إِنَّ اللَّهَ يَغْفِرُ الذُّنُوبَ جَمِيعًا ۚ إِنَّهُ هُوَ الْغَفُورُ الرَّحِيمُ}$$

*‘Say, ˹O Prophet, that Allah says,˺ "O My servants who have transgressed against themselves! Do not despair of the mercy of Allah. Indeed, Allah forgives all sins. Truly He is the All-Forgiving, Most Merciful."’*

Classical scholars call this the single most hopeful verse in the entire Qur'an.

Listen to how Allah begins:  
He doesn't say, *‘O you hypocrites.’* He doesn't say, *‘O you disgraced sinners.’*  
He says: **Yā 'Ibādī**—*‘O My servants.’*  
Even when you have squandered your time. Even when you have exceeded all bounds.  
He still claims you as His own."

---

### 01:30 – 02:45 | SECTION 3: TAFSIR & THE STORY OF THE REGRETFUL
**[VISUAL]**  
*A stone mountain path slowly clearing as the rain softens into gentle sunlight breaking through grey clouds.*

**(VOICEOVER):**  
"Imam al-Bukhari records why this verse descended.

A group of people in Makkah who had spent their entire lives in darkness—committing murder, practicing idolatry, indulging in every excess—came to the Prophet ﷺ with broken hearts.  
They said: *‘What you teach is so beautiful. But is there any expiation for someone who has done what we have done?’*

And Allah revealed this ayah in response.

Mufti Muhammad Shafi explains that Shaytan plays a cruel game with believers:  
Before you sin, he tells you: *‘Allah is forgiving, go ahead.’*  
And after you sin, he whispers: *‘You are irredeemable. Allah doesn't want you back.’*

This verse exists to crush that second whisper.  
Allah makes it a divine command: **Lā taqnatū**—*Do not despair.*  
Despair is not humility. Despair is doubting the ocean of Allah's mercy."

---

### 02:45 – 04:00 | SECTION 4: TADABBUR — SINS REACHING THE CLOUDS
**[VISUAL]**  
*Wide, vast shot of mountain ridges enveloped in rolling clouds at sunset. Luminous amber light breaking through the peaks.*

**(VOICEOVER):**  
"In an authentic Hadith Qudsi recorded by Imam at-Tirmidhi, Allah speaks directly to this fear:

*‘O son of Adam, if your sins were to reach the clouds of the sky, and then you asked forgiveness of Me, I would forgive you, and I would not mind.’*

Look up at the sky. Imagine filling every inch of that atmosphere with your past mistakes, your missed prayers, your broken promises.  
Allah says: *If you come to Me asking for forgiveness, I will meet you with an earthful of pardon.*

The Qur'an was not sent for people who never stumble.  
The Qur'an was sent as the medicine for those who are bleeding."

---

### 04:00 – 05:00 | SECTION 5: PRACTICAL ACTION & QUIET CTA
**[VISUAL]**  
*Hand gently opening the Qur'an to Surah Az-Zumar. The atmosphere is warm, quiet, and grounded.*

**(VOICEOVER):**  
"If you have been away from Allah for weeks, months, or years... today is your day of return.

Do not wait until tomorrow.  
Do not wait until you feel worthy.

Tonight, open Surah Az-Zumar, verse 53. Read it out loud.  
Let the words settle into your chest.  
Then raise your hands and whisper:  
*‘Ya Allah, I have transgressed against my own soul. But I have returned to Your mercy.’*

Read it. Understand it. Live it.  
And come back tomorrow."
