# Huurs Studio: Executive Calling Card & Contemplation Anchor Card Specifications

**Design Philosophy:** Minimalist Islamic Luxury • Tactile Reverence • Clean Negative Space

## 1. Executive Business Card (3.5" × 2.0" / 89mm × 51mm)

### Dimensions & Bleed
* **Trim Size:** `3.5 in × 2.0 in` (88.9 mm × 50.8 mm)
* **Bleed Size:** `3.75 in × 2.25 in` (95.25 mm × 57.15 mm — 0.125 in bleed on all 4 sides)
* **Safe Zone:** `3.25 in × 1.75 in` (Keep all critical text and icons within this boundary)

### Paper Stock & Finishing Specifications
* **Stock:** `450 gsm (32 pt) Ultra-Heavyweight Silk Matte Cover Stock`
* **Finish:** `Double-sided Soft-Touch Velvet Lamination` (subtle tactile warmth, anti-fingerprint)
* **Foil Stamping:** `Hot-Stamped Satin Antique Gold Foil (Kurz Luxor 428)` on the primary wordmark (*HUURS STUDIO*) and motto (*READ. REFLECT. RETURN.*)
* **Spot Finish:** `Blind Debossing` on the geometric archway emblem (subtle indented relief)
* **Edges:** Optional `Gilded Antique Gold Foil Edge-Gilding` for executive founder sets

### Typography & Palette
* **Primary Display:** *Cinzel* (Semibold 10pt / Kerning +180)
* **Body & Contact:** *Inter* (Regular & Light 7.5pt / Kerning +40)
* **Scripture Quote:** *Cormorant Garamond* (Italic 9pt)
* **Color Palette:**
  * Obsidian Black: `#0a0d13` (Rich CMYK: 60/40/40/100)
  * Metallic Antique Gold: `#d4af37` (Foil Stamp)
  * Muted Sand White: `#e2e8f0` (CMYK: 0/0/0/10)

## 2. Pocket Contemplation Anchor Card (Wallet / Pocket Size)

### Concept & Purpose
A tactile reminder card distributed to partners, event attendees, and major patrons. It serves as a pocket sanctuary during moments of worldly stress or decision-making.

### Front Design
* Visual motif: Mountain stream rippling over pebbles in dawn light.
* Headline: *The Lowered Wing Anchor*
* Verse: *"My Lord, expand for me my chest, and ease for me my task."* (Surah Ta-Ha 20:25–26)
* Action: *"Take three deep breaths. Disconnect from worldly panic. Return to your Creator."*

### Back Design
* Discrete QR code leading directly to the offline audio reflection stream.
* URL: `https://huurs.com/return`
* Studio mark and year of production.

## 3. Recommended Print Vendors & Setup Instructions
* **Vendor Options:** Moo (Luxe 600gsm with Gold Foil), Moo / MOO Size, VistaPrint Pro, or local boutique letterpress printers.
* **Vector Output:** CMYK 300 DPI vector PDF with outlined fonts and separate foil mask layer (`Foil_Mask_K100`).
