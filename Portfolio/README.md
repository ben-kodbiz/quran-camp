## 4. Production & Printing Execution Guide

### Printing the Trifold Brochure
1. **Paper Stock:** 150–170 gsm silk/satin coated text stock (prevents show-through while allowing crisp folding).
2. **Printer Settings:** Standard US Letter (8.5" x 11") or A4, **Landscape orientation**, **Scale: 100% (Do not fit/shrink)**.
3. **Duplexing:** Set printer to **Two-Sided (Flip on Short Edge)**.
4. **Folding:** Fold Panel 5 inward first from the left, then fold Panel 1 (Front Cover) over top from the right.

### Printing the Business Cards
1. **Paper Stock:** 400–450 gsm heavyweight silk card stock.
2. **Finishing:** Soft-touch velvet matte lamination with antique gold hot-foil stamping on card borders and logos.
3. **Printer Settings:** Standard US Letter (8.5" x 11") Portrait, **Two-Sided (Flip on Long Edge)**.
4. **Trimming:** Follow the corner crop tick marks with a rotary guillotine cutter to achieve exact 3.5" x 2.0" dimensions.
