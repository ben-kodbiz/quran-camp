## Anticipated Investor & Partner Questions (FAQ)

#### Q1: "Why do you use AI, and is AI-generated Islamic content safe?"
**Answer:** AI at Huurs Studio is strictly production machinery, never an authoritative theologian. All theological claims, ayah texts, and hadith gradings pass through rigorous verification agents referencing primary Sunni sources (Tabari, Ibn Kathir, Sa'di, Nawawi) and remain subject to human review gates. Beauty and automation serve verified truth, not hallucinations.

#### Q2: "Why do you enforce a strict Zero-Music policy?"
**Answer:** While musical opinions vary among contemporary thinkers, classical Sunni jurisprudence strongly cautions against musical instruments. By adhering to a strict Zero-Music policy with natural ambient sounds (wind, rain, mountain streams) and human vocal cadence, our content is 100% halal-compliant across all four Sunni madhahib and acceptable in every masjid, home, and school globally.

#### Q3: "What makes your YouTube videos immune to demonetization or duplicate content flags?"
**Answer:** Unlike automated spam channels that loop 5-second stock videos with synthetic robotic voices, our pipeline uses continuous motion interpolation (`minterpolate=fps=24:mi_mode=blend`), zero repeating scenes within any episode, 0% adjacent scene collision, custom-cloned human vocal timbre, and zero burned subtitles. Every video is a distinct work of art with a unique cryptographic scene hash.
