## 2. AGENT-15: Islamic Quality Assurance Audit (P0 Non-Negotiable)

### A. Sunni Theological & Textual Integrity Audit
1. **Qur'anic Ayaat Text Verification:**
   - All Arabic ayaat across all 114 Surah chapters and 10 Flagship scripts conform strictly to the Medina Uthmani standard (*Mushaf al-Madinah an-Nabawiyyah*).
   - Zero omission or transposition of vocalization marks (*tashkeel*), nunations, or stopping symbols (*waqf*).
2. **Translation Standardization:**
   - English translations universally adhere to *The Clear Quran* by Dr. Mustafa Khattab (approved by Al-Azhar), with cross-checks against Sahih International.
3. **Classical Tafsir Discipline:**
   - Every chapter incorporates commentary from the four authoritative Sunni pillars:
     - *Tafsir al-Qur'an al-'Azim* (Imam Ibn Kathir)
     - *Taysir al-Karim al-Rahman* (Shaykh Abdur-Rahman as-Sa'di)
     - *Al-Jami' li-Ahkam al-Qur'an* (Imam al-Qurtubi)
     - *Ma'arif-ul-Qur'an* (Mufti Muhammad Shafi)
4. **Hadith Authentication Protocol:**
   - 100% of cited hadith traditions are authenticated directly from primary canonical collections (*Sahih al-Bukhari*, *Sahih Muslim*, *Sunan Abi Dawud*, *Jami' at-Tirmidhi*, *Sunan an-Nasa'i*, *Sunan Ibn Majah*, and *Musnad Ahmad*).
   - Zero weak (*da'if*) or fabricated (*mawdu'*) traditions incorporated into core theological or legislative claims.
5. **Strict Tafsir vs. Tadabbur Separation:**
   - Every artifact maintains an impermeable boundary between objective scholarly exegesis (*Tafsir*) and guided personal reflection (*Tadabbur*). Contemplation prompts are explicitly framed as subjective introspection, never as authoritative divine intent.
