# Claim-Level Verification Report: Surah As-Saffat

### Claim 1: Angelic Rows in Prayer and Battle
- **Source:** *Sahih Muslim* (Hadith 430). The Prophet ﷺ said: "Will you not line up as the angels line up before their Lord?" The Sahabah asked how they line up, and he replied: "They complete the foremost rows and keep close to one another in the row."
- **Status:** Authenticated (Sahih).

### Claim 2: Meteors Incinerating Eavesdropping Devils (37:6–10)
- **Source:** *Sahih al-Bukhari* (Hadith 4701) from Abu Hurayrah (RA). The Prophet ﷺ explained how devils climb on each other's shoulders to overhear decrees, and the shooting star strikes them.
- **Status:** Authenticated (Sahih).

### Claim 3: Identity of the Sacrificial Son (*Al-Dhabeeh*) as Ismail (37:102–107)
- **Source:** *Tafsir al-Tabari* (21/72), *Tafsir Ibn Kathir* (7/28), *Al-Mustadrak lil-Hakim* (2/556). Soundest Sunni consensus holds that Ismail was the sacrifice because Isaac's birth was accompanied by the glad tidings of his grandson Jacob (11:71), which would preclude sacrificing Isaac in childhood.
- **Status:** Sunni Exegetical Consensus.

### Claim 4: The Gourdtree (*Yaqteen*) and Yunus's Deliverance (37:146)
- **Source:** *Tafsir Ibn Kathir* (7/45) citing Ibn Mas'ud and Ibn Abbas: *Al-Yaqteen* is the pumpkin/gourd, beneficial because it repels flies, has delicate shade, and nutritious soft pulp.
- **Status:** Authenticated Companion Exegesis.

### Claim 5: The Liturgical Closure (37:180–182)
- **Source:** *Sunan Abi Dawud*, *Musnad Ahmad*. The Prophet ﷺ used to recite *Subhana Rabbika Rabbil-'Izzati 'amma yasifoon...* at the end of every prayer.
- **Status:** Authenticated Sunnah.
