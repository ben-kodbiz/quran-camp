## 3. Theological Red-Line Audit

1. **Prophetic Infallibility (*'Ismah*):** Regarding *Wa laqad hammat bihi wa hamma biha*: Sunni authorities (At-Tabari, Al-Baghawi, Ibn Kathir, Ar-Razi) reject baseless mythological interpolations. Yusuf experienced natural mortal inclination (*Hamma tabi'iyyan*), but because he saw the *Burhan* of his Lord, he firmly restrained himself without committing any sin, transgression, or touch. He was divinely preserved as an absolute model of chastity (*Al-Mukhliseen*).
2. **Prostration of the Parents and Brothers:** Clarified that the prostration (*Sujud*) was an ancient cultural prostration of greeting and honor (*Sujud Tahiyyah*), strictly legal in the Shari'ah of previous prophets, but definitively abrogated in Islam where prostration is exclusive to Allah alone.
3. **Seeking Worldly Office:** Yusuf requested administrative authority (*Khaza'in al-Ard*) strictly for public service and famine relief, possessing unique competence (*Hafeezun 'Aleem*), without greed for power or wealth.
4. **Crying and Grief in Trials:** Ya'qub's weeping to the point of blindness does not contradict *Sabr Jameel*. Grief in the heart and tears in the eyes are natural human compassion; *Sabr Jameel* forbids vocal despair, questioning divine wisdom, and complaining to creation.
5. **The Shirt as a Means of Healing:** The restoration of Ya'qub's vision was a miraculous divine sign granted through the prophetic shirt, not an independent occult talisman.
6. **Forgiveness and Legal Amnesty:** Yusuf's pardon (*La tathreeba 'alaykumul-yawm*) illustrates the highest prophetic virtue of magnanimity when possessing absolute power to execute retribution.
