## 3. Evaluated Theological Red Lines

1. **The Beatific Vision (*Ru'yatullah*):** The interpretation of *Az-Ziyadah* as looking upon the Countenance of Allah is certified strictly through authentic hadiths in *Sahih Muslim* and orthodox Sunni Ash'ari/Maturidi/Athari consensus, affirming vision without bodily form or directional limitation.
2. **Prophetic Infallibility:** Prophet Yunus's departure before explicit command is framed with canonical reverence—an understandable human haste that led to divine testing in the whale, resulting in profound Tasbeeh (*La ilaha illa Anta subhanaka inni kuntu min az-zalimeen*).
3. **Pharaoh's Rejected Repentance:** The late repentance of Pharaoh is confirmed as completely void and rejected by consensus, as it occurred during the agony of death (*Hadd al-Ya's*).
4. **Preservation of Pharaoh's Corpse:** Affirming *Nunajjeeka bi-badanika* as physical preservation of his corpse on the shores of the Red Sea for historical contemplation, consistent with Quranic tafsir and modern archaeology.
5. **Human Free Will & Divine Will:** Reconciling *Had your Lord willed, all on earth would have believed* with the moral accountability of rejecting truth; rejecting fatalism and affirming responsible human volition.
6. **The Status of the Awliya:** Affirmed as righteous believers characterized by faith and Taqwa, explicitly rejecting any claims of infallibility (*'Ismah*) or exemption from Shari'ah obligations.
