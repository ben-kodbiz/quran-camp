## 3. Theological Red Lines Cleared

1. **Divine Transcendence in Agency:** Verse 8:17 (*"You threw not when you threw, but Allah threw"*) is interpreted in accordance with orthodox Sunni creed (*Ahl al-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah*): Affirming human acquisition of actions (*Kasb*) while establishing that God is the Creator of all acts and ultimate causes.
2. **Defensive Deterrence vs. Aggression:** Verse 8:60 (*Turhiboona bihi*) is contextualized as defensive deterrence designed to prevent war, reinforced by the immediate command in 8:61: *"And if they incline to peace, incline unto it"*.
3. **No Sanctification of Plunder:** The opening verse decisively strips material greed from conflict; spoils belong to God and the Messenger, subordinated to public welfare.
4. **Historical Realism of Badr:** Affirmed as a historical, miraculous event supported by Mutawatir scriptural texts, refuting mythological or naturalistic reductions.
