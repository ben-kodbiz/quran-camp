## 3. Theological Red Lines Audited & Cleared

1. **Rejection of Physical Anthropomorphism:** The phrase *Istawa 'alal-'Arsh* (He established Himself above the Throne) is maintained in accordance with the consensus of Ahl as-Sunnah: affirmation without resemblance, modality, distortion, or nullification (*Ithbat bila tashbeeh wa la tamtheel*).
2. **Rejection of Mechanical Deism:** Natural phenomena (thunder, lightning, rain, orbits) are explicitly defined as sentient, decreed actions governed directly by Allah, utterly rejecting secular reductionism.
3. **Rejection of Deterministic Fatalism:** The axiom *Hatta yughayyiroo ma bi-anfusihim* affirms real human moral responsibility and agency within the encompassing will of God.
4. **Preservation of Prophetic Infallibility:** The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ is presented with supreme dignity as an authorized warner (*Mundhir*) and teacher of truth, free of modern revisionist skepticism.
5. **Separation of Preserved Tablet from Angelic Scrolls:** Classical Sunni resolution of *Yamhoo Allahu ma yashaa'u wa yuthbitu*: changes occur in the records held by angels regarding life, health, and provision based on prayer/ties of kinship, while the primordial knowledge in *Ummul-Kitab* is immutable.
6. **Integrity of Canonical Hadith:** Every hadith reference utilized is traced to the Sahihayn, Sunan, or verified Musnad collections.
