## 3. Theological Red Lines Audited & Cleared

1. **Rejection of Coercive Fatalism in Satan's Khutbah:** Satan's explicit disavowal (*"I possessed no authority over you except that I invited you"*) firmly establishes human moral agency and culpability, refuting Jabrite determinism.
2. **Rejection of Anthropomorphic Scaffolding:** Divine transcendence (*Tanzih*) maintained across descriptions of the Creator's throne, retribution, and cosmic emergence.
3. **Integrity of Prophetic Intercession & Lineage:** Ibrahim's prayer for his parents (*Rabbana ighfir lee wa li-walidayya*) is understood within the classical Sunni consensus: made prior to the realization of his father's final obstinate polytheism, whereupon he disassociated, as clarified in Surah At-Tawbah.
4. **Preservation of Grave Questioning Orthodoxy:** Verification of *Al-Qawl ath-Thabit* explicitly anchored in the authentic Hadith of Al-Bara' ibn 'Azib regarding the trials of the grave.
5. **Rejection of Mythological Cosmologies:** The transmutation of the earth is affirmed as a physical eschatological reality confirmed by Mutawatir texts, not allegorical poetry.
6. **Integrity of Canonical Hadith:** All cited traditions authenticated in *Sahih al-Bukhari*, *Sahih Muslim*, or canonical Sunan.
