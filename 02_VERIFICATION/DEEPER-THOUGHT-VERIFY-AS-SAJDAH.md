## 1. Executive Summary

This document establishes the claim-level verification audit for Surah As-Sajdah (Surah 32), certifying that every theological statement, prophetic narration, legal determination, and historical exegesis adheres strictly to authentic classical Sunni sources (*Ahl al-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah*).
