# Claim-Level Verification Report: Surah Sad

### Claim 1: The Asbab of the Quraysh Delegation at Abu Talib's Deathbed (38:1–5)
- **Source:** *Jami' at-Tirmidhi* (Hadith 3232, certified Sahih by Al-Albani), *Musnad Ahmad* (1/362), *Al-Mustadrak lil-Hakim* (2/432). Ibn Abbas confirms that Abu Jahl and other chieftains protested against *La ilaha illallah*, stating: "Has he made the gods into one God?"
- **Status:** Authenticated (Sahih).

### Claim 2: Dawud's Prostration in Ayah 24
- **Source:** *Sahih al-Bukhari* (Hadith 4807). Ibn Abbas (RA) stated that the prostration in Surah Sad is not from the obligatory عزائم, but he saw the Prophet ﷺ prostrating during its recitation.
- **Status:** Authenticated (Sahih).

### Claim 3: Infallibility of Dawud and Refutation of the Uriah Slander (38:21–25)
- **Source:** *Tafsir Ibn Kathir* (7/57), *Tafsir al-Qurtubi* (15/164), *Al-Shifa* of Qadi Iyad. Sunni scholarship unanimously rejects the biblical myth that Dawud plotted the death of Uriah to marry his wife. The test was purely judicial: ruling prematurely on a dispute before hearing the other party.
- **Status:** Sunni Orthodoxy & Theological Consensus.

### Claim 4: The Dispute of the High Council (*Al-Mala' al-A'la*) (38:69)
- **Source:** *Jami' at-Tirmidhi* (Hadith 3233, Sahih). The Prophet ﷺ explained that the Heavenly Assembly was discussing the expiations (*Kaffarat*) and elevating deeds.
- **Status:** Authenticated (Sahih).
