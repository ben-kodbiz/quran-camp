## 3. Evaluation of Theological Red Lines

1. **Textual Incorruptibility of the Qur'an:** Authenticated CLM-HJ02 confirms that Allah Himself is the sole guardian of the Qur'anic text, thoroughly refuting any claims of alteration, omission, or historical erosion.
2. **Angelic Infallibility:** Authenticated CLM-HJ11 (*Kulluhum ajma'oon*) proves all angels prostrated without exception; Iblis was not a fallen angel, but one of the jinn as explicitly settled by Surah Al-Kahf (18:50).
3. **No Coercive Power for Satan:** Authenticated CLM-HJ16 establishes that Satan possesses zero coercive authority; human beings bear total moral responsibility for their freely chosen transgressions.
4. **Authenticity of Al-Sab' al-Mathani:** Authenticated CLM-HJ23 relies on explicit, unequivocal Sahih al-Bukhari narrations establishing that Surah Al-Fatihah is the Seven Oft-Repeated verses bestowed upon the Prophet.
5. **No Fatalistic Despair:** Authenticated CLM-HJ20 affirms that despair of divine mercy is the hallmark of deviation; divine power transcends human biological limitations.
6. **Interpretation of Al-Yaqeen:** Authenticated CLM-HJ24 confirms that *Al-Yaqeen* refers strictly to death, refuting antinomian heretical claims that spiritual enlightenment excuses one from formal ritual worship.
