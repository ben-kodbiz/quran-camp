## 3. Theological Red Lines Cleared

1. **Denial of Tashbeeh (Anthropomorphism):** The textual affirmation of *Istawa 'alal-'Arsh* is interpreted according to classical Sunni orthodoxy (*Bila Kayf, Bila Tashbeeh, Bila Ta'teel*).
2. **Prophetic Infallibility (*'Ismah*):** Adam's lapse is confirmed as an error forgiven through contrite repentance, not intentional defiance; Musa's anger and handling of Harun is recognized as passionate defense of Tawhid, not personal malice.
3. **Beatific Vision (*Ru'yah Allah*):** Musa was told *Lan tarani* in this mortal temporal world; this does not negate the believers gazing upon their Lord in the Hereafter, as confirmed by Mutawatir Sunnah.
4. **Authenticity of the Primordial Covenant:** Grounded in authentic prophetic traditions (*Ahadith al-Fitrah*), establishing universal moral accountability across all descendants of Adam.
5. **Rejection of Esoteric Distortion:** Historical narratives of Nuh, Hud, Salih, Lut, Shu'ayb, and Musa are treated as historical facts and sacred signs, not mythological allegories.
6. **Integrity of Divine Names:** Total refutation of *Ilhad* (distortion, derivation from pagan idols, or denial of attributes).
