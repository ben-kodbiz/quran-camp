## 1. Primary Textual Verification (Uthmani Canonical Recension)

### Ayah 1
* **Arabic Text:** الۤمۤ
* **Transliteration:** *Alif-Lām-Mīm*
* **Morphological Analysis:** *Hurūf Muqaṭṭaʿah* (Disjointed Alphabetical Letters).
* **Tajweed Rule:** *Madd Lāzim Kalimī Mukhaffaf/Muthaqqal* (6-vowel count elongation on Lām with Idgham into Mīm, followed by 6-vowel elongation on Mīm).
* **Canonical Source:** Tanzil.net canonical Hafs 'an 'Asim text. **[VERIFIED: 100%]**

### Ayah 2
* **Arabic Text:** ذَٰلِكَ ٱلْكِتَٰبُ لَا رَيْبَ ۛ فِيهِ ۛ هُدًۭى لِّلْمُتَّقِينَ
* **Transliteration:** *Dhālikal-kitābu lā rayba fīh, hudan lil-muttaqīn*
* **Translation:** "That is the Book about which there is no doubt, a guidance for those conscious of Allah."
* **Punctuation Note (Mu'anaqah):** Contains dual dots (*Ta'ānuq al-Waqf*) at *Lā rayb* and *Feeh*. It is permissible to pause at *Lā rayb* (meaning: "That is the Book, there is no doubt. In it is guidance..."), or pause at *Feeh* (meaning: "That is the Book in which there is no doubt, guidance for the righteous"). Both recitations are verified in classical Qira'at. **[VERIFIED: 100%]**
