   - Verses 148 decisively refutes the polytheists' fatalistic argument (*"If Allah willed, we would not have committed shirk"*), confirming human moral volition and responsibility.

   - Decisive eradication of grave worship, astrology, divination, and arbitrary pagan taboos.
