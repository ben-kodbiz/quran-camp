## 3. Theological Red-Line Audit

1. **Divine Attributes & Istiwa upon the Throne:** Affirmed in strict accordance with the Sunni methodology of *Ithbat bila Tashbeeh wa Tanzih bila Ta'teel* (affirming literal reality while negating anthropomorphic likeness).
2. **Prophetic Infallibility (*'Ismah*):** Prophet Nuh's question regarding his son was an inquiry grounded in his understanding of the initial divine covenant, not a rebellious objection; his immediate repentance and seeking refuge in Allah (*A'oodhu bika*) models supreme prophetic piety.
3. **Biological vs. Spiritual Kinship:** Cleared of sectarian or genealogical favoritism. Faith in Tawhid is the sole criteria of salvation in the Divine Court.
4. **Economic Ethics in Islam:** Prophet Shu'ayb's dialogue refutes secular economic autonomy; commerce is strictly bound by ethical jurisprudence (*Mu'amalat*).
5. **The Nature of Divine Retribution:** Natural disasters that destroyed 'Ad, Thamud, Sodom, and Madyan were direct historical retributions (*'Adhab al-Isti'sal*) following the rejection of direct prophetic warnings, executing absolute divine justice.
6. **The Balance of Istiqamah:** *Istiqamah* requires standing firm without negligence (*Tafreet*) and without extremism/transgression (*Tughyan*), refuting both antinomian laxity and radical excess.
