## 3. Evaluated Theological Red Lines

1. **The Character of the Sahabah:** No depiction of the Companions who delayed at Tabuk as hypocrites. The text strictly distinguishes between the hypocrites (*Munafiqoon*) who lied and the sincere believers (*Ka'b, Hilal, Murarah*) who spoke absolute truth and were divinely pardoned.
2. **Treaty Law & Rules of Engagement:** The "Sword Verses" (*Ayat as-Sayf*) are rigorously contextualized in accordance with classical Sunni jurisprudence as applicable against belligerent aggressors who violated sworn covenants, not as a blanket mandate against peaceful non-combatants.
3. **The Omission of the Basmalah:** Verified via authentic Sunni reports from Sayyiduna Ali and Sayyiduna Ibn Abbas, rejecting any apocryphal sectarian additions or scribal errors.
4. **Prophetic Infallibility in Conveyance:** Affirming that divine gentle reproach (*'Afallahu 'ank*) was an honorific confirmation of the Prophet's compassionate nature, not a sinful error.
5. **The Eight Categories of Zakah:** Maintained as an immutable divine law preventing discretionary embezzlement.
6. **The Intercession and Status of the Prophet ﷺ:** Reaffirming the Prophet's supreme tender concern (*'Azeezun 'alayhi ma 'anittum hareesun 'alaykum*) as the pinnacle of prophetic character.
