**Aspect Ratio:** 16:9 Landscape  
**Semantic Equation:** Pathway (Sirat) + Forest (Steadfast Growth / Living Creation) + Dawn Light (Hidayah / Divine Light) = Huurs Visual Language  

---

## 1. Generation Parameters & Prompt
* **Core Prompt:**  
  `Cinematic wide documentary photography of an ancient, clean cobblestone pathway leading straight forward through a serene, misty cedar and pine forest at early dawn. In the distance along the straight path, soft warm golden morning sunlight breaks through the atmospheric morning mist. High vertical tree trunks framing the pathway symmetrically. Minimal, contemplative, grounded, tranquil, 8k resolution, photorealistic, no people, no fantasy glowing effects, ample negative space in the upper third for subtle typography.`
* **Aspect Ratio:** 16:9
* **Negative Space:** $\ge 40\%$ in the misty canopy and upper framing, engineered for typography.
* **Palette:** Deep forest green, earth brown, slate cobblestone, soft mist white, warm amber sunrise glow.
