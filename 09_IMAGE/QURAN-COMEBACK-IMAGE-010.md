## 1. Generation Parameters & Prompt
* **Core Prompt:**  
  `Cinematic wide documentary photography of a magnificent, ancient sacred olive tree standing alone atop a verdant hill at sunrise. Its powerful, gnarled wooden trunk and visible ancient roots grip the mossy earth, while its lush green canopy reaches high into a clear, tranquil dawn sky illuminated by warm golden sunbeams. In the background, a soft misty mountain valley and distant calm lake are softly bathed in morning light. Serene, majestic, grounded, eternal, 8k resolution, authentic, no people, no fantasy elements, ample negative space in the upper sky for elegant typography.`
* **Aspect Ratio:** 16:9
* **Negative Space:** $\ge 40\%$ in the glowing sky and distant valley expanse, engineered for reflective campaign finale typography.
* **Palette:** Olive green, earthy bark brown, hillside moss, soft dawn sky blue, warm sunrise gold.
