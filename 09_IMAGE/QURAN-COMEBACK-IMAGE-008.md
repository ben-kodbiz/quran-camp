# Image Specification & Prompt Record (`QURAN-COMEBACK-IMAGE-008`)

**Associated Asset:** `09_IMAGE/QURAN-COMEBACK-ROUTINE-008.jpg`  
**Episode:** Episode 8 (*"A Qur'an Routine for Someone Who Has No Time"*)  
**Aspect Ratio:** 16:9 Landscape  
**Semantic Equation:** Open Qur'an (Daily Guidance) + Cedar Study Desk (Habit / Discipline) + Mountain Sunrise (Renewal / Hope) = Huurs Visual Language  

---

## 1. Generation Parameters & Prompt
* **Core Prompt:**  
  `Cinematic wide documentary photography of an uncluttered, minimalist warm cedar wood desk at dawn. On the desk rests an open Holy Quran, a clean ceramic cup of warm morning tea emitting gentle steam, and a simple linen-bound notebook with a fountain pen. Warm golden morning sunlight streams through a large clear window, illuminating the wood grain. Through the window, misty mountain peaks and a serene sunrise horizon are visible. Peaceful, contemplative, disciplined, 8k resolution, authentic, no people, no fantasy elements, ample negative space in the upper portion for elegant typography.`
* **Aspect Ratio:** 16:9
* **Negative Space:** $\ge 40\%$ in the upper window pane and sky area, engineered for title and practical habit typography.
* **Palette:** Warm cedar, organic ceramic cream, tea amber, sunrise gold, misty slate blue mountain ridge.

---

## 2. Visual QA Audit (`AGENT-16`)
* **Religious Imagery Compliance:** Passed (Authentic layout of Mushaf, no human figures, no fantasy tropes).
* **Photorealistic Naturalism:** 10/10 (Steam physics, morning natural light diffusion, realistic wood texture).
* **Thematic Alignment:** Visually embodies the serene, focused 10-minute micro-routine.
