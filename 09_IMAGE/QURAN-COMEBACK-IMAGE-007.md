# Image Specification & Prompt Record (`QURAN-COMEBACK-IMAGE-007`)

**Associated Asset:** `09_IMAGE/QURAN-COMEBACK-TARTIL-007.jpg`  
**Episode:** Episode 7 (*"Why Does Allah Ask Us to Read Slowly?"*)  
**Aspect Ratio:** 16:9 Landscape  
**Semantic Equation:** Qur'an on Cedar Stand (Knowledge / Revelation) + Brass Lamp (Inner Light / Sakinah) + Night Sky (Contemplation / Tahajjud) = Huurs Visual Language  

---

## 1. Generation Parameters & Prompt
* **Core Prompt:**  
  `Cinematic wide documentary photography of an authentic, open Holy Quran resting on a clean dark cedar wood stand in a tranquil, dimly lit room at night. A single warm vintage brass oil lamp softly illuminates the textured parchment pages with golden amber glow. In the background, a large arched stone window is slightly open, revealing a tranquil, quiet starlit night sky and distant dark pine trees. Contemplative, peaceful, grounded, realistic, 8k resolution, elegant, minimal, no people, no fantasy glowing effects, ample negative space in the upper portion for typography.`
* **Aspect Ratio:** 16:9
* **Negative Space:** $\ge 40\%$ in the quiet stone wall and upper shadow areas, engineered for reflective title typography.
* **Palette:** Deep umber wood, warm amber flame, charcoal stone, midnight indigo sky, soft star white.

## 2. Visual QA Audit (`AGENT-16`)

* **Photorealistic Naturalism:** 10/10 (Natural flame luminescence, realistic glass texture on lantern, authentic wood grain).
* **Thematic Alignment:** Perfectly captures the intimate late-night quiet of *Qiyam al-Layl* and *Tartil* from Surah Al-Muzzammil.
