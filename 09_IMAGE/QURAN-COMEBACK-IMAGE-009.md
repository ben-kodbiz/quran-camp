# Image Specification & Prompt Record (`QURAN-COMEBACK-IMAGE-009`)

**Associated Asset:** `09_IMAGE/QURAN-COMEBACK-TIME-009.jpg`  
**Episode:** Episode 9 (*"The Surah That Explains Why You're Losing"*)  
**Aspect Ratio:** 16:9 Landscape  
**Semantic Equation:** Stone Sundial (Al-'Asr / Fleeting Time) + Coastal Cliff (Steadfastness / Reality) + Ocean Sunset (Waning Lifespan / Horizon) = Huurs Visual Language  

---

## 1. Generation Parameters & Prompt
* **Core Prompt:**  
  `Cinematic wide documentary photography of an ancient weathered stone sundial resting on the edge of a rugged coastal cliff at sunset. The low golden sun sinks toward a calm, vast ocean horizon, casting a long, distinct shadow across the carved Roman/Arabic numerals of the dial. In the background, tranquil ocean waves gently lap against distant sea stacks under a serene sky glowing in soft amber, peach, and deep indigo twilight. Contemplative, poignant, realistic, 8k resolution, minimal, no people, no fantasy elements, ample negative space in the upper sky for elegant typography.`
* **Aspect Ratio:** 16:9
* **Negative Space:** $\ge 40\%$ in the glowing sky and calm ocean expanse, engineered for reflective typography.
* **Palette:** Slate stone, moss green, deep ocean indigo, warm amber sunlight, soft peach twilight.

---

## 2. Visual QA Audit (`AGENT-16`)
* **Religious Imagery Compliance:** Passed (No human figures, no fantasy light rays, no pseudo-Arabic).
* **Photorealistic Naturalism:** 10/10 (Weathered stone texture, accurate shadow cast by gnomon, authentic coastal topography).
* **Thematic Alignment:** Poignant visual metaphor of life slipping away like a late afternoon shadow.
