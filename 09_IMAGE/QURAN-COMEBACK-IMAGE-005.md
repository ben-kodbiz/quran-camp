# Image Specification & Prompt Record (`QURAN-COMEBACK-IMAGE-005`)

**Associated Asset:** `09_IMAGE/QURAN-COMEBACK-EASE-005.jpg`  
**Episode:** Episode 5 (*"When Life Becomes Heavy"*)  
**Aspect Ratio:** 16:9 Landscape  
**Semantic Equation:** Mountain (Steadfastness) + Stream (Fresh Sustenance / Ease) + Dawn Light (Renewal / Relief) = Huurs Visual Language  

---

## 1. Generation Parameters & Prompt
* **Core Prompt:**  
  `Cinematic wide photography of a tranquil mountain valley at dawn after a heavy storm. In the foreground, clean slate stone and a crystal-clear mountain stream cascading gently over mossy rocks. In the background, majestic distant mountain peaks partially veiled in soft, lifting morning mist. Golden morning sunlight breaks through the clouds, illuminating the cool valley with warm amber light. Serene, tranquil, realistic, contemplative, 8k documentary style photography, minimal, uncluttered with ample negative space in the upper sky for typography, no people, no fantasy elements.`
* **Aspect Ratio:** 16:9
* **Negative Space:** $\ge 40\%$ in the sky and cloud layer, engineered for title and quotation typography.
* **Palette:** Slate grey, moss green, deep granite, cold morning mist, warm amber sunrise glow.

---

## 2. Visual QA Audit (`AGENT-16`)
* **Religious Imagery Compliance:** Passed (No human figures, no fantasy light rays, no pseudo-Arabic).
* **Photorealistic Naturalism:** 10/10 (Documentary aesthetic, natural geological textures, realistic light diffusion).
* **Emotional Resonation:** Translates the heavy burden yielding to divine clarity and flowing ease.
