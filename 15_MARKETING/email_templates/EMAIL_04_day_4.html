As-salamu alaykum,

One of the greatest tricks of the ego is convincing us that guilt is piety. We tell ourselves: "I have sinned too much. I've missed prayers. I haven't opened the Qur'an in months. I feel like a hypocrite opening it now."

Despair is not humility. Despair is doubting the infinite breadth of Allah's Rahmah.

In Surah Az-Zumar 39:53, Allah addresses those who crossed every line:
"Say, O My servants who have transgressed against themselves: Do not despair of the mercy of Allah. Indeed, Allah forgives all sins."

Notice the title: "My servants." He did not strip away your relationship to Him because you fell.

Take one step toward Him tonight. Just one. He will come to you with haste.
