The grammar of relief in Surah Ash-Sharh.
Huurs Studio • Read. Reflect. Return.

As-salamu alaykum,

When we face burnout, chronic anxiety, or grief, we usually pray: "Ya Allah, let this end so ease can arrive."

We assume ease comes AFTER hardship.

Look carefully at Surah Ash-Sharh (94:5–6):
"Fa-inna ma'a al-'usri yusrā. Inna ma'a al-'usri yusrā."
"Indeed, WITH hardship comes ease. Indeed, WITH hardship comes ease."

The word is Ma'a (with), not Ba'd (after).

The ease is not waiting for your trial to terminate; the ease is already woven into the fabric of the trial itself. It is the patience you didn't know you had. The friend who reached out. The peace that descended during sujud.

Open Day 5 of your guide tonight. Sit with all eight verses of Surah 94. Breathe out.
