# The Illusion of Distance

* **Sequence Number:** 3
* **Timing / Day:** Day 3
* **Trigger:** 48 hours after Email 1.
* **Subject:** He is closer than you think (Day 3).
* **Preview Text:** Notice the word Allah did NOT say in 2:186.

---

## Email Body

As-salamu alaykum,

Whenever the companions asked the Prophet ﷺ about a religious matter in the Qur'an, Allah instructed him:
"Qul" — Say to them.

They asked about the new moons: "Say, they are measurements of time."
They asked about spending: "Say, spend what is beyond your needs."

Except once.

In Surah Al-Baqarah, verse 186, when they asked: "Is our Lord near so we may whisper to Him, or far so we must call out to Him?"
Allah did not say "Say to them."
He answered directly Himself:
"Fa-innī qarīb" — "I am indeed near."

There is no middleman between your whispered tear and the Lord of the heavens and earth.

Tonight, open Day 3 of your guide. Raise your hands. Speak to Him in your own words, in your own language. He is listening.
