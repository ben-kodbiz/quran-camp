As-salamu alaykum,

Have you ever treated the Qur'an like a decorative talisman? A book kissed and placed on the highest shelf, but rarely consulted when making life decisions?

Today's reflection in your guide focuses on Surah Al-Baqarah 2:2:
"This is the Book! There is no doubt about it—a guide for those mindful of Allah."

Notice that Allah called it Hudan: Guidance.

A guide is a map you look at when you are in unfamiliar territory. When Umar ibn al-Khattab was asked what mindfulness (Taqwa) meant, the answer was simple: walking with care along a path covered in thorns so your garments do not catch.

Today's action:
Before you open your social feed or start your workday, open the Qur'an to page 2. Look at verse 2. Tell Allah: "Guide my steps today."
