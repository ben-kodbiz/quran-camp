# Ethical Marketing Funnel & Landing Page Suite (`QURAN-COMEBACK-FUNNEL-001`)

**Campaign:** Come Back to the Qur'an  
**Objective:** Lead capture & nurturing for the free 7-Day Guided Contemplation Journal  
**Assigned Agent:** `AGENT-14` (Marketing Agent)  
**Ethical Charter:** Zero fear-based marketing, zero fake scarcity, zero manipulative emotional pressure.  

---

## 1. Landing Page Copy Architecture

### Above the Fold (Hero Section)
* **Pre-Headline:** *A gentle invitation for the busy or distant believer*
* **Headline:** **Come Back to the Qur'an.**
* **Sub-Headline:** *A quiet, 7-day guided journey to help you slow down, understand what you read, and build a lasting relationship with the Book of Allah—one ayah at a time.*
* **Primary CTA Button:** **[Download the Free 7-Day Guide (PDF)]**
* **Micro-Copy:** *100% Free. No spam, no advertisements. Just quiet, verified reflection.*

---

### The Problem Diagnosis (Empathy & Reality)
```text
We live in an age of constant Islamic reminders.
We watch videos, save inspirational clips, and follow reminders on social media.

Yet deep down, something feels incomplete.

You remember a time when reciting the Qur'an brought tears to your eyes. 
You remember wanting to understand what Allah was saying to you.
And then... life got busy. Work piled up. Arabic felt too difficult. 
And before you knew it, months passed without sitting quietly with the Book of Allah.

You don't need another 2-hour lecture to feel guilty about.
You need a gentle, realistic way back.
```

---

### What You Will Discover in "7 Days Back to the Qur'an"
1. **Day 1: The Divine Invitation** — Why Allah commands reflection (*tadabbur*) and how to ask Him to unlock your heart (Surah Muhammad 47:24).
2. **Day 2: The Purpose of Guidance** — Overcoming the feeling that the Qur'an is an inaccessible academic text (Surah Al-Baqarah 2:2).
3. **Day 3: He Is Near** — How to make your supplication an intimate conversation with your Lord (Surah Al-Baqarah 2:186).
4. **Day 4: When the Heart Softens** — What classical tafsir teaches about melting spiritual hardness (Surah Az-Zumar 39:23).
5. **Day 5: Do Not Despair** — Returning to Allah even after months or years of negligence (Surah Az-Zumar 39:53).
6. **Day 6: The 5-Minute Habit Routine** — The sustainable daily architecture for someone who has no time.
7. **Day 7: The Lifelong Companion** — Transforming short-term inspiration into an unbroken lifelong return.

## 2. Automated Email Onboarding Sequence

### Email 0: Instant Delivery (Sent immediately upon signup)
* **Subject Line:** *Your 7-day journey begins today: Download your guide*
* **Preview Text:** *Take a quiet breath. The door is always open.*

```text
Assalamu Alaykum,

Thank you for choosing to make this return.

Your copy of "7 Days Back to the Qur'an: A Quiet Journey to Reconnect Your Heart" is attached to this email and ready for download:

[DOWNLOAD YOUR PDF GUIDE HERE]

Before you dive into it, here is our only advice:
Do not rush to finish it. 
Do not try to read all seven days in one sitting.

Today, just open Day 1. It takes less than five minutes.
Read Surah Muhammad, verse 24. 
Answer the two journaling prompts. 
And tonight, before you go to sleep, complete the 5-Minute Return Protocol.

You don't need to fix everything today. Just take one small step.

With warmth and prayers,
Huurs Studio
Read. Reflect. Return.
```

### Email 1: Day 1 Follow-Up (Sent 24 hours later)
* **Subject Line:** *Did you find your five minutes yesterday?*
* **Preview Text:** *A quiet check-in on your first step.*

```text
Assalamu Alaykum,

Yesterday, we invited you to spend five quiet minutes with Surah Muhammad:
"Do they not then reflect upon the Qur'an? Or are there locks upon their hearts?"

If you were able to sit quietly and read, alhamdulillah.
If your day was chaotic and you didn't manage to open the guide... do not let Shaytan whisper that you have failed.

Spiritual return is not an all-or-nothing test. It is simply turning your face back to your Creator whenever you notice you have drifted.

Today is Day 2: "The Purpose of Guidance."
We look at Surah Al-Baqarah (2:2) and why Allah described this Book as guidance, not a burden.

Find your five quiet minutes today. The words of your Lord are waiting for you.

[OPEN DAY 2 IN YOUR GUIDE]

Warmly,
Huurs Studio
```
