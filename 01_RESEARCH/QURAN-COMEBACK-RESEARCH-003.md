## 1. Primary Qur'anic Passage: Surah Al-Baqarah (2:186)

* **Arabic Uthmani:**  
  $$\text{وَإِذَا سَأَلَكَ عِبَادِي عَنِّي فَإِنِّي قَرِيبٌ ۖ أُجِيبُ دَعْوَةَ الدَّاعِ إِذَا دَعَانِ ۖ فَلْيَسْتَجِيبُوا لِي وَلْيُؤْمِنُوا بِي لَعَلَّهُمْ يَرْشُدُونَ}$$
* **Standard Translation (The Clear Quran):**  
  *"When My servants ask you ˹O Prophet˺ concerning Me: I am indeed near. I respond to the call of the caller when he calls upon Me. So let them respond with obedience to Me, and believe in Me, perhaps they may be guided."*
* **Linguistic Architecture:**
  - **فَإِنِّي قَرِيبٌ (*Fa-innī Qarīb*):** Direct personal pronoun with the emphatic particle *Inna*. Note the structural omission of the imperative *Qul* ("Say"). When Moses asked where Allah is, or when companions asked about rulings, Allah commanded: *Say*. But regarding His nearness to the supplicant, Allah responds directly, removing even the Prophet ﷺ as an intermediary between the heart and its Lord.
  - **أُجِيبُ دَعْوَةَ الدَّاعِ (*Ujību da'wata ad-dā'*):** Present-continuous verb (*ujību*), indicating continuous, unfailing divine response to every sincere petition.

## 2. Classical Sunni Tafsir Analysis

### 2.1 Tafsir Ibn Kathir
* **Context of Revelation:** Recorded from Ibn Abi Hatim: A man asked the Prophet ﷺ: *"Is our Lord near so that we may whisper to Him, or is He far so that we must call out aloud to Him?"* Thereupon Allah revealed: *“When My servants ask you concerning Me: I am indeed near.”*
* **Placement Inside the Passages of Fasting:** Ibn Kathir highlights the profound wisdom of placing verse 186 directly between the rules of fasting (2:183–185 and 2:187). It teaches that acts of worship exist to awaken the heart to supplication, and that the supplication of the obedient believer is honored.

### 2.2 Ma'arif-ul-Qur'an (Mufti Muhammad Shafi)
* **The Elimination of Protocol:** In human courts, entering the presence of a king requires appointments, chamberlains, and etiquette. But with Allah, the Sovereign of all creation, access is immediate. No palace doors, no waiting room, no linguistic barrier. The moment you whisper in the depths of the night, He is listening.

## 3. Supporting Hadiths

1. **You Are Not Calling One Who Is Deaf or Far:**  
   *Sahih al-Bukhari* (4205), *Sahih Muslim* (2704) from Abu Musa al-Ash'ari (رضي الله عنه): The Prophet ﷺ said:  
   *"O people! Be gentle with yourselves, for you are not calling upon one who is deaf or absent. Rather, you are calling upon One Who is All-Hearing, All-Near, and He is with you [by His knowledge and encompassment]."*
2. **The Modesty and Generosity of Allah in Answering:**  
   *Jami' at-Tirmidhi* (3373), *Sunan Abi Dawud* (1481) from Salman al-Farsi (رضي الله عنه): The Prophet ﷺ said:  
   *"Indeed, your Lord is Generous and Shy. When His servant raises his hands to Him, He is shy to return them empty."*  
   Grading: *Sahih* (Authenticated by At-Tirmidhi, An-Nawawi, and Al-Albani).
