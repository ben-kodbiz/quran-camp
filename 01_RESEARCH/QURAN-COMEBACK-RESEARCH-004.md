## 1. Primary Qur'anic Passage: Surah Az-Zumar (39:53)

* **Arabic Uthmani:**  
  $$\text{قُلْ يَا عِبَادِيَ الَّذِينَ أَسْرَفُوا عَلَىٰ أَنفُسِهِمْ لَا تَقْنَطُوا مِن رَّحْمَةِ اللَّهِ ۚ إِنَّ اللَّهَ يَغْفِرُ الذُّنُوبَ جَمِيعًا ۚ إِنَّهُ هُوَ الْغَفُورُ الرَّحِيمُ}$$
* **Standard Translation (The Clear Quran):**  
  *"Say, ˹O Prophet, that Allah says,˺ 'O My servants who have transgressed against themselves! Do not despair of the mercy of Allah. Indeed, Allah forgives all sins. Truly He is the All-Forgiving, Most Merciful.'"*
* **Linguistic Architecture:**
  - **يَا عِبَادِيَ (*Yā 'Ibādī*):** "O My servants!" An honorific and deeply affectionate possessive construction. Even when addressing those who have committed excessive wrongdoing, Allah claims them as His own.
  - **أَسْرَفُوا عَلَىٰ أَنفُسِهِمْ (*Asrafū 'alā anfusihim*):** *Israf* denotes exceeding limits or squandering resources. Sinning is framed as self-inflicted harm rather than harm to Allah.
  - **لَا تَقْنَطُوا (*Lā taqnatū*):** Prohibitive command against *qunut* (despair). Despair is not just a sorrowful emotion; it is an ideological sin that denies divine mercy.
  - **جَمِيعًا (*Jamī'an*):** Absolute universal term: *all* sins without exception upon sincere repentance.

## 2. Classical Sunni Tafsir Analysis

### 2.1 Tafsir Ibn Kathir
* **The Most Hopeful Ayah in Revelation:** Ibn Kathir notes that classical scholars regarded this verse as the most expansive proclamation of divine hope in the Qur'an.
* **Occasion of Revelation (*Sabab al-Nuzul*):**  
  Recorded by Imam al-Bukhari (Hadith 4810) from Abdullah ibn Abbas (رضي الله عنهما): People among the pagans of Makkah who had committed murder extensively and indulged in fornication extensively came to the Messenger of Allah ﷺ and said: *"Indeed, what you say and call to is beautiful, if only you could inform us whether there is an expiation for what we have committed?"* In response, Allah revealed this ayah, opening the door of immediate redemption.

### 2.2 Ma'arif-ul-Qur'an (Mufti Muhammad Shafi)
* **The Psychology of the Returning Sinner:**  
  Mufti Shafi explains that Shaytan employs a two-phase deception against humanity:
  1. *Phase 1 (Before the sin):* Whispers that Allah is forgiving and the sin is trivial, encouraging disobedience.
  2. *Phase 2 (After the sin):* Whispers that the sin is unforgivable, making the believer feel hypocritical and unworthy of reading the Qur'an.
  Verse 39:53 directly shatters Phase 2, ordering the believer: *Do not despair.*

## 3. Supporting Hadiths

1. **Mercy Overcoming Wrath:**  
   *Sahih al-Bukhari* (7553), *Sahih Muslim* (2751) from Abu Hurairah (رضي الله عنه): The Prophet ﷺ said:  
   *"When Allah created the creation, He ordained in His Book which is with Him above the Throne: 'Indeed, My mercy overcomes My wrath.'"*
2. **Hadith Qudsi on the Vastness of Forgiveness:**  
   *Jami' at-Tirmidhi* (3540) from Anas ibn Malik (رضي الله عنه): The Prophet ﷺ said:  
   *Allah the Exalted said: "O son of Adam, as long as you call upon Me and put your hope in Me, I have forgiven you for what was in you, and I do not mind. O son of Adam, if your sins were to reach the clouds of the sky and then you asked forgiveness of Me, I would forgive you. O son of Adam, if you were to come to Me with sins nearly as great as the earth and were then to face Me without ascribing any partner to Me, I would bring you an earthful of forgiveness."*  
   Grading: *Sahih* (Authenticated by At-Tirmidhi, An-Nawawi, and Al-Albani).
