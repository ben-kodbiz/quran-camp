# Academic Research Monograph: Surah 66 (At-Tahrim)

**Classification:** Madani | **Verses:** 12 | **Architecture:** 8 Master Plates, 16 Structural Pillars, 64 Thematic Nodes.

## Structural & Thematic Cartography

### Plate 01: THE DOMESTIC CRISIS & THE OATH OF PROHIBITION
*Pillars 01 & 02: Lima Tuharrimu Ma Ahalla Allah, Seeking Wives' Pleasure, Expiation of Oaths Ordained & The Protector*
- **PILLAR 01: THE DIVINE REPROACH OVER PROHIBITION**: Why Prohibit What Allah Made Lawful?, Seeking Pleasure of Spouses & Ghafoorun Raheem
  - Node 01: **The Gentle Divine Reproach to the Prophet** — Ya ayyuha an-nabiyyu lima tuharrimu ma ahalla Allahu lak: why do you prohibit that which Allah has made lawful for you?
  - Node 02: **The Incident of the Sacred Vow** — The Prophet took an oath to abstain from honey (or his concubine Mariyah) to placate the jealousy of his wives.
  - Node 03: **Seeking the Pleasure of Mortal Spouses** — Tabtaghee mardata azwajik: seeking the contentment of your wives at the expense of lawful divine permissions.
  - Node 04: **The Reservoir of Divine Pardon** — Wallahu Ghafoorun Raheem: and Allah is Oft-Forgiving, Most Merciful, releasing His Messenger from human pressure.
- **PILLAR 02: THE DISSOLUTION OF UNJUST OATHS**: Qad Farada Allahu Tahillata Aymanikum, Allah is Your Mawla & The All-Knowing Wise
  - Node 05: **The Ordained Expiation of Oaths** — Qad farada Allahu lakum tahillata aymanikum: Allah has already ordained for you the legal expiation of your oaths.
  - Node 06: **Nullifying Oaths That Prohibit the Halal** — Teaching that swearing to forbid what God made permissible is invalid and must be broken via Kaffarah.
  - Node 07: **Allah as Supreme Guardian (Mawla)** — Wallahu Mawlakum: and Allah is your ultimate Protector, Guardian, and Master in all life affairs.
  - Node 08: **Omniscience and Wisdom in Legislation** — Wa Huwa al-'Aleemu al-Hakeem: and He is the All-Knowing of human weakness, the All-Wise in social remedies.

### Plate 02: THE DISCLOSED SECRET & THE CELESTIAL ALLIANCE
*Pillars 03 & 04: The Confided Secret Disclosed, Revealing Part & Concealing Part, Who Informed You? & Knower*
- **PILLAR 03: THE BREACH OF DOMESTIC CONFIDENCE**: Asarra an-Nabiyyu ila Ba'di Azwajihi Hadeethan, Disclosed It & Allah Made It Manifest
  - Node 09: **The Confidential Prophetic Disclosure** — When the Prophet confided a private matter in strict confidence to one of his wives (Hafsah).
  - Node 10: **The Disclosure to Her Co-Wife** — Falamma nabba'at bih: but when she breached confidence and informed 'A'ishah of the secret conversation.
  - Node 11: **Divine Revelation Unmasking the Breach** — Wa adhhara-hu Allahu 'alayh: and Allah made the private breach manifest and known to the Messenger.
  - Node 12: **Noble Prophetic Discretion in Reproach** — 'Arrafa ba'dahu wa a'rada 'an ba'd: he made known part of what she disclosed and graciously overlooked part.
- **PILLAR 04: THE OMNISCIENT SOURCE OF PROPHECY**: Man Anba'aka Hadha?, Nabba'aniya al-'Aleemu al-Khabeer & The Piercing Insight
  - Node 13: **The Stunned Question of Hafsah** — When the Prophet questioned her, she gasped in astonishment: 'Who informed you of this private matter?'
  - Node 14: **The Majestic Prophetic Reply** — Qala nabba'aniya al-'Aleemu al-Khabeer: He said: 'I was informed by the All-Knowing, the Fully Acquainted!'
  - Node 15: **Demonstrating Prophetic Authenticity** — Proving to the domestic household that divine revelation monitors the most intimate whispers behind curtains.
  - Node 16: **The Sacred Gravity of Trust** — Elevating the confidentiality of marital discourse into a sacred moral trust witnessed by the Creator.

### Plate 03: THE CALL TO REPENTANCE & THE CELESTIAL GUARDS
*Pillars 05 & 06: In Tatooba ila Allah, Swerving Hearts, Backing Each Other & Allah, Gabriel, Righteous Believers & Angels*
- **PILLAR 05: THE ADMONITION FOR SWERVING HEARTS**: In Tatooba ilallahi fa-qad Saghat Quloobukuma, Wavering Hearts & The Warning
  - Node 17: **The Call to Sincere Heart Repentance** — In tatooba ila Allahi fa-qad saghat quloobukuma: if you two wives repent to Allah, your hearts have indeed swerved.
  - Node 18: **Addressing the Two Mothers of the Believers** — A direct, loving yet piercing admonition addressed to 'A'ishah and Hafsah regarding domestic factionalism.
  - Node 19: **Acknowledging Human Frailty in Great Souls** — Demonstrating that even the most elevated human companions were susceptible to jealousy and required divine correction.
  - Node 20: **The Prerequisite of Heart Alignment** — Teaching that true love for the Prophet demands absolute alignment with his divine mission and peace.
- **PILLAR 06: THE MONUMENTAL CELESTIAL COALITION**: Tadhahara 'Alayhi, Allah His Mawla, Jibreel, Salihu al-Mu'mineen & The Angels
  - Node 21: **The Warning Against Domestic Collusion** — Wa in tadhahara 'alayhi: but if you collaborate and gang up against him to inflict emotional distress...
  - Node 22: **The Supreme Protector: Allah Himself** — Fa-inna Allaha huwa Mawlah: then know that Allah Himself is his Sovereign Protector and Defender!
  - Node 23: **The Archangel and the Righteous Vanguard** — Wa Jibreelu wa salihu al-mu'mineen: and Gabriel and the righteous among the believers stand as his shields.
  - Node 24: **The Angelic Armies in Battle Array** — Wal-mala'ikatu ba'da dhalika dhaheer: and the angels moreover are his resolute, mobilized reinforcements!

### Plate 04: THE THREAT OF DIVORCE & THE SIX ATTRIBUTES
*Pillars 07 & 08: 'Asa Rabbuhu In Tallaqakunna, Replacement with Better Wives, Muslimat, Mu'minat, Qanitat, Ta'ibat*
- **PILLAR 07: THE CONTINGENCY OF REPLACEMENT**: 'Asa Rabbuhu In Tallaqakunna an Yubdilah, Better Wives & The Humbling of Egos
  - Node 25: **The Chilling Prophetic Ultimatum** — 'Asa Rabbuhu in tallaqakunna an yubdilahu azwajan khayran minkunna: perhaps his Lord, if he divorces you, will replace you!
  - Node 26: **Umar's Prophetic Concordance** — Umar bin al-Khattab testified: 'My words coincided with my Lord in three matters, including this exact verse.'
  - Node 27: **Shattering Domestic Indispensability** — Reminding the Mothers of the Believers that their status was a gift of divine honor, not an unassailable right.
  - Node 28: **Total Subordination to the Prophetic Call** — Establishing that no human relationship, no matter how cherished, can hold the divine mission hostage.
- **PILLAR 08: THE SIX CROWNING SPIRITUAL QUALITIES**: Muslimat, Mu'minat, Qanitat, Ta'ibat, 'Abidat, Sa'ihat, Thayyibat & Abkara
  - Node 29: **The First Triad: Islam, Iman, and Qunoot** — Muslimatin (surrendering to God), Mu'minatin (firm in faith), Qanitatin (devoutly obedient in worship).
  - Node 30: **The Second Triad: Repentance, Worship, and Fasting** — Ta'ibatin (perpetually penitent), 'Abidatin (constant in prayer), Sa'ihatin (fasting or emigrating in truth).
  - Node 31: **The Diversity of Matrimonial Backgrounds** — Thayyibatin wa abkara: whether previously married widows or virgins, united by unshakeable moral excellence.
  - Node 32: **The Blueprint for the Ideal Believing Woman** — Providing the timeless Quranic standard for spiritual maturity, modesty, and dedicated devotion.

### Plate 05: PROTECTING THE HOUSEHOLD FROM THE BLAZING FUEL
*Pillars 09 & 10: Qoo Anfusakum wa Ahleekum Naran, Fuel of Men and Stones, Fierce Stern Angels & Unquestioning Obedience*
- **PILLAR 09: THE MANDATE OF HOUSEHOLD SALVATION**: Save Yourselves and Families from Fire, Fuel of Men and Stones & The Great Trust
  - Node 33: **The Universal Paternal and Domestic Command** — Ya ayyuha alladhina amanoo qoo anfusakum wa ahleekum nara: save yourselves and your families from a Fire!
  - Node 34: **The Grotesque Fuel of the Inferno** — Wa qooduha an-nasu wal-hijarah: whose combustion fuel is human beings and sulfurous pagan stones!
  - Node 35: **Ali's Commentary on Household Protection** — Ali ibn Abi Talib explained: 'Save them by educating them in divine law and disciplining them in good character.'
  - Node 36: **Accountability Beyond the Self** — Demanding that believers take proactive responsibility for the spiritual and moral cultivation of their homes.
- **PILLAR 10: THE IMMACULATE ANARCHY-FREE GUARDS**: 'Alayha Mala'ikatun Ghiladhun Shidad, They Disobey Not Allah & Execute All Commands
  - Node 37: **The Wardens of the Inferno** — 'Alayha mala'ikatun ghiladhun shidad: over it are appointed angels fierce, formidable, and immensely strong.
  - Node 38: **Total Freedom from Defiance** — La ya'soona Allaha ma amarahum: they never disobey Allah in whatever He commands them throughout eternity.
  - Node 39: **Instantaneous Flawless Execution** — Wa yaf'aloona ma yu'maroon: and they execute precisely whatever they are commanded without hesitation.
  - Node 40: **No Bribes or Emotional Pity** — Shattering any fantasy that sinners can negotiate, bribe, or soften the guardians of divine retribution.

### Plate 06: THE REPENTANCE OF SINCERITY (TAWBAH NASOOHAH)
*Pillars 11 & 12: Apologies Refused to Deniers, Tawbatan Nasoohan, Radiant Light on the Bridge & Rabbana Atmim Lana*
- **PILLAR 11: THE REFUSAL OF POSTHUMOUS EXCUSES**: O Disbelievers Make No Excuses Today, Innama Tujzawna Ma Kuntum Ta'maloon
  - Node 41: **The Rejection of Deathbed Excuses** — Ya ayyuha alladhina kafaroo la ta'tadhiloo al-yawm: O you who disbelieve, make no excuses this Day!
  - Node 42: **Pure Reflection of Earthly Deeds** — Innama tujzawna ma kuntum ta'maloon: you are only being recompensed for the exact reality of what you did.
  - Node 43: **The Expiration of the Theatre of Trial** — Judgment Day is the realm of reckoning and consequence; the gate for apologetic rationalization is sealed.
  - Node 44: **The Urgency of Worldly Penitence** — Warning humanity to execute sincere repentance now, before the arrival of the day of zero excuses.
- **PILLAR 12: THE SUBLIME CHARTER OF TAWBAH NASOOHAH**: Turn to Allah in Sincere Repentance, Expiating Sins & The Radiant Light of Believers
  - Node 45: **The Call to Sincere Pure Repentance** — Ya ayyuha alladhina amanoo tooboo ila Allahi tawbatan nasooha: turn to Allah in sincere, unadulterated repentance!
  - Node 46: **Umar's Definition of Nasooh Repentance** — Repenting from sin with deep remorse and never returning to it, just as milk never returns to the udder.
  - Node 47: **The Day Allah Will Not Disgrace the Prophet** — Yawma la yukhzee Allahu an-nabiyya walladhina amanoo ma'ah: the Day Allah will not disgrace the Prophet and believers.
  - Node 48: **The Supplication for Perfected Light** — Their light streaming before them, praying: 'Rabbana atmim lana noorana waghfir lana innaka 'ala kulli shay'in Qadeer!'

### Plate 07: STRIVING AGAINST FOES & THE TREACHEROUS WIVES
*Pillars 13 & 14: Jahidi al-Kuffara wal-Munafiqeen, Wife of Noah & Wife of Lot, Betrayal of Prophecy & Fire Destined*
- **PILLAR 13: THE RESOLUTE STANCE AGAINST SUBVERSION**: Strive Against Disbelievers and Hypocrites, Be Firm with Them & Hell as Destiny
  - Node 49: **The Sovereign Command of Firmness** — Ya ayyuha an-nabiyyu jahidi al-kuffara wal-munafiqeen: strive against the disbelievers and the hypocrites!
  - Node 50: **Uncompromising Moral Rigor** — Waghludh 'alayhim: and be firm and uncompromising with them in upholding the boundaries of justice.
  - Node 51: **Differentiating Battle from Argumentation** — Confronting external military aggression with arms, and internal hypocritical subversion with piercing argument.
  - Node 52: **The Dreadful Final Abode** — Wa ma'wahum jahannamu wa bi'sa al-maseer: and their ultimate refuge is Hell, and wretched is that destination.
- **PILLAR 14: THE PARABLE OF NOAH'S AND LOT'S WIVES**: Two Righteous Servants, Betrayed Them & Availing Nothing Against Allah
  - Node 53: **The Parable of Betrayed Proximity** — Allah presents a parable for those who disbelieve: the wife of Noah and the wife of Lot.
  - Node 54: **Under the Care of Two Righteous Prophets** — Kanata tahta 'abdayni min 'ibadina salihayni: they were married to two of Our righteous prophetic servants.
  - Node 55: **The Treachery of Faithlessness** — Fa-khanatahuma: but they betrayed them spiritually—Noah's wife mocked his ark, and Lot's wife aided sodomites.
  - Node 56: **Holy Lineage Avails Nothing Against God** — Their prophetic husbands could not protect them in the least against Allah: 'Enter the Fire with those who enter!'

### Plate 08: PARAGONS OF FAITH: ASIYA & MARYAM
*Pillars 15 & 16: Asiya Wife of Pharaoh, Build Me a House in Paradise, Maryam Bint 'Imran & Pure Obedience*
- **PILLAR 15: ASIYA: THE TRANSCENDENT QUEEN**: Wife of Pharaoh, Build for Me Near You a House in Paradise & Save Me from Tyranny
  - Node 57: **The Parable of the Saintly Empress** — And Allah sets forth an example for the believers: the wife of Pharaoh (Asiya bint Muzahim).
  - Node 58: **The Sublime Supplication of Proximity** — When she prayed under torture: 'Rabbi ibni lee 'indaka baytan fee al-jannah: My Lord, build for me near You a house in Paradise!'
  - Node 59: **Deliverance from Tyranny and Evil Deeds** — Wa najjinee min Fir'awna wa 'amalih: and save me from Pharaoh and his corrupt deeds, and deliver me from oppressors.
  - Node 60: **Spiritual Autonomy Amidst Totalitarianism** — Living inside the palace of the greatest tyrant in history, yet preserving pristine monotheism in her heart.
- **PILLAR 16: MARYAM: THE MONUMENT OF PURITY**: Maryam Bint 'Imran, Guarded Her Chastity, We Breathed of Our Spirit & The Qaniteen
  - Node 61: **The Archetype of Immaculate Chastity** — Wa Maryama ibnata 'Imrana allati ahsanat farjaha: and Mary the daughter of 'Imran, who guarded her chastity.
  - Node 62: **The Divine Breathing of the Spirit** — Fa-nafakhna feehi min roohina: so We breathed into her body through Our created angel Gabriel.
  - Node 63: **Validating the Words of Her Lord** — Wa saddaqat bi-kalimati Rabbiha wa kutubih: and she attested to the truth of the words of her Lord and His scriptures.
  - Node 64: **The Crown of Perpetual Devotion** — Wa kanat mina al-qaniteen: and she was of the devoutly obedient, concluding Surah At-Tahrim in celestial radiance.
