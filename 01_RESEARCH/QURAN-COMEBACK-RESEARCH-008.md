## 1. Primary Qur'anic Passages

### 1.1 Surah Ta-Ha (20:2)
* **Arabic Uthmani:**  
  $$\text{مَا أَنزَلْنَا عَلَيْكَ الْقُرْآنَ لِتَشْقَىٰ}$$
* **Standard Translation (The Clear Quran):**  
  *"We have not sent down to you the Quran that you should be distressed."*
* **Tafsir Ibn Kathir:**  
  When the Prophet ﷺ prayed until his feet swelled, the pagans mockingly said: *"This religion has only brought you hardship."* Allah revealed this verse to declare that revelation was sent as mercy, light, and solace, not an oppressive burden leading to burnout or misery.

### 1.2 Surah Al-Muzzammil (73:20)
* **Arabic Uthmani:**  
  $$\text{فَاقْرَءُوا مَا تَيَسَّرَ مِنَ الْقُرْآنِ ۚ عَلِمَ أَن سَيَكُونُ مِنكُم مَّرْضَىٰ ۛ وَآخَرُونَ يَضْرِبُونَ فِي الْأَرْضِ يَبْتَغُونَ مِن فَضْلِ اللَّهِ ۛ وَآخَرُونَ يُقَاتِلُونَ فِي سَبِيلِ اللَّهِ ۖ فَاقْرَءُوا مَا تَيَسَّرَ مِنْهُ}$$
* **Standard Translation (The Clear Quran):**  
  *"...So recite what is easy [for you] of the Quran. He knows that there will be among you the sick and others traveling throughout the land seeking of the bounty of Allah... so recite what is easy from it..."*
* **Key Insight:**  
  Allah acknowledges human vulnerability, sickness, exhausting travel, and the demands of earning a livelihood, twice repeating the divine permission: **Faqra'ū mā tayassara minh**—*Recite whatever is easy and manageable for you.*

## 2. Prophetic Sunnah on Sustainable Habits

### 2.1 The Beloved Small Deed
* **Source:** *Sahih al-Bukhari* (Hadith 6464), *Sahih Muslim* (Hadith 782) from Aisha (رضي الله عنها).  
* **Text:** The Messenger of Allah ﷺ was asked: *"Which deeds are most beloved to Allah?"* He replied:  
  $$\text{أَحَبُّ الأَعْمَالِ إِلَى اللَّهِ أَدْوَمُهَا وَإِنْ قَلَّ}$$  
  *"The most beloved of deeds to Allah are those that are most consistent, even if they are small."*

### 2.2 The Psychology of Spiritual Overload
* **Source:** *Sahih al-Bukhari* (Hadith 39) from Abu Hurayrah (رضي الله عنه).  
* **Text:** The Prophet ﷺ said:  
  *"Indeed, this religion is easy. No one overburdens himself in the religion except that it will overcome him. So seek what is right, come near to perfection, and receive glad tidings..."*
* **Ibn Hajar's Commentary (*Fath al-Bari*):**  
  Spiritual exhaustion occurs when a person attempts an unsustainable burst of worship that causes them to abandon the practice altogether. The winner in the spiritual race is the one who establishes a modest, unbreakable baseline that endures throughout a lifetime.

## 3. The 10-Minute Micro-Return Protocol
* **Minute 1–3 (Preparation & Recitation):** 3 minutes to recite 3 to 5 verses slowly with full presence.  
* **Minute 4–6 (Translation & Core Meaning):** 3 minutes to read the accurate English translation and check the core lesson.  
* **Minute 7–8 (Tadabbur & Journaling):** 2 minutes to write one sentence: *How does this verse apply to my life today?*  
* **Minute 9–10 (Supplication / Return):** 2 minutes of quiet, unhurried dua asking Allah for help to live by the verse.
