## 1. Primary Qur'anic Passage: Surah Al-Asr (103:1–3)

* **Arabic Uthmani:**  
  $$\text{وَالْعَصْرِ (1) إِنَّ الْإِنسَانَ لَفِي خُسْرٍ (2) إِلَّا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا وَعَمِلُوا الصَّالِحَاتِ وَتَوَاصَوْا بِالْحَقِّ وَتَوَاصَوْا بِالصَّبْرِ (3)}$$
* **Standard Translation (The Clear Quran):**  
  *"By time! Indeed, mankind is in loss, except for those who have believed and done righteous deeds and advised each other to truth and advised each other to patience."*
* **Linguistic Architecture:**
  - **الْعَصْرِ (*Al-'Asr*):** Refers to time that is compressed, waning, or drawing toward its end (like late afternoon before sunset). The oath highlights that human life is rapidly running out.
  - **لَفِي خُسْرٍ (*Lafī khusr*):** The preposition *fī* ("in") indicates immersion. Humanity is not merely risking loss; mankind is born submerged inside an ocean of compounding loss unless rescued by the four conditions.

## 2. Classical Sunni Tafsir Analysis

### 2.1 The Statement of Imam ash-Shafi'i (d. 204 AH)
* Ibn Kathir records: Imam ash-Shafi'i famously observed:  
  $$\text{لَوْ تَدَبَّرَ النَّاسُ هَٰذِهِ السُّورَةَ لَكَفَتْهُمْ}$$  
  *"If the people were to reflect deeply upon this single surah alone, it would suffice them."*  
  Because it outlines the entire blueprint of human salvation in three short verses.

### 2.2 Fakhr al-Din ar-Razi & The Parable of the Ice Merchant
* In *Mafatih al-Ghayb*, Imam ar-Razi quotes a scholar who said:  
  *"I did not truly understand the meaning of Surah Al-Asr until I heard an ice vendor shouting in the summer marketplace: 'Have mercy upon a man whose capital is melting away!' (إِرْحَمُوا مَنْ يَذُوبُ رَأْسُ مَالِهِ).  
  I said: This is the reality of the human being. Your capital is your lifespan. With every passing second, your life is melting. If you spend it without investing in faith and righteous deeds, you end in absolute bankruptcy."*

## 3. Prophetic Hadiths on Time and the Companions' Tradition

1. **The Deception of Free Time:**  
   *Sahih al-Bukhari* (Hadith 6412) from Ibn Abbas (رضي الله عنه): The Prophet ﷺ said:  
   *"There are two blessings in which many people are defrauded (lose out): health and free time."*
2. **The Companions' Covenant on Surah Al-Asr:**  
   *Al-Mu'jam al-Awsat* (At-Tabarani, Hadith 5124) from Abu Madinah ad-Darimi (رضي الله عنه):  
   *"Whenever two companions of the Messenger of Allah ﷺ met, they would never part ways until one of them recited to the other Surah Al-Asr in its entirety, and then they would exchange greetings of peace."*  
   *(Graded Sahih by Shaykh al-Albani in As-Silsilah as-Sahihah 2648)*.
