# Primary Research Monograph: Surah As-Saffat (الصَّافَّات)
### Angelic Arrays, Shooting Stars, The Tree of Zaqqum & Ibrahim's Supreme Surrender

---

## 1. Structural Architecture & Placement in Qur'anic Canon

Surah As-Saffat is the 37th chapter of the Qur'an, comprising 182 verses revealed during the middle Meccan period. Situated directly following Surah Ya-Sin, it opens with a triad of cosmic oaths regarding the angelic realms:
1. *Was-Saaffati Saffa* (By those [angels] lined up in rows)
2. *Faz-Zajirati Zajra* (And those who drive the clouds)
3. *Fat-Taliyati Dhikra* (And those who recite the message)

The surah systematically demolishes the theological pretensions of pagan Makkah: the belief that the angels are daughters of Allah, the fabricated kinship with the jinn, and the denial of bodily resurrection. It replaces these false constructs with a magnificent tapestry of prophetic constancy (Nuh, Ibrahim, Ismail, Ishaq, Musa, Harun, Ilyas, Lut, Yunus) and culminates in the divine guarantee: *Wa inna jundana lahumu al-ghaliboon* (Our soldiers will certainly be victorious).

---

## 2. Key Lexical & Thematic Foci

- **As-Saaffat (الصَّافَّات):** The angels standing in uninterrupted ranks before the Divine Throne and executing celestial commands.
- **Shihabun Thaqib (شِهَابٌ ثَاقِبٌ):** A piercing meteoric flame pursuing eavesdropping jinn.
- **Waqifoohum Innahum Mas'ooloon (وَقِفُوهُمْ ۖ إِنَّهُم مَّسْئُولُونَ):** The cosmic arrest halting transgressors before Hell for formal inquest.
- **Shajarat al-Zaqqum (شَجَرَةَ الزَّقُّومِ):** A tree springing from the depths of Hellfire whose fruits resemble the heads of devils.
- **Dhibhin 'Azeem (ذِبْحٍ عَظِيمٍ):** The tremendous horned ram from Paradise ransoming Ismail from slaughter.
- **Al-Yaqteen (الْيَقْطِينِ):** The broad-leafed gourd plant providing shade, insect repellent, and moisture to Prophet Yunus.
- **Subhana Rabbika Rabbil-'Izzah (سُبْحَانَ رَبِّكَ رَبِّ الْعِزَّةِ عَمَّا يَصِفُونَ):** The supreme triadic doxology sealing the surah.
