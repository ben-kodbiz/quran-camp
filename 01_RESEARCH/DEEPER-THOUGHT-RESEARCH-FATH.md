# Primary Research Monograph: Surah Al-Fath (الفتح)
### The Manifest Victory, Treaty of Hudaybiyyah, Bay'at ar-Ridwan, Triple Descent of Sakeenah, Divine Forgiveness & Torah and Gospel Parables

## 1. Structural Architecture & Placement in Qur'anic Canon
Surah Al-Fath is the 48th chapter of the Holy Qur'an, comprising 29 verses revealed in Madani. It establishes essential ontological truths, prophetic integrity, statecraft, and social ethics.

## 2. Key Exegetical Foci
- Classical authorities: Jami' al-Bayan (Al-Tabari), Tafsir al-Qur'an al-'Azim (Ibn Kathir), Al-Jami' li-Ahkam al-Qur'an (Al-Qurtubi), Mafatih al-Ghayb (Al-Razi), and Ma'alim at-Tanzil (Al-Baghawi).
