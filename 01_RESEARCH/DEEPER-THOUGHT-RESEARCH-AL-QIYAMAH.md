# Academic Research Monograph: Surah 75 (Al-Qiyamah)

**Classification:** Makki | **Verses:** 40 | **Architecture:** 8 Master Plates, 16 Structural Pillars, 64 Thematic Nodes.

## Structural & Thematic Cartography

### Plate 01: THE TWIN OATHS & THE RECONSTRUCTED FINGERTIPS
*Pillars 01 & 02: Biyawmi al-Qiyamah, An-Nafsi al-Lawwamah, Assembling Bones & Shaping the Bananah*
- **PILLAR 01: THE OATH OF RESURRECTION & SELF-REPROACH**: La Uqsimu bi-Yawmi al-Qiyamah wa la Uqsimu bin-Nafsi al-Lawwamah
  - Node 01: **The Sovereign Oath by Judgment Day** — I swear by the Day of Resurrection, when all worldly accounts are summoned for cosmic trial.
  - Node 02: **The Oath by the Self-Reproaching Soul** — And I swear by the reproachful conscience (An-Nafsi al-Lawwamah) that constantly audits its own shortcomings.
  - Node 03: **The Internal Moral Courtroom** — The human conscience itself serves as an internal witness that accountability is woven into the fabric of creation.
  - Node 04: **The Cynical Question: Will God Gather Bones?** — Does man presume that We will never assemble his scattered, decomposed, decayed earthly bones?
- **PILLAR 02: RECONSTRUCTING THE VERY FINGERTIPS**: Bala Qadireena 'ala an Nusawwiya Bananah & Desiring Sin
  - Node 05: **Yes Indeed! Able to Reconstruct Fingertips** — Bala qadireena 'ala an nusawwiya bananah: yea, We are fully able to restore even his exact fingertips!
  - Node 06: **The Miracle of Unique Dermatoglyphics** — Reconstructing the microscopic ridges, fingerprints, and bone joints with absolute anatomical precision.
  - Node 07: **Desiring to Continue in Sins Ahead** — Nay, man desires to continue sinning forward in reckless abandon, demanding: 'When is this Day of Resurrection?'
  - Node 08: **The Moral Denial Behind Skepticism** — Skepticism regarding Judgment Day is driven not by intellectual inquiry, but by a desire to sin without guilt.

### Plate 02: THE DAZED EYE & THE ECLIPSED MOON
*Pillars 03 & 04: Bariqa al-Basar, Khasafa al-Qamar, Sun and Moon Conjoined & Where is the Refuge?*
- **PILLAR 03: CELESTIAL ALIGNMENT & DISSOLUTION**: Fa-idha Bariqa al-Basar, Khasafa al-Qamar & Jumi'a ash-Shamsu wal-Qamar
  - Node 09: **When the Human Eye is Dazed with Shock** — Fa-idha bariqa al-basar: when eyesight is blinded and dazzled by the sudden terrifying cosmic light.
  - Node 10: **The Moon Plunged into Eternal Eclipse** — Wa khasafa al-qamar: and the moon loses all its luminescence, darkened and stripped of reflective beauty.
  - Node 11: **Sun and Moon Conjoined Together** — Wa jumi'a ash-shamsu wal-qamar: the sun and moon joined together in gravitational and celestial collision.
  - Node 12: **The Collapse of the Terrestrial Clock** — The two great luminaries that calculated human months and seasons crash in cosmic termination.
- **PILLAR 04: THE DESPERATE PLEA: WHERE TO FLEE?**: Ayna al-Mafar, Kalla la Wazar & Ila Rabbika al-Mustaqarr
  - Node 13: **Man Will Cry: Where is the Escape?** — Yaqoolu al-insanu yawma'idhin ayna al-mafar: on that Day man will cry in panic: 'Where is the refuge?!'
  - Node 14: **Never! There is No Fortress of Refuge** — Kalla la wazar: absolutely never! There is no mountain cave, underground bunker, or fortress to shield you.
  - Node 15: **Unto Your Lord is the Sole Final Rest** — Ila Rabbika yawma'idhin al-mustaqarr: unto your Lord alone on that Day is the final, permanent settlement.
  - Node 16: **Complete Foreclosure of All Human Options** — Standing transparently before the Creator with zero escape routes and zero allies.

### Plate 03: THE FORENSIC AUDIT & THE SPEED OF REVELATION
*Pillars 05 & 06: Informed of First and Last, Man as Witness on Himself & La Tuharrik bihi Lisanaka*
- **PILLAR 05: INFORMED OF ALL DEEDS AND EXCUSES**: Yunabba'u al-Insan, Balil-Insanu 'ala Nafsihi Baseerah & Ma'adheer
  - Node 17: **Informed of What He Sent and Kept Back** — Man will be informed on that Day of what he sent forward in deeds and what he left behind in legacy.
  - Node 18: **Man is a Piercing Witness Over Himself** — Balil-insanu 'ala nafsihi baseerah: nay, man is a keen, unmistakable witness against his own soul.
  - Node 19: **Even Though He Puts Forth Excuses** — Wa law alqa ma'adheerah: even though he presents elaborate philosophical excuses and rationalizations.
  - Node 20: **The Inefficacy of Self-Justification** — The organs and conscious memory testify directly, making self-deception completely impossible.
- **PILLAR 06: DO NOT HASTEN YOUR TONGUE WITH IT**: La Tuharrik bihi Lisanaka li-Ta'jala Bih & Inna 'Alayna Jam'ahu
  - Node 21: **Move Not Your Tongue in Haste** — Do not move your tongue with the Qur'an to rush through it out of anxiety that you might forget.
  - Node 22: **Upon Us is Its Collection and Recitation** — Inna 'alayna jam'ahu wa qur'anah: it is upon Us to assemble it in your breast and cause you to recite it perfectly.
  - Node 23: **Follow Its Recitation When Read** — So when We have recited it through Gabriel, follow its recitation with disciplined, quiet listening.
  - Node 24: **Upon Us is Its Complete Clarification** — Thumma inna 'alayna bayanah: then it is upon Us to explain its deep meanings and legal application.

### Plate 04: LOVING THE FLEETING & THE RADIANT FACES
*Pillars 07 & 08: Loving Al-'Ajilah, Leaving Al-Akhirah, Wujoohun Nadhirah & Gazing at the Lord*
- **PILLAR 07: ADDICTION TO THE FLEETING DUNYA**: Tuhibboona al-'Ajilah wa Tadharoona al-Akhirah
  - Node 25: **Nay! You Love the Immediate Transient World** — Kalla bal tuhibboona al-'ajilah: nay, the root failure is your obsessive love for the fleeting present dunya.
  - Node 26: **And You Abandon the Eternal Hereafter** — Wa tadharoona al-akhirah: while you heedlessly cast behind your backs the everlasting life of the Hereafter.
  - Node 27: **The Pathology of Short-Term Gratification** — Trading an infinite ocean of eternal peace for a few drops of temporary, anxious worldly pleasure.
  - Node 28: **The Great Divide of Human Destinies** — The parting of humanity into two distinct visual archetypes: radiant illuminated faces versus darkened despair.
- **PILLAR 08: RADIANT FACES GAZING AT THEIR LORD**: Wujoohun Yawma'idhin Nadhirah, Ila Rabbiha Nadhirah & The Beatific Vision
  - Node 29: **Faces Radiant with Transcendent Joy** — Wujoohun yawma'idhin nadhirah: faces on that Day will be glowing, blooming, and radiant with divine grace.
  - Node 30: **Gazing Directly at Their Creator** — Ila Rabbiha nadhirah: looking directly upon the majestic Countenance of their Lord without veil.
  - Node 31: **The Zenith of Paradise Delights** — The Beatific Vision: the greatest reward of the believers, surpassing all physical gardens and palaces.
  - Node 32: **Purity Reflecting Divine Glory** — The light of Allah's beauty reflected in the joyful eyes of those who spent their earthly lives in faithful longing.

### Plate 05: DESPAIRING FACES & THE SOUL IN THE COLLARBONES
*Pillars 09 & 10: Wujoohun Basirah, Expecting Spine-Breaking Torment & The Soul at the Taraqee*
- **PILLAR 09: SCOWLING FACES AWAITING THE BACKBREAKER**: Wujoohun Yawma'idhin Basirah, Tazunnu an Yuf'ala biha Faqirah
  - Node 33: **Faces Sullen and Dismal with Horror** — And other faces on that Day will be scowling (Basirah), pitch-black, and contorted with sheer terror.
  - Node 34: **Expecting a Spine-Breaking Calamity** — Tazunnu an yuf'ala biha faqirah: knowing with certainty that a back-breaking catastrophe is about to fall upon them.
  - Node 35: **The Contrast of the Two Countenances** — The external face mirrors the internal soul: purity produces radiant light, while guilt produces crushing darkness.
  - Node 36: **The Finality of the Great Inversion** — The worldly mockers who sneered in arrogance are now frozen in irreversible, trembling disgrace.
- **PILLAR 10: WHEN THE SOUL REACHES THE COLLARBONES**: Kalla Idha Balaghati at-Taraqee, Wa Qeela Man Raq & Sakrat al-Mawt
  - Node 37: **When the Soul Reaches the Collarbones** — Kalla idha balaghati at-taraqee: nay, when the departing soul reaches the clavicle collarbones in the agony of death,
  - Node 38: **And It is Cried: Who Can Cure Him?** — Wa qeela man raq: and family cry out in desperation: 'Is there any physician or healer who can rescue him?'
  - Node 39: **Certainty That It is the Final Departure** — And the dying soul concludes with absolute certainty that this is the final, irrevocable parting from Dunya.
  - Node 40: **Legs Entwined with Legs in Agony** — Wat-taffati as-saqu bis-saq: and the leg is wound with the other leg in the convulsion of the death struggle.

### Plate 06: THE DRIVEN MARCH & THE SHAMELESS STRUT
*Pillars 11 & 12: Ila Rabbika al-Masaq, Neither Believed Nor Prayed & Strutting to Family in Pride*
- **PILLAR 11: THE MARCH TO THE SOVEREIGN LORD**: Ila Rabbika Yawma'idhin al-Masaq & The Irreversible Transit
  - Node 41: **Unto Your Lord is the Driven March** — Ila Rabbika yawma'idhin al-masaq: unto your Lord on that Day is the driving of all souls for judgment.
  - Node 42: **Neither Affirmed Truth Nor Prayed** — Fala saddaqa wa la salla: for in worldly life, he neither believed in the truth nor established prayer.
  - Node 43: **Denied and Turned Away in Defiance** — Wa lakin kadh-dhaba wa tawalla: but rather he actively belied divine revelation and turned away in contempt.
  - Node 44: **The Double Crime of the Arrogant** — Abandoning both ideological truth (Sadaqah) and liturgical submission (Salah).
- **PILLAR 12: STRUTTING HOME IN VANITY & THE NEAR WOE**: Yatamatta, Awla Laka fa-Awla & The Approaching Destruction
  - Node 45: **Strutting to His Kin in Conceit** — Thumma dhahaba ila ahlihi yatamatta: then he swaggered back to his family strutting in haughty self-satisfaction.
  - Node 46: **Woe Unto You, and Woe!** — Awla laka fa-awla: woe to you O arrogant denier, and again woe! Appropriate is destruction unto you!
  - Node 47: **Then Woe Unto You, and Woe!** — Thumma awla laka fa-awla: then woe to you, and again woe! Approaching nearer and nearer is your doom!
  - Node 48: **The Prophetic Confrontation of Abu Jahl** — The historical words spoken by the Prophet ﷺ to Abu Jahl outside the Ka'bah, immortalized in divine scripture.

### Plate 07: THE INQUEST OF SUDA & THE SEMINAL DROP
*Pillars 13 & 14: Ayahsabu al-Insanu an Yutraka Suda, Nutfatan min Maniyy & The Creation of Male and Female*
- **PILLAR 13: DOES MAN THINK HE WILL BE LEFT AIMLESS?**: A-Yahsabu al-Insanu an Yutraka Suda & The Denial of Moral Purpose
  - Node 49: **Does Man Presume He is Left Aimless?** — A-yahsabu al-insanu an yutraka suda: does man think that he will be left neglected, without moral reckoning?
  - Node 50: **The Rejection of Secular Nihilism** — Creation is not an accidental accident; humanity was not endowed with consciousness to dissolve into nothingness.
  - Node 51: **Every Creature Possesses Divine Order** — If the microscopic cell operates with purpose, how could human moral choices be deemed irrelevant?
  - Node 52: **The Inevitability of Moral Accountability** — The very concept of justice demands that the oppressor and oppressed meet before an impartial Judge.
- **PILLAR 14: THE MIRACLE OF SEMINAL FASHIONING**: Alam Yaku Nutfatan, 'Alaqatan, Fasawwa & The Two Genders
  - Node 53: **Was He Not an Emitted Seminal Drop?** — Alam yaku nutfatan min maniyyin yumna: was he not a mere drop of fluid emitted into the womb?
  - Node 54: **Then a Clinging Clot ('Alaqah)** — Then he became a clinging embryo, whereupon Allah fashioned and proportioned his physical form with balance.
  - Node 55: **Fashioning the Dual Genders: Male & Female** — Fa-ja'ala minhu az-zawjayni adh-dhakara wal-untha: and He produced from that microscopic drop the twin pairs: male and female.
  - Node 56: **Is He Not Able to Revive the Dead?** — A-laysa dhalika bi-qadirin 'ala an yuhyiya al-mawta: is not the One who performed that original creation fully able to revive the dead?

### Plate 08: SUNNI EXEGESIS & TADABBUR OF AL-QIYAMAH
*Pillars 15 & 16: Bukhari 7437 on Ru'yatullah, The Reconstructed Bananah & Subhanaka fa-Bala*
- **PILLAR 15: CANONICAL EXEGESIS OF RU'YATULLAH**: Sahih al-Bukhari 7437: Seeing the Lord Like the Full Moon
  - Node 57: **Seeing the Lord as You See the Full Moon** — In Bukhari 7437, the Prophet ﷺ said: 'Indeed, you will see your Lord with your own eyes just as you see this full moon.'
  - Node 58: **Sunni Consensus on the Beatific Vision** — Ahl as-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah affirm that believers will behold Allah in the Hereafter without modal confinement (Bi-la kayf).
  - Node 59: **The Linguistic Analysis of 'Ila Rabbiha Nadhirah'** — Grammarians note that 'Nadhara' followed by the preposition 'Ila' uniquely signifies optical vision with the eyes.
  - Node 60: **The Denial of the Wicked from the Vision** — In contrast, disbelievers are veiled from seeing their Lord ('Innahum 'an Rabbihim yawma'idhin la-mahjooboon').
- **PILLAR 16: LIVING WITH AN-NAFSI AL-LAWWAMAH**: Cultivating the Self-Reproaching Conscience & Subhanaka fa-Bala
  - Node 61: **Nurturing the Conscience (Al-Lawwamah)** — The hallmark of the righteous believer is constantly reproaching himself: 'What did I intend by that word? How can I improve?'
  - Node 62: **The Deathbed Clavicle Reflection** — Pondering the moment the soul reaches the collarbones (At-Taraqee), when all worldly resumes dissolve.
  - Node 63: **The Liturgical Response to 75:40** — Sunnah practice records that when reciting the final verse, one responds: 'Subhanaka fa-bala!' (Glory be to You, yes indeed You are able!).
  - Node 64: **Daily Readiness for the Driven March** — Living every day with consciousness that our final march is 'Ila Rabbika al-masaq', preparing light for our countenances.
