## 1. Primary Qur'anic Passages

### 1.1 Surah Al-Fatihah (1:6–7)
* **Arabic Uthmani:**  
  $$\text{اهْدِنَا الصِّرَاطَ الْمُسْتَقِيمَ (6) صِرَاطَ الَّذِينَ أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ غَيْرِ الْمَغْضُوبِ عَلَيْهِمْ وَلَا الضَّالِّينَ (7)}$$
* **Standard Translation (The Clear Quran):**  
  *"Guide us to the straight path—the path of those You have blessed, not of those who have evoked [Your] anger or of those who are astray."*

### 1.2 Surah Al-An'am (6:153)
* **Arabic Uthmani:**  
  $$\text{وَأَنَّ هَٰذَا صِرَاطِي مُسْتَقِيمًا فَاتَّبِعُوهُ ۖ وَلَا تَتَّبِعُوا السُّبُلَ فَتَفَرَّقَ بِكُمْ عَن سَبِيلِهِ ۚ ذَٰلِكُمْ وَصَّاكُم بِهِ لَعَلَّكُمْ تَتَّقُونَ}$$
* **Standard Translation (The Clear Quran):**  
  *"And, [moreover], this is My path, which is straight, so follow it; and do not follow [other] ways, for you will be separated from His way. This has He instructed you that you may become righteous."*

## 2. Classical Sunni Tafsir & Spiritual Analysis

### 2.1 Why Does a Practicing Muslim Beg for Guidance 17 Times a Day?
* **The Core Inquiry:** If a believer is already standing in prayer, facing the Qiblah, why are they commanded to ask: *"Guide us to the Straight Path"* in every single rak'ah?
* **Ibn Kathir's Explanation:**  
  Guidance is not a permanent trophy received once and kept forever; the human condition is in perpetual need of Allah’s support in every breath. A person needs guidance in what they do not yet know, steadfastness (*thabat*) in what they already know, and protection against future deviation.
* **Ibn al-Qayyim (*Madarij as-Salikin*):**  
  The human need for guidance is far greater than the need for food and water. For every choice faced in life—in speech, business, family, emotion, and thought—there is a Straight Path. Without divine light (*nur*), the ego slips into extremes: either willful negligence (*al-maghdub 'alayhim*) or ignorant drifting (*ad-dallin*).

## 3. The Prophetic Parable of the Straight Path
* **Primary Citation:** *Jami' at-Tirmidhi* (Hadith 2859), *Musnad Ahmad* (Hadith 17233). Graded **Sahih** by At-Tirmidhi and Al-Albani.
* **Narration of An-Nawwas ibn Sam'an (رضي الله عنه):**  
  The Prophet ﷺ said:  
  *"Allah has struck a parable of a straight path. Along both sides of the path are two walls with open doors, over which curtains are hanging. At the entrance of the path is a caller saying: 'O people! Enter the straight path all together and do not veer off!' Above the path is another caller who calls whenever a person attempts to open any of those doors: 'Woe to you! Do not open it, for if you open it you will enter it!'  
  The straight path is Islam; the walls are the boundaries of Allah; the open doors are the things Allah has forbidden; the caller at the entrance is the Book of Allah; and the caller above the path is the admonisher of Allah placed inside the heart of every Muslim."*
