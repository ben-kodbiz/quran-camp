## 1. Primary Qur'anic Passages

### 1.1 Surah Ar-Ra'd (13:11)
* **Arabic Uthmani:**  
  $$\text{إِنَّ اللَّهَ لَا يُغَيِّرُ مَا بِقَوْمٍ حَتَّىٰ يُغَيِّرُوا مَا بِأَنفُسِهِمْ}$$
* **Standard Translation (The Clear Quran):**  
  *"Indeed, Allah will not change the condition of a people until they change what is in themselves."*
* **Tafsir Analysis:**  
  Ibn Kathir notes that external transformation, spiritual peace, and divine assistance are contingent upon internal volition: taking the first step of genuine repentance, turning the heart away from negligence, and taking practical action.

### 1.2 Surah Ibrahim (14:24–25)
* **Arabic Uthmani:**  
  $$\text{أَلَمْ تَرَ كَيْفَ ضَرَبَ اللَّهُ مَثَلًا كَلِمَةً طَيِّبَةً كَشَجَرَةٍ طَيِّبَةٍ أَصْلُهَا ثَابِتٌ وَفَرْعُهَا فِي السَّمَاءِ (24) تُؤْتِي أُكُلَهَا كُلَّ حِينٍ بِإِذْنِ رَبِّهَا}$$
* **Standard Translation (The Clear Quran):**  
  *"Do you not see how Allah presents a parable: A good word is like a good tree, its root firmly fixed and its branches high in the sky? It produces its fruit at all times, by permission of its Lord."*
* **Ibn Abbas Commentary:**  
  The "good word" is *La ilaha illallah* and the revelation of the Qur'an planted in the heart of the believer. Just as a deep-rooted tree survives droughts, storms, and winter winds, a soul rooted in daily contemplation of the Qur'an remains unshakable in the storms of life.

## 2. Prophetic Hadiths on Divine Proximity

1. **Walking to Allah, Allah Running to the Servant:**  
   *Sahih al-Bukhari* (Hadith 7405), *Sahih Muslim* (Hadith 2675):  
   The Prophet ﷺ reported that Allah said in a Hadith Qudsi:  
   *"...And if he draws near to Me a hand’s span, I draw near to him an arm’s length; and if he draws near to Me an arm’s length, I draw near to him a fathom’s length; and if he comes to Me walking, I go to him running."*
2. **The Crown of Character:**  
   *Sahih al-Bukhari* (Hadith 5027) from Uthman ibn Affan (رضي الله عنه):  
   The Prophet ﷺ said: *"The best among you are those who learn the Qur'an and teach it."*
