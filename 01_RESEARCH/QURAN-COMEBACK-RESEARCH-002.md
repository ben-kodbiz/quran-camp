## 1. Primary Qur'anic Passage: Surah Al-Baqarah (2:1–5)

### The Primary Verse (2:2)
* **Arabic Uthmani:**  
  $$\text{ذَٰلِكَ الْكِتَابُ لَا رَيْبَ ۛ فِيهِ ۛ هُدًى لِّلْمُتَّقِينَ}$$
* **Standard Translation (The Clear Quran):**  
  *"This is the Book! There is no doubt about it—a guide for those mindful of Allah."*
* **Linguistic Nuances:**
  - **ذَٰلِكَ (*Dhālika*):** Literally "That" (distal demonstrative pronoun). Classical rhetoricians note that using "That Book" instead of "This Book" signifies the immense elevated status, majesty, and sublime perfection of the divine speech.
  - **لَا رَيْبَ فِيهِ (*Lā rayba fīh*):** *Rayb* is not merely doubt, but doubt accompanied by agitation, suspicion, and unease. The Qur'an contains zero internal contradiction, falsity, or deception.
  - **هُدًى (*Hudan*):** Guidance. Mentioned as an absolute verbal noun (*masdar*), meaning the Book is not merely guiding, but is the very embodiment of guidance itself.
  - **لِّلْمُتَّقِينَ (*Lil-Muttaqīn*):** For those who possess *Taqwa*—the conscious effort to place a protective shield between oneself and the displeasure of Allah through obedience and reverence.

## 2. Classical Sunni Tafsir Analysis

### 2.1 Tafsir Ibn Kathir
* **Why Guidance for the Muttaqin Specifically?**  
  Ibn Kathir explains: While the Qur'an is guidance for all humanity in general (*hudan lin-nās* per 2:185), it is described here specifically as *hudan lil-muttaqīn* because **only those with humility, awareness, and sincere desire for truth actually benefit from it**. A haughty person reads the Qur'an and increases in arrogance, whereas a humble seeker reads it and finds direction.
* **Definition of Taqwa Recorded by Ibn Kathir:**  
  Umar ibn al-Khattab asked Ubayy ibn Ka'b (رضي الله عنهم) about *Taqwa*. Ubayy asked: *"Have you ever walked along a path full of thorns?"* Umar said: *"Yes."* Ubayy asked: *"What did you do?"* Umar replied: *"I tucked up my garments and tread with utmost care."* Ubayy said: *"That is Taqwa."*

### 2.2 Ma'arif-ul-Qur'an (Mufti Muhammad Shafi)
* **The Fundamental Misconception:**  
  Mufti Shafi points out that many people approach the Qur'an with the wrong expectations:
  1. Expecting it to be a history textbook arranged chronologically.
  2. Expecting it to be a philosophical treatise or technical law book.
  3. Treating it as a decorative amulet to keep on a high shelf for blessing without reading.
* **The Reality:** The Qur'an is a **Roadmap for the Human Journey**. It provides the precise spiritual compass required to navigate daily moral, family, emotional, and social choices.
