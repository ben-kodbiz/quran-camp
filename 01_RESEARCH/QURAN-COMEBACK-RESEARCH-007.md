## 1. Primary Qur'anic Passages

### 1.1 Surah Al-Muzzammil (73:4)
* **Arabic Uthmani:**  
  $$\text{أَوْ زِدْ عَلَيْهِ وَرَتِّلِ الْقُرْآنَ تَرْتِيلًا}$$
* **Standard Translation (The Clear Quran):**  
  *"or a little more; and recite the Quran ˹properly˺ in a measured way."*
* **Linguistic Analysis of *Tartīl*:**  
  From the root *r-t-l*, signifying regularity, neat ordering, and clear spacing. In speech, it refers to enunciating words deliberately, without haste, articulating each letter clearly, and pausing between phrases so that the listener and the speaker can digest the meaning.

### 1.2 Surah Al-Furqan (25:32)
* **Arabic Uthmani:**  
  $$\text{وَقَالَ الَّذِينَ كَفَرُوا لَوْلَا نُزِّلَ عَلَيْهِ الْقُرْآنُ جُمْلَةً وَاحِدَةً ۚ كَذَٰلِكَ لِنُثَبِّتَ بِهِ فُؤَادَكَ ۖ وَرَتَّلْنَاهُ تَرْتِيلًا}$$
* **Standard Translation (The Clear Quran):**  
  *"The disbelievers say, 'If only the Quran had been sent down to him all at once!' ˹We have revealed it˺ in this manner so that We may strengthen your heart with it. And We have revealed it in deliberate stages."*

## 2. Classical Sunni Tafsir & Athar

### 2.1 The Classical Warning Against Speed-Reading
* **The Famous Counsel of Abdullah ibn Mas'ud (رضي الله عنه):**  
  *"Do not scatter the Qur'an like poor dates that fall from a tree, and do not chant it rapidly like poetry. Pause at its wonders, move your hearts with it, and let not the sole concern of any one of you be merely to reach the end of the surah."*  
  *(Recorded by Al-Baghawi in Sharh as-Sunnah, Al-Bayhaqi in Shu'ab al-Iman, Ibn Abi Shaybah in Al-Musannaf)*.
* **The Statement of Abdullah ibn Abbas (رضي الله عنه):**  
  *"To recite a single short surah such as Az-Zalzalah or Al-Qari'ah with slow contemplation (tadabbur) is more beloved to me than reciting Surah Al-Baqarah and Ali 'Imran rapidly without reflection."*

### 2.2 Tafsir Ibn Kathir (Surah 73:4)
* Reciting with *tartīl* allows the meanings to penetrate the intellect and soften the heart.  
* The Prophet ﷺ did not rush through recitations; his recitation was punctuated, verse by verse, pausing after every ayah to allow the cosmic reality of each statement to settle.

## 3. Prophetic Hadiths on Measured Recitation

1. **The Pace of the Messenger of Allah ﷺ:**  
   *Sahih al-Bukhari* (Hadith 5046): Anas ibn Malik was asked about the recitation of the Prophet ﷺ. He answered: *"It was characterized by elongation (maddan). When he recited 'Bismillahir-Rahmanir-Rahim', he prolonged Bismillah, prolonged ar-Rahman, and prolonged ar-Rahim."*
2. **Ascending Through Tartīl:**  
   *Sunan Abi Dawud* (Hadith 1464), *Jami' at-Tirmidhi* (Hadith 2914): The Prophet ﷺ said:  
   *"It will be said to the companion of the Qur'an: 'Recite and ascend; recite with measured rhythm (rattil) as you used to recite with measured rhythm in the world. For indeed your station will be at the last verse you recite.'"*
