## 3. X (Twitter) Thread (6 Tweets)

```text
1/6 What if the answer to that persistent spiritual emptiness isn't another motivational video, but five quiet minutes with the speech of Allah?

2/6 In Surah Muhammad (47:24), Allah asks:
"Do they not then reflect upon the Qur'an? Or are there locks upon their hearts?"
The word used is *tadabbur*—looking deeply into the consequences and meanings of divine speech.

3/6 Ibn Kathir notes that when hearts are consumed by distraction, spiritual locks settle upon them. As a young man once noted before Umar ibn al-Khattab (RA): the locks remain until we ask Allah to unlock them.

4/6 The early believers didn't just study the Qur'an; they wept with it. When Ibn Mas'ud recited Surah An-Nisa to the Prophet ﷺ, the Prophet's eyes overflowed with tears (Sahih al-Bukhari 5050). The Qur'an was sent to soften us, not merely inform us.

5/6 Don't attempt an impossible 2-hour study routine if you've been away for months. Start with 5 intentional minutes tonight.
- Read Surah Al-Baqarah 2:186 ("I am near").
- Pause.
- Sincerity over speed.

6/6 The Qur'an is not meant to sit on a high shelf. 
Come back to it today.
Read. Reflect. Return.
Full episode & free 7-day guided journal: [Link]
```
