### #Twitter
1/10 When the doctor says there is no cure, when the court date looks hopeless, when age and circumstances are completely against you—listen to the quiet whisper of Zakariya. A thread on Surah Maryam (Surah Maryam 19:4, 9, 23–26) and finding peace in turbulent times. 🧵

2/10 Most people try to solve internal spiritual exhaustion with more external consumption. But the heart cannot rest on noise.

3/10 Allah revealed: "[The angel] said, 'Thus [it will be]; your Lord says, 'It is easy for Me, for I created you before, while you were nothing.''" (Surah Maryam 19:4, 9, 23–26)

4/10 Ibn Kathir portrays Zakariya calling upon Allah privately (*Nida'an Khafiyya*) so as not to be accused of senility, yet holding absolute certainty that Allah's capacity transcends biology. When Maryam wished she had died before her trial, Allah comforted her with fresh dates and a running spring.

5/10 Notice how gentle Allah is with our human limitations. He never demands perfection overnight.

6/10 He demands sincerity, direction, and turning back (*Inabah*).

7/10 Practical habit for today: The Silent Dua of Zakariya: Tonight, wake up 10 minutes before Fajr. Ask Allah for the one thing in your life you feel is 'too late' or 'impossible'. Recite verse 19:9: 'Qala Rabbuka huwa 'alayya hayyin' (My Lord says: It is easy for Me).

8/10 When your forehead touches the prayer mat tonight, remember that Allah heard your whisper before you even formed the words.

9/10 Share this with a friend who has been feeling overwhelmed.
