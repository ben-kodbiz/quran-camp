## 1. Instagram Carousel (10 Slides)

### Hook
> *"We don't lack time. We give our best hours to algorithms and our leftover scraps to Allah."*

### Slide Content Breakdown
* **Slide 1 (Cover):**  
  A Qur'an routine for someone who has zero time. (The 10-Minute Return Protocol).

* **Slide 2:**  
  Check your phone's screen time: 2 hours on Instagram, 1 hour on TikTok, 45 minutes on messaging.

* **Slide 3:**  
  We have time. We just lack intentional boundaries. Allah gave you 1,440 minutes today. Can you protect ten for Him?

* **Slide 4:**  
  Surah Ta-Ha (20:2): 'Mā anzalnā 'alaykal-Qur'āna li-tashqā'—'We have not sent down to you the Qur'an that you be distressed.'

* **Slide 5:**  
  And in 73:20: 'Recite what is easy of the Qur'an.' Allah did not demand 2 hours daily; He loves consistent small deeds.

* **Slide 6:**  
  The 10-Minute Protocol:  
• 2 min: Recite Arabic  
• 2 min: Read English Translation

* **Slide 7:**  
  • 3 min: Read authentic Tafsir summary  
• 2 min: Tadabbur reflection ('What does this mean for me?')

* **Slide 8:**  
  • 1 min: Action commitment & closing du'a. Ten minutes total. Zero overwhelm.

* **Slide 9:**  
  The Prophet ﷺ: 'The most beloved deeds to Allah are those that are consistent, even if they are small.'

* **Slide 10 (End Card):**  
  Come Back to the Qur'an. Free 7-Day Guide in Bio. Huurs Studio | Read. Reflect. Return.

## 2. X (Twitter) Long-Form Reflection Thread

1/6 'I want to read Qur'an, but I have zero time between work, family, and commuting.' Sound familiar?

2/6 The average adult spends 3 hours a day on social media. The problem isn't a lack of time; it's a lack of sacred prioritization.

3/6 In Sahih al-Bukhari, the Prophet ﷺ taught: 'The deeds most beloved to Allah are those that are consistent, even if they are small.'

4/6 Don't wait for a mythical 2-hour free block. It will never come. Build an unbreakable 10-minute fortress every morning.

5/6 The 10-Min Blueprint: 2 min Arabic, 2 min translation, 3 min tafsir, 2 min reflection, 1 min practical action.

6/6 Ten minutes of daily light will illuminate the other 23 hours and 50 minutes of your day. Start tomorrow morning.
