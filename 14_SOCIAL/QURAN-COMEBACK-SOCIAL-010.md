## 1. Instagram Carousel (10 Slides)

### Hook
> *"Allah does not change your condition until you change what is inside your own heart."*

### Slide Content Breakdown
* **Slide 1 (Cover):**  
  30 days from now, you could be completely different. (The Parable of the Good Tree).

* **Slide 2:**  
  In Surah Ar-Ra'd (13:11), Allah reveals the eternal law of transformation:

* **Slide 3:**  
  'Indeed, Allah will not change the condition of a people until they change what is in themselves.'

* **Slide 4:**  
  We wait for circumstances to change, for life to become peaceful, before we return to Allah. Allah says: Move your heart first; I will move the world.

* **Slide 5:**  
  In Surah Ibrahim (14:24), Allah gives the metaphor: A heart rooted in the Qur'an is like a Good Tree—its roots firm in the bedrock, its branches reaching into the sky.

* **Slide 6:**  
  When worldly storms hit, shallow weeds are ripped up in seconds. But the Good Tree stands unbreakable, giving fruit in every season.

* **Slide 7:**  
  On Judgment Day, the Prophet ﷺ will lament: 'O my Lord, indeed my people took this Qur'an as something abandoned.' (25:30).

* **Slide 8:**  
  Break the abandonment today. Press the Book of your Lord to your chest and say: 'Ya Allah, I have returned.'

* **Slide 9:**  
  The finish line on earth is simply turning back to Al-Fatihah. The true finish line is when Allah says in Jannah: 'Recite and ascend!'

* **Slide 10 (End Card):**  
  Come Back to the Qur'an. Master 114-Surah Book Available. Huurs Studio | Read. Reflect. Return.

## 2. X (Twitter) Long-Form Reflection Thread

1/6 Thirty days from now, you will either be thirty days older, or you will be a person whose heart has been revived by the Speech of Allah.

2/6 In Surah Ar-Ra'd 13:11, Allah gives the formula: Change your internal priorities, and He will transform your external reality.

3/6 Surah Ibrahim compares the believer to a deep-rooted tree. When your roots drink from the Qur'an daily, no psychological or worldly storm can knock you down.

4/6 Don't be among those the Prophet ﷺ laments on Judgment Day: 'O my Lord, my people took this Qur'an as something abandoned.' (25:30).

5/6 Ending this journey is not the end of your study; it is your beginning. When you reach Surah An-Nas, immediately turn back to Al-Fatihah.

6/6 May Allah make the Qur'an the light of your chest, the banisher of your grief, and your companion in Jannah. Come back to the Qur'an.
