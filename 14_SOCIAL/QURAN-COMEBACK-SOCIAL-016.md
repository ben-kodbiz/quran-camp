### #Twitter
1/10 We complain about the 2 things that didn't go our way today, while ignoring the 10,000 biological miracles that kept us alive since sunrise. A thread on Surah An-Nahl (Surah An-Nahl 16:18, 68–69, 90) and finding peace in turbulent times. 🧵

2/10 Most people try to solve internal spiritual exhaustion with more external consumption. But the heart cannot rest on noise.

3/10 Allah revealed: "And if you should count the favors of Allah, you could not enumerate them. Indeed, Allah is Forgiving and Merciful." (Surah An-Nahl 16:18, 68–69, 90)

4/10 Ibn Kathir notes that Surah An-Nahl is titled 'The Surah of Blessings' because it enumerates the clouds, honey, rain, livestock, and navigation. Verse 90 is considered by Abdullah ibn Mas'ud to be the most comprehensive moral verse in the Qur'an.

5/10 Notice how gentle Allah is with our human limitations. He never demands perfection overnight.

6/10 He demands sincerity, direction, and turning back (*Inabah*).

7/10 Practical habit for today: The Natural Sight Walk: Step outside for 10 minutes without headphones. Look at the leaves, the flight of birds, the cool air entering your lungs, and acknowledge the unseen mercy sustaining you.

8/10 When your forehead touches the prayer mat tonight, remember that Allah heard your whisper before you even formed the words.

9/10 Share this with a friend who has been feeling overwhelmed.
