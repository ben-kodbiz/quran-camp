### #Twitter
1/10 Have you ever felt so spiritually exhausted that just holding on to your faith felt like holding a burning ember? A thread on Surah Hud (Surah Hud 11:112, 114–115) and finding peace in turbulent times. 🧵

2/10 Most people try to solve internal spiritual exhaustion with more external consumption. But the heart cannot rest on noise.

3/10 Allah revealed: "So remain on a right course as you have been commanded, [you] and those who have turned back with you [to Allah], and do not transgress. Indeed, He is Seeing of what you do." (Surah Hud 11:112, 114–115)

4/10 Ibn Kathir records that Ibn Abbas said no ayah in the entire Qur'an was heavier upon the Prophet ﷺ than 11:112. Istiqamah does not mean perfection; verse 114 immediately follows with the divine relief: 'Indeed, good deeds wipe out evil deeds.'

5/10 Notice how gentle Allah is with our human limitations. He never demands perfection overnight.

6/10 He demands sincerity, direction, and turning back (*Inabah*).

7/10 Practical habit for today: The 3-Good-Deeds Anchor: When you feel your faith wavering today, do not punish yourself with paralyzing guilt. Immediately perform three silent micro-good deeds (a hidden charity, two rak'ahs of duha, or removing harm from a path).

8/10 When your forehead touches the prayer mat tonight, remember that Allah heard your whisper before you even formed the words.

9/10 Share this with a friend who has been feeling overwhelmed.
