### #Twitter
1/10 Why do so many Muslims carry a heavy burden of guilt, feeling that religion is an endless list of impossible rules designed to break their spirit? A thread on Surah Ta-Ha (Surah Ta-Ha 20:2–3, 14, 25–28, 44) and finding peace in turbulent times. 🧵

2/10 Most people try to solve internal spiritual exhaustion with more external consumption. But the heart cannot rest on noise.

3/10 Allah revealed: "We did not send down the Qur'an that you be distressed, but only as a reminder for those who fear." (Surah Ta-Ha 20:2–3, 14, 25–28, 44)

4/10 Ibn Kathir explains that when the pagans saw the Prophet standing in long night prayers, they claimed the Qur'an brought distress (*Shaqa'*). Allah rejected this decisively: the Qur'an is peace, clarity, and life for the heart. When sending Musa to the greatest tyrant on earth, Allah commanded gentle speech (*Qawlan Layyina*).

5/10 Notice how gentle Allah is with our human limitations. He never demands perfection overnight.

6/10 He demands sincerity, direction, and turning back (*Inabah*).

7/10 Practical habit for today: The Speech and Chest Expansion Dua: Memorize and recite Musa's prayer (20:25-28: 'Rabbi ishrah lee sadree, wa yassir lee amree') whenever stepping into a difficult meeting, interview, or challenging family conversation.

8/10 When your forehead touches the prayer mat tonight, remember that Allah heard your whisper before you even formed the words.

9/10 Share this with a friend who has been feeling overwhelmed.
