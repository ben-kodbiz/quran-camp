### #Twitter
1/10 Why do the people closest to you sometimes cause the deepest wounds—and why does Allah let righteous people suffer years of injustice? A thread on Surah Yusuf (Surah Yusuf 12:18, 86, 100) and finding peace in turbulent times. 🧵

2/10 Most people try to solve internal spiritual exhaustion with more external consumption. But the heart cannot rest on noise.

3/10 Allah revealed: "He said, 'I only complain of my suffering and my grief to Allah, and I know from Allah that which you do not know.'" (Surah Yusuf 12:18, 86, 100)

4/10 Ibn al-Qayyim notes that Allah's Lutf (subtlety) works through apparent disasters. Had Yusuf not been cast into the well, he would never have reached Egypt; had he not been falsely imprisoned, he would never have interpreted Pharaoh's dream. Every door that slammed shut was guiding him to his throne.

5/10 Notice how gentle Allah is with our human limitations. He never demands perfection overnight.

6/10 He demands sincerity, direction, and turning back (*Inabah*).

7/10 Practical habit for today: The Private Grievance Protocol: Today, whatever betrayal or delay is keeping you awake at night, refuse to complain to human beings on social media or group chats. Turn off your screen, make wudu, sit in the dark, and whisper verse 12:86 to Allah alone.

8/10 When your forehead touches the prayer mat tonight, remember that Allah heard your whisper before you even formed the words.

9/10 Share this with a friend who has been feeling overwhelmed.
