### #Twitter
1/10 When someone mocks your character, spreads rumors about you, or betrays your trust, what happens inside your chest—and what is the Qur'an's exact antidote? A thread on Surah Al-Hijr (Surah Al-Hijr 15:9, 97–99) and finding peace in turbulent times. 🧵

2/10 Most people try to solve internal spiritual exhaustion with more external consumption. But the heart cannot rest on noise.

3/10 Allah revealed: "And We already know that your chest is constrained by what they say. So glorify [Allah] with praise of your Lord and be among those who prostrate." (Surah Al-Hijr 15:9, 97–99)

4/10 Ibn Kathir explains that Allah acknowledges the constriction of the heart (Deeq as-Sadr) caused by insults and cruelty. Allah does not tell the Prophet to retaliate with venom; He prescribes the celestial remedy: Tasbeeh (elevating Allah above human flaws) and Sujood (placing your forehead on the earth in vulnerability).

5/10 Notice how gentle Allah is with our human limitations. He never demands perfection overnight.

6/10 He demands sincerity, direction, and turning back (*Inabah*).

7/10 Practical habit for today: The Sajdah of Relief: If anyone made you feel humiliated or hurt this week, do not fire back an angry text. Make two rak'ahs and make your Sujood 3 minutes long. Lay your forehead on the earth and tell Allah exactly where it hurts.

8/10 When your forehead touches the prayer mat tonight, remember that Allah heard your whisper before you even formed the words.

9/10 Share this with a friend who has been feeling overwhelmed.
