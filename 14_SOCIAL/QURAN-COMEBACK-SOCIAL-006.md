## 1. Instagram Carousel (10 Slides)

### Hook
> *"Why do believers ask for guidance 17 times a day if they are already Muslim?"*

### Slide Content Breakdown
* **Slide 1 (Cover):**  
  The most important du'a you say every day (and why we rush it).

* **Slide 2:**  
  Out of every request in the universe—wealth, health, marriage, relief—Allah chose ONE prayer for you to repeat 17 times daily:

* **Slide 3:**  
  'Ihdināṣ-ṣirāṭ al-mustaqīm'—'Guide us to the Straight Path.'

* **Slide 4:**  
  Why ask for guidance if you already believe? Because guidance is a continuous necessity, not a one-time status.

* **Slide 5:**  
  A car doesn't stay in its lane by steering once; the driver makes continuous micro-adjustments every second to prevent crashing.

* **Slide 6:**  
  You need guidance when choosing a job, when speaking to your spouse, when spending money, and when fighting personal desires.

* **Slide 7:**  
  Hadith Qudsi: When you say 'Ihdinas-sirat al-mustaqim', Allah personally answers: 'This is for My servant, and My servant shall have what he asked for.'

* **Slide 8:**  
  Transform your Salah: Pause for 2 full seconds after every ayah of Al-Fatihah. Feel the Creator answering you.

* **Slide 9:**  
  Take your compass back from social media trends and place it into the hands of Allah.

* **Slide 10 (End Card):**  
  Come Back to the Qur'an. Free 7-Day Guide in Bio. Huurs Studio | Read. Reflect. Return.

## 2. X (Twitter) Long-Form Reflection Thread

1/6 You say it at least 17 times a day. But are you actually praying, or are you reciting from muscle memory?

2/6 In Surah Al-Fatihah, Allah commands us to ask for one thing: 'Ihdinas-sirat al-mustaqim' (Guide us along the Straight Path).

3/6 Ibn Kathir explains: We ask for guidance because we need firmness upon what we know, guidance in what we don't know, and repentance when we stray.

4/6 In a Sahih Hadith Qudsi, Allah says that every time you recite Al-Fatihah, He replies directly to your words verse by verse.

5/6 Next time you pray, stop rushing. Pause after every verse. Wait for the Creator's response.

6/6 Guidance is the only asset that guarantees peace in this life and salvation in the next. Cherish Al-Fatihah.
