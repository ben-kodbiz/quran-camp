## 1. Instagram Carousel (10 Slides)

### Hook
> *"Your capital is melting away in the midday sun. Are you buying eternity or bankruptcy?"*

### Slide Content Breakdown
* **Slide 1 (Cover):**  
  The Surah that explains why humanity is losing. (The 3 verses of Surah Al-'Asr).

* **Slide 2:**  
  An early scholar said: 'I finally understood Surah Al-'Asr from an ice vendor in the marketplace shouting: Have mercy on a man whose capital is melting away!'

* **Slide 3:**  
  Your lifespan is that block of ice. Every second you spend scrolling, arguing, or heedless—a piece of your capital is gone forever.

* **Slide 4:**  
  Surah Al-'Asr (103:1–2): 'Wal-'Aṣr. Innal-insāna lafī khusr.'—'By time, indeed mankind is in total loss.'

* **Slide 5:**  
  Default human status is bankruptcy. Only FOUR pillars rescue you from ruin:

* **Slide 6:**  
  1. Authentic Faith (Iman)  
2. Righteous Deeds ('Amal Salih)  
3. Mutual Counsel to Truth (Tawasi bil-Haqq)

* **Slide 7:**  
  4. Mutual Counsel to Patience (Tawasi bis-Sabr). Notice: You cannot save yourself in isolation!

* **Slide 8:**  
  Islam rejects individualistic faith. You need a righteous community that pulls you up when you slip.

* **Slide 9:**  
  Forgotten Sunnah: The Companions would never part ways until one of them recited Surah Al-'Asr to the other.

* **Slide 10 (End Card):**  
  Come Back to the Qur'an. Free 7-Day Guide in Bio. Huurs Studio | Read. Reflect. Return.

## 2. X (Twitter) Long-Form Reflection Thread

1/6 Imam Ash-Shafi'i famously remarked: 'If people were to reflect upon Surah Al-'Asr alone, it would suffice them.'

2/6 Why? Because in three short verses, Allah delivers the ultimate audit of human existence: Every human being is bankrupt by default.

3/6 Time is the only currency you cannot replenish. Money lost can be earned back; a wasted hour is dead forever.

4/6 The 4 conditions of salvation: Faith, Righteous Works, Counseling to Truth, and Counseling to Patience.

5/6 Notice that you must advise OTHERS to truth and patience. Faith is communal. If you isolate yourself, the wolf eats the stray sheep.

6/6 Share Surah Al-'Asr with a friend today. Remind each other of the finish line.
