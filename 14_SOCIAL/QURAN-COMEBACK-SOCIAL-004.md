## 1. Instagram Carousel (10 Slides)

### Hook
> *"Even when you ran away from Him, He never stopped calling you 'My servant.'"*

### Slide Content Breakdown
* **Slide 1 (Cover):**  
  What if you've been away from Allah for years? (Read this before you give up).

* **Slide 2:**  
  Shaytan's greatest lie is telling the sinner: 'You've messed up too many times. You are a hypocrite. Fix your life first, then pray.'

* **Slide 3:**  
  In Surah Az-Zumar (39:53), Allah directly destroys this trap: 'Qul yā 'ibādiya alladhīna asrafū 'alā anfusihim lā taqnaṭū min raḥmatillāh...'

* **Slide 4:**  
  'Say: O My servants who have transgressed against themselves, do not despair of the mercy of Allah. Indeed, Allah forgives all sins.'

* **Slide 5:**  
  Notice: He addresses those who 'exceeded all bounds' in sin. And what does He call them? 'Yā 'ibādī'—'O My servants.'

* **Slide 6:**  
  His ownership of your soul was not severed by your mistakes. You are still His. He still invites you home.

* **Slide 7:**  
  The Prophet ﷺ said: 'Allah is more joyful with the repentance of His servant than one of you who finds his lost camel in the barren desert.'

* **Slide 8:**  
  The Qur'an is a hospital for the sick, not a trophy room for the perfect. Bring your broken pieces to Him.

* **Slide 9:**  
  Tonight, wipe the slate clean: Make wudu, pray two quiet rak'ahs of Tawbah, and leave the past behind.

* **Slide 10 (End Card):**  
  Come Back to the Qur'an. Free 7-Day Guide in Bio. Huurs Studio | Read. Reflect. Return.

## 2. X (Twitter) Long-Form Reflection Thread

1/6 The most dangerous trick of the devil isn't tempting you to sin. It's making you believe you are too dirty to be forgiven after you sin.

2/6 Look at Surah Az-Zumar 39:53. Allah addresses those who 'asrafu'—committed excesses, crossed boundaries, lived in rebellion.

3/6 But He still calls them: 'Yā 'ibādī' (O My servants!). He doesn't say 'O criminals.' He preserves your dignity even while inviting you to repent.

4/6 'Inna Allāha yaghfiru adh-dhunūba jamī'ā'—Indeed Allah forgives ALL sins. Jamī'an means with zero exceptions, as long as you return with sincere regret.

5/6 Don't wait until you become 'religious' before opening the Qur'an. You open the Qur'an precisely because you need to be healed.

6/6 Make wudu tonight. Pray two rak'ahs of repentance. Allah will wipe your slate cleaner than newborn snow. Return home.
