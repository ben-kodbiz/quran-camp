*"And your Lord has decreed that you not worship except Him, and to parents, good treatment. Whether one or both of them reach old age with you, say not to them [so much as], 'uff,' and do not repel them but speak to them a noble word."*

### #Twitter
1/10 Why is it that we can speak with infinite patience to colleagues and strangers, yet unleash our shortest tempers on the aging parents who raised us? A thread on Surah Al-Isra (Surah Al-Isra 17:23–24, 79, 82) and finding peace in turbulent times. 🧵

2/10 Most people try to solve internal spiritual exhaustion with more external consumption. But the heart cannot rest on noise.

3/10 Allah revealed: "And your Lord has decreed that you not worship except Him, and to parents, good treatment. Whether one or both of them reach old age with you, say not to them [so much as], 'uff,' and do not repel them but speak to them a noble word." (Surah Al-Isra 17:23–24, 79, 82)

4/10 Ibn Kathir highlights that Allah paired His singular worship with devotion to parents. The prohibition of saying 'Uff' outlaws even an audible sigh of exasperation when caring for frail parents in their old age.

5/10 Notice how gentle Allah is with our human limitations. He never demands perfection overnight.

6/10 He demands sincerity, direction, and turning back (*Inabah*).

7/10 Practical habit for today: The Lowered Wing Call: If your parents are alive, call them today with no agenda other than to ask how their day was, speaking with the gentle cadence commanded in 17:24. If they have passed, make a sincere du'a for their forgiveness.

8/10 When your forehead touches the prayer mat tonight, remember that Allah heard your whisper before you even formed the words.

9/10 Share this with a friend who has been feeling overwhelmed.
