## 1. Instagram Carousel (10 Slides)

### Hook
> *"He didn't say 'Tell them I am near.' He skipped every intermediary and spoke directly to you."*

### Slide Content Breakdown
* **Slide 1 (Cover):**  
  Allah is closer than you think. (The linguistic secret of 2:186).

* **Slide 2:**  
  In the Qur'an, whenever people asked the Prophet ﷺ about religious rulings, Allah commanded: 'Qul'—'Say to them...'

* **Slide 3:**  
  They asked about the crescent moons: 'Say, they are measurements of time.' They asked about charity: 'Say, spend what is beyond your needs.'

* **Slide 4:**  
  Except in ONE verse: In Surah Al-Baqarah (2:186), when they asked: 'Is our Lord near so we may whisper, or far so we must shout?'

* **Slide 5:**  
  Allah did NOT say 'Tell them I am near.' He removed even the Prophet from between them! He spoke directly: 'Fa-innī qarīb'—'Indeed, I am near.'

* **Slide 6:**  
  There is no waiting room. No secretary. No elite access pass. Between your aching chest and the Sovereign of the cosmos is only your whispered voice.

* **Slide 7:**  
  Ma'arif-ul-Qur'an notes: This verse sits right in the middle of Ramadan verses! When your body is hungry and weak, your heart is closest to the divine response.

* **Slide 8:**  
  The Prophet ﷺ said: 'Allah is shy and generous. When His servant raises his hands to Him, He is shy to return them empty.'

* **Slide 9:**  
  Tonight, talk to Him in your own mother tongue. No rehearsed lines. Just tell Him what is breaking your heart.

* **Slide 10 (End Card):**  
  Come Back to the Qur'an. Free 7-Day Guide in Bio. Huurs Studio | Read. Reflect. Return.

## 2. X (Twitter) Long-Form Reflection Thread

1/6 There is a linguistic miracle in Surah Al-Baqarah 186 that will change how you make du'a forever.

2/6 Throughout the Qur'an, when Companions asked questions, Allah replied: 'Qul' (Say to them). When asked about alcohol, orphans, or charity—always 'Qul'.

3/6 But when they asked about Allah Himself in 2:186, Allah drops the word 'Qul'. He does not say 'Tell them I am near.' He speaks directly: 'Fa-inni qareeb'—'Indeed, I am near.'

4/6 He eliminated all intermediaries. He didn't want even a split-second delay between your plea and His proximity. The King of the heavens is closer to you than your jugular vein.

5/6 We send long WhatsApp paragraphs to human friends who leave us on read. Meanwhile, the Lord of the Universe says: 'Call Me, I will personally respond.'

6/6 Tonight, close your door. Turn off the lights. Raise your hands and talk to Allah in your own words. He is waiting.
