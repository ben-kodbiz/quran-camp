### #Twitter
1/10 What if the reason you feel constant anxiety about losing your career, health, or loved ones is because you haven't locked them with the divine key of Shukr? A thread on Surah Ibrahim (Surah Ibrahim 14:7, 24–25) and finding peace in turbulent times. 🧵

2/10 Most people try to solve internal spiritual exhaustion with more external consumption. But the heart cannot rest on noise.

3/10 Allah revealed: "And [remember] when your Lord proclaimed, 'If you are grateful, I will surely increase you [in favor]; but if you show ingratitude, indeed, My punishment is severe.'" (Surah Ibrahim 14:7, 24–25)

4/10 Ibn Kathir notes that Shukr is both the preserver of existing bounties and the summoner of unseen additions. Verses 24-25 demonstrate that a sound statement of faith rooted in gratitude produces spiritual nourishment throughout all seasons of life.

5/10 Notice how gentle Allah is with our human limitations. He never demands perfection overnight.

6/10 He demands sincerity, direction, and turning back (*Inabah*).

7/10 Practical habit for today: The Uncounted Blessing Audit: Write down 5 things you currently enjoy that you never prayed for (e.g. functioning kidneys, peaceful oxygen, parents' sacrifices). Say Alhamdulillah for each with deliberate contemplation.

8/10 When your forehead touches the prayer mat tonight, remember that Allah heard your whisper before you even formed the words.

9/10 Share this with a friend who has been feeling overwhelmed.
