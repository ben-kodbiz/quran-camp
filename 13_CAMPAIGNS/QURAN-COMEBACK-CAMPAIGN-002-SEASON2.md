## 1. Season 2 Executive Directive

Season 1 established the personal habit of returning to the Qur'an. **Season 2 (Episodes 11–20)** moves the believer into the grand narratives of the Prophets and existential reflections spanning **Surah Hud through Surah Ta-Ha**.

Here, believers discover how Allah's subtle grace (*Lutf*) operates behind prolonged personal trials, family estrangement, slander, burnout, and overwhelming burdens.
