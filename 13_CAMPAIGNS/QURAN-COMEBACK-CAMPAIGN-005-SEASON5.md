## 1. Season 5 Executive Architecture

Season 5 deepens into intellectual maturity and personal sanctuary:
- **Episode 31 (Surah Luqman):** The mustard seed inside the rock, boundless words of Allah beyond all oceans, prayer and patience, and walking with humble moderation.
- **Episode 32 (Surah As-Sajdah):** The miraculous origin of man, forsaking beds in the quiet of the night, tears of prostration, and unseen joy stored for the faithful.
- **Episode 33 (Surah Al-Ahzab):** The gale of Khandaq, the sublime prophetic model (*Uswah Hasanah*), abundant remembrance at dawn and dusk, and the cosmic Amanah.
- **Episode 34 (Surah Saba):** Dawud echoing praise with mountains and birds, the fertile twin gardens of Ma'rib, the consequences of ingratitude, and divine sustenance.
- **Episode 35 (Surah Fatir):** The celestial wings of angels, winds navigating clouds to parched lands, the two distinct seas, and true reverent awe (*Khashyah*) born of knowledge.

Every episode strictly implements:
1. **Section 40D Zero-Cost Curation:** 100% curated from verified local 720p HD nature clips (`10_VIDEO/renders/`).
2. **Deep Semantic Ayah Mapping:** Matching the exact natural phenomena, creatures, and spiritual settings described in each Surah.
3. **Strict Mathematical Spacing:** 0 intra-episode duplicates; minimum 3-episode reuse spacing.
4. **Local RTX 3060 F5-TTS Voiceover:** Mastered to EBU R128 (-14 LUFS, 48kHz, Zero Music Policy).
