## 1. Season 3 Executive Architecture

Season 1 anchored the daily habit of opening the Book. Season 2 illuminated prophetic endurance through personal heartbreak and patient perseverance. **Season 3 (Episodes 21–25)** scales outward to the grand cosmological matrix, the divine deliverance of Yunus from the abyss, the quivering of dead earth under rain, the inner state of humble believers, the cosmic light verse of Surah An-Nur, and the criteria separating truth from illusion.

Every episode strictly implements:
1. **Surah Scene Mapping (§40B):** Examining the precise ayah and spiritual reality conveyed by each Surah, utilizing Tier 1 literal scene phenomena when describable, and Tier 2 generic contemplative nature when abstract.
2. **720p HD Hard Ceiling:** Preserving account credits and eliminating upscale charges by enforcing native 720p generation.
3. **1-Click Google Flow Delivery (§40C):** Centralized interactive prompt dispatch via `10_VIDEO/google_flow_master_console.html`.
4. **Broadcast Audio Mastering:** Local RTX 3060 F5-TTS zero-shot voice cloning matched to the Huurs narrator profile, loudness-normalized to -14 LUFS with organic nature foley beds.
