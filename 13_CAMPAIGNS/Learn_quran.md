3. EDITORIAL PRINCIPLES
Rule 1 — Qur'an First

The Qur'an is the primary source.
Rule 2 — Tafsir Before Tadabbur

Understand the established meaning before proposing reflection.
Rule 3 — Evidence Before Emotion

An emotionally powerful statement is not automatically a valid Islamic statement.
Rule 4 — No Invented Meaning

Never write:

    "Allah is telling you that..."

unless the statement is actually supported by the source material and context.

Prefer:

    "This ayah teaches..."

or:

    "The classical tafsir explains..."

or:

    "One practical reflection we can take is..."

Rule 5 — Label Uncertainty

If scholars differ:

    Scholarly note: There are multiple interpretations of this passage. The campaign uses X interpretation because...

Do not hide legitimate scholarly disagreement.
Rule 6 — Hadith Authentication

Do not use a hadith merely because an AI model finds it.

Every hadith used in published content must have:

    collection;

    book/chapter where practical;

    hadith number where stable;

    grading/source;

    wording checked against a reliable source.

Prefer Sahih al-Bukhari and Sahih Muslim where applicable, followed by other reliable collections with authentication.
Rule 7 — No AI Religious Authority

AI is a research/editorial assistant.

AI is NOT:

    a mufti;

    a mufassir;

    a hadith grader;

    an authority that resolves aqidah disputes.
