## 1. Season 4 Executive Architecture

Season 1 anchored opening the Book. Season 2 illuminated prophetic endurance. Season 3 scaled to cosmic signs and deliverance.  
**Season 4 (Episodes 26–30)** journeys deep into the prophetic heart:
- **Episode 26 (Surah Ash-Shu'ara):** Musa standing firm at the Red Sea, Ibrahim's intimate litany of healing, and the only lasting currency—the pure, tranquil Sound Heart (*Qalb Salim*).
- **Episode 27 (Surah An-Naml):** Sulayman smiling at the ant's warning, the speech of birds, and mountains moving like clouds.
- **Episode 28 (Surah Al-Qasas):** The mother of Musa placing her infant into the Nile, Moses at the well of Madyan, and trusting divine promises in the dark.
- **Episode 29 (Surah Al-Ankabut):** The fragility of the spider's house, the purification of prayer, and Allah guiding those who strive.
- **Episode 30 (Surah Ar-Rum):** The revival of dead earth, signs in the heavens, and love and mercy placed between kindred souls.

Every episode strictly implements:
1. **Section 40D Zero-Cost Curation:** 100% curated from verified local 720p HD nature clips (`10_VIDEO/renders/`).
2. **Deep Semantic Ayah Mapping:** Matching the exact natural phenomena, creatures, and spiritual settings described in each Surah.
3. **Strict Mathematical Spacing:** 0 intra-episode duplicates; minimum 3-episode reuse spacing (averaging 9.3 episodes).
4. **Local RTX 3060 F5-TTS Voiceover:** Mastered to EBU R128 (-14 LUFS, 48kHz, Zero Music Policy).
