## 1. Visual & Theological Context Analysis
* **What Surah Luqman Is Conveying:**
  1. The mustard seed in the mountain rock: «fatakun fī ṣakhratin... ya'ti bihā Allāh» (31:16).
  2. The inexhaustible words of Allah beyond all trees and seas: «mā nafidat kalimātu Allāh» (31:27).
  3. The cosmic weaving of night into day: «yūliju al-layla fī an-nahāri» (31:29).
  4. The trio of spiritual poise: prayer, enjoining good, and patience in trial: «wa-ṣbir 'alā mā aṣābak» (31:17).
  5. Walking the earth with humble moderation: «Waqṣid fī mashyika waghḍuḍ min ṣawtik» (31:19).
