## 1. Visual Overview & Motifs
* **Surah:** An-Nur (24:35: *Ayat an-Nur* — The Parable of Light; 24:40: The Ocean of Darkness; 24:41: Birds in Flight)
* **Core Emotional Tone:** Illumination piercing Blindness; Sacred Clarity; Transcendence.
* **Primary Semantic Motifs:** Abyssal Ocean Waves, Stone Niche (*Mishkat*), Glass Lantern (*Zujajah*), Geometric Sunbeams, White Birds Gliding.

* **Shot Type:** Deep Oceanic Void / Layered Darkness
* **Visual Description:** Midnight deep sea in heavy storm. Massive black waves crash over secondary deep swells (*Mawjun min fawqihi mawj*), under pitch-black thunderclouds. Total darkness where even lifting one’s hand would yield no sight. Represents existence devoid of divine guidance.
* **Camera Movement:** Unsettled camera bobbing in heavy dark oceanic swells, sinking slowly downward.
* **AI Video Generation Prompt (720p Ceiling):**

### Scene 2 (00:45–02:00) — The Divine Ayah: The Niche & The Crystal Lamp
* **Shot Type:** Macro Architectural Alcove / Radiant Luminous Warmth
* **Visual Description:** Sudden transition into a quiet, sacred stone interior. In an ancient curved limestone niche (*Mishkat*), rests a hand-blown clear glass lantern (*Zujajah*). Inside, an oil wick glows with an intense, steady golden radiance like a brilliant celestial star (*Ka'annaha kawkabun durriyy*), casting a soft warm aura on the stone.
* **Camera Movement:** Slow, reverent push-in toward the glowing glass lantern (0.1 m/s).
* **AI Video Generation Prompt (720p Ceiling):**  
  `Cinematic macro shot of an ancient glass oil lantern glowing inside a carved stone wall niche, warm golden amber flame, realistic glass refractions, peaceful sacred interior, 720p master, Rembrandt lighting, quiet reverent beauty --ar 16:9 --no hands, artificial neon glow, fantasy sparkles`

### Scene 3 (02:00–04:00) — Classical Tafsir Insight: Geometric Sunbeams
* **Shot Type:** Volumetric Atmospheric Light / Stone Sanctuary
* **Visual Description:** Morning sunbeams streaming down at a sharp angle through a high carved stone lattice window (*Mashrabiya*). The dust particles suspended in the air drift peacefully through the light columns, projecting sharp geometric tessellations onto a cool, polished white marble floor.
* **Camera Movement:** Slow upward tilt from the geometric shadow patterns on the floor toward the window.
* **AI Video Generation Prompt (720p Ceiling):**  
  `Cinematic medium shot of volumetric sunbeams streaming through an ancient Islamic stone lattice window, dust motes floating in light beams, casting patterns on cool marble floor, 720p resolution, serene documentary atmosphere --ar 16:9 --no people, modern items, distortion`

### Scene 4 (04:00–05:30) — The Tadabbur Mirror: The Birds of Praise
* **Shot Type:** Slow-Motion Aerial Telephoto / Wings in Sky
* **Visual Description:** A flock of pure white doves gliding gracefully through the open morning sky above an ancient stone courtyard, their wings outstretched and catching the golden rim of the sun (*At-tayru saffatin, kullun qad 'alima salatahu wa tasbeehahu*). Total harmony between flight, wind, and worship.
* **Camera Movement:** Fluid pan following the flock's curved flight trajectory against a pristine cobalt sky.
* **AI Video Generation Prompt (720p Ceiling):**

* **Shot Type:** Luminous Morning Transition / Sacred Outro
* **Visual Description:** The flame inside the glass lantern continues to burn quietly, but full dawn light now floods the room, uniting the lamp's light with the morning sky (*Noorun 'ala noor*). Darkness has vanished completely. The stone room radiates profound tranquility and warmth.
