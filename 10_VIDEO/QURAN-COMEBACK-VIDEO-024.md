## 1. Visual & Theological Context Analysis
* **What Surah An-Nur Is Conveying:**
  1. The cosmic reality: Allah is the Light of the heavens and the earth (24:35).
  2. The psychological delusion of disbelief: deeds like a mirage in a barren desert where a thirsty traveler expects water (24:39).
  3. The claustrophobic darkness of misguidance: layered ocean darkness with waves piled upon waves (24:40).
  4. The universal praise of nature: birds soaring with wings outspread, knowing their prayer and praise (24:41).
* **Audio Track Source:** Authentic recitation of Surah An-Nur (Verses 35, 39–41) by a classical Murattal Qari + English spoken tadabbur + ocean waves and birdsong foley.
* **Cost Protection:** Strictly locked to `1280x720` resolution.

## 2. Shot-by-Shot Scene Blueprint (720p HD Standard)

### Scene 1 (00:00–00:45) — The Hook: The Ocean of Layered Darkness
* **Classification:** `TIER 1 (Describable Physical Phenomenon in Surah)`
* **What the Surah Conveys:** *«Aw ka-zulumatin fee bahrin lujjiyyin yaghshahu mawjun min fawqihi mawjun min fawqihi sahab»* — Deep sea darkness with waves upon waves under heavy clouds (24:40).
* **Visual Description:** Midnight storm over the deep open ocean. Towering dark waves crashing over secondary subsurface swells under black thunderclouds. Total lack of light where no horizon is visible. Cold, oppressive, photorealistic documentary realism.
* **Camera Movement:** Unsteady oceanic drift bobbing in heavy dark water swells, sinking slowly downward.

### Scene 2 (00:45–02:00) — The Mirage of the Thirsty Soul
* **Classification:** `TIER 1 (Describable Desert Phenomenon in Surah)`
* **What the Surah Conveys:** *«Ka-sarabin bi-qee'atin yahsabuhu az-zam'anu ma'an»* — Like a shimmering mirage in a low plain which the thirsty person thinks is water (24:39).
* **Visual Description:** Ground-level telephoto shot across vast, shimmering white salt flats in scorching midday sun. Heat waves ripple across the horizon, creating a convincing optical illusion of a vast blue lake of water. As camera slowly pushes forward, the water evaporates into dry cracked salt crystals.
* **Camera Movement:** Slow 135mm telephoto push-in revealing optical illusion dissipating.
* **AI Generation Prompt (720p HD Ceiling):**

* **Classification:** `TIER 1 (Describable Sacred Parable in Surah)`

* **Visual Description:** An ancient hand-carved stone alcove (*Mishkat*) in a quiet sanctuary. Inside sits a clear, hand-blown glass lantern (*Zujajah*) holding a pure oil wick burning with a steady, luminous amber flame. The flame casts warm, intricate glass caustics upon the limestone walls.
* **Camera Movement:** Slow reverent push-in toward the glass lantern (0.1 m/s).
