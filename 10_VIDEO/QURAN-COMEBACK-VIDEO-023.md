## 1. Visual Overview & Motifs
* **Surah:** Al-Mu'minun (23:1–2: *«Qad aflaha al-mu'minoon, alladheena hum fee salatihim khashi'oon»*; 23:20: The Olive Tree of Sinai)
* **Core Emotional Tone:** Stillness amidst Turmoil; Internal Tranquility (*Khushu'*); Deep Rooted Faith.
* **Primary Semantic Motifs:** Scattering Autumn Leaves, Ancient Rooted Olive Tree, Virgin Olive Oil Macro, Glassy Forest Pool, Sacred Prayer Mat.

* **Shot Type:** High-Speed Wind Motion / Dark Slate Texture
* **Visual Description:** Fast gust of autumn wind violently whipping dry brown leaves and dust across a dark slate stone pathway. Camera looks downward at the erratic, chaotic motion. Symbolizes the modern distracted mind, full of frantic worry and noise.
* **Camera Movement:** Static downward composition with chaotic organic wind gusts blowing through frame.
* **AI Video Generation Prompt (720p Ceiling):**

### Scene 2 (00:45–02:00) — The Divine Ayah: The Rooted Mountain Olive
* **Shot Type:** Majestic Wide Landscape / Ancient Tree
* **Visual Description:** Sudden transition into profound atmospheric stillness. On a high rocky mountain ridge stands a monumental, thousand-year-old olive tree with thick gnarled roots anchored deep into stone fractures (*Shajaratan takhruju min toori seena'*). Its silvery-green canopy is heavy with ripe fruit, motionless against a peaceful blue sky.
* **Camera Movement:** Slow upward pedestal crane shot from rocky roots up to the olive branches.
* **AI Video Generation Prompt (720p Ceiling):**  
  `Wide cinematic shot of an ancient gnarled olive tree rooted into rocky mountain ridge, calm clear sky, warm soft sunlight, deep peaceful stability, 720p master, National Geographic landscape realism --ar 16:9 --no people, glowing magic, saturated colors`

### Scene 3 (02:00–04:00) — Classical Tafsir Insight: The Flow of Pure Oil
* **Shot Type:** Macro Liquid Viscosity / Golden Warmth
* **Visual Description:** Macro shot of cold-pressed, luminous golden-green olive oil slowly pouring from a simple clay spout into a hand-carved stone dish (*Tanbutu bid-duhni wa sibghin lil-akileen*). The oil flows with thick, unbroken grace, catching soft natural daylight. Represents pure spiritual nourishment.
* **Camera Movement:** 85mm macro lens, slow lateral slider movement.
* **AI Video Generation Prompt (720p Ceiling):**  
  `Macro cinematic shot of golden olive oil pouring smoothly into an ancient stone bowl, rich green-gold liquid texture, warm natural window light, 720p resolution, tactile documentary realism, quiet beauty --ar 16:9 --no hands, artificial shine, neon`

### Scene 4 (04:00–05:30) — The Tadabbur Mirror: The Still Waters of Khushu'
* **Shot Type:** Extreme Slow-Motion Pool Reflection
* **Visual Description:** A crystal-clear, glassy natural pool in a mountain clearing. The water surface is so utterly still it acts as a perfect mirror of the sky and pines above. A single small olive leaf detaches and drops onto the water, resting on the surface tension without creating a splash—pure quietude (*Khashi'oon*).
* **Camera Movement:** Low-angle locked shot right at the water boundary at 120fps.
* **AI Video Generation Prompt (720p Ceiling):**  
  `Slow-motion macro shot of a single green olive leaf landing gently on the calm glass-like surface of clear spring water, subtle micro-ripples, mirror reflection of trees and sky, 720p documentary, serene meditative stillness --ar 16:9 --no people, distortion, text`

* **Shot Type:** Quiet Stone Porch & Sacred Horizon
* **Visual Description:** A simple woven palm-leaf prayer mat lies neatly rolled out upon cool flagstones of an open veranda facing east. The soft orange and gold light of the morning sun spills across the floorboards. The world is awake, quiet, and in prayer.
* **Camera Movement:** Slow push-in over the prayer mat toward the glowing morning light outside.
