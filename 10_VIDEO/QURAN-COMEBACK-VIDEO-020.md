## 2. Shot-by-Shot Curated Clip Blueprint (1280 × 720 @ 24fps)

### Scene 1: The Exhaustion of Performance (Section 1)
* **Clip Source:** `ep04_scene01.mp4`
* **Living Creation Motif:** Rain Cascading on Glass Window with Misty Pine Forest
* **Visual Composition:** Intimate macro shot of rain trickling down clear glass with soft misty pine forest in background. Gentle rhythm of falling water, deep contemplative melancholy, emotional surrender.
* **Spoken Alignment:** The modern exhaustion of perpetual performance, religious burnout, and spiritual fatigue.

### Scene 2: The Revelation That Does Not Crush (Section 2)
* **Clip Source:** `ep07_scene02.mp4`
* **Living Creation Motif:** Desert Dunes Under Luminous Full Moon & Starfield
* **Visual Composition:** Vast cinematic night landscape of wind-swept sand dunes bathed in silver moonlight beneath billions of quiet stars. Vastness, coolness, divine mercy.
* **Spoken Alignment:** *Ma anzalna 'alayka al-Qur'ana li-tashqa* ("We have not sent down to you the Qur'an that you be distressed").

### Scene 3: The Request for an Expanded Chest (Section 3)
* **Clip Source:** `ep07_scene04.mp4`
* **Living Creation Motif:** Jewel Hummingbird Hovering at Blossom
* **Visual Composition:** Ultra-slow-motion capture of a hummingbird suspended weightlessly in mid-air beside vibrant mountain blossoms. Effortless equilibrium, breath, lightness.
* **Spoken Alignment:** Musa's supplication: *Rabbi ishrah li sadri* ("My Lord, expand for me my breast, and ease for me my task").

### Scene 4: Gentleness in the Face of Arrogance (Section 4)
* **Clip Source:** `ep08_scene01.mp4`
* **Living Creation Motif:** Regal Peacock with Iridescent Plumage
* **Visual Composition:** Cinematic medium shot of a noble peacock displaying shimmering turquoise and emerald feathers in soft ambient courtyard daylight. Majesty, dignified beauty, restraint.
* **Spoken Alignment:** Even when confronting Pharaoh, Allah commands: *Fa-qula lahu qawlan layyina* ("Speak to him with gentle speech, perhaps he may be reminded").

### Scene 5: The Meeting of Two Seas (Section 5)
* **Clip Source:** `ep06_scene02.mp4`
* **Living Creation Motif:** The Meeting of Two Seas (Majma' al-Bahrayn)
* **Visual Composition:** Dramatic aerial cinematic view of two vast bodies of water meeting with a visible natural barrier. Deep blue oceanic waters meeting turquoise coastal tides without transgressing.
* **Spoken Alignment:** The Season 2 finale anchor: returning to the Qur'an as an unshakeable boundary and sanctuary of peace.
