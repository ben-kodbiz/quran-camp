### *Surah Al-Furqan (25:45–46, 25:53, 25:63) — Production Blueprint*

## 1. Visual & Theological Context Analysis
* **What Surah Al-Furqan Is Conveying:**
  1. The cosmic harmony of light and shade: Allah extending the shadow and making the sun its guide (25:45–46).
  2. The natural physical barrier: the confluence where sweet fresh river water meets salty bitter ocean without mixing (25:53).
  3. The moral portrait of divine servants: *'Ibad ar-Rahman* walking with gentleness, dignity, and saying words of peace to hostility (25:63).
  4. The criterion (*Al-Furqan*) separating truth from empty arrogance.
* **Audio Track Source:** Authentic recitation of Surah Al-Furqan (Verses 45–54, 63) by a classical Murattal Qari + English spoken commentary + river, ocean, and forest wind foley.
* **Cost Protection:** Strictly locked to `1280x720` resolution.

* **Visual Description:** Enormous, violent stormy ocean breakers smashing against jagged coastal sea cliffs at twilight. Exploding white spray in cold air, turbulent and abrasive. Visual metaphor for the exhausting noise of worldly disputes and ego battles.

### Scene 2 (00:45–02:00) — The Divine Ayah: The Two Seas Meeting
* **Classification:** `TIER 1 (Describable Natural Phenomenon in Surah)`
* **What the Surah Conveys:** *«Wa huwa alladhee maraja al-bahrayni hadha 'adhbun furatun wa hadha milhun ujaj, wa ja'ala baynahuma barzakhan wa hijram mahjoora»* — He released the two seas, sweet and salty, placing an unseen barrier between them (25:53).
* **Visual Description:** Spectacular high-altitude aerial drone shot looking straight down at the meeting point where a clear turquoise freshwater river enters the deep navy-blue ocean. A distinct, tranquil color line separates the two waters, neither overtaking the other. Undisturbed natural equilibrium.
* **Camera Movement:** Slow vertical drone descent rotating smoothly over the boundary line.

### Scene 3 (02:00–04:00) — Classical Tafsir: The Elongation of Shadows

* **Visual Description:** Time-lapse of late afternoon golden hour across rolling grass hills. The long, elegant shadows of ancient cypress trees stretch slowly and peacefully across amber fields as the sun sinks. Every shadow bows in unhurried submission to cosmic law.
