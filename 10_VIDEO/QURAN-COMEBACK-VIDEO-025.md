* **Core Emotional Tone:** Serene Dignity; Boundaries of Truth and Falsehood; Humility before Creation.

## 2. Shot-by-Shot Blueprint (5 Master Scenes)

* **Shot Type:** High-Speed Telephoto / Turbulent Coastal Edge
* **Visual Description:** Enormous, chaotic ocean breakers smashing against jagged coastal sea cliffs during stormy twilight. Violent white sea foam sprays 15 meters into the air, colliding and churning with unrelenting fury. Represents the friction, ego, and agitation of worldly arguments.
* **Camera Movement:** Static locked telephoto lens capturing the violent impact of waves against rock.
* **AI Video Generation Prompt (720p Ceiling):**  
  `Cinematic shot of powerful stormy ocean waves crashing violently against dark jagged sea cliffs, white spray exploding in cold twilight air, moody atmospheric ocean power, 720p documentary realism, dramatic natural force --ar 16:9 --no people, ships, lighthouses, cartoon`

### Scene 2 (00:45–02:00) — The Divine Ayah: The Two Seas Meeting
* **Shot Type:** High Aerial Drone / Natural Estuary Confluence
* **Visual Description:** Spectacular aerial bird’s-eye view where a clear emerald-green freshwater river discharges into the deep navy-blue ocean (*Maraja al-bahrayni hadha 'adhbun furatun wa hadha milhun ujaj*). A distinct, serene color boundary line separates the two waters, neither overtaking the other (*Barzakhun wa hijrum mahjoor*). A breathtaking proof of divine balance.
* **Camera Movement:** Slow downward vertical drone drift rotating gently over the water boundary.
* **AI Video Generation Prompt (720p Ceiling):**  
  `Cinematic aerial drone shot looking down at the meeting of two bodies of water, turquoise fresh river water flowing into deep navy ocean water, visible serene boundary line between colors, calm surface, 720p master, BBC Planet Earth quality --ar 16:9 --no boats, people, artificial lines, text`

### Scene 3 (02:00–04:00) — Classical Tafsir Insight: The Winding Trail of Dignity
* **Shot Type:** Low-Angle Ground Tracking / Dewy Forest Trail
* **Visual Description:** A solitary, smooth stone footpath winding gracefully through a quiet, ancient pine forest at sunrise (*Alladheena yamshoona 'ala al-ardi hawna*). Fresh dewdrops rest undisturbed on moss-covered rocks alongside the trail. Gentle mist drifts through tree trunks; the atmosphere exudes unhurried peace and gentle grace.
* **Camera Movement:** Smooth gimbal glide 30cm above the stone path moving forward at a peaceful walking pace.
* **AI Video Generation Prompt (720p Ceiling):**  
  `Cinematic low tracking shot gliding along a peaceful stone pathway in a dewy pine forest, morning mist filtering sunlight through tall trees, mossy forest floor, 720p resolution, meditative gentle journey, National Geographic realism --ar 16:9 --no people, animals, footprints, modern objects`

### Scene 4 (04:00–05:30) — The Tadabbur Mirror: The Stretching Shadows
* **Shot Type:** Time-Lapse Golden Hour / Valley Contemplation
* **Visual Description:** Expansive view across rolling hills as golden hour approaches sunset (*Alam tara ila rabbika kayfa madda adh-dhill*). The shadows of ancient stone pillars and cypress trees stretch out slowly and elegantly across the amber grass, showing how even shadows bow and submit to cosmic law.
* **Camera Movement:** Slow time-lapse showing shadows elongating peacefully across the valley.
* **AI Video Generation Prompt (720p Ceiling):**  
  `Cinematic time-lapse shot of long peaceful shadows stretching across golden grass hills at sunset, cypress trees casting long shadows, warm amber and violet evening sky, 720p documentary, serene cosmic rhythm --ar 16:9 --no people, cars, modern structures`

### Scene 5 (05:30–06:45) — The Return (Outro)
* **Shot Type:** Sunset Sanctuary & Open Scripture
* **Visual Description:** An open Qur'an rests on a carved wooden bookstand beside an arched stone window overlooking the sea at dusk. The glowing orange sun sinks gently into the water. A calm evening breeze turns a single page, coming to rest as the sky turns into deep peaceful indigo.
* **Camera Movement:** Slow upward tilt from the resting scripture toward the calm evening horizon.
* **AI Video Generation Prompt (720p Ceiling):**  
  `Atmospheric cinematic shot from a quiet stone window with an open book on a wooden stand, looking out toward a calm sunset sea horizon, soft violet and amber twilight, peaceful sacred solitude, 720p resolution, timeless stillness --ar 16:9 --no people, modern items, text`
