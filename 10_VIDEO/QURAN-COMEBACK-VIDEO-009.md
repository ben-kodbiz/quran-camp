# Episode 9 Video Storyboard Specification (`QURAN-COMEBACK-VIDEO-009`)

**Campaign:** Come Back to the Qur'an  
**Episode 9 Title:** *"The Surah That Explains Why You're Losing"*  
**Focal Scripture:** Surah Al-Asr 103:1–3  
**Assigned Agents:** `AGENT-10` (Video Agent) & `AGENT-08` (Visual Director)  
**Visual Core Equation:** `NATURE + KNOWLEDGE + REFLECTION + TRANQUILITY = HUURS VISUAL LANGUAGE`  
**Living Creation Directive (Effective Ep 09+):** 100% Living creation, marine depths, cenderawasih, coral ecosystems, and infinite cosmic majesty. Zero stone pathways, zero archways, zero inanimate objects.

---

## 1. Executive Cinematic Direction & Visual Rhythm

* **Cinematic Philosophy:** Moving contemplation through the awe of divine creation. The camera never rushes; it breathes. Avoid all frantic YouTube tropes (no rapid cuts, no zoomer shakes, no neon graphics, no clickbait arrows).
* **Aspect Ratio & Resolution:** 16:9 Widescreen (1280 × 720 HD Master, 24.00 fps) — HARD CEILING: 720p PER USER MANDATE.
* **Color Palette & Atmosphere:** Deep cosmic sapphire, midnight ocean indigo, electric turquoise, emerald rainforest green, and golden-hour mountain amber.
* **Assigned Visual Motifs:**
  - Scene 1: Infinite Spiral Galaxies & Glowing Nebulae (Cosmic Scale of Time)
  - Scene 2: Deep Ocean Depths with Sunbeams & Schools of Silver Fish (Submerged in Time)
  - Scene 3: Vibrant Living Coral Reef & Tropical Fish (Living Synergy & Righteous Community)
  - Scene 4: Bird of Paradise (Cenderawasih) in Misty Rainforest (Beauty of Sincerity & Parting Admonition)
  - Scene 5: Epic Green Mountain Panorama with Cascading Waterfalls & Sunset (Steadfastness & Patience)
* **Default Visual Rhythm:**
  - `0–3 sec`: Majestic establishing shot + contemplative opening premise.
  - `3–10 sec`: Visual immersion and atmospheric expansion.
  - `10–30 sec`: Insight, Ayah typography emergence in negative space.
  - `30–45 sec`: Emotional and reflective depth.
  - `45–60 sec+`: Quiet resolution and practical invitation.

* **Shot Primitive:** `Shot A (Cosmic Galaxy) / Astrophotography`
* **Visual Framing & Action:** Boundless expanse of deep space where an immense spiral galaxy rotates smoothly amidst glowing violet and indigo nebulae and crystalline star clusters.
* **Camera Movement:** Slow, subtle cinematic drift forward through the starfield.
* **Lighting & Atmosphere:** Luminous galactic core casting soft radiance across cosmic dust.


## 3. Post-Production Editing & Master Conformance

* **Transitions:** Only natural cuts on movement, 1.5-second cross-dissolves, or organic fades through light. Glitch, spin, warp, and fast whip transitions are strictly prohibited.
* **Color Grading LUT:** `HUURS_NATURAL_EARTH_V2` (Soft natural contrast, highlights rolled off at 92 IRE, rich shadows preserved at 5 IRE, zero green tint on skin tones, warm golden dawn tones).
* **Visual Integrity Audit (`AGENT-16`):**
  - [x] Zero humanoid depictions of prophets, companions, or angels.
  - [x] Absolute Zero-Human Mandate: zero people, faces, silhouettes, or hands across all 5 scenes.
  - [x] Full Living Creation Mandate compliance: galaxies, deep ocean, fish schools, coral reefs, cenderawasih, and mountain waterfalls.
  - [x] Zero AI fantasy tropes (no neon glowing geometric holograms, no fake Arabic gibberish).
  - [x] Negative space ratio: Minimum 40% open sky, water, or neutral background across all shots.
  - [x] Verified authentic Quranic script typography.
