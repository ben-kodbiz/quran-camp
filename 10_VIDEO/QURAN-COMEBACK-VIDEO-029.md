## 1. Visual & Theological Context Analysis
* **What Surah Al-Ankabut Is Conveying:**
  1. The inevitability of tests: «A-hasiba an-nāsu an yutrakū an yaqūlū āmannā wa hum lā yuftanūn» (29:2).
  2. The fragility of reliance on created things, likened to the spider's house: «wa inna awhana al-buyūti la-baytu al-'ankabūt» (29:41).
  3. The effortless divine nourishment of creatures in the deep: «Wa ka-ayyin min dābbatin lā tahmilu rizqahā Allāhu yarzuquhā» (29:60).
  4. The divine guarantee of multiple paths for those who strive: «Wa-lladhīna jāhadū fīnā la-nahdiyannahum subulanā» (29:69).
  5. The sanctuary of prayer and the preeminence of divine remembrance: «wa ladhikru Allāhi akbar» (29:45).
