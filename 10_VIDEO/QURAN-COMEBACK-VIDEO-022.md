## 1. Visual Overview & Motifs
* **Surah:** Al-Hajj (22:5: Barren Earth Quivering into Life; 22:27: The Universal Call Across Mountain Passes)
* **Core Emotional Tone:** Transformation; Awe of Resurrection; Awakening after Despair.
* **Primary Semantic Motifs:** Cracked Desert Soil, Rain on Parched Ground, Mountain Highways, Sprouting Flora, Stone Courtyard Basin.
* **Strict Prohibitions:** Zero human figures, zero facial depictions, zero musical instruments, zero fantasy neon.

## 2. Shot-by-Shot Blueprint (5 Master Scenes)

### Scene 1 (00:00–00:45) — The Hook: The Parched Earth
* **Shot Type:** Extreme Macro / Arid Landscape
* **Visual Description:** Ground-level camera looking across vast, desolate, cracked clay soil baked under harsh dry desert heat. Dust motes drift lazily in the shimmering hot air. No signs of moisture or life. Represents spiritual exhaustion and feeling internally lifeless.
* **Camera Movement:** Low, unhurried linear push-in across geometric soil cracks (0.15 m/s).
* **AI Video Generation Prompt (720p Ceiling):**  
  `Cinematic low-angle macro shot of dry cracked clay desert ground, heat haze shimmer, extreme ground texture, warm dusty earth tones, photorealistic 720p documentary, stillness and silence --ar 16:9 --no people, skulls, fantasy effects, saturated colors`

### Scene 2 (00:45–02:00) — The Divine Ayah: The Quivering Earth
* **Shot Type:** Ultra Slow-Motion Rain Impact / Botanical Awakening
* **Visual Description:** Massive, cool drops of rain strike the dry parched earth (*Ihtazzat wa rabat*). Dust settles immediately as dark moisture blooms across the cracked clay. In extreme slow motion, tiny dormant grass shoots begin to stir and push through the rehydrated mud.
* **Camera Movement:** 100fps slow-motion macro tracking shot at soil level.
* **AI Video Generation Prompt (720p Ceiling):**  
  `Ultra slow-motion close-up of heavy rain drops hitting dry cracked soil, moisture blooming into rich dark earth, tiny green grass blades beginning to stir, 720p master, National Geographic biology documentary, organic revival --ar 16:9 --no people, CGI cartoon, distortion`

### Scene 3 (02:00–04:00) — Classical Tafsir Insight: The Ancient Highway
* **Shot Type:** Wide Geological Aerial / Pilgrimage Highway
* **Visual Description:** Immense granite and sandstone canyons in the desert, showing an ancient carved stone caravan path winding through deep gorges toward distant misty peaks (*Wa adhdhin fin-nasi bil-hajj*). The sky above is vast, clear, and serene.
* **Camera Movement:** Majestic high-angle drone tracking shot following the curve of the ancient mountain road.
* **AI Video Generation Prompt (720p Ceiling):**  
  `Wide cinematic aerial shot of an ancient stone pathway winding through rugged sandstone mountain canyons, early morning soft desert light, vast landscape, 720p resolution, realistic natural beauty, timeless journey --ar 16:9 --no people, cars, modern structures`

### Scene 4 (04:00–05:30) — The Tadabbur Mirror: The Sprouting Meadow
* **Shot Type:** Medium Botanical Field / Morning Wind
* **Visual Description:** The once-dry ground is now blanketed in vibrant emerald wild grasses and small white clover flowers, swaying rhythmically under a cool fresh mountain breeze. Water droplets sparkle like diamonds in the early morning sunlight.
* **Camera Movement:** Lateral tracking shot skimming just above the flowering meadow.
* **AI Video Generation Prompt (720p Ceiling):**  
  `Cinematic tracking shot over a lush green wild clover and grass meadow in morning wind, sparkling dew drops, soft golden hour sunlight, peaceful serene movement, 720p documentary, tranquil nature --ar 16:9 --no animals, humans, artificial glare`

### Scene 5 (05:30–06:45) — The Return (Outro)
* **Shot Type:** Stone Courtyard & Water Fountain Basin
* **Visual Description:** An empty traditional stone courtyard at sunrise. In the center, a hand-hewn marble water basin overflows smoothly, sending gentle symmetrical ripples across clear water that mirrors the peach-colored dawn sky.
* **Camera Movement:** Slow upward tilt from the calm water reflection to the open sky.
* **AI Video Generation Prompt (720p Ceiling):**  
  `Atmospheric cinematic shot of a peaceful stone fountain basin with gentle water ripples, reflecting an early dawn pastel sky, serene courtyard architecture, 720p resolution, quiet contemplative morning --ar 16:9 --no people, modern items, text`
