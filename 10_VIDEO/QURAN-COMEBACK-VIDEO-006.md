## 2. Shot-by-Shot Production Sequence

### Scene 1 (00:00–00:35): The Straight Stone Path (Seventeen Times a Day)
* **File Target:** `EP06_SCENE01.mp4` / `QURAN-COMEBACK-VEO-006-SCENE01.mp4`
* **Theme Anchor:** Surah Al-Fatihah 1:6 (*Ihdināṣ-Ṣirāṭ al-Mustaqīm*)
* **Visual Framing & Action:** An ancient stone path cutting straight across rugged terrain toward the horizon. First light of dawn dissolving morning fog. Visual anchor of spiritual direction and unwavering focus.
* **Camera Movement:** Slow, steady forward tracking shot at eye level along the centerline.
* **Lighting:** Cool pale blue transitioning into soft golden sunrise rays.

### Scene 2 (00:35–01:45): The Meeting of Two Seas (Guidance Is a Current, Not a Monument)
* **File Target:** `EP06_SCENE02.mp4` / `QURAN-COMEBACK-VEO-006-SCENE02.mp4`
* **Theme Anchor:** Surah Ar-Rahmān 55:19–20 (*Maraja al-Bahrayn*) & Surah Al-Furqān 25:53
* **Visual Framing & Action:** Aerial glide over two majestic ocean currents meeting without overflowing their boundary. Dynamic life, constant movement, and divine preservation.
* **Camera Movement:** High-angle slow cinematic forward drift.
* **Lighting:** Clear natural morning daylight, turquoise and deep navy contrasts.

### Scene 3 (01:45–03:00): Symmetrical Stone Colonnade (The Prophetic Parable of the Walled Path)
* **File Target:** `EP06_SCENE03.mp4` / `QURAN-COMEBACK-VEO-006-SCENE03.mp4`
* **Theme Anchor:** Musnad Ahmad / Jami` at-Tirmidhi (Parable of the Straight Path with Two Walls)
* **Visual Framing & Action:** A majestic straight corridor framed by ancient carved stone pillars and soft amber morning sunlight. Straight boundary of divine law protecting the traveler.
* **Camera Movement:** Smooth linear dolly tracking through the colonnade.
* **Lighting:** High-contrast architectural morning shadows and warm sandstone highlights.

### Scene 4 (03:00–04:00): The Reed Pen & Parchment (Balance of Knowledge & Action)
* **File Target:** `EP06_SCENE04.mp4` / `QURAN-COMEBACK-VEO-006-SCENE04.mp4`
* **Theme Anchor:** Surah Al-Qalam 68:1 (*Nūn wal-Qalam*) & Surah Al-Fatihah 1:7
* **Visual Framing & Action:** Handcrafted reed pen resting beside an inkwell on cream parchment. Scholarly dignity representing knowledge that guards against deviation (*Ghayr il-Maghdūb wa lad-Dāllīn*).
* **Camera Movement:** Macro slow rotational pan around the pen and inkwell.
* **Lighting:** Soft diffuse window lighting with subtle floating dust motes.

### Scene 5 (04:00–05:00): The Traditional Ship on Ocean Swell (Keep My Feet Firm)
* **File Target:** `EP06_SCENE05.mp4` / `QURAN-COMEBACK-VEO-006-SCENE05.mp4`
* **Theme Anchor:** Surah Ash-Shūra 42:32 (*Al-Jawār fil-Bahr*) & Surah Ibrahim 14:32
* **Visual Framing & Action:** A traditional wooden sailing dhow sailing smoothly across calm open ocean waters toward the radiant morning horizon. Metaphor for steadfast progress and ultimate return to Allah.
* **Camera Movement:** Telephoto slow tracking shot following the graceful gliding vessel.
* **Lighting:** Luminous pastel golden dawn, 60% negative space in sky.
