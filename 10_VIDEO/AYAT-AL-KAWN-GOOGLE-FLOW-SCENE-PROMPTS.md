## 0. Universal Google Flow Configuration & Negative Prompts

When deploying these prompts into **Google Flow**, **Google Veo**, or **Vertex AI Video FX**, prepend the scene description with the camera direction and apply the universal negative parameters:

### Universal Negative Prompt Block (Copy into Negative Prompt Box):
```text
humans, people, faces, silhouettes, hands, man, woman, child, religious figures, cartoon, anime, 3d render, CGI look, fantasy art, glowing magical auras, neon laser beams, floating pseudo-Arabic text, distorted geometry, jitter, morphing artifacts, fast whip pans, motion blur smearing, oversaturated chromatic aberration, horror, dark dystopia
```

### Universal Production Parameters:
* **Frame Rate:** `24.0 fps` (Cinematic standard)
* **Motion Quality / Rate:** `4` or `5` (Slow, contemplative, fluid, zero frantic camera shakes)
* **Color Science:** ARRI Alexa Mini LF / RED V-Raptor 8K color science; naturalistic contrast; deep blacks (`#05080f`), warm gold grazing light (`#d4af37`), emerald foliage, clean atmospheric blues.
* **Audio Policy:** Pure organic nature soundscape (halal foley: wind, water, waves, birdsong, rustling leaves). **Strictly zero musical instruments or synth chords.**

## 13. Quality Assurance & Brand Visual System Checklist

Before approving any generated render output from Google Flow or Veo, the **Visual QA Agent (`AGENT-16`)** must verify:

- [ ] **Zero-Human Check:** No people, faces, silhouettes, or hands appear in any frame.
- [ ] **Permissible Fauna Check:** Animals (butterfly, ants, turtle, birds, rooster) behave naturally without surreal anthropomorphic actions or cartoonish physics.
- [ ] **Negative Space Check:** At least 35% of the frame is clean negative space (sky, water surface, open field) suitable for contemplative pauses.
- [ ] **No Fantasy Lighting:** No glowing neon laser borders, magical sparkles, or fantasy sci-fi effects. Lighting is strictly natural (dawn, golden hour, twilight, starlight).
- [ ] **Camera Discipline:** Slow, deliberate, dignified camera motion; zero chaotic zooms or frenetic MTV-style cuts.
- [ ] **Halal Sound Design:** Rendered videos must use organic acoustic foley only, adhering to the brand's **Zero-Music Mandate**.
