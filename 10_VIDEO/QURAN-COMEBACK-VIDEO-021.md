## 1. Visual Overview & Motifs
* **Surah:** Al-Anbiya (21:30: The Cleaving of Heavens & Earth; 21:87–88: The Supplication of Dhun-Nun / Yunus)
* **Core Emotional Tone:** Solitude turning into Deliverance; Infinite Divine Power meeting Human Fragility.
* **Primary Semantic Motifs:** Deep Oceanic Void, Water Flow, Primordial Celestial Starlight, Ancient Cracked Desert Rock, Sacred Open Book.

## 2. Shot-by-Shot Blueprint (5 Master Scenes)

* **Shot Type:** Macro Underwater Submersion / Dark Chiaroscuro
* **Visual Description:** Submerged camera skimming 2 meters beneath the surface of churning dark midnight ocean waters. Inky black and deep indigo depths below. A solitary shaft of pale moonlight pierces downward through the dark swell, illuminating suspended bubbles before fading into the abyss. Conveys total helplessness and emotional isolation.
* **Camera Movement:** Slow, fluid descent downward into the dark water column (0.2 m/s).
* **AI Video Generation Prompt (720p Ceiling):**

### Scene 2 (00:45–02:00) — The Divine Ayah & Primordial Origin
* **Shot Type:** Wide Cosmic Astrophotography & Cloud Dissolve
* **Visual Description:** Expansive celestial panorama showing a dense starfield and deep indigo cosmic nebulae (*Ratqan fa-fataqnahuma*). The camera slowly tilts downward through thinning nighttime clouds to reveal ancient rugged mountains on earth as torrential rain begins to fall upon barren rock.
* **Camera Movement:** Majestic vertical tilt-down transitioning from space into terrestrial rain.
* **AI Video Generation Prompt (720p Ceiling):**  
  `Wide cinematic shot transitioning from starry cosmic night sky down to rugged mountain silhouette, heavy gentle rain falling on ancient mountain cliffs, dramatic natural atmosphere, 720p resolution, realistic National Geographic cinematography --ar 16:9 --no fantasy magic, spaceships, text`

### Scene 3 (02:00–04:00) — Classical Tafsir Insight: The Seed in the Stone
* **Shot Type:** Macro Focus Rack / Geological Realism
* **Visual Description:** Weathered dark granite cliffside enduring mountain winds. In a razor-thin fissure of the rock, a tiny resilient emerald green seedling emerges, damp with morning dew. Illustrates *«Wa kadhalika nunji al-mu'mineen»*—deliverance engineered in impossible circumstances.
* **Camera Movement:** Slow rack-focus from harsh dry stone texture to the delicate green sprout.
* **AI Video Generation Prompt (720p Ceiling):**  
  `Macro cinematic close-up of a small vibrant green plant sprout growing through a narrow crack in ancient dark granite rock, morning mist, moisture droplets, 720p master, lifelike botanical realism, hope and resilience --ar 16:9 --no people, artificial colors, distortion`

### Scene 4 (04:00–05:30) — The Tadabbur Mirror: Water as Life
* **Shot Type:** Slow-Motion Water Flow / Crystalline Stream
* **Visual Description:** Crystal-clear mountain river rushing over smooth dark basalt stones (*Wa ja'alna min al-ma'i kulla shay'in hayy*). Sunlight filtering through distant cedar trees causes golden reflections to dance on the riverbed. Pure cleansing motion and spiritual revival.
* **Camera Movement:** Low-angle static camera skimming water surface at 60fps.
* **AI Video Generation Prompt (720p Ceiling):**  
  `Slow-motion close-up of pure mountain spring water cascading smoothly over dark river stones, morning sunbeams creating golden light reflections, 720p resolution, meditative organic flow, pristine nature documentary --ar 16:9 --no neon, saturation, people`

* **Shot Type:** Sacred Reading Alcove & Open Horizon
* **Visual Description:** Traditional dark walnut bookstand (*rihal*) holding an open Qur'an manuscript in an open stone arched portico. Outside, the first light of dawn washes over a calm, peaceful valley of olive groves. A cool morning breeze turns a single parchment page.
* **Camera Movement:** Slow pull-back and upward tilt from the book toward the expansive golden dawn horizon.
