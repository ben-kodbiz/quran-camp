## 1. Visual & Theological Context Analysis
* **What Surah Al-Qasas Is Conveying:**
  1. The mother of Musa placing her child on the water, trusting divine protection: «wa lā takhāfī wa lā taḥzanī» (28:7).
  2. Musa in exile at the well of Madyan, declaring absolute dependency: «Rabbi innī limā anzalta ilayya min khayrin faqīr» (28:24).
  3. The sacred call at the burning bush in the blessed spot of Tuwa: «mina ash-shajarati an yā Mūsā innī anā Allāh» (28:30).
  4. The tragedy of Qarun's pride and the myth of self-made wealth: «Innamā ūtītuhu 'alā 'ilmin 'indī» (28:76–78).
  5. The eternal reality of the Divine Face: «kullu shay'in hālikun illā wajhah» (28:88).
