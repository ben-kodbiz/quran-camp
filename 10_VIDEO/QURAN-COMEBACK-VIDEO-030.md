## 1. Visual & Theological Context Analysis
* **What Surah Ar-Rum Is Conveying:**
  1. The moral and environmental crisis: «Zahara al-fasādu fī al-barri wa al-baḥri» (30:41).
  2. The cosmic diversity of creation: «Wa min āyātihi khalqu as-samāwāti wa al-arḍi» (30:22).
  3. The revival of dead soil by rain: «fa-yuḥyī bihi al-arḍa ba'da mawtihā» (30:24).
  4. The ocean pathways propelled by mercy: «an yursila ar-riyāḥa mubashshirātin» (30:46).
  5. The steadfast return to the uncorrupted Fitrah: «fiṭrata Allāhi allatī faṭara an-nāsa 'alayhā» (30:30).
