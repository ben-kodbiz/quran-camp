**Project:** Huurs / Huur Studio  
**Core Equation:** `NATURE + KNOWLEDGE + REFLECTION + TRANQUILITY = HUURS VISUAL LANGUAGE`  
**Foundational Principle:** **READ. REFLECT. RETURN.**  
**Theological Baseline:** Strict Traditional Sunni Orthodoxy (Ahl as-Sunnah wal-Jamā'ah)

## 1. Executive Theological Governance

This document establishes the **immutable permanent themes** permitted in Huurs Studio visual and auditory media. Every scene generated in Google Flow, Veo, or rendered locally must draw from these approved categories.

### 1.1 The Non-Negotiable Triad

1. **Absolute Zero-Human Mandate:**
   - **Zero People, Zero Faces, Zero Silhouettes, Zero Hands, Zero Human Figures.**
   - No crowds, no praying figures, no hands turning pages, no shadows of persons.
   - Contemplation is invited through **the majesty of creation and sacred objects**, never modeled through actors.

2. **Strict Zero-Music Mandate:**
   - **Zero musical instruments, zero synth melodies, zero rhythmic beat pads.**
   - Permitted audio: Spoken word narration, natural room acoustic resonance, and authentic organic foley (wind, rainfall, gentle streams, distant ocean waves, desert breeze, rustling leaves, birdsong).

3. **Strict Anti-Fantasy & Theological Purity:**
   - **No depiction of the Unseen (*Al-Ghayb*):** Zero visual depiction of Allah ﷻ, Angels (*Malā'ikah*), Jinn, Prophets (*Anbiyā'*), or Companions (*Sahābah*).
   - **No Sci-Fi / Fantasy Tropes:** No glowing neon auras, no floating laser particles, no mystical vortexes, no surreal gravity-defying environments.
   - **No Fake Arabic:** No distorted, unreadable, AI-hallucinated pseudo-calligraphy. Mushaf text must be crisp, authentic Uthmanic script or represented by dignified, non-distorted traditional manuscript layout.

## 5. Visual Prompt Quality Checklist (Mandatory for Every Prompt)

Before copying any prompt to Google Flow or image generator, verify:
- [ ] **Zero-Human Check:** Did you explicitly append `no humans, no faces, no silhouettes, no hands, unpopulated`?
- [ ] **Zero-Music Check:** Is the audio planned strictly for voiceover + room tone + halal foley?
- [ ] **No Distorted Arabic:** Is the text prompt either free of on-screen text or explicitly specifying `clean traditional calligraphy`?
- [ ] **Lighting & Tone:** Is the lighting natural, cinematic, and soft (no neon, no laser, no hyper-saturated sci-fi glow)?
- [ ] **Resolution Constraint:** Is the generation targeted for 720p maximum (`1280x720` or `720x1280`)?
- [ ] **Atmosphere:** Does the scene evoke quiet contemplation, humility, and reverence (*Khashyah* & *Sakīnah*)?
