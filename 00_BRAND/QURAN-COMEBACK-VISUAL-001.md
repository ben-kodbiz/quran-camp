# Episode 1 Visual Direction & Cinematic Shot List (`QURAN-COMEBACK-VISUAL-001`)

**Campaign:** Come Back to the Qur'an  
**Episode 1 Title:** *"You Don't Need Another Islamic Video — You Need the Qur'an"*  
**Visual Director:** `AGENT-08`  
**North Star:** **READ. REFLECT. RETURN.**  
**Visual Equation:** `NATURE + KNOWLEDGE + REFLECTION + TRANQUILITY = HUURS`  

---

## 1. Visual DNA & Environmental Atmosphere

* **Primary Motifs:** **Book + Window Light** (The awakening of knowledge) & **Pathway at Sunrise** (The journey back to Allah).
* **Secondary Motifs:** **Still Water Ripples** (Contemplation deepening) & **Ocean Horizon** (The infinite vastness of divine mercy).
* **Atmospheric Mood:** Quiet, premium, contemplative, timeless, and uncluttered. Feels like a peaceful dawn after a long night.
* **Color Palette:**
  - Base: Warm linen white, aged paper cream, and deep slate stone.
  - Accent: Soft morning amber (sunrise) and muted Mediterranean ocean blue.
  - Absence: No neon greens, no harsh saturated golds, no hyper-HDR glow.
* **Lighting Language:** 100% natural, soft diffused side light; early morning window shafts; atmospheric dust motes dancing in stillness.

---

## 2. Master Composition & Negative Space Rules

* **Aspect Ratios:** 16:9 for YouTube Flagship; 9:16 for Platform Shorts; 1:1 for Reflection Carousels.
* **Rule of Thirds & Negative Space:** Place focal subjects (hands opening a page, a quiet window silhouette) strictly in the lower or lateral third. The top 40% of the frame must remain clean, breathable negative space reserved for title typography and Qur'anic Arabic.
* **Camera Movement:** Extremely slow, deliberate push-ins (5–10 mm/s) or static locked-off shots. No frantic camera shakes, fast cuts, or dizzying drone flyovers.
