# Huurs Studio Source & Commercial Licensing Ledger

**Project:** Huurs Studio  
**Role:** AGENT-12 (Product Agent) & AGENT-19 (Artifact Librarian)  
**Applies to:** Free campaigns, YouTube episodes, social media, and commercial digital products  

---

## 1. Golden Legal Principle

> **Availability on public platforms (e.g., Quran.com, Al-Maktaba al-Shamela) does NOT imply commercial redistribution rights.**

The commercial digital book (*Come Back to Qur'an — 114 Surahs*) must not be a copy-paste aggregate of copyrighted modern English translations or commentaries. It must consist of **original pedagogical synthesis, structured reflection, and original editorial exposition**.

---

## 2. Textual Asset Licensing Classifications

| Source Category | Intellectual Property Status | Commercial Publishing Policy |
|---|---|---|
| **Original Arabic Qur'an (Uthmani)** | Divine Revelation / Public Domain | Permitted; use standardized digital Uthmani typography (Tanzil project / King Fahd Complex). |
| **Classical Arabic Tafsir (Ibn Kathir, Tabari)** | Historical texts / Public Domain | Permitted for scholarly synthesis; original English translations must be original summaries. |
| **Modern English Translations (e.g., The Clear Quran)** | Copyright © Dr. Mustafa Khattab / Furqaan Institute | Permitted for brief fair-use quotation in educational video scripts; for commercial digital books, use original translations, public-domain translations (e.g., Pickthall/Yusuf Ali edited), or formal written license. |
| **Ma'arif-ul-Qur'an (English)** | Darul Uloom Karachi / Maktaba Darul Uloom | Use for internal research synthesis; do not reproduce extended verbatim excerpts in commercial products. |
| **Hadith Collections (Bukhari/Muslim Arabic)** | Public Domain | Permitted; translations should be carefully verified original or standard scholarly English renderings. |
| **Original Huurs Reflections & Frameworks** | Copyright © Huurs Studio | Proprietary asset; protected under standard copyright. |

---

## 3. Commercial Ebook Release Gate

Before compiling any commercial edition for sale:
1. Every cited passage must have an explicit record in this ledger.
2. If license status is `UNKNOWN`, the publication status is immediately held at **BLOCKED**.
3. Originality score across all chapters must achieve $\ge 85\%$ original synthesis.
