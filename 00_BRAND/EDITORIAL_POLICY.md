# Huurs Studio Editorial Policy: Islamic Source Discipline

**Project:** Huurs Studio  
**Applies to:** All campaigns, scripts, ebooks, videos, reflections, and social posts  
**North Star:** **READ. REFLECT. RETURN.**  

---

## 1. The Core Categorical Separation

Every piece of educational content must strictly distinguish between the following six tiers. Conflating any of these levels is an immediate QA failure:

1. **The Word of Allah (Qur'an):** Absolute, divine, immutable Arabic text.
2. **Authentic Sunnah (Hadith):** Verified Prophetic actions, sayings, and approvals.
3. **Classical Sunni Tafsir:** Recognized classical and authoritative scholarship explaining context, linguistic meanings, and consensus (e.g., Ibn Kathir, At-Tabari, Al-Qurtubi).
4. **Scholarly Explanatory Commentary:** Authoritative contemporary explanatory works (e.g., Ma'arif-ul-Qur'an by Mufti Muhammad Shafi).
5. **Tadabbur (Reflection):** Personal contemplation and internalization grounded strictly within established meanings, framed humbly as: *"One reflection we can take is..."* Never framed as divine intent.
6. **Practical Application:** Concrete actions to implement the verse in contemporary daily life.

---

## 2. Mandatory Language Formulas

### Permitted Phrasing
* *"This ayah teaches us..."*
* *"In Tafsir Ibn Kathir, the scholars explain that..."*
* *"Mufti Muhammad Shafi notes in Ma'arif-ul-Qur'an that..."*
* *"The Prophet ﷺ said in a sahih hadith recorded by Al-Bukhari..."*
* *"When contemplating this verse, a believer may reflect upon..."*

### Strictly Prohibited Phrasing
* *"Allah is telling you personally that [unverified assertion]..."*
* *"The secret meaning behind this verse that scholars missed is..."*
* *"This ayah predicted modern scientific phenomenon X..."*
* *"Reciting this ayah 40 times will guarantee wealth or ease..."* (without authentic proof)
* *"Allah's intention in this word is definitely [personal speculation]..."*

---

## 3. Representation of Scholarly Disagreement

Where classical scholars have recorded differing opinions (*ikhtilaf*):
1. **Never suppress legitimate diversity** to enforce artificial unanimity.
2. **State the views transparently:** *"While some classical mufassirun interpreted this word to mean X, others held the view that it refers to Y."*
3. Clarify which opinion is highlighted in the episode while maintaining respect for alternative valid Sunni interpretations.

---

## 4. Rejection of Sensationalist Clichés

The following tropes are strictly banned from all Huurs publications:
* Numerology, letter counting, and hidden mathematical codes.
* Sensationalist "scientific miracles" that compromise theological stability.
* Politically or ideologically motivated selective quotations.
* Clickbait promises of spiritual shortcuts or effortless guarantees.
