## 1. Executive Cartography Overview

Part 8 climaxes the Abrahamic narrative, establishes the prophetic curriculum of the Final Messenger, and shatters the retrofitting of later sectarian identities onto early patriarchs:

- **Page 1: Raising the Foundations & The Prophetic Curriculum**
  - Pillar 1: Raising Sacred Foundations (Labor of Ibrahim & Isma'il, *Rabbana Taqabbal Minna*, supplication for perpetual Islam, *Arina Manasikana*).
  - Pillar 2: The Tripartite Prophetic Curriculum (*Wab'ath Feehim Rasoola*, Reciting Signs, Scripture & Wisdom / *Al-Hikmah*, Moral Purification / *Tazkiyah*).
- **Page 2: Self-Alienation & The Deathbed Bequest**
  - Pillar 3: The Folly of Rejecting Monotheism (*Safiha Nafsah*, chosen in both worlds, instant capitulation / *Aslamtu Li-Rabbil-'Alameen*).
  - Pillar 4: The Deathbed Bequest of Patriarchs (Bequest of Ibrahim & Ya'qub, *Ma Ta'budoona Min Ba'di?*, *Ilahan Wahida*, dying upon Islam).
- **Page 3: Individual Accountability & Sibghatallah**
  - Pillar 5: The Principle of Personal Reckoning (*Tilka Ummatun Qad Khalat*, personal consequence, *Qul Bal Millata Ibraheem*).
  - Pillar 6: The Universal Creed & Sibghatallah (Declaration of *Qooloo Amanna*, *La Nufarriqu Bayna Ahad*, the baptism of Allah / *Sibghatallah*).
- **Page 4: Chronological Fallacies & Concealed Testimony**
  - Pillar 7: The Chronological Fallacy Exposed (Disputing about God, refuting sectarian retconning of patriarchs, *A-Antum A'lamu Amillah?*).
  - Pillar 8: The Crime of Concealing Testimony (*Wa Man Azlamu Mimman Katama Shahadah*, *Wa Mallahu Bi-Ghafilin*, commissioning the new global Ummah).
