```text
SURAH AL-BAQARAH — PART 1 (02:02:41)
│
├── [00:00 - 22:49] SECTION 1: EPISTEMOLOGY OF REVELATION & THE FIVE MARKS OF TAQWA
│   ├── Peak of the Qur'an (Sanam al-Qur'an): Elevation and structural flow (Tanasub)
│   ├── Huruf Muqatta'at (Alif-Lam-Meem):
│   │   ├── Al-Razi: Intellect subordinated to divine mystery; syllabus of humility
│   │   ├── Al-Sha'rawi: Unlettered Prophet dictating spelling names stuns rabbinic elite
│   │   └── Al-Zamakhshari: Inimitable revelation built from ordinary human alphabet
│   ├── The Immutable Celestial Canon:
│   │   ├── Dhalika: Exalted celestial altitude in Al-Lawh Al-Mahfuz
│   │   ├── La Rayba Feeh: Absolute absence of agitating, sleepless doubt (Rayb)
│   │   └── Taqwa: 36+ occurrences; proactive self-preservation and hyper-vigilance
│   └── The Five Hallmarks of the Muttaqin:
│       ├── 1. Faith in the Unseen (Ghayb)
│       ├── 2. Establishing Prayer (Salah) firmly and unbrokenly
│       ├── 3. Infaq: Ethical spending as transfer between Dunya and Akhirah accounts
│       ├── 4. Reverence for past revelation; seal of prophethood confirmed
│       ├── 5. Conviction in the Afterlife (Yaqeen); certainty like job security
│       └── Harvest of Success (Al-Muflihun): Sustained labor of the spiritual farmer (Fallah)
│
├── [22:50 - 34:36] SECTION 2: THE SEALED DENIERS & THE PSYCHOLOGY OF HYPOCRISY
│   ├── The Incurable Deniers (Kafaru):
│   │   ├── Refers to leaders who rejected da'wah across a full decade
│   │   ├── Sealing Sequence: Heart sealed first -> Hearing sealed -> Sight veiled
│   │   ├── Contrast with Al-Jathiyah (45:23): Ears sealed first due to auditory rebellion
│   │   └── Penalty: Great punishment ('Adhabun 'Azim)
│   └── The Hypocrites (Al-Munafiqun):
│       ├── Grammar of Camouflage: Singular Man yaqulu vs. plural Amanna ("We believe")
│       ├── Absolute Negation: Wa ma hum bi-mu'minin (Zero true faith)
│       ├── Two Categories of Hypocrisy:
│       │   ├── 1. Conscious infiltrators and political spies
│       │   └── 2. Unconscious hypocrites (Wa ma yash'urun) who think they are righteous
│       ├── Terror of the Sahabah: Umar inquiring if his name was on the secret list
│       ├── Degenerative Disease: Sickness in heart grows with moral compromise
│       ├── Root Cause: Habitual lying and narrative fabrication (Bima kanu yakdhibun)
│       └── Acute Retribution: 'Adhabun Aleem (unrelenting acute pain, harsher than kuffar)
│
├── [34:37 - 63:46] SECTION 3: THE DOUBLE-EXIT BURROW & THE TWO MASTER PARABLES
│   ├── Tactics of Deceit:
│   │   ├── False Peacemakers (Innama nahnu muslihun) keeping backdoors open with enemies
│   │   ├── Slandering the Sahabah as "Fools" (Sufaha') for making real sacrifices
│   │   ├── Allah's Direct Defense: The hypocrites are the true fools
│   │   ├── Etymology of Munafiq: The desert lizard's double-exit burrow (Nafaqa')
│   │   └── The 300-Foot Leash: Allowing them to sprint freely until the violent snap
│   └── The Two Master Parables of Hypocrisy:
│       ├── Parable 1: The Kindled Fire (Istawqada Nara)
│       │   ├── Loss of internal light (Dhahaballahu bi-nurihim)
│       │   ├── Simultaneous total sensory shutdown: Deaf, mute, blind (Summ, Bukm, 'Umy)
│       │   └── Fahum la yarji'un: Stranded forever, incapable of returning to guidance
│       └── Parable 2: The Thunderous Rainstorm (Sayyib)
│           ├── Black cloud, thunder, and lightning
│           ├── Thrusting fingers in ears in futile fear of death (Hadharal-mawt)
│           ├── Conditional walking: Moving only when lightning flashes; freezing in hardship
│           └── Exposure of fair-weather, opportunistic faith
│
└── [63:47 - 122:41] SECTION 4: COSMIC SANCTUARY, INIMITABLE CHALLENGE & THE RETURN
    ├── Universal Call to Humanity:
    │   ├── Enslave yourselves to your Master (U'budu rabbakum)
    │   ├── Cosmic Sanctuary: Earth as a furnished bed (Firasha); sky as ceiling (Bina'an)
    │   ├── Luxury of Diverse Fruits (Thamarat): Joy and taste proving divine love
    │   └── Prohibition of Rivals (Andad): Warning against elevating wealth, career, ego
    ├── The Inimitable Challenge (Tahaddi):
    │   ├── Fa'tu bi suratim mim-mithlih: Final verdict after 10 years of rejection
    │   ├── Submission of Arab Masters: Tufayl al-Dawsi & Otba ibn Rabi'a
    │   └── Guaranteed Impotence: Wa lan taf'alu (You will NEVER be able to do it)
    ├── The Abode of Eternal Delight:
    │   ├── Waterfront mansions, flowing rivers, elevated residences
    │   ├── Ever-novel fruits (Hadhalladhi ruziqna min qabl): Familiar appearance, infinite flavor
    │   └── Purified spouses (Azwajun mutahharah) & eternal residence (Khalidun)
    ├── The Parable of the Mosquito (Ba'udatan fa ma fawqaha):
    │   ├── Divine wisdom in microscopic creation; modern science confirms lethal complexity
    │   └── Dual effect: The same revelation misguides the corrupt and guides the sincere
    ├── The Corrupt (Al-Fasiqun):
    │   ├── Breaking the primordial soul covenant (Mithaq / Alastu bi-rabbikum)
    │   └── Severing what Allah commanded to be joined: Kinship ties & Quran-Sunnah unity
    └── The Cosmic Return:
        ├── 4-Stage Lifecycle: Non-existence -> Life -> Death -> Resurrection -> Return
        ├── The Seven Balanced Heavens: Humbling human arrogance before the All-Knowing
        └── Bi-kulli shay'in 'aleem: Absolute surrender to divine wisdom
```
