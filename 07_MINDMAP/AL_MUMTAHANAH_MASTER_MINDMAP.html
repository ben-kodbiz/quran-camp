Plate 01: THE CLANDESTINE EPISTLE & REBUKED ALLIANCE

Pillars 01 & 02: Do Not Take My Enemy as Ally, Secret Affection Rebuked, Expelling the Messenger & Hatib's Letter

PLATE 01 : SECRET EPISTLE
PILLAR 01: THE INTERDICTION OF ENEMY ALLIANCES
La Tattakhidhoo 'Aduwwi wa 'Aduwwakum Awliya', Casting Affection & Truth Denied
01 The Opening Stern Injunction
  • Ya ayyuha alladhina amanoo la tattakhidhoo 'aduwwi wa 'aduwwakum awliya': take not My enemy and yours as patrons.
02 Bestowing Clandestine Affection
  • Tulqoona ilayhim bil-mawaddah: reaching out to them with secret affection while they openly reject revealed truth.
03 Expelling the Prophet and Believers
  • Yukhrijoona ar-rasoola wa iyyakum: driving out the Messenger and yourselves purely because you believe in Allah.
04 Exposing the Duplicity of Motives
  • If you have truly marched out striving in My cause and seeking My pleasure, how can you secretly harbor love?
PILLAR 02: THE PERIL OF CLANDESTINE TREASON
Overpowered as Foes, Stretching Hands and Tongues & Inutility of Kinship
05 The Reality of Enemy Malice
  • In yathqafoo-kum yakoonoo lakum a'da'an: if they overpower you, they will instantly emerge as vicious foes.
06 Stretching Hands and Tongues in Evil
  • Striking you with physical violence and venomous tongues of mockery, yearning that you relapse into disbelief.
07 The Absolute Inutility of Earthly Blood
  • Lan tanfa'akum arhamukum wa la awladukum: neither your blood relations nor your children will avail you on Reckoning Day.
08 The Day of Absolute Separation
  • Yawma al-qiyamati yafsilu baynakum: on the Day of Resurrection He will judge between you; Allah sees all deeds.

Plate 02: THE ABRAHAMIC PARADIGM OF DIVORCE FROM TYRANNY

PLATE 02 : ABRAHAMIC MODEL
PILLAR 03: THE EXCELLENT ABRAHAMIC ARCHETYPE
Qad Kanat Lakum Uswatun Hasanah, Inna Bura'a'u Minkum & Animosity Declared

  • Qad kanat lakum uswatun hasanatun fee Ibraheema: you have an excellent role model in Abraham and his followers.

  • When they proclaimed to their people: 'Inna bura'a'u minkum: we are utterly free of you and what you worship.'
11 Renouncing Tyrannical Idolatries
  • Kafarna bikum: we reject you, and perpetual enmity and hatred has emerged between us until you believe in Allah alone.
12 Uncompromising Monotheistic Loyalty
  • Establishing that spiritual allegiance to the Creator takes unconditional precedence over biological affinity.
PILLAR 04: THE EXCEPTION OF ABRAHAM'S SUPPLICATION
Seeking Forgiveness for Father, No Power with Allah & Complete Reliance

  • Except for Abraham's pledge to his idolatrous father: 'I will surely ask forgiveness for you from my Lord.'
14 Impotence Against Divine Decrees
  • Wa ma amliku laka mina Allahi min shay': yet I possess not the least power to protect you against Allah.
15 The Sublime Supplication of Surrender
  • Rabbana 'alayka tawakkalna wa ilayka anabna: our Lord, in You we place our trust, and to You we turn in penitence.
16 The Final Return to Divine Majesty
  • Wa ilayka al-maseer: and unto You alone is the ultimate destiny and return of all created entities.

PILLAR 05: PRAYER AGAINST BECOMING A TRIAL

  • Waghfir lana Rabbana: and forgive our shortcomings, our Lord; indeed You are the Almighty, the All-Wise.

  • Laqad kana lakum feehim uswatun hasanah: in them is an inspiring model for whoever hopes in Allah and the Last Day.

  • 'Asa Allahu an yaj'ala baynakum wa bayna alladhina 'adaytum minhum mawaddah: perhaps Allah will foster love.

Plate 04: THE DUAL METRIC OF FOREIGN RELATIONS

Pillars 07 & 08: Equity and Kindness to Peaceful Neighbors, Combatant Aggressors Barred, Expellers Rebuked & True Justice

PLATE 04 : FOREIGN RELATIONS
PILLAR 07: BENEVOLENCE & JUSTICE TO NON-BELLIGERENTS
La Yanhakumu Allahu, Those Who Did Not Fight You, An Tabarroohum wa Tuqsitoo
25 The Charter of Peaceful Coexistence
  • La yanhakumu Allahu 'an alladhina lam yuqatilookum fee ad-deen: Allah does not forbid you regarding non-combatants.
26 Protecting Those Who Never Expelled You
  • Those who never waged war against your religion nor aided in driving you out of your ancestral homes.
27 The Command of Birr and Qist
  • An tabarroohum wa tuqsitoo ilayhim: that you treat them with profound kindness (Birr) and execute absolute equity (Qist).
28 Divine Love for the Equitable
  • Inna Allaha yuhibbu al-muqsiteen: indeed, Allah loves those who act with objective, uncompromising justice.
PILLAR 08: THE BAN AGAINST ACTIVE BELLIGERENTS
Fought You in Religion, Drove You Out & Allied Against You, Unjust Allies
29 The Three Criteria of Hostile Aggressors
  • Innama yanhakumu Allahu: Allah only forbids you regarding those who fought you in religion, expelled you, or aided it.
30 The Prohibition of Political Alliance
  • An tawallawhum: forbidding that you take them as intimate allies, protectors, or confidants in security matters.
31 The Censure of Complicit Collaborators
  • Wa man yatawallahum fa-ula'ika humu adh-dhalimoon: and whoever allies with active oppressors are the true wrongdoers.
32 Strategic Distinction in Geopolitics
  • Differentiating masterfully between peaceful civilians deserving benevolence and violent oppressors requiring resistance.
