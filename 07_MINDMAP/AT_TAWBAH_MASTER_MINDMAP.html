THE DIVINE ULTIMATUM & THE SACRED SANCTUARY

Pillars 1 & 2: Disavowal of Polytheism, Four Months Respite, Treaty Terms & Asylum for Seekers of Truth

[PLATE 01 : ULTIMATUM & TREATIES]

PILLAR 1: DISAVOWAL OF POLYTHEISM & THE GRAND PROCLAMATION

Bara'ah, The Four-Month Respite & Proclamation on Yawm al-Hajj al-Akbar

1

The Omission of the Basmalah & The Sword of Truth

  • Revealed without Basmalah; Ali: Basmalah is security (Aman), while Bara'ah revokes false peace.
  • A stern declaration demanding absolute moral accountability and terminating pagan deceit.
  • Dividing the insincere opportunists from those of steadfast monotheistic faith.
2

Proclamation on Yawm al-Hajj al-Akbar

  • Ali dispatched to Mina on the Prophet's camel to announce the decree to assembled tribes.
  • No polytheist shall perform pilgrimage after this year, nor circumambulate naked.
  • Restoring the pristine Abrahamic purity of the Ka'bah as the monotheistic sanctuary.
3

The Four-Month Respite (Faseehoo)

  • A grace period granted to treaty-violators to travel freely and contemplate their state.
  • Proclaiming that the deniers can never frustrate the divine will or escape accountability.
  • A balanced transition combining unyielding truth with generous temporal reprieve.
4

Asylum for Seekers of Truth (Istajaraka)

  • If an enemy combatant seeks protection, grant him asylum immediately without condition.
  • Allow him to hear the recited Word of Allah so truth becomes clear without coercion.
  • Safely escort him to his place of absolute security if he chooses not to embrace faith.

PILLAR 2: TREATY ETHICS & SANCTITY OF COVENANTS

Honoring Unbroken Pacts, Perfidy Rebuked & Maintenance of Sanctuaries

5

Honoring Treaties to Their Appointed Term

  • Tribes who never breached covenants nor aided enemies are strictly protected.
  • Fulfill all agreements until their designated expiration; Allah loves the righteous.
  • Islamic law holds sworn covenants sacred even amidst active international tensions.
6

The Elapse of the Sacred Months (Al-Ashhur al-Hurum)

  • Once the sacred months elapse, confront persistent aggressors who broke sworn pacts.
  • Yet if they sincerely repent, establish regular prayer, and give Zakah, clear their way.
  • Affirming that repentance restores full fraternal equality within the community.
7

Treacherous Disregard of Kinship & Oaths

  • Polytheistic chieftains observe neither kinship ties nor sworn treaties when dominant.
  • They flatter with sweet words while their hearts harbor deep animosity and treachery.
  • Exposing the hypocrisy of those who trade away divine signs for petty worldly advantage.
8

The True Maintainers of the Sacred Sanctuaries

  • It is not for idolaters to maintain the Masajid while witnessing against their own disbelief.
  • True custodianship belongs exclusively to those who believe, pray, give Zakah, and fear Allah alone.
  • Mere blood lineage or providing water to pilgrims cannot equal true faith and spiritual striving.

FALSE SECURITY, THE LESSON OF HUNAYN & THE CAVE

Pillars 3 & 4: Battle of Hunayn, The Pitfall of Numbers, Descent of Sakinah & The Cave of Thawr

[PLATE 02 : HUNAYN & THE CAVE]

PILLAR 3: THE CRUCIBLE OF HUNAYN & VANITY OF NUMBERS

Battle of Hunayn, Illusion of Abundance & Descent of Divine Sakinah

9

The Illusion of Abundance ('Ajabatkum Kathratukum)

  • At Hunayn, 12,000 Muslim warriors took pride in their unprecedented military size.
  • Hawazin's archers ambushed the columns, shattering overconfident human pride.
  • Proving that military mass is worthless without divine assistance and humble hearts.
10

The Constriction of the Vast Earth (Daqat 'Alaykumul-Ard)

  • The battlefield felt suffocatingly narrow despite its vast geographical expanse.
  • Believers retreated in disorder, testing the resilience and sincerity of the ranks.
  • Squeezing the soul until it recognizes that all strength belongs solely to Allah.
11

Descent of Divine Tranquility (As-Sakinah)

  • Allah sent down tranquil composure upon His Messenger and the steadfast believers.
  • Reinforced by invisible angelic legions that turned the tide of conflict.
  • Transforming chaotic rout into decisive victory through divine intervention.
12

Barring Polytheism from the Sacred Sanctuary

  • Idolatrous beliefs are spiritually impure; let them not approach the Ka'bah after this year.
  • Reassuring believers who feared economic loss: Allah will enrich you from His bounty.
  • Material sustenance is guaranteed by the Creator; religious purity must never be compromised.

PILLAR 4: THE CAVE OF THAWR & PROPHETIC RELIANCE

Thani Ithnayn, 'Do Not Grieve' & The Divine Inscription of History

13

If You Do Not Aid Him, Allah Has Already Aided Him

  • Reminding the community that the Prophet's mission does not rely on mortal armies.
  • Allah protected him when disbelievers drove him out with neither troops nor weapons.
  • The divine decree operates independently of human numbers and worldly calculations.
14

The Solitary Two in the Cave (Thani Ithnayn)

  • The Prophet and Abu Bakr sheltered in the dark cave of Thawr, surrounded by trackers.
  • Abu Bakr wept out of concern: 'If one of them looks under his feet, he will see us.'
  • The sublime prophetic response: 'What do you think of two when Allah is their Third?'
15

'Do Not Grieve; Indeed Allah is with Us'

  • La Tahzan inna Allaha Ma'ana: The eternal formula of absolute reliance (Tawakkul).
  • Divine Sakinah descended upon him, backed by unseen heavenly legions.
  • True peace is granted during the height of the trial, not merely after its resolution.
16

The Word of Disbelief Made the Lowest

  • Allah cast the plots and proclamations of the deniers into the lowest abyss.
  • The Word of Allah remains supreme, exalted, and victoriously transcendent.
  • He is the All-Mighty, the All-Wise, governing history with flawless justice.

THE CRUCIBLE OF TABUK & UNMASKING HYPOCRISY

Pillars 5 & 6: The Call to March, Clinging to the Earth, Hypocrisy Unmasked & Mockery Exposed

[PLATE 03 : TABUK & HYPOCRISY]

PILLAR 5: THE CALL TO MARCH & WORLDLY HESITATION

Infiroo Khifafan wa Thiqaalan, Love of Ease & The Divine Reprimand

17

Clinging Heavily to the Earth (Ith-thaqaltum)

  • 'Why is it that when told to march in Allah's cause, you cling heavily to the earth?'
  • Testing attachment: Preferring transient worldly shade over eternal felicity in the Hereafter.
  • The pleasure of this mortal existence is infinitesimally brief compared to the next life.
18

March Forth Light & Heavy (Infiroo Khifafan wa Thiqaalan)

  • Mobilize regardless of worldly condition: young or old, wealthy or poor, busy or free.
  • Strive with your wealth and lives; that is far better for you if you only knew.
  • Overcoming comfort and convenience to answer the call of divine truth.
19

The Pretexts of the Insincere (Law Kana 'Aradan Qareeban)

  • Had it been immediate worldly profit and an easy journey, they would have followed.
  • But the long, scorching distance toward the Byzantine frontier seemed too far.
  • Swearing false oaths: 'Had we been able, we would have marched with you!'
20

Divine Clemency & The Litmus Test ('Afallahu 'Ank)

  • 'May Allah pardon you! Why did you grant them leave before the truthful were distinguished?'
  • A loving divine inquiry highlighting the Prophet's compassionate nature.
  • The crucible of hardship clarifies the sincere from the fabricators of false excuses.

PILLAR 6: THE ANATOMY OF HYPOCRISY (NIFAQ)

Mockery of Revelation, False Oaths & The Sickness of the Inward

21

Those Who Seek Exemption are Those Who Doubt

  • Sincere believers never request exemption from striving with their wealth and lives.
  • Only those whose hearts harbor deep doubt and hesitation ask to be excused.
  • Lingering in uncertainty, their spiritual paralysis prevents righteous action.
22

Subversive Rejoicing at Calamity

  • If good fortune touches the believers, it grieves the hypocrites to their core.
  • If difficulty strikes, they gloat: 'We took our precaution beforehand!' and turn away.
  • Sincere believers reply: 'Nothing will afflict us except what Allah has decreed for us.'
23

Mockery Under the Mask of Jest (Innama Kunna Nal'ab)

  • Exposed mocking the Prophet and Companions, they claim: 'We were only jesting!'
  • Proclaim: 'Was it at Allah, His signs, and His Messenger you were mocking?'
  • 'Make no excuses; you have disbelieved after your belief!' Mockery dissolves faith.
24

Invalidation of Reluctant Charity

  • Their financial donations are utterly rejected and unprofitable in the sight of God.
  • Because they come to prayer only sluggishly and spend in charity only with resentment.
  • Exterior acts of worship are void when disconnected from inward sincerity.

THE SOCIAL ARCHITECTURE OF HYPOCRISY VS TRUE FAITH

Pillars 7 & 8: Networks of Nifaq, Forgetting God, Mutual Alliance of Believers & The 8 Recipients of Zakah

[PLATE 04 : SOCIAL ARCHITECTURE & ZAKAH]

PILLAR 7: THE NETWORK OF HYPOCRISY & FORGETTING GOD

Munafiqoon Enjoin Evil, Withhold Hands & Receive the Divine Curse

25

Homogeneity of the Hypocrites

  • The hypocrite men and hypocrite women are of one another: unified in spiritual pathology.
  • They enjoin what is wrong, forbid what is right, and close their fists in stinginess.
  • A toxic collective network operating to undermine the moral health of society.
26

They Forgot Allah, So He Forgot Them (Nasu Allaha)

  • Deliberately ignoring divine guidance resulted in divine abandonment.
  • The hypocrites are the truly defiant transgressors, alienated from spiritual peace.
  • Stripped of divine light, their souls wander in existential darkness.
27

The Ruined Civilizations of the Past

  • Reminding them of prior nations: 'Ad, Thamud, people of Ibrahim, and Madyan.
  • They were mightier in strength and possessed greater wealth and children, yet were destroyed.
  • They enjoyed their fleeting earthly portion, then vanished into historical disgrace.
28

The Eternal Fire of Jahannam

  • Allah has promised the hypocrites and disbelievers the blazing fire of Hell eternally.
  • An inescapable consequence matching the duplicity and betrayal of their earthly lives.
  • For them is an enduring torment and the total revocation of divine mercy.

PILLAR 8: THE BELIEVING COMMUNITY & THE EIGHT RECIPIENTS OF ZAKAH

Awliya of One Another, Enjoining Right, Gardens of Eden & Zakah Distribution

29

Believers as Mutual Allies (Awliya)

  • Believing men and believing women are protective allies and guardians of one another.
  • They enjoin all that is virtuous, forbid vice, establish prayer, and pay Zakah.
  • Bound by transcendent love and divine purpose, they obey Allah and His Messenger.
30

Divine Mercy & Gardens of Perpetual Bliss ('Adn)

  • Upon this sincere community Allah will shower His boundless, transformational mercy.
  • Promised gardens beneath which rivers flow and glorious dwellings in Gardens of Eden.
  • Eternal peace prepared for souls that remained loyal to their divine pledge.
31

Ridwanullah: The Supreme Achievement

  • Wa Ridwanun min Allahi Akbar: The good pleasure of Allah is greater than all Paradise.
  • Beyond rivers, palaces, and garments, the highest joy is divine acceptance.
  • That is the supreme triumph, where the soul rests eternally in the Lord's love.
32

The Eight Exclusive Recipients of Zakah

  • Innamas-Sadaqatu: Zakah is divinely allocated exclusively across eight categories.
  • The poor (Fuqara), needy (Masakeen), administrators, reconciled hearts, freeing captives.
  • The debt-burdened, in the cause of Allah, and the stranded wayfarer; an ordained decree.

THE SINS OF MISERLINESS, TAUNTING CHARITY & FALSE SANCTUARIES

Pillars 9 & 10: Broken Pledges of Wealth, Taunting the Poor & The Subversion of Masjid ad-Dirar

[PLATE 05 : WEALTH & FALSE SANCTUARIES]

PILLAR 9: THE BROKEN PLEDGES OF THE MISERLY

Pledging to Spend, Backtracking in Greed & Inward Hypocrisy Seeded

33

The False Vow: 'If He Enriches Us, We Will Give Charity'

  • Those who swore solemnly that if Allah granted them wealth, they would be righteous spenders.
  • Yet when divine bounty enriched them, they grew stingy and turned away defiantly.
  • Worldly abundance exposing the hollow deceit of their pre-wealth declarations.
34

Hypocrisy Sealed in the Heart (Fa A'qabahum Nifaqan)

  • Because they broke their promise to Allah and lied, hypocrisy was sealed in their hearts.
  • A permanent spiritual disease festering within until the Day they meet their Creator.
  • Warning that broken vows to God alter the inward spiritual reality irrevocably.
35

Mocking Generous Donors & Sneering at the Poor

  • Hypocrites sneer at wealthy believers who give large donations, accusing them of show.
  • And when the poor donate their humble day's labor, the hypocrites mock their meager gift.
  • Allah mocks their arrogance, and for them is an excruciating, humiliating punishment.
36

Banning Funeral Prayer for the Arch-Hypocrite

  • Commanded never to pray the funeral prayer over deceased hypocrites who died in defiance.
  • Nor stand beside their graves; their outward claims cannot sanitize inward rebellion.
  • Demarcating the ultimate boundary between sincere faith and obstinate duplicity.

PILLAR 10: THE MOSQUE OF SUBVERSION (MASJID AD-DIRAR)

Counterfeit Sanctuaries, Sowing Discord & The Mosque Founded on Taqwa

37

The Counterfeit Mosque of Subversion

  • Built by hypocrites intending harm, disbelief, and division among the Muslim ranks.
  • Serving as an intelligence outpost for hostile forces plotting against Islam.
  • Cloaking treacherous sectarian sabotage in the guise of religious devotion.
38

False Oaths of Benevolent Intent

  • The conspirators swear solemnly: 'We intended nothing but the absolute best and charity!'
  • Yet Allah testifies that they are unvarnished, deceitful liars.
  • Exposing that religious structures built on malicious intent are abhorrent to God.
39

'Never Stand to Pray Within It!' (La Taqum Feehi Abada)

  • The Prophet is commanded never to enter or pray within that subversive structure.
  • Its foundations are built upon the crumbling brink of a precipice falling into Hell.
  • Demolished and burned to preserve the sacred cohesion of the believing community.
40

The Sanctuary Founded Upon Piety (Masjid Quba)

  • A mosque founded upon God-consciousness from its first day is truly worthy of prayer.
  • Within it are believers who love to purify themselves physically and spiritually.
  • And Allah loves those who continuously cleanse their hearts, bodies, and intentions.

[PLATE 06 : THE DIVINE PURCHASE]

PILLAR 11: THE DIVINE PURCHASE OF LIVES & WEALTH

Inna Allaha-shtara, The Covenant of Scripture & The Nine Transcendent Hallmarks

41

The Supreme Commerce: Lives & Wealth for Jannah

  • Inna Allaha-shtara: Allah has purchased from the believers their lives and their wealth.
  • Decreeing in exchange the ultimate price: eternal inheritance of the Gardens of Paradise.
  • A sacred transaction transferring mortal selfhood into divine custodianship.
42

The Tripartite Covenant of Truth

  • A binding promise inscribed in truth across the Torah, the Gospel, and the Holy Qur'an.
  • Who is truer to his covenant than the Creator of the heavens and the earth?
  • An unshakeable divine guarantee celebrated across all authentic prophetic dispensations.
43

'Rejoice in the Bargain You Have Struck!'

  • Fastabshiroo bi-bay'ikum: Rejoice in the magnificent contract you have concluded.
  • That is indeed the supreme, incomparable triumph (Al-Fawz al-'Azeem).
  • Transforming human struggle into eternal celebration in the divine presence.
44

The Nine Hallmarks of the Redeemed

  • The repentant, worshippers, praisers, journeyers, the bowing, and the prostrating.
  • Enforcers of virtue, restrainers of evil, and vigilant guardians of divine limits.
  • Give glad tidings to these sincere believers who embody this comprehensive rectitude.

PILLAR 12: SEPARATION OF TIES & THE EXAMPLE OF IBRAHIM

Prohibition of Istighfar for Polytheists & Ibrahim's Conditional Promise

45

Prohibition of Praying for Unrepentant Polytheists

  • It is not for the Prophet and believers to pray for forgiveness for idolatrous kin.
  • Once it has become evident that they died upon rejection as companions of Hell.
  • Loyalty to the divine covenant takes precedence over biological sentimentality.
46

Ibrahim's Supplication: A Conditional Promise

  • Ibrahim sought forgiveness for his father Azar only because of an explicit prior vow.
  • But when it became manifest that Azar was an enemy to Allah, he disassociated from him.
  • Clarifying prophetic behavior: absolute submission to Tawhid eclipses family ties.
47

Ibrahim: Tender-Hearted & Forbearing (Awwahun Haleem)

  • Despite his unwavering monotheistic stance, Ibrahim's heart was intensely compassionate.
  • Awwah: One who sighs frequently out of deep reverent sorrow, love, and fear of God.
  • Combining intellectual strength and theological clarity with emotional tenderness.
48

Divine Justice: Never Misleading After Guidance

  • Allah would never cause a people to stray after guiding them until He clarifies all boundaries.
  • Accountability is predicated upon clear, uncorrupted knowledge and fair warning.
  • Indeed, Allah is Knower of all things, ruling creation with immaculate equity.

THE TRIAL OF TABUK & THE THREE WHO STAYED BEHIND

[PLATE 07 : TAWBAH & THE THREE]

PILLAR 13: THE AGONY OF THE THREE & THE CONSTRICTED EARTH

Ath-Thalathat alladhina Khullifoo, Boycott of Madinah & The Suffocation of Souls

49

Divine Pardon for the Prophet, Muhajiroon & Ansar

  • Allah pardoned the Prophet, Emigrants, and Helpers who followed in the hour of hardship.
  • When the hearts of a faction nearly wavered under the immense trial of Tabuk.
  • He accepted their repentance; indeed, He is to them full of kindness and mercy.
50

The Three Who Were Left Behind (Ka'b, Hilal, Murarah)

  • Sincere believers who had no excuse yet refused to fabricate lies like the hypocrites.
  • Their case was deferred: subject to a complete social boycott for fifty agonizing days.
  • Choosing painful worldly truth over the transient comfort of a fraudulent excuse.
51

The Vast Earth Constricted (Daqat 'Alayhimul-Ard)

  • The earth grew suffocatingly narrow despite its vastness; their own souls felt alien.
  • Cut off from community, ignored by friends, and tested by foreign royal temptations.
  • An intense spiritual crucible designed to cleanse every trace of complacency.
52

No Refuge from Allah Except Unto Him

  • The profound realization of absolute Tawhid: Wa zannoo an la malja'a min Allahi illa ilayh.
  • You cannot flee from God; you can only flee to God in broken, weeping contrition.
  • The essential breakthrough that unlocks the gates of transformative divine mercy.

PILLAR 14: THE DIVINE ACCEPTANCE OF REPENTANCE

Thumma Taba 'Alayhim li-Yatooboo, Truthfulness & The Inscribed Hardships

53

He Turned to Them That They Might Repent

  • Thumma taba 'alayhim li-yatooboo: Divine grace precedes and enables human repentance.
  • At dawn after fifty days, revelation descended announcing their total forgiveness.
  • The Prophet's face shone like the full moon as he gave Ka'b the joyous news.
54

Accompany the Truthful (Koonoo Ma'as-Sadiqeen)

  • 'O you who have believed, fear Allah and align yourselves always with the truthful.'
  • Truthfulness in speech, action, and covenant is the bedrock of moral survival.
  • The enduring lesson of Ka'b: Truth brings temporary trial but eternal salvation.
55

Loyalty to the Person of the Messenger

  • It was never fitting for the people of Madinah to lag behind the Messenger of Allah.
  • Nor to prefer their own mortal lives, comfort, or safety over his sacred mission.
  • The standard of prophetic companionship requires selfless devotion and sacrifice.
56

Every Thirst, Fatigue & Step Recorded

  • No thirst, hunger, or exhaustion afflicts them in Allah's cause except it is inscribed.
  • Every step that challenges oppression and every spent coin is written as a righteous deed.
  • Allah will reward them for the very best of what they accomplished with sincerity.

SCHOLARSHIP, INNER PURIFICATION & PROPHETIC COMPASSION

PILLAR 15: THE EXPEDITION OF SACRED KNOWLEDGE

Tafaqquh fid-Deen, Admonishing Communities & The Litmus Test of New Surahs

57

Civilizational Mandate: Tafaqquh fid-Deen

  • It is not proper for all believers to go forth simultaneously to physical battle.
  • A dedicated contingent must remain to gain deep, verified understanding of religion.
  • Establishing scholarship and legal knowledge as an indispensable pillar of society.
58

Admonishing Communities Upon Return

  • Scholars and jurists are commissioned to instruct and guide returning communities.
  • Warning them of spiritual pitfalls and grounding society in verified divine ethics.
  • Knowledge is not an ivory-tower indulgence, but an active communal protection.
59

New Revelation as a Litmus Test of Hearts

  • Whenever a new Surah is revealed, the hypocrites mock: 'Whom did this increase in faith?'
  • For the believers, it dynamically increases their faith and they rejoice in guidance.
  • For those with diseased hearts, it adds filth upon filth, dying in stubborn denial.
60

The Blindness of Chronic Disregard

  • Do they not see they are tested with trials once or twice every single year?
  • Yet they neither turn in repentance nor do they take heed and remember.
  • Turning away from truth, Allah turns their hearts because they refuse to comprehend.

PILLAR 16: THE PROPHETIC HEART & THE SUFFICIENCY OF GOD

Azeezun 'Alayhi Ma 'Anittum, Ra'oofun Raheem & The Fortress of Hasbiyallah

61

A Messenger from Among Yourselves (Min Anfusikum)

  • A noble Messenger has arrived from within your human reality, walking among you.
  • Familiar with your trials, flaws, and hopes, embodying perfection of prophetic adab.
  • Bridging divine transcendence and earthly existence with supreme moral beauty.
62

Pained by Your Hardships ('Azeezun 'Alayhi)

  • 'Azeezun 'alayhi ma 'anittum: He feels profound internal anguish over whatever harms you.
  • Any physical difficulty, moral failure, or spiritual torment that afflicts you pains his soul.
  • The supreme empathy of a shepherd who weeps for the vulnerability of his flock.
63

Ardently Devoted & Full of Mercy (Ra'oofun Raheem)

  • Hareesun 'alaykum: Intensely, passionately concerned for your salvation and guidance.
  • Bestowed two of Allah's own names: Ra'oof (Tenderly Pitying) and Raheem (Merciful).
  • A boundless fountain of love and gentleness toward all who believe in the truth.
64

The Ultimate Fortress: Hasbiyallah

  • If the entire world turns away in rejection, stand firm upon this solitary mountain:
  • 'Hasbiyallahu la ilaha illa Huwa, 'alayhi tawakkaltu wa Huwa Rabbul-'Arshil-'Azeem!'
  • Allah alone suffices; upon Him I rely, and He is the Lord of the Magnificent Throne.
