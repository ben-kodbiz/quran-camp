# Surah At-Talaq: Master Mindmap Knowledge Architecture
### 16:9 Landscape Cartography • 8 Plates • 16 Thematic Pillars • 64 Analytical Cards
**Source Authority:** Classical Sunni Exegesis (Tabari, Ibn Kathir, Qurtubi, Razi, Baghawi, Ibn al-Qayyim)
**Operating Principle:** Read. Reflect. Return.

---

## Plate 01: SACRED PROTOCOL OF MARITAL DISSOLUTION
**Section:** `PLATE 01 : MARITAL PROTOCOL` | **Focus:** Pillars 01 & 02: Divorcing at Prescribed Periods, Counting the 'Iddah, Do Not Evict Wives & Sacred Boundaries

### PILLAR 01: THE PRECISE LITURGICAL TIMING
*Idha Tallaqtumu an-Nisa'a fa-Talliqoohunna li-'Iddatihinna, Ahsoo al-'Iddah*

#### [01] The Sovereign Direction to the Prophet
- Ya ayyuha an-nabiyyu idha tallaqtumu an-nisa'a: O Prophet, when you divorce women, divorce at their term.

#### [02] Divorcing During Purity Without Intimacy
- Divorcing strictly during a period of ritual purity (Tuhr) in which no marital intimacy took place.

#### [03] The Sacred Duty of Counting the 'Iddah
- Wa ahsoo al-'iddah: and calculate the waiting period with meticulous precision to protect lineage.

#### [04] Reverence for the Sustainer
- Wattaqoo Allaha Rabbakum: and maintain awe of Allah your Lord throughout all domestic disputes.

### PILLAR 02: THE PROHIBITION OF DOMESTIC EVICTION
*La Tukhrijoohunna min Buyootihinna, Nor Shall They Leave & Transgressing Bounds*

#### [05] Absolute Sanctuary of the Marital Home
- La tukhrijoohunna min buyootihinna: do not evict them from their homes during the waiting period.

#### [06] The Wife's Right to Remain Sheltered
- Wa la yakhrujna: nor should they depart voluntarily; the home remains their protective sanctuary.

#### [07] The Sole Exception: Proven Indecency
- Illa an ya'teena bi-fahishatin mubayyinah: except if they commit clear, manifest moral indecency.

#### [08] The Sacred Boundaries of Allah (Hudood)
- Wa tilka hudoodu Allahi: those are Allah's limits; whoever transgresses them wrongs his own soul.

## Plate 02: THE CRITICAL THRESHOLD & TWO JUST WITNESSES
**Section:** `PLATE 02 : CRITICAL THRESHOLD` | **Focus:** Pillars 03 & 04: Reaching Term: Retaining in Kindness or Releasing Honourably, Two Just Witnesses & Sincere Admonition

### PILLAR 03: THE BINARY AT THE APPOINTED TERM
*Fa-Idha Balaghna Ajalahunna, Retain in Kindness (Ma'roof) or Separate in Kindness*

#### [09] Approaching the Term's Conclusion
- Fa-idha balaghna ajalahunna: and when they have almost reached the end of their prescribed period.

#### [10] The First Option: Retaining in Honour
- Fa-amsikoohunna bi-ma'roof: retain them in recognized honour, mutual respect, and restored love.

#### [11] The Second Option: Honourable Parting
- Aw fariqoohunna bi-ma'roof: or separate from them in dignity, generosity, and complete absence of malice.

#### [12] Eradicating Domestic Spite
- Barring men from holding wives in emotional limbo or abusing legal loopholes to inflict psychological harm.

### PILLAR 04: THE REQUIREMENT OF TWO JUST WITNESSES
*Ash-hidoo Dhaway 'Adlin Minkum, Establish Testimony for Allah & Sincere Counsel*

#### [13] Formal Legal Institutionalization
- Wa ash-hidoo dhaway 'adlin minkum: and call to witness two just, upright men from among your community.

#### [14] Witnessing Both Return and Separation
- Securing public legal clarity whether the marriage is restored or formally dissolved, preventing fraud.

#### [15] Establishing Testimony Solely for Allah
- Wa aqeemoo ash-shahadata lillah: and establish the testimony purely for Allah, free from bias or bribery.

#### [16] Admonition for Believers in Eternity
- Dhalikum yoo'adhu bihi man kana yu'minu billahi wal-yawmi al-akhir: counsel for whoever believes in Allah and the Last Day.

## Plate 03: THE GOLDEN AXIOM OF DELIVERANCE & PROVISION
**Section:** `PLATE 03 : DELIVERANCE & TAWAKKUL` | **Focus:** Pillars 05 & 06: Wa Man Yattaqi Allaha Yaj'al Lahu Makhraja, Sustenance Beyond Calculation, Tawakkul & Qadr

### PILLAR 05: THE DIVINE WAY OUT FROM BLIND ALLEYS
*Wa Man Yattaqi Allaha Yaj'al Lahu Makhraja, Breaking Deadlocks & Unforeseen Provision*

#### [17] The Supreme Promise to the God-Conscious
- Wa man yattaqi Allaha yaj'al lahu makhraja: and whoever fears Allah—He will create for him a miraculous way out!

#### [18] Deliverance from Financial & Emotional Traps
- When domestic crisis, divorce, or financial collapse seems hopeless, Taqwa unlocks unseen exits.

#### [19] Sustenance Beyond All Calculations
- Wa yarzuqhu min haythu la yahtasib: and He will provide for him from sources he could never imagine!

#### [20] Rewarding Uncompromising Integrity
- Proving that choosing ethical righteousness over deceitful advantage always yields divine abundance.

### PILLAR 06: TOTAL SUFFICIENCY IN TAWAKKUL
*Wa Man Yatawakkal 'ala Allahi fa-Huwa Hasbuh, Inna Allaha Balighu Amrih & Qadr*

#### [21] Allah as Absolute Sufficiency
- Wa man yatawakkal 'ala Allahi fa-huwa hasbuh: and whoever places his reliance upon Allah—He is all-sufficient for him!

#### [22] The Unstoppable Execution of Divine Will
- Inna Allaha balighu amrih: indeed, Allah will inevitably accomplish and execute His sovereign purpose.

#### [23] The Ordained Measure for Everything
- Qad ja'ala Allahu li-kulli shay'in qadra: Allah has already set for everything a precise, appointed measure.

#### [24] Patience Within Decreed Timelines
- Surrendering anxiety, knowing that every grief, hardship, and trial has a predetermined expiration date.

## Plate 04: TAXONOMY OF THE WAITING PERIOD ('IDDAH)
**Section:** `PLATE 04 : TAXONOMY OF IDDAH` | **Focus:** Pillars 07 & 08: Post-Menopausal Women, Those Without Menses, Pregnant Women Deliver & Ease in Hardship

### PILLAR 07: SPECIALIZED WAITING PERIODS CLARIFIED
*Allati Ya'isna min al-Maheed, Three Months & Those Who Have Not Menstruated*

#### [25] The Inquiry of the Companions
- When the three menstrual cycles of Al-Baqarah were revealed, Ubayy ibn Ka'b asked about non-menstruating women.

#### [26] Post-Menopausal Women ('Iddah)
- Wallati ya'isna mina al-maheedi min nisa'ikum: those who have despaired of menstruation—their term is three months.

#### [27] Women with Irregular or Absent Menses
- Wallati lam yahidna: and those who have not yet menstruated or have amenorrhea—their term is likewise three months.

#### [28] Eliminating Ambiguity in Marital Law
- Establishing clear, objective temporal benchmarks to protect paternity, financial rights, and emotional closure.

### PILLAR 08: THE PREGNANT WOMAN & PROMISES OF EASE
*Wa Ulatul-Ahmali Ajaluhunna an Yada'na Hamlahunn, Ease in Command & Expiation*

#### [29] The Waiting Period of Pregnant Women
- Wa ulatu al-ahmali ajaluhunna an yada'na hamlahunn: and for pregnant women, their term ends when they deliver.

#### [30] Immediate Conclusion Upon Childbirth
- Whether delivery occurs hours after divorce or months later, giving birth instantly terminates the 'Iddah.

#### [31] Facilitating Life Through God-Consciousness
- Wa man yattaqi Allaha yaj'al lahu min amrihi yusra: and whoever fears Allah—He will make his matter easy for him!

#### [32] Expiating Evils and Magnifying Rewards
- Dhalika amru Allahi: whoever heeds Allah's command will have sins expiated and rewards multiplied manifold.

## Plate 05: HOUSING MANDATES & REVENUE PROPORTIONS
**Section:** `PLATE 05 : HOUSING & MAINTENANCE` | **Focus:** Pillars 09 & 10: Askinoohunna min Haythu Sakantum, Do Not Harass, Financial Support for Pregnant Wives & Nursing

### PILLAR 09: THE MANDATORY HOUSING STANDARD
*Askinoohunna min Haythu Sakantum min Wujdikum, Do Not Harm to Constrict*

#### [33] Shelter Commensurate with Living Standards
- Askinoohunna min haythu sakantum min wujdikum: lodge them where you dwell, according to your means.

#### [34] Strict Prohibition of Harassment
- Wa la tudarroohunna li-tudayyiqoo 'alayhinn: and do not harass them in order to make their living conditions unbearable.

#### [35] Protecting Vulnerable Divorced Women
- Banning passive-aggressive domestic cruelty designed to force the woman to abandon her shelter prematurely.

#### [36] The Dignity of Continuous Maintenance
- Ensuring that separation does not degrade the material standard of living she enjoyed during matrimony.

### PILLAR 10: FULL MAINTENANCE FOR PREGNANT MOTHERS
*Wa In Kunna Ulati Hamlin fa-Anfiqoo 'Alayhinn, Breastfeeding Compensation & Consultation*

#### [37] Compulsory Support for Expectant Mothers
- Wa in kunna ulati hamlin fa-anfiqoo 'alayhinna hatta yada'na hamlahunn: spend on pregnant wives until birth.

#### [38] Fair Compensation for Nursing Mothers
- Fa-in arda'na lakum fa-atoohunna ujoorahunn: and if they breastfeed your child after divorce, pay their due wages.

#### [39] Mutual Consultation in Goodness (Ma'roof)
- Wa'tamiroo baynakum bi-ma'roof: and consult together in recognized goodness and mutual goodwill.

#### [40] Contingency for Impasse: Wet Nurses
- Wa in ta'asartum fa-saturdi'u lahu ukhra: if you encounter difficult friction, another woman may breastfeed.

## Plate 06: CAPACITY-BASED SPENDING & HARDSHIP TRANSCENDED
**Section:** `PLATE 06 : CAPACITY & EASE` | **Focus:** Pillars 11 & 12: Li-Yunfiq Dhu Sa'atin min Sa'atih, Spending According to Divine Provision, Ease After Hardship

### PILLAR 11: EXPENDITURE COMMENSURATE TO WEALTH
*Li-Yunfiq Dhu Sa'ah, The Constrained Spends from What Allah Gave & No Burden*

#### [41] The Affluent Must Spend Proportionately
- Li-yunfiq dhu sa'atin min sa'atih: let the man of vast means spend generously according to his wealth.

#### [42] The Realism for the Impoverished
- Wa man qudira 'alayhi rizquhu: and whoever has his provision restricted, let him spend from what Allah granted him.

#### [43] No Soul Burdened Beyond Divine Gifts
- La yukallifu Allahu nafsan illa ma ataha: Allah never tasks any soul beyond that which He has bestowed upon it.

#### [44] Protecting Both Spouses from Injustice
- Preventing wives from demanding impossible luxury, and preventing wealthy husbands from pleading artificial poverty.

### PILLAR 12: THE GUARANTEE OF EASE AFTER HARDSHIP
*Sayaj'alu Allahu Ba'da 'Usrin Yusra, Temporal Nature of Crisis & Divine Guarantee*

#### [45] The Infallible Sovereign Guarantee
- Sayaj'alu Allahu ba'da 'usrin yusra: Allah will soon bring about, after intense hardship, expansive ease!

#### [46] Hardship Bound by Two Eases
- Echoing Surah Ash-Sharh: distress is temporary, fragile, and inevitably overtaken by divine relief.

#### [47] Mental Solace in Financial Strain
- Comforting families undergoing divorce or poverty that their current constriction is not their permanent destiny.

#### [48] Anchoring Hope in the Sustainer
- Teaching the faithful to look through the storm of immediate difficulty toward the promised dawn of relief.

## Plate 07: THE RUINS OF REBELLIOUS TOWNSHIPS
**Section:** `PLATE 07 : REBELLIOUS TOWNSHIPS` | **Focus:** Pillars 13 & 14: How Many Cities Rebelled, Severe Accounting, Tasting Catastrophic Fruit & Prepared Severe Doom

### PILLAR 13: THE HISTORICAL RETRIBUTION OF ARROGANCE
*Wa Ka'ayyin min Qaryatin 'Atat, Rebellious Against Lord and Messengers, Stern Accounting*

#### [49] The Warning from Vanished Empires
- Wa ka'ayyin min qaryatin 'atat 'an amri Rabbiha wa rusulih: how many townships insolently rebelled against their Lord!

#### [50] The Rigorous Sovereign Audit
- Fa-hasabnaha hisaban shadeedan: so We called them to a severe, unsparing accounting for their corruption.

#### [51] Terrible Unheard-of Chastisement
- Wa 'adhdhabnaha 'adhaban nukra: and We punished them with an unprecedented, dreadful chastisement.

#### [52] Domestic Law Linked to Civilizational Health
- Connecting marital justice directly to the survival of civilizations: moral rot in homes destroys whole cities.

### PILLAR 14: TASTING THE BITTER HARVEST OF DEFIANCE
*Fa-Dhaqat Wabala Amriha, The End Was Utter Loss & Severe Torment Prepared*

#### [53] Tasting the Evil Fruit of Rebellion
- Fa-dhaqat wabala amriha: so each rebellious society tasted the catastrophic consequence of its conduct.

#### [54] The Final Outcome: Total Perdition
- Wa kana 'aqibatu amriha khusra: and the ultimate culmination of their arrogant affair was absolute loss!

#### [55] Prepared Chastisement for Deniers
- A'adda Allahu lahum 'adhaban shadeedan: Allah has prepared for them a crushing, inescapable punishment.

#### [56] The Call to Men of Understanding
- Fattaqoo Allaha ya olee al-albabi alladhina amanoo: so fear Allah, O people of profound intellect who believe!

## Plate 08: THE GUIDING APOSTLE & THE SEVEN HEAVENS
**Section:** `PLATE 08 : SEVEN HEAVENS` | **Focus:** Pillars 15 & 16: Sending a Reminder, Messenger Bringing from Darkness to Light, Seven Heavens & Encompassing Knowledge

### PILLAR 15: THE APOSTLE OF LIGHT & IMMORTAL REWARD
*Qad Anzala Allahu Lakum Dhikra, Rasoolan Yatloo Ayatillah, Darkness to Light*

#### [57] The Descent of the Living Reminder
- Qad anzala Allahu ilaykum dhikra: Allah has indeed sent down unto you an awakening Reminder (Scripture).

#### [58] The Messenger Reciting Manifest Signs
- Rasoolan yatloo 'alaykum ayati Allahi mubayyinat: an apostle reciting clear divine signs of justice and truth.

#### [59] Liberation from Darkness into Radiant Light
- Li-yukhrija alladhina amanoo wa 'amiloo as-salihati mina adh-dhulumati ila an-noor: guiding from shadows to light.

#### [60] Perpetual Abodes with Flowing Springs
- Admitting believers into eternal paradises beneath which rivers flow: Qad ahsana Allahu lahu rizqa!

### PILLAR 16: COSMIC ARCHITECTURE & ENCOMPASSING KNOWLEDGE
*Seven Heavens and of the Earth Like Them, Command Descends & Knowing All Things*

#### [61] The Sevenfold Multiverse Architecture
- Allahu alladhi khalaqa sab'a samawatin wa mina al-ardi mithlahunn: Allah created seven heavens and of the earth like them.

#### [62] The Continuous Descent of Cosmic Decrees
- Yatanazzalu al-amru baynahunna: the divine command continuously descends through their immense cosmic strata.

#### [63] Omnipotence Verified by Cosmology
- Li-ta'lamoo anna Allaha 'ala kulli shay'in Qadeer: that you may know with absolute certainty that Allah is Omnipotent!

#### [64] Total Knowledge Encompassing Creation
- Wa anna Allaha qad ahata bi-kulli shay'in 'ilma: and that Allah has completely encompassed all things in uncreated knowledge!
