Plate 01: THE CALL TO ACTION & SEVEN ETHICAL COMMANDS

Pillars 01 & 02: Ya Ayyuha al-Muddaththir, Qum fa-Andhir, Purifying Garments & Shunning Idols

PLATE 01 : THE APOSTOLIC CALL
PILLAR 01: THE AWAKENING TO PUBLIC MISSION
Ya Ayyuha al-Muddaththir, Qum fa-Andhir & Wa Rabbaka fa-Kabbir
01 The Cloaked One Addressed
  • Ya ayyuha al-muddaththir: O you wrapped in your cloak! The divine voice summoning the Prophet to global mission.
02 Arise and Warn Humanity
  • Qum fa-andhir: arise from restful seclusion and sound the alarm of warning against spiritual ruin.
03 Magnify Your Lord Alone
  • Wa Rabbaka fa-kabbir: declare the absolute supreme greatness of your Lord above all false idols and empires.
04 Transition from Seclusion to Action
  • Marking the monumental turning point where Prophethood (Nubuwwah) transitioned into active Apostolic Mission (Risalah).
PILLAR 02: THE PROTOCOLS OF MORAL PURIFICATION
Wa Thiyabaka fa-Tahhir, War-Rujza fahjur & Zero Self-Righteousness
05 Purify Your Garments and Conduct
  • Wa thiyabaka fa-tahhir: purify your outer clothes, physical body, and inner moral character from all filth.
06 Shun All Idolatry and Defilement
  • War-rujza fahjur: completely abandon and keep far away from all pagan idols, sins, and spiritual impurities.
07 Give Not Seeking Increase (Tastakthir)
  • Wa la tamnun tastakthir: do not confer favors or give charity with the selfish motive of demanding greater return.
08 Patience Solely for Your Lord
  • Wa li-Rabbika fasbir: anchor your steadfast perseverance purely for the sake of your Lord through trials.

Plate 02: THE TRUMPET BLAST & THE UNGRATEFUL OLIGARCH

Pillars 03 & 04: The Nakir Blast, Walid ibn al-Mughirah, Ample Wealth & Spreading Paths of Ease

PLATE 02 : OLIGARCH & TRUMPET
PILLAR 03: THE SEVERE DAY OF THE TRUMPET
Fa-idha Nuqira fee an-Naqoor, Yawmun 'Aseer & Not Easy for Deniers
09 When the Trumpet is Decisively Sounded
  • Fa-idha nuqira fee an-naqoor: when the cosmic horn is blown to initiate the final resurrection.
10 A Day of Unrelenting Hardship
  • Fa-dhalika yawma'idhin yawmun 'aseer: that Day will be an intensely difficult, harrowing time for the wicked.
11 Far from Easy for Disbelievers
  • 'Ala al-kafireena ghayru yaseer: devoid of any ease, comfort, or leniency for those who mocked revelation.
12 The Leveling of All Worldly Rank
  • Where Meccan pedigree, slave ownership, and aristocratic dominance count for absolute zero.
PILLAR 04: LEAVE ME WITH WHOM I CREATED ALONE
Dharnee wa Man Khalaqtu Waheeda, Mamdood Wealth & Present Sons
13 Created Alone from the Womb
  • Leave Me to deal with the one whom I created solitary, naked, and destitute from his mother's womb.
14 Bestowed Extensive Flowing Wealth
  • And granted him sprawling, abundant riches (Malan Mamdoodan) stretching across Mecca and Ta'if.
15 Surrounded by Prominent Sons
  • And blessed him with sons constantly present at his side (Baneena Shuhooda) ensuring honor.
16 Paved for Him Smooth Paths of Ease
  • Wa mahhadtu lahu tamheeda: smoothed out for him every pathway of worldly luxury, prestige, and power.

Plate 03: THE FATAL DELIBERATION & THE SCORCHING SAQAR

Pillars 05 & 06: He Thought and Plotted, May He Be Destroyed, Frowning & Sa-Usleehi Saqar

PLATE 03 : SAQAR & CONSPIRACY
PILLAR 05: THE CONSPIRACY OF WALID IBN AL-MUGHIRAH
Fakkara wa Qaddar, Qutila Kayfa Qaddar, 'Abasa wa Basar
17 Greedily Demanding More Wealth
  • Yet after all these gifts, he greedily expects that I should give him even greater increase!
18 Obstinate Enemy to Our Signs
  • Kalla: never! Indeed, he has been an obstinate, stubborn opponent to Our divine verses and proofs.
19 Exhausting Ascent on Fiery Rock
  • Sa-urhiquhu sa'ooda: I will compel him to climb an exhausting, slippery mountain of fire in Hell.
20 The Toxic Deliberation: He Plotted
  • Innahu fakkara wa qaddar: he calculated, deliberated, frowned, scowled, and claimed: 'This is ancient magic!'
PILLAR 06: THE INFERNO OF SAQAR
Sa-Usleehi Saqar, La Tubqee wa la Tadhar & Lawwahatun lil-Bashar
21 I Will Plunge Him into Saqar
  • Sa-usleehi Saqar: I will cast him into the scorching abyss of Saqar to experience the consequence of his arrogance.
22 What Can Make You Know What Saqar Is?
  • And what can convey to you the terrifying reality of the infernal furnace of Saqar?
23 It Spares Nothing and Leaves Nothing
  • La tubqee wa la tadhar: it devours flesh, bone, and organs, sparing nothing and leaving no trace unburnt.
24 Blackening and Scorching Human Skin
  • Lawwahatun lil-bashar: scorching and charring the human skin until it turns pitch-black, visible from afar.

Plate 04: THE NINETEEN ANGELIC WARDENS & COSMIC OATHS

Pillars 07 & 08: 'Alayha Tis'ata 'Ashar, The Trial of the Number 19 & Oaths by Moon and Night

PLATE 04 : THE NINETEEN WARDENS
PILLAR 07: THE NINETEEN GUARDS & THE TRIAL OF FAITH
'Alayha Tis'ata 'Ashar, Angels as Guardians & Testing Sick Hearts
25 Over It Are Nineteen Angelic Guards
  • 'Alayha tis'ata 'ashar: posted over Saqar are nineteen colossal, merciless angelic wardens.
26 Guards Created Solely from Angels
  • We appointed none but angels as keepers of the Fire, whose strength surpasses all mortal conception.
27 The Number as a Multi-Tiered Trial
  • We made their specific number (19) a trial to confound disbelievers and strengthen the certainty of the People of the Book.
28 None Knows the Armies of God But He
  • Wa ma ya'lamu junooda Rabbika illa Huwa: none comprehends the limitless legions of your Lord except He alone.
PILLAR 08: THE TRIPLE OATH: MOON, NIGHT & DAWN
Wal-Qamar, Wal-Layli idh Adbar, Was-Subhi idha Asfar & Akbar Cataclysm
29 Swearing by the Luminous Moon
  • Kalla wal-qamar: nay, I swear by the radiant moon that illuminates the dark night skies.
30 By the Night as It Departs
  • Wal-layli idh adbar: and by the receding night as it withdraws its shadowy veil at dawn.
31 By the Dawn as It Radiates Light
  • Was-subhi idha asfar: and by the morning as its golden brilliance shines across creation.
32 One of the Greatest Realities (Kubara)
  • Innaha la-ihda al-kubar: truly, Saqar and the Day of Judgment are among the greatest, most monumental realities.

Plate 05: EVERY SOUL PLEDGED & WHY WE ARE IN SAQAR

Pillars 09 & 10: Kullu Nafsin bi-Ma Kasabat Raheenah, As-hab al-Yameen & The Four Confessions of Hell

PLATE 05 : PLEDGED SOULS
PILLAR 09: EVERY SOUL PLEDGED FOR ITS DEEDS
Kullu Nafsin bi-Ma Kasabat Raheenah, Illa As-hab al-Yameen
33 Every Soul is Held in Collateral
  • Kullu nafsin bi-ma kasabat raheenah: every human soul is held hostage and pledged for what it earned in life.
34 The Glorious Exemption of the Right Hand
  • Except the Companions of the Right (As-hab al-Yameen), whose faith and righteous deeds have ransomed them.
35 Dwelling in Peaceful Celestial Gardens
  • Fee jannatin yatasa'aloon: residing in serene gardens, conversing and inquiring about the guilty criminals.
36 The Interrogation Directed to the Condemned
  • Looking down into the abyss and demanding: 'What has led you into the searing furnace of Saqar?'
PILLAR 10: THE FOUR DEADLY CONFESSIONS
Lam Naku minal-Musalleen, Lam Naku Nut'imu al-Miskeen & Wading in Vanity
37 We Were Not of Those Who Prayed
  • Qaloo lam naku minal-musalleen: their first confession—we never established devoted prayer to our Maker.
38 Nor Did We Feed the Starving Destitute
  • Wa lam naku nut'imu al-miskeen: we lacked social empathy and stone-heartedly refused to feed the hungry.
39 We Used to Wade with the Vain Dabblers
  • Wa kunna nakhoodu ma'al-kha'ideen: we actively joined cynical crowds in mocking, frivolous debates against truth.
40 We Denied the Day of Recompense
  • Wa kunna nukadh-dhibu bi-yawmi ad-deen: we derisively denied the Day of Judgment until the certainty of death arrived.

Pillars 11 & 12: No Intercession Avails, Fleeing from Lion Like Wild Donkeys & Demanding Open Scrolls

PLATE 06 : FRIGHTENED ZEBRAS
PILLAR 11: THE FUTILITY OF FALSE INTERCESSION
Fa-ma Tanfa'uhum Shafa'atu ash-Shafi'een & Turning from Dhikr

  • Fa-ma tanfa'uhum shafa'atu ash-shafi'een: no mediation by prophets, angels, or righteous can rescue the obstinate denier.
42 Why Do They Turn Away from the Reminder?
  • What is the matter with them that they turn away in blind terror from this divine admonition?
43 Total Disconnect from Wisdom
  • Fleeing from the very scripture that was sent to grant them spiritual life, salvation, and eternal peace.
44 The Tragic Perversity of Rejection
  • Choosing the fiery ruins of Saqar over the dignified palaces of Paradise out of sheer tribal pride.
PILLAR 12: LIKE WILD ZEBRAS FLEEING A LION
Ka-annahum Humurun Mustanfirah, Farrat min Qaswarah & Open Scrolls
45 Like Frightened Stampeding Zebras
  • Ka-annahum humurun mustanfirah: depicted like terrified wild donkeys stampeding in blind, panicked frenzy.
46 Fleeing in Panic from a Roaring Lion
  • Farrat min qaswarah: running frantically from a pursuing lion or archer, devoid of all reason.
47 Demanding Personal Unfolded Scrolls
  • Nay, every arrogant aristocrat among them demands that personal scrolls of revelation be delivered to his doorstep!
48 They Simply Do Not Fear the Hereafter
  • Kalla bal la yakhafoona al-akhirah: nay, the root illness is simply that their hearts possess zero fear of the Akhirah.

  • Wa ahlu al-maghfirah: and He alone is the boundless Source of all pardon and restorative mercy.
