# Surah Al-Jathiyah: Master Mindmap Knowledge Architecture
### 16:9 Landscape Cartography • 8 Plates • 16 Thematic Pillars • 64 Analytical Cards
**Source Authority:** Classical Sunni Exegesis (Tabari, Ibn Kathir, Qurtubi, Razi, Baghawi, Ibn al-Qayyim)
**Operating Principle:** Read. Reflect. Return.

---

## Plate 01: COSMIC SIGNS FOR THE CERTAIN BELIEVERS
**Section:** `PLATE 01 : THE LIVING SIGNS` | **Focus:** Pillars 01 & 02: Ha-Meem Revelation, Signs in Heavens and Earth, Human Genesis & Dispersing Beasts

### PILLAR 01: HA-MEEM & HEAVENLY SIGNS
*Tanzil al-Kitab min Allah, Signs in Skies and Soil & Absolute Conviction*

#### [01] The Final Ha-Meem Inauguration
- Ha-Meem: the crowning seventh chapter of the Hawameem descending with divine wisdom and authority.

#### [02] Revelation from the Almighty, All-Wise
- Tanzil al-Kitabi min Allahi al-'Azeez al-Hakeem: emanating from the Source of absolute power and wisdom.

#### [03] Signs in the Heavens and Earth for Believers
- Inna fee as-samawati wal-ardi la-ayatin lil-mu'mineen: cosmic architecture displaying deliberate design.

#### [04] Certainty Through Contemplation
- Every celestial movement, atmospheric layer, and ecological balance anchors the soul in unshakable certainty.

### PILLAR 02: HUMAN GENESIS & DISPERSED FAUNA
*Creation of Man, Dispersal of Living Creatures & People of Certain Faith*

#### [05] Signs in the Biological Creation of Man
- Wa fee khalqikum: the marvelous complexity of human physiology, embryology, cognition, and soul.

#### [06] The Multitude of Dispersed Beasts (Dabbah)
- And in what He disperses of diverse living creatures across earth are signs for people of firm conviction (*Yooqinoon*).

#### [07] Alternation of Planetary Night and Day
- The astronomical precision of rotating circadian cycles bringing darkness for sleep and light for sustenance.

#### [08] Water Reviving Dead Soil & Shifting Winds
- Rainfall restoring sterile dirt into fertile bloom, and directed wind currents driving clouds: signs for thinkers.

## Plate 02: THE SINFUL LIAR & RIDICULE OF SIGNS
**Section:** `PLATE 02 : ARROGANT DISDAIN` | **Focus:** Pillars 03 & 04: Waylun li-Kulli Affakin Atheem, Arrogant Persistence, Slighting Revelation & Hellfire Ahead

### PILLAR 03: WOE TO EVERY SINFUL FABRICATOR
*Affakin Atheem, Hears Verses Recited & Persists as Though Hearing Nothing*

#### [09] Woe to Every Deceitful Sinful Fabricator
- Waylun li-kulli affakin atheem: severe ruin decreed for every persistent slanderer steeped in deep transgression.

#### [10] Hearing Divine Verses Recited Daily
- Yasma'u ayati Allahi tutla 'alayh: he hears the verses of Allah recited with crystalline moral clarity.

#### [11] Persisting in Arrogant Defiance
- Thumma yusirru mustakbiran: yet he obstinately persists in arrogance as though he never heard a single word.

#### [12] Give Him Tidings of Grievous Torment
- Fa-bashshirhu bi-'adhabin aleem: announce to him the arrival of a painful, humiliating divine retribution.

### PILLAR 04: TURNING VERSES INTO MOCKERY
*Taking Revelations as a Joke, Humiliating Punishment & Beyond Them Hell*

#### [13] Taking Divine Revelation as a Joke (Huzuwa)
- When he learns anything of Our verses, he turns it into comedy, satire, and cynical public mockery.

#### [14] For Them is a Disgracing Punishment
- Ula'ika lahum 'adhabun muheen: for them is a degrading punishment tailored to break their inflated ego.

#### [15] Before Them Awaits the Roaring Abyss
- Min wara'ihim Jahannam: directly ahead of them lies Hell, from which no power can offer escape.

#### [16] Wealth and False Allies Avail Nothing
- Neither what they accumulated of wealth nor the false protectors they took will profit them in the least.

## Plate 03: OCEANIC SUBJUGATION & FORGIVING BELIEVERS
**Section:** `PLATE 03 : EMBEDDED BLESSINGS` | **Focus:** Pillars 05 & 06: Sea Subdued for Ships, Subjugation of Heavens and Earth & Forgiving Those Who Hope Not

### PILLAR 05: THE SUBJUGATION OF SEAS & SPACE
*Sakh-khara Lakum al-Bahr, Ships Cleaving Waters & Everything in Heavens and Earth*

#### [17] Allah Subjugated the Great Ocean for You
- Allah is He Who subjected the sea that ships may navigate through it by His command for your trade.

#### [18] Seeking of His Abundant Provision
- Cleaving waters to harvest sustenance, deep-sea riches, pearls, and global commercial exchange with gratitude.

#### [19] Subjugated Everything in Skies and Earth
- Sakh-khara lakum ma fee as-samawati: subjecting the entire cosmos for human stewardship as a favor from Him.

#### [20] Signs for People Who Reflect Deeply
- Inna fee dhalika la-ayatin li-qawmin yatafakkaroon: profound signs for those who exercise genuine contemplation.

### PILLAR 06: PARDONING THOSE WHO HOPE NOT FOR GOD
*Tell Believers to Forgive, Days of Allah & Each Recompensed for What He Earned*

#### [21] Tell the Believers to Pardon Their Insults
- Qul lil-ladheena amanoo yaghfiroo: instruct believers to overlook the offenses of those who fear not God's days.

#### [22] Those Who Do Not Hope for the Days of God
- Those who live heedless of divine historical retribution; Allah will personally repay each group for their deeds.

#### [23] Whoever Acts Righteously Benefits Himself
- Man 'amila salihan fa-li-nafsih: whoever does good does so for his own soul's salvation and elevation.

#### [24] Whoever Works Evil Harms His Own Soul
- Wa man asa'a fa-'alayha: whoever commits injustice harms only his own being; then to your Lord you return.

## Plate 04: THE CHILDREN OF ISRAEL & CLEAR ORDINANCES
**Section:** `PLATE 04 : THE SACRED TRUST` | **Focus:** Pillars 07 & 08: Scripture, الحكم & Prophethood Given, Splintered by Envy & Established on the Shari'ah

### PILLAR 07: FAVORS GRANTED TO ISRAEL & SCHISMS
*Kitab, Hukm & Prophethood Bestowed, Clear Proofs & Splintered Only Out of Envy*

#### [25] Bestowed with Scripture, Wisdom & Prophethood
- We gave the Children of Israel the Torah, judicial statecraft (*Hukm*), and a succession of noble prophets.

#### [26] Sustained with Pure Good Provisions
- Nourishing them with wholesome worldly blessings and favoring them above the contemporary nations of their era.

#### [27] Clear Evidences in Religious Matters
- Granted explicit, luminous proofs regarding religious commands, leaving no excuse for deviation.

#### [28] Splintered Only After Knowledge Came
- They did not fracture into sectarian rivalry until after clear knowledge reached them, driven by internal jealousy.

### PILLAR 08: ESTABLISHED UPON THE SACRED SHARI'AH
*Shari'atin minal-Amr, Follow It & Do Not Follow Desires of the Ignorant*

#### [29] Placed Upon an Ordained Highway of Faith
- Thumma ja'alnaka 'ala shari'atin minal-amri fattabi'ha: establishing you upon a clear sacred Law, so follow it!

#### [30] Do Not Follow the Desires of the Ignorant
- Do not yield to the capricious passions (*Ahwa'*) of those who possess no true divine knowledge.

#### [31] They Cannot Protect You from God at All
- The corrupt can never shield you from Allah in the least; the unjust are allies only to one another.

#### [32] Allah is the Protector of the God-Conscious
- Wallahu Waliyyu al-muttaqeen: Allah alone is the Guardian and Protector of those who revere Him.

## Plate 05: THE ILLUSION OF EQUALITY & DAHRIYYAH SKEPTICISM
**Section:** `PLATE 05 : EXISTENTIAL ILLUSIONS` | **Focus:** Pillars 09 & 10: Shall Evildoers Equal Believers?, Death Destined, Namootu wa Nahya & Nothing Kills Except Time

### PILLAR 09: SHALL EVIL EQUAL RIGHTEOUSNESS?
*Am Hasiba Alladheena Ijtarahoo, Equal in Life and Death? & Evil is Their Judgment*

#### [33] Do Evildoers Assume They Equal Believers?
- Do those who commit crimes assume We will make them equal to those who believe and work righteousness?

#### [34] Equal in Their Living and Their Dying?
- Sawa'an mahyahum wa mamatuhum: could their worldly lives and deaths ever be identical in value?

#### [35] How Evil is the Judgment They Make!
- Sa'a ma yahkumoon: how corrupt and perverse is their moral deduction equating justice with oppression!

#### [36] Heavens and Earth Created in Absolute Truth
- Allah created the celestial spheres and earth with purpose, that every soul be recompensed for what it earned.

### PILLAR 10: THE DAHRIYYAH MATERIALIST FALLACY
*Nothing But Worldly Life, We Live and Die & Nothing Destroys Us Except Time*

#### [37] The Atheist Philosophy: Only This Life!
- They say: There is nothing except our earthly biological life; we die and we live, and that is all!

#### [38] Nothing Destroys Us Except Time (Dahr)
- Wa ma yuhlikuna illa ad-dahru: attributing mortality to blind time rather than divine decree.

#### [39] They Possess No Knowledge, Only Conjectures
- They have no empirical knowledge whatsoever of this claim; they are merely indulging in wild guesses.

#### [40] Allah Gives You Life, Then Causes Death
- Say: Allah gives you life, then causes your death, then gathers you to the Day of Resurrection without doubt.

## Plate 06: THE KNEELING NATIONS (JATHIYAH) & LEDGERS OPEN
**Section:** `PLATE 06 : THE KNEELING ASSEMBLY` | **Focus:** Pillars 11 & 12: Every Nation Kneeling, Summoned to its Book, This Book Speaks Truth & Believers Admitted

### PILLAR 11: EVERY NATION ON ITS KNEES (JATHIYAH)
*Wa Tara Kulla Ummatin Jathiyah, Summoned to Record & Our Book Speaks Absolute Truth*

#### [41] You Will See Every Nation Kneeling (Jathiyah)
- Wa tara kulla ummatin jathiyatan: behold every civilization kneeling on its knees in trembling awe.

#### [42] Every Nation Summoned to its Own Record
- Kullu ummatin tud'a ila kitabiha: every community is called to the forensic register of its deeds.

#### [43] Today You Are Recompensed for What You Did
- Al-Yawma tujzawna ma kuntum ta'maloon: today you receive precise recompense for your past choices.

#### [44] This Our Book Pronounces Truth Over You
- Hadha kitabuna yantiqu 'alaykum bil-haqq: Our celestial ledger speaks over you with flawless accuracy.

### PILLAR 12: RECORDING DEEDS & MERCY FOR THE FAITHFUL
*Inna Kunna Nastansikh, Believers in Lord's Mercy & The Manifest Triumph*

#### [45] We Kept Infallible Transcript of All You Did
- Inna kunna nastansikhu ma kuntum ta'maloon: We recorded an exact duplicate of all your actions.

#### [46] Believers Admitted into Their Lord's Mercy
- As for those who believed and worked righteousness, their Lord admits them into His vast mercy.

#### [47] That is Indeed the Manifest Triumph
- Dhalika huwa al-fawzu al-mubeen: that is the unmistakable, glorious achievement of eternity.

#### [48] Challenging the Arrogant Criminals
- As for those who disbelieved: Were My verses not recited to you? But you grew arrogant and were criminals!

## Plate 07: FORGOTTEN AS THEY FORGOT THIS DAY
**Section:** `PLATE 07 : FORGOTTEN IN RUIN` | **Focus:** Pillars 13 & 14: Promise of God is True, Mocking the Hour, Today We Forget You & Deluded by Dunya

### PILLAR 13: SKEPTICISM REGARDING THE HOUR
*Inna Wa'dallahi Haqq, We Know Not What the Hour Is & Sins Surround Them*

#### [49] When Told God's Promise is True, You Doubted
- When it was announced: Indeed, Allah's promise is truth and the Hour is certain, you said: We know not what it is!

#### [50] Indulging in Cynical Agnostic Guesses
- We deem it only a vague conjecture, and we are not convinced that resurrection will ever occur.

#### [51] The Evils of What They Did Become Manifest
- The vile reality of their deeds becomes exposed to them, and what they mocked surrounds them on all sides.

#### [52] The Retributive Sentence Declared
- It is proclaimed to them: Today We will forget you just as you forgot the meeting of this Day of yours!

### PILLAR 14: DELUDED BY THE WORLD & LEFT IN FIRE
*Al-Yawma Nansakum, Abode in Fire, Taking Verses in Mockery & Deluded by World*

#### [53] Today We Forget You as You Forgot This Meeting
- Al-Yawma nansakum kama naseetum liqa'a yawmikum hadha: abandoned in retribution without relief.

#### [54] Your Abode is Fire with None to Help
- Your ultimate refuge is Hellfire, and you possess not a single savior to assist or plead for you.

#### [55] Because You Took Allah's Signs in Ridicule
- That is because you treated the verses of Allah as a joke and were deluded by worldly life (*Gharratkumu al-hayat*).

#### [56] No Exit from the Fire Nor Chance to Appease
- Today they are not extracted from it, nor are they asked or allowed to appease their Lord.

## Plate 08: SUPREME PRAISE & SOVEREIGN MAJESTY (KIBRIYA')
**Section:** `PLATE 08 : SUPREME MAJESTY` | **Focus:** Pillars 15 & 16: Praise to Lord of Heavens and Earth, To Him Belongs All Majesty & Azizun Hakeem

### PILLAR 15: ALL PRAISE TO THE LORD OF ALL REALMS
*Falillahi al-Hamd, Lord of Heavens, Lord of Earth & Lord of the Universe*

#### [57] All Praise Belongs Exclusively to Allah
- Fa-lillahi al-hamd: all praise and supreme gratitude belong solely to Allah across all existence.

#### [58] Lord of the Expanding Celestial Heavens
- Rabbi as-samawati: the Creator, Sustainer, and Nourisher of the vast cosmic heavens.

#### [59] Lord of the Terrestrial Planetary Earth
- Wa Rabbi al-ard: the Sovereign Controller of the earth, its ecosystems, and its mortal inhabitants.

#### [60] Lord of All Existing Realities (Rabbil-'Alameen)
- Rabbi al-'alameen: the Cherisher of all creation, seen and unseen, angelic, jinn, and human.

### PILLAR 16: TO HIM BELONGS ALL MAJESTY (KIBRIYA')
*Wa Lahul-Kibriya'u fis-Samawati wal-Ard, He is the Almighty, The All-Wise*

#### [61] To Him Belongs All Majesty in Heaven and Earth
- Wa lahu al-kibriya'u fee as-samawati wal-ard: all glory, grandeur, and supreme pride belong to Him alone.

#### [62] No Mortal May Claim Pride Before God
- Human arrogance is a grotesque delusion; absolute majesty (*Kibriya'*) is the exclusive mantle of the Creator.

#### [63] He is the Almighty (Al-'Azeez)
- Wa Huwa al-'Azeez: the Irresistible, Invincible Sovereign Whose decrees cannot be opposed or overturned.

#### [64] He is the All-Wise (Al-Hakeem)
- Al-Hakeem: the Possessor of absolute wisdom, sealing the seven Hawameem in transcendent perfection.
