# Surah Ar-Rahman: Master Mindmap Knowledge Architecture
### 16:9 Landscape Cartography • 8 Plates • 16 Thematic Pillars • 64 Analytical Cards
**Source Authority:** Classical Sunni Exegesis (Tabari, Ibn Kathir, Qurtubi, Razi, Baghawi, Ibn al-Qayyim)
**Operating Principle:** Read. Reflect. Return.

---

## Plate 01: AR-RAHMAN: TEACHER OF QUR'AN & SPEECH
**Section:** `PLATE 01 : COSMIC TEACHER` | **Focus:** Pillars 01 & 02: Ar-Rahman Bestowing Bayan & Sun and Moon in Precise Orbits while Stars and Trees Prostrate

### PILLAR 01: AR-RAHMAN & THE BESTOWAL OF BAYAN
*Ar-Rahman, 'Allama al-Qur'an, Khalaqa al-Insan & 'Allamahu al-Bayan*

#### [01] The Sovereign Inauguration of Ar-Rahman
- Ar-Rahman: the Most Merciful whose boundless compassion encompasses all creation and existence.

#### [02] He Taught the Divine Qur'an to Humanity
- 'Allama al-Qur'an: He taught the Qur'an as the supreme ontological guide preceding human creation.

#### [03] He Created the Human Being in Beauty
- Khalaqa al-insan: He brought forth the human being endowed with consciousness, dignity, and capacity.

#### [04] He Taught Him Articulate Speech (Al-Bayan)
- 'Allamahu al-bayan: He equipped him with articulate speech, expressive language, and moral discernment.

### PILLAR 02: COSMIC ORBITS & CELESTIAL PROSTRATION
*Sun and Moon in Calculation (Husban) & Stars and Trees Prostrating*

#### [05] Sun and Moon Moving in Flawless Calculation
- Ash-shamsu wal-qamaru bi-husban: the sun and the moon traverse their orbits with mathematical precision.

#### [06] Governing Time, Seasons, and Life on Earth
- Calibrating day and night, planetary balance, and ecological cycles with unerring regularity.

#### [07] The Stars and Trees Prostrate in Total Surrender
- Wan-najmu wash-shajaru yasjudaan: the celestial stars and earthly vegetation bow in humble adoration.

#### [08] Universal Cosmic Liturgy of Creation
- All nature participates in an unbroken symphony of monotheistic submission to the Creator.

## Plate 02: THE SACRED BALANCE & EARTH PROVISIONS
**Section:** `PLATE 02 : THE SACRED BALANCE` | **Focus:** Pillars 03 & 04: Raising the Sky and Establishing Mizan & Earth Spread Wide with Dates, Grain and Fragrant Herbs

### PILLAR 03: ELEVATION OF SKY & THE SACRED MIZAN
*Raised the Sky, Established the Balance & Transgress Not in the Scale*

#### [09] He Raised the Vaulted Canopy of Heaven
- Was-sama'a rafa'aha: He elevated the vast cosmic heavens high above the terrestrial sphere.

#### [10] And He Established the Universal Balance (Mizan)
- Wa wada'a al-mizan: and He established the sacred scale of justice, equilibrium, and physical law.

#### [11] That You Transgress Not Within the Balance
- Alla tatghaw feel-mizan: that you violate not the cosmic and moral balance through injustice or greed.

#### [12] Establish Weight with Equity and Do Not Defraud
- Wa aqeemoo al-wazna bil-qisti wa la tukhsiroo al-mizan: observe weights with justice and never cheat!

### PILLAR 04: THE TERRESTRIAL CRADLE FOR LIVING SOULS
*Earth Spread for Creatures, Palms, Grain on Husks & The 1st Refrain*

#### [13] The Earth Laid Out as a Cradle for Living Beings
- Wal-arda wada'aha lil-anam: and the earth He laid down hospitably for all living creatures.

#### [14] Bearing Diverse Fruits and Clustered Date-Palms
- Feeha fakihatun wan-nakhlu dhatu al-akmam: therein are fruits and clustered date-palms with sheaths.

#### [15] Grain with Its Protective Husks and Sweet Herbs
- Wal-habbu dhu al-'asfi war-rayhan: and grain with husk for sustenance and fragrant, aromatic plants.

#### [16] The 1st Doxological Refrain of Ar-Rahman
- Fa-bi'ayyi ala'i Rabbikuma tukadhdhiban: so which of the limitless favors of your Lord will you both deny?!

## Plate 03: DUAL GENESIS & THE LORD OF TWO EASTS
**Section:** `PLATE 03 : DUAL GENESIS` | **Focus:** Pillars 05 & 06: Creation of Man from Clay and Jinn from Smokeless Fire & Rabbu al-Mashriqayn wal-Maghribayn

### PILLAR 05: MAN FROM CLAY & JINN FROM FIRE
*Man from Potter's Clay (Fakhkhar) & Jinn from Smokeless Flame (Marij)*

#### [17] Created the Human Being from Dried Potter's Clay
- Khalaqa al-insana min salsalin kal-fakhkhar: He created mortal man from dried resonant clay like pottery.

#### [18] Created the Jinn from a Pure Smokeless Flame
- Wa khalaqa al-janna min marijin min nar: and He created jinnkind from a smokeless blaze of intense fire.

#### [19] Dual Complements of Accountable Consciousness
- Pairing the physical, earthen mortal with the ethereal, fiery entity under divine moral law.

#### [20] So Which of the Favors of Your Lord Will You Deny?
- Fa-bi'ayyi ala'i Rabbikuma tukadhdhiban: confronting both jinn and men with their origins.

### PILLAR 06: LORD OF THE TWO EASTS & TWO WESTS
*Rabbu al-Mashriqayn, Rabbu al-Maghribayn & Cosmic Solstices*

#### [21] Lord of the Two Celestial Easts (Mashriqayn)
- Rabbu al-mashriqayni: the Sovereign Lord who governs the dual extremes of sunrise across summer and winter.

#### [22] Lord of the Two Celestial Wests (Maghribayn)
- Wa Rabbu al-maghribayn: and Lord of the dual seasonal settings of the sun across planetary time.

#### [23] Governing the Inclination and Orbit of the Earth
- Maintaining seasonal life, agricultural cycles, and planetary warmth with unswerving precision.

#### [24] So Which of the Favors of Your Lord Will You Deny?
- Fa-bi'ayyi ala'i Rabbikuma tukadhdhiban: celebrating planetary mechanics as signs of divine grace.

## Plate 04: CONVERGING SEAS & SHIPS LIKE MOUNTAINS
**Section:** `PLATE 04 : THE CONVERGING SEAS` | **Focus:** Pillars 07 & 08: The Barrier Between the Two Seas (Barzakh), Pearls and Coral & Ships Moving Like Mountains

### PILLAR 07: THE UNSEEN BARRIER BETWEEN SEAS
*Maraja al-Bahrayni Yaltaqiyaan, Baynahuma Barzakhun La Yabghiyaan & Marine Balance*

#### [25] He Released the Two Vast Seas Meeting Together
- Maraja al-bahrayni yaltaqiyaan: He set free the two bodies of water, flowing and meeting in contact.

#### [26] Between Them is an Unseen Barrier (Barzakh)
- Baynahuma barzakhun la yabghiyaan: between them stands an invisible physical barrier they transgress not!

#### [27] Preserving the Salinity and Marine Integrity
- Fresh and saline aquatic bodies coexisting side by side without overwhelming each other's ecosystems.

#### [28] So Which of the Favors of Your Lord Will You Deny?
- Fa-bi'ayyi ala'i Rabbikuma tukadhdhiban: prompting human wonder at hydrological design.

### PILLAR 08: PEARLS, CORAL & SHIPS LIKE MOUNTAINS
*Lu'lu' and Marjan, Ships Gliding Like Mountains (Kal-A'lam) & Ocean Wealth*

#### [29] From Them Both Emerge Pearls and Vibrant Coral
- Yakhruju minhuma al-lu'lu'u wal-marjan: from them come forth lustrous pearls and radiant precious coral.

#### [30] Subjugating the Ocean for Human Enterprise
- Bestowing jewelry, maritime resources, and aesthetic beauty from the deep ocean floor.

#### [31] His Are the Mighty Ships Rising Like Mountains
- Wa lahu al-jawari al-munsha'atu feel-bahri kal-a'lam: unto Him belong the great vessels elevated like landmarks.

#### [32] Floating by the Divine Law of Buoyancy
- Carrying human commerce and civilizations across vast stormy oceans in complete security.

## Plate 05: KULLU MAN 'ALAYHA FAN: ALL SHALL PASS
**Section:** `PLATE 05 : TRANSCENDENT ETERNITY` | **Focus:** Pillars 09 & 10: All on Earth Shall Perish, The Eternal Countenance of Majesty & Every Day in a New Affair

### PILLAR 09: THE MORTAL EXTINCTION OF ALL LIFE
*Kullu Man 'Alayha Fan, Finite Extinction of Creation & Ceasing of All Worldly Power*

#### [33] Every Single Being Upon Earth Will Perish!
- Kullu man 'alayha fan: all that lives, breathes, and dwells upon the terrestrial plane shall vanish!

#### [34] The Irreversible Mortality of All Created Life
- Kings, tyrants, wealthy oligarchs, animals, and jinn—all will succumb to inevitable dissolution.

#### [35] The Vanishing of Worldly Empire and Hubris
- All mortal arrogance, borders, and monuments reduced to silent dust before the sovereign Creator.

#### [36] So Which of the Favors of Your Lord Will You Deny?
- Fa-bi'ayyi ala'i Rabbikuma tukadhdhiban: reminding humanity that mortality is the door to justice.

### PILLAR 10: ONLY THE COUNTENANCE OF MAJESTY REMAINS
*Yabqa Wajhu Rabbika Dhu'l-Jalali wal-Ikram & Kulla Yawmin Huwa fee Sha'n*

#### [37] There Remains Forever the Face of Your Lord
- Wa yabqa wajhu Rabbika: and there endures eternally the glorious Countenance of your Lord!

#### [38] Possessor of Unrivaled Majesty and Honor (Dhu'l-Jalal)
- Dhu'l-jalali wal-ikram: the Sole Owner of supreme, terrifying majesty and boundless generosity.

#### [39] All in Heavens and Earth Beseech Him for Need
- Yas'aluhu man fees-samawati wal-ard: all creatures in the cosmos implore Him constantly for life and aid.

#### [40] Every Single Day He is Executing a New Affair
- Kulla yawmin huwa fee sha'n: every day He is bringing forth a new decree, forgiving, elevating, and guiding.

## Plate 06: PENETRATING HEAVENS & SCORCHING FLAMES
**Section:** `PLATE 06 : THE CHALLENGE` | **Focus:** Pillars 11 & 12: Penetrating the Diameters of Heavens, Flaming Sulfur & Sinners Marked by Their Faces

### PILLAR 11: THE CHALLENGE TO JINN AND MANKIND
*Ya Ma'shara al-Jinni wal-Ins, Penetrate the Diameters & You Cannot Save with Sultan*

#### [41] O Assembly of Jinn and Men: Pass Beyond If You Can!
- Ya ma'shara al-jinni wal-insi inistata'tum an tanfudhoo: if you have power to penetrate the cosmic zones!

#### [42] Penetrate the Boundaries of Heavens and Earth!
- Escape from the confines and jurisdictions of the heavens and earth if you dare!

#### [43] You Can Never Escape Except by Sovereign Power
- La tanfudhoona illa bi-sultan: you shall never escape except by granted sovereign authority from God!

#### [44] The Absolute Impossibility of Fleeing Divine Reach
- Confronting cosmic pride with the inescapable jurisdiction of the Creator's sovereignty.

### PILLAR 12: UNLEASHED FLARES OF SMOKELESS FIRE
*Shuwazun min Nar, Molten Brass & The Rosy Sky Like Melted Leather (Wardatan)*

#### [45] Unleashing Blazing Flares of Fire and Brass
- Yursalu 'alaykuma shuwazun min narin wa nuhasun: sent against you are flames of fire and molten brass!

#### [46] You Will Find Zero Defense or Refuge
- Fala tantasiraan: and you will find no victory, shield, or allies to defend your rebellious souls.

#### [47] When the Sky Rifts Open Like Red Rosy Leather
- Fa-idha inshaqqati as-sama'u fa-kanat wardatan kad-dihan: when heaven fractures into crimson leather!

#### [48] Sinners Known Instantly by Their Dark Marks
- Yu'rafu al-mujrimoona bi-seemahum: criminals recognized by their faces and seized by forelocks and feet!

## Plate 07: THE TWIN HIGHER GARDENS (JANNATAN)
**Section:** `PLATE 07 : THE TWIN HIGHER GARDENS` | **Focus:** Pillars 13 & 14: For Him Who Feared Standing Before His Lord (Two Gardens) & Reclining on Brocade with Modest Companions

### PILLAR 13: FOR THE GOD-FEARING: TWO HIGHER GARDENS
*Wa Li-Man Khafa Maqama Rabbihi Jannatan, Dhawatay Afnan & Twin Flowing Springs*

#### [49] For He Who Feared Standing Before His Lord: Two Gardens!
- Wa li-man khafa maqama Rabbihi jannatan: for whoever reverently feared standing before his Lord: two Gardens!

#### [50] Possessing Spreading, Lush Branches (Dhawatay Afnan)
- Endowed with rich, sprawling, fruit-laden canopies shading celestial pathways with emerald beauty.

#### [51] Two Fresh Springs Gushing with Sweet Waters
- Feehima 'aynani tajriyaan: within both Gardens are two living springs flowing without cessation.

#### [52] Pairs of Every Splendid Delicious Fruit
- Feehima min kulli fakihatin zawjaan: within both are two pairs of every variety of luscious fruit.

### PILLAR 14: BROCADE THRONES & RADIANT COMPANIONS
*Reclining on Silk Brocade, Modest Companions Like Rubies & Hal Jaza'u al-Ihsan*

#### [53] Reclining on Couches Lined with Rich Silk Brocade
- Muttaki'eena 'ala furushin bata'inuha min istabraq: resting on couches lined with pure heavy brocade.

#### [54] Harvests of Both Gardens Hanging Within Arm's Reach
- Wa jana al-jannatayni dan: the luscious ripe fruits of both Gardens bending down intimately near.

#### [55] Companions of Modest Glances Radiant as Rubies
- Therein companions of restrained gaze, untouched, luminous with the beauty of rubies and coral.

#### [56] Is the Reward for Excellence Anything But Excellence?!
- Hal jaza'u al-ihsani illa al-ihsan: is the recompense for moral excellence anything other than divine excellence?!

## Plate 08: THE TWO DESCENDING GARDENS & DOXOLOGY
**Section:** `PLATE 08 : THE FINAL DOXOLOGY` | **Focus:** Pillars 15 & 16: Two Further Verdant Gardens (Mudhammatan), Pavilions & Tabaraka Ismu Rabbik Dhee al-Jalali wal-Ikram

### PILLAR 15: TWO FURTHER VERDANT GARDENS
*Wa Min Doonihima Jannatan, Mudhammatan Deep Dark Green & Springs of Abundance*

#### [57] And Beside Those Two Are Two Further Gardens
- Wa min doonihima jannatan: and besides those elevated two are two further magnificent Gardens.

#### [58] Deep, Saturated Dark Green in Moisture (Mudhammatan)
- Mudhammatan: glowing with intense, lush dark-green verdure from perpetual abundance of water.

#### [59] Two Springs Gushing Forth with Living Water
- Feehima 'aynani naddakhataan: within both are two fountains bubbling forth with pure refreshing drink.

#### [60] Abundance of Date-Palms and Pomegranates
- Feehima fakihatun wa nakhlu wa rumman: abundant with dates, pomegranates, and divine delights.

### PILLAR 16: BLESSED IS THE NAME OF YOUR LORD!
*Companions in Pavilions, Reclining on Silk & Tabaraka Ismu Rabbik Dhu'l-Jalal*

#### [61] Pure Companions Sheltered Within Sacred Pavilions
- Hoorun maqsooratun feel-khiyam: fair companions sheltered in privacy within luminous pavilions.

#### [62] Reclining on Emerald Cushions and Rare Carpets
- Muttaki'eena 'ala rafrafin khudrin wa 'abqariyyin hisan: resting upon green cushions and rich tapestries.

#### [63] So Which of the Favors of Your Lord Will You Deny?
- Fa-bi'ayyi ala'i Rabbikuma tukadhdhiban: concluding the 31-fold symphony of divine gratitude.

#### [64] Blessed is the Name of Your Lord, Owner of Majesty!
- Tabaraka ismu Rabbika dhee al-jalali wal-ikram: blessed and exalted is the Name of your Lord, Owner of Majesty and Honor!
