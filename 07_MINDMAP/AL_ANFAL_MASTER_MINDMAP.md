## Plate 01: THE SPOILS OF WAR, THE HEART OF FAITH & THE EVE OF BADR
**Section Badge:** `[PLATE 01 : BADR & PILLARS OF FAITH]`  
**Overview:** Pillars 1 & 2: Subordination of Spoils, Hallmarks of True Faith, Reluctance for Battle & The 1,000 Angels  

### PILLAR 1: SPOILS OF WAR & HALLMARKS OF FAITH
*Al-Anfal Subordinated, Reconciling Internal Disputes & The Trembling Heart*  

#### 1. Spoils of War Belong to Allah & Messenger
- The Companions inquired concerning the allocation of the spoils of Badr.
- Revelation shifts focus: Spoils belong to Allah and His Messenger unconditionally.
- Divine command: Cultivate Taqwa and reconcile fraternal disputes among believers.

#### 2. The Hallmarks of True Faith (Innamal-Mu'minoon)
- Believers are those whose hearts tremble with awe when Allah is remembered.
- Faith is not static; it increases dynamically upon hearing divine verses recited.
- Sincere believers place their unconditional reliance (Tawakkul) exclusively upon Him.

#### 3. Pillars of Righteous Practice: Salah & Spending
- True believers establish regular prayer and spend from divine provision.
- Those who embody these traits are the believers in absolute truth and reality.
- For them are elevated degrees with their Lord, divine forgiveness, and noble bounty.

#### 4. Reluctance Before the Decreed Encounter
- As your Lord brought you forth from your home in truth, a group disliked it.
- Disputing concerning the truth after it had become manifest, driven toward death.
- Human aversion to hardship contrasted with the divine decree of ultimate triumph.

### PILLAR 2: THE TWO PARTIES & THE ANGELIC LEGIONS
*Caravan vs Army, Uprooting Falsehood & Seeking Rescue (Istighathah)*  

#### 5. The Two Parties: Unarmed vs Heavily Armed
- Believers wished to intercept the unarmed merchant caravan of Abu Sufyan.
- Allah willed to establish the truth through His Words and sever the roots of deniers.
- Proving that divine wisdom directs events toward lasting historical triumph.

#### 6. To Confirm the Truth & Abolish Falsehood
- The clash was engineered to manifest the supremacy of truth over falsehood.
- Even though the stubborn criminals and polytheistic chieftains detested it.
- Badr established the independent sovereignty and moral authority of the Ummah.

#### 7. Seeking Rescue: Reinforcement with 1,000 Angels
- In the bower at Badr, the Prophet pleaded passionately in desperate Istighathah.
- Allah responded: 'I will reinforce you with a thousand angels in succession.'
- Made as glad tidings and tranquil reassurance; victory is only from Allah, the Mighty.

#### 8. The Descending Slumber & Purifying Rain
- Divine slumber (An-Nu'as) enveloped anxious warriors as a shield of peaceful serenity.
- Pure rain descended from heaven to cleanse, remove Satan's whispers, and pack the sand.
- Fortifying their beating hearts and establishing their feet firmly upon the battlefield.

## Plate 02: DIVINE INTERVENTION, THE CAST STAFF & PROHIBITIONS OF RETREAT
**Section Badge:** `[PLATE 02 : DIVINE INTERVENTION]`  
**Overview:** Pillars 3 & 4: Angelic Battlefield Assistance, Retreat Forbidden, 'Allah Threw' & The Deaf/Dumb Beasts  

### PILLAR 3: ANGELIC WARFARE & THE CRIME OF RETREAT
*Striking Necks, Defying Revelation & The Mortal Sin of Battlefield Flight*  

#### 9. Angelic Warfare: Striking Necks & Fingertips
- Allah inspired the angels: 'I am with you, so strengthen those who have believed.'
- 'I will cast terror into the hearts of those who disbelieved; strike above the necks.'
- Severing their fingertips because they defied Allah and His Messenger in rebellion.

#### 10. Severe Retribution for Arrogant Rebellion
- Whoever defies Allah and His Messenger meets the severe punishment of God.
- Taste this earthly degradation; for the disbelievers is the torment of the Fire.
- Arrogant reliance on superior numbers and military hardware shattered completely.

#### 11. Inviolable Law: Never Turn Backs in Battle
- When meeting advancing hostile armies in battle, never turn your backs in flight.
- Turning back incurs the wrath of Allah; their final eternal refuge is Hellfire.
- Permitted only as a tactical maneuver to reposition or rejoin another detachment.

#### 12. The Mortal Sin of Fleeing the Encounter
- The Prophet classified fleeing the battlefield as one of seven mortal sins (Mubiqat).
- Faith requires unshakeable resolve when defending truth against armed aggression.
- The steadfast stand of 313 believers established an eternal benchmark of courage.

### PILLAR 4: DIVINE CAUSALITY & THE RECEPTIVE EAR
*The Handful of Dust, Gracious Examination & The Deaf and Dumb Who Reason Not*  

#### 13. Divine Causality: 'You Threw Not, But Allah Threw'
- 'You killed them not, but Allah killed them; you threw not when you threw, but Allah threw.'
- The Prophet cast dust toward Quraysh; divine power carried it into every hostile eye.
- Human effort is merely an obedient vessel; ultimate triumph belongs to God alone.

#### 14. A Gracious Examination for Believers
- That He might test the believers with a gracious, purifying trial of faith.
- Indeed, Allah is All-Hearing of supplications and All-Knowing of internal motives.
- Divine strategy weakens the plots of the disbelievers and dismantles their schemes.

#### 15. Turn Not Away While You Hear Revelation
- 'O you who believe, obey Allah and His Messenger and do not turn away from him.'
- Do not be like hypocrites who say 'We hear' while their hearts are completely deaf.
- Hypocritical auditory compliance without spiritual obedience is spiritual suicide.

#### 16. The Worst of Beasts: Deaf & Dumb Who Reason Not
- The vilest living creatures in Allah's sight are the deaf and dumb who understand not.
- Had Allah known any goodness in them, He would have caused them to hear and reflect.
- Even if made to hear without sincerity, they would have turned away in stubborn aversion.

## Plate 03: THE CALL TO LIFE, SPIRITUAL INTERVENTIONS & THE PLOTS OF MAKKAH
**Section Badge:** `[PLATE 03 : LIFE & INWARD INTERVENTION]`  
**Overview:** Pillars 5 & 6: Responding to What Gives Life, Sovereignty Over Hearts, Universal Fitnah & Dar an-Nadwah  

### PILLAR 5: THE CALL TO LIFE & SOVEREIGNTY OVER HEARTS
*Revelation as Vitality, Allah Intervening Between Hearts & Avoiding Fitnah*  

#### 17. Answer the Call to What Grants Authentic Life
- 'Respond to Allah and to the Messenger when he calls you to that which gives you life.'
- Divine commandments are not restrictive shackles, but the fountain of true life.
- Living without revelation is spiritual necrosis, reducing man to an empty shell.

#### 18. Allah Intervenes Between Man & His Heart
- Know that Allah intervenes between a human being and his most intimate heart.
- You do not own your heart; spiritual steadfastness is a continuous divine gift.
- To Him you will be gathered; delaying repentance risks permanent spiritual blindness.

#### 19. Beware the Fitnah That Afflicts Beyond Wrongdoers
- Fear a trial that will not strike the oppressors among you exclusively.
- Passive tolerance of corruption and failure to enjoin good invites collective trials.
- Society is an interconnected vessel; letting sinners drill holes sinks everyone.

#### 20. Remember When You Were Few & Oppressed
- Remember when you were a vulnerable, oppressed minority in the lands of Makkah.
- Fearing that hostile factions would abduct and destroy you at any moment.
- Allah sheltered you, strengthened you with His aid, and provided wholesome tayyibat.

### PILLAR 6: FIDUCIARY INTEGRITY & THE DAR AN-NADWAH PLOT
*Betraying Trusts, Wealth and Children, Taqwa as Criterion & The Best of Planners*  

#### 21. Betray Not Allah, Messenger & Your Trusts
- Do not betray Allah and the Messenger, nor betray your mutual trusts knowingly.
- Fiduciary purity in commercial, military, and spiritual trusts is a sacred duty.
- Believers protect covenants and secrets with utmost integrity and religious honor.

#### 22. Wealth & Offspring: A Delicate Cosmic Trial
- Know that your material wealth and your children are an examination (Fitnah).
- Excessive love for family and money must never induce compromises in divine law.
- With Allah alone is an immense, incomparable, and eternal reward.

#### 23. Taqwa as Furqan: The Criterion of Discernment
- If you maintain Taqwa of Allah, He will grant you a Furqan (divine criterion).
- Furqan grants razor-sharp moral clarity to discern truth from alluring falsehood.
- He will expiate your evil deeds and forgive you; Allah possesses immense grace.

#### 24. The Dar an-Nadwah Conspiracy Foiled
- The Makkan council plotted three options: to imprison, assassinate, or exile you.
- 'They plot, and Allah plans; and Allah is the best of all planners.'
- Divine counter-strategy delivered the Messenger safely to the sanctuary of Madinah.

## Plate 04: THE PRE-ISLAMIC MINDSET, THE SHIELD OF FORGIVENESS & THE CRITERION
**Section Badge:** `[PLATE 04 : THE CRITERION & THE DUAL SHIELDS]`  
**Overview:** Pillars 7 & 8: Tales of the Ancients, Imprecation of Brimstone, The Shield of Istighfar & The Fifth Share  

### PILLAR 7: SKEPTICISM, WHISTLING PRAYERS & THE TWO SHIELDS
*Pagan Dismissals, The Shield of the Prophet, Power of Istighfar & Empty Rituals*  

#### 25. Dismissing Revelation as 'Tales of the Ancients'
- When divine verses are recited, they dismiss: 'We have heard; these are ancient legends.'
- Arrogant intellectual cynicism blinds the soul to moral and metaphysical depth.
- Packaging revelation as outdated myths is an ancient, repetitive skeptical tropes.

#### 26. Demanding Brimstone: The Arrogance of Imprecation
- Cynical prayer: 'O Allah, if this is the truth from You, rain upon us stones from heaven!'
- Instead of asking for guidance to truth, they demanded catastrophic destruction.
- Psychological obstinacy prefers obliteration over humbling the ego before God.

#### 27. The Twin Shields of Earthly Security
- Allah would not punish them while the Prophet walked physically among them.
- Nor will Allah punish a people while they actively seek forgiveness (Istighfar).
- The second shield remains permanently active in the hands of believers until the Hour.

#### 28. Empty Pagan Rituals: Whistling & Clapping
- Their prayer at the Sacred House was nothing but whistling and clapping (Muka'an).
- Sensory distraction designed to drown out the recitation of the Holy Qur'an.
- Empty ritualism devoid of reverence, humility, and moral transformation.

### PILLAR 8: SPENDING TO OBSTRUCT & THE FIFTH SHARE (KHUMS)
*Squandering Wealth, Separating Filth from Pure & The Welfare Share*  

#### 29. Squandering Wealth to Obstruct Truth
- Disbelievers spend their fortunes to hinder humanity from the pathway of Allah.
- They will spend it, then it will become a source of bitter, burning regret for them.
- Ultimately overcome and vanquished; gathered into the roaring abyss of Hell.

#### 30. Separating the Filthy from the Pure
- That Allah may separate the vile and impure (Al-Khabeeth) from the good and pure.
- Piling the impure one upon another, heaping it all together into the Fire.
- Ethical polarization: History systematically sifts moral purity from corrupt arrogance.

#### 31. Ceasing Hostilities: Forgiveness for Past Sins
- Say to those who disbelieved: If they cease hostility, their past will be forgiven.
- But if they return to aggression, the precedent of ancient nations has passed.
- Islam opens the door of complete redemption even to formerly bitter combatants.

#### 32. The Fifth Share (Al-Khums): Social Welfare & State
- One-fifth of captured spoils is reserved for Allah, Messenger, and public welfare.
- Dedicated to the Prophet's kin, orphans, the poor, and destitute wayfarers.
- Sanctifying material gains by anchoring them in social justice and public good.

## Plate 05: THE GEOMETRY OF CONFLICT, PROPHETIC DREAMS & PSYCHOLOGICAL ORDER
**Section Badge:** `[PLATE 05 : STRATEGIC COHESION]`  
**Overview:** Pillars 9 & 10: Topography of Badr, Minimizing Dreams, Four Pillars of Victory & Avoiding Disputes  

### PILLAR 9: THE TOPOGRAPHY OF BADR & PROPHETIC VISIONS
*The Near and Far Slopes, Unplanned Rendezvous, Dreams of Minimization & Divine Decree*  

#### 33. The Topography of Badr: Near & Far Slopes
- Believers on the near slope (Ad-Dunya), pagans on the far slope (Al-Quswa).
- The commercial caravan positioned on the coastal plain below them.
- An unplanned convergence orchestrated by God to accomplish a decreed reality.

#### 34. Prophetic Vision: Minimizing Enemy Force
- Allah showed the enemy few in the Prophet's dream before the engagement.
- Had He shown them numerous, you would have faltered and disputed the decision.
- Divine psychological calibration preserved communal resolve and courage.

#### 35. The Optical Illusion of Mutual Minimization
- Upon meeting on the sands, Allah made each force appear small in the other's eyes.
- That the pagans would not retreat, and the believers would advance with courage.
- Executing the predetermined decree: All cosmic affairs return ultimately to Allah.

#### 36. The Inevitable Execution of Divine Will
- Neither army operated by random coincidence or independent human calculation.
- The watershed encounter was guided by divine design to establish truth on earth.
- Badr transformed a struggling faith into an unshakeable geopolitical reality.

### PILLAR 10: FOUR PILLARS OF VICTORY & DISPUTE PROHIBITION
*Steadfastness, Abundant Dhikr, Obedience & 'Lest You Falter and Lose Strength'*  

#### 37. Pillar 1 & 2: Firm Steadfastness & Abundant Dhikr
- When meeting a hostile force, stand firm with immovable physical and moral resolve.
- Remember Allah abundantly in the crucible of battle that you may achieve triumph.
- Prayer and divine mindfulness are the spiritual foundation of martial courage.

#### 38. Pillar 3 & 4: Sincere Obedience & No Dispute
- Obey Allah and His Messenger in every tactical and strategic directive.
- 'And do not dispute with one another, lest you falter and your strength depart.'
- Internal factional arguing causes courage to collapse and the wind of victory to vanish.

#### 39. The Shield of Sabr: Allah is with the Patient
- Endure with unwavering patience; indeed, Allah is with those who are patient.
- Panic and internal blame destroy armies and organizations faster than enemy weapons.
- Sincere patience under fire draws down divine reinforcement and angelic peace.

#### 40. Shun Boastful Ostentation: The March of Quraysh
- Do not be like the Makkan chieftains who marched out with vanity, pride, and singing girls.
- Seeking public applause and hindering humanity from the sacred path of Allah.
- Arrogant self-exaltation before battle is the guaranteed harbinger of humiliating defeat.

## Plate 06: THE BETRAYAL OF SATAN, THE FATE OF TYRANTS & TREATY ETHICS
**Section Badge:** `[PLATE 06 : SATAN'S RETREAT & TREATY LAWS]`  
**Overview:** Pillars 11 & 12: Cowardice of Iblis, Death Throes of Oppressors, Changing Inward Blessings & Treaty Ethics  

### PILLAR 11: SATAN'S BETRAYAL & THE DEATH THROES OF TYRANTS
*Satan Flees in Terror, Hypocrites' Delusion Taunt & Angels Striking Faces and Backs*  

#### 41. Satan's Deceptive Boast: 'None Can Overcome You!'
- Satan beautified their wicked march: 'None among mankind can defeat you today!'
- Pledging false protection and brotherhood in the physical guise of Suraqah.
- Seducing oppressors into overconfidence through arrogant imperial delusions.

#### 42. The Demon Flees: 'I See What You See Not!'
- When the two hosts came in sight, Satan turned on his heels and fled into the desert.
- He cried in terror: 'I am free of you! I see what you do not see; I fear Allah!'
- The arch-seducer abandons his disciples the moment divine power manifests.

#### 43. The Hypocrites' Taunt: 'Their Religion Deluded Them'
- Hypocrites and diseased hearts scoffed at the believers: 'Their faith deluded them!'
- Judging reality solely by material weapons, physical numbers, and outward armaments.
- But whoever relies upon Allah finds that Allah is Mighty, Wise, and Invincible.

#### 44. Striking Faces & Backs: Death of Oppressors
- If you could see when the angels take the souls of those who disbelieved at death.
- Striking their faces and their backs: 'Taste the punishment of the burning Fire!'
- Just retribution for what their hands forwarded; Allah is never unjust to servants.

### PILLAR 12: THE LAW OF DECLINE & TREATY INTEGRITY
*Pharaoh's Example, Changing Inward States, Habitual Treaty-Breakers & Open Repudiation*  

#### 45. The Fate of Pharaoh: Denying Signs of Their Lord
- Like the precedent of Pharaoh's dynasty and those who flourished before them.
- They rejected the signs of their Lord; He destroyed them for their persistent sins.
- Drowning Pharaoh's hosts; every oppressive civilization meets identical ruin.

#### 46. The Law of Social Transformation
- 'Allah does not change a blessing He bestowed until they change what is in themselves.'
- Cultural decline and geopolitical ruin begin with internal moral and spiritual decay.
- Preserving divine grace requires active preservation of gratitude, justice, and faith.

#### 47. The Worst of Beasts: Habitual Treaty-Breakers
- The vilest creatures before Allah are those who disbelieve and will not have faith.
- Those with whom you establish a solemn treaty, yet they break their pact every time.
- Devoid of moral conscience, honor, or fear of God; prioritizing treason over truth.

#### 48. Scattering Aggressors: Disciplining Treachery
- If you encounter them in battle, strike them decisively to disperse those behind them.
- Creating profound deterrent examples so that future conspirators take heed.
- If treachery is feared from an ally, throw their covenant back with strict equity.

## Plate 07: READINESS FOR DEFENSE, THE INCLINATION TO PEACE & DIVINE AFFECTION
**Section Badge:** `[PLATE 07 : DEFENSIVE DETERRENCE & PEACE]`  
**Overview:** Pillars 13 & 14: Preparing Maximum Strength, Incline to Peace, Miracle of Reconciled Hearts & Spiritual Ratios  

### PILLAR 13: STRATEGIC DETERRENCE & THE MANDATE OF PEACE
*Mobilizing Capacity, Archery Deterrence, Incline to Peace & Sufficiency of Allah*  

#### 49. Mobilize Maximum Capacity: Preparing Deterrence
- 'Prepare against them whatever strength and cavalry you are capable of mobilizing.'
- The goal is deterrence (Turhiboona bihi): Preventing warmongers from attacking.
- The Prophet explained from the pulpit: 'Unquestionably, strength is projectile deterrence.'

#### 50. Whatever You Spend in God's Cause is Repaid
- Whatever wealth, time, and resources you spend in God's path will be fully repaid.
- You shall never be wronged or diminished in divine compensation in both realms.
- National defense and communal security are sacred collective obligations (Fard Kifayah).

#### 51. The Mandate of Peace: If They Incline, Incline Unto It
- 'And if they incline to peace (Silm), incline unto it, and place your trust in Allah.'
- Islam prioritizes reconciliation and peaceful coexistence over endless war.
- Sincere readiness to accept peace offers, anchoring trust in Allah's protection.

#### 52. Deceptive Treaties: Sufficient for You is Allah
- If they intend to deceive you through treaties, Allah is completely sufficient for you.
- He is the One who supported you with His sovereign aid and with the sincere believers.
- The faithful negotiate from a position of moral integrity, unafraid of betrayal.

### PILLAR 14: UNIFIED HEARTS & THE RATIOS OF ENDURANCE
*The Miracle of Reconciled Hearts, Prophetic Rousing & The 1:2 Alleviated Standard*  

#### 53. The Miracle of Reconciled Hearts (Allafa Baynahum)
- 'If you had spent all that is in the earth, you could not have united their hearts.'
- Century-long blood feuds between Aws and Khazraj healed exclusively by divine grace.
- Faith is the only transcendent chemistry that dissolves tribal and racial bigotry.

#### 54. Sufficiency of Allah for Prophet & Believers
- 'O Prophet, sufficient for you is Allah and whoever follows you of the believers.'
- Numbers and weapons are secondary to divine sufficiency and sincere companionship.
- Mobilizing the community with pure trust in the Almighty's unassailable protection.

#### 55. Rousing to Battle: The 20 vs 200 Standard
- 'O Prophet, rouse the believers to battle: If there are twenty steadfast, they overcome two hundred.'
- A spiritual asymmetry ratio of 1:10 rooted in superior moral conviction and understanding.
- Disbelievers are a people who comprehend not the transcendent reality of existence.

#### 56. Divine Alleviation: The 100 vs 200 Standard
- 'Now Allah has lightened your burden, knowing that within you is human weakness.'
- If there are one hundred steadfast, they overcome two hundred (1:2 ratio).
- Divine realism acknowledging mortal limits: Retreating before twice one's size is forbidden.

## Plate 08: CAPTIVES OF WAR, THE BOND OF BROTHERHOOD & THE PRIMACY OF KIN
**Section Badge:** `[PLATE 08 : BROTHERHOOD & CAPTIVES]`  
**Overview:** Pillars 15 & 16: The Judgment on Captives, Lawful Spoils, The Pact of Wilayah & The Primacy of Blood Kin  

### PILLAR 15: CAPTIVES OF WAR & DIVINE CONSOLATION
*Ransoms Rebuked, Consolation for Prisoners, Lawful Tayyibat & Rebuffing Treachery*  

#### 57. The Prophetic Precedent on Captives of War
- Rebuking premature taking of ransoms before securing moral authority upon the earth.
- You desired worldly goods (ransoms), while Allah desired the Hereafter and justice.
- Were it not for a prior divine decree, severe punishment would have touched you.

#### 58. Divine Consolation for Prisoners of War
- 'Say to the captives in your hands: If Allah knows good in your hearts, He will give you better.'
- Granting them better than the ransom taken from them, and forgiving their past sins.
- Abbas ibn Abd al-Muttalib and other captives embraced Islam and found immense bounty.

#### 59. Consuming Lawful & Pure Provisions (Halalan Tayyiba)
- 'So consume what you took of spoils as lawful and pure (Halalan Tayyiba).'
- Fear Allah in all your dealings; indeed, Allah is Most Forgiving, Ever Merciful.
- Sanctifying provisions through adherence to divine limits and social justice.

#### 60. Dealing with Treachery: Allah Gave Mastery
- If captives intend betrayal, they had already betrayed Allah prior to the encounter.
- So He gave the believers mastery over them; Allah is All-Knowing and All-Wise.
- Faith provides the psychological shield against being demoralized by bad faith.

### PILLAR 16: THE PACT OF WILAYAH & INHERITANCE OF KIN
*Muhajiroon and Ansar, Believers Remaining Behind, Global Counter-Alliance & Ulul-Arham*  

#### 61. The Sacred Alliance: Muhajiroon & Ansar
- Those who believed, emigrated, and strived with wealth and lives in the path of Allah.
- And those who sheltered and aided them—these are the true allies (Awliya') of one another.
- The foundation of the Islamic social order based on shared sacrifice and faith.

#### 62. Believers Remaining in the Domain of War
- Those who believed but did not emigrate: You have no obligation of alliance with them.
- Until they make the Hijrah; yet if they seek your aid in religion, you must assist them.
- Except against a people with whom you have established a binding diplomatic treaty.

#### 63. The Global Counter-Alliance & Social Chaos
- Those who disbelieve are allies and protectors of one another across the earth.
- 'If you do not do so (unite in mutual alliance), there will be turmoil and vast corruption.'
- Failing to maintain Islamic solidarity abandons the weak to tyrannical oppression.

#### 64. The Primacy of Blood Relations (Ulul-Arham)
- Believers who emigrated and struggled together are the believers in ultimate truth.
- Concluding decree: Blood relatives (Ulul-Arham) have greater priority in inheritance.
- Transitioning from wartime adoptive brotherhood to the permanent legal order of the Shari'ah.
