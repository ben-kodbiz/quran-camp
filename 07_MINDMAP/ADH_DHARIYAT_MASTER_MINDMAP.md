# Surah Adh-Dhariyat: Master Mindmap Knowledge Architecture
### 16:9 Landscape Cartography • 8 Plates • 16 Thematic Pillars • 64 Analytical Cards
**Source Authority:** Classical Sunni Exegesis (Tabari, Ibn Kathir, Qurtubi, Razi, Baghawi, Ibn al-Qayyim)
**Operating Principle:** Read. Reflect. Return.

---

## Plate 01: THE FOUR COSMIC WINDS & THE INEVITABLE PROMISE
**Section:** `PLATE 01 : COSMIC WINDS` | **Focus:** Pillars 01 & 02: The Four Celestial Forces, The Certainty of Judgment & The Woven Tracks of the Sky

### PILLAR 01: THE FOUR CELESTIAL DISTRIBUTORS
*Dhariyat Scattering, Hamilat Clouds, Jariyat Ships & Muqassimat Angels*

#### [01] By the Winds Scattering Dust Afar
- Wadh-dhariyati dharwa: by the cosmic winds scattering dust and seeds across atmospheric currents.

#### [02] By the Clouds Bearing Heavy Burdens of Rain
- Fal-hamilati wiqra: by the dense rain-clouds loaded with tons of fresh water for parched soil.

#### [03] By the Vessels Gliding Gracefully on Waters
- Fal-jariyati yusra: by the ships and celestial bodies gliding smoothly along their ordained paths.

#### [04] By the Angels Apportioning Decrees
- Fal-muqassimati amra: by the angelic administrators apportioning provisions and divine commands.

### PILLAR 02: INEVITABLE CERTAINTY OF DIVINE JUDGMENT
*Innama Too'adoona la-Sadiq, Recompense Inevitable & Sky of Woven Tracks*

#### [05] What You Are Promised is Infallibly True
- Innama too'adoona la-sadiq: that which you are promised of resurrection and reckoning is undeniably true!

#### [06] The Final Judgment is Bound to Occur
- Wa inna ad-deena la-waqi': and the divine recompense is inevitably bound to fall upon all creation.

#### [07] By the Sky with Its Woven Orbits (Dhatil-Hubuk)
- By the celestial expanse adorned with magnificent orbital pathways, galaxies, and interwoven stars.

#### [08] Mankind Trapped in Conflicting Conjectures
- You are divided in discordant, conflicting opinions: turned away from truth is he who is deluded.

## Plate 02: CHARACTER OF THE PIOUS & THE NIGHT VIGIL
**Section:** `PLATE 02 : THE RIGHTEOUS VIGIL` | **Focus:** Pillars 03 & 04: Doom of the Reckless Conjecturers & The Righteous in Gardens Sleeping Little at Night

### PILLAR 03: DOOM OF THE RECKLESS CONJECTURERS
*Qutila al-Kharrasoon, Floundering in Heedlessness & Day of Testing by Fire*

#### [09] Cursed are the Speculative Liars (Kharrasoon)
- Qutila al-kharrasoon: ruined and destroyed are the arrogant conjectures who fabricate lies against God.

#### [10] Floundering in Ignorant Confusion
- Alladheena hum fee ghamratin sahoon: those who remain submerged in heedless complacency and vanity.

#### [11] Mockingly Asking: When is the Day of Judgment?
- They ask in cynical derision: When will this promised Day of Retribution ever take place?

#### [12] The Day They Are Scorched Over the Fire
- The Day they are tested and scorched over the Fire: Taste your trial; this is what you sought to hasten!

### PILLAR 04: THE PIOUS IN GARDENS & NIGHT VIGILS
*Sleeping Little at Night, Seeking Forgiveness at Dawn & Benefactors of Good*

#### [13] The Righteous Luxuriating in Gardens and Springs
- Innal-muttaqeena fee jannatin wa 'uyoon: the God-conscious dwell amid paradisiacal gardens and springs.

#### [14] Receiving What Their Lord Grants Them
- Rejoicing in the sublime honors and bounties their Lord bestows upon them for their past Ihsan.

#### [15] Sleeping But Little of the Night in Vigil
- Kanoo qaleelan minal-layli ma yahja'oon: they used to sleep but a small portion of the night in prayer.

#### [16] Imploring Forgiveness in the Hours Before Dawn
- Wa bil-ashari hum yastaghfiroon: and in the quiet hours before dawn, they earnestly seek divine pardon.

## Plate 03: POOR RIGHTS, SIGNS IN SOULS & SKY SUSTENANCE
**Section:** `PLATE 03 : SIGNS IN SOULS` | **Focus:** Pillars 05 & 06: Rights of Beggars and Deprived, Signs Within Souls & Sustenance in the Sky True as Speech

### PILLAR 05: RIGHTS OF THE POOR & SIGNS WITHIN SOULS
*Known Right for Sa'il and Mahroom, Signs on Earth & Signs in Your Own Selves*

#### [17] In Their Wealth is a Right for the Destitute
- Wa fee amwalihim haqqun lis-sa'ili wal-mahroom: in their wealth is an established right for the petitioner and deprived.

#### [18] Economic Compassion as an Article of Faith
- Recognizing that wealth is a divine trust to be shared with those who ask and those too dignified to beg.

#### [19] Signs on the Earth for Those of Certainty
- And across the terrestrial earth are manifest signs and marvels for those endowed with firm certainty.

#### [20] And Within Your Very Souls: Will You Not See?
- Wa fee anfusikum afala tubsiroon: and within your own biological and conscious selves—will you not see?!

### PILLAR 06: YOUR SUSTENANCE IS IN THE HEAVENS
*Wa Fees-Sama'i Rizqukum, Infallible Divine Oath & True as Your Very Speech*

#### [21] Your Sustenance and Destiny Lie in the Heavens
- Wa fees-sama'i rizqukum wa ma too'adoon: and in the celestial sky is your provision and all that you are promised!

#### [22] By the Lord of the Heavens and Earth It is True
- Fa-wa Rabbi as-sama'i wal-ardi innahu la-haqq: so by the Lord of the cosmos, this promise is absolute truth!

#### [23] As Objective and Real as the Fact That You Speak
- Mithla ma annakum tantiqoon: just as certain, objective, and real as the reality that you are capable of speech!

#### [24] Eradicating Worldly Anxiety Over Livelihood
- Liberating the human spirit from bowing to mortal masters for sustenance, anchoring hope in God alone.

## Plate 04: THE GUESTS OF IBRAHIM & SACRED HOSPITALITY
**Section:** `PLATE 04 : SACRED HOSPITALITY` | **Focus:** Pillars 07 & 08: Honored Angelic Guests, The Fat Roasted Calf & Glad Tidings of a Knowledgeable Son

### PILLAR 07: THE HONORED GUESTS & THE ROASTED CALF
*Hal Ataka Hadeethu Dayfi Ibrahim, Salam Answered with Salam & The Roasted Calf*

#### [25] Has the Chronicle of Ibrahim's Guests Reached You?
- Has there come to you the story of the honored, celestial guests of Ibrahim (Dayfi Ibrahima al-Mukrameen)?

#### [26] Entering with 'Peace', He Replied 'Peace!'
- When they entered upon him and said: 'Peace!' He replied: 'Peace! A people unfamiliar to us.'

#### [27] Hurrying to His Household Without Delay
- He slipped away quietly and swiftly to his family to prepare an abundant, generous reception.

#### [28] Presenting a Fat Roasted Calf to the Guests
- Bringing out a succulent roasted calf ('Ijlun Haneed), placing it before them: Will you not partake?

### PILLAR 08: FEAR CALMED & GLAD TIDINGS OF A WISE SON
*Concealing Fear When Hands Touched Not Food, Glad Tidings of Ishaq & Barren Wife*

#### [29] Concealing Inward Apprehension from Them
- When their hands did not reach toward the meat, he harbored a secret fear; they said: Fear not!

#### [30] Glad Tidings of a Knowledgeable Boy (Ghulam 'Aleem)
- They gave him the astonishing glad tidings of a son endowed with profound spiritual wisdom (Ishaq).

#### [31] His Wife Crying Out in Wonder and Awe
- His elderly wife Sarah came forward crying out, striking her forehead: A barren, elderly woman?!

#### [32] Thus Said Your Lord: All-Wise, All-Knowing
- They replied: Thus has declared your Lord; indeed, He alone is the All-Wise, the All-Knowing!

## Plate 05: RETRIBUTION UPON SODOM & THE RESCUE
**Section:** `PLATE 05 : SODOM RETRIBUTION` | **Focus:** Pillars 09 & 10: The Retributive Mission to Transgressors & Extraction of the Single Muslim Household

### PILLAR 09: THE ERRAND OF DIVINE RETRIBUTION
*What is Your Mission?, Sent to Criminal People & Raining Stones of Baked Clay*

#### [33] Ibrahim Inquires: What is Your Ultimate Mission?
- He said: What then is your monumental errand, O celestial messengers sent from on high?

#### [34] Dispatched Against a Degenerate Criminal Nation
- They replied: We have been dispatched against an iniquitous, persistently corrupt people (Sodom).

#### [35] Raining Down Upon Them Stones of Baked Clay
- To unleash upon them devastating projectiles of marked, baked clay (Hijaratan min Teen).

#### [36] Orchestrated by Your Lord for the Transgressors
- Specially designated and marked in divine foreknowledge for the reckless, unrepentant transgressors.

### PILLAR 10: EXTRACTION OF THE FAITHFUL HOUSEHOLD
*Extracting the Believers, A Single Household of Muslims & Lasting Sign for the Fearful*

#### [37] Evacuating Whoever Was Within of True Believers
- So We extracted and delivered whoever was residing within the settlement of genuine faithful souls.

#### [38] Found Therein Only a Single Muslim Household
- Fa-ma wajadna feeha ghayra baytin minal-muslimeen: We found therein only a solitary household of Muslims (Lut).

#### [39] The Rest of the Metropolis Overturned in Doom
- While the remainder of the corrupt society was overturned and annihilated under showering stones.

#### [40] Leaving Behind a Lasting Monument for the Fearful
- And We left behind therein an enduring historical sign for those who fear agonizing retribution.

## Plate 06: THE FIVE CHRONICLES OF PROPHETIC MEMORY
**Section:** `PLATE 06 : PROPHETIC MEMORY` | **Focus:** Pillars 11 & 12: Moses Confronting Pharaoh's Might & The Vanished Nations of 'Ad, Thamud and Noah

### PILLAR 11: MUSA CONFRONTING PHARAOH'S POWER
*Musa with Manifest Authority, Pharaoh Turning on Pillars & Cast into Sea*

#### [41] And in Moses: Sent with Clear Authority
- And in the chronicle of Moses when We dispatched him unto Pharaoh bearing undeniable, manifest authority.

#### [42] Pharaoh Turned Away Relying on His Legions
- Yet Pharaoh turned haughtily away relying on his military pillars, barking: A sorcerer or a madman!

#### [43] Seized Him and His Armies and Cast into the Sea
- So We seized him and his vast armies, casting them into the deep sea while he was utterly blameworthy.

#### [44] The Collapse of Arrogant Imperial Hubris
- Demonstrating that mortal empires crumbling into water cannot resist the sovereign decree of God.

### PILLAR 12: THE VANISHED TRAGEDIES OF 'AD, THAMUD & NUH
*'Ad Struck by Barren Gale, Thamud Stunned by Thunderbolt & Nuh's Generation*

#### [45] 'Ad Struck by the Barren Gale (Reeh al-'Aqeen)
- And in 'Ad when We unleashed upon them the sterile, devastating gale that left nothing standing.

#### [46] Reducing Everything to Rotting Ancient Dust
- Rendering everything it touched like rotten, crumbling debris: physical might wiped out in hours.

#### [47] Thamud Stunned by the Piercing Thunderbolt
- And in Thamud when they were told: Enjoy yourselves for a while! Then the thunderbolt struck them down.

#### [48] And the Generation of Nuh Sunk of Old
- And the people of Nuh before them: indeed, they were a persistently corrupt, defiant people.

## Plate 07: EXPANDING COSMOS & FLEEING UNTO ALLAH
**Section:** `PLATE 07 : COSMIC EXPANSION` | **Focus:** Pillars 13 & 14: Universe Built with Power and Expanding, Earth Spread & Fa-firroo ila Allah (Flee to God!)

### PILLAR 13: THE EXPANDING COSMIC CANOPY
*Was-Sama'a Banaynaha bi-Aydin, Inna la-Moosi'oon & Everything Created in Pairs*

#### [49] We Built the Universe with Boundless Power
- Was-sama'a banaynaha bi-aydin: and the celestial universe We constructed with majestic creative power.

#### [50] And Indeed We Are Vastly Expanding It (Moosi'oon)
- Wa inna la-moosi'oon: and indeed, We are expanding it continuously across space and cosmic majesty!

#### [51] The Earth Spread Out: How Excellent the Spreader!
- Wal-arda farashnaha fa-ni'mal-mahidon: and the terrestrial earth We spread wide as an hospitable cradle.

#### [52] And of Everything We Created Pairs to Reflect
- Wa min kulli shay'in khalaqna zawjayni la'allakum tadhakkaroon: creating all things in pairs for reflection.

### PILLAR 14: FLEE UNTO ALLAH! (FA-FIRROO ILA ALLAH)
*Fa-firroo ila Allah, Clear Warner & Set Not Beside Him Any Rival Deity*

#### [53] Flee Unconditionally Unto Allah!
- Fa-firroo ila Allah: flee therefore from ignorance, sin, and fleeting illusions directly unto God!

#### [54] I Am a Plain, Clear Warner to You from Him
- Innee lakum minhu nadheerun mubeen: indeed, I am an earnest, clear warner sent from Him unto you.

#### [55] Set Not Beside Allah Any Fabricated Deity
- Wa la taj'aloo ma'allahi ilahan akhar: do not set up alongside Allah any other deity or mortal partner.

#### [56] The Ultimate Sovereign Sanctuary of Souls
- True safety and tranquility are found nowhere in the universe except in returning to the Creator.

## Plate 08: THE PURPOSE OF CREATION: LI-YA'BUDOON
**Section:** `PLATE 08 : SOVEREIGN PURPOSE` | **Focus:** Pillars 15 & 16: I Created Jinn and Men Only to Worship Me & Allah the Supreme Provider (Ar-Razzaq)

### PILLAR 15: I CREATED JINN AND MEN ONLY TO WORSHIP ME
*Wa Ma Khalaqtu al-Jinna wal-Insa Illa Li-Ya'budoon, No Sustenance Demanded*

#### [57] The Grand Ontological Charter of Existence
- Wa ma khalaqtu al-jinna wal-insa illa li-ya'budoon: I did not create jinn and mankind except to worship Me!

#### [58] Worship as the Realization of Human Potential
- Ibada is not mere ritual, but the total alignment of the heart, mind, and life with divine pleasure.

#### [59] I Desire No Worldly Sustenance from Them
- Ma ureedu minhum min rizqin: I seek no provision, nourishment, or economic return from My creation.

#### [60] Nor Do I Demand That They Feed Me
- Wa ma ureedu an yut'imoon: nor do I require that they feed Me; the Creator is transcendent above need.

### PILLAR 16: ALLAH IS THE ALL-POWERFUL SUPREME PROVIDER
*Innallaha Huwa ar-Razzaq Dhu'l-Quwwati al-Mateen & The Portion of Oppressors*

#### [61] Indeed, Allah is the Supreme Provider (Ar-Razzaq)
- Innallaha Huwa ar-Razzaqu: indeed, it is Allah alone who is the unceasing, universal Provider.

#### [62] Possessor of All-Consuming, Unshakeable Might
- Dhu'l-Quwwati al-Mateen: Owner of supreme, invincible power and unshakeable, resolute strength.

#### [63] The Oppressors Will Incur Their Appointed Portion
- For those who do wrong is an appointed share of punishment like the share of their ancient fellows.

#### [64] Woe to the Deniers on Their Promised Day
- So let them not urge Me to hasten it! Woe to those who disbelieve from their Day which they were promised!
