Plate 01: THE SETTING STAR & THE CELESTIAL VISION

Pillars 01 & 02: Wan-Najmi Idha Hawa, Infallible Wahy & Gabriel on the Highest Horizon at Two Bow-Lengths

PLATE 01 : THE STAR DESCENT
PILLAR 01: THE OATH OF AN-NAJM & PROPHETIC INFALLIBILITY
Wan-Najmi Idha Hawa, Ma Dalla Sahibukum & In Huwa Illa Wahyun Yooha
01 By the Star When It Descends (Wan-Najm)
  • Wan-najmi idha hawa: by the star as it sinks below the horizon in celestial choreography.
02 Your Companion Has Not Strayed Nor Erred
  • Ma dalla sahibukum wa ma ghawa: your companion Muhammad has neither strayed nor deviated into error.
03 He Does Not Speak from Personal Caprice
  • Wa ma yantiqu 'anil-hawa: he does not utter speech from selfish desires, caprice, or human ambition.
04 Pure Revelation Inspired from on High
  • In huwa illa wahyun yooha: it is nothing other than an authentic divine inspiration revealed to him.
PILLAR 02: GABRIEL'S APPARITION ON THE HORIZON
'Allamahu Shadeed al-Quwa, Two Bow-Lengths (Qaba Qawsayn) & Heart Belied Not
05 Taught by One Mighty in Power (Gabriel)
  • 'Allamahu shadeedu al-quwa: taught by the mighty archangel Gabriel endowed with profound strength.
06 Standing Revealed on the Highest Horizon
  • Dhu mirratin fastawa: possessing sound celestial form, he appeared majestic upon the highest horizon.
07 Drawing Near at Two Bow-Lengths Distance
  • Then he approached and descended until he was at the distance of two bow-lengths (*Qaba Qawsayn*) or nearer!
08 The Heart Belied Not What It Witnessed
  • Ma kadhaba al-fu'adu ma ra'a: the prophetic heart did not lie about what his physical eyes beheld.

Sidrat al-Muntaha, Jannat al-Ma'wa & Divine Splendor Shrouding the Tree

12 When the Lote-Tree Was Shrouded in Divine Radiance
  • Idh yaghsha as-sidrata ma yaghsha: when there enveloped the tree that which enveloped of sublime light.

  • Ma zagha al-basaru wa ma tagha: the eye of the Prophet swerved not aside, nor did it exceed its bound.

  • Demonstrating absolute discipline and reverent focus in the closest created proximity to God.
15 Witnessing the Greatest Signs of His Lord
  • Laqad ra'a min ayati Rabbihi al-kubra: he certainly beheld of the monumental, supreme signs of his Lord!

  • Cementing the authenticity of the heavenly journey as an objective physical and spiritual reality.

PLATE 03 : IDOLS DEMOLISHED
PILLAR 05: HOLLOWNESS OF AL-LAT, 'UZZA & MANAT
Al-Lat, 'Uzza, Manat, Male vs Female Division & Mere Fabricated Names

  • A-lakumu adh-dhakaru wa lahu al-untha: do you attribute sons to yourselves and daughters to God?!
19 That is an Unjust and Disgraceful Partition!
  • Tilka idhan qismatun deeza: that is an intrinsically absurd, biased, and disgraceful division!
20 Merely Invented Names You and Your Forefathers Conjured
  • In hiya illa asma'un sammaytumooha: they are nothing but hollow names you and your ancestors invented!
PILLAR 06: FOLLOWING CONJECTURE & SOUL CAPRICE
Following Az-Zann and Desires, Guidance Arrived & To Allah Belongs the End
21 They Follow Nothing but Frivolous Conjectures
  • In yattabi'oona illa az-zanna wa ma tahwa al-anfus: they pursue nothing except speculation and base desires.
22 When Pristine Guidance Has Arrived from Their Lord
  • Even though the manifest, unambiguous guidance has reached them directly from their Creator.
23 Does the Human Being Have Whatever He Wishes?
  • Am lil-insani ma tamanna: does mortal man possess a sovereign right to whatever fantasy he desires?
24 To Allah Alone Belongs the Hereafter and the World
  • Fa-lillahi al-akhiratu wal-oola: to Allah alone belongs the eternal Hereafter and this transient earthly realm.

PLATE 04 : DIVINE EXPANSIVENESS
PILLAR 07: VAST FORGIVENESS & MORAL REALISM
Avoiding Major Sins Except Lamam, Wasi'u al-Maghfirah & Do Not Claim Self-Purity

  • Alladheena yajtaniboona kaba'ira al-ithmi wal-fawahisha: those who stay clear of mortal sins and obscenity.
26 Except for Inadvertent Minor Lapses (Al-Lamam)
  • Illa al-lamam: except fleeting minor lapses swiftly followed by sincere remorse and rectification.
27 Indeed, Your Lord is Limitless in Forgiveness
  • Inna Rabbaka wasi'u al-maghfirah: your Lord is boundlessly expansive in forgiving His repentant servants.
28 Do Not Proclaim Your Own Souls Pure
  • Fala tuzakkoo anfusakum: do not boast of your righteousness; He knows best who possesses genuine Taqwa.
PILLAR 08: THE SCROLLS OF MOSES & IBRAHIM
Suhuf of Moses and Ibrahim Who Fulfilled, No Bearer Bears Another & Man Earns Striving

  • Have they not been informed of what was written in the ancient scriptures revealed to Moses?

  • Wa Ibrahima alladhee waffa: and of Ibrahim who fulfilled every single trial and covenant with perfection.
31 No Soul Shall Bear the Burden of Another
  • Alla taziru waziratun wizra ukhra: no burdened soul shall bear the moral load of another on Judgment Day.
32 Man Receives Only That for Which He Strove
  • Wa an laysa lil-insani illa ma sa'a: and human beings shall inherit nothing except what they actively strove for.

  • Wa anna 'alayhi an-nash'ata al-ukhra: and upon Him rests the certainty of the second physical resurrection.
39 It is He Who Enriches and Bestows Possessions
  • Wa annahu huwa aghna wa aqna: it is He who grants independent wealth and provides lasting contentment.
