Plate 01: THE TWIN OATHS & THE RECONSTRUCTED FINGERTIPS

Pillars 01 & 02: Biyawmi al-Qiyamah, An-Nafsi al-Lawwamah, Assembling Bones & Shaping the Bananah

PLATE 01 : OATHS & FINGERTIPS
PILLAR 01: THE OATH OF RESURRECTION & SELF-REPROACH
La Uqsimu bi-Yawmi al-Qiyamah wa la Uqsimu bin-Nafsi al-Lawwamah
01 The Sovereign Oath by Judgment Day
  • I swear by the Day of Resurrection, when all worldly accounts are summoned for cosmic trial.
02 The Oath by the Self-Reproaching Soul
  • And I swear by the reproachful conscience (An-Nafsi al-Lawwamah) that constantly audits its own shortcomings.
03 The Internal Moral Courtroom
  • The human conscience itself serves as an internal witness that accountability is woven into the fabric of creation.
04 The Cynical Question: Will God Gather Bones?
  • Does man presume that We will never assemble his scattered, decomposed, decayed earthly bones?
PILLAR 02: RECONSTRUCTING THE VERY FINGERTIPS
Bala Qadireena 'ala an Nusawwiya Bananah & Desiring Sin
05 Yes Indeed! Able to Reconstruct Fingertips
  • Bala qadireena 'ala an nusawwiya bananah: yea, We are fully able to restore even his exact fingertips!
06 The Miracle of Unique Dermatoglyphics
  • Reconstructing the microscopic ridges, fingerprints, and bone joints with absolute anatomical precision.
07 Desiring to Continue in Sins Ahead
  • Nay, man desires to continue sinning forward in reckless abandon, demanding: 'When is this Day of Resurrection?'
08 The Moral Denial Behind Skepticism
  • Skepticism regarding Judgment Day is driven not by intellectual inquiry, but by a desire to sin without guilt.

Plate 02: THE DAZED EYE & THE ECLIPSED MOON

Pillars 03 & 04: Bariqa al-Basar, Khasafa al-Qamar, Sun and Moon Conjoined & Where is the Refuge?

PLATE 02 : COSMIC ECLIPSE
PILLAR 03: CELESTIAL ALIGNMENT & DISSOLUTION
Fa-idha Bariqa al-Basar, Khasafa al-Qamar & Jumi'a ash-Shamsu wal-Qamar
09 When the Human Eye is Dazed with Shock
  • Fa-idha bariqa al-basar: when eyesight is blinded and dazzled by the sudden terrifying cosmic light.
10 The Moon Plunged into Eternal Eclipse
  • Wa khasafa al-qamar: and the moon loses all its luminescence, darkened and stripped of reflective beauty.
11 Sun and Moon Conjoined Together
  • Wa jumi'a ash-shamsu wal-qamar: the sun and moon joined together in gravitational and celestial collision.
12 The Collapse of the Terrestrial Clock
  • The two great luminaries that calculated human months and seasons crash in cosmic termination.
PILLAR 04: THE DESPERATE PLEA: WHERE TO FLEE?
Ayna al-Mafar, Kalla la Wazar & Ila Rabbika al-Mustaqarr
13 Man Will Cry: Where is the Escape?
  • Yaqoolu al-insanu yawma'idhin ayna al-mafar: on that Day man will cry in panic: 'Where is the refuge?!'
14 Never! There is No Fortress of Refuge
  • Kalla la wazar: absolutely never! There is no mountain cave, underground bunker, or fortress to shield you.
15 Unto Your Lord is the Sole Final Rest
  • Ila Rabbika yawma'idhin al-mustaqarr: unto your Lord alone on that Day is the final, permanent settlement.
16 Complete Foreclosure of All Human Options
  • Standing transparently before the Creator with zero escape routes and zero allies.

Plate 03: THE FORENSIC AUDIT & THE SPEED OF REVELATION

Pillars 05 & 06: Informed of First and Last, Man as Witness on Himself & La Tuharrik bihi Lisanaka

PLATE 03 : AUDIT & REVELATION
PILLAR 05: INFORMED OF ALL DEEDS AND EXCUSES
Yunabba'u al-Insan, Balil-Insanu 'ala Nafsihi Baseerah & Ma'adheer
17 Informed of What He Sent and Kept Back
  • Man will be informed on that Day of what he sent forward in deeds and what he left behind in legacy.
18 Man is a Piercing Witness Over Himself
  • Balil-insanu 'ala nafsihi baseerah: nay, man is a keen, unmistakable witness against his own soul.
19 Even Though He Puts Forth Excuses
  • Wa law alqa ma'adheerah: even though he presents elaborate philosophical excuses and rationalizations.
20 The Inefficacy of Self-Justification
  • The organs and conscious memory testify directly, making self-deception completely impossible.
PILLAR 06: DO NOT HASTEN YOUR TONGUE WITH IT
La Tuharrik bihi Lisanaka li-Ta'jala Bih & Inna 'Alayna Jam'ahu
21 Move Not Your Tongue in Haste
  • Do not move your tongue with the Qur'an to rush through it out of anxiety that you might forget.
22 Upon Us is Its Collection and Recitation
  • Inna 'alayna jam'ahu wa qur'anah: it is upon Us to assemble it in your breast and cause you to recite it perfectly.
23 Follow Its Recitation When Read
  • So when We have recited it through Gabriel, follow its recitation with disciplined, quiet listening.
24 Upon Us is Its Complete Clarification
  • Thumma inna 'alayna bayanah: then it is upon Us to explain its deep meanings and legal application.

Plate 04: LOVING THE FLEETING & THE RADIANT FACES

Pillars 07 & 08: Loving Al-'Ajilah, Leaving Al-Akhirah, Wujoohun Nadhirah & Gazing at the Lord

PLATE 04 : THE BEATIFIC VISION
PILLAR 07: ADDICTION TO THE FLEETING DUNYA
Tuhibboona al-'Ajilah wa Tadharoona al-Akhirah
25 Nay! You Love the Immediate Transient World
  • Kalla bal tuhibboona al-'ajilah: nay, the root failure is your obsessive love for the fleeting present dunya.
26 And You Abandon the Eternal Hereafter
  • Wa tadharoona al-akhirah: while you heedlessly cast behind your backs the everlasting life of the Hereafter.
27 The Pathology of Short-Term Gratification
  • Trading an infinite ocean of eternal peace for a few drops of temporary, anxious worldly pleasure.
28 The Great Divide of Human Destinies
  • The parting of humanity into two distinct visual archetypes: radiant illuminated faces versus darkened despair.
PILLAR 08: RADIANT FACES GAZING AT THEIR LORD
Wujoohun Yawma'idhin Nadhirah, Ila Rabbiha Nadhirah & The Beatific Vision
29 Faces Radiant with Transcendent Joy
  • Wujoohun yawma'idhin nadhirah: faces on that Day will be glowing, blooming, and radiant with divine grace.
30 Gazing Directly at Their Creator
  • Ila Rabbiha nadhirah: looking directly upon the majestic Countenance of their Lord without veil.
31 The Zenith of Paradise Delights
  • The Beatific Vision: the greatest reward of the believers, surpassing all physical gardens and palaces.
32 Purity Reflecting Divine Glory
  • The light of Allah's beauty reflected in the joyful eyes of those who spent their earthly lives in faithful longing.

Plate 05: DESPAIRING FACES & THE SOUL IN THE COLLARBONES

Pillars 09 & 10: Wujoohun Basirah, Expecting Spine-Breaking Torment & The Soul at the Taraqee

PLATE 05 : THE DYING THROAT
PILLAR 09: SCOWLING FACES AWAITING THE BACKBREAKER
Wujoohun Yawma'idhin Basirah, Tazunnu an Yuf'ala biha Faqirah
33 Faces Sullen and Dismal with Horror
  • And other faces on that Day will be scowling (Basirah), pitch-black, and contorted with sheer terror.
34 Expecting a Spine-Breaking Calamity
  • Tazunnu an yuf'ala biha faqirah: knowing with certainty that a back-breaking catastrophe is about to fall upon them.
35 The Contrast of the Two Countenances
  • The external face mirrors the internal soul: purity produces radiant light, while guilt produces crushing darkness.
36 The Finality of the Great Inversion
  • The worldly mockers who sneered in arrogance are now frozen in irreversible, trembling disgrace.
PILLAR 10: WHEN THE SOUL REACHES THE COLLARBONES
Kalla Idha Balaghati at-Taraqee, Wa Qeela Man Raq & Sakrat al-Mawt
37 When the Soul Reaches the Collarbones
  • Kalla idha balaghati at-taraqee: nay, when the departing soul reaches the clavicle collarbones in the agony of death,
38 And It is Cried: Who Can Cure Him?
  • Wa qeela man raq: and family cry out in desperation: 'Is there any physician or healer who can rescue him?'
39 Certainty That It is the Final Departure
  • And the dying soul concludes with absolute certainty that this is the final, irrevocable parting from Dunya.
40 Legs Entwined with Legs in Agony
  • Wat-taffati as-saqu bis-saq: and the leg is wound with the other leg in the convulsion of the death struggle.

Plate 06: THE DRIVEN MARCH & THE SHAMELESS STRUT

Pillars 11 & 12: Ila Rabbika al-Masaq, Neither Believed Nor Prayed & Strutting to Family in Pride

PLATE 06 : THE DRIVEN MARCH
PILLAR 11: THE MARCH TO THE SOVEREIGN LORD
Ila Rabbika Yawma'idhin al-Masaq & The Irreversible Transit
41 Unto Your Lord is the Driven March
  • Ila Rabbika yawma'idhin al-masaq: unto your Lord on that Day is the driving of all souls for judgment.
42 Neither Affirmed Truth Nor Prayed
  • Fala saddaqa wa la salla: for in worldly life, he neither believed in the truth nor established prayer.
43 Denied and Turned Away in Defiance
  • Wa lakin kadh-dhaba wa tawalla: but rather he actively belied divine revelation and turned away in contempt.
44 The Double Crime of the Arrogant
  • Abandoning both ideological truth (Sadaqah) and liturgical submission (Salah).
PILLAR 12: STRUTTING HOME IN VANITY & THE NEAR WOE
Yatamatta, Awla Laka fa-Awla & The Approaching Destruction
45 Strutting to His Kin in Conceit
  • Thumma dhahaba ila ahlihi yatamatta: then he swaggered back to his family strutting in haughty self-satisfaction.
46 Woe Unto You, and Woe!
  • Awla laka fa-awla: woe to you O arrogant denier, and again woe! Appropriate is destruction unto you!
47 Then Woe Unto You, and Woe!
  • Thumma awla laka fa-awla: then woe to you, and again woe! Approaching nearer and nearer is your doom!
48 The Prophetic Confrontation of Abu Jahl
  • The historical words spoken by the Prophet ﷺ to Abu Jahl outside the Ka'bah, immortalized in divine scripture.

Plate 07: THE INQUEST OF SUDA & THE SEMINAL DROP

Pillars 13 & 14: Ayahsabu al-Insanu an Yutraka Suda, Nutfatan min Maniyy & The Creation of Male and Female

PLATE 07 : BIOLOGICAL ARGUMENT
PILLAR 13: DOES MAN THINK HE WILL BE LEFT AIMLESS?
A-Yahsabu al-Insanu an Yutraka Suda & The Denial of Moral Purpose
49 Does Man Presume He is Left Aimless?
  • A-yahsabu al-insanu an yutraka suda: does man think that he will be left neglected, without moral reckoning?
50 The Rejection of Secular Nihilism
  • Creation is not an accidental accident; humanity was not endowed with consciousness to dissolve into nothingness.
51 Every Creature Possesses Divine Order
  • If the microscopic cell operates with purpose, how could human moral choices be deemed irrelevant?
52 The Inevitability of Moral Accountability
  • The very concept of justice demands that the oppressor and oppressed meet before an impartial Judge.
PILLAR 14: THE MIRACLE OF SEMINAL FASHIONING
Alam Yaku Nutfatan, 'Alaqatan, Fasawwa & The Two Genders
53 Was He Not an Emitted Seminal Drop?
  • Alam yaku nutfatan min maniyyin yumna: was he not a mere drop of fluid emitted into the womb?
54 Then a Clinging Clot ('Alaqah)
  • Then he became a clinging embryo, whereupon Allah fashioned and proportioned his physical form with balance.
55 Fashioning the Dual Genders: Male & Female
  • Fa-ja'ala minhu az-zawjayni adh-dhakara wal-untha: and He produced from that microscopic drop the twin pairs: male and female.
56 Is He Not Able to Revive the Dead?
  • A-laysa dhalika bi-qadirin 'ala an yuhyiya al-mawta: is not the One who performed that original creation fully able to revive the dead?

Plate 08: SUNNI EXEGESIS & TADABBUR OF AL-QIYAMAH

Pillars 15 & 16: Bukhari 7437 on Ru'yatullah, The Reconstructed Bananah & Subhanaka fa-Bala

PLATE 08 : SUNNI SYNTHESIS
PILLAR 15: CANONICAL EXEGESIS OF RU'YATULLAH
Sahih al-Bukhari 7437: Seeing the Lord Like the Full Moon
57 Seeing the Lord as You See the Full Moon
  • In Bukhari 7437, the Prophet ﷺ said: 'Indeed, you will see your Lord with your own eyes just as you see this full moon.'
58 Sunni Consensus on the Beatific Vision
  • Ahl as-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah affirm that believers will behold Allah in the Hereafter without modal confinement (Bi-la kayf).
59 The Linguistic Analysis of 'Ila Rabbiha Nadhirah'
  • Grammarians note that 'Nadhara' followed by the preposition 'Ila' uniquely signifies optical vision with the eyes.
60 The Denial of the Wicked from the Vision
  • In contrast, disbelievers are veiled from seeing their Lord ('Innahum 'an Rabbihim yawma'idhin la-mahjooboon').
PILLAR 16: LIVING WITH AN-NAFSI AL-LAWWAMAH
Cultivating the Self-Reproaching Conscience & Subhanaka fa-Bala
61 Nurturing the Conscience (Al-Lawwamah)
  • The hallmark of the righteous believer is constantly reproaching himself: 'What did I intend by that word? How can I improve?'
62 The Deathbed Clavicle Reflection
  • Pondering the moment the soul reaches the collarbones (At-Taraqee), when all worldly resumes dissolve.
63 The Liturgical Response to 75:40
  • Sunnah practice records that when reciting the final verse, one responds: 'Subhanaka fa-bala!' (Glory be to You, yes indeed You are able!).
64 Daily Readiness for the Driven March
  • Living every day with consciousness that our final march is 'Ila Rabbika al-masaq', preparing light for our countenances.
