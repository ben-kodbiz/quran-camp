  • Inna 'adhaba Rabbika la-waqi': indeed, the threatened punishment of your Lord will surely come to pass!

  • Ma lahu min dafi': there is no power in creation capable of averting, blocking, or preventing it.
07 The Day the Sky Shakes with Violent Motion
  • On the Day when the heavens shudder and reel with tremendous cosmic convulsions.
08 And Mountains Move Shifting Away
