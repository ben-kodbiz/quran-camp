### 16:9 Landscape Cartography • 8 Plates • 16 Thematic Pillars • 64 Analytical Cards
**Source Authority:** Classical Sunni Exegesis (Tabari, Ibn Kathir, Qurtubi, Razi, Baghawi, Ibn al-Qayyim)

## Plate 01: PURPOSEFUL CREATION & THE ANCIENT TRACE
**Section:** `PLATE 01 : COSMIC PURPOSE` | **Focus:** Pillars 01 & 02: Ha-Meem & Cosmic Purpose, Deconstructing False Deities & The Trace of Ancient Knowledge

### PILLAR 01: HA-MEEM & HEAVENS CREATED IN TRUTH
*Bi'l-Haqqi wa Ajalin Musamma, Transcendent Truth & Warning to the Careless*

#### [01] The Inauguration of Ha-Meem
- Ha-Meem: the seventh and crowning opening of the Hawameem cycle of divine revelation.

#### [02] Heavens and Earth Created in Truth
- Ma khalaqna as-samawati wal-arda wa ma baynahuma illa bil-haqq: cosmos created with purposeful truth.

#### [03] The Appointed Term of Existence
- Everything bound to an appointed duration (Ajalin Musamma), balancing physical law with moral accountability.

#### [04] Careless Turning from Warning
- Those who disbelieve stubbornly turn their backs away from the warnings sent down to awaken their consciences.

### PILLAR 02: THE ILLUSION OF RIVAL INTERCESSORS
*Show Me What They Created, Partners in Skies & Atharatin min 'Ilm*

#### [05] Show Me Any Sector of Creation They Made
- Challenge the polytheists: Show Me what portion of the physical earth their idols created ex nihilo!

#### [06] Do They Possess Partnership in the Skies?
- Do they claim any share or architectural partnership in governing celestial orbits and galaxies?

#### [07] Bring a Scripture or Trace of Ancient Knowledge
- Produce a verified scripture preceding this or an authentic remnant of scholarly knowledge (Atharatin min 'Ilm)!

#### [08] The Blind Stupidity of Supplicating Stone
- Who is more astray than one who calls upon lifeless entities that can never respond until the Day of Resurrection?

## Plate 02: THE SLANDER OF SORCERY & THE ARABIC CONFIRMATION
**Section:** `PLATE 02 : DIVINE EPISTEMOLOGY` | **Focus:** Pillars 03 & 04: The Charge of Manifest Sorcery, Prophetic Novelty Negated & The Arabic Confirmation of Moses

### PILLAR 03: REJECTING REVELATION AS MANIFEST MAGIC
*Sihrun Mubeen Slander, Ma Kuntu Bid'an & Knowing Not Tomorrow*

#### [09] This Is Clear Sorcery (Sihrun Mubeen)
- When pristine truth comes to them, the arrogant dismiss divine speech as intoxicating sorcery.

#### [10] I Am No Unprecedented Novelty
- Qul ma kuntu bid'an minar-rusul: declare that I am not an unprecedented innovator among the messengers.

#### [11] I Know Not What Befalls Me or You Tomorrow
- I do not claim knowledge of the unseen tomorrow; I merely follow that which is inspired into my heart.

#### [12] The Pure Calling of a Clear Warner
- I am nothing other than an earnest, plain warner (Nazeerun Mubeen) calling mankind to eternal safety.

### PILLAR 04: THE TORAH WITNESS & THE ARABIC TONGUE
*Witness from Banu Israel, Elite Disdain & Lisanan 'Arabiyyan*

#### [13] A Scholarly Witness from the Children of Israel
- If this Qur'an is from Allah and an Israelite scholar testifies to its divine truth yet you disbelieve?

#### [14] The Elite Fallacy: Had It Been Good They Preceded Us
- Pagan oligarchs boast: Had this religion possessed any goodness, these weak slaves would never have beaten us to it!

#### [15] The Book of Moses as Guide and Mercy
- Preceded by the Scripture of Moses as a guiding light and sovereign mercy for all seeking truth.

#### [16] A Corroborating Scripture in Eloquent Arabic
- Kitabun musaddiqun lisanan 'arabiyyan: confirming revelation in clear Arabic to warn wrongdoers and give glad tidings.

## Plate 03: FILIAL PIETY & THE FORTIETH YEAR COVENANT
**Section:** `PLATE 03 : MORAL COVENANT` | **Focus:** Pillars 05 & 06: Dutifulness to Parents, The Maternal Ordeal & The 40th Year Du'a vs The Rebellious Offspring

### PILLAR 05: HONORING PARENTS & THE 40TH YEAR SUPPLICATION
*Carried in Hardship, 30 Months Gestation & The Prayer of Gratitude*

#### [17] Commandment of Excellence to Parents (Ihsan)
- We commanded the human being to show sublime devotion and unconditional excellence to his parents.

#### [18] The Maternal Crucible of Gestation and Weaning
- His mother bore him with travail and delivered him in hardship: thirty months of bearing and suckling.

#### [19] Reaching Full Maturity at Forty Years
- When he reaches full physical strength and attains the reflective threshold of forty years of age.

#### [20] The Crowning Prayer for Righteous Offspring
- Rabbi awzi'nee an ashkura ni'matak: my Lord, enable me to be grateful and rectify for me my descendants!

### PILLAR 06: THE TRAGEDY OF THE REBELLIOUS SON
*Uffun Lakuma Contempt, Denial of Resurrection & The Tragic Loss*

#### [21] The Insolent Son Saying: Uff to You Both!
- He who snarls at his righteous parents: Fie on you! Do you promise me resurrection when generations vanished?

#### [22] Parents Weeping and Pleading for Faith
- Both parents implore Allah's aid, crying: Woe to you! Believe! Indeed, Allah's promise is truth!

#### [23] Dismissing Divine Warning as Ancient Fables
- He arrogantly sneers: This is nothing but old myths and fairy tales fabricated by ancient ancestors!

#### [24] Realization of Doom Upon Vanished Generations
- Those are the ones against whom the decree of doom is realized among bygone nations of jinn and men.

## Plate 04: THE TRAGEDY OF 'AD & THE DUNE OF EXTINCTION
**Section:** `PLATE 04 : THE SAND DUNES` | **Focus:** Pillars 07 & 08: Hud's Warning at the Dunes of Al-Ahqaf, The Dense Cloud Mistaken for Rain & The Searing Gale

### PILLAR 07: HUD'S ENVOY TO THE PEOPLE OF AL-AHQAF
*Brother of 'Ad at the Dunes, Worship Allah Alone & The Fear of Great Torment*

#### [25] Remember the Brother of 'Ad at the Sand Dunes
- Commemorate Hud when he warned his mighty nation amid the wind-swept sand ridges of Al-Ahqaf.

#### [26] Warners Before and Behind Him
- Pleading with his people: Worship none but Allah; indeed, I fear for you the punishment of a tremendous Day!

#### [27] Did You Come to Turn Us from Our Ancestral Gods?
- They shouted: Have you come to divert us from our deities? Bring upon us what you threaten if you are truthful!

#### [28] Knowledge is with Allah Alone
- Hud replied: True knowledge of the appointed hour rests with God alone; I merely convey my mandate.

### PILLAR 08: THE CLOUD MISTAKEN FOR LIFE-GIVING RAIN
*Dense Cloud Approaching Valleys, Reehun Feeha 'Adhab & Empty Dwellings*

#### [29] The Dense Black Cloud Rising in the Horizon
- When they saw a massive dark formation advancing across the desert sky toward their dried river valleys.

#### [30] Rejoicing in Delusion: This Cloud Brings Us Rain!
- They exulted foolishly: This is a heavy storm-cloud that will pour down refreshing rain upon our lands!

#### [31] Nay, It is the Wrath You Hastened!
- Nay, it is that which you demanded: a furious hurricane bearing an excruciating, devastating retribution!

#### [32] Nothing Left Visible Except Their Empty Mansions
- Destroying all life by the decree of its Lord, so that at dawn nothing remained visible except their desolate abodes.

*Endowed with Strength Beyond Quraysh, Hearing, Sight and Hearts Deprived*

#### [33] We Established Them in Might Far Beyond You
- We granted the nation of 'Ad physical strength, architecture, and longevity far surpassing your stature.

#### [34] Endowed with Hearing, Eyesight and Keen Intellects
- We endowed them with sharp hearing, keen vision, and perceptive hearts to perceive truth.

#### [35] Senses Rendered Utterly Useless Against Wrath
- Neither their hearing, sight, nor minds profited them when they obstinately rejected the signs of Allah.

#### [36] Enveloped by the Very Truth They Mocked
- And they were completely surrounded and overwhelmed by that cosmic retribution which they used to ridicule.

*Ruins Around Makkah, Diversified Signs & The Dispersal of Fabricated Lies*

#### [37] We Destroyed Many Civilizations Surrounding You
- Consider the extinct settlements around the Arabian peninsula destroyed for persistent transgressions.

#### [38] Signs Alternated and Repeated for Repentance
- We varied and repeated signs across history so that obstinate nations might turn back in sincere humility.

#### [39] Why Did Their Supposed Intercessors Not Save Them?
- Why did those false gods they worshiped as mediating intercessors not step forward to rescue them?

#### [40] Total Evaporation of Fabricated Lies
- Nay, their gods vanished into thin air: such was their deceit and the delusional mythology they fabricated.

*Jinn Redirected, Ansitool Listen in Silence & Returning as Preachers*

#### [41] We Directed Toward You a Cohort of Jinn
- When We turned toward you a delegation of jinn to listen attentively to the recitation of the Qur'an.

#### [42] Hush and Listen! (Ansitool)
- When they entered its presence, they commanded one another in solemn reverence: Listen in complete silence!

#### [43] Reverent Awe at the Celestial Words
- They absorbed the linguistic magnificence and spiritual majesty of divine speech without interruption.

#### [44] Returning to Their People as Earnest Warners
- When the recitation concluded, they hurried back to their communities transformed into passionate warners.

### PILLAR 12: THE JINN'S SERMON TO THEIR NATION
*Scripture After Moses, Confirming Truth & Refuge from Agonizing Torment*

#### [45] O Our People, We Heard a Book After Moses!
- They proclaimed: O our folk! We have listened to a divine scripture revealed after Moses!

#### [46] Confirming Ancient Truth & Guiding Straight
- Corroborating previous revelation and guiding unerringly to absolute truth and an upright path.

#### [47] Respond to the Divine Summoner (Da'iya Allah)
- O our people! Respond with conviction to Allah's caller and believe in Him; He will forgive your sins!

#### [48] He Who Rejects Cannot Escape on Earth
- Whoever fails to answer the divine summoner cannot escape God's reach on earth, nor find any protector.

## Plate 07: CREATION EX NIHILO & SOMATIC RESURRECTION
**Section:** `PLATE 07 : SOMATIC RESURRECTION` | **Focus:** Pillars 13 & 14: He Who Created Heavens Never Tired, Somatic Revival with Ease & The Inquest Before Hell

### PILLAR 13: THE UNTIRED CREATOR OF THE COSMOS
*Wa Lam Ya'ya bi-Khalqihinna, Reviving Dead Bones & Sovereign Omnipotence*

#### [49] Did They Not Contemplate the Genesis of Cosmos?
- Do they not realize that Allah created the vast heavens and earth without fatigue or exhaustion?

#### [50] Never Fatigued by Creating Galactic Systems
- Wa lam ya'ya bi-khalqihinna: He experienced no weariness or rest in forging the cosmic architecture.

#### [51] Able to Revive the Dead with Supreme Ease
- Is not such a boundless Creator fully capable of restoring life to dead bones and disintegrated flesh?

#### [52] Indeed, He is Omnipotent Over All Things
- Bala innahu 'ala kulli shay'in Qadeer: yes indeed, His sovereign power governs all dimensions of creation.

### PILLAR 14: THE SOLEMN ARREST BEFORE THE FIRE
*Is This Not the Objective Reality?, Swearing by the Lord & Tasting Retribution*

#### [53] The Day Transgressors Are Exposed to Hell
- On the Day when the defiant deniers are brought directly before the blazing furnace of Hellfire.

#### [54] The Divine Inquest: Is This Not the Truth?
- They are interrogated: Is this inescapable retribution not the absolute, objective reality?

#### [55] Their Broken Confession: Yes, by Our Lord!
- They cry out in absolute submission and despair: Yes, by our Lord, it is the undeniable truth!

#### [56] Taste the Punishment for Your Willful Denial
- The divine decree replies: Taste the torment because you used to obstinately deny the resurrection!

- Fasbir kama sabara ulu al-'azmi minar-rusul: endure with patience as did the resolute arch-messengers!

#### [58] The Five Resolute Giants of Faith
- Emulating the monumental perseverance of Nuh, Ibrahim, Musa, 'Isa, and Muhammad ﷺ.

#### [59] Do Not Seek to Hasten Punishment for Them
- Do not impatiently urge the swift descent of divine judgment upon the mockers; their day is appointed.

#### [60] Spiritual Steadfastness Across Adversity
- True prophetic fortitude maintains gentle mercy and steadfast conviction amidst fierce hostility.

#### [61] When They Witness What They Were Promised
- On the Day they see the terrifying reality of what was promised to them in the scriptures.

#### [62] Worldly Existence Vanishes into a Single Hour
- It will seem to them as though they had lingered on earth for no more than an hour of a single afternoon!

#### [63] Balagh: The Decisive Divine Conveyance
- Balagh: this revelation is a complete, pristine, and sufficient proclamation for all humanity!

#### [64] None Are Destroyed Except Defiant Transgressors
