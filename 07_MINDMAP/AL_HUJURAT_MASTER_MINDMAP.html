PLATE 01 : PROPHETIC PROTOCOLS
PILLAR 01: DO NOT PRECEDE ALLAH AND HIS MESSENGER
La Tuqaddimoo, Humility in Judgment & Subordinating Opinion to Revelation
01 Do Not Precede Allah and His Messenger
  • La tuqaddimoo bayna yaday Allah wa Rasoolih: do not advance your opinions or judgments before divine law.
02 Conscious Reverence of the All-Hearing
  • Wattaqoo Allah: be mindful of Allah, for He hears every whispered counsel and knows the secrets of hearts.
03 Subordinating Mortal Intellect to Revelation
  • True faith requires deferring personal intellect, tribal customs, and passions to prophetic guidance.
04 The Foundation of Islamic Jurisprudence
  • Classical jurists derived the legal principle: no human legislation can supersede an authentic text of revelation.
PILLAR 02: LOWERING VOICES BEFORE THE MESSENGER
Do Not Raise Voices, Lest Deeds Be Struck Void & Hearts Tested for Taqwa
05 Do Not Raise Your Voices Above the Prophet's
  • O you who believe, do not elevate your vocal tone above the voice of the Messenger in speech.
06 Do Not Shout at Him as You Shout at Each Other
  • Do not address the Prophet with harsh casual familiarity as you shout across streets to your peers.
07 Lest Your Good Deeds Be Rendered Void
  • An tahbata a'malukum wa antum la tash'uroon: lest your righteous works be erased without you realizing.
08 Hearts Tested and Purified for Taqwa
  • Those who lower their voices in his presence: Allah has refined and tested their hearts for true piety.

PLATE 02 : VERIFICATION CHARTER
PILLAR 03: CALLING OUT FROM BEHIND APARTMENTS
Shouting from Behind Hujurat, Lack of Decency & Waiting Patiently
09 Those Shouting from Behind Private Rooms
  • Those desert tribesmen who holler rudely from outside your domestic apartments (Al-Hujurat).
10 Most of Them Lack Discernment and Decency
  • Aktharuhum la ya'qiloon: the majority of them lack civilizational refinement, empathy, and intellect.
11 Far Better Had They Waited Patiently
  • Had they shown patient etiquette until you emerged to meet them, it would have been far better for them.
12 Allah is Immensely Forgiving and Merciful
  • Wallahu Ghafoorun Raheem: Allah overlooks past uncouth ignorance when people embrace proper decorum.
PILLAR 04: THE EPISTEMIC CHARTER: FATABAYYANOO!
When a Fasiq Brings News, Investigating Truth & Averting Tragic Remorse
13 When an Iniquitous Person Brings News
  • If a corrupt, untrustworthy person (*Fasiq*) brings you information, never react or publish in haste!
14 The Command to Verify and Investigate (Fatabayyanoo)
  • Fatabayyanoo / Fatathabbatoo: verify the facts, cross-examine sources, and investigate thoroughly!
15 Lest You Harm Innocent People in Ignorance
  • Lest you strike an innocent community in unverified anger, inflicting grave injustice and harm.
16 Becoming Stricken with Bitter Remorse
  • Fa-tusbihoo 'ala ma fa'altum nadimeen: and become stricken with agonizing, irreparable regret.

PLATE 03 : SPIRITUAL BEAUTY
PILLAR 05: THE MESSENGER OF ALLAH AMONG YOU
Prophet in Your Midst, Disastrous Consequence of Blind Compliance & Divine Wisdom
17 Know That the Messenger of Allah is Among You
  • Recognize the profound blessing and weight that the Prophet of God resides and walks in your presence.
18 If He Obeyed You in Most Matters You Would Suffer
  • Were he to obey your emotional demands and rumors in many affairs, you would surely fall into disaster.
19 Divine Protection from Chaos and Conflict
  • Prophetic leadership anchors the community in transcendent wisdom rather than reactive hysteria.
20 Humility Before Apostolic Leadership
  • Believers find safety by aligning their loyalties with verified guidance rather than mob impulses.
PILLAR 06: FAITH MADE BELOVED & ADORNED IN HEARTS
Habbaba Ilaykumu al-Iman, Sins Made Repulsive & The Truly Rightly Guided
21 Allah Endeared Faith to Your Inner Souls
  • Walakinna Allaha habbaba ilaykumu al-imana: He made faith beloved and exquisitely adorned inside hearts.
22 Disbelief, Iniquity and Rebellion Made Hateful
  • And He made disbelief, corruption, and moral defiance naturally repulsive and detestable to your nature.
23 Those Are the Truly Rightly Guided (Rashidoon)
  • Ula'ika humu ar-rashidoon: those gifted with this pure spiritual aesthetic are the truly mature and guided.
24 A Grace and Sovereign Bounty from Allah
  • Fadlan mina Allahi wa ni'mah: a purely unearned bounty from Allah, who is All-Knowing, All-Wise.

PILLAR 07: ARBITRATION BETWEEN FIGHTING BELIEVERS
Two Factions at War, Reconciling Fairly & Striking the Transgressor
25 When Two Groups of Believers Fall into Conflict
  • If two factions among the faithful clash in armed conflict, intervene immediately to make peace.
26 Reconciling Between Them with Impartial Justice
  • Facilitate swift arbitration based on truth, restorative justice, and genuine reconciliation.
27 Combating the Obstinate Aggressor (Tabghee)
  • If one faction defies arbitration and continues violent transgression, fight the oppressor collectively!
28 Until It Returns to the Command of Allah
  • Hatta tafee'a ila amrillahi: until the rogue faction yields and submits back to the divine decree of justice.
PILLAR 08: THE CHARTER OF UNIVERSAL BROTHERHOOD
Innamal-Mu'minoona Ikhwah, Absolute Equity (Aqsitoo) & Receiving Mercy
29 Reconciling with Absolute Equity (Aqsitoo)
  • When the aggressor complies, make peace between them with fairness and be meticulously just!
30 Allah Loves the Meticulously Just (Muqsiteen)
  • Innallaha yuhibbu al-muqsiteen: Allah deeply loves those who establish uncompromising equity.
31 The Sovereign Declaration: Believers Are Brethren!
  • Innama al-mu'minoona ikhwatun: believers are nothing less than an indivisible spiritual family and brotherhood!
32 Reconcile Between Your Two Brothers to Find Mercy
  • So reconcile between your brothers and maintain Taqwa so that you may receive divine mercy.

PLATE 05 : PRESERVING DIGNITY
PILLAR 09: NO GROUP RIDICULING ANOTHER GROUP
La Yaskhar Qawmun min Qawm, The Mocked May Be Better & Ban on Condescension
33 Let No Community Ridicule Another Community
  • O you who believe, let not one group mock or demean another group in arrogant superiority.
34 Perhaps the Mocked Are Far Better Before God
  • It may well be that the mocked are vastly superior in character, devotion, and divine honor.
35 Nor Shall Women Ridicule Other Women
  • Nor should women scoff at or demean other women: divine prohibition against all social snobbery.
36 Eradicating Toxic Elitism and Social Sarcasm
  • Surah Al-Hujurat tears down the haughty habits of tribal, class, and aesthetic condescension.
PILLAR 10: BAN ON DEFAMATION & OFFENSIVE NICKNAMES
Wa La Talmizoo Anfusakum, No Tanabuz bil-Alqab & Unrepentant Are Wrongdoers
37 Do Not Defame or Slander Yourselves
  • Wa la talmizoo anfusakum: do not insult, taunt, or defame one another, for believers are one soul.
38 Do Not Revile Each Other with Sarcastic Nicknames
  • Wa la tanabazoo bil-alqab: do not brand each other with derogatory, hurtful, or sarcastic labels.
39 Wretched is the Title of Iniquity After Faith
  • Bi'sa al-ismu al-fusooqu ba'dal-iman: how foul to label someone with sin after they entered faith!
40 Those Who Do Not Repent Are the Real Transgressors
  • And whoever does not desist and repent: those are the persistent, arrogant wrongdoers (Zalimoon).

Plate 06: BANNING UNFOUNDED SUSPICION, SPYING & GHEEBAH

Pillars 11 & 12: Avoid Much Conjecture, Wa La Tajassasoo (Do Not Spy) & The Repulsive Parable of Backbiting

PLATE 06 : INNER PURITY
PILLAR 11: AVOIDING MUCH UNFOUNDED CONJECTURE (ZANN)
Ijtaniboo Katheeran minaz-Zann, Some Suspicion is Sin & Protecting Minds
41 Avoid Much Groundless Suspicion (Zann)
  • Ijtaniboo katheeran minaz-zann: steer far clear of entertaining unwarranted suspicions and cynical paranoia.
42 Indeed, Some Suspicion is an Outright Sin
  • Inna ba'da az-zanni ithm: harbor no malicious assumptions, for unverified suspicion corrupts hearts.
43 Preserving the Sanctity of Good Conjectures
  • Believers must grant fellow brethren the benefit of the doubt and assume virtuous intent by default.
44 The Psychological Shield of Communal Trust
  • Protecting the social fabric from rotting under secret distrust, jealousy, and imagined slights.
PILLAR 12: DO NOT SPY & CONSUMING A DEAD BROTHER'S FLESH
Wa La Tajassasoo, Wa La Yaghtab & The Horrific Parable of Necrophagy
45 Do Not Spy into Private Faults (Wa La Tajassasoo)
  • Do not pry, snoop, wiretap, or investigate the private shortcomings and hidden flaws of people.
46 Do Not Backbite One Another (Wa La Yaghtab)
  • Do not speak of your brother behind his back in a manner that he would dislike or find offensive.
47 Would You Love to Eat the Flesh of Your Dead Brother?
  • A-yuhibbu ahadukum an ya'kula lahma akheehi maytan: would any of you relish eating dead rotting flesh?!
48 You Loathe It! So Fear Allah Who Accepts Repentance
  • Fakarihtumooh: you abhor it instinctively! So fear Allah; indeed, Allah is ever Accepter of Repentance.

63 Striving with Their Wealth and Lives in Allah's Way
  • Wa jahadoo bi-amwalihim wa anfusihim fee sabeelillah: sacrificing fortune and life for truth.
