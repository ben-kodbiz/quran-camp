Plate 01: COSMIC EMISSARIES & THE INEVITABLE SUMMONS

Pillars 01 & 02: Wal-Mursalat, An-Nashirat, Al-Fariqat & Innama Too'adoona La-Waqi'

PLATE 01 : OATHS & INEVITABILITY
PILLAR 01: THE FIVE COSMIC EMISSARIES
Wal-Mursalat 'Urfa, Fal-'Asifati 'Asfa & Fal-Mulqiyati Dhikra
01 The Emissaries Sent in Succession
  • By the winds or angelic couriers sent forth in continuous succession like a horse's mane ('urfa).
02 The Raging Cosmic Tempests
  • And by the raging gusts that blast furiously, demonstrating the immense kinetic power of divine forces.
03 The Dispersers of Life and Cloud
  • And by the scatterers that spread rain and life across parched earthly plains (wan-nashirati nashra).
04 The Bearers of the Sacred Reminder
  • And by the celestial couriers casting down the holy Reminder, whether as an excuse or a solemn warning.
PILLAR 02: THE INEVITABILITY OF THE PROMISE
Innama Too'adoona La-Waqi' & The Approaching Hour
05 The Unfailing Promise of Reckoning
  • Innama too'adoona la-waqi': Truly, all that you are promised shall inexorably come to pass.
06 The Fallacy of Eternal Status Quo
  • The materialist delusion that natural cycles run endlessly without cosmic conclusion is shattered.
07 Cosmic Forces Bearing Witness
  • The physical universe itself stands as a colossal witness to the impending moral reckoning.
08 The Urgency of Moral Awakening
  • Awareness of the certainty of resurrection instantly transmutes every daily choice into eternal weight.

Plate 02: COSMIC DISMANTLING & THE DAY OF SORTING

Pillars 03 & 04: The Obliterated Stars, Rent Heavens & Li-Ayyi Yawmin Ujjilat

PLATE 02 : COSMIC DISMANTLING
PILLAR 03: THE DISMANTLING OF THE FIRMAMENT
Fa-Idhan-Nujoomu Tumisat, Was-Sama'u Furijat & Al-Jibalu Nusifat
09 The Extinction of the Stars
  • When the celestial stars lose their brilliant radiance and are extinguished into darkness (tumisat).
10 The Rending of the Vaulted Sky
  • When the colossal canopy of heaven is fractured and torn wide apart (furijat).
11 The Pulverization of the Mountains
  • When the titanic granite mountains are ground into floating dust and scattered away (nusifat).
12 The Assembly of the Messengers
  • When the noble Messengers of all epochs are gathered to deliver their testimony over humanity.
PILLAR 04: THE AWESOME DAY OF DECISION
Li-Ayyi Yawmin Ujjilat? Li-Yawmil-Fasl! & Waylun Yawma'idh
13 For What Epoch Was It Deferred?
  • Li-ayyi yawmin ujjilat: For what monumental day has this supreme gathering been appointed?
14 The Day of Absolute Separation
  • For the Day of Sorting (Yawm al-Fasl)! When truth is forever distinguished and severed from falsehood.
15 The Incomprehensible Gravity
  • Wa ma adraka ma yawmul-fasl: And what can make you comprehend the colossal gravity of that Day?
16 The Opening Refrain of Woe
  • Waylun yawma'idhin lil-mukadh-dhibeen: Woe that Day to those who obstinately denied the truth!

Plate 03: HISTORICAL ANNIHILATION OF PAST TYRANTS

Pillars 05 & 06: Alam Nuhlikil-Awwaleen, Thumma Nutbi'uhumul-Akhireen & The Law of Justice

PLATE 03 : LESSONS OF HISTORY
PILLAR 05: THE EXTINCTION OF ANCIENT NATIONS
Alam Nuhlikil-Awwaleen & Thumma Nutbi'uhumul-Akhireen
17 Destruction of Ancient Deniers
  • The vanished civilizations of 'Ad, Thamud, and the people of Nuh swept away in their pride.
18 Succession of Later Transgressors
  • Then We caused subsequent generations who duplicated their arrogance to follow them in ruin.
19 The Inflexible Law of Retribution
  • Kadalika naf'alu bil-mujrimeen: Thus do We invariably deal with hardened criminals and tyrants.
20 Ruins as Enduring Warnings
  • The silent desolate ruins scattered across the deserts stand as enduring warnings to heedless moderns.
PILLAR 06: THE REITERATED WARNING ON ARROGANCE
Waylun Yawma'idhin lil-Mukadh-dhibeen: The Historical Refrain
21 Refrain Two: Historical Blindness
  • Woe that Day to those who gaze upon ruined civilizations and remain heedlessly blind.
22 The Illusion of Invulnerability
  • No empire, technology, or wealth shields a society that systematically denies moral truth.
23 The Inevitable Fall of Hubris
  • Every nation that replaces divine humility with arrogant tyranny invites internal decay and destruction.
24 Historical Wisdom as Safeguard
  • Contemplating history protects the believer from being seduced by temporal worldly dominance.

Plate 04: BIOLOGICAL GENESIS & THE RECEPTACLE OF EARTH

Pillars 07 & 08: Alam Nakhluqkum min Ma'in Maheen, Fi Qararin Makeen & The Earth as Kifat

PLATE 04 : BIOLOGY & THE LIVING EARTH
PILLAR 07: HUMAN ORIGIN FROM CONTEMPTIBLE FLUID
Ma'in Maheen, Qararin Makeen & Qadarin Ma'loom
25 From a Humble Liquid Drop
  • Alam nakhluqkum min ma'in maheen: Did We not create you from a modest, contemptible fluid droplet?
26 The Fortified Maternal Sanctuary
  • Lodged safely in the secure, protected haven of the maternal womb (qararin makeen).
27 Governed by an Ordained Schedule
  • Formed until an appointed and measured duration (ila qadarin ma'loom) without human intervention.
28 The Perfection of Divine Measure
  • Fa-qadarna fa-ni'mal-qadiroon: So We measured with precision—how excellent is Our divine determining!
PILLAR 08: THE EARTH AS THE EMBRACING HAVEN (KIFAT)
Alam Naj'alil-Arda Kifata, Ahya'an wa Amwata & Rawasiya Shamikhat
29 Refrain Three & Four: Denying Biology
  • Woe to those who behold the miracle of human embryology yet deny the Creator who fashioned it.
30 The Earth as a Universal Receptacle
  • Alam naj'alil-arda kifata: Did We not make the earth an all-embracing vessel?
31 Haven for the Living and the Dead
  • Gathering the living upon its surface and sheltering the deceased within its sheltering depths.
32 Lofty Mountains and Sweet Water
  • Anchored with towering mountains (rawasiya shamikhat) and pouring down cool, sweet water (furata).

Plate 05: THE SHADOW OF SMOKE & SPARKS OF GEHENNA

Pillars 09 & 10: Intaliqoo ila Zillin Dhee Thalathi Shu'ab & Innahat Tarmee bi-Shararin kal-Qasr

PLATE 05 : THREE-BRANCHED SHADOW
PILLAR 09: THE TRIPLE-BRANCHED CANOPY OF SMOKE
Zillin Dhee Thalathi Shu'ab, La Zaleelin wa La Yughnee minal-Lahab
33 Summons to What Was Denied
  • Intaliqoo ila ma kuntum bihi tukadh-dhiboon: Depart toward that which you obstinately denied!
34 The Deceptive Three-Branched Shadow
  • Advance into the shadow of rising smoke branching into three suffocating plumes.
35 Zero Cooling and Zero Defense
  • Affording no soothing coolness and offering zero protection against the leaping fire (al-lahab).
36 Refrain Five & Six: Deceptive Havens
  • Woe that Day to those who trusted worldly shelters and ignored the genuine divine haven.
PILLAR 10: SPARKS LIKE TOWERS & YELLOW CAMELS
Innahat Tarmee bi-Shararin kal-Qasr, Ka'annahu Jimalatun Sufr
37 Sparks Vast as Massive Castles
  • Behold! It hurls forth sparks as vast and high as towering fortresses (kal-qasr).
38 Like Flocks of Tawny Camels
  • Swirling through the blackness like swift, frantic herds of yellow/tawny camels (jimalatun sufr).
39 The Overwhelming Scale of Judgment
  • The sheer magnitude of a single spark conveys the incomprehensible terror of the raging pit.
40 Melting of Worldly Defenses
  • All worldly defenses, wealth, and aristocratic illusions melt before the uncontainable furnace.

Plate 06: THE SPEECHLESS SILENCE & GATHERING OF GENERATIONS

Pillars 11 & 12: Hadha Yawmu La Yantiqoon, La Yu'dhanu Lahum & Hadha Yawmul-Fasli Jama'nakum

PLATE 06 : THE SPEECHLESS DAY
PILLAR 11: THE STRIPPING OF SPEECH AND EXCUSES
Hadha Yawmu La Yantiqoon & Wa La Yu'dhanu Lahum Fa-Ya'tadhirun
41 The Day of Stunned Silence
  • Hadha yawmu la yantiqoon: This is a Day on which they shall not speak a single word.
42 No Permission to Plead Excuses
  • Nor shall permission be granted unto them to fabricate hollow excuses or self-justifications.
43 Exposure of Moral Nakedness
  • All deceptive rhetoric, political spin, and worldly rationalizations are rendered utterly void.
44 Refrain Seven: The Silence of Defeat
  • Woe that Day to the deniers whose clever arguments collapse into frozen humiliation.
PILLAR 12: THE GATHERING OF ALL EPOCHS
Hadha Yawmul-Fasli Jama'nakum wal-Awwaleen & Fa-In Kana Lakum Kaydun
45 The Comprehensive Assembly
  • Hadha yawmul-fasl: This is the Day of Sorting! We have assembled you alongside the ancients.
46 The Defiant Challenge (Fa-Keedoon)
  • If you possess any plot, scheme, or trick, then deploy it against Me now!
47 The Absolute Impotence of Creation
  • Human power, conspiracies, and artificial structures are exposed as utterly powerless before Allah.
48 Refrain Eight: The Folly of Contention
  • Woe that Day to those who plotted against divine messengers, only to be trapped in their own folly.

Plate 07: THE COOL GROVES OF THE RIGHTEOUS

Pillars 13 & 14: Innal-Muttaqeena Fee Zilalin wa 'Uyoon, Wa Fawakih & Kuloo wash-Raboo Hanee'an

PLATE 07 : GROVES OF THE RIGHTEOUS
PILLAR 13: THE SHADED SPRINGS & DESIRED FRUITS
Fee Zilalin wa 'Uyoon & Wa Fawakiha Mimma Yashtahoon
49 Sanctuary of the God-Conscious
  • Innal-muttaqeena fee zilalin wa 'uyoon: Truly, the reverent dwell amid cool shade and flowing springs.
50 Fruits of Pure Heart's Desire
  • And fruits of every variety that their souls genuinely crave and yearn for (yashtahoon).
51 Wholesome Delight Without Regret
  • Kuloo wash-raboo hanee'an bima kuntum ta'maloon: Eat and drink with relish as recompense for deeds.
52 Generosity Bestowed on the Good
  • Inna kadhalika najzil-muhsineen: Truly, thus do We reward those who perfected excellence in character.
PILLAR 14: THE CONTRAST OF MORAL DESTINIES
Waylun Yawma'idhin lil-Mukadh-dhibeen & Kuloo wa Tamatta'oo Qaleelan
53 Refrain Nine: Contrast of Despair
  • Woe that Day to the deniers who forfeited eternal springs for the mud of worldly corruption.
54 Eat and Indulge a Little While
  • Kuloo wa tamatta'oo qaleelan innakum mujrimoon: Eat and indulge a little while—you are criminals!
55 The Ephemeral Feast of the Ungodly
  • Sensual indulgence without moral boundaries is an ephemeral indulgence preceding ruin.
56 Eternal Dignity of the Muhsineen
  • The God-conscious are vindicated in eternal honor, crowned with divine contentment.

Plate 08: THE REFUSAL TO BOW & THE SUPREME DISCOURSE

Pillars 15 & 16: Wa Idha Qeela Lahumur-Ka'oo La Yarka'oon & Fa-Bi-Ayyi Hadeethin Ba'dahu Yu'minoon?

PLATE 08 : REFUSAL TO BOW & FINAL FAITH
PILLAR 15: THE STUBBORN REFUSAL TO BOW
Wa Idha Qeela Lahumur-Ka'oo La Yarka'oon & The Pride of Rebellion
57 When Commanded to Bow (Irka'oo)
  • And when it is said to them: 'Bow down in humble submission,' they refuse to bow.
58 The Stiff Neck of Spiritual Pride
  • Arrogance in this world prevents them from the physical and spiritual posture of humble surrender.
59 The Tragedy of Missed Repentance
  • Every opportunity to submit to the Creator was spurned in favor of haughty self-worship.
60 Refrain Ten: Climax of Warning
  • Waylun yawma'idhin lil-mukadh-dhibeen: Woe that Day to those who obstinately denied the truth!
PILLAR 16: IN WHAT DISCOURSE BEYOND THIS WILL THEY BELIEVE?
Fa-Bi-Ayyi Hadeethin Ba'dahu Yu'minoon? & The Finality of the Qur'an
61 The Ultimate Rhetorical Challenge
  • Fa-bi-ayyi hadeethin ba'dahu yu'minoon: In what speech, then, after this Qur'an, will they place their faith?
62 Exhaustion of All Moral Proofs
  • If cosmic oaths, history, human embryology, and the majesty of truth fail to move them, nothing will.
63 Supreme Authority of the Qur'an
  • The Book of Allah stands as the definitive, unmatched miracle and ultimate discourse for mankind.
64 The Solemn Closure of Juz Tabarak
  • Juz 29 concludes with this haunting question, summoning every human soul to surrender to its Maker.
