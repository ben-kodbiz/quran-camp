Plate 01: SUPREME SOVEREIGNTY & THE CRUCIBLE OF EXISTENCE

PLATE 01 : DOMINION & TRIAL
PILLAR 01: SOVEREIGN MAJESTY & MORAL PURPOSE
Tabarakalladhi bi-Yadihi'l-Mulk, Omnipotence & Testing Deeds
01 The Sovereign Hand of Dominion
  • Blessed is He in Whose Hand rests absolute kingship over all cosmos, possessing unrestricted omnipotence.
02 Creation of Death and Life as a Crucible
  • Khalaqa al-mawta wal-hayata li-yabluwakum: death and life engineered specifically to test moral excellence in conduct.
03 The Balance of Might and Forgiveness
  • He is Al-'Azeez (The Irresistible in Power) yet Al-Ghafoor (The Perpetual Pardoner of repentant souls).
04 The Purposeful Design of Mortality
  • Earthly life is not arbitrary duration but a temporary proving ground for spiritual sincerity and righteous action.
PILLAR 02: THE SEVEN FLAWLESS CELESTIAL TIERS
Tibaqan, No Disproportion in Mercy's Creation & Repeating the Gaze
05 Seven Layered Vaults of Heaven
  • Architectured in seven harmonious cosmic strata (Tibaqan), perfectly balanced in celestial equilibrium.
06 Zero Disproportion in Divine Creation
  • Ma tara fee khalqi ar-Rahmani min tafawut: impossible to discover any flaw, fissure, or defect in cosmic order.
07 The Exhaustion of the Scrutinizing Eye
  • Repeat your gaze once more, then turn your vision twice: human eyesight returns humbled, dazzled, and exhausted.
08 The Lower Sky Adorned with Lamps
  • We adorned the lowest heaven with radiant stellar lamps (Masabeeh), serving both as beauty and missiles against devils.

  • Takadu tamayyazu min al-ghaydh: the retributive abyss almost violently shatters apart from sheer rage at rebellion.

  • Every time a faction is cast into its furnace, its angelic guardians demand with piercing courtroom solemnity.
12 Did No Warner Come to You?
  • Alam ya'tikum nadheer: the solemn interrogation establishing that divine justice precedes all punishment.
PILLAR 04: THE TRAGIC CONFESSION OF LOST REASON
Bala Qad Ja'ana Nadheer, Law Kunna Nasma'u & Confessing Sin
13 The Confession of Worldly Denial
  • They answer: Yes, a warner came to us, but we denied him and claimed Allah revealed nothing at all.
14 If Only We Had Listened or Reasoned
  • Law kunna nasma'u aw na'qilu: lamenting that had they exercised genuine audition or rational intellect, they would be saved.
15 The Self-Acknowledged Guilt
  • Fa'tarafoo bi-dhanbihim: they openly acknowledge their deliberate wrongdoing before the cosmic courtroom.
16 Banishment from Divine Grace
  • Su-hqan li-as-habi as-sa'eer: so away with the companions of the blazing flame, cut off from all divine mercy.

PLATE 03 : OMNISCIENCE & EARTH
PILLAR 05: REVERENCE FOR THE UNSEEN & HIDDEN SPEECH
Yakhshawna Rabbahum bil-Ghayb, Forgiveness & Knowing Chests

  • Those who fear their Lord unseen receive boundless divine forgiveness and a magnificent, eternal reward.
18 Concealed Whispers and Loud Utterances
  • Wa asirroo qawlakum aw ijharoo bih: whether you conceal your speech in stealth or broadcast it aloud, it is identical.
19 Knower of the Innermost Chests
  • Innahu 'Aleemun bi-dhati as-sudoor: fully cognizant of the microscopic flutterings and secret thoughts of every human heart.
20 Does He Who Created Not Know?
  • Ala ya'lamu man khalaqa: how could the Architect of all existence not know His creation, being Al-Lateef, Al-Khabeer?
PILLAR 06: THE SUBJUGATED EARTH & CELESTIAL THREATS
Dhaloolan, Walking Paths, Eating Provision & Fear of Sinking

  • Huwalladhi ja'ala lakumu al-arda dhaloolan: He rendered the earth docile and stable for human habitation and industry.

  • Traverse its broad highways, eat of His bestowed provision, and remember that unto Him alone is the Resurrection.
23 Do You Feel Secure from Sudden Swallowing?
  • Are you confident that He who rules heaven will not cause the earth to violently convulse and swallow you whole?
24 The Violent Storm of Piercing Stones
  • Or do you feel safe that He will not unleash upon your heads a furious hurricane of stones (Haseeban)?
