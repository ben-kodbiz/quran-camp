## Plate 01: COSMIC WISDOM, CREATION & THE SIGNS OF TIME
**Section Badge:** `[PLATE 01 : CREATION & CHRONOMETRY]`  
**Overview:** Pillars 1 & 2: The Wise Book, The Throne Administration, Solar Radiance & Lunar Chronometry  

### PILLAR 1: THE BOOK OF WISDOM & SOVEREIGN GOVERNANCE
*Kitabin Hakeem, Six Epochs of Creation & The Throne Administration*  

#### 1. Alif-Lam-Ra: The Book of Supreme Wisdom
- Verses of the Wise Scripture revealed to awaken humanity from slumber.
- Addressing human bewilderment that a mortal man was chosen to convey revelation.
- Establishing that divine guidance is the highest cosmic mercy bestowed on creation.

#### 2. The Cosmic Hexaemeron & Sovereign Throne
- Allah created the heavens and earth in six epochs, then established His Throne.
- Yudabbirul-Amr: Sovereignly directing and administering all celestial and earthly affairs.
- No particle in existence moves outside His flawless knowledge and sovereign decree.

#### 3. No Intercessor Except by Divine Leave
- Demolishing polytheistic fantasies of independent, automatic intercessors.
- No mediation occurs in the divine court except after His explicit permission.
- That is Allah your Lord; therefore worship Him alone; will you not reflect?

#### 4. The Return of All Souls & Justice of Creation
- Unto Him is the return of all humanity; a promise of Allah binding in truth.
- He originates creation then restores it to reward the believers with absolute equity.
- Deniers face excruciating consequences for rejecting objective cosmic reality.

### PILLAR 2: CELESTIAL CHRONOMETRY & THE DAY OF ACCOUNTING
*Sun as Radiance, Moon as Luminescence & The Reckoning of Years*  

#### 5. The Radiant Sun & The Luminescent Moon
- Precise lexical demarcation: Sun made Diya' (radiant heat/light); Moon made Noor.
- Distinguishing active solar radiance from cool, serene reflected lunar illumination.
- Allah created not all of this except in profound, purposeful cosmic truth (Bil-Haqq).

#### 6. Orbital Precision & The Calculation of Eras
- Calibrating the lunar trajectory into 28 distinct astronomical stations (Manazil).
- Enabling human civilization to reckon the count of years and calculate time.
- Detailing celestial signs for a people possessing deep intellectual discernment.

#### 7. Alternation of Night & Day as Divine Signs
- In the cyclic rotation of nocturnal dark and diurnal light are magnificent signs.
- Manifesting divine wisdom in all that Allah created throughout the heavens and earth.
- A continuous living testament reserved for those who cultivate God-consciousness (Taqwa).

#### 8. The Tragedy of Worldly Satisfaction
- Those who anticipate no encounter with their Lord, satisfied with mortal life.
- Finding false tranquility in transient adornments while heedless of eternal signs.
- Their ultimate refuge is the Fire for what their own hands consistently earned.

## Plate 02: THE HUMAN PSYCHOLOGICAL PARADOX & THE FLEETING WORLD
**Section Badge:** `[PLATE 02 : HUMAN PARADOX & PARABLES]`  
**Overview:** Pillars 3 & 4: Man in Distress vs Relief, The Maritime Storm & Earthly Meadows Pulverized  

### PILLAR 3: THE UNGRATEFUL SOUL IN DISTRESS & RELIEF
*Calling Upon God Reclining/Sitting/Standing & Walking Away in Ingratitude*  

#### 9. Supplication in Agony & Immediate Forgetfulness
- When harm touches man, he calls upon Us in every posture: on his side, sitting, or standing.
- Yet when relief is granted, he passes on indifferently as if he had never prayed.
- Exposing the shallow spiritual opportunism of the heedless human ego.

#### 10. Generations Destroyed for Injustice
- We destroyed generations before you when they committed sustained systemic tyranny.
- Their Messengers brought clear proofs, yet they obstinately refused to believe.
- Thus does divine retribution inevitably recompense stubborn criminal communities.

#### 11. Successors Upon the Earth (Khala'if)
- Then We established you as terrestrial stewards after them to see how you behave.
- Power, resources, and longevity are an active moral examination, not an entitlement.
- Accountability awaits every community regarding its stewardship of the earth.

#### 12. Rejecting Demands to Alter Revelation
- Deniers demand: 'Bring a Quran other than this, or alter its unbending truth!'
- Proclaim: 'It is not for me to change it of my own accord; I follow only revelation.'
- The Messenger transmits divine truth purely, fearing the chastisement of a Great Day.

### PILLAR 4: PARABLES OF THE TRANSIENT WORLD (AL-HAYAT AD-DUNYA)
*The Sudden Storm, Earthly Splendor as Stubble & Inviting to Dar as-Salam*  

#### 13. The Maritime Parable: The Violent Sea Storm
- Ships gliding smoothly through the ocean with joyous, favorable winds.
- Suddenly, a tempest strikes; waves surge from every direction; mortals feel encircled.
- Stripping all pretense: Pleading with Allah in pure, unadulterated monotheistic sincerity.

#### 14. Desperate Vows on the Waves & Insolvent Return
- Crying out: 'If You rescue us from this peril, we will surely be of the grateful!'
- Yet when brought safely to dry shore, they immediately resume rebellion and injustice.
- O humanity, your injustice is only against yourselves; brief worldly pleasure, then return.

#### 15. The Blooming Meadow Pulverized Overnight
- Rain falls; earth adorns itself in lush splendor; humans think they master it.
- Our command strikes by night or day, rendering it reaped stubble overnight.
- Ka'an lam taghna bil-ams: As if it had never flourished yesterday; reflect, O thinkers!

#### 16. The Invitation to the Abode of Peace (Dar as-Salam)
- While the world decays into stubble, Allah invites humanity to the Abode of Peace.
- Guiding whom He wills to an unswerving, luminous Straight Path (Sirat Mustaqeem).
- Trading fleeting mortal vanity for eternal, unshakeable sanctuary in the presence of God.

## Plate 03: ESCHATOLOGICAL CERTAINTY & THE SUPREME VISION
**Section Badge:** `[PLATE 03 : AZ-ZIYADAH & ESCHATOLOGY]`  
**Overview:** Pillars 5 & 6: Az-Ziyadah (The Divine Countenance), Radiant Faces vs Darkness & Idols Disavowed  

### PILLAR 5: AZ-ZIYADAH: GAZING UPON THE DIVINE COUNTENANCE
*Lilladhina Ahsanoo al-Husna wa Ziyadah & The Radiance of the Redeemed*  

#### 17. Al-Husna: The Ultimate Reward of Paradise
- For those who practiced spiritual excellence (Ihsan) is the ultimate good: Jannah.
- An abode of pristine beauty, unblemished joy, and eternal physical felicity.
- The magnificent fruition of patient faith, righteous conduct, and moral struggle.

#### 18. Az-Ziyadah: Gazing Upon the Lord's Countenance
- Verified in Sahih Muslim: The veil is lifted; dwellers of Jannah behold their Lord.
- The supreme climax of existence: Gazing upon the transcendent Beauty of Allah.
- Eclipsing all sensory delights of Paradise; the ultimate increase of the redeemed.

#### 19. Faces Free from Humiliation & Dust
- Neither darkness, gloom, nor disgrace shall touch the radiant countenances of believers.
- Illuminated by divine light, reflecting the peace of eternal divine acceptance.
- These are the true companions of Paradise; therein they will dwell forever.

#### 20. Faces Shrouded in Blackness (Ka'annama Ughshiyat)
- Those who earned evil: Recompense of evil is its like, covered in suffocating disgrace.
- Faces shrouded in black gloom as if draped in fragments of pitch-black night.
- They have no protector against Allah; they are the eternal companions of the Fire.

### PILLAR 6: THE INTERROGATION OF FALSE DEITIES
*Severed Polytheistic Claims, Idols Disavowing Worship & The Sovereign Provider*  

#### 21. Gathering the Nations & Severing False Alliances
- On the Day We gather all creation, We say to the polytheists: 'Stay in your places!'
- We separate them from their fabricated partners, dissolving all pagan illusions.
- The terrifying moment when all worldly crutches and false deities vanish into nothingness.

#### 22. The Disavowal of the Idols
- Their fabricated deities speak out: 'You were never truly worshipping us!'
- 'Allah is sufficient as witness between us; we were utterly unaware of your worship.'
- Polytheists betrayed by the very entities they traded away their souls to venerate.

#### 23. Who Provides from the Heavens and the Earth?
- Challenge them: Who provides your sustenance? Who controls hearing and sight?
- Who brings forth the living from the dead, and rules all affairs? They say: 'Allah!'
- Say: 'Will you then not fear Him and abandon your illogical idolatrous associations?'

#### 24. Beyond Truth, What Remains Except Error?
- Fa-dhalikumullahu Rabbukumul-Haqq: That is Allah, your true Sovereign Lord.
- What can exist after the manifest truth except catastrophic delusion and error?
- How then can you be turned away from cosmic reality into irrational falsehood?

## Plate 04: THE MIRACLE OF THE QUR'AN & THE HEALING OF HEARTS
**Section Badge:** `[PLATE 04 : INIMITABILITY & HEALING]`  
**Overview:** Pillars 7 & 8: Inimitability Challenge, The Fourfold Medicine of Revelation & Rejoicing in Divine Grace  

### PILLAR 7: INIMITABILITY & THE DIVINE PROVENANCE OF REVELATION
*Could Never Be Produced by Other Than Allah & The Challenge of a Single Surah*  

#### 25. The Impossibility of Human Authorship
- This Quran could never have been authored or produced by other than Almighty Allah.
- Confirming the authentic scriptures revealed before it and detailing divine law.
- An uncorrupted, infallible revelation sent from the Lord of all the Worlds.

#### 26. The Ultimate Challenge: Produce a Single Surah
- If you claim: 'He forged it!' then produce a single Surah like it in eloquence and truth.
- Call upon whomsoever you can muster besides Allah, if you are indeed truthful.
- The permanent, unanswerable challenge that broke the literary pride of Arabia.

#### 27. Denying What Lies Beyond Their Comprehension
- Rather, they denied that which their intellect could not encompass in knowledge.
- Rejecting the unseen reality before its undeniable fulfillment has arrived.
- Thus did earlier deniers belie their prophets; look at the final end of wrongdoers!

#### 28. Apostles to Every Nation with Absolute Justice
- For every community of humanity there is a divine Messenger sent to warn them.
- When their Messenger arrives, they are judged with absolute, immaculate justice.
- Never is any human soul wronged in the divine court; truth is established fairly.

### PILLAR 8: THE FOURFOLD TRANSFIGURATION OF REVELATION
*Maw'idhah, Shifa', Huda & Rahmah: Rejoicing in Divine Bounty*  

#### 29. The Admonition from Your Lord (Maw'idhah)
- O humanity, there has arrived to you a transformative admonition from your Lord.
- Penetrating moral counsel shaking the human conscience free from worldly intoxication.
- Awaking the slumbering soul before the irreversible gates of eternity close.

#### 30. The Healing for the Breasts (Shifa'un lima fis-Sudoor)
- A profound clinical medicine healing spiritual pathology within the human chest.
- Eradicating arrogance, envy, hypocrisy, paralyzing anxiety, and existential doubt.
- Restoring pristine fitrah and radiating healthy spiritual life throughout the soul.

#### 31. Guidance & Mercy for the Believers (Hudan wa Rahmah)
- An infallible intellectual and legal compass navigating complex ethical dilemmas.
- Coupled with divine Rahmah: Showering the faithful in peace, comfort, and forgiveness.
- Transforming broken mortal lives into monuments of righteous, purposeful living.

#### 32. Rejoicing Exclusively in Divine Grace
- Qul bi-fadlillahi wa bi-rahmatihi fa-bidhalika fal-yafrahoo: Rejoice in His grace!
- Holding divine revelation in your heart is infinitely superior to all mortal wealth.
- Elevating human ambition above transient bank balances into eternal divine treasure.

## Plate 05: THE ALLIES OF ALLAH & DIVINE OMNISCIENCE
**Section Badge:** `[PLATE 05 : AWLIYA'ULLAH & OMNISCIENCE]`  
**Overview:** Pillars 9 & 10: The Sanctuary of Awliya'ullah, Glad Tidings in Both Worlds & The Weight of an Atom  

### PILLAR 9: THE SANCTUARY OF AWLIYA'ULLAH
*No Fear nor Grief, Faith Combined with Taqwa & Glad Tidings in Both Worlds*  

#### 33. The Allies of Allah: Free from Fear and Grief
- Ala inna awliya'allahi la khawfun 'alayhim wa la hum yahzanoon.
- Protected from fear of what lies ahead in the grave and grief over worldly loss.
- Resting within an impenetrable psychological and spiritual fortress of serenity.

#### 34. The Two Keys: Authentic Faith & Continual Taqwa
- Defined precisely: Alladheena amanoo wa kanoo yattaqoon.
- True sainthood in Islam requires no esoteric pedigree; it demands Iman and Taqwa.
- Every pious, God-conscious believer is an honored, beloved ally of Almighty Allah.

#### 35. Glad Tidings in This World & the Next (Al-Bushra)
- For them are joyous tidings in worldly life: Righteous dreams, praise, and inner peace.
- And in the Hereafter: Angelic greetings upon death, expansive graves, and Jannah.
- Unbroken divine companionship accompanying the righteous soul through all realms.

#### 36. No Alteration in the Words of Allah
- La tabdeela li-kalimatillah: The promises and laws of Allah are immutable.
- That is indeed the magnificent, ultimate attainment (Al-Fawz al-'Azeem).
- Let not the arrogant speech or slander of deniers cause your heart sorrow.

### PILLAR 10: OMNIPRESENT WITNESS & THE WEIGHT OF AN ATOM
*Ma Takoonu fee Sha'n, Cosmic Ledger & Rebuking Fabricated Prohibitions*  

#### 37. Divine Presence in Every Moment
- In whatever matter you are engaged, or reciting scripture, We are Witnesses over you.
- Total existential surveillance: No action is performed in isolation from divine sight.
- Instilling acute awareness (Muraqabah) that sanctifies work, worship, and speech.

#### 38. Not the Weight of an Atom Escapes Him (Mithqala Dharrah)
- Not an atom's weight in the earth or the heavens escapes your Sovereign Lord.
- Nothing smaller than that nor larger exists except recorded in a Clear Book.
- Absolute cosmic data integrity preserving every good intention and hidden tear.

#### 39. Rebuking Invented Religious Prohibitions
- Denouncing those who declare lawful provisions unlawful based on cultural whims.
- 'Has Allah permitted you this, or do you invent fabrications against Allah?'
- Protecting the purity of Shari'ah from arbitrary human tampering and false asceticism.

#### 40. What Do Fabricators Expect on the Last Day?
- What will those who invent lies against Allah think on the Day of Resurrection?
- Allah is indeed full of boundless bounty to humanity, yet most mortals are ungrateful.
- Calling humanity back to humble gratitude before the Day of ultimate accounting.

## Plate 06: THE PROPHETIC CYCLE: NUH & THE CRUCIBLE OF MUSA
**Section Badge:** `[PLATE 06 : NUH & MUSA'S CRUCIBLE]`  
**Overview:** Pillars 11 & 12: Nuh's Unshakable Reliance, The Ark of Survival & Musa Confronting Sorcery  

### PILLAR 11: THE DEFIANCE OF NUH & THE ARK OF SURVIVAL
*Confronting Polytheist Conspiracies, No Recompense Demanded & Drowning Oppressors*  

#### 41. Nuh's Challenge to His Hostile Nation
- Nuh said to his people: 'If my station and reminders of Allah's signs offend you...'
- 'Then upon Allah alone I rely; gather your associates and execute your plot without delay!'
- The supreme fearlessness of a solitary prophet armored only in absolute Tawakkul.

#### 42. I Ask of You No Recompense (In Ajriya illa 'alallah)
- 'And if you turn away, I have never asked you for any material wage or wealth.'
- 'My recompense is only with Allah, and I am commanded to be of those who submit.'
- Complete purity of prophetic purpose: Liberated from all worldly dependency.

#### 43. The Ark of Salvation & The Inundation
- They denied him, so We saved Nuh and those aboard the Ark, making them successors.
- While We drowned those who rejected Our signs in the cataclysmic deluge.
- Illustrating that moral rectitude survives while tyrannical civilizations drown.

#### 44. The Consequence of the Warned Nations
- Gaze upon the final end of those who were warned yet persisted in arrogance.
- After Nuh, Messengers arrived with clear proofs, yet communities refused to believe.
- Thus does Allah seal the hearts of those who transgress beyond all moral boundaries.

### PILLAR 12: MUSA & HARUN CONFRONTING PHARAOH'S SORCERY
*Magic Dismissed as Truth, Staff Swallowing Illusions & Pharaoh's Tyranny*  

#### 45. Commissioning Musa & Harun with Clear Signs
- Dispatched to Pharaoh and his ruling elite with unmistakable divine miracles.
- Yet they behaved with haughty pride and were an entrenched criminal people.
- Power blinded to truth, scorning prophetic warnings as subversive threats.

#### 46. Denouncing Truth as Sorcery (Asihrun Hadha?)
- When divine truth arrived, the regime proclaimed: 'This is manifest sorcery!'
- Musa said: 'Do you say this of the truth when it has come to you? Can this be magic?'
- Proclaiming with divine certainty: 'Sorcerers and deceivers will never triumph!'

#### 47. Summoning the Magicians of the Empire
- Pharaoh commanded: 'Bring before me every master sorcerer in the empire!'
- A public spectacle designed to preserve imperial hegemony through optical illusion.
- Musa said to the assembled magicians: 'Cast down whatever you are going to cast!'

#### 48. The Staff Annihilating Imperial Illusion
- Musa proclaimed: 'What you brought is sorcery; Allah will surely nullify it!'
- 'Indeed, Allah does not prosper the corrupt work of those who spread mischief.'
- Allah establishes truth by His words, even though hardened criminals detest it.

## Plate 07: THE SEA CRUCIBLE, PHARAOH'S DROWNING & HIS PRESERVED BODY
**Section Badge:** `[PLATE 07 : EXODUS & PHARAOH PRESERVED]`  
**Overview:** Pillars 13 & 14: Homes as Sanctuaries (Buyootakum Qiblah), Parting the Sea & The Preserved Corpse  

### PILLAR 13: THE EXODUS & SPLITTING OF THE RED SEA
*Turning Domestic Homes into Sanctuaries, Establishing Prayer & The Parted Sea*  

#### 49. Turning Domestic Homes into Sanctuaries
- Commanded Musa and Harun: 'Settle your people in homes and make them places of prayer!'
- When public congregational worship was suppressed, homes became spiritual citadels.
- Establish Salah with diligence and give joyous glad tidings to the faithful believers.

#### 50. Musa's Imprecation Against Pharaoh's Wealth
- 'Our Lord, You granted Pharaoh and his chiefs splendor and wealth in this life...'
- 'Obliterate their wealth and harden their hearts so they believe not until painful doom!'
- An imprecation against systemic oppressors who weaponize resources against truth.

#### 51. The Answered Supplication: Be Steadfast!
- Allah declared: 'Your prayer has been answered; so remain steadfast and straight!'
- 'And follow not the reckless path of those who know not cosmic truth and law.'
- The divine promise fulfilled through unshakeable prophetic persistence.

#### 52. The Red Sea Parting & Pharaoh's Pursuit
- We led the Children of Israel across the sea through miraculous parted dry beds.
- Pharaoh and his legions pursued them in insolent tyranny and aggressive hatred.
- Setting the stage for the definitive historical demise of the ancient superpower.

### PILLAR 14: THE DEATHBED CONFESSION & THE PRESERVED CORPSE
*Pharaoh Drowning, 'Al-Aana', The Sign for Generations & Bani Israel Delivered*  

#### 53. Surging Waves & The Deathbed Surrender
- When drowning waters overwhelmed him, Pharaoh cried: 'I believe in Israel's God!'
- 'I testify that there is no deity except Him, and I am of those who submit (Muslimoon)!'
- The terrifying collapse of imperial arrogance in the face of inevitable mortality.

#### 54. The Divine Rejection: 'Al-Aana?' (Now?)
- 'Now you believe? When you rebelled previously and were of the corrupters?'
- Repentance in the agony of death (Hadd al-Ya's) is universally rejected by God.
- Free will terminates when the veil of the unseen is ripped aside by doom.

#### 55. Preservation of the Tyrant's Body (Bi-Badanika)
- Fa-l-yawma nunajjeeka bi-badanika: Today We rescue your physical corpse from the sea.
- Cast upon the shores so that you become an enduring sign for all future generations.
- An astonishing archaeological prophecy preserved in history down to the modern era.

#### 56. Deliverance of Bani Israel & Warning Against Doubt
- We settled the Children of Israel in an honorable settlement and provided good sustenance.
- Yet they differed only after verified knowledge arrived; your Lord will judge between them.
- Warning the Ummah against following the sectarian pitfalls of past delivered nations.

## Plate 08: THE MIRACLE OF YUNUS'S PEOPLE & THE SUFFICIENCY OF ALLAH
**Section Badge:** `[PLATE 08 : YUNUS'S NATION & TAWHID]`  
**Overview:** Pillars 15 & 16: The Unique Repentance of Yunus's Nation, Free Will & The Fortress of Tawhid  

### PILLAR 15: THE UNIQUE EXEMPTION OF THE PEOPLE OF YUNUS
*Collective Repentance, Lifting Worldly Torment & The Bounds of Free Will*  

#### 57. The Unprecedented Repentance of Yunus's Nation
- Why was there not a single city that believed and benefited except Yunus's people?
- When signs of retribution loomed, the entire population of Nineveh wept in contrition.
- Casting off pride, they prostrated collectively before the storm of doom descended.

#### 58. The Removal of Worldly Degradation
- Lamma amanoo kashafna 'anhum: When they believed, We lifted shameful worldly torment.
- Granted them wholesome enjoyment of life for an appointed season.
- Proving that sincere, collective societal repentance has power to alter history.

#### 59. The Inviolability of Moral Free Will
- Had your Lord willed, all on earth would have believed together; will you compel them?
- Faith cannot be coerced by sword or state force; it is an act of free moral choice.
- The prophetic duty is immaculate conveyance; guidance belongs exclusively to Allah.

#### 60. No Soul Believes Except by Divine Leave
- No soul can believe except by the permission and decree of Almighty Allah.
- And He places disgraceful filth (Rijs) upon those who obstinately refuse to reason.
- Look at what is in the heavens and earth! Yet signs and warnings avail not the deniers.

### PILLAR 16: THE ULTIMATE PROTECTION & PROPHETIC PROCLAMATION
*Fa in Kunta fee Shakkin, If Allah Touches with Harm & Follow Revelation*  

#### 61. Consulting Prior Scriptures: Absolute Certainty
- If you are in doubt, ask those who have been reading the Scripture before you.
- The truth has arrived from your Lord, so be not of those who waver in uncertainty.
- Corroborating the eternal monotheistic chain connecting all authentic prophets.

#### 62. If Allah Touches You with Harm (Bi-Durrin)
- If Allah touches you with affliction, none can remove or relieve it except Him.
- And if He intends good for you, none can turn back or repel His boundless bounty.
- He causes it to reach whom He wills of His servants; He is Forgiving, Merciful.

#### 63. O Humanity, The Truth Has Arrived from Your Lord
- Proclaim: 'O people, the truth has come to you from your Sovereign Lord!'
- Whoever is guided is guided only for the eternal benefit of his own soul.
- And whoever strays strays only against himself; I am not a warden over you.

#### 64. Follow Revelation & Patiently Await Allah's Judgment
- Follow what is revealed to you, and be patient until Allah issues His judgment.
- For He is the absolute Best of Judges (Khayrul-Hakimeen), ruling with flawless equity.
- The eternal conclusion: Anchor the heart in patient endurance and divine trust.
