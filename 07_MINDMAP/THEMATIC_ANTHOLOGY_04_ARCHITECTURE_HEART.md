# Surah A'mal al-Qulub: Master Mindmap Knowledge Architecture
### 16:9 Landscape Cartography • 8 Plates • 16 Thematic Pillars • 64 Analytical Cards
**Source Authority:** Classical Sunni Exegesis (Tabari, Ibn Kathir, Qurtubi, Razi, Baghawi, Ibn al-Qayyim)
**Operating Principle:** Read. Reflect. Return.

---

## Plate 01: THE SOUND HEART & SALVIFIC CRITERION
**Section:** `PLATE 01 : QALB SALEEM` | **Focus:** Pillars 01 & 02: Qalb Saleem as the Sole Resurrection Currency & Ibrahim's Pure Devotion

### PILLAR 01: THE SOLE CURRENCY OF SALVATION
*Yawma La Yanfa'u Malun wa La Banoon, Illa Man Atallaha bi-Qalbin Saleem*

#### [01] The Futility of Earthly Currencies
- A Day on which neither billions in wealth nor legions of powerful sons can purchase ransom.

#### [02] The Sound Heart Defined (Qalb Saleem)
- Except one who arrives before Allah possessing a sound heart free from doubt, arrogance, and malice.

#### [03] Purification from Spiritual Toxins
- Cleansed of polytheism, envy, hatred, greed, and hypocritical duplicity before its Creator.

#### [04] The Inner Temple of Truth
- Understanding that while humans gaze upon outward forms, the Creator inspects the inner heart.

### PILLAR 02: IBRAHIM'S UNBLEMISHED POSTURE
*Idh Ja'a Rabbahu bi-Qalbin Saleem & Surrendering Idols*

#### [05] Approaching the Lord with Purity
- When he came to his Lord with a heart whole, intact, and dedicated solely to monotheistic truth.

#### [06] The Dismantling of Cultural Idols
- Challenging his people: Do you worship carved stone while Allah created you and what you manufacture?

#### [07] The Surrender of Fatherly Longing
- Submitting even his beloved son to divine decree without resentment or theological rebellion.

#### [08] The Model of Pure Monotheism (Hanif)
- Establishing the standard of authentic faith that remains an eternal lighthouse across generations.

## Plate 02: SERENITY IN REMEMBRANCE & SENSITIVE HEARTS
**Section:** `PLATE 02 : ITMI'NAN & DHIKR` | **Focus:** Pillars 03 & 04: The Absolute Rest of Dhikr & The Trembling Skin at Divine Recitation

### PILLAR 03: THE TRUE SETTLING OF THE SOUL
*Ala bi-Dhikrillahi Tatma'innul-Quloob & Healing Restlessness*

#### [09] The Primal Restlessness of Man
- The human heart possesses an innate void that no earthly pleasure, rank, or possession can satiate.

#### [10] The Sole Authentic Home (Itmi'nan)
- Ala bi-dhikrillahi: Verily, in the active remembrance of Allah alone do hearts find genuine tranquility.

#### [11] Dissolving Existential Anxiety
- Connecting with the Eternal anchor dispels panic over fleeting loss and future uncertainty.

#### [12] The Rhythmic Pulse of the Devout
- Transforming daily routine into continuous prayer through morning and evening invocations.

### PILLAR 04: THE TREMBLING AT REVELATION
*Taqsha'irru Minhu Julood & Thumma Taleenu Quloobuhum*

#### [13] The Descent of the Best Speech
- Allah has revealed the most beautiful discourse—a consistent Book repeating its warnings and glad tidings.

#### [14] Skins Shivering in Awe
- Skins of those who fear their Lord shiver and tremble at its cosmic majesty and eschatological proofs.

#### [15] The Softening of Body and Spirit
- Thumma taleenu julooduhum wa quloobuhum: Then their skins and hearts soften to the remembrance of Allah.

#### [16] The Light of Divine Guidance
- That is the guidance of Allah; He guides with it whomever seeks authentic truth with sincerity.

## Plate 03: THE CRUCIBLE OF SINCERITY & THE EYE OF GOD
**Section:** `PLATE 03 : PURE IKHLAS` | **Focus:** Pillars 05 & 06: Mukhlisan Lahud-Deen & Feeding Purely for the Countenance of Allah

### PILLAR 05: UNADULTERATED RELIGION (IKHLAS)
*Fa'budillaha Mukhlisan Lahud-Deen, Ala Lillahid-Deenul-Khalis*

#### [17] The Sole Mandate of Worship
- Worship Allah alone, keeping your faith, dedication, and intention entirely purified for Him.

#### [18] Unadulterated Devotion (Ad-Deen al-Khalis)
- Ala lillahid-deenul-khalis: Unquestionably, for Allah alone is pure, unmixed, unpolluted religion.

#### [19] Eradicating Hidden Ostentation (Riya')
- Purging spiritual practice from seeking human approval, social prestige, or moral superiority.

#### [20] The Gold Standard of Accepted Deeds
- A tiny deed executed with absolute sincerity outweighs mountains of actions tainted by self-worship.

### PILLAR 06: FEEDING FOR THE DIVINE FACE ALONE
*Innama Nut'imukum li-Wajhillah & Disdaining Human Thanks*

#### [21] Serving the Vulnerable Despite Need
- Surrendering food despite love and intense physical hunger to the poor, orphan, and captive.

#### [22] The Anthem of Pure Altruism
- Innama nut'imukum li-wajhillah: We feed you solely for the Countenance of Allah, not for earthly favor.

#### [23] Disdaining Even Verbal Gratitude
- La nureedu minkum jazaa'an wa la shukoora: We desire from you neither material reward nor a whisper of thanks.

#### [24] Protection on the Frowning Day
- Safeguarded by Allah from the terror of the grim Day, crowned with luminous radiance and inner joy.

## Plate 04: THE SICKNESS OF COMPLACENCY & HARDENED HEARTS
**Section:** `PLATE 04 : QASAWA & KHUSHU'` | **Focus:** Pillars 07 & 08: Has the Time Not Come to Soften? & Hearts Harder Than Fractured Granite

### PILLAR 07: THE GENTLE ADMONITION OF AL-HADID
*Alam Ya'ni Lilladheena Amanoo an Takhsha'a Quloobuhum*

#### [25] The Tender Divine Interrogation
- Alam ya'ni lilladheena amanoo: Has the time not yet arrived for those who have believed to humble their hearts?

#### [26] Humbling at Divine Remembrance
- An takhsha'a quloobuhum li-dhikrillah: To tremble and surrender in awe before the truth revealed.

#### [27] Warning Against the Prolonged Span
- Wa la yakoonoo kalladheena ootul-kitaba min qablu: Be not like those given scripture before, whose hearts hardened.

#### [28] The Insidious Creep of Spiritual Rust
- How routine without contemplation gradually encases the living heart in callous indifference.

### PILLAR 08: HEARTS HARDER THAN SPLITTING STONES
*Thumma Qasat Quloobukum Fahya kal-Hijarati aw Ashaddu Qaswah*

#### [29] The Metaphor of Petrified Hearts
- Their hearts became callous after seeing signs—like stones, or even harder in cold inflexibility.

#### [30] The Granite That Gushes Forth Water
- Truly, among stones are those from which rivers gush forth, and those that split yielding fresh water.

#### [31] The Rocks That Fall in Fear of God
- And truly, among stones are those that tumble down from precipices in awesome reverence of Allah.

#### [32] The Tragic Inflexibility of Arrogance
- Unmasking the horror of human pride that resists truth when inanimate geological boulders submit.

## Plate 05: THE EXPANDED BREAST & TOTAL RELIANCE
**Section:** `PLATE 05 : SHARH AS-SADR` | **Focus:** Pillars 09 & 10: Sharh as-Sadr for Divine Illumination & Anchoring in the Living King

### PILLAR 09: EXPANSION OF THE BREAST FOR LIGHT
*Afaman Sharahallahu Sadrahu lil-Islami fa-Huwa 'ala Noor*

#### [33] The Miracle of Spiritual Expansion
- Is one whose breast Allah has expanded to Islam, so that he walks in luminous light, like the blind?

#### [34] Walking upon Divine Illumination
- Fa-huwa 'ala noorin min rabbih: Navigating moral dilemmas with clarity granted by the Lord.

#### [35] Woe to Those with Callous Chests
- Fa-waylul-lil-qasiyati quloobuhum min dhikrillah: Woe to those whose hearts are hardened against Dhikr!

#### [36] The Manifest Error of the Closed Mind
- They are trapped in manifest blindness, immune to beauty and insensible to divine warnings.

### PILLAR 10: TETHERING TO THE IMMORTAL SOVEREIGN
*Wa Tawakkal 'alal-Hayyilladhee La Yamoot & Ceasing Fear*

#### [37] The Delusion of Human Anchors
- Trusting mortal patrons, political structures, or bank accounts leads invariably to betrayal and grief.

#### [38] Reliance Upon the Ever-Living Lord
- Wa tawakkal 'alal-Hayy: Anchor your complete trust exclusively upon the One who never dies.

#### [39] Glorification with Constant Praise
- Wa sabbih bi-hamdih: Exalt Him with praise; He is completely sufficient as Aware of servant sins.

#### [40] The Freedom of Genuine Tawakkul
- The soul that relies upon God is emancipated from the slavery of fawning over created mortals.

## Plate 06: SPIRITUAL PATHOLOGY & THE CURTAINED HEART
**Section:** `PLATE 06 : SICKNESS & SEALING` | **Focus:** Pillars 11 & 12: The Sickness of Hypocrisy & The Divine Sealing of the Arrogant Tyrant

### PILLAR 11: THE PROTOCOL OF SPIRITUAL SICKNESS
*Fee Quloobihim Maradun Fa-Zadahumullahu Marada*

#### [41] The Diagnosis of Internal Disease
- Fee quloobihim marad: In their hearts is a spiritual disease of doubt, malice, cowardice, and duplicity.

#### [42] The Compounding of Pathology
- Fa-zadahumullahu marada: So Allah allowed their disease to fester and multiply as a natural consequence.

#### [43] The Illusion of Reform (Muslihoon)
- When told: 'Do not cause corruption on earth,' they protest: 'We are merely enlightened reformers!'

#### [44] The Self-Deception of Duplicity
- Unmasking the tragedy of those who seek to deceive God and believers, only deceiving themselves.

### PILLAR 12: THE SEAL UPON THE HAUGHTY TYRANT
*Yatba'ullahu 'ala Kulli Qalbin Mutakabbirin Jabbar*

#### [45] Arrogant Contention Against Truth
- Those who dispute concerning the signs of Allah without any authoritative proof sent unto them.

#### [46] Greatly Odious in the Sight of God
- Kabura maqtan 'indallahi: Their haughty cynicism is immensely hateful in the sight of God and believers.

#### [47] The Inevitable Sealing of the Heart
- Kadhalika yatba'ullahu: Thus does Allah stamp and seal the heart of every arrogant, tyrannical soul.

#### [48] The Loss of Moral Perception
- When arrogance becomes supreme, the soul loses the faculty to distinguish beauty from ugliness.

## Plate 07: THE RETURNING PENITENT HEART (QALB MUNEEB)
**Section:** `PLATE 07 : QALB MUNEEB` | **Focus:** Pillars 13 & 14: Fearing the Merciful in Unseen Solitude & Stripping Rancor from the Breasts

### PILLAR 13: REVERENCE IN SOLITUDE
*Man Khashiyar-Rahmana bil-Ghaybi wa Ja'a bi-Qalbin Muneeb*

#### [49] Bringing the Garden in Close Proximity
- The Garden brought near to the God-conscious, not far off: This is what you were promised!

#### [50] Fearing the Merciful in Secret (Bil-Ghayb)
- For everyone who was constantly returning and penitent, revering the Merciful in private solitude.

#### [51] Arriving with a Penitent Heart
- Wa ja'a bi-qalbin muneeb: And who arrived before his Lord with a heart ever turning back in repentance.

#### [52] Enter in Serene Peace (Udkhulooha bi-Salam)
- Enter it in peace! This is the Day of Eternity; therein they possess whatever their souls crave.

### PILLAR 14: STRIPPING RANCOR & MALICE (GHILL)
*Wa Naza'na Ma fee Sudoorihim min Ghill & Fraternal Peace*

#### [53] Purging Inner Resentments in Eden
- Wa naza'na ma fee sudoorihim min ghill: We will strip away whatever hidden rancor or malice was in their chests.

#### [54] Reclining in Fraternal Delight
- Brothers reclining upon couches facing one another, freed from worldly competition and jealousy.

#### [55] The Prayer of the Faithful Generations
- Rabbana-ghfir lana wa li-ikhwanina... wa la taj'al fee quloobina ghillan lilladheena amanoo.

#### [56] The Cleansing of the Community
- Sanctifying the Muslim collective by praying that no bitter grievance or envy taints brotherhood.

## Plate 08: THE FINAL AUDIT OF SECRET INTENTIONALITY
**Section:** `PLATE 08 : THE FINAL AUDIT` | **Focus:** Pillars 15 & 16: Divine Omniscience of Cheating Eyes & The Laying Bare of Chest Contents

### PILLAR 15: THE GAZE AND THE SECRET CHEST
*Ya'lamu Kha'inatal-A'yuni wa Ma Tukhfis-Sudoor*

#### [57] The Absolute Omniscience of God
- Allah knows the stealthy, deceitful glance of the eyes and whatever the chests conceal in secret.

#### [58] No Thought Hidden from the Maker
- Whether you conceal your speech or disclose it loudly, He is fully Aware of what lies in breasts.

#### [59] Closer Than the Jugular Vein
- We created man and We know what his soul whispers to him; We are closer to him than his jugular vein.

#### [60] The Bedrock of Authentic Mindfulness
- Realizing that every internal thought, grievance, and longing is open before the divine gaze.

### PILLAR 16: THE LAYING BARE OF SECRETS
*Wa Hussila Ma fis-Sudoor & The Unvarnished Truth*

#### [61] The Overturning of the Graves
- Does man not know when the contents of the graves are poured forth into living reality?

#### [62] The Extracting of Chest Intentions
- Wa hussila ma fis-sudoor: And that which was hidden inside human chests is laid bare and scrutinized.

#### [63] The Stripping of Hypocritical Masks
- Every worldly disguise, public persona, and political posturing collapses into transparent reality.

#### [64] The Absolute Knowledge of the Lord
- Truly their Lord is fully Informed of them on that monumental Day, rewarding pure hearts with peace.
