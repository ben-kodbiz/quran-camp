# Surah At-Tur: Master Mindmap Knowledge Architecture
### 16:9 Landscape Cartography • 8 Plates • 16 Thematic Pillars • 64 Analytical Cards
**Source Authority:** Classical Sunni Exegesis (Tabari, Ibn Kathir, Qurtubi, Razi, Baghawi, Ibn al-Qayyim)
**Operating Principle:** Read. Reflect. Return.

---

## Plate 01: THE FIVE SACRED OATHS & THE INEVITABLE TORMENT
**Section:** `PLATE 01 : THE SACRED OATHS` | **Focus:** Pillars 01 & 02: The Five Oaths (Sinai, Inscribed Book, Ka'bah, Canopy & Sea) & The Inevitable Fall of Doom

### PILLAR 01: THE FIVE COSMIC AND LITURGICAL OATHS
*Wat-Tur, Kitabin Mastoor, Baytil-Ma'moor, Saqfil-Marfoo' & Bahril-Masjoor*

#### [01] By the Mount of Sinai (Wat-Tur)
- Wat-tur: by the blessed mountain of revelation where Moses spoke directly with the Divine.

#### [02] By the Inscribed Sacred Scripture
- Wa kitabin mastoor: by the eternal scripture inscribed on unrolled parchment of light.

#### [03] By the Much-Frequented House (Al-Bayt al-Ma'moor)
- By the celestial sanctuary directly above the Ka'bah where seventy thousand angels pray daily.

#### [04] By the Raised Ceiling & Swelling Ocean
- By the vaulted canopy of heaven and the swelling ocean filled with roaring subterranean fire.

### PILLAR 02: THE INEVITABLE DESCENT OF PUNISHMENT
*Inna 'Adhaba Rabbika la-Waqi', None Can Avert It & Sky Shaking with Tremors*

#### [05] The Retribution of Your Lord is Inevitable
- Inna 'adhaba Rabbika la-waqi': indeed, the threatened punishment of your Lord will surely come to pass!

#### [06] None Has Power to Ward It Off
- Ma lahu min dafi': there is no power in creation capable of averting, blocking, or preventing it.

#### [07] The Day the Sky Shakes with Violent Motion
- On the Day when the heavens shudder and reel with tremendous cosmic convulsions.

#### [08] And Mountains Move Shifting Away
- And the massive stabilizing mountains dissolve, moving away like drifting clouds of dust.

## Plate 02: CONFRONTATION WITH THE SEARING FIRE
**Section:** `PLATE 02 : SEARING FLAMES` | **Focus:** Pillars 03 & 04: Woe to Reckless Conjecturers & Thrusting into Hell: Is This Magic or Do You Not See?

### PILLAR 03: WOE TO THE SPECULATIVE DENIERS
*Waylun Yawma'idhin lil-Mukadhdhibeen, Playing in Vain & Thrust into Jahannam*

#### [09] Woe on That Day to the Deniers of Truth
- Waylun yawma'idhin lil-mukadhdhibeen: destruction and woe on that Day to all who rejected reality.

#### [10] Those Who Flounder in Frivolous Conjectures
- Those who spent their earthly lives playing carelessly in cynical debate and frivolous distractions.

#### [11] Thrust Violently into the Heart of Jahannam
- The Day they are shoved and thrust violently into the scorching blaze of Hellfire.

#### [12] This is the Fire You Used to Deny!
- They are told: This is the very Fire whose existence you mockingly denied in the world!

### PILLAR 04: IS THIS MAGIC OR DO YOU NOT SEE?
*Afa-Sihrun Hadha?, Burn Therein, Patience Equal to Despair & Recompensed for Deeds*

#### [13] Is This Retribution Magic, or Are You Blind?
- Afa-sihrun hadha am antum la tubsiroon: is this inescapable reality magic, or do you still fail to see?!

#### [14] Burn Therein: Patient Endurance Avails Nothing
- Roast within it: whether you endure with patience or wail in despair, it is entirely equal for you!

#### [15] Recompensed Exactly for What You Committed
- Innama tujzawna ma kuntum ta'maloon: you are only receiving the direct harvest of your own deeds.

#### [16] The Absolute Mathematical Equity of Divine Justice
- No soul is burdened beyond what it earned, and no criminal escapes his verified responsibility.

## Plate 03: THE DELIGHTS OF AT-TUR & PARADISE LUXURY
**Section:** `PLATE 03 : EDEN LUXURY` | **Focus:** Pillars 05 & 06: The God-Conscious in Bliss, Shielded from Hell & Reclining on Couches Paired with Spouses

### PILLAR 05: THE RIGHTEOUS LUXURIATING IN BLISS
*Innal-Muttaqeena fee Jannatin wa Na'eem, Shielded from Hell & Eat and Drink Joyfully*

#### [17] The Pious in Everlasting Gardens and Bliss
- Innal-muttaqeena fee jannatin wa na'eem: the God-conscious are immersed in Gardens and unbroken bliss.

#### [18] Delighting in What Their Lord Bestowed
- Fakiheena bima atahum Rabbuhum: rejoicing exultantly in the sublime gifts granted by their Lord.

#### [19] Shielded Completely from the Searing Fire
- Wa waqahum Rabbuhum 'adhaba al-jaheem: and their Lord has permanently shielded them from Hell.

#### [20] Eat and Drink with Healthy Joy for Your Deeds
- Kuloo washraboo hanee'an bima kuntum ta'maloon: partake in wholesome joy as reward for your devotion!

### PILLAR 06: RECLINING ON ARRAYED THRONES WITH SPOUSES
*Muttaki'eena 'ala Suroorin Masfoofah, Paired with Pure Spouses & Exquisite Splendor*

#### [21] Reclining on Thrones Arranged in Ranks
- Muttaki'eena 'ala suroorin masfoofah: resting comfortably upon elevated couches aligned in majestic ranks.

#### [22] Paired with Pure Companions of Luminous Eyes
- Wa zawwajnahum bi-hoorin 'een: and We pair them with celestial companions of radiant, pure beauty.

#### [23] Complete Absence of Resentment or Fatigue
- Souls cleansed of all earthly grievances, surrounded by tranquil companionship and eternal peace.

#### [24] The Visual Harmony of Paradisiacal Palaces
- Surrounded by flawless architectural aesthetics reflecting divine generosity and sovereign love.

## Plate 04: THE FAMILY REUNION & THE IMMORTAL GOBLETS
**Section:** `PLATE 04 : FAMILY REUNION` | **Focus:** Pillars 07 & 08: Joined with Righteous Offspring in Jannah & Serving Goblets Untouched by Idle Talk or Sin

### PILLAR 07: REUNITED WITH BELIEVING OFFSPRING
*Alhaqna Bihim Dhurriyyatahum, Deeds Not Diminished & Every Soul Pledged for What It Earned*

#### [25] Believers Reunited with Their Righteous Offspring
- Those who believed and whose children followed them in faith: We reunite them with their families in Jannah!

#### [26] Never Diminishing Their Rewards in the Least
- Wa ma alatnahum min 'amalihim min shay': We will never reduce the reward of their deeds to elevate their kin.

#### [27] A Pure Gift of Divine Generosity and Honor
- Children elevated to the high stations of their righteous parents to complete their eternal happiness.

#### [28] Every Soul is Held Pledged for What It Earned
- Kullu imri'in bima kasaba raheen: yet every individual human being remains personally accountable.

### PILLAR 08: THE WINE UNTOUCHED BY IDLE TALK OR SIN
*Passing Goblets of Wine, La Laghwun feeha wa la Ta'theem & Circulating Youths Like Pearls*

#### [29] Passing Between Themselves Goblets of Pure Wine
- Yatanaza'oona feeha ka'san: they pass to one another exquisite chalices of celestial drink.

#### [30] No Idle Talk (Laghw) Nor Any Sin Therein
- La laghwun feeha wa la ta'theem: free from vain, nonsensical speech, headache, or moral iniquity.

#### [31] Circulating Attendants Like Well-Guarded Pearls
- Attended by youthful servers moving gracefully like hidden, untouched pearls preserved in shells.

#### [32] An Atmosphere of Unbroken Dignity and Peace
- Conversing in joyful serenity, recalling their past earthly trials with deep gratitude.

## Plate 05: CONFESSION OF THE BLESSED: WE LIVED IN AWE
**Section:** `PLATE 05 : REMEMBERING FEAR` | **Focus:** Pillars 09 & 10: Inna Kunna Qablu fee Ahlina Mushfiqeen & Saved from the Searing Scorching Wind (Samoom)

### PILLAR 09: THE CONFESSION: WE LIVED IN AWE
*Turning in Inquiry, Inna Kunna Mushfiqeen & Reverent Humility in the Home*

#### [33] They Turn to One Another Inquiring and Reflecting
- And they turn to one another in intimate conversation, reminiscing over their mortal journey.

#### [34] Indeed, Within Our Earthly Families We Lived in Awe
- Qaloo inna kunna qablu fee ahlina mushfiqeen: they say: Indeed, amidst our families on earth we lived in fear!

#### [35] Fear of Falling into Sin and Moral Deviation
- Conscious of divine scrutiny, guarding the sanctity of the household against corruption.

#### [36] Humility in Daily Life Produces Eternal Peace
- The awe experienced in the private home blossoms into everlasting security in the presence of God.

### PILLAR 10: DELIVERANCE FROM THE SEARING SCORCHING WIND
*Fa-Manna Allahu 'Alayna, Saved from Samoom & Innahu Huwa al-Barr ar-Raheem*

#### [37] So Allah Conferred His Boundless Grace Upon Us
- Fa-manna Allahu 'alayna: so Allah showered His generous favor upon us and preserved our faith.

#### [38] Shielded from the Poisonous Scorching Wind (Samoom)
- Wa waqana 'adhaba as-samoom: and He delivered us from the scorching, penetrating wind of Hellfire!

#### [39] Indeed, We Used to Implore Him of Old
- Inna kunna min qablu nad'ooh: indeed, during our earthly life we used to call upon Him in supplication!

#### [40] Truly, He is the Beneficent, the Most Merciful
- Innahu Huwa al-Barru ar-Raheem: He alone is the Fountain of All Goodness, the Ever-Merciful!

## Plate 06: SLANDERS REFUTED & PROPHETIC SANITY VINDICATED
**Section:** `PLATE 06 : SLANDERS REFUTED` | **Focus:** Pillars 11 & 12: Remind! You Are Neither Soothsayer nor Madman & Refuting the Lie of a Poet Awaiting Fate

### PILLAR 11: NEITHER SOOTHSAYER NOR MADMAN
*Fa-Dhakkir, Neither Kahin Nor Majnoon & Divine Vindication of Prophetic Sanity*

#### [41] Continue to Remind with Unshakable Resolve
- Fa-dhakkir: so remind mankind persistently, for your apostolic message is rooted in absolute clarity.

#### [42] By Your Lord's Favor, You Are No Soothsayer
- Fa-ma anta bi-ni'mati Rabbika bi-kahin: by your Lord's grace, you are no fortune-teller or soothsayer!

#### [43] Nor Are You in Any Way Possessed or Mad
- Wa la majnoon: nor are you mad or afflicted by jinn: infallible divine vindication of prophetic mind.

#### [44] The Resplendent Lucidity of Divine Revelation
- The coherence and ethical perfection of the Qur'an completely shatter pagan accusations.

### PILLAR 12: OR DO THEY SAY: A POET AWAITING CALAMITY?
*Sha'irun Natarabbasu bihi Raybal-Mamoon, Say Wait! & Does Intellect Command This?*

#### [45] Or Do They Say: A Poet Awaiting Fate?
- Or do they mutter: He is merely a poet for whom we wait the misfortune of time to silence him?

#### [46] Say: Wait! Indeed, I am Waiting Alongside You!
- Qul tarabbasoo fa-innee ma'akum minal-mutarabbiseen: say: Wait then; I too am waiting alongside you!

#### [47] Does Their Supposed Intellect Command This Folly?
- Am ta'muruhum ahlamuhum bi-hadha: do their lauded minds and intellects dictate such irrational slanders?

#### [48] Or Are They Simply a Transgressing, Rebellious People?
- Am hum qawmun taghoon: or are they an insolent people driven by arrogant tribal rebellion?

## Plate 07: THE FOUR PIERCING INQUESTS (JUBAYR'S CONVERSION)
**Section:** `PLATE 07 : PIERCING INQUEST` | **Focus:** Pillars 13 & 14: Were They Created from Nothing?, Did They Create the Skies? & Do They Own God's Treasuries?

### PILLAR 13: WERE THEY CREATED FROM NOTHING?!
*Am Khuliqoo min Ghayri Shay'in, Or Are They the Creators? & No Certainty in Hearts*

#### [49] Were They Created from Absolutely Nothing?
- Am khuliqoo min ghayri shay'in: were they created without any cause or Creator from absolute void?!

#### [50] Or Are They Themselves the Creators of Existence?
- Am humu al-khalifoon: or do they dare claim that they are the creators of their own beings?!

#### [51] Or Did They Create the Cosmic Heavens and Earth?
- Am khalaqoo as-samawati wal-ard: or did they fashion the celestial galaxies and terrestrial plane?!

#### [52] Nay, They Possess No True Certainty (La Yooqinoon)
- Bal la yooqinoon: nay, they possess no conviction, floundering in hollow intellectual arrogance.

### PILLAR 14: DO THEY OWN THE TREASURIES OF YOUR LORD?
*Am 'Indahum Khaza'inu Rabbik, Or Are They the Controllers? & Stairway to Heaven*

#### [53] Do They Own the Limitless Treasuries of Your Lord?
- Am 'indahum khaza'inu Rabbika: do they hold the sovereign keys to divine mercy and provision?

#### [54] Or Are They the Absolute Controllers of the Universe?
- Am humu al-musaytiroon: or are they the supreme rulers dictating the course of destiny?!

#### [55] Or Do They Possess a Stairway to Eavesdrop on Heaven?
- Am lahum sullamun yastami'oona feeh: do they have a ladder climbing to the celestial council?

#### [56] Let Their Eavesdropper Produce Clear Authority
- Fal-ya'ti mustami'uhum bi-sultanin mubeen: then let their secret listener bring forth clear proof!

## Plate 08: DIVINE PROTECTION & PRAISE IN NIGHT AND STARS
**Section:** `PLATE 08 : DIVINE VIGIL` | **Focus:** Pillars 15 & 16: You Are Under Our Very Eyes (Fa-innaka bi-A'yunina) & Praise When Arising and at Star Setting

### PILLAR 15: YOU ARE UNDER OUR VERY EYES (BI-A'YUNINA)
*Fasbir li-Hukmi Rabbika, Fa-innaka bi-A'yunina & Shielded from Pagan Deceit*

#### [57] Wait Patiently for the Sovereign Decree of Your Lord
- Fasbir li-hukmi Rabbika: endure with serene patience for the unfolding judgment of your Lord.

#### [58] For Indeed, You Are Under Our Very Eyes (Bi-A'yunina)
- Fa-innaka bi-a'yunina: for you are constantly under Our watchful eyes, protection, and loving gaze!

#### [59] The Ultimate Reassurance to the Prophetic Heart
- Shielding the Messenger ﷺ from assassination, ambush, and depression amidst intense Meccan hatred.

#### [60] Divine Custody Over the Bearers of Revelation
- Every sincere servant of truth walking in prophetic footsteps resides under special divine care.

### PILLAR 16: GLORIFYING GOD IN NIGHT AND RETIRING STARS
*Glorify When Arising, Exalt During the Night & At the Setting of the Stars (Idbara an-Nujoom)*

#### [61] Glorify the Praise of Your Lord Whenever You Arise
- Wa sabbih bi-hamdi Rabbika heena taqoom: glorify the praise of your Lord whenever you stand up for prayer.

#### [62] And During the Stillness of the Night Exalt Him
- Wa minal-layli fa-sabbih-hu: and in the quiet depths of the night celebrate His transcendent holiness.

#### [63] And at the Receding of the Stars at Dawn (Idbara an-Nujoom)
- Wa idbara an-nujoom: and at the retirement and setting of the stars as dawn illuminates the horizon.

#### [64] Sealing Revelation in Unbroken Celestial Liturgy
- Connecting mortal devotion with the rhythmic cosmic movements of celestial planetary spheres.
