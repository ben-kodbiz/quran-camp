Plate 01: THE DOMESTIC CRISIS & THE OATH OF PROHIBITION

PLATE 01 : DOMESTIC CRISIS
PILLAR 01: THE DIVINE REPROACH OVER PROHIBITION
Why Prohibit What Allah Made Lawful?, Seeking Pleasure of Spouses & Ghafoorun Raheem
01 The Gentle Divine Reproach to the Prophet
  • Ya ayyuha an-nabiyyu lima tuharrimu ma ahalla Allahu lak: why do you prohibit that which Allah has made lawful for you?
02 The Incident of the Sacred Vow
  • The Prophet took an oath to abstain from honey (or his concubine Mariyah) to placate the jealousy of his wives.
03 Seeking the Pleasure of Mortal Spouses
  • Tabtaghee mardata azwajik: seeking the contentment of your wives at the expense of lawful divine permissions.
04 The Reservoir of Divine Pardon
  • Wallahu Ghafoorun Raheem: and Allah is Oft-Forgiving, Most Merciful, releasing His Messenger from human pressure.
PILLAR 02: THE DISSOLUTION OF UNJUST OATHS
Qad Farada Allahu Tahillata Aymanikum, Allah is Your Mawla & The All-Knowing Wise
05 The Ordained Expiation of Oaths
  • Qad farada Allahu lakum tahillata aymanikum: Allah has already ordained for you the legal expiation of your oaths.
06 Nullifying Oaths That Prohibit the Halal
  • Teaching that swearing to forbid what God made permissible is invalid and must be broken via Kaffarah.
07 Allah as Supreme Guardian (Mawla)
  • Wallahu Mawlakum: and Allah is your ultimate Protector, Guardian, and Master in all life affairs.
08 Omniscience and Wisdom in Legislation
  • Wa Huwa al-'Aleemu al-Hakeem: and He is the All-Knowing of human weakness, the All-Wise in social remedies.
