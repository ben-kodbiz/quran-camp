Plate 01: THE DEMANDED TORMENT & THE ASCENDING WAYS

Pillars 01 & 02: Sa'ala Sa'ilun, The Ascending Stairways of Angels & The 50,000-Year Day

PLATE 01 : THE ASCENT & DAY
PILLAR 01: THE INEVITABLE RETRIBUTIVE STRIKE
Sa'ala Sa'ilun bi-'Adhabin Waqi' & Dhi'l-Ma'arij Sovereignty
01 The Cynical Demand for Punishment
  • A questioner cynically demanded an immediate taste of the promised punishment, oblivious to its irreversible finality.
02 None Can Repel It from Deniers
  • For the disbelievers, none can deflect or avert this retribution when its appointed moment arrives.
03 From Allah, Lord of Ascending Ways
  • Min Allahi Dhi'l-Ma'arij: originating from the Supreme Lord who commands all celestial stairs of ascent.
04 Graceful Patience Mandated (Sabran Jameela)
  • Command to the Prophet: endure their insults with graceful patience untouched by bitterness or complaint.
PILLAR 02: THE 50,000-YEAR COSMIC ASCENT
Ta'ruju al-Mala'ikatu war-Roohu & Distant to Men, Near to God
05 The Ascent of Angels and Gabriel
  • The angels and the Spirit (Jibreel) ascend to Him across dimensional thresholds of unfathomable expanse.
06 The Day of Fifty Thousand Years
  • Fee yawmin kana miqdaruhu khamseena alfa sanah: a single Day spanning fifty thousand human terrestrial years.
07 They See It as Far and Impossible
  • The skeptics perceive the Resurrection as remote, distant, and scientifically impossible.
08 We Behold It Proximate and Imminent
  • Wa narahu qareeba: while We know with absolute certainty that its occurrence is imminent and near.

Plate 02: MOLTEN COPPER SKIES & THE RANSOM OF KIN

Pillars 03 & 04: The Muhl Sky, Carded Wool Mountains & Willing to Ransom Children and Spouses

PLATE 02 : METALLIC SKY & RANSOM
PILLAR 03: CELESTIAL AND GEOLOGICAL DISSOLUTION
Kal-Muhl Sky, Kal-'Ihn Mountains & No Friend Asks of Friend
09 The Sky Like Molten Dross (Al-Muhl)
  • The heavens dissolve into churning, multicolored molten brass or boiling sulfurous oil.
10 Mountains Dispersed Like Carded Wool
  • The colossal rocky peaks fragmented into weightless, floating tufts of dyed wool (Kal-'Ihn).
11 No Intimate Friend Inquires of Another
  • Wa la yas'alu hameemun hameema: overwhelmed by personal terror, no loyal friend asks after another.
12 Beholding Loved Ones in Plain Sight
  • Yubassaroonahum: seeing one another clearly, yet frozen in cosmic self-preservation without mutual aid.
PILLAR 04: THE DESPERATE DESIRE TO RANSOM LOVED ONES
Yawaddu al-Mujrimu Law Yaftadi, Sons, Spouses & Clinging Clan
13 Willing to Ransom One's Own Children
  • The criminal desperately longs to ransom himself from the torment by sacrificing his dearest children.
14 Surrendering Spouse and Brother
  • Offering his devoted wife and protective brother to be hurled into the flames in his place.
15 Sacrificing His Entire Protecting Clan
  • Wa faseelatihi allatee tu'weeh: offering his extended tribal ancestry that shielded him in earthly life.
16 And Everyone on the Entire Globe
  • Yea, wishing everyone on the entire face of the earth could perish if only it could rescue his soul.

Plate 03: THE RAGING BLAZE & PSYCHOLOGY OF ANXIETY

Pillars 05 & 06: Kalla Innaha Ladha, Tearing Scalps & Inna al-Insana Khuliqa Halu'a

PLATE 03 : LADHA & ANXIETY
PILLAR 05: THE RAGING LADHA FLAME
Nazwa'atan li-sh-Shawa, Summoning Hoarders & Deserters of Truth
17 Never! It is the Raging Flame (Ladha)
  • Kalla innaha ladha: absolutely never! It is the leaping, roaring blaze that burns with sentient fury.
18 Stripping Away Scalps and Limbs
  • Nazza'atan li-sh-shawa: peeling away epidermal tissue, skin, and skull-caps from the charred body.
19 Calling Out to Those Who Retreated
  • Calling aloud by name to every individual who turned his back on faith and retreated in pride.
20 Those Who Accumulated and Hoarded
  • Targeting those who amassed financial wealth and greedily locked it away from charitable circulation.
PILLAR 06: THE PSYCHOPATHOLOGY OF SECULAR MAN
Khuliqa Halu'an: Jazu'an in Adversity & Manu'an in Prosperity
21 Created Restless and Anxious (Halu'a)
  • Inna al-insana khuliqa halu'a: human nature left unguided is inherently impatient, agitated, and unstable.
22 Despairing Panic in Adversity (Jazu'a)
  • When touched by financial loss, sickness, or misfortune, he collapses into bitter panic and rage.
23 Withholding Greed in Prosperity (Manu'a)
  • When touched by wealth, ease, and surplus, he transforms into an arrogant, tight-fisted hoarder.
24 The Divine Exception: The Faithful
  • Illa al-musalleen: entirely exempted from this neurotic cycle are those established in devoted prayer.

Plate 04: THE CHARTER OF THE FAITHFUL (AL-MUSALLOON)

Pillars 07 & 08: Constant Prayer, Apportioned Charity, Believing in Judgment & Chaste Integrity

PLATE 04 : THE MUSALLOON
PILLAR 07: DEVOTION, FINANCIAL CHARITY & DAY OF DECREE
Da'imoon on Prayer, Haqqun Ma'loom & Certainty of Recompense
25 Perpetual Constancy in Prayer
  • Those who maintain unbroken discipline and spiritual presence in their compulsory prayers (Da'imoon).
26 A Recognized Right in Their Wealth
  • Allocating a defined, non-negotiable portion (Haqqun Ma'loom) of their capital for societal welfare.
27 For the Beggar and the Deprived
  • Specifically serving the vocal beggar (Sa'il) and the dignified, silent poor who never beg (Mahroom).
28 Affirming the Day of Final Judgment
  • Living with vivid, unshakable conviction in the reality of the Day of Recompense (Yawm ad-Deen).
PILLAR 08: CHASTE INTEGRITY & FULFILLING SACRED TRUSTS
Guarding Chastity, Amanat, Covenants, Witness & Honored in Gardens
29 Guarding Private Chastity
  • Preserving moral purity and sexual discipline, restricting intimacy exclusively to lawful matrimony.
30 Faithful to Trusts and Covenants
  • Meticulously fulfilling contractual obligations, financial deposits (Amanat), and solemn oaths.
31 Upright and Uncompromising in Witness
  • Standing firm and fearless when testifying to truth, refusing to conceal facts or favor the powerful.
32 Honored Guests in Eternal Gardens
  • Ula'ika fee jannatin mukramoon: such righteous souls will dwell as honored dignitaries in perpetual Eden.

Plate 05: RACING DENIERS & THE LAW OF REPLACEMENT

Pillars 09 & 10: Craning Necks in Mockery, Right and Left Factions & Replacing Disobedient Hosts

PLATE 05 : LAW OF REPLACEMENT
PILLAR 09: DISBELIEVERS CRANING IN RIDICULE
Muhti'een, 'Anil-Yameeni wa 'Anish-Shimaali 'Izeen & Desiring Paradise?
33 Why Do Deniers Rush Around You?
  • What is with those who disbelieve, craning their necks and rushing eagerly toward you in derision?
34 Flocking in Splinter Groups ('Izeen)
  • Gathering on your right and left in mocking cliques and sectarian factions, laughing at revelation.
35 Does Every Man Expect Paradise?
  • Does every arrogant transgressor among them expect to be ushered into a Garden of pure Delight?
36 Never! Created from What They Know
  • Kalla: absolutely not! We created them from a humble, fluid substance they know well, yet they boast.
PILLAR 10: LORD OF EASTS AND WESTS & REPLACEMENT
Bi-Rabbi al-Mashariqi wal-Magharib, We Are Able & Never Outstripped
37 Swearing by the Lord of Easts and Wests
  • Fala uqsimu bi-Rabbi al-mashariqi wal-magharib: oath by the sovereign Lord of all cosmic celestial horizons.
38 Absolute Power of Replacement
  • Inna la-qadiroon 'ala an nubaddila khayran minhum: We possess absolute omnipotence to replace them with a superior nation.
39 We Are Never Outstripped or Frustrated
  • Wa ma nahnu bi-masbooqeen: We can never be defeated, pre-empted, or outmaneuvered by human arrogance.
40 Leave Them to Wade and Play
  • Fa-dharhum yakhoodoo wa yal'aboo: leave them to drown in frivolous debates until they meet their promised Day.

Plate 06: EMERGING LIKE RACERS TO IDOLS

PLATE 06 : THE RESURRECTION SURGE
PILLAR 11: THE SUDDEN EMERGENCE FROM GRAVES

  • The Day they will burst forth from subterranean tombs in swift haste, summoned by the cosmic call.
42 Racing as if to Pagan Altars (Nusub)
  • Rushing forward blindly, just as they once eagerly sprinted to worship and sacrifice at idol stones.
43 The Poetic Irony of Retribution
  • The frantic zeal they displayed for idolatry in life is enacted against their will on the plain of Judgment.
44 Gathered Toward Inescapable Reckoning
  • Propelled inexorably toward the grand divine tribunal from which no escape or concealment is possible.
PILLAR 12: OVERWHELMING DISGRACE & THE CLOSING WARNING
Khashi'atan Absaruhum, Tarhaquhum Dhillah & Hadha al-Yawm
45 Downcast and Shamed Eyes
  • Khashi'atan absaruhum: eyes cast downward in profound humiliation, unable to gaze upward in defiance.
46 Enveloped in Humiliating Disgrace
  • Tarhaquhum dhillah: completely blanketed and suffocated by catastrophic ignominy and terror.
47 This is the Day You Were Promised
  • Dhalika al-yawmu alladhi kanoo yoo'adoon: this is the undeniable Day you were repeatedly warned against.
48 The End of Temporal Vanity
  • All Meccan mockery, wealth parades, and arrogant sneers erased beneath the crushing reality of Judgment.

PILLAR 13: THE OCCASION OF AL-NADR'S CHALLENGE
Exegesis of Sa'ala Sa'ilun across Kutub as-Sittah

  • Documented that Al-Nadr petitioned: 'O Allah, if this is the truth from You, rain upon us stones!'

  • Fakhr al-Din al-Razi explains Ma'arij as celestial ranks of angels and divine spiritual favors.
52 Qurtubi on the Nature of Sabran Jameela
  • Defining beautiful patience as endurance devoid of self-pity or complaint directed to creatures.
PILLAR 14: THE PSYCHOLOGICAL EXPANSION OF TIME
The 50,000-Year Day: Agony for Deniers, Moments for Believers
53 The Agony of Temporal Dilation
  • Ibn Kathir cites authentic traditions that the Day is 50,000 years of agony for disbelievers.
54 Relief for the Righteous Believer
  • In Musnad Ahmad, the Prophet ﷺ explained: 'By Him in Whose Hand is my soul, it is lightened for the believer until it is shorter than a single obligatory prayer.'
55 The Seven Virtues of the Musalloon
  • Classical jurists analyze how the eight qualities described in 70:22-34 form the complete Islamic ethical code.
56 Haqqun Ma'loom: Beyond Compulsory Zakah
  • Scholars note that establishing regular charity cultivates empathy and shields the heart from Halu'a.
