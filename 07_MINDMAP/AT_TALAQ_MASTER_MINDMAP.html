Plate 01: SACRED PROTOCOL OF MARITAL DISSOLUTION

Pillars 01 & 02: Divorcing at Prescribed Periods, Counting the 'Iddah, Do Not Evict Wives & Sacred Boundaries

PLATE 01 : MARITAL PROTOCOL
PILLAR 01: THE PRECISE LITURGICAL TIMING
Idha Tallaqtumu an-Nisa'a fa-Talliqoohunna li-'Iddatihinna, Ahsoo al-'Iddah
01 The Sovereign Direction to the Prophet
  • Ya ayyuha an-nabiyyu idha tallaqtumu an-nisa'a: O Prophet, when you divorce women, divorce at their term.
02 Divorcing During Purity Without Intimacy
  • Divorcing strictly during a period of ritual purity (Tuhr) in which no marital intimacy took place.
03 The Sacred Duty of Counting the 'Iddah
  • Wa ahsoo al-'iddah: and calculate the waiting period with meticulous precision to protect lineage.
04 Reverence for the Sustainer
  • Wattaqoo Allaha Rabbakum: and maintain awe of Allah your Lord throughout all domestic disputes.
PILLAR 02: THE PROHIBITION OF DOMESTIC EVICTION
La Tukhrijoohunna min Buyootihinna, Nor Shall They Leave & Transgressing Bounds
05 Absolute Sanctuary of the Marital Home
  • La tukhrijoohunna min buyootihinna: do not evict them from their homes during the waiting period.
06 The Wife's Right to Remain Sheltered
  • Wa la yakhrujna: nor should they depart voluntarily; the home remains their protective sanctuary.
07 The Sole Exception: Proven Indecency
  • Illa an ya'teena bi-fahishatin mubayyinah: except if they commit clear, manifest moral indecency.
08 The Sacred Boundaries of Allah (Hudood)
  • Wa tilka hudoodu Allahi: those are Allah's limits; whoever transgresses them wrongs his own soul.

  • Fa-idha balaghna ajalahunna: and when they have almost reached the end of their prescribed period.

  • Fa-amsikoohunna bi-ma'roof: retain them in recognized honour, mutual respect, and restored love.
11 The Second Option: Honourable Parting
  • Aw fariqoohunna bi-ma'roof: or separate from them in dignity, generosity, and complete absence of malice.
12 Eradicating Domestic Spite
  • Barring men from holding wives in emotional limbo or abusing legal loopholes to inflict psychological harm.

Ash-hidoo Dhaway 'Adlin Minkum, Establish Testimony for Allah & Sincere Counsel
13 Formal Legal Institutionalization
  • Wa ash-hidoo dhaway 'adlin minkum: and call to witness two just, upright men from among your community.
14 Witnessing Both Return and Separation
  • Securing public legal clarity whether the marriage is restored or formally dissolved, preventing fraud.
15 Establishing Testimony Solely for Allah
  • Wa aqeemoo ash-shahadata lillah: and establish the testimony purely for Allah, free from bias or bribery.
16 Admonition for Believers in Eternity
  • Dhalikum yoo'adhu bihi man kana yu'minu billahi wal-yawmi al-akhir: counsel for whoever believes in Allah and the Last Day.
