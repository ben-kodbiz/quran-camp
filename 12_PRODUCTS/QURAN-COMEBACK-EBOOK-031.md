# CHAPTER 31: SURAH LUQMAN (سُورَةُ لقمان)
**Classification:** Makki  
**Order in Mushaf:** 31  
**Total Verses:** 34  
**Famous Names:** Surat Luqman, Surat al-Hikmah (The Chapter of Wisdom)  

---

## 1. Why This Surah Matters
Surah Luqman is the divine masterclass in mindful parenting and spiritual pedagogy. It centers on the wise sage Luqman as he mentors his son through intimate life lessons: warning against the cosmic injustice of polytheism (*Shirk*), gratitude to parents, the microscopic accountability of human deeds—even the weight of a mustard seed hidden inside a rock (*Habbatin min khardal*)—establishing prayer, enjoining good with patience, and walking on the earth without strutting or arrogance. It closes with the 'Five Keys of the Unseen' known only to Allah.

---

## 2. Surah Context
Revealed in Makkah when polytheist parents exerted intense emotional pressure on their young sons and daughters who embraced Islam. Surah Luqman established the delicate balance: honor and serve your parents with kindness in worldly life, but never compromise your monotheism or divine values.

---

## 3. Central Theme
**Divine Wisdom (*Al-Hikmah*), Ethical Mentorship of Youth, Microscopic Accountability, and the Five Unseen Realms (*Mafatih al-Ghayb*).**

---

## 4. Key Passages
### Luqman's First Advice to His Son (31:13)
$$\text{وَإِذْ قَالَ لُقْمَانُ لِابْنِهِ وَهُوَ يَعِظُهُ يَا بُنَيَّ لَا تُشْرِكْ بِاللَّهِ ۖ إِنَّ الشِّرْكَ لَظُلْمٌ عَظِيمٌ}$$
*"And [mention, O Muhammad], when Luqman said to his son while he was instructing him, 'O my son, do not associate [anything] with Allah. Indeed, association [with Him] is great injustice.'"*

### The Mustard Seed of Accountability (31:16)
$$\text{يَا بُنَيَّ إِنَّهَا إِن تَكُ مِثْقَالَ حَبَّةٍ مِّنْ خَرْدَلٍ فَتَكُن فِي صَخْرَةٍ أَوْ فِي السَّمَاوَاتِ أَوْ فِي الْأَرْضِ يَأْتِ بِهَا اللَّهُ ۚ إِنَّ اللَّهَ لَطِيفٌ خَبِيرٌ}$$
*"O my son, indeed if wrong should be the weight of a mustard seed and should be within a rock or [anywhere] in the heavens or in the earth, Allah will bring it forth. Indeed, Allah is Subtle and Acquainted."*

### The Blueprint of Noble Character (31:17–19)
$$\text{يَا بُنَيَّ أَقِمِ الصَّلَاةَ وَأْمُرْ بِالْمَعْرُوفِ وَانْهَ عَنِ الْمُنكَرِ وَاصْبِرْ عَلَىٰ مَا أَصَابَكَ ۖ إِنَّ ذَٰلِكَ مِنْ عَزْمِ الْأُمُورِ ۝ وَلَا تُصَعِّرْ خَدَّكَ لِلنَّاسِ وَلَا تَمْشِ فِي الْأَرْضِ مَرَحًا ۖ إِنَّ اللَّهَ لَا يُحِبُّ كُلَّ مُخْتَالٍ فَخُورٍ ۝ وَاقْصِدْ فِي مَشْيِكَ وَاغْضُضْ مِن صَوْتِكَ ۚ إِنَّ أَنكَرَ الْأَصْوَاتِ لَصَوْتُ الْحَمِيرِ}$$
*"O my son, establish prayer, enjoin what is right, forbid what is wrong, and be patient over what befalls you. Indeed, [all] that is of the matters [requiring] determination. And do not turn your cheek [in contempt] toward people and do not walk through the earth exultantly. Indeed, Allah does not like everyone self-deluded and boastful. And be moderate in your pace and lower your voice; indeed, the most disagreeable of sounds is the voice of donkeys."*

## 5. Translation Highlights (The Clear Quran)
* *31:14:* 'And We have enjoined upon man [care] for his parents. His mother carried him, [increasing her] in weakness upon weakness, and his weaning is in two years. Be grateful to Me and to your parents; to Me is the [final] destination.'
* *31:27:* 'And if whatever trees upon the earth were pens and the sea [was ink], replenished thereafter by seven [more] seas, the words of Allah would not be exhausted. Indeed, Allah is Exalted in Might and Wise.'
* *31:34:* 'Indeed, Allah [alone] has knowledge of the Hour and sends down the rain and knows what is in the wombs...'

---

## 6. What Classical Tafsir Explains
* **Why Shirk is the Greatest Injustice (Verse 13):** Ibn Kathir relates that when verse 6:82 was revealed ('Those who believe and do not mix their belief with injustice'), the Companions were distressed and asked, 'Who among us does not wrong himself?' The Prophet ﷺ clarified: 'It is not as you think; have you not heard what the righteous servant Luqman said to his son? *Indeed, Shirk is a tremendous injustice!*'
* **Turning the Cheek in Contempt (*La Tusa''ir*) (Verse 18):** Imam al-Qurtubi explains that *Sa'ar* was an Arabian disease that twisted a camel's neck sideways. Metaphorically, Luqman warns against twisting one's face away from people in disgust, snobbery, or social superiority.
---

## 7. Important Supporting Hadiths
1. **The Keys of the Unseen:**  
   *Sahih al-Bukhari (Hadith 4627) from Ibn Umar (RA):*  
   "The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: 'The keys of the Unseen are five, which no one knows except Allah.' Then he recited verse 31:34: *'Indeed, Allah [alone] has knowledge of the Hour and sends down the rain and knows what is in the wombs. And no soul perceives what it will earn tomorrow, and no soul perceives in what land it will die. Indeed, Allah is Knowing and Acquainted.'*"
2. **Wisdom and Gratitude:**  
   *Al-Mustadrak 'ala al-Sahihayn (Hadith 3928), Sahih:*  
   "Ibn Abbas (RA) stated: 'Luqman was an Ethiopian carpenter endowed with profound wisdom (*Hikmah*). When asked how he attained it, he said: Truthful speech, fulfilling trusts, and leaving what does not concern me.'"
---

## 8. Key Concepts
1. **Al-Hikmah (Wisdom):** The ability to place every thought, action, and speech in its proper, God-fearing proportion.
2. **Mithqala Habbatin min Khardal (The Mustard Seed):** The vivid Qur'anic symbol for absolute cosmic accountability where not a microgram of deed is overlooked.
3. **Wahnan 'ala Wahn (Weakness upon Weakness):** The physical, emotional, and biological toll of pregnancy and motherhood commanding lifelong filial gratitude.
4. **Mafatih al-Ghayb (Keys of the Unseen):** The five absolute cosmic domains known exclusively to the Divine Creator.
---

## 9. Tadabbur (Guided Contemplation)
* **Parenting with Tenderness: 'Ya Bunayya' (31:13, 16, 17):** Notice how Luqman opens every difficult teaching with the diminutive of affection: *Ya Bunayya* ('O my dear little son'). Before you correct a child or subordinate, build the bridge of warmth. Guidance delivered without love feels like an assault; guidance delivered with tender affection softens the heart.
* **The Secret Sin Inside the Rock (31:16):** Imagine a mustard seed embedded inside an unyielding dark boulder at the bottom of the ocean or lost in the outer cosmos. Allah knows its exact coordinates and will bring it forth. How then can you believe that your private tears, your hidden sacrifices, or your secret sins escape His notice? Live with acute awareness of *Latifun Khabir*.
---

## 10. What This Means For Me
* I mentor my children and loved ones with deep affection (*Ya Bunayya*), prioritizing Tawhid and character before secular accomplishments.
* I honor my parents with proactive kindness, serving my mother with tender appreciation for her sacrifices during pregnancy and infancy.
* I walk through streets and corporate halls with humble posture, never turning my face away in arrogance or speaking with abrasive volume.

---

## 11. One Practical Action
**Express Filial Gratitude Today:**  
Call or visit your parents today. Kiss your mother's forehead or hands, tell her explicitly: 'May Allah reward you for carrying me in weakness upon weakness,' and perform an act of service for your father.

## 12. Reflection Questions
1. Am I living with the consciousness that even a mustard seed of good or evil will be brought forth by Allah (31:16)?
2. How do I balance filial respect with upholding Islamic values when they seem to clash (31:14–15)?
3. Does my posture, body language, and vocal volume convey humility or subconscious snobbery (31:18–19)?

## 13. Closing Supplication
$$\text{اللَّهُمَّ آتِنَا حِكْمَةً تُصْلِحُ بِهَا أُمُورَنَا ، وَارْزُقْنَا بِرَّ وَالِدَيْنَا ، رَبِّ ارْحَمْهُمَا كَمَا رَبَّيَانِي صَغِيرًا ، وَاجْعَلْنَا مِنَ الْمُتَوَاضِعِينَ الْمُخْبِتِينَ}$$  
*"O Allah, grant us wisdom through which You rectify our affairs, and bless us with devotion to our parents: My Lord, have mercy upon them as they brought me up when I was small, and make us among the humble and penitent. Amin."*
