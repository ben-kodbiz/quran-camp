# CHAPTER 79: SURAH AN-NAZI'AT (سُورَةُ النازعات)
**Classification:** Makki  
**Order in Mushaf:** 79  
**Total Verses:** 46  
**Famous Names:** Surat an-Nazi'at, As-Sahirah, At-Tammah  

---

## 1. Why This Surah Matters
Surah An-Nazi'at is the heart-stopping cinematic drama of the soul's departure and cosmic resurrection. It opens with the visceral contrast between two types of angelic soul extraction: *An-Nazi'ati gharqa*—angels ripping out the souls of wicked deniers with violent, drowning force like thorny briars dragged through wet wool; and *An-Nashitati nashta*—angels gently, tenderly extracting the souls of the righteous like pure water slipping from a flask. It recounts the imperial arrogance of Pharaoh claiming: 'I am your supreme lord' (*Ana Rabbukumul-a'la*), and his sudden drowning. It concludes with the Great Overwhelming Calamity (*At-Tammah al-Kubra*), when man will remember everything he strove for, and the life of this world will feel like nothing more than a brief afternoon or its morning (79:46).

---

## 2. Surah Context
Revealed in early Makkah to shatter the illusions of proud tribal aristocrats who, like Pharaoh, believed their worldly wealth and status made them immune to divine accountability.

---

## 3. Central Theme
**The Extraction of Souls, the Overthrow of Tyranny (Pharaoh), the Great Calamity (*At-Tammah al-Kubra*), and the Fleeting Illusion of Worldly Time.**

---

## 4. Key Passages
### The Contrast of Soul Extractions (79:1–2)
$$\text{وَالنَّازِعَاتِ غَرْقًا ۝ وَالنَّاشِطَاتِ نَشْطًا}$$
*"By those [angels] who extract with violence, And those who draw out [the souls of believers] gently."*

### Pharaoh's Fatal Arrogance (79:24–25)
$$\text{فَقَالَ أَنَا رَبُّكُمُ الْأَعْلَىٰ ۝ فَأَخَذَهُ اللَّهُ نَكَالَ الْآخِرَةِ وَالْأُولَىٰ}$$
*"And said, 'I am your most exalted lord.' So Allah seized him in exemplary punishment for the last and the first [transgression]."*

### The Great Overwhelming Calamity (79:34–35)
$$\text{فَإِذَا جَاءَتِ الطَّامَّةُ الْكُبْرَىٰ ۝ يَوْمَ يَتَذَكَّرُ الْإِنسَانُ مَا سَعَىٰ}$$
*"The Day man will remember that for which he strove, But when there comes the greatest Overwhelming Calamity—"*

### The Illusion of Worldly Duration (79:46)
$$\text{كَأَنَّهُمْ يَوْمَ يَرَوْنَهَا لَمْ يَلْبَثُوا إِلَّا عَشِيَّةً أَوْ ضُحَاهَا}$$
*"It will be, on the Day they see it, as though they had not remained [in the world] except for an afternoon or a morning thereof."*

---

## 5. Translation Highlights (The Clear Quran)
* *79:27:* 'Are you a more difficult creation or is the heaven? Allah constructed it.'
* *79:37–39:* 'So as for he who transgressed, And preferred the life of the world, Indeed, Hellfire will be his refuge.'
* *79:40–41:* 'But as for he who feared the position of his Lord and prevented the soul from [unlawful] inclination, Indeed, Paradise will be his refuge.'

---

## 6. What Classical Tafsir Explains
* **The Difference Between Nazi'at and Nashitat (Verses 1–2):** Ibn Abbas (RA) explained that *An-Nazi'at* refers to the death-angels pulling the soul of the disbeliever from the depths of his body with violent agony; while *An-Nashitat* describes the angels of mercy gently unlocking the believer's soul with the perfume of Jannah, slipping out as effortlessly as a water droplet from the mouth of a waterskin.
* **The Golden Formula of Jannah (Verses 40–41):** Imam Ibn Kathir and Al-Qurtubi highlight the profound simplicity of attaining Paradise: It requires two conditions: (1) Reverential fear of standing before your Lord (*Khafa maqama Rabbih*), and (2) Conquering your base desires (*Naha an-nafsa 'anil-hawa*). Whoever masters their impulses enters Jannah.
---

## 7. Important Supporting Hadiths
1. **The Soul of the Believer and Disbeliever at Death:**  
   *Musnad Ahmad (Hadith 18534) - Long Hadith of Al-Bara' ibn 'Azib (Sahih):*  
   "The Prophet ﷺ described death: 'When the believer is dying, angels with faces like the sun descend with shrouds from Paradise; the Angel of Death sits by his head and says: *O pure soul, come out to forgiveness and pleasure from Allah!* It flows out gently like water drops... But for the disbeliever, the angel says: *O foul soul, come out to the wrath of Allah!* It tears through the body like a multi-pronged skewer dragged through wet wool!'"
2. **The Worldly Life as an Afternoon:**  
   *Sahih al-Bukhari (Hadith 6416) from Abdullah ibn Umar (RA):*  
   "The Prophet ﷺ took my shoulder and said: 'Be in this world as though you were a stranger or a traveler on a path.'"
---

## 8. Key Concepts
1. **An-Nazi'at vs. An-Nashitat (Violent vs. Gentle Extraction):** The profound divergence in the physical and spiritual experience of death based on faith.
2. **At-Tammah al-Kubra (The Supreme Overwhelming Calamity):** The cataclysm of the second trumpet blast that overwhelms every other worldly sorrow.
3. **Naha an-Nafsa 'an al-Hawa (Restraining the Lower Ego):** The daily spiritual discipline of vetoing sinful impulses for the sake of divine pleasure.
4. **'Ashiyyatan aw Duhaha (An Afternoon or Its Morning):** The ultimate revelation of life's brevity: eighty years on earth will feel like a brief two-hour afternoon.
---

## 9. Tadabbur (Guided Contemplation)
* **Eighty Years Reduced to an Afternoon (79:46):** When you stand on the plains of Judgment Day and look back at your entire biological existence—your childhood, your high school years, your college graduation, your career, your retirement, your illnesses—how long will it feel? *Ka-annahum yawma yarawnaha lam yalbathu illa 'ashiyyatan aw duhaha.* Just one afternoon. Just a morning walk. All of human history is a blink. Do not sell an eternity of joy for a two-hour afternoon.
* **The Battle Against 'Hawa' (79:40–41):** Notice the guarantee: *Wa nahan-nafsa 'anil-hawa fa-innal-Jannata hiyal-ma'wa.* Jannah does not require moving mountains; it requires saying 'No' to your lower ego. When your eyes want to click on the prohibited image, say No. When your tongue wants to fire off the venomous reply, say No. Every time you restrain your desire out of fear of Allah, you purchase a palace in Paradise.
---

## 10. What This Means For Me
* I make it my daily spiritual discipline to conquer unlawful desires (*Hawa*) in pursuit of Paradise (79:40–41).
* I live with the awareness that my entire worldly life is merely a fleeting afternoon (*'Ashiyyatan aw duhaha*) (79:46).
* I prepare for the moment of death, asking Allah to grant me the gentle extraction of *An-Nashitat* rather than the violent wrench of *An-Nazi'at* (79:1–2).

---

## 11. One Practical Action
**Veto One Forbidden Desire Today:**  
Practice verse 79:40 (*Wa nahan-nafsa 'anil-hawa*): Identify one specific urge today (e.g., eating junk food when fasting, looking at a forbidden image, gossiping, or raising your voice in anger). Actively stop yourself for the sake of Allah, claiming the promise of Paradise.

---

## 12. Reflection Questions
1. How will the angels extract my soul at death—with the gentle draw of *Nashitat* or the violent tear of *Nazi'at* (79:1–2)?
2. Am I actively restraining my ego from unlawful desires (*Naha an-nafsa 'anil-hawa*) (79:40)?
3. When I realize life is only an afternoon long (79:46), why do I obsess over temporary worldly drama?

## 13. Closing Supplication
$$\text{اللَّهُمَّ تَوَفَّنَا مَعَ الْأَبْرَارِ ، وَاجْعَلْ خُرُوجَ أَرْوَاحِنَا كَالنَّاشِطَاتِ نَشْطًا ، اللَّهُمَّ إِنَّا نَخَافُ مَقَامَكَ فَانْهَ نُفُوسَنَا عَنِ الْهَوَىٰ ، وَاجْعَلِ الْجَنَّةَ هِيَ الْمَأْوَىٰ}$$  
*"O Allah, take our souls in death with the righteous, and make the departure of our souls gentle and peaceful. O Allah, indeed we fear the standing before You, so restrain our souls from base desires, and make Paradise our eternal refuge. Amin."*
