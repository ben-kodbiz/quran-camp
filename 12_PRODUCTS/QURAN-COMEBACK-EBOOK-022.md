# CHAPTER 22: SURAH AL-HAJJ (سُورَةُ الحج)
**Classification:** Madani  
**Order in Mushaf:** 22  
**Total Verses:** 78  
**Famous Names:** Surat al-Hajj  

---

## 1. Why This Surah Matters
Surah Al-Hajj is unique across the entire Mushaf: it contains verses revealed in Makkah and Madinah, night and day, in travel and at home, and features two separate verses of prostration (*Sajdah*). It opens with the earth-shattering tremor of the Day of Resurrection—where nursing mothers abandon their babies in sheer shock—and links this ultimate gathering directly to the pilgrimage of Hajj, where millions strip themselves of worldly status, donning two pieces of white cloth in anticipation of their shroud. It teaches that the true essence of sacrifice is neither flesh nor blood, but the internal piety (*Taqwa*) reaching Allah.

---

## 2. Surah Context
Revealed partially at the dawn of the Madinan period when Muslims were permitted for the first time to defend themselves against violent tyranny (22:39). It also integrates deep Makkan theological reminders about embryonic development and cosmic resurrection, establishing Hajj as an eternal Abrahamic institution.

---

## 3. Central Theme
**The Dread of the Final Hour, the Sacred Rites of Abrahamic Monotheism, the Defense of Religious Sanctuaries, and Piety as the Core of Ritual Worship.**

---

## 4. Key Passages
### The Tremor of the Final Hour (22:1–2)
$$\text{يَا أَيُّهَا النَّاسُ اتَّقُوا رَبَّكُمْ ۚ إِنَّ زَلْزَلَةَ السَّاعَةِ شَيْءٌ عَظِيمٌ ۝ يَوْمَ تَرَوْنَهَا تَذْهَلُ كُلُّ مُرْضِعَةٍ عَمَّا أَرْضَعَتْ وَتَضَعُ كُلُّ ذَاتِ حَمْلٍ حَمْلَهَا وَتَرَى النَّاسَ سُكَارَىٰ وَمَا هُم بِسُكَارَىٰ وَلَٰكِنَّ عَذَابَ اللَّهِ شَدِيدٌ}$$
*"O mankind, fear your Lord. Indeed, the earthquake of the [final] Hour is a terrible thing. On the Day you see it every nursing mother will be distracted from that which she was nursing, and every pregnant woman will drop her load, and you will see the people [appearing] intoxicated while they are not intoxicated; but the punishment of Allah is severe."*

### The Proclamation of Hajj (22:27)
$$\text{وَأَذِّن فِي النَّاسِ بِالْحَجِّ يَأْتُوكَ رِجَالًا وَعَلَىٰ كُلِّ ضَامِرٍ يَأْتِينَ مِن كُلِّ فَجٍّ عَمِيقٍ}$$
*"And proclaim to the people the Hajj [pilgrimage]; they will come to you on foot and on every lean camel; they will come from every distant pass."*

### The Essence of Sacrificial Worship (22:37)
$$\text{لَن يَنَالَ اللَّهَ لُحُومُهَا وَلَا دِمَاؤُهَا وَلَٰكِن يَنَالُهُ التَّقْوَىٰ مِنكُمْ}$$
*"Their meat will not reach Allah, nor will their blood, but what reaches Him is piety from you."*

---

## 5. Translation Highlights (The Clear Quran)
* *22:11:* 'And of the people is he who worships Allah on an edge. If he is touched by good, he is reassured by it; but if he is struck by trial, he turns on his face [to the other direction]. He has lost [this] world and the Hereafter.'
* *22:32:* 'That [is so]. And whoever honors the symbols of Allah—indeed, it is from the piety of hearts.'
* *22:78:* 'And strive for Allah with the striving due to Him. He has chosen you and has not placed upon you in the religion any difficulty...'

---

## 6. What Classical Tafsir Explains
* **Worshipping Allah 'On the Edge' (Verse 11):** Ibn Abbas (RA) narrated that this was revealed regarding bedouins who came to Madinah: if their wives gave birth to sons and their horses bred well, they said 'This is a good religion.' But if their crops failed or they faced illness, they abandoned Islam. Al-Qurtubi emphasizes that faith is not a transactional business contract with God; it is unconditional surrender in storm and sunshine alike.
* **The Abrahamic Call Echoing Through Centuries (Verse 27):** Ibn Kathir relates that when Ibrahim (AS) built the Ka'bah, Allah commanded him to call humanity to pilgrimage. Ibrahim asked, 'My Lord, how will my voice reach all mankind?' Allah said, 'You proclaim, and upon Me is the transmission.' That ancient call in a desolate desert valley continues to draw millions of beating hearts from every corner of the earth every year.
---

## 7. Important Supporting Hadiths
1. **The Two Sajdahs of Surah Al-Hajj:**  
   *Sunan Abi Dawud (Hadith 1403), Hasan:*  
   "Amr ibn al-'As (RA) reported that the Messenger of Allah ﷺ taught him fifteen prostrations in the Qur'an, including two in Surah Al-Hajj."
2. **The Reward of an Accepted Hajj:**  
   *Sahih al-Bukhari (Hadith 1773) from Abu Hurayrah (RA):*  
   "The Prophet ﷺ said: 'Whoever performs Hajj for the sake of Allah and does not utter obscenities nor commit wickedness will return [free of sin] as on the day his mother bore him.'"
---

## 8. Key Concepts
1. **Sha'a'irullah (Sacred Symbols of Allah):** The physical sites, times, and rituals (like Safa, Marwa, sacrifice, Ka'bah) designated by Allah for special reverence.
2. **'Ibadat al-Harf (Conditional Faith):** The shallow spiritual attitude that only serves God when life is comfortable and flees when trials arrive.
3. **Taqwat al-Qulub (Piety of the Heart):** The internal reverential awe that gives life and meaning to outward physical acts of worship.
4. **Jihad an-Nafs wal-Haqq (Striving for Truth):** The continuous moral, spiritual, and physical exertion to uphold righteousness without hardship.
---

## 9. Tadabbur (Guided Contemplation)
* **Hajj as the Rehearsal of the Resurrection (22:1–2):** Why are the verses of Resurrection juxtaposed with the verses of Hajj? Because the plains of 'Arafat are the dress rehearsal of Yawm al-Qiyamah. You discard your designer clothes, titles, passports, and wealth. You stand under an open sky beside royalty and laborers alike, covered in two unstitched sheets, chanting *Labbayk* (Here I am at Your service). If you can remember that moment, the illusions of worldly status vanish.
* **Honoring What Allah Honors (22:32):** Piety is measured by how you treat what Allah has declared sacred. Do you venerate the Adhan, the Qur'an, the prayer times, the months of Ramadan and Dhul-Hijjah? A callous, dismissive heart shows spiritual illness; a tender heart that honors sacred things reveals genuine Taqwa.
---

## 10. What This Means For Me
* I refuse to have conditional faith—I will praise and obey Allah whether I am experiencing professional triumph or sudden grief.
* I instill sacred reverence in my home for the symbols of Islam: respecting prayer times, reciting the Qur'an with care, and honoring holy months.
* I keep my focus on the internal spirit of worship, ensuring that my prayers and charity are driven by pure Taqwa rather than mechanical routine.

---

## 11. One Practical Action
**Audit Your Faith for 'Conditional Worship':**  
Sit privately for 10 minutes and ask yourself: 'Does my devotion increase when things go well, and wither when I face financial or emotional setbacks?' Recommit today to praying on time with excellence regardless of worldly stress.

---

## 12. Reflection Questions
1. Am I worshipping Allah 'on the edge' (22:11), expecting worldly rewards in exchange for my prayers?
2. How does the imagery of the Day of Judgment in verses 1–2 calibrate my daily priorities and fears?
3. What reaches Allah from my sacrifices—is it empty show, or true sincerity of heart (22:37)?

## 13. Closing Supplication
$$\text{اللَّهُمَّ ارْزُقْنَا حَجَّ بَيْتِكَ الْحَرَامِ ، وَاجْعَلْنَا مِمَّنْ يُعَظِّمُ شَعَائِرَكَ ، وَلَا تَجْعَلْنَا مِمَّنْ يَعْبُدُكَ عَلَىٰ حَرْفٍ يَا رَبَّ الْعَالَمِينَ}$$  
*"O Allah, bless us with pilgrimage to Your Sacred House, make us among those who venerate Your sacred symbols, and do not make us of those who worship You on an edge. O Lord of the worlds, grant us steadfast faith. Amin."*
