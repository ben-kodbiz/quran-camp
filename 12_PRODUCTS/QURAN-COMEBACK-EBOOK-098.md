# CHAPTER 98: SURAH AL-BAYYINAH (سُورَةُ البينة)
**Classification:** Madani  
**Order in Mushaf:** 98  
**Total Verses:** 8  
**Famous Names:** Surat al-Bayyinah, Lam Yakun  

---

## 1. Why This Surah Matters
Surah Al-Bayyinah establishes the indispensable necessity of divine revelation to rescue humanity from dogmatic confusion and moral decadence. Neither the people of previous scripture nor the polytheists would ever abandon their entrenched corruptions until 'there came to them clear proof: a Messenger from Allah reciting purified scriptures' (98:1–2). The Surah strips away theological pretension and distills the eternal essence of all divine religions into three core commands: pure sincerity to Allah (*Ikhlas*), establishing the prayer, and giving purifying charity. It draws the ultimate dividing line between the worst of creation (*Sharrul Bariyyah*) and the best of creation (*Khayrul Bariyyah*).

---

## 2. Surah Context
Revealed in Madinah to address the attitudes of both Jewish communities and Arab polytheists regarding the advent of Muhammad ﷺ, whose arrival they had anticipated but subsequently rejected out of tribal envy.

---

## 3. Central Theme
**The Clarifying Proof of Prophethood, the Core Pillar of Religious Sincerity (*Ikhlas*), and the Radical Contrast between the Worst and Best of Creation.**

---

## 4. Key Passages
### The Advent of the Clear Proof (98:1–2)
$$\text{لَمْ يَكُنِ الَّذِينَ كَفَرُوا مِنْ أَهْلِ الْكِتَابِ وَالْمُشْرِكِينَ مُنفَكِّينَ حَتَّىٰ تَأْتِيَهُمُ الْبَيِّنَةُ ۝ رَسُولٌ مِّنَ اللَّهِ يَتْلُو صُحُفًا مُّطَهَّرَةً}$$
*"Those who disbelieved among the People of the Scripture and the polytheists were not to be parted [from disbelief] until there came to them clear evidence—A Messenger from Allah, reciting purified scriptures."*

### The Core Creed of All Prophets (98:5)
$$\text{وَمَا أُمِرُوا إِلَّا لِيَعْبُدُوا اللَّهَ مُخْلِصِينَ لَهُ الدِّينَ حُنَفَاءَ وَيُقِيمُوا الصَّلَاةَ وَيُؤْتُوا الزَّكَاةَ ۚ وَذَٰلِكَ دِينُ الْقَيِّمَةِ}$$
*"And they were not commanded except to worship Allah, [being] sincere to Him in religion, inclining to truth, and to establish prayer and to give zakah. And that is the correct religion."*

### The Best of Creation (98:7–8)
$$\text{إِنَّ الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا وَعَمِلُوا الصَّالِحَاتِ أُولَٰئِكَ هُمْ خَيْرُ الْبَرِيَّةِ ۝ جَزَاؤُهُمْ عِندَ رَبِّهِمْ جَنَّاتُ عَدْنٍ تَجْرِي مِن تَحْتِهَا الْأَنْهَارُ خَالِدِينَ فِيهَا أَبَدًا ۚ رَّضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُمْ وَرَضُوا عَنْهُ ۚ ذَٰلِكَ لِمَنْ خَشِيَ رَبَّهُ}$$
*"Indeed, they who have believed and done righteous deeds—those are the best of creatures. Their reward with their Lord will be gardens of perpetual residence beneath which rivers flow... Allah being pleased with them and they with Him. That is for whoever has feared his Lord."*

---

## 5. Translation Highlights (The Clear Quran)
* *98:2:* 'A Messenger from Allah, reciting purified pages (*Suhufan Mutahharah*).'
* *98:5:* 'And that is the upright religion (*Deenul Qayyimah*).'
* *98:6:* 'Indeed, those who disbelieve... are the worst of creatures (*Sharrul Bariyyah*).'
* *98:8:* 'Allah is pleased with them, and they are pleased with Him (*Radiyallahu 'anhum wa radu 'anh*).'

---

## 6. What Classical Tafsir Explains
* **Purified Pages Containing Upright Books (Verses 2–3):** Imam Ibn Kathir notes that *Suhufan Mutahharah* means scriptures elevated above falsehood, doubts, and corruption. Within them are *Kutubun Qayyimah*—just, balanced, and perfect laws that preserve human dignity and societal justice without flaw.
* **The Universality of Sincerity (*Ikhlas*) (Verse 5):** Al-Qurtubi and As-Sa'di explain that every revealed scripture—the Torah, Gospel, and Qur'an—shares the identical foundational core: worship directed exclusively to Allah without partnership, upright monotheism (*Hunafa'*), regular prayer, and social compassion (*Zakah*).
* **The Best of Creation (*Khayrul Bariyyah*) (Verses 7–8):** Many scholars, including Abu Hurayrah (RA), inferred from this verse that righteous believers among humankind can reach a rank superior even to the angels, because humans worship Allah despite combating internal desires and worldly tribulations.
---

## 7. Important Supporting Hadiths
1. **Allah Commanded the Recitation to Ubayy:**  
   *Sahih al-Bukhari (Hadith 4961) & Sahih Muslim (Hadith 799) from Anas ibn Malik (RA):*  
   "The Prophet ﷺ said to Ubayy ibn Ka'b: 'Indeed, Allah has commanded me to recite to you: *Lam yakunil ladheena kafaru...* (Surah Al-Bayyinah).' Ubayy said: 'Did Allah mention me to you by name?' The Prophet ﷺ said: 'Yes, Allah mentioned you by name.' Thereupon, Ubayy began to weep."
2. **The Essential Foundation of Deeds:**  
   *Sahih al-Bukhari (Hadith 1) from 'Umar ibn al-Khattab (RA):*  
   "The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: 'Deeds are only by their intentions, and every person will have only what they intended.'"
---

## 8. Key Concepts
1. **Al-Bayyinah (The Irrefutable Proof):** The clear manifestation of prophetic truth that removes all rational doubt.
2. **Ikhlas (Pure Sincerity):** Dedicating heart, worship, and action solely to the Creator, devoid of ostentation.
3. **Hunafa' (Upright Monotheists):** Turning away from all forms of idolatry and compromise toward undivided devotion.
4. **Khayrul Bariyyah (The Best of Creation):** Those who unite authentic faith (*Iman*) with righteous works (*'Amal Salih*).
5. **Rida' (Mutual Divine Pleasure):** The ultimate summit of paradise where Allah is pleased with the servant, and the servant is overjoyed with his Lord.
---

## 9. Tadabbur (Guided Contemplation)
* **The Weeping of Ubayy:** Reflect on Ubayy ibn Ka'b's tears when he heard the Lord of the Universe mentioned his personal name. How would it feel to know Allah knows your private struggles and sincerity?
* **Stripping Religion of Complications:** Look at verse 5: faith, sincere worship, prayer, charity. Meditate on the liberating simplicity of true Islam versus culture, gossip, and unnecessary burdens.
* **Am I Becoming Khayr or Sharr?:** Assess your actions today: Are you building character through faith and righteousness, or falling into spiritual heedlessness?
---

## 10. What This Means For Me
* My worship is invalid if my motive is social validation; *Ikhlas* is the breath of life in every deed.
* Being from the 'best of creation' is not an inherited ethnic privilege; it is earned through faith, humility, and moral integrity.
* The greatest prize in existence is not just physical Jannah, but hearing Allah say: 'I am pleased with you, and will never be displeased with you.'

## 11. One Practical Action
**The Pure Intention Reset:**  
Pick one routine daily deed you perform for others (e.g., cooking, financial provision, cleaning, volunteering). Intentionally make a silent du'a before doing it: 'O Allah, I do this solely for Your pleasure, seeking no thanks or praise from anyone.'
