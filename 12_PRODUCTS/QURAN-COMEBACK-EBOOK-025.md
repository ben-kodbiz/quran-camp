# CHAPTER 25: SURAH AL-FURQAN (سُورَةُ الفرقان)
**Classification:** Makki  
**Order in Mushaf:** 25  
**Total Verses:** 77  
**Famous Names:** Surat al-Furqan  

---

## 1. Why This Surah Matters
Surah Al-Furqan is the celestial standard of discernment, named after the Qur'an itself as *Al-Furqan*—the ultimate Criterion that separates absolute truth from falsehood, light from darkness, and wisdom from delusion. It refutes every skeptical objection leveled against the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ and the Qur'an, exposing the intellectual bankruptcy of those who deify their own whims. The surah concludes with one of the most breathtaking portraits in Islamic scripture: the twelve sublime characteristics of *'Ibad ar-Rahman* (The Servants of the Most Merciful), illustrating how divine faith transforms human walking, speaking, spending, praying, and family life.

---

## 2. Surah Context
Revealed in Makkah during peak polytheist defiance. The Makkan leaders mocked the Prophet ﷺ: 'What is this messenger that eats food and walks in the markets? Why wasn't an angel sent down to him?' (25:7). Surah Al-Furqan answered their cynical objections, affirming that divine wisdom chooses human messengers to establish relatable empathy and righteous example.

---

## 3. Central Theme
**The Holy Qur'an as the Supreme Moral Criterion (*Al-Furqan*), the Refutation of Skepticism, and the Spiritual Profile of *'Ibad ar-Rahman*.**

---

## 4. Key Passages
### The Blessing of the Criterion (25:1)
$$\text{تَبَارَكَ الَّذِي نَزَّلَ الْفُرْقَانَ عَلَىٰ عَبْدِهِ لِيَكُونَ لِلْعَالَمِينَ نَذِيرًا}$$
*"Blessed is He who sent down the Criterion upon His Servant that he may be to the worlds a warner."*

### Deifying One's Desires (25:43)
$$\text{أَرَأَيْتَ مَنِ اتَّخَذَ إِلَٰهَهُ هَوَاهُ أَفَأَنتَ تَكُونُ عَلَيْهِ وَكِيلًا}$$
*"Have you seen the one who takes as his god his own desire? Then would you be responsible for him?"*

### The Profile of the Servants of the Merciful (25:63–65)
$$\text{وَعِبَادُ الرَّحْمَٰنِ الَّذِينَ يَمْشُونَ عَلَى الْأَرْضِ هَوْنًا وَإِذَا خَاطَبَهُمُ الْجَاهِلُونَ قَالُوا سَلَامًا ۝ وَالَّذِينَ يَبِيتُونَ لِرَبِّهِمْ سُجَّدًا وَقِيَامًا ۝ وَالَّذِينَ يَقُولُونَ رَبَّنَا اصْرِفْ عَنَّا عَذَابَ جَهَنَّمَ ۖ إِنَّ عَذَابَهَا كَانَ غَرَامًا}$$
*"And the servants of the Most Merciful are those who walk upon the earth easily [with dignity and humility], and when the ignorant address them [harshly], they say [words of] peace. And those who spend [part of] the night to their Lord prostrating and standing. And those who say, 'Our Lord, avert from us the punishment of Hell. Indeed, its punishment is ever adhering.'"*

### The Supreme Family Supplication (25:74)
$$\text{وَالَّذِينَ يَقُولُونَ رَبَّنَا هَبْ لَنَا مِنْ أَزْوَاجِنَا وَذُرِّيَّاتِنَا قُرَّةَ أَعْيُنٍ وَاجْعَلْنَا لِلْمُتَّقِينَ إِمَامًا}$$
*"And those who say, 'Our Lord, grant us from among our wives and offspring comfort to our eyes and make us an example for the righteous.'"*

## 5. Translation Highlights (The Clear Quran)
* *25:20:* '...And We have made some of you [people] as trial for others—will you have patience? And ever is your Lord Seeing.'
* *25:67:* 'And [they are] those who, when they spend, do so not excessively or sparingly but are ever, between that, [justly] moderate.'
* *25:70:* 'Except for those who repent, believe and do righteous work. For them Allah will replace their evil deeds with good. And ever is Allah Forgiving and Merciful.'

---

## 6. What Classical Tafsir Explains
* **Walking with 'Hawn' (Verse 63):** Imam Ibn Kathir and Al-Hasan al-Basri explain that *yamshuna 'ala al-ardi hawna* does not mean walking artificially slowly or feigning weakness; it means walking with tranquil dignity, calm modesty, and without an ounce of arrogance or pompous self-importance.
* **Transforming Sins into Good Deeds (Verse 70):** Scholars of Tafsir highlight two meanings for *yubaddilullahu sayyi'atihim hasanat*: First, Allah replaces their sinful habits in this world with righteous inclinations; second, on the Day of Resurrection, through genuine repentance (*Tawbah Nasuh*), their confessed sins recorded in the scrolls are supernaturally flipped into positive good deeds by Allah's boundless grace.
---

## 7. Important Supporting Hadiths
1. **The Comfort of the Eyes in Prayer:**  
   *Sunan an-Nasa'i (Hadith 3940), Hasan:*  
   "The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: 'Made beloved to me from the world are women and perfume, and the coolness of my eyes (*Qurratu 'ayni*) has been placed in prayer.'"
2. **The Moderation in Spending:**  
   *Musnad Ahmad (Hadith 7140), Sahih:*  
   "The Prophet ﷺ said: 'Moderation in expenditure is half of one's livelihood, showing affection to people is half of one's wisdom, and asking good questions is half of one's knowledge.'"
---

## 8. Key Concepts
1. **Al-Furqan (The Criterion):** The divine power of revelation to cut cleanly between moral truth and alluring falsehood.
2. **'Ibad ar-Rahman (Servants of the Merciful):** The peak spiritual archetype of humanity characterized by humility, night prayer, restraint, and kindness.
3. **Ittikhadh al-Hawa Ilahan (Deifying Desires):** Subjugating conscience, truth, and scripture to personal impulses, lusts, and cultural trends.
4. **Qurratu A'yun (Coolness of the Eyes):** A deep Arabic idiom signifying profound joy, spiritual tranquility, and absolute emotional relief.
---

## 9. Tadabbur (Guided Contemplation)
* **Responding to Ignorance with Peace (25:63):** When toxic, rude, or ignorant people attack the servants of the Merciful, notice what they do: They do not counter-insult, write paragraph rants, or stoop into the mud. They say: *Salam.* Their silence and peaceful exit is not weakness; it is supreme emotional mastery. They refuse to let fools dictate their character.
* **Leaders for the Righteous (25:74):** Look at the soaring ambition of the du'a: *Waj'alna lil-muttaqina imama* ('And make us leaders for the righteous'). They do not just ask to scrape into Jannah; they ask Allah to make their home and family a beacon of guidance that inspires an entire generation of believers to love God.
---

## 10. What This Means For Me
* I walk through life, work, and community with humility and calm, never using my speech or demeanor to intimidate or look down on others.
* I guard my spending against both reckless consumerism and stingy hoarding, practicing balanced financial stewardship (25:67).
* I adopt the du'a of verse 74 as a foundational daily prayer for my spouse, children, and spiritual legacy.

---

## 11. One Practical Action
**Incorporate the 'Qurratu A'yun' Du'a into Every Fard Prayer:**  
Memorize and recite *Rabbana hab lana min azwajina wa dhurriyyatina qurrata a'yunin waj'alna lil-muttaqina imama* (25:74) after every obligatory prayer today, focusing on your family's spiritual well-being.

---

## 12. Reflection Questions
1. Am I living as a servant of the Merciful (*'Abd ar-Rahman*) or as a servant of my own shifting impulses and desires (25:43)?
2. How do I respond when someone provokes me online or in person—do I escalate, or do I embody *Qalu Salama*?
3. What concrete steps am I taking to make my home a sanctuary of coolness and peace for my family?

## 13. Closing Supplication
