# CHAPTER 74: SURAH AL-MUDDATHTHIR (سُورَةُ المدثر)
**Classification:** Makki  
**Order in Mushaf:** 74  
**Total Verses:** 56  
**Famous Names:** Surat al-Muddaththir  

---

## 1. Why This Surah Matters
Surah Al-Muddaththir marks the monumental shift from private prophethood to public world-historical action. While Surah Al-'Alaq made Muhammad ﷺ a prophet (*Nabi*), Surah Al-Muddaththir commissioned him as a public messenger (*Rasul*) to all mankind: 'O you who covers himself [with a garment], Arise and warn!' (*Qum fa-andhir*). It delivers the seven timeless operating commands of Islamic activism: Arise and warn; magnify your Lord; purify your garments; abandon all defilement; give not expecting more in return; and for your Lord be patient (74:1–7). It provides the terrifying dissection of Hell's prison: *Saqar*, guarded by nineteen angels, and reveals the conversation of the condemned who explain why they fell into the Fire: 'We were not of those who prayed, nor did we feed the poor' (74:43–44).

---

## 2. Surah Context
Revealed in Makkah following the brief cessation of revelation (*Fatrat al-Wahy*). The Prophet ﷺ saw the Archangel Gabriel seated on a throne between heaven and earth, returned home trembling, and cried to Khadijah: 'Cover me! Cover me!' (*Daththiruni!*). Gabriel descended with the opening of Surah Al-Muddaththir.

---

## 3. Central Theme
**The Command to Arise and Warn (*Qum Fa-Andhir*), the Seven Protocols of Leadership, the Reality of Saqar, and the Four Causes of Damnation.**

---

## 4. Key Passages
### The Call to Universal Action (74:1–7)
$$\text{يَا أَيُّهَا الْمُدَّثِّرُ ۝ قُمْ فَأَنذِرْ ۝ وَرَبَّكَ فَكَبِّرْ ۝ وَثِيَابَكَ فَطَهِّرْ ۝ وَالرُّجْزَ فَاهْجُرْ ۝ وَلَا تَمْنُن تَسْتَكْثِرُ ۝ وَلِرَبِّكَ فَاصْبِرْ}$$
*"O you who covers himself [with a garment], Arise and warn! And your Lord magnify! And your clothing purify! And uncleanliness avoid! And do not confer favor to acquire more! And for your Lord be patient!"*

### The Four Reasons for Entering Hellfire (74:42–47)
$$\text{مَا سَلَكَكُمْ فِي سَقَرَ ۝ قَالُوا لَمْ نَكُ مِنَ الْمُصَلِّينَ ۝ وَلَمْ نَكُ نُطْعِمُ الْمِسْكِينَ ۝ وَكُنَّا نَخُوضُ مَعَ الْخَائِضِينَ ۝ وَكُنَّا نُكَذِّبُ بِيَوْمِ الدِّينِ ۝ حَتَّىٰ أَتَانَا الْيَقِينُ}$$
*"[And asking them], 'What put you into Saqar?' They will say, 'We were not of those who prayed, Nor did we used to feed the poor. And we used to enter into vain discourse with those who engaged [in it], And we used to deny the Day of Recompense, Until there came to us the certainty [death].'"*

### Fleeing Zebra Metaphor (74:49–51)
$$\text{فَمَا لَهُمْ عَنِ التَّذْكِرَةِ مُعْرِضِينَ ۝ كَأَنَّهُمْ حُمُرٌ مُّسْتَنفِرَةٌ ۝ فَرَّتْ مِن قَسْوَرَةٍ}$$
*"Then what is [the matter] with them that they are, from the reminder, turning away? As if they were alarmed donkeys [wild zebras], Fleeing from a lion?"*

* *74:38:* 'Every soul, for what it has earned, will be retained.'
* *74:56:* 'And they will not remember except that Allah wills. He is worthy of being feared and worthy of [granting] forgiveness.'

---

## 6. What Classical Tafsir Explains
* **The Seven Commands of Leadership (Verses 1–7):** Imam Ibn Kathir and classical commentators note that these seven short imperatives formed the total operating system of prophetic leadership: (1) Active Da'wah (*Qum fa-andhir*), (2) Exalting Allah alone above all human idols (*Wa Rabbaka fakabbir*), (3) Outer and inner purity (*Wa thiyabaka fatahhir*), (4) Abandoning all sins and idols (*War-rujza fahjur*), (5) Pure sincerity in charity without expecting worldly returns (*Wa la tamnun tastakthir*), and (6) Patient perseverance for God's sake (*Wa li-Rabbika fasbir*).
* **The Four Causes of Falling into Saqar (Verses 42–47):** Scholars emphasize that the inmates of Saqar identify four fatal sins: two vertical failures with God (neglecting daily prayer and denying resurrection), and two horizontal failures with society (starving the poor and joining mobs in toxic, frivolous discourse).
---

## 7. Important Supporting Hadiths
1. **The Revelation of Al-Muddaththir:**  
   *Sahih al-Bukhari (Hadith 4922) from Jabir ibn Abdillah (RA):*  
   "The Prophet ﷺ spoke of the period when revelation ceased: 'While I was walking, I heard a voice from the sky. I looked up and saw the angel who had visited me at Hira seated upon a chair between heaven and earth. I was terrified, returned home and said: *Zammiluni, daththiruni!* (Wrap me up!). Then Allah revealed: *Ya ayyuhal-muddaththir! Qum fa-andhir...* After this, revelation began to flow intensely and continuously.'"
2. **The Four Characteristics:**  
   *Jami' at-Tirmidhi (Hadith 2415), Sahih:*  
   "Regarding verse 74:43, the Prophet ﷺ said: 'The covenant between us and them is prayer; whoever abandons it has committed disbelief.'"
---

## 8. Key Concepts
1. **Qum Fa-Andhir (Arise and Warn):** The transition from private spiritual retreat to public prophetic mobilization and advocacy.
2. **Saqar (The Scorching Pit):** A specific level of Hellfire that neither spares nor leaves, guarded by nineteen angelic wardens.
3. **Al-Khawd ma'al-Kha'idin (Joining the Mob in Vain Discourse):** The fatal social trap of participating in frivolous online gossip, mockery of religion, and cultural herd-thinking.
4. **La Tamnun Tastakthir (Sincere Service Without Entitlement):** Giving, serving, and mentoring without viewing one's deeds as a favor or demanding downstream benefits.
---

## 9. Tadabbur (Guided Contemplation)
* **The Sleep is Over: Qum! (74:2):** As Khadijah (RA) saw the Prophet ﷺ pacing the room after this revelation, she said: 'Why don't you sleep, O Messenger of Allah?' He looked at her with tears in his eyes and replied: *Mada'a 'ahdun-nawmi ya Khadijah* ('The era of sleep has ended, O Khadijah!'). When you realize that souls around you are heading toward eternal fire, how can you spend your life sleeping, binge-watching, and scrolling? Arise and warn.
* **Joining the Cynical Mob (74:45):** Look at the third reason people enter Saqar: *Wa kunna nakhudu ma'al-kha'idin* ('And we used to join in vain discourse with the crowd'). You didn't necessarily invent the heresy, the slander, or the mockery; you just joined the comments section. You laughed along, retweeted the cynical jokes, and swam with the cultural current. Disengage from toxic crowds before you share their destination.
---

## 10. What This Means For Me
* I cast off complacency and arise to serve my family and community with proactive Islamic advocacy (*Qum fa-andhir*).
* I guard my five daily prayers and feed the poor, steering clear of the four paths to Saqar (74:43–44).
* I refuse to engage in frivolous, cynical discourse (*Al-Khawd*) on social media or in person (74:45).
* I serve and give charity purely for Allah, never expecting praise, favors, or return benefits (74:6).

---

## 11. One Practical Action
**Feed Someone in Need Today:**  
Directly counteract the second failure of Saqar (*Wa lam naku nut'imu al-miskin*): Buy a nutritious meal, package groceries, or send money today specifically to feed a hungry person or family.

---

## 12. Reflection Questions
1. Am I living in the 'era of sleep' or have I answered the call to arise and warn (*Qum fa-andhir*) (74:2)?
2. Do I participate in vain, cynical gossip (*Nakhudu ma'al-kha'idin*) with online mobs or friends (74:45)?
3. Are my charity and service free of entitlement, or do I give expecting worldly praise and favors (74:6)?

## 13. Closing Supplication
$$\text{اللَّهُمَّ يَا رَبَّنَا كَبِّرْنَا بِتَقْوَاكَ ، وَطَهِّرْ ثِيَابَنَا وَقُلُوبَنَا ، وَنَجِّنَا مِنْ سَقَرَ ، وَلَا تَجْعَلْنَا مِمَّنْ يَخُوضُونَ مَعَ الْخَائِضِينَ ، وَاجْعَلْنَا مِنَ الْمُصَلِّينَ الْمُطْعِمِينَ لِلْمِسْكِينِ}$$  
*"O Allah, our Lord, magnify us with Your piety, purify our garments and our hearts, deliver us from Saqar, and do not make us among those who join in vain discourse. Make us among those who pray and feed the poor. Amin."*
