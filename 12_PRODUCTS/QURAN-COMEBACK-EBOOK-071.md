# CHAPTER 71: SURAH NUH (سُورَةُ نوح)
**Classification:** Makki  
**Order in Mushaf:** 71  
**Total Verses:** 28  
**Famous Names:** Surat Nuh  

---

## 1. Why This Surah Matters
Surah Nuh is the master narrative of heroic perseverance in calling people to God. Across twenty-eight verses, it details the monumental 950-year mission of Prophet Nuh (AS), who called his people night and day, publicly and in private whispers, utilizing every pedagogical method only to be met with arrogance, fingers thrust into ears, and garments wrapped around faces. It contains the golden, universal formula for unlocking divine blessings in this world: Sincere Repentance (*Istighfar*). Nuh promises his people: 'Ask forgiveness of your Lord; indeed, He is ever a Perpetual Forgiver. He will send [rain from] the sky upon you in showers, and give you increase in wealth and children and provide for you gardens and provide for you rivers' (71:10–12).

---

## 2. Surah Context
Revealed in Makkah during the darkest days of persecution to comfort the Messenger of Allah ﷺ. It reminded him that his struggle in Makkah was brief compared to Nuh's nearly millennium-long persistence against relentless defiance.

---

## 3. Central Theme
**The Legendary Patience of Da'wah, the Material and Spiritual Fruits of *Istighfar*, Cosmic Proofs of Tawhid, and the Final Prayer for Believers.**

---

## 4. Key Passages
### The Relentless Day-and-Night Calling (71:5–9)
$$\text{قَالَ رَبِّ إِنِّي دَعَوْتُ قَوْمِي لَيْلًا وَنَهَارًا ۝ فَلَمْ يَزِدْهُمْ دُعَائِي إِلَّا فِرَارًا ۝ وَإِنِّي كُلَّمَا دَعَوْتُهُمْ لِتَغْفِرَ لَهُمْ جَعَلُوا أَصَابِعَهُمْ فِي آذَانِهِمْ وَاسْتَغْشَوْا ثِيَابَهُمْ وَأَصَرُّوا وَاسْتَكْبَرُوا اسْتِكْبَارًا ۝ ثُمَّ إِنِّي دَعَوْتُهُمْ جِهَارًا ۝ ثُمَّ إِنِّي أَعْلَنتُ لَهُمْ وَأَسْرَرْتُ لَهُمْ إِسْرَارًا}$$
*"He said, 'My Lord, indeed I invited my people night and day. But my invitation increased them not except in flight. And indeed, every time I invited them that You might forgive them, they put their fingers in their ears, covered themselves with their garments, persisted, and were arrogant with [great] arrogance. Then I invited them publicly. Then I announced to them and [also] confided to them secretly.'"*

### The Formula of Istighfar (71:10–12)
$$\text{فَقُلْتُ اسْتَغْفِرُوا رَبَّكُمْ إِنَّهُ كَانَ غَفَّارًا ۝ يُرْسِلِ السَّمَاءَ عَلَيْكُم مِّدْرَارًا ۝ وَيُمْدِدْكُم بِأَمْوَالٍ وَبَنِينَ وَيَجْعَل لَّكُمْ جَنَّاتٍ وَيَجْعَل لَّكُمْ أَنْهَارًا}$$
*"And said, 'Ask forgiveness of your Lord. Indeed, He is ever a Perpetual Forgiver. He will send [rain from] the sky upon you in showers, And give you increase in wealth and children and provide for you gardens and provide for you rivers.'"*

### Nuh's Universal Closing Du'a (71:28)
$$\text{رَّبِّ اغْفِرْ لِي وَلِوَالِدَيَّ وَلِمَن دَخَلَ بَيْتِيَ مُؤْمِنًا وَلِلْمُؤْمِنِينَ وَالْمُؤْمِنَاتِ وَلَا تَزِدِ الظَّالِمِينَ إِلَّا تَبَارًا}$$
*"My Lord, forgive me and my parents and whoever enters my house a believer and the believing men and believing women. And do not increase the wrongdoers except in destruction."*

## 5. Translation Highlights (The Clear Quran)
* *71:13–14:* 'What is [the matter] with you that you do not attribute to Allah [due] grandeur, While He has created you in stages?'
* *71:15–16:* 'Do you not consider how Allah has created seven heavens in layers, And made the moon therein a reflected light and made the sun a burning lamp?'
* *71:23:* 'And they said, "Never leave your gods and never leave Wadd or Suwa' or Yaghuth and Ya'uq and Nasr."'

---

## 6. What Classical Tafsir Explains
* **The Secret of Al-Hasan al-Basri and Istighfar (Verses 10–12):** A man complained to Al-Hasan al-Basri of drought; he said: 'Seek forgiveness of Allah.' Another complained of poverty; he said: 'Seek forgiveness of Allah.' Another complained of infertility; he said: 'Seek forgiveness of Allah.' When asked why he gave the exact same prescription for different ailments, Al-Hasan recited verses 10–12 of Surah Nuh, proving that *Istighfar* is the divine key that unlocks rainfall, wealth, and children.
* **The Origin of Idolatry (Verse 23):** Ibn Abbas (RA) explained in Sahih al-Bukhari that Wadd, Suwa', Yaghuth, Ya'uq, and Nasr were originally righteous men from the people of Nuh. When they died, Shaytan inspired the people to erect statues of them to remember their worship. Over generations, knowledge was lost, and the statues were worshipped as deities. Surah Nuh warns against excessive veneration turning into idolatry.
---

## 7. Important Supporting Hadiths
1. **Istighfar as a Relief from Every Distress:**  
   *Sunan Abi Dawud (Hadith 1518), Hasan:*  
   "The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: 'Whoever adheres consistently to seeking forgiveness (*Istighfar*), Allah will make for him from every distress a way out, and from every anxiety relief, and will provide for him from where he does not expect.'"
2. **The History of the First Idols:**  
   *Sahih al-Bukhari (Hadith 4920) from Ibn Abbas (RA):*  
   "Ibn Abbas detailed the origin of the idols mentioned in Surah Nuh (verse 23), showing how the corruption of righteous memory gave birth to polytheism across humanity."
---

## 8. Key Concepts
1. **Barakat al-Istighfar (The Bounties of Repentance):** The spiritual and physical prosperity—rainfall, children, wealth, agriculture—unlocked through constant repentance.
2. **Ad-Da'wah bi-Kulli Wasilah (Multi-Channel Mentorship):** Nuh's comprehensive communication strategy: night and day, public lectures, and private one-on-one counseling.
3. **Waqarullah (Reverence for God's Grandeur):** Possessing acute awe and solemn respect for the majesty and sovereignty of the Creator.
4. **Asl ash-Shirk (The Genesis of Idolatry):** The historical trap where veneration of righteous human heroes degenerates into shirk.
---

## 9. Tadabbur (Guided Contemplation)
* **The Exhaustive Effort of Nuh (71:5–9):** Look at Nuh's persistence: *Laylan wa nahara* (night and day), *Jihara* (openly in town squares), *I'lan* (mass announcements), and *Israr* (quiet, private whispers over coffee). For 950 years, he never threw his hands up in frustration saying, 'These people are hopeless.' When you mentor your children or invite people to goodness, don't give up after two weeks. Diversify your methods with loving patience.
* **Need Rain, Wealth, or Children? (71:10–12):** If you are facing financial stagnation, infertility, or career deadlocks, look at Nuh's prescription: *Fastaghfiru Rabbakum innahu kana Ghaffara.* When you cleanse your sins through *Astaghfirullah*, the clouds of divine mercy open up. Repentance is not just a spiritual cleanser for the soul; it is a rainmaker for your worldly sustenance.
---

## 10. What This Means For Me
* I make *Istighfar* (seeking forgiveness) a constant habit throughout the day to unlock both spiritual and worldly blessings (71:10–12).
* I persist with loving patience when advising family or mentoring others, learning from Nuh's multi-channel dedication (71:5–9).
* I recite Nuh's beautiful closing prayer for myself, my parents, and the global community of believers (71:28).

Dedicate a 10-minute quiet block today to recite *Astaghfirullah wa atubu ilayh* 100 times, consciously meditating on Nuh's promise: forgiveness, rainfall, wealth, and children (71:10–12).

## 13. Closing Supplication
$$\text{رَّبِّ اغْفِرْ لِي وَلِوَالِدَيَّ وَلِمَن دَخَلَ بَيْتِيَ مُؤْمِنًا وَلِلْمُؤْمِنِينَ وَالْمُؤْمِنَاتِ ، اللَّهُمَّ إِنَّا نَسْتَغْفِرُكَ إِنَّكَ كُنْتَ غَفَّارًا ، فَأَرْسِلِ السَّمَاءَ عَلَيْنَا مِدْرَارًا ، وَامْدُدْنَا بِأَمْوَالٍ وَبَنِينَ}$$  
*"My Lord, forgive me and my parents and whoever enters my house a believer and the believing men and believing women. O Allah, we ask You for forgiveness; indeed You are ever a Perpetual Forgiver, so send the sky upon us in showers and grant us increase in wealth and children. Amin."*
