# CHAPTER 101: SURAH AL-QARI'AH (سُورَةُ القارعة)
**Classification:** Makki  
**Order in Mushaf:** 101  
**Total Verses:** 11  
**Famous Names:** Surat al-Qari'ah  

---

## 1. Why This Surah Matters
Surah Al-Qari'ah delivers the deafening shockwave of the Last Day. Named 'The Striking Calamity' (*Al-Qari'ah*) because it strikes human ears and terrorizes hearts with overwhelming thunder, the Surah reduces all human self-importance to scattered moths (*Farash mabthuth*) rushing frantically into blinding flames, and pulverizes colossal mountains into weightless carded wool (*'Ihn manfush*). In the midst of this cataclysm, only one metric determines eternal fate: the Divine Scales of Justice (*Al-Mawazin*). Whoever's scale of good deeds is heavy enters a life of ecstatic satisfaction; whoever's scale is light plunges into *Hawiyah*—a bottomless abyss of blazing fire.

---

## 2. Surah Context
Revealed in early Makkah to shock a materialistic society obsessed with status, genealogies, and physical fortresses into realizing that all cosmic structures will disintegrate.

---

## 3. Central Theme
**The Deafening Strike of the Last Day, the Dissolution of Mountains and Men, and the Final Verdict of the Divine Scale.**

---

## 4. Key Passages
### The Striking Calamity (101:1–5)
$$\text{الْقَارِعَةُ ۝ مَا الْقَارِعَةُ ۝ وَمَا أَدْرَاكَ مَا الْقَارِعَةُ ۝ يَوْمَ يَكُونُ النَّاسُ كَالْفَرَاشِ الْمَبْثُوثِ ۝ وَتَكُونُ الْجِبَالُ كَالْعِهْنِ الْمَنفُوشِ}$$
*"The Striking Calamity—What is the Striking Calamity? And what can make you know what is the Striking Calamity? It is the Day when people will be like moths, dispersed, And the mountains will be like wool, fluffed up."*

### The Heavy Scales and the Pleasing Life (101:6–7)
$$\text{فَأَمَّا مَن ثَقُلَتْ مَوَازِينُهُ ۝ فَهُوَ فِي عِيشَةٍ رَّاضِيَةٍ}$$
*"Then as for one whose scales are heavy [with good deeds], He will be in a pleasant life."*

### The Light Scales and the Blazing Pit (101:8–11)
$$\text{وَأَمَّا مَنْ خَفَّتْ مَوَازِينُهُ ۝ فَأُمُّهُ هَاوِيَةٌ ۝ وَمَا أَدْرَاكَ مَا هِيَهْ ۝ نَارٌ حَامِيَةٌ}$$
*"But as for one whose scales are light, His refuge will be an abyss. And what can make you know what that is? It is a Fire, intensely hot."*

---

## 5. Translation Highlights (The Clear Quran)
* *101:1:* 'The Calamity! (*Al-Qari'ah*)'
* *101:4:* 'The Day when people will be like scattered moths.'
* *101:7:* 'He will be in a life of sheer delight (*'Eeshatin radiyah*).'
* *101:9:* 'His mother will be an abyss (*Fa-ummuhu hawiyah*).'

---

## 6. What Classical Tafsir Explains
* **Why People are Like Scattered Moths (Verse 4):** Imam Ibn Kathir and Al-Qurtubi explain that unlike locusts, which move with unified coordination, moths fly erratically in blinding confusion, smashing heedlessly into flames. Such will be humanity's utter disorientation when resurrected from graves.
* **His Mother is the Abyss (*Fa-ummuhu Hawiyah*) (Verse 9):** Classical exegetes highlight a tragic linguistic metaphor: Just as a mother embraces her crying child and shelters it in her lap, the roaring pit of Hell (*Hawiyah*) will embrace the sinner, and they will never escape its agonizing custody.
* **A Life That is Pleased (*'Eeshatin Radiyah*) (Verse 7):** Grammarians note that instead of saying 'a life that pleases him,' Allah says *Radiyah*—the life itself is pleased, meaning every aspect of Paradise radiates effortless contentment, joy, and peace without a trace of fatigue.
---

## 7. Important Supporting Hadiths
1. **Words that Are Heavy on the Scale:**  
   *Sahih al-Bukhari (Hadith 6682) & Sahih Muslim (Hadith 2694) from Abu Hurayrah (RA):*  
   "The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: 'Two phrases are light on the tongue, heavy on the Scale, and beloved to the Most Merciful: *Subhanallahi wa bihamdihi, Subhanallahil 'Azeem* (Glory be to Allah and His praise, Glory be to Allah the Almighty).'"
2. **Good Character Weighs Heaviest:**  
   *Sunan Abi Dawud (Hadith 4799) & Jami' at-Tirmidhi (Hadith 2003), Sahih:*  
   "The Prophet ﷺ said: 'Nothing is placed on the Scale heavier than good character (*Husn al-Khuluq*).'"
---

## 8. Key Concepts
1. **Al-Qari'ah (The Striking Doom):** The shock of the Hour that pulverizes illusions of permanence.
2. **Farash Mabthuth (Scattered Moths):** The terrifying loss of control and frantic helplessness of resurrected humanity.
3. **Mawazin (The Divine Scales):** The objective measurement of spiritual deeds based on sincerity and Sunnah.
4. **'Eeshah Radiyah (The Contented Life):** The eternal state of unblemished joy in Paradise.
5. **Hawiyah (The Blazing Abyss):** The mother-like inescapable embrace of eternal hellfire for unrepented defiance.
---

## 9. Tadabbur (Guided Contemplation)
* **Moths to the Flame:** Observe how insects dash blindly toward bright porch lights that incinerate them. Reflect: How often do you chase glittering worldly attractions that burn your soul?
* **Weighing Your Day:** At the end of tonight, visualize placing your 24 hours onto the scales: How much of it had spiritual substance, and how much was hollow fluff like carded wool?
* **The Weight of Good Character:** Remember that a gentle smile, suppressing anger, and forgiving an apology weigh heavier on the scales than thousands of empty verbal claims.
---

## 10. What This Means For Me
* My social status, job title, and physical fitness will be completely weightless on the Day of Calamity.
* I must make my scale heavy today with sincere remembrance, good manners, and quiet charity.
* I must fear *Hawiyah*—the pit of fire—and constantly beg Allah for the shelter of His mercy.

---

## 11. One Practical Action
**Heavying the Scales Daily:**  
Adopt the daily habit of reciting *Subhanallahi wa bihamdihi, Subhanallahil 'Azeem* 100 times every morning and evening. With every repetition, visualize placing an immense golden boulder of reward onto your scale of deeds.

---

## 12. Reflection Questions
1. What activities consume most of your daily hours that will carry zero weight on the *Mawazin*?
2. How can practicing good character at home with your family become your easiest path to a heavy scale?
3. What feelings arise in your heart when you contemplate the image of Hell embracing its inhabitants like a mother?

---

## 13. Closing Supplication
$$\text{اللَّهُمَّ ثَقِّلْ مَوَازِينَنَا بِالصَّالِحَاتِ وَأَجِرْنَا مِنْ نَارِ هَاوِيَةٍ وَارْزُقْنَا عِيشَةً رَاضِيَةً}$$  
*"O Allah, make heavy our scales with righteous deeds, protect us from the fire of the abyss, and grant us an eternally pleasing life in Paradise."*
