# CHAPTER 53: SURAH AN-NAJM (سُورَةُ النجم)
**Classification:** Makki  
**Order in Mushaf:** 53  
**Total Verses:** 62  
**Famous Names:** Surat an-Najm  

---

## 1. Why This Surah Matters
Surah An-Najm is the breathtaking celestial journey into the highest reaches of the cosmos. Named after the descending Star (*Wan-Najmi idha hawa*), it confirms the infallibility of the Prophet's ﷺ speech: 'Nor does he speak from [his own] desire; it is not but a revelation revealed' (53:3–4). It documents the miraculous ascension of Al-Mi'raj, taking the reader to the cosmic frontier of all creation: *Sidrat al-Muntaha* (The Lote Tree of the Utmost Boundary), beyond which no created being may pass, where the Prophet's gaze did not waver or swerve (53:17). When the Prophet ﷺ first recited this surah in the courtyard of the Ka'bah, its concluding verse of prostration (*Fasjudu lillahi wa'budu*) struck the audience with such overwhelming majesty that the believers and every single polytheist fell to the earth in involuntary prostration.

---

## 2. Surah Context
Revealed in Makkah around the 5th year of prophethood. It was the first surah the Messenger of Allah ﷺ openly proclaimed in the public sanctuary of the Ka'bah before the assembled chieftains of Quraysh.

---

## 3. Central Theme
**The Infallibility of Prophetic Revelation, the Celestial Vision of Al-Mi'raj, the Ultimate Boundary (*Sidrat al-Muntaha*), and the Solemn Prostration of Surrender.**

---

## 4. Key Passages
### The Infallibility of Prophetic Speech (53:3–4)
$$\text{وَمَا يَنطِقُ عَنِ الْهَوَىٰ ۝ إِنْ هُوَ إِلَّا وَحْيٌ يُوحَىٰ}$$
*"Nor does he speak from [his own] desire. It is not but a revelation revealed."*

### The Vision at the Utmost Boundary (53:13–18)
$$\text{وَلَقَدْ رَآهُ نَزْلَةً أُخْرَىٰ ۝ عِندَ سِدْرَةِ الْمُنتَهَىٰ ۝ عِندَهَا جَنَّةُ الْمَأْوَىٰ ۝ إِذْ يَغْشَى السِّدْرَةَ مَا يَغْشَىٰ ۝ مَا زَاغَ الْبَصَرُ وَمَا طَغَىٰ ۝ لَقَدْ رَأَىٰ مِنْ آيَاتِ رَبِّهِ الْكُبرَىٰ}$$
*"And he certainly saw him in another descent, At the Lote Tree of the Utmost Boundary—Near it is the Garden of Refuge—When there covered the Lote Tree that which covered [it]. The sight [of the Prophet] did not swerve, nor did it transgress [its limit]. He certainly saw of the greatest signs of his Lord."*

### The Finality of the Lord (53:42–44)
$$\text{وَأَنَّ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ الْمُنتَهَىٰ ۝ وَأَنَّهُ هُوَ أَضْحَكَ وَأَبْكَىٰ ۝ وَأَنَّهُ هُوَ أَمَاتَ وَأَحْيَا}$$
*"And that to your Lord is the finality. And that it is He who makes [one] laugh and weep. And that it is He who causes death and gives life."*

### The Concluding Command of Sujud (53:62)
$$\text{فَاسْجُدُوا لِلَّهِ وَاعْبُدُوا}$$
*"So fall down in prostration to Allah and worship [Him]."*

---

## 5. Translation Highlights (The Clear Quran)
* *53:31:* '...That He may recompense those who do evil with what they have done and recompense those who do good with the best [reward].'
* *53:32:* 'Those who avoid the major sins and immoralities, only [incurring] slight errors. Indeed, your Lord is vast in forgiveness... So do not claim yourselves to be pure; He is most knowing of who fears Him.'
* *53:38–39:* 'That no bearer of burdens will bear the burden of another, And that there is not for man except that [good] for which he strives.'

---

## 6. What Classical Tafsir Explains
* **The Lote Tree of the Utmost Boundary (Verse 14):** Abdullah ibn Mas'ud (RA) in Sahih Muslim explains: 'When the Messenger of Allah ﷺ reached Sidrat al-Muntaha in the sixth or seventh heaven, it was the boundary where everything that ascends from earth stops, and where everything that descends from above stops.' Beyond it lies the unapproachable majesty of the Divine.
* **To Your Lord is the Finality (Verse 42):** Imam al-Qurtubi noted: 'To your Lord ends all wisdom, all destiny, all creation, and all judgment.' Everything that exists traces its origin back to Allah, and every path ultimately terminates before Him.
---

## 7. Important Supporting Hadiths
1. **The Prostration of Surah An-Najm:**  
   *Sahih al-Bukhari (Hadith 1070) from Abdullah ibn Mas'ud (RA):*  
   "Ibn Mas'ud narrated: 'The Prophet ﷺ recited Surah An-Najm in Makkah and prostrated at its end, and everyone who was with him prostrated, except for an old man who picked up a handful of earth, raised it to his forehead, and said: "This suffices me." I later saw him killed as a disbeliever (Umayyah ibn Khalaf).'"
2. **Vast Forgiveness for Minor Slips:**  
   *Sahih al-Bukhari (Hadith 4860) from Ibn Abbas (RA):*  
   "Regarding verse 53:32 (*Alladhina yajtanibuna kaba'ir al-ithmi... illal-lamam*), Ibn Abbas said: 'I have not seen anything resembling *Al-Lamam* (slight errors) more than the gaze or thoughts that flash across the mind, which Allah forgives when major sins are avoided.'"
---

## 8. Key Concepts
1. **Sidrat al-Muntaha (The Utmost Lote Tree):** The celestial frontier marking the end of the created cosmos and the threshold of divine presence.
2. **Ma Yantiqu 'an al-Hawa (Prophetic Infallibility):** The divine assurance that the Prophet's religious teachings are protected from human bias and error.
3. **Ilal-Muntaha (The Ultimate Finality):** The cosmic principle that all journeys, philosophical questions, and souls terminate at God.
4. **Al-Lamam (Minor Involuntary Flashes):** Minor slips and stray thoughts instantly forgiven through prayer and avoidance of major sins.
---

## 9. Tadabbur (Guided Contemplation)
* **He Causes Laughter and Weeping (53:43):** Contemplate this profound reality: *Wa annahu Huwa adhaka wa abka* ('And it is He who makes you laugh and makes you weep'). The exact neurochemistry of your joy and your grief is governed by Allah. When your heart is crushed by grief, remember: The One who made you weep is the only One who holds the power to make you smile again. Run to Him with your tears.
* **Do Not Praise Yourself (53:32):** Allah gives an essential antidote to spiritual vanity: *Fala tuzakku anfusakum; Huwa a'lamu bi-manittaqa* ('So do not claim yourselves to be pure; He knows best who has Taqwa'). Never look down on someone struggling with sin while congratulating yourself on your prayers. Sincerity is quiet; self-righteousness is poison.
---

## 10. What This Means For Me
* I submit my personal opinions and modern cultural trends to the infallible Sunnah of the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ (53:3–4).
* I eradicate self-righteousness (*Fala tuzakku anfusakum*), striving for humility before Allah (53:32).
* Whenever I face emotional turbulence, I turn to the One who controls both laughter and tears (53:43).
* I prostrate in reverence whenever I recite or hear the final verse of Surah An-Najm.

## 11. One Practical Action
**Perform Sajdat at-Tilawah for Surah An-Najm:**  
Recite verse 53:62 (*Fasjudu lillahi wa'budu*), say *Allahu Akbar*, fall into a prolonged prostration of recitation, and whisper *Sajada wajhiya lilladhi khalaqahu wa sawwarahu*.

## 13. Closing Supplication
$$\text{اللَّهُمَّ إِنَّا نَسْجُدُ لِعَظَمَتِكَ وَنَعْبُدُكَ لَا نُشْرِكُ بِكَ شَيْئًا ، اللَّهُمَّ يَا مَنْ أَضْحَكَ وَأَبْكَىٰ اجْعَلْ حُزْنَنَا فَرَحًا وَخَوْفَنَا أَمْنًا ، وَإِلَيْكَ الْمُنْتَهَىٰ يَا رَبَّ الْعَالَمِينَ}$$  
*"O Allah, we prostrate to Your majesty and worship You, associating nothing with You. O Allah, O One who causes laughter and weeping, turn our sorrow into joy and our fear into security. And to You is the finality, O Lord of the worlds. Amin."*
