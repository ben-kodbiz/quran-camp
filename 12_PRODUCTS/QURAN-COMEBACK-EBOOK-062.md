# CHAPTER 62: SURAH AL-JUMU'AH (سُورَةُ الجمعة)
**Classification:** Madani  
**Order in Mushaf:** 62  
**Total Verses:** 11  
**Famous Names:** Surat al-Jumu'ah  

---

## 1. Why This Surah Matters
Surah Al-Jumu'ah is the weekly spiritual heartbeat of the Muslim Ummah. Named after the sacred day of Friday (*Yawm al-Jumu'ah*), it legislates the mandatory Friday congregational prayer and sermon (*Khutbah*). It provides the devastating intellectual warning in verse 5: comparing people who are entrusted with divine scripture but fail to embody or understand its wisdom to a pack donkey carrying heavy volumes of sacred books on its back (*Kamathalil-himari yahmilu asfara*). It concludes with the strict economic command: when the Adhan of Jumu'ah is called, drop all commercial transactions and rush to the remembrance of Allah, returning to the markets only after the prayer is completed to seek Allah's bounty.

---

## 2. Surah Context
Revealed in Madinah around 7 AH. While the Prophet ﷺ was delivering the Friday Khutbah from the pulpit, a lavish commercial trade caravan led by Dihyah al-Kalbi arrived from Syria with drums beating and goods displayed. Most attendees sprinted out of the mosque to the marketplace, leaving only twelve companions standing with the Prophet ﷺ. Surah Al-Jumu'ah descended to establish the sacred sanctity of the Friday prayer.

---

## 3. Central Theme
**The Fourfold Prophetic Mission (62:2), the Parable of the Donkey Carrying Books, the Inevitability of Death, and the Sacred Priority of Friday Prayer.**

---

## 4. Key Passages
### The Fourfold Prophetic Mission (62:2)
$$\text{هُوَ الَّذِي بَعَثَ فِي الْأُمِّيِّينَ رَسُولًا مِّنْهُمْ يَتْلُو عَلَيْهِمْ آيَاتِهِ وَيُزَكِّيهِمْ وَيُعَلِّمُهُمُ الْكِتَابَ وَالْحِكْمَةَ وَإِن كَانُوا مِن قَبْلُ لَفِي ضَلَالٍ مُّبِينٍ}$$
*"It is He who has sent among the unlettered a Messenger from themselves reciting to them His verses and purifying them and teaching them the Book and wisdom—although they were before in manifest error."*

### The Donkey Carrying Sacred Books (62:5)
$$\text{مَثَلُ الَّذِينَ حُمِّلُوا التَّوْرَاةَ ثُمَّ لَمْ يَحْمِلُوهَا كَمَثَلِ الْحِمَارِ يَحْمِلُ أَسْفَارًا ۚ بِئْسَ مَثَلُ الْقَوْمِ الَّذِينَ كَذَّبُوا بِآيَاتِ اللَّهِ}$$
*"The example of those who were entrusted with the Torah and then did not uphold it is like that of a donkey who carries volumes [of books]. Wretched is the example of the people who deny the signs of Allah."*

### The Mandatory Call to Friday Prayer (62:9–10)
$$\text{يَا أَيُّهَا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا إِذَا نُودِيَ لِلصَّلَاةِ مِن يَوْمِ الْجُمُعَةِ فَاسْعَوْا إِلَىٰ ذِكْرِ اللَّهِ وَذَرُوا الْبَيْعَ ۚ ذَٰلِكُمْ خَيْرٌ لَّكُمْ إِن كُنتُمْ تَعْلَمُونَ ۝ فَإِذَا قُضِيَتِ الصَّلَاةُ فَانتَشِرُوا فِي الْأَرْضِ وَابْتَغُوا مِن فَضْلِ اللَّهِ وَاذْكُرُوا اللَّهَ كَثِيرًا لَّعَلَّكُمْ تُفْلِحُونَ}$$
*"O you who have believed, when [the adhan] is called for the prayer on the day of Jumu'ah [Friday], then hasten to the remembrance of Allah and leave all trade. That is better for you, if you should know. And when the prayer has been concluded, disperse within the land and seek from the bounty of Allah, and remember Allah often that you may succeed."*

---

## 5. Translation Highlights (The Clear Quran)
* *62:8:* 'Say, "Indeed, the death from which you flee—indeed, it will meet you. Then you will be returned to the Knower of the unseen and the witnessed, and He will inform you about what you used to do."'
* *62:11:* 'And when they saw a transaction or a diversion, [O Muhammad], they rushed to it and left you standing. Say, "What is with Allah is better than diversion and than a transaction, and Allah is the best of providers."'

---

## 6. What Classical Tafsir Explains
* **The Four Pillars of Education (Verse 2):** Imam Ibn Kathir notes that the Prophet ﷺ transformed an illiterate desert population into masters of global civilization through four structured educational pillars: (1) Reciting revelation (*Yatlu*), (2) Moral and spiritual purification of character (*Yuzakkihim*), (3) Legal comprehension of scripture (*Yu'allimuhum al-Kitab*), and (4) Practical wisdom (*Al-Hikmah*—the Sunnah).
* **Leaving All Business for Friday Prayer (Verse 9):** Imam al-Qurtubi and Islamic jurists derive that from the second the second Adhan of Jumu'ah is called, all commercial transactions, retail sales, contracts, and business negotiations become strictly prohibited (*Haram*) for Muslim men obligated to attend prayer until the prayer concludes.
---

## 7. Important Supporting Hadiths
1. **The Friday Caravan Incident:**  
   *Sahih al-Bukhari (Hadith 4899) from Jabir ibn Abdillah (RA):*  
   "Jabir reported: 'While we were praying Friday prayer with the Prophet ﷺ, a caravan arrived carrying food. The people dispersed toward it until only twelve men remained with the Prophet ﷺ, among whom were Abu Bakr and Umar. Then Allah revealed: *"And when they saw a transaction or a diversion, they rushed to it and left you standing"* (62:11).'"
2. **The Friday Sunnah Recitation:**  
   *Sahih Muslim (Hadith 877) from Ibn Abbas (RA):*  
   "The Messenger of Allah ﷺ used to recite in the Friday prayer: Surah Al-Jumu'ah (62) and Surah Al-Munafiqun (63)."
---

## 8. Key Concepts
1. **Tazkiyah wa Ta'lim (Purification and Education):** The dual engine of Islamic pedagogy: moral purification of the heart paired with rigorous intellect.
2. **Himarun Yahmilu Asfara (The Donkey Carrying Books):** The tragic state of possessing academic religious knowledge without moral comprehension or action.
3. **Fas'aw ila Dhikrillah (Hastening to the Remembrance):** The non-negotiable obligation to prioritize Friday congregational worship over commercial profit.
4. **Wabtaghu min Fadlillah (Seeking Divine Bounty):** The balanced Islamic work ethic combining deep spiritual devotion with proactive economic ambition.
---

## 9. Tadabbur (Guided Contemplation)
* **The Death You Flee From is Coming (62:8):** Notice the terrifying linguistic precision: *Qul innal-mawtalladhi tafirruna minhu fa-innahu mulaqikum.* Allah does not say death is chasing you from behind; He says death will *meet you face-to-face*! You are running toward the very appointment you are trying to escape. Every step you take, every year that passes, brings you closer to that collision. Prepare for the meeting.
* **Don't Be a Library on a Donkey's Back (62:5):** A donkey loaded with fifty rare encyclopedias gains nothing from them except a sore back. Similarly, a person who downloads thousands of Islamic lectures, buys libraries of books, and debates on social media, but does not pray Fajr on time, lies on tax forms, or treats their spouse with cruelty is embodying this exact verse. Transform your knowledge into living Taqwa.

## 10. What This Means For Me
* I organize my weekly schedule to prioritize the Friday Jumu'ah prayer, arriving early to listen attentively to the Khutbah (62:9).
* I apply everything I learn from Islamic knowledge immediately into my personal character, refusing to be like the donkey carrying books (62:5).
* I engage in honest economic enterprise with excellence, remembering that true provision belongs to Allah (62:11).

## 11. One Practical Action
**Arrive 20 Minutes Early for Jumu'ah This Week:**  
Honor the command *Fas'aw ila dhikrillah* (62:9): Make a firm intention to arrive at the mosque at least 20 minutes before the Khutbah begins this Friday, pray Tahiyyat al-Masjid, recite Surah Al-Kahf, and listen in complete silence.

## 12. Reflection Questions
1. Am I translating the Islamic knowledge I read into daily character, or am I acting like the donkey carrying books (62:5)?
2. Do I treat Friday prayer as an annoying disruption to my workday or as the sacred highlight of my week (62:9)?
3. How am I preparing for the inevitable moment when death meets me face-to-face (62:8)?

## 13. Closing Supplication
$$\text{اللَّهُمَّ عَلِّمْنَا مَا يَنْفَعُنَا وَانْفَعْنَا بِمَا عَلَّمْتَنَا وَزِدْنَا عِلْمًا ، اللَّهُمَّ زَكِّ نُفُوسَنَا أَنْتَ خَيْرُ مَنْ زَكَّاهَا ، وَاجْعَلْنَا مِمَّنْ يُسَارِعُونَ إِلَىٰ ذِكْرِكَ فِي يَوْمِ الْجُمُعَةِ}$$  
*"O Allah, teach us what benefits us, benefit us with what You have taught us, and increase us in knowledge. O Allah, purify our souls; You are the best to purify them. And make us among those who hasten to Your remembrance on the Day of Jumu'ah. Amin."*
