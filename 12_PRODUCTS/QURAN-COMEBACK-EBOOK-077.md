# CHAPTER 77: SURAH AL-MURSALAT (سُورَةُ المرسلات)
**Classification:** Makki  
**Order in Mushaf:** 77  
**Total Verses:** 50  
**Famous Names:** Surat al-Mursalat, Wal-'Urf  

---

## 1. Why This Surah Matters
Surah Al-Mursalat is the stern, atmospheric symphony of divine oaths, meteorological signs, and apocalyptic reckoning. Named after *Al-Mursalat*—the emissary winds and angels dispatched in succession (*'Urfa*)—it builds a breathtaking crescendo of cosmic oaths by the storm winds, the cloud-scattering gusts, and the angels delivering revelation. It is structurally anchored by one of the most haunting refrains in the Qur'an, repeated like a solemn hammer ten distinct times: 'Woe, that Day, to the deniers' (*Waylun yawma'idhin lil-mukadhdhibin*). It concludes with the poignant, ultimate question to human intellect: 'Then in what statement after this will they believe?' (77:50).

---

## 2. Surah Context
Revealed in a cave in Mina during the Prophet's ﷺ pilgrimage, shortly before the Hijrah. Abdullah ibn Mas'ud narrated: 'We were with the Prophet ﷺ in a cave when Surah Al-Mursalat was revealed to him, and he recited it while its revelation was still fresh from his mouth.'

---

## 3. Central Theme
**Cosmic Meteorological Oaths, the Terrifying Fate of Deniers, Earth as a Sanctuary for the Living and the Dead, and the Ultimate Authority of Revelation.**

---

## 4. Key Passages
### The Oaths of Cosmic Winds and Emissaries (77:1–7)
$$\text{وَالْمُرْسَلَاتِ عُرْفًا ۝ فَالْعَاصِفَاتِ عَصْفًا ۝ وَالنَّاشِرَاتِ نَشْرًا ۝ فَالْفَارِقَاتِ فَرْقًا ۝ فَالْمُلْقِيَاتِ ذِكْرًا ۝ عُذْرًا أَوْ نُذْرًا ۝ إِنَّمَا تُوعَدُونَ لَوَاقِعٌ}$$
*"By the [winds] sent forth in succession, And the storm winds that blow violently, And the [winds] that scatter [rain] widely, And those that separate [truth from falsehood], And those that deliver revelation, As justification or warning: Indeed, what you are promised is to occur."*

### The Earth as a Container for the Living and Dead (77:25–26)
$$\text{أَلَمْ نَجْعَلِ الْأَرْضَ كِفَاتًا ۝ أَحْيَاءً وَأَمْوَاتًا}$$
*"Have We not made the earth a container [housing] The living and the dead?"*

### The Piercing Final Question (77:49–50)
$$\text{وَيْلٌ يَوْمَئِذٍ لِّلْمُكَذِّبِينَ ۝ فَبِأَيِّ حَدِيثٍ بَعْدَهُ يُؤْمِنُونَ}$$
*"Woe, that Day, to the deniers. Then in what statement after this will they believe?"*

## 5. Translation Highlights (The Clear Quran)
* *77:8–10:* 'So when the stars are obliterated, And when the heaven is opened, And when the mountains are blown away...'
* *77:20–21:* 'Did We not create you from a liquid disdained? And We placed it in a firm lodging...'
* *77:48:* 'And when it is said to them, "Bow [in prayer]," they do not bow.'

---

## 6. What Classical Tafsir Explains
* **The Meaning of 'Kifata' (Verses 25–26):** Ibn Abbas (RA) and classical mufassirun explain: *Kifatan* is derived from *Kaft* (to encompass, shelter, and hold). The earth is a merciful cradle that holds and shelters billions of living human beings upon its surface, and tenderly conceals and holds their decomposed bodies within its soil after death.
* **The Tenfold Warning: 'Waylun Yawma'idhin' (Repeated 10 Times):** Imam Ibn Kathir notes that the ten repetitions of *Waylun yawma'idhin lil-mukadhdhibin* are not mere rhetorical repetition; each repetition follows a completely distinct proof: after meteorological oaths, after embryonic creation, after geological balance, after the Day of Sorting, and after the description of Paradise.
---

## 7. Important Supporting Hadiths
1. **Revealed in the Cave of Mina:**  
   *Sahih al-Bukhari (Hadith 4934) from Abdullah ibn Mas'ud (RA):*  
   "Ibn Mas'ud narrated: 'While we were with the Prophet ﷺ in a cave at Mina, *Wal-Mursalat* was revealed to him. He was reciting it, and I was receiving it fresh from his mouth, when suddenly a snake sprang at us. The Prophet ﷺ said: *Kill it!* We raced to kill it, but it escaped. The Prophet ﷺ said: *It was saved from your evil, and you were saved from its evil.*'"
2. **The Last Surah Recited by the Prophet ﷺ:**  
   *Sahih al-Bukhari (Hadith 4429) from Umm al-Fadl bint al-Harith (RA):*  
   "She said: 'I heard the Messenger of Allah ﷺ recite *Wal-Mursalat 'Urfa* in the Maghrib prayer, and he never led us in prayer again after that until he was taken by Allah.'"
---

## 8. Key Concepts
1. **Al-Mursalat (The Emissary Winds/Angels):** The cosmic forces dispatched by God in succession carrying rain, weather, and revelation.
2. **Al-Kifat (The Earth as a Sanctuary):** The divine provisioning of earth as an embracing home for both the living and the dead.
3. **Waylun lil-Mukadhdhibin (Woe to the Deniers):** The recurring ten-fold warning against cynical skepticism toward divine truth.
4. **Bi-Ayyi Hadithin Ba'dahu (The Ultimate Question):** The challenge: If the magnificent Qur'an does not move your soul to faith, what text ever will?
---

## 9. Tadabbur (Guided Contemplation)
* **The Earth as Your Mother (77:25–26):** Look at the word *Kifata*: It implies an embracing mother holding a baby to her chest. While you are alive, the earth feeds you, grows your crops, and supports your feet. When you die, the same earth opens her soil, hugs your corpse, and conceals your decay until resurrection. Respect the earth, walk upon it with humility, and prepare for your return to its soil.
* **If Not the Qur'an, Then What? (77:50):** Ponder the final sentence of this surah: *Fabi-ayyi hadithin ba'dahu yu'minun?* If the living speech of your Creator, filled with cosmic majesty, intellectual proofs, psychological depth, and emotional warmth does not wake you up—what book, what philosophy, what podcast, or what self-help author could possibly save your soul? Come back to the Qur'an today.

## 10. What This Means For Me
* I take the final question of the surah as my personal anchor: The Qur'an is my ultimate, infallible source of guidance (77:50).
* I walk upon the earth with humility and gratitude, mindful that it is my container in life and my grave in death (77:25–26).
* Whenever I am commanded to bow before Allah in prayer, I rush immediately without hesitation or pride (77:48).

## 12. Reflection Questions
1. If the divine words of the Qur'an do not awaken my heart to faith, what worldly speech ever will (77:50)?
2. How does viewing the earth as *Kifat* (sheltering container for living and dead) calibrate my relationship with nature and mortality (77:25–26)?
3. Do I bow willingly before Allah in all five prayers, or am I guilty of hesitation (77:48)?

## 13. Closing Supplication
$$\text{اللَّهُمَّ إِنَّا نُؤْمِنُ بِكَلَامِكَ وَكِتَابِكَ ، فَلَا حَدِيثَ بَعْدَ حَدِيثِكَ ، اللَّهُمَّ اجْعَلِ الْأَرْضَ لَنَا كِفَاتًا فِي طَاعَتِكَ ، وَلَا تَجْعَلْنَا مِنَ الْمُكَذِّبِينَ ، وَثَبِّتْ أَقْدَامَنَا يَوْمَ تَرْجُفُ الْأَرْضُ وَالسَّمَاءُ}$$  
*"O Allah, we believe in Your speech and Your Book; there is no statement after Your statement. O Allah, make the earth a container for us in Your obedience, do not make us among the deniers, and make our feet firm on the Day the earth and heaven tremble. Amin."*
