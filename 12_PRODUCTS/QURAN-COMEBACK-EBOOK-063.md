# CHAPTER 63: SURAH AL-MUNAFIQUN (سُورَةُ المنافقون)
**Classification:** Madani  
**Order in Mushaf:** 63  
**Total Verses:** 11  
**Famous Names:** Surat al-Munafiqun  

---

## 1. Why This Surah Matters
Surah Al-Munafiqun is the clinical diagnostic manual on the psychology of religious hypocrisy (*Nifaq*). It exposes the leader of Madinan hypocrites, Abdullah ibn Ubayy ibn Salul, and his inner circle, unveiling their superficial charm: 'When you see them, their bodies please you, and if they speak, you listen to their speech; [yet] they are as if they were propped-up timbers' (*Khashabun musannadah*), terrified that every loud sound is directed at them. It concludes with the ultimate warning against letting wealth and children distract from Allah's remembrance, and depicts the desperate, futile plea of the dying person who begs: 'My Lord, if only You would delay me for a brief term so I would give charity and be among the righteous' (63:10), met by the absolute divine verdict: 'And never will Allah delay a soul when its appointed time has come.'

---

## 2. Surah Context
Revealed in Madinah around 5–6 AH after the expedition of Banu al-Mustaliq, when Abdullah ibn Ubayy uttered the treasonous threat: 'If we return to Madinah, the more honorable will surely expel the more humble therefrom' (63:8). The teenage companion Zayd ibn Arqam overheard and reported it; Ibn Ubayy swore falsely that he never said it, and Surah Al-Munafiqun descended from heaven to vindicate Zayd's truthfulness.

---

## 3. Central Theme
**The Psychological Profile of Hypocrisy (*An-Nifaq*), Seditious Speech, and the Urgent Command to Spend Before Death Stems All Second Chances.**

---

## 4. Key Passages
### The Hollow Fascade of the Hypocrites (63:4)
$$\text{وَإِذَا رَأَيْتَهُمْ تُعْجِبُكَ أَجْسَامُهُمْ ۖ وَإِن يَقُولُوا تَسْمَعْ لِقَوْلِهِمْ ۖ كَأَنَّهُمْ خُشُبٌ مُّسَنَّدَةٌ ۖ يَحْسَبُونَ كُلَّ صَيْحَةٍ عَلَيْهِمْ ۚ هُمُ الْعَدُوُّ فَاحْذَرْهُمْ ۚ قَاتَلَهُمُ اللَّهُ ۖ أَنَّىٰ يُؤْفَكُونَ}$$
*"And when you see them, their forms please you, and if they speak, you listen to their speech. [Yet] they are as if they were pieces of wood propped up—they think that every shout is against them. They are the enemy, so beware of them. May Allah destroy them; how are they deluded?"*

### Honor Belongs Only to Allah and His Messenger (63:8)
$$\text{يَقُولُونَ لَئِن رَّجَعْنَا إِلَى الْمَدِينَةِ لَيُخْرِجَنَّ الْأَعَزُّ مِنْهَا الْأَذَلَّ ۚ وَلِلَّهِ الْعِزَّةُ وَلِرَسُولِهِ وَلِلْمُؤْمِنِينَ وَلَٰكِنَّ الْمُنَافِقِينَ لَا يَعْلَمُونَ}$$
*"They say, 'If we return to al-Madinah, the more honorable [for sure] will expel therefrom the more humble.' And to Allah belongs [all] honor, and to His Messenger, and to the believers, but the hypocrites do not know."*

### The Futile Begging of the Dying (63:9–11)
$$\text{يَا أَيُّهَا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا لَا تُلْهِكُمْ أَمْوَالُكُمْ وَلَا أَوْلَادُكُمْ عَن ذِكْرِ اللَّهِ ۚ وَمَن يَفْعَلْ ذَٰلِكَ فَأُولَٰئِكَ هُمُ الْخَاسِرُونَ ۝ وَأَنفِقُوا مِن مَّا رَزَقْنَاكُم مِّن قَبْلِ أَن يَأْتِيَ أَحَدَكُمُ الْمَوْتُ فَيَقُولَ رَبِّ لَوْلَا أَخَّرْتَنِي إِلَىٰ أَجَلٍ قَرِيبٍ فَأَصَّدَّقَ وَأَكُن مِّنَ الصَّالِحِينَ ۝ وَلَن يُؤَخِّرَ اللَّهُ نَفْسًا إِذَا جَاءَ أَجَلُهَا ۚ وَاللَّهُ خَبِيرٌ بِمَا تَعْمَلُونَ}$$
*"O you who have believed, let not your wealth and your children divert you from remembrance of Allah. And whoever does that—then those are the losers. And spend [in the way of Allah] from what We have provided you before death comes to one of you and he says, 'My Lord, if only You would delay me for a brief term so I would give charity and be among the righteous.' But never will Allah delay a soul when its time has come. And Allah is Acquainted with what you do."*

## 5. Translation Highlights (The Clear Quran)
* *63:1:* 'When the hypocrites come to you, [O Muhammad], they say, "We testify that you are the Messenger of Allah." And Allah knows that you are His Messenger, and Allah testifies that the hypocrites are liars.'
* *63:2:* 'They have taken their oaths as a cover, so they averted [people] from the way of Allah. Indeed, it was evil that they were doing.'
* *63:7:* 'They are the ones who say, "Do not spend on those who are with the Messenger of Allah until they disband." And to Allah belong the depositories of the heavens and the earth, but the hypocrites do not understand.'

---

## 6. What Classical Tafsir Explains
* **Like Propped-Up Wood (Verse 4):** Ibn Abbas (RA) and Imam Ibn Kathir explain that the hypocrites were tall, physically attractive, wore fine clothes, and possessed silver-tongued rhetoric; yet internally, their hearts were dead and hollow like hollowed timber leaning against a wall—empty of faith, courage, and sincerity.
* **Why Does the Dying Person Beg to Give Charity? (Verse 10):** Ibn Abbas famously observed: 'No person who falls short in paying his Zakah or performing his Hajj faces death except that he desperately begs for an extension (*Raj'ah*).' The dying person asks specifically to give charity (*Fa-assaddaqa*) because at death, the veil is removed and he directly witnesses the stupendous, immediate light and protection that Sadaqah provides in the grave.
---

## 7. Important Supporting Hadiths
1. **The Vindication of Zayd ibn Arqam:**  
   *Sahih al-Bukhari (Hadith 4900) from Zayd ibn Arqam (RA):*  
   "Zayd said: 'I heard Abdullah ibn Ubayy say: *Do not spend on those with the Messenger of Allah until they disband* and *If we return to Madinah, the honorable will expel the humble.* I informed the Prophet ﷺ, but Ibn Ubayy swore he never said it, and people blamed me. I was so distressed that I stayed in my tent. Then the Prophet ﷺ took my ear, pinched it gently with love, and said: *This boy's ear spoke the truth to Allah!* and Allah revealed Surah Al-Munafiqun.'"
2. **The Signs of a Hypocrite:**  
   *Sahih al-Bukhari (Hadith 33) from Abu Hurayrah (RA):*  
   "The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: 'The signs of a hypocrite are three: when he speaks, he lies; when he promises, he breaks it; and when he is entrusted, he betrays.'"
---

## 8. Key Concepts
1. **An-Nifaq (Hypocrisy):** The grave spiritual disease of professing faith outwardly while harboring skepticism, malice, or self-interest inwardly.
2. **Khashabun Musannadah (Propped-Up Timber):** The vivid Quranic metaphor for people with attractive external appearances but completely hollow spiritual cores.
3. **Al-'Izzah (True Honor):** The absolute truth that dignity and honor belong exclusively to Allah, His Messenger, and the believers.
4. **At-Tadarruk Qabl al-Mawt (Spending Before Death):** The urgency of giving voluntary charity and fulfilling obligations before the non-negotiable arrival of death.
---

## 9. Tadabbur (Guided Contemplation)
* **Style Without Substance (63:4):** In our modern culture of curated Instagram feeds, polished influencer videos, and charismatic corporate pitchmen, verse 4 is a piercing diagnosis: *Tu'jibuka ajsamuhum wa in yaqulu tasma' li-qawlihim.* Do not be hypnotized by smooth talk, attractive fashion, or prestigious accents. A person can have a million followers and speak with breathtaking eloquence, yet in the sight of Allah be nothing more than dead timber (*Khashabun musannadah*). Look for character, humble prayer, and integrity.
* **The Cry of the Dying: Just Five More Minutes (63:10):** Picture the final gasp of life when the angel of death stands beside your bed. You will not ask for more time to check your investments, close another deal, or watch another show. You will beg: *Rabbi lawla akhkhartani ila ajalin qaribin fa-assaddaqa!* ('My Lord, just give me five more minutes so I can give away all my wealth in charity!'). You have that time right now. Spend before the window slams shut.

## 10. What This Means For Me
* I ruthlessly audit my character against the signs of hypocrisy: being honest in speech, fulfilling promises, and protecting trusts.
* I never let my career, bank accounts, or children divert me from establishing prayer and remembering Allah (63:9).
* I give charity proactively today, ensuring I will not be among those crying for a delayed deathbed extension (63:10).

## 11. One Practical Action
**Give an Immediate 'Pre-Death' Sadaqah Today:**  
Act on verse 63:10 (*Wa anfiqu min ma razaqnakum min qabli an ya'tiya ahadakum al-mawt*): Open your banking app or charity portal right now, transfer an intentional donation for the sake of Allah, and whisper: 'O Allah, accept this from me before death arrives.'

## 13. Closing Supplication
$$\text{اللَّهُمَّ إِنَّا نَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنَ النِّفَاقِ وَالشِّقَاقِ وَسُوءِ الْأَخْلَاقِ ، اللَّهُمَّ لَا تُلْهِنَا أَمْوَالُنَا وَلَا أَوْلَادُنَا عَنْ ذِكْرِكَ ، وَارْزُقْنَا أَنْ نُنْفِقَ فِي سَبِيلِكَ قَبْلَ أَنْ يَأْتِيَ الْمَوْتُ}$$  
*"O Allah, we seek refuge in You from hypocrisy, discord, and evil character. O Allah, let not our wealth or our children divert us from Your remembrance, and bless us to spend in Your cause before death arrives. Amin."*
