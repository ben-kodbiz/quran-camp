# CHAPTER 99: SURAH AZ-ZALZALAH (سُورَةُ الزلزلة)
**Classification:** Madani  
**Order in Mushaf:** 99  
**Total Verses:** 8  
**Famous Names:** Surat az-Zalzalah, Idha Zulzilat  

---

## 1. Why This Surah Matters
Surah Az-Zalzalah delivers an earth-shattering reality check regarding the precision of divine accountability. When the earth is shaken with its final cataclysmic convulsion and disgorges its buried burdens—corpses, hidden secrets, and minerals—humanity will ask in shock: 'What is wrong with it?' (99:3). On that day, the earth itself transforms into a vocal, legal witness, narrating everything that took place upon its surface. The Surah concludes with the most precise equation of justice in divine literature: 'Whoever does an atom's weight of good will see it, and whoever does an atom's weight of evil will see it' (99:7–8).

---

## 2. Surah Context
Revealed in Madinah to establish meticulous consciousness of small deeds, cautioning believers against belittling minor sins and encouraging them never to undervalue small acts of kindness.

---

## 3. Central Theme
**The Terminal Earthquake of the Earth, the Earth Testifying Against Human Deeds, and Absolute Atom-Level Accountability.**

---

## 4. Key Passages
### The Cataclysmic Convulsion (99:1–3)
$$\text{إِذَا زُلْزِلَتِ الْأَرْضُ زِلْزَالَهَا ۝ وَأَخْرَجَتِ الْأَرْضُ أَثْقَالَهَا ۝ وَقَالَ الْإِنسَانُ مَا لَهَا}$$
*"When the earth is shaken with its [final] earthquake, And the earth discharges its burdens, And man says, 'What is [wrong] with it?'"*

### The Earth Narrating Her History (99:4–5)
$$\text{يَوْمَئِذٍ تُحَدِّثُ أَخْبَارَهَا ۝ بِأَنَّ رَبَّكَ أَوْحَىٰ لَهَا}$$
*"That Day, it will report its news, Because your Lord has commanded it."*

### The Scale of the Atom (99:6–8)
$$\text{يَوْمَئِذٍ يَصْدُرُ النَّاسُ أَشْتَاتًا لِّيُرَوْا أَعْمَالَهُمْ ۝ فَمَن يَعْمَلْ مِثْقَالَ ذَرَّةٍ خَيْرًا يَرَهُ ۝ وَمَن يَعْمَلْ مِثْقَالَ ذَرَّةٍ شَرًّا يَرَهُ}$$
*"That Day, the people will depart separated [into categories] to be shown [the result of] their deeds. So whoever does an atom's weight of good will see it, And whoever does an atom's weight of evil will see it."*

## 5. Translation Highlights (The Clear Quran)
* *99:1:* 'When the earth is shaken with its ultimate quake (*Zilzalaha*).'
* *99:2:* 'And the earth throws out its buried burdens (*Athqalaha*).'
* *99:7:* 'So whoever does an atom's weight of good will see it (*Mithqala dharratin khayran yarah*).'
* *99:8:* 'And whoever does an atom's weight of evil will see it (*Mithqala dharratin sharran yarah*).'

---

## 6. What Classical Tafsir Explains
* **The Earth as a Living Witness (Verses 4–5):** Imam Ibn Kathir quotes the narration: 'Do you know what its news is?' The Prophet ﷺ said: 'Its news is that it will bear witness against every servant, male or female, about what they did on its surface: You did such-and-such on such-and-such day.' The very soil beneath your feet is recording your footsteps, prayers, tears, and sins.
* **The Concept of *Dharrah* (Atom's Weight) (Verses 7–8):** Classical commentators explain *Dharrah* originally referred to the tiniest speck of dust visible in a beam of sunlight, or the weight of a tiny red ant. In modern scientific terminology, it is the atom or subatomic particle. No smile, no crumb given to a hungry animal, and no whispered slander is too microscopic to escape divine records.
* **The Comprehensive Nature of the Surah:** Scholars call this Surah *Al-Jami'ah* (The Comprehensive). Sa'id ibn Jubayr narrated that when this verse was revealed, believers understood that no good is too small to neglect, and no sin is too minor to tolerate.
---

## 7. Important Supporting Hadiths
1. **The Earth's Testimony:**  
   *Jami' at-Tirmidhi (Hadith 2429), Hasan:*  
   "The Prophet ﷺ recited: *Yawma'idhin tuhaddithu akhbaraha*, then said: 'Do you know what its reports are?' They said: 'Allah and His Messenger know best.' He said: 'Its reports are that it will testify against every slave regarding what they did upon its back.'"
2. **Guard Yourself Against Hell Even with Half a Date:**  
   *Sahih al-Bukhari (Hadith 1417) & Sahih Muslim (Hadith 1016) from 'Adi ibn Hatim (RA):*  
   "The Prophet ﷺ said: 'Guard yourselves against the Fire, even with half a date [given in charity], and if one cannot find that, then with a kind word.'"
3. **Beware of Belittling Minor Sins:**  
   *Musnad Ahmad (Hadith 3811), Sahih:*  
   "The Prophet ﷺ said: 'Beware of minor sins, for they accumulate upon a person until they destroy him.'"
---

## 8. Key Concepts
1. **Az-Zalzalah (The Cosmic Quake):** The final seismic rupture of the planet releasing its spiritual and physical record.
2. **Mithqala Dharrah (The Weight of an Atom):** Divine precision in justice where the most microscopic intention or gesture is weighed.
3. **Shahadat al-Ard (The Earth's Testimony):** The physical creation speaking under divine command to testify to human behavior.
4. **Ash-Shat (The Divided Throngs):** Humankind emerging from graves in separate spiritual streams according to their records.
---

## 9. Tadabbur (Guided Contemplation)
* **Walking Gently on the Earth:** Contemplate: Every patch of ground you step on will either testify for your prostrations and good deeds, or against your transgressions. Walk with humility and mindfulness.
* **The Value of the Microscopic Good:** Think of an 'atom's weight' of good: a warm smile to a tired cashier, removing a pebble from a walkway, whispering *Alhamdulillah*. Never underestimate its weight on your scale.
* **The Hazard of Minor Slips:** Reflect on minor sins: a sarcastic glance, a frivolous comment, 10 wasted minutes. Realize how atoms combine to form towering mountains.
---

## 10. What This Means For Me
* Nothing in my life is lost or forgotten; Allah's scale calculates every micro-moment with infinite justice.
* I should never hold back from doing small good deeds thinking they won't matter; they may be the tipping point of my salvation.
* I must seek Allah's forgiveness immediately whenever I commit even the smallest mistake before the earth logs it.

---

## 11. One Practical Action
**Planting a Witness on the Earth:**  
Go outside today, find a quiet patch of earth, lawn, or sidewalk, and make a conscious act of worship: pick up a piece of litter, feed a stray animal, or make *Tasbih* while walking. Intend for that exact soil to testify for your faith on Judgment Day.
