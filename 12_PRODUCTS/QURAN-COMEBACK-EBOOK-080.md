# CHAPTER 80: SURAH 'ABASA (سُورَةُ عبس)
**Classification:** Makki  
**Order in Mushaf:** 80  
**Total Verses:** 42  
**Famous Names:** Surat 'Abasa, As-Sakhkhah, As-Safaratan al-Kiram  

---

## 1. Why This Surah Matters
Surah 'Abasa is the ultimate testament to the divine integrity of the Qur'an and a masterclass in moral priorities. If the Holy Qur'an were authored by the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ, this surah would never exist: it begins with a direct divine reprimand of the Prophet for frowning and turning away from a poor, blind seeker of knowledge (Abdullah ibn Umm Maktum) while eagerly pitching Islam to affluent pagan aristocrats. Allah set the eternal hierarchy of value: the sincere, trembling seeker who cannot see outweighs all the billionaire chieftains of the earth. The surah ends with the Deafening Blast (*As-Sakhkhah*), when every human being will flee in absolute panic from their own brother, their mother, their father, their spouse, and their children (80:34–37).

---

## 2. Surah Context
Revealed in early Makkah when the Prophet ﷺ was passionately engaged in dialogue with the wealthiest chieftains of Quraysh (Utbah, Shaybah, Abu Jahl, Umayyah ibn Khalaf), hoping their conversion would protect the weak Muslims. The blind companion Ibn Umm Maktum approached, asking to be taught revelation. Annoyed by the interruption, the Prophet ﷺ frowned slightly. Revelation descended immediately to correct his priority.

---

## 3. Central Theme
**Divine Correction of Priorities, the Inestimable Value of the Sincere Seeker, the Arrogance of Human Self-Sufficiency (*Istighna'*), and the Fleeing from Family on Judgment Day (*As-Sakhkhah*).**

---

## 4. Key Passages
### The Divine Correction (80:1–4)
$$\text{عَبَسَ وَتَوَلَّىٰ ۝ أَن جَاءَهُ الْأَعْمَىٰ ۝ وَمَا يُدْرِيكَ لَعَلَّهُ يَزَّكَّىٰ ۝ أَوْ يَذَّكَّرُ فَتَنفَعَهُ الذِّكْرَىٰ}$$
*"The Prophet frowned and turned away, Because there came to him the blind man, [interrupting]. But what would make you perceive, [O Muhammad], that perhaps he might be purified? Or be reminded and the reminder would benefit him?"*

### The Reprimand of Chasing the Arrogant (80:5–10)
$$\text{أَمَّا مَنِ اسْتَغْنَىٰ ۝ فَأَنتَ لَهُ تَصَدَّىٰ ۝ وَمَا عَلَيْكَ أَلَّا يَزَّكَّىٰ ۝ وَأَمَّا مَن جَاءَكَ يَسْعَىٰ ۝ وَهُوَ يَخْشَىٰ ۝ فَأَنتَ عَنْهُ تَلَهَّىٰ}$$
*"As for he who thinks himself without need, To him you give attention. And not upon you [is any blame] if he will not be purified. But as for he who came to you striving [for knowledge] While he fears [Allah]—From him you are distracted."*

### Fleeing From Family on Judgment Day (80:33–37)
$$\text{فَإِذَا جَاءَتِ الصَّاخَّةُ ۝ يَوْمَ يَفِرُّ الْمَرْءُ مِنْ أَخِيهِ ۝ وَأُمِّهِ وَأَبِيهِ ۝ وَصَاحِبَتِهِ وَبَنِيهِ ۝ لِكُلِّ امْرِئٍ مِّنْهُمْ يَوْمَئِذٍ شَأْنٌ يُغْنِيهِ}$$
*"But when there comes the Deafening Blast, On the Day a man will flee from his brother, And his mother and his father, And his wife and his children: For every man, that Day, will be a matter adequate for him."*

## 5. Translation Highlights (The Clear Quran)
* *80:15–16:* '[Carried] by the hands of messenger-angels, Noble and dutiful.'
* *80:17:* 'Cursed is man; how disbelieving is he!'
* *80:38–39:* '[Some] faces, that Day, will be bright—Laughing, rejoicing at good news.'

---

## 6. What Classical Tafsir Explains
* **The Honor Given to Ibn Umm Maktum:** Imam Al-Qurtubi and Ibn Kathir record that after Surah 'Abasa was revealed, whenever the Prophet ﷺ saw the blind companion Abdullah ibn Umm Maktum, he would spread his cloak on the ground for him to sit on and say warmly: 'Welcome to the one regarding whom my Lord reprimanded me!' The Prophet ﷺ later appointed Ibn Umm Maktum as the governor of Madinah twice when he left for military expeditions.
* **Why Do We Flee From Family? (Verses 34–37):** Ibn Abbas (RA) explained two reasons: First, out of sheer terror that your mother or child will demand their rights or ask for one good deed from your scales; second, because the overwhelming horror of the Deafening Blast (*As-Sakhkhah*) renders every human being utterly paralyzed by their own personal fate (*Li-kulli-mri'in minhum yawma'idhin sha'nun yughnih*).
---

## 7. Important Supporting Hadiths
1. **The Revelation of Surah 'Abasa:**  
   *Jami' at-Tirmidhi (Hadith 3331), Sahih:*  
   "Aisha (RA) narrated: 'Surah 'Abasa was revealed regarding Abdullah ibn Umm Maktum the blind man, who came to the Messenger of Allah ﷺ saying: *O Messenger of Allah, guide me!* while the Prophet ﷺ was engaged with a leader among the polytheist chieftains...'"
2. **Barefoot, Naked, and Uncircumcised:**  
   *Sahih al-Bukhari (Hadith 6527) from Aisha (RA):*  
   "The Prophet ﷺ said regarding verse 80:37: 'Humanity will be resurrected barefoot, naked, and uncircumcised.' Aisha asked: 'O Messenger of Allah, won't men and women look at each other?' The Prophet ﷺ replied: 'O Aisha, the matter is far too severe for them to look at one another!'"
---

## 8. Key Concepts
1. **Ta'dib an-Nabi (Divine Refining of the Prophet):** The sublime transparency of revelation correcting even the subtlest facial expression of the Prophet ﷺ.
2. **Al-Istighna' (The Arrogance of Self-Sufficiency):** The spiritual delusion of the wealthy believing they have no need of divine revelation.
3. **As-Sakhkhah (The Deafening Blast):** The earth-shattering trumpet blast that deafens ears and shatters all family attachments.
4. **Firaran min al-Ahl (The Ultimate Flight from Family):** The eschatological separation where every soul is isolated in the terror of their personal accountability.
---

## 9. Tadabbur (Guided Contemplation)
* **Proof That Muhammad ﷺ Was a True Messenger (80:1–2):** Consider this deeply: If the Prophet ﷺ was inventing the Qur'an for power and status, would he ever canonize an eternal chapter that immortalizes his own frown and reprimands him for turning away from a blind beggar? No false prophet would preserve a divine rebuke of his own leadership. Surah 'Abasa is irrefutable proof that the Qur'an is the unedited word of God.
* **Value People as Allah Values Them (80:5–10):** How often do we shower attention on the influential executive, the wealthy client, or the social media celebrity who couldn't care less about faith, while neglecting the sincere, struggling believer who genuinely wants to learn and serve? Surah 'Abasa commands us: Invert your worldly priorities. Treasure the humble seeker who comes striving (*Yas'a wa huwa yakhsha*).
---

## 10. What This Means For Me
* I honor and serve every sincere seeker of knowledge, regardless of their financial, social, or physical status (80:1–4).
* I refuse to chase or flatter arrogant people who feel self-sufficient (*Istaghna*) without God (80:5–7).
* I live knowing that on Judgment Day, even my closest family cannot save me, anchoring my primary reliance on Allah alone (80:34–37).

---

## 11. One Practical Action
**Honor and Assist a Vulnerable Person Today:**  
Directly fulfill the lesson of Surah 'Abasa: Reach out to, visit, or financially help an elderly, disabled, or quiet person in your community today, treating them with the utmost honor as the Prophet ﷺ treated Ibn Umm Maktum.

## 12. Reflection Questions
1. Do I flatter affluent, prestigious people while looking down upon or ignoring humble, sincere believers (80:5–10)?
2. How does the image of fleeing from my mother, father, spouse, and children (80:34–36) clarify my daily priorities?
3. Will my face be bright, laughing, and rejoicing on that Day, or covered in dust and darkness (80:38–41)?

## 13. Closing Supplication
$$\text{اللَّهُمَّ بَيِّضْ وُجُوهَنَا يَوْمَ تَبْيَضُّ وُجُوهٌ وَتَسْوَدُّ وُجُوهٌ ، وَاجْعَلْنَا مِنَ الَّذِينَ وُجُوهُهُمْ مُسْفِرَةٌ ضَاحِكَةٌ مُسْتَبْشِرَةٌ ، وَارْزُقْنَا إِكْرَامَ الْمَسَاكِينِ وَأَهْلِ الْقُرْآنِ}$$  
*"O Allah, brighten our faces on the Day faces are brightened and faces are blackened. Make us among those whose faces are radiant, laughing, and rejoicing at good tidings. Bless us to honor the poor, the vulnerable, and the people of the Qur'an. Amin."*
