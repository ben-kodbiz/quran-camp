# CHAPTER 32: SURAH AS-SAJDAH (سُورَةُ السجدة)
**Classification:** Makki  
**Order in Mushaf:** 32  
**Total Verses:** 30  
**Famous Names:** Surat as-Sajdah, Al-Madaji' (The Sleeping Places)  

---

## 1. Why This Surah Matters
Surah As-Sajdah is the sublime anthem of night devotion and physical surrender. It is beloved to the Sunnah: the Messenger of Allah ﷺ recited it every Friday morning in Fajr prayer and refused to sleep at night until reciting it alongside Surah Al-Mulk. It contrasts two physical postures: the arrogant disbelievers whose necks are forced down in humiliation on the Day of Judgment, and the humble believers who willingly prostrate (*kharru sujjadan*) when reminded of Allah's verses, their flanks forsaking their warm beds in the dead of night to call upon their Lord in fear and yearning (*Tatajafa junubuhum 'anil-madaji'*). For them, Allah has concealed delights that no human eye has ever seen.

---

## 2. Surah Context
Revealed in Makkah to confront the obstinate skepticism surrounding resurrection. The Quraysh scoffed: 'When we are lost within the earth, will we indeed be in a new creation?' (32:10). Surah As-Sajdah dismantled their doubt through the marvels of human biological creation and cosmic order.

---

## 3. Central Theme
**The Honor of Volition Prostration (*As-Sujud*), Night Prayer (*Qiyam al-Layl*), and the Unimaginable Delights Concealed for the Righteous.**

---

## 4. Key Passages
### The Voluntary Prostration of the Humble (32:15)
$$\text{إِنَّمَا يُؤْمِنُ بِآيَاتِنَا الَّذِينَ إِذَا ذُكِّرُوا بِهَا خَرُّوا سُجَّدًا وَسَبَّحُوا بِحَمْدِ رَبِّهِمْ وَهُمْ لَا يَسْتَكْبِرُونَ}$$
*"Only those believe in Our verses who, when they are reminded by them, fall down in prostration and exalt [Allah] with praise of their Lord, and they are not arrogant."*

### Forsaking Beds for Night Prayer (32:16)
$$\text{تَتَجَافَىٰ جُنُوبُهُمْ عَنِ الْمَضَاجِعِ يَدْعُونَ رَبَّهُمْ خَوْفًا وَطَمَعًا وَمِمَّا رَزَقْنَاهُمْ يُنفِقُونَ}$$
*"They forsake their beds; they supplicate their Lord in fear and aspiration, and from what We have provided them, they spend."*

### The Concealed Delights of Paradise (32:17)
$$\text{فَلَا تَعْلَمُ نَفْسٌ مَّا أُخْفِيَ لَهُم مِّن قُرَّةِ أَعْيُنٍ جَزَاءً بِمَا كَانُوا يَعْمَلُونَ}$$
*"And no soul knows what has been hidden for them of comfort for eyes as reward for what they used to do."*

---

## 5. Translation Highlights (The Clear Quran)
* *32:9:* 'Then He proportioned him and breathed into him from His [created] soul and made for you hearing and vision and hearts; little are you grateful.'
* *32:18:* 'Then is one who was a believer like one who was defiantly disobedient? They are not equal.'
* *32:24:* 'And We made from among them leaders guiding by Our command when they were patient and [when] they were certain of Our signs.'

---

## 6. What Classical Tafsir Explains
* **The Secret Reward for Secret Devotion (Verse 17):** Al-Hasan al-Basri noted: 'A people concealed their righteous deeds in the darkness of the night, so Allah concealed for them an unimaginable reward that no human eye has seen or ear heard.' Because their night prayer was hidden from human applause, their eternal reward is hidden until unveiled in Jannah.
* **Leadership Through Patience and Certainty (Verse 24):** Shaykh al-Islam Ibn Taymiyyah famously observed regarding verse 24: 'By patience (*Sabr*) and certainty (*Yaqin*), leadership in religion (*Imamah fid-Din*) is attained.' Patience protects against base desires, while certainty repels intellectual doubts.
---

## 7. Important Supporting Hadiths
1. **The Friday Fajr Recitation:**  
   *Sahih al-Bukhari (Hadith 891) from Abu Hurayrah (RA):*  
   "The Prophet ﷺ used to recite in the Fajr prayer on Fridays: *Alif, Lam, Meem. Tanzil* (Surah As-Sajdah) and *Hal ata 'alal-insan* (Surah Al-Insan)."
2. **The Nightly Recitation Before Sleep:**  
   *Jami' at-Tirmidhi (Hadith 2892), Sahih:*  
   "Jabir (RA) narrated: 'The Prophet ﷺ would never sleep until he recited *Alif-Lam-Meem Tanzil* (As-Sajdah) and *Tabarak alladhi bi-yadihil-Mulk* (Al-Mulk).'"
---

## 8. Key Concepts
1. **Tatajafi al-Junub (Forsaking Beds):** The spiritual discipline of rising in the quiet stillness before dawn for intimate connection with Allah.
2. **Qurratu A'yun Makhfiyyah (Concealed Eye-Delights):** The supreme rewards of Paradise hidden from human comprehension as a mirror to secret deeds.
3. **Imamah fid-Din (Spiritual Leadership):** True religious moral leadership earned exclusively through the combination of Sabr and Yaqin.
4. **Khurur as-Sujud (Spontaneous Prostration):** The swift bodily reflex of dropping to the floor in awe when divine truth touches the heart.
---

## 9. Tadabbur (Guided Contemplation)
* **The Comfort of Sleep vs. The Taste of Sujud (32:16):** Look at the word *Tatajafa*: it implies an aversion or restlessness against the mattress. When the heart tastes the sweetness of speaking to the Creator in Tahajjud, the softest Egyptian cotton sheets feel uncomfortable. The bed is calling the body to sleep, but the soul is yearning for the divine presence. Even two rak'ahs before Fajr change the spiritual trajectory of your day.
* **Are They Equal? (32:18):** Modern culture asserts that all lifestyles, moral choices, and ideologies are essentially interchangeable. Allah asks with absolute clarity: *Afaman kana mu'minan kaman kana fasiqa? La yastawun.* A life spent in humble service to God cannot and will not have the same eternal outcome as a life wasted in arrogance and defiance.
---

## 10. What This Means For Me
* I make it a habit to wake up 15 minutes before Fajr to pray Tahajjud and taste the sweetness of forsaking my bed (32:16).
* I recite Surah As-Sajdah regularly before sleeping, following the beloved Sunnah of the Prophet ﷺ.
* I cultivate both patience (*Sabr*) and certainty (*Yaqin*) in my daily trials to qualify for spiritual maturity.

---

## 11. One Practical Action
**Wake Up for 2 Rak'ahs of Tahajjud Tonight:**  
Set your alarm 20 minutes before Fajr tomorrow morning. Leave your warm bed, perform wudu, and pray two quiet rak'ahs in private, supplicating in Sujud for your loved ones.

## 12. Reflection Questions
1. Does my body ever forsake its bed in yearning for Allah, or am I enslaved to comfort and sleep (32:16)?
2. When I hear the verses of Allah, is my immediate reaction humble submission (*Kharru sujjadan*) or intellectual pride?
3. How am I combining *Sabr* (restraining impulses) and *Yaqin* (unshakeable faith) in my current challenges?

## 13. Closing Supplication

*"O Allah, make us among those whose flanks forsake their beds, calling upon You in fear and aspiration. O Allah, grant us the concealed delight of our eyes in the Gardens of Bliss. Amin."*
