# CHAPTER 70: SURAH AL-MA'ARIJ (سُورَةُ المعارج)
**Classification:** Makki  
**Order in Mushaf:** 70  
**Total Verses:** 44  
**Famous Names:** Surat al-Ma'arij, Sa'ala Sa'il  

---

## 1. Why This Surah Matters
Surah Al-Ma'arij maps the cosmic stairways of ascending angels across a Day whose measure is fifty thousand years (*Fi yawmin kana miqdaruhu khamsina alfa sanah*). It provides the definitive Quranic psychological diagnosis of human anxiety and scarcity mindset in verses 19–21: 'Indeed, man was created anxious and impatient: when evil touches him, he is desperate; and when good touches him, he is withholding.' It immediately follows this diagnosis with the divine therapy: a sublime nine-point behavioral charter of the steadfast—beginning with those who are constant in prayer (*Alladhina hum 'ala salatihim da'imun*), who allocate a recognized right in their wealth for the beggar and deprived, who guard their chastity, fulfill their trusts, and stand firm in their testimonies.

---

## 2. Surah Context
Revealed in Makkah when the arrogant polytheist An-Nadr ibn al-Harith sarcastically challenged the Prophet ﷺ: 'O Allah, if this is the truth from You, rain down upon us stones from heaven!' (70:1–2). Surah Al-Ma'arij responded by depicting the terrifying reality of that imminent punishment.

---

## 3. Central Theme
**The Ascending Cosmic Stairways (*Al-Ma'arij*), the Psychology of Impatience (*Al-Hala'*), and the Behavioral Charter of the Resilient Believers.**

---

## 4. Key Passages
### The Cosmic Day of Fifty Thousand Years (70:4)
$$\text{تَعْرُجُ الْمَلَائِكَةُ وَالرُّوحُ إِلَيْهِ فِي يَوْمٍ كَانَ مِقْدَارُهُ خَمْسِينَ أَلْفَ سَنَةٍ ۝ فَاصْبِرْ صَبْرًا جَمِيلًا}$$
*"The angels and the Spirit will ascend to Him during a Day the extent of which is fifty thousand years. So be patient with gracious patience."*

### The Psychology of the Anxious Human (70:19–22)
$$\text{إِنَّ الْإِنسَانَ خُلِقَ هَلُوعًا ۝ إِذَا مَسَّهُ الشَّرُّ جَزُوعًا ۝ وَإِذَا مَسَّهُ الْخَيْرُ مَنُوعًا ۝ إِلَّا الْمُصَلِّينَ}$$
*"Indeed, man was created impatient: When evil touches him, irritable; And when good touches him, withholding [of good]—Except the observers of prayer."*

### The Hallmarks of the Resilient (70:23–35)
$$\text{الَّذِينَ هُمْ عَلَىٰ صَلَاتِهِمْ دَائِمُونَ ۝ وَالَّذِينَ فِي أَمْوَالِهِمْ حَقٌّ مَّعْلُومٌ ۝ لِّلسَّائِلِ وَالْمَحْرُومِ... أُولَٰئِكَ فِي جَنَّاتٍ مُّكْرَمُونَ}$$
*"Those who are constant in their prayer, And those within whose wealth is a known right For the petitioner and the deprived... Those will be in gardens, honored."*

---

## 5. Translation Highlights (The Clear Quran)
* *70:10–14:* 'And no friend will ask about a friend, Though they are made to see each other. The criminal will wish that he could be ransomed from the punishment of that Day by his children, And his wife and his brother, And his nearest kindred who sheltered him...'
* *70:32:* 'And those who are to their trusts and their promises attentive.'
* *70:34:* 'And those who [strictly] guard their prayers.'

---

## 6. What Classical Tafsir Explains
* **What is 'Sabr Jamil'? (Verse 5):** Imam Ibn Kathir and Mujahid explain that *Sabrun Jamil* (Gracious / Beautiful Patience) is endurance that is completely free of complaining to human beings, free of hysterical panic, and free of resentment toward God's decree. The believer weeps only to Allah.
* **The Meaning of 'Halu'a' (Verse 19):** The Qur'an itself defines *Halu'a* in the subsequent verses: When difficulty or illness hits him, he falls into hopeless despair (*Jazu'a*); and when wealth and ease arrive, he becomes stingy and selfish (*Manu'a*). Only prayer (*Illa al-Musallin*) reconstructs this erratic psychology into emotional equilibrium.
---

## 7. Important Supporting Hadiths
1. **The Lightening of the Fifty-Thousand-Year Day:**  
   *Musnad Ahmad (Hadith 11721), Sahih:*  
   "The Companions asked: 'O Messenger of Allah, how long is a Day of fifty thousand years?' The Prophet ﷺ replied: 'By the One in whose Hand is my soul, it will be lightened for the believer until it feels shorter than a single obligatory prayer prayed in worldly life!'"
2. **Constant in Prayer:**  
   *Sahih Muslim (Hadith 782) from Aisha (RA):*  
   "Regarding verse 70:23 (*'Ala salatihim da'imun*), the Prophet ﷺ said: 'The most beloved deeds to Allah are those that are most consistent, even if they are small.'"
---

## 8. Key Concepts
1. **Al-Ma'arij (The Ascending Stairways):** The celestial levels and dimensions through which the angels and Gabriel ascend to the divine presence.
2. **Al-Hala' (Erratic Impatience):** The flawed biological human temperament swinging between panic in hardship and greed in prosperity.
3. **Sabrun Jamil (Gracious Patience):** Patience marked by absolute dignity, serene composure, and zero bitter venting to creation.
4. **Haqqun Ma'lum (The Recognized Right):** Allocating an intentional, budgeted portion of income for the poor, transforming charity from an afterthought to an obligation.
---

## 9. Tadabbur (Guided Contemplation)
* **Prayer as Emotional Therapy (70:19–23):** Notice the divine diagnosis: You are naturally anxious, impatient, irritable under stress, and greedy in abundance (*Halu'a*). How do you cure this chronic emotional instability? Look at the exception: *Illa al-Musallin* ('Except those who pray!'). When you connect your forehead to the floor five times a day, prayer acts as a spiritual shock-absorber. It grounds your panic in difficulty and softens your greed in prosperity.
* **Ransoming Your Own Children (70:11–14):** Look at the terrifying terror of the criminal on Judgment Day: He would eagerly push his own children, his beloved spouse, his brother, and the entire population of the earth into the flames of Hell if it could ransom him for one second! The very family you sacrificed your ethics for in this life will be the first you try to push into the Fire to save yourself tomorrow. Live for Allah first.
---

## 10. What This Means For Me
* I cultivate *Sabr Jamil* during setbacks, refusing to complain to people or exhibit hysterical panic (70:5).
* I cure my natural anxiety and irritability by maintaining unwavering consistency in my five daily prayers (70:22–23).
* I establish a fixed charitable budget (*Haqqun Ma'lum*) in my monthly finances for the needy and deprived (70:24–25).

---

## 11. One Practical Action
**Establish a 'Haqqun Ma'lum' Budget Today:**  
Act on verse 70:24: Calculate a specific, fixed monthly percentage of your income (e.g., 2.5%, 5%, or a recurring donation) dedicated permanently for the poor, transforming your charity into a recognized right.

---

## 12. Reflection Questions
1. Do I exhibit the erratic temperament of *Al-Hala'*—panicking in hardship and withholding in prosperity (70:19–21)?
2. Is my prayer regular and disciplined (*Da'imun*), or do I pray erratically depending on mood (70:23)?
3. Does my wealth contain a 'known, recognized right' (*Haqqun Ma'lum*) for the destitute (70:24)?

## 13. Closing Supplication
$$\text{اللَّهُمَّ إِنَّا نَسْأَلُكَ صَبْرًا جَمِيلًا ، اللَّهُمَّ اجْعَلْنَا مِنَ الْمُصَلِّينَ الَّذِينَ هُمْ عَلَىٰ صَلَاتِهِمْ دَائِمُونَ ، وَاجْعَلْ فِي أَمْوَالِنَا حَقًّا مَعْلُومًا لِلسَّائِلِ وَالْمَحْرُومِ ، وَأَدْخِلْنَا جَنَّاتِكَ مُكْرَمِينَ}$$  
*"O Allah, we ask You for gracious patience. O Allah, make us among the worshippers who are constant in their prayer, place within our wealth a recognized right for the petitioner and the deprived, and admit us into Your Gardens, honored. Amin."*
