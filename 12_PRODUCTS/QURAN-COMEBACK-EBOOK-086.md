# CHAPTER 86: SURAH AT-TARIQ (سُورَةُ الطارق)
**Classification:** Makki  
**Order in Mushaf:** 86  
**Total Verses:** 17  
**Famous Names:** Surat at-Tariq, An-Najm ath-Thaqib  

---

## 1. Why This Surah Matters
Surah At-Tariq is the cosmic and biological wake-up call to human humility and divine accountability. Opening with an oath by the night sky and *At-Tariq*—the pulsating 'Piercing Star' (*An-Najm ath-Thaqib*) that knocks and pierces through cosmic darkness—it declares that every human soul is accompanied by a divine guardian angel (*In kullu nafsin lamma 'alayha hafidh*). It commands human beings to contemplate their humble biological origin: created from a spurting fluid emerging from between the backbone and the ribs, proving that the God who engineered life from fluid is easily able to resurrect it. It delivers the spine-chilling warning in verse 9: 'The Day when all secrets will be laid bare and tested' (*Yawma tubla as-sara'ir*).

---

## 2. Surah Context
Revealed in early Makkah when the pagan Quraysh plotted secret conspiracies to murder the Prophet ﷺ and assassinate his character. Surah At-Tariq exposed the futility of human scheming against the ultimate divine plan (*Innahum yakiduna kayda wa akidu kayda*).

---

## 3. Central Theme
**The Piercing Star (*At-Tariq*), Embryonic Origins as Proof of Resurrection, the Day of Tested Secrets (*Tubla as-Sara'ir*), and the Decisive Word.**

---

## 4. Key Passages
### The Piercing Star and the Guardian Angel (86:1–4)
$$\text{وَالسَّمَاءِ وَالطَّارِقِ ۝ وَمَا أَدْرَاكَ مَا الطَّارِقُ ۝ النَّجْمُ الثَّاقِبُ ۝ إِن كُلُّ نَفْسٍ لَّمَّا عَلَيْهَا حَافِظٌ}$$
*"By the sky and the night comer—And what can make you know what is the night comer? It is the piercing star—There is no soul but that it has over it a protector [guardian angel]."*

### The Biological Origin (86:5–8)
$$\text{فَلْيَنظُرِ الْإِنسَانُ مِمَّ خُلِقَ ۝ خُلِقَ مِن مَّاءٍ دَافِقٍ ۝ يَخْرُجُ مِن بَيْنِ الصُّلْبِ وَالتَّرَائِبِ ۝ إِنَّهُ عَلَىٰ رَجْعِهِ لَقَادِرٌ}$$
*"So let man observe from what he was created. He was created from a fluid, ejected, Emerging from between the backbone and the ribs. Indeed, Allah, to return him [to life], is Able."*

### The Day Secrets Are Laid Bare (86:9–10)
$$\text{يَوْمَ تُبْلَى السَّرَائِرُ ۝ فَمَا لَهُ مِن قُوَّةٍ وَلَا نَاصِرٍ}$$
*"The Day when secrets will be put on trial [tested and exposed], Then man will have no power or any helper."*

## 5. Translation Highlights (The Clear Quran)
* *86:11–12:* 'By the sky which returns [rain], And [by] the earth which cracks open [with sprouts]...'
* *86:13–14:* 'Indeed, the Quran is a decisive statement, And it is not amusement.'
* *86:15–17:* 'Indeed, they are planning a plan, But I am planning a plan. So allow time for the disbelievers. Leave them awhile.'

---

## 6. What Classical Tafsir Explains
* **The Meaning of 'Yawma Tubla as-Sara'ir' (Verse 9):** Ibn Umar and Imam Ibn Kathir explain: *As-Sara'ir* are the deeply buried secrets of the heart—intentions behind prayers, fasts, financial dealings, hidden lusts, secret grudges, and private sins. In this world, judgments are based on outward appearances; but on that Day, the internal secrets of the soul will be put on public trial. The clean heart will shine with radiance; the corrupted heart will be exposed.
* **The Cosmic Knocking of the Pulsar (Verse 1–3):** Modern astrophysics discovered neutron stars (pulsars) that emit regular, rapid radio pulses like someone knocking (*Tariq*) on a celestial door, and whose ultra-dense radiation pierces (*Thaqib*) through interstellar space.
---

## 7. Important Supporting Hadiths
1. **Recitation of Surah At-Tariq in Night Prayers:**  
   *Sunan an-Nasa'i (Hadith 998), Sahih:*  
   "Jabir ibn Samurah reported that the Messenger of Allah ﷺ used to recite in Zuhr and 'Asr prayers: *Was-Sama'i wat-Tariq* (86) and *Was-Sama'i Dhatil-Buruj* (85)."
2. **Secret Deeds on the Day of Judgment:**  
   *Sunan Ibn Majah (Hadith 4245), Sahih:*  
   "The Prophet ﷺ warned: 'I certainly know people from my Ummah who will come on the Day of Resurrection with good deeds like white mountains of Tihamah, but Allah will make them scattered dust because when they were alone in private, they violated the sacred prohibitions of Allah.'"
---

## 8. Key Concepts
1. **At-Tariq / An-Najm ath-Thaqib (The Piercing Knocker):** The cosmic star whose brilliance and radiation pierces through absolute darkness.
2. **Tubla as-Sara'ir (The Examination of Secrets):** The eschatological auditing where hidden motives, private browser histories, and secret sins are laid bare.
3. **Ma'un Dafiq (The Spurting Fluid):** The reminder of human humble embryonic origins extinguishing all claims of arrogance.
4. **Qawlun Fasl (The Decisive Word):** The absolute moral gravity of the Qur'an: a serious, razor-sharp criterion, not entertainment.
---

## 9. Tadabbur (Guided Contemplation)
* **The Testing of Your Secrets (86:9):** Consider this deeply: *Yawma tubla as-sara'ir.* In this world, you can wear a tailored suit, a beautiful smile, and speak eloquent Islamic terms while your heart harbors envy, vanity, or lust. But on that Day, the physical body becomes transparent and your *Sara'ir* (hidden secrets) will be tested. What secrets are you keeping right now that would terrify you if displayed on a public billboard tomorrow? Cleanse them today through secret repentance.
* **The Qur'an is Not Entertainment (86:13–14):** Allah describes His book: *Innahu la-qawlun fasl, wa ma huwa bil-hazl* ('Indeed, it is a decisive word, and it is not a joke or amusement'). Do not treat the Qur'an as pleasant ambient background music or an aesthetic calligraphy piece on your living room wall. It is a razor-sharp sword that separates truth from delusion. Live by its decrees.
---

## 10. What This Means For Me
* I guard my private secrets and intentions, knowing they will be publicly tested on Judgment Day (*Tubla as-sara'ir*) (86:9).
* I eliminate human arrogance by remembering my humble biological origin from fluid (86:5–7).
* I take the Qur'an with utmost seriousness as a decisive criterion (*Qawlun Fasl*), refusing to treat religion as entertainment (86:13–14).

---

## 11. One Practical Action
**Perform One Secret Righteous Deed Today:**  
Counteract *Tubla as-sara'ir* by creating a righteous secret between you and Allah: Pray two rak'ahs in a locked room, give charity online where no one knows your name, or fast a voluntary day without telling anyone.

---

## 12. Reflection Questions
1. If all my private thoughts, search histories, and secrets were displayed today, would I be honored or disgraced (86:9)?
2. Do I treat the Qur'an as a decisive guide for my life (*Qawlun Fasl*) or as occasional spiritual amusement (86:13–14)?
3. How does knowing that an angel guards and records my soul (86:4) influence my private conduct?

## 13. Closing Supplication
$$\text{اللَّهُمَّ طَهِّرْ سَرَائِرَنَا كَمَا طَهَّرْتَ عَلَانِيَتَنَا ، وَاجْعَلْ سَرِيرَتَنَا خَيْرًا مِنْ عَلَانِيَتِنَا ، اللَّهُمَّ لَا تَفْضَحْنَا يَوْمَ تُبْلَى السَّرَائِرُ ، وَاجْعَلِ الْقُرْآنَ لَنَا قَوْلًا فَصْلًا وَنُورًا}$$  
*"O Allah, purify our secret inner lives just as You have purified our public demeanor, and make our inner secrets better than our public image. O Allah, do not disgrace us on the Day secrets are laid bare, and make the Qur'an for us a decisive word and guiding light. Amin."*
