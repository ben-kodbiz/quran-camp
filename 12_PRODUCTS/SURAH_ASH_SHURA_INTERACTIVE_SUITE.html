There is Nothing Like Unto Him (42:11)

Foundational axiom of Sunni creed: complete negation of likeness (Tanzih) paired with active attributes (Ithbat).

Affirming hearing and sight without anthropomorphism or denial.

Authorities: Sharh al-'Aqeedah al-Tahawiyyah • Tafsir al-Qurtubi (16/9) • Tafsir Ibn Kathir

Affection for the Prophet's Kinfolk (42:23)

Sahih al-Bukhari 4818: Ibn Abbas explains the Prophet was related to every Quraysh branch, asking for womb ties.

Asking that they maintain family affection rather than persecuting the message.

Authorities: Sahih al-Bukhari 4818 • Tafsir Ibn Kathir (7/202) • Fath al-Bari

The Ethics of Shura & Deliberative Governance (42:38)

Surah Ash-Shura places consultation between ritual prayer and charitable giving.

Despotism is incompatible with true believing character; leaders govern through consensus and collective wisdom.

Authorities: Tafsir al-Tabari (21/530) • Ahkam al-Qur'an (Al-Jassas) • Al-Bahr al-Muheet

The Modalities of Divine Inspiration (42:51–52)

Three exclusive modes: direct inspiration (Wahy), from behind a veil, or via an angelic envoy (Gabriel).

Describing the Qur'an as Roohan min amrina: breathing spiritual life into humanity.

Authorities: Sahih al-Bukhari • Tafsir Ibn Kathir (7/216) • Al-Burhan fee 'Uloom al-Qur'an
