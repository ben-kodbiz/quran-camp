# CHAPTER 75: SURAH AL-QIYAMAH (سُورَةُ القيامة)
**Classification:** Makki  
**Order in Mushaf:** 75  
**Total Verses:** 40  
**Famous Names:** Surat al-Qiyamah, La Uqsimu bi-Yawmil-Qiyamah  

---

## 1. Why This Surah Matters
Surah Al-Qiyamah is the intimate, heartbeat-by-heartbeat chronicle of human conscience and eschatology. It opens with twin solemn divine oaths: by the Day of Resurrection, and by the self-reproaching soul (*An-Nafs al-Lawwamah*—the internal moral conscience that audits one's sins). It affirms the biological miracle of fingerprint reconstitution: 'Yes, [We are] Able [even] to proportion his fingertips' (*Bananah*). It contains the divine reassurance to the Prophet ﷺ during revelation: 'Move not your tongue with it, [O Muhammad], to hasten with recitation; indeed, upon Us is its collection and its recitation' (75:16–17). It peaks with the supreme beatific vision of eternity: 'Radiant faces that Day looking at their Lord' (*Wujuhun yawma'idhin nadhirah, ila Rabbiha nadhirah*).

---

## 2. Surah Context
Revealed in early Makkah to refute polytheists who mocked physical resurrection, demanding to know how decomposed dust could ever be reconstituted. It also comforted the Prophet ﷺ who moved his lips frantically while listening to Gabriel out of fear of forgetting verses.

---

## 3. Central Theme
**The Resurrection of the Body and Fingertips, the Inner Moral Conscience (*An-Nafs al-Lawwamah*), the Gathering of the Qur'an, and the Beatific Vision of God.**

---

## 4. Key Passages
### The Conscience and the Fingertips (75:1–4)
$$\text{لَا أُقْسِمُ بِيَوْمِ الْقِيَامَةِ ۝ وَلَا أُقْسِمُ بِالنَّفْسِ اللَّوَّامَةِ ۝ أَيَحْسَبُ الْإِنسَانُ أَلَّن نَّجْمَعَ عِظَامَهُ ۝ بَلَىٰ قَادِرِينَ عَلَىٰ أَن نُّسَوِّيَ بَنَانَهُ}$$
*"I swear by the Day of Resurrection; And I swear by the self-reproaching soul: Does man think that We will not assemble his bones? Yes. [We are] Able [even] to proportion his fingertips."*

### The Divine Preservation of Revelation (75:16–19)
$$\text{لَا تُحَرِّكْ بِهِ لِسَانَكَ لِتَعْجَلَ بِهِ ۝ إِنَّ عَلَيْنَا جَمْعَهُ وَقُرْآنَهُ ۝ فَإِذَا قَرَأْنَاهُ فَاتَّبِعْ قُرْآنَهُ ۝ ثُمَّ إِنَّ عَلَيْنَا بَيَانَهُ}$$
*"Move not your tongue with it, [O Muhammad], to hasten with recitation. Indeed, upon Us is its collection and its recitation. So when We have recited it [through Gabriel], then follow its recitation. Then upon Us is its clarification."*

### The Supreme Vision of Allah's Face (75:22–23)
$$\text{وُجُوهٌ يَوْمَئِذٍ نَّاضِرَةٌ ۝ إِلَىٰ رَبِّهَا نَاظِرَةٌ}$$
*"[Some] faces, that Day, will be radiant, Looking at their Lord."*

### The Collarbones of Death (75:26–30)
$$\text{كَلَّا إِذَا بَلَغَتِ التَّرَاقِيَ ۝ وَقِيلَ مَنْ ۜ رَاقٍ ۝ وَظَنَّ أَنَّهُ الْفِرَاقُ ۝ وَالْتَفَّتِ السَّاقُ بِالسَّاقِ ۝ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ يَوْمَئِذٍ الْمَسَاقُ}$$
*"No! When the soul has reached the collar bones, And it is said, 'Who will cure [him]?' And he is certain that it is parting, And one leg is twisted with the other leg, To your Lord, that Day, will be the drive."*

---

## 5. Translation Highlights (The Clear Quran)
* *75:14–15:* 'Rather, man, against himself, will be a witness, Even if he presents his excuses.'
* *75:20–21:* 'No! But you love the immediate, And leave the Hereafter.'
* *75:36:* 'Does man think that he will be left aimless?'

---

## 6. What Classical Tafsir Explains
* **The Self-Reproaching Soul (Verse 2):** Al-Hasan al-Basri noted: 'By Allah, the true believer is always reproaching his own soul, asking: *What did I mean by those words? What did I intend by that meal? Why did I think that thought?* But the corrupt sinner marches forward without ever checking his conscience!' A healthy conscience is proof of a living heart.
* **The Miracle of the Fingertips (Verse 4):** Scholars of Quranic exegesis and modern forensics marvel at *Bananah*: Why did Allah specifically mention reconstructing the fingertips? Because in the 19th century, forensic science discovered that human fingerprints are completely unique to every individual on earth across all millennia. Reconstructing the fingertips signifies the total, microscopic restoration of exact personal identity.
* **Looking Directly at the Face of Allah (Verses 22–23):** Ahl as-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah affirm the greatest joy of Paradise: Believers will physically and directly gaze upon the glorious Face of Allah (*Ru'yatullah*) with their eyes, causing their faces to shine with radiant splendor (*Nadhirah*).
---

## 7. Important Supporting Hadiths
1. **The Revelation of Verses 16–17:**  
   *Sahih al-Bukhari (Hadith 4927) from Ibn Abbas (RA):*  
   "Ibn Abbas explained: 'The Prophet ﷺ used to endure severe strain during revelation, moving his lips quickly to memorize the words. Allah revealed: *"Move not your tongue with it to hasten... indeed upon Us is its collection and its recitation"* (75:16–17). After that, when Gabriel descended, the Prophet ﷺ listened in silence, and when Gabriel departed, the Prophet ﷺ recited it identically.'"
2. **Gazing at the Moon and Gazing at Allah:**  
   *Sahih al-Bukhari (Hadith 554) from Jarir ibn Abdillah (RA):*  
   "We were sitting with the Prophet ﷺ looking at the full moon. He said: 'Indeed, you will see your Lord with your own eyes just as you see this moon, without being crowded or having difficulty in seeing Him!'"
---

## 8. Key Concepts
1. **An-Nafs al-Lawwamah (The Self-Reproaching Soul):** The enlightened conscience that constantly audits motives, repents from slips, and strives for perfection.
2. **Taswiyat al-Banan (Fingerprint Reconstruction):** The divine power restoring the microscopic individuality of every human being at resurrection.
3. **Ru'yatullah (The Beatific Vision):** The supreme crown jewel of Paradise: gazing upon the majestic Face of the Lord of the worlds.
4. **Bulugh at-Taraqi (The Reaching of the Collarbones):** The final agonizing physical stage of dying when the soul exits the body through the throat.
---

## 9. Tadabbur (Guided Contemplation)
* **Man is an Eyewitness Against Himself (75:14–15):** You can fool your boss, your spouse, your community, and your social media followers with polished rationalizations and articulate excuses. But Allah says: *Balil-insanu 'ala nafsihi basirah, wa law alqa ma'adhirah* ('Rather, man is a clear witness against his own soul, even if he presents excuses'). Deep down, in the quiet of your conscience, you know whether you were sincere or pretending. Drop the excuses before Allah.
* **The Leg Twisted with the Leg (75:29):** Picture the final moments described in verse 29: *Wal-taffatis-saqu bis-saq.* The dying person's legs twist together in the agony of death, or the hardships of leaving this world twist with the arrival of the next world. You will leave behind all your property, your clothes, and your titles. To your Lord, that Day, will be the drive. What have you prepared for the journey?
---

## 10. What This Means For Me
* I cultivate an active, self-reproaching conscience (*Nafs Lawwamah*), auditing my daily intentions before sleeping (75:2).
* I drop all defensive excuses and rationalizations, knowing I am an eyewitness against my own soul (75:14).
* I live my life yearning for the supreme prize of Paradise: gazing upon the radiant Face of Allah (75:22–23).

---

## 11. One Practical Action
**Engage in a 5-Minute 'Muhasabah' Tonight:**  
Before closing your eyes to sleep, act on *An-Nafs al-Lawwamah*: Audit your day. Ask: 'What did I speak today that displeased Allah? What prayer did I rush?' Repent sincerely for every slip before sleeping.

---

## 12. Reflection Questions
1. Is my conscience alive as a self-reproaching soul (*Nafs Lawwamah*) (75:2), or has it become numb to sin?
2. What excuses am I making to justify sins or neglected duties, knowing that I am a witness against myself (75:14–15)?
3. How does the promise of seeing Allah's radiant Face (75:22–23) motivate me to purify my heart?

## 13. Closing Supplication
$$\text{اللَّهُمَّ إِنَّا نَسْأَلُكَ لَذَّةَ النَّظَرِ إِلَىٰ وَجْهِكَ الْكَرِيمِ ، وَالشَّوْقَ إِلَىٰ لِقَائِكَ ، فِي غَيْرِ ضَرَّاءَ مُضِرَّةٍ وَلَا فِتْنَةٍ مُضِلَّةٍ ، اللَّهُمَّ اجْعَلْ نُفُوسَنَا لَوَّامَةً مُخْبِتَةً مُنِيبَةً}$$  
*"O Allah, we ask You for the delight of gazing upon Your Noble Face, and the yearning to meet You, without harmful hardship or misleading trial. O Allah, make our souls self-reproaching, humble, and ever-returning to You. Amin."*
