# CHAPTER 60: SURAH AL-MUMTAHANAH (سُورَةُ الممتحنة)
**Classification:** Madani  
**Order in Mushaf:** 60  
**Total Verses:** 13  
**Famous Names:** Surat al-Mumtahanah, Surat al-Imtihan  

---

## 1. Why This Surah Matters
Surah Al-Mumtahanah is the constitutional charter of principled foreign policy, national security, and interfaith ethics. It addresses the dramatic treason incident of Hatib ibn Abi Balta'ah, a veteran of Badr who attempted to secretly dispatch a letter warning the Quraysh of the Prophet's ﷺ imminent march to liberate Makkah. While severely reprimanding the leak, the surah articulates the timeless, compassionate foundation of Muslim relations with non-Muslims in verse 8: 'Allah does not forbid you from those who do not fight you because of religion and do not expel you from your homes—from being righteous toward them and acting justly toward them. Indeed, Allah loves those who act justly' (*An tabarruhum wa tuqsitu ilayhim*). It holds up the Abrahamic archetype of moral courage (60:4) and concludes with the historic pledge of allegiance for believing women.

---

## 2. Surah Context
Revealed in Madinah in 8 AH just prior to the Conquest of Makkah (*Fath Makkah*). Hatib sent a courier woman with a secret letter to protect his vulnerable children in Makkah. The Prophet ﷺ intercepted the letter via Ali and al-Miqdad at Rawdat Khakh, modeling supreme mercy and contextual justice.

---

## 3. Central Theme
**National Security and Spiritual Loyalty (*Al-Wala'*), the Golden Rule of Interfaith Justice and Kindness (60:8), and the Examined Believing Women.**

---

## 4. Key Passages
### The Charter of Interfaith Justice and Kindness (60:8–9)
$$\text{لَّا يَنْهَاكُمُ اللَّهُ عَنِ الَّذِينَ لَمْ يُقَاتِلُوكُمْ فِي الدِّينِ وَلَمْ يُخْرِجُوكُم مِّن دِيَارِكُمْ أَن تَبَرُّوهُمْ وَتُقْسِطُوا إِلَيْهِمْ ۚ إِنَّ اللَّهَ يُحِبُّ الْمُقْسِطِينَ ۝ إِنَّمَا يَنْهَاكُمُ اللَّهُ عَنِ الَّذِينَ قَاتَلُوكُمْ فِي الدِّينِ وَأَخْرَجُوكُم مِّن دِيَارِكُمْ وَظَاهَرُوا عَلَىٰ إِخْرَاجِكُمْ أَن تَوَلَّوْهُمْ ۚ وَمَن يَتَوَلَّهُمْ فَأُولَٰئِكَ هُمُ الظَّالِمُونَ}$$
*"Allah does not forbid you from those who do not fight you because of religion and do not expel you from your homes—from being righteous toward them and acting justly toward them. Indeed, Allah loves those who act justly. Allah only forbids you from those who fight you because of religion and expel you from your homes and aid in your expulsion—[forbids] that you make allies of them. And whoever makes allies of them, then it is those who are the wrongdoers."*

### The Sublime Abrahamic Model (60:4)
$$\text{قَدْ كَانَتْ لَكُمْ أُسْوَةٌ حَسَنَةٌ فِي إِبْرَاهِيمَ وَالَّذِينَ مَعَهُ إِذْ قَالُوا لِقَوْمِهِمْ إِنَّا بُرَآءُ مِنكُمْ وَمِمَّا تَعْبُدُونَ مِن دُونِ اللَّهِ}$$
*"There has already been for you an excellent pattern in Abraham and those with him, when they said to their people, 'Indeed, we are disassociated from you and from whatever you worship other than Allah...'"*

### The Historic Pledge of Believing Women (60:12)
$$\text{يَا أَيُّهَا النَّبِيُّ إِذَا جَاءَكَ الْمُؤْمِنَاتُ يُبَايِعْنَكَ عَلَىٰ أَن لَّا يُشْرِكْنَ بِاللَّهِ شَيْئًا وَلَا يَسْرِقْنَ وَلَا يَزْنِينَ وَلَا يَقْتُلْنَ أَوْلَادَهُنَّ وَلَا يَأْتِينَ بِبُهْتَانٍ... فَبَايِعْهُنَّ وَاسْتَغْفِرْ لَهُنَّ اللَّهَ}$$
*"O Prophet, when the believing women come to you pledging to you that they will not associate anything with Allah, nor will they steal, nor will they commit unlawful sexual intercourse, nor will they kill their children, nor will they bring forth a slander... then accept their pledge and ask forgiveness for them of Allah."*

## 5. Translation Highlights (The Clear Quran)
* *60:3:* 'Never will your relatives or your children benefit you on the Day of Resurrection; He will judge between you. And Allah, of what you do, is Seeing.'
* *60:7:* 'Perhaps Allah will put, between you and those to whom you have been enemies among them, affection. And Allah is Competent, and Allah is Forgiving and Merciful.'
* *60:13:* 'O you who have believed, do not make allies of a people with whom Allah has become angry...'

---

## 6. What Classical Tafsir Explains
* **The Balance of Birr and Qist (Verse 8):** Imam Ibn Kathir and Al-Qurtubi highlight the exquisite vocabulary used in verse 8: Allah uses the word *Tabarruhum* (from *Birr*—the exact same word used for dutiful devotion to parents: *Birr al-Walidayn*!) and *Tuqsitu ilayhim* (from *Qist*—scrupulous, objective justice). This permanently refutes the claim that Muslims must be hostile to non-Muslims; so long as people are peaceful and do not wage warfare, Muslims are religiously commanded to treat them with active benevolence, gifts, and fairness.
* **The Prophet's Mercy to Hatib ibn Abi Balta'ah:** When Umar drew his sword and said: 'O Messenger of Allah, let me strike the neck of this hypocrite!', the Prophet ﷺ replied with profound emotional intelligence: 'He fought at Badr, O Umar! And what do you know, perhaps Allah looked at the people of Badr and said: Do whatever you wish, for I have forgiven you!' Umar wept and sheared his anger.

## 7. Important Supporting Hadiths
1. **Kindness to Non-Muslim Relatives:**  
   *Sahih al-Bukhari (Hadith 2620) from Asma' bint Abi Bakr (RA):*  
   "Asma' said: 'My mother came to visit me in Madinah while she was still a polytheist. I asked the Prophet ﷺ: "My mother has come and she desires my company; should I maintain good relations with her?" The Prophet ﷺ replied: *Yes, maintain good relations with your mother!* Regarding this, Allah revealed: *"Allah does not forbid you from those who do not fight you..."* (60:8).'"
2. **The Story of Hatib's Letter:**  
   *Sahih al-Bukhari (Hadith 4890) from Ali ibn Abi Talib (RA):*  
   "Ali narrated the full interception of the secret letter sent by Hatib ibn Abi Balta'ah at Rawdat Khakh, leading to the revelation of Surah Al-Mumtahanah."
---

## 8. Key Concepts
1. **Birr wa Qist (Benevolence and Justice):** The mandatory ethical posture of kindness, charity, and fair dealing toward peaceful non-Muslim neighbors and citizens.
2. **Fiqh al-Wala' wal-Bara' (Spiritual Loyalty and Disassociation):** Holding ultimate theological loyalty to Allah while treating fellow human beings with civility and equity.
3. **Bay'at an-Nisa' (The Women's Moral Covenant):** The foundational political and moral recognition of women's independent agency and citizenship in the Islamic state.
4. **Ahlu Badr (The Veterans of Badr):** The elite generation of companions granted divine forgiveness due to their unshakeable historic sacrifice.
---

## 9. Tadabbur (Guided Contemplation)
* **The Prophetic Etiquette Toward Mistakes (60:1):** Look at how the Prophet ﷺ handled Hatib's catastrophic treason: He did not publicly execute him or cancel him. He asked: *'What led you to do this, O Hatib?'* He gave him a fair trial, listened to his emotional vulnerability about his unprotected children, and recognized his past twenty years of sacrifice at Badr. True leaders evaluate human beings comprehensively, not by their worst single moment.
* **Kindness to Non-Muslim Neighbors (60:8):** Notice that Allah did not say 'You may tolerate them.' He commanded *An tabarruhum*—to show them *Birr* (proactive love, warm food, assistance, and genuine care). Your non-Muslim colleagues, neighbors, and relatives are entitled to your highest ethical character, honesty, and kindness. Let your character be their gateway to Islam.
---

## 10. What This Means For Me
* I treat peaceful non-Muslim neighbors, colleagues, and relatives with proactive benevolence (*Birr*) and scrupulous justice (60:8).
* I evaluate fellow believers with compassion and context, remembering their past sacrifices rather than canceling them for a mistake.
* I place my ultimate loyalty with Allah and the believers, refusing to compromise Islamic principles for worldly approval.

## 11. One Practical Action
**Perform an Act of 'Birr' for a Non-Muslim Neighbor:**  
Act directly upon verse 60:8: Bake or purchase a treat, write a kind note, or perform a helpful favor for a non-Muslim neighbor or coworker today, embodying the Quranic ethic of *Tabarruhum*.

## 13. Closing Supplication
$$\text{رَبَّنَا لَا تَجْعَلْنَا فِتْنَةً لِّلَّذِينَ كَفَرُوا وَاغْفِرْ لَنَا رَبَّنَا ۖ إِنَّكَ أَنتَ الْعَزِيزُ الْحَكِيمُ ، اللَّهُمَّ ارْزُقْنَا الْعَدْلَ وَالْبِرَّ مَعَ عِبَادِكَ ، وَثَبِّتْ قُلُوبَنَا عَلَىٰ وَلَائِكَ وَوَلَاءِ رَسُولِكَ}$$  
*"Our Lord, make us not [objects of] torment for the disbelievers and forgive us, our Lord. Indeed, it is You who are the Exalted in Might, the Wise. O Allah, grant us justice and benevolence toward Your creation, and anchor our hearts upon loyalty to You and Your Messenger. Amin."*
