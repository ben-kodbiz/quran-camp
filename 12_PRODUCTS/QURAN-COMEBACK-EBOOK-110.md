# CHAPTER 110: SURAH AN-NASR (سُورَةُ النصر)
**Classification:** Madani  
**Order in Mushaf:** 110  
**Total Verses:** 3  
**Famous Names:** Surat an-Nasr, Idha Ja'a Nasrullah, Surat at-Tawdi'  

---

## 1. Why This Surah Matters
Surah An-Nasr is the final complete Surah revealed in the Qur'anic canon, representing the triumphant crescendo and bittersweet farewell (*At-Tawdi'*) of the Prophetic mission. In three majestic verses, Allah announces the arrival of ultimate Divine Support (*Nasrullah*), the bloodless Conquest of Makkah (*Al-Fath*), and the historic sight of entire tribes and nations entering the religion of Allah in multitudes (*Afwaja*). Yet, in profound contrast to worldly conquerors who celebrate victories with parades, arrogance, and revenge, the Prophet ﷺ is instructed in the true spiritual posture of Islamic triumph: Glorification (*Tasbih*), Praise (*Tahmid*), and deep Seeking of Forgiveness (*Istighfar*). It was the divine signal that Muhammad's ﷺ earthly mission was fulfilled, and his departure to the Highest Companion was at hand.

---

## 2. Surah Context
Revealed in Mina during the Farewell Pilgrimage (or shortly before the Conquest of Makkah). Deep companions like Ibn 'Abbas (RA) and 'Umar (RA) wept upon hearing it, realizing it was the eulogy announcing the approaching death of the Messenger of Allah ﷺ.

---

## 3. Central Theme
**The Triumphant Fulfillment of the Prophetic Mission, the Spiritual Etiquette of Victory, and the Divine Signal of the Prophet's Approaching Return to Allah.**

---

## 4. Key Passages
### The Advent of Victory and Multitudes (110:1–2)
$$\text{إِذَا جَاءَ نَصْرُ اللَّهِ وَالْفَتْحُ ۝ وَرَأَيْتَ النَّاسَ يَدْخُلُونَ فِي دِينِ اللَّهِ أَفْوَاجًا}$$
*"When the victory of Allah has come and the conquest, And you see the people entering into the religion of Allah in multitudes..."*

### The Posture of Spiritual Humility (110:3)
$$\text{فَسَبِّحْ بِحَمْدِ رَبِّكَ وَاسْتَغْفِرْهُ ۚ إِنَّهُ كَانَ تَوَّابًا}$$
*"Then exalt [Him] with praise of your Lord and ask forgiveness of Him. Indeed, He is ever Accepting of repentance."*

---

## 5. Translation Highlights (The Clear Quran)
* *110:1:* 'When Allah's ultimate help arrives and the conquest (*Al-Fath*) is achieved.'
* *110:2:* 'And you see crowds entering God's religion in droves (*Afwaja*).'
* *110:3:* 'Then glorify the praises of your Lord and seek His forgiveness; He is always ready to accept repentance (*Innahu kana tawwaba*).'

---

## 6. What Classical Tafsir Explains
* **The Insight of Ibn 'Abbas and 'Umar:** Imam Ibn Kathir records that 'Umar (RA) gathered senior veterans of Badr and brought the young Ibn 'Abbas with them. 'Umar asked the elders: 'What do you say about Allah's saying: *Idha ja'a nasrullahi wal fath*?' They said: 'He commanded us to praise Him and ask forgiveness when He gives us victory.' 'Umar then asked Ibn 'Abbas: 'Do you say the same?' He said: 'No! It is the announcement of the lifespan of the Messenger of Allah ﷺ; Allah informed him: When victory and the conquest come, that is the sign of your approaching death.' 'Umar said: 'I know nothing from it except what you have said.'
* **Why Ask Forgiveness at the Pinnacle of Triumph? (Verse 3):** As-Sa'di explains that asking forgiveness (*Istighfar*) at the end of acts of worship (like prayer, Hajj, and here, the entire 23-year prophetic mission) guards against pride, self-satisfaction, and arrogance. It acknowledges that no matter how magnificent our efforts, our gratitude and worship can never truly match Allah's infinite majesty.
* **Entering in Droves (*Afwaja*):** Before the Conquest of Makkah, individuals embraced Islam in ones and twos under persecution. After Makkah was opened, delegations from all corners of Arabia arrived embracing Islam in hundreds and thousands daily.
---

## 7. Important Supporting Hadiths
1. **The Prophet's Devotion in Ruku' and Sujud:**  
   *Sahih al-Bukhari (Hadith 817) & Sahih Muslim (Hadith 484) from 'A'ishah (RA):*  
   "The Messenger of Allah ﷺ used to say frequently in his bowing and prostrating after this Surah was revealed: *Subhanakallahumma Rabbana wa bihamdika, Allahummaghfirli* (Glory be to You, O Allah, our Lord, and all praise; O Allah, forgive me), acting upon the instruction of the Qur'an."
2. **The Prophet Entering Makkah in Humility:**  
   *Sahih al-Bukhari (Hadith 4287):*  
   "On the day of the Conquest of Makkah, the Messenger of Allah ﷺ entered the city riding his camel with his head bowed so low out of humility and gratitude to Allah that his beard almost touched the saddle."
---

## 8. Key Concepts
1. **Nasrullah (The Divine Support):** Victory originates strictly from Allah, not from human brilliance or military might.
2. **Al-Fath (The Great Opening):** The moral and spiritual conquest of Makkah, establishing Tawheed forever.
3. **Afwajan (Crowds in Droves):** The geometric expansion of faith once hypocrisy and oppression are dismantled.
4. **Tasbih & Istighfar at the Finish Line:** Concluding major achievements with self-effacing praise and repentance instead of self-glorification.
---

## 9. Tadabbur (Guided Contemplation)
* **The Etiquette of Success:** When you achieve a major milestone (graduating, landing a dream job, buying a home, completing a project), is your first reaction to post boastful photos, or do you fall into Sajdah of gratitude and say: *Astaghfirullah*?
* **The Bitter Sweetness of Completion:** Reflect on Ibn 'Abbas realizing that the peak of earthly victory signaled the Prophet's departure. Every beginning has an end; every worldly achievement reminds us that our true home is the Hereafter.
* **Emulating the Prophetic Sajdah:** Learn the du'a the Prophet ﷺ recited obsessively after this Surah was revealed: *Subhanakallahumma Rabbana wa bihamdika, Allahummaghfirli*.
---

## 10. What This Means For Me
* All success in my life is 100% *Nasrullah* (Allah's help); I have zero justification for boasting or arrogance.
* I must conclude every good deed—my prayer, my fast, my charitable work—with sincere *Istighfar* for my shortcomings.
* Humility at the moment of triumph is the hallmark of genuine prophetic character.

---

## 11. One Practical Action
**The Bowing and Prostration Supplication:**  
Incorporate the prophetic supplication from Surah An-Nasr into all your prayers starting today: in every Ruku' and every Sujud, add: *Subhanakallahumma Rabbana wa bihamdika, Allahummaghfirli*. Recite it with deep awareness of your absolute need for Allah's pardon.

## 12. Reflection Questions
1. How does the Islamic etiquette of victory differ from modern celebrations of power and triumph?
2. Why did Allah combine praise (*Hamd*) with seeking forgiveness (*Istighfar*) in verse 3?
3. What major project or phase in your life is coming to a close, and how can you seal it with spiritual humility?
