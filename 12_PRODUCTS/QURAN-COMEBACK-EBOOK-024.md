# CHAPTER 24: SURAH AN-NUR (سُورَةُ النور)
**Classification:** Madani  
**Order in Mushaf:** 24  
**Total Verses:** 64  
**Famous Names:** Surat an-Nur (The Chapter of Light)  

---

## 1. Why This Surah Matters
Surah An-Nur is the divine fortress of societal purity and the spiritual sanctuary of divine illumination. It addresses the sanctity of human honor, marriage, modesty, and family life. Containing the legal rulings on slander (*Qadhf*), lowering the gaze, the rules of hijab, and home privacy, it centers around the Incident of the Slander (*Hadith al-Ifk*) against the Mother of the Believers, Aisha (RA). At its very core sits one of the most sublime and celebrated passages in all of scripture: Ayat an-Nur (24:35), where Allah compares His divine light to a lamp shining inside a crystal niche, fueled by the oil of a blessed olive tree.

---

## 2. Surah Context
Revealed in Madinah around 5–6 AH after the Expedition of Banu al-Mustaliq. Hypocrites led by Abdullah ibn Ubayy weaponized malicious rumors against Aisha (RA) to fracture the Muslim community and wound the Prophet ﷺ. Surah An-Nur descended directly from above the seven heavens to vindicate her purity and lay down ironclad legal protections against defamation and sexual immorality.

---

## 3. Central Theme
**The Light of Allah (*Nurullah*), the Sanctity of Personal Honor, Societal Modesty, and the Eradication of Slander.**

---

## 4. Key Passages
### The Parable of the Divine Light (24:35)
$$\text{اللَّهُ نُورُ السَّمَاوَاتِ وَالْأَرْضِ ۚ مَثَلُ نُورِهِ كَمِشْكَاةٍ فِيهَا مِصْبَاحٌ ۖ الْمِصْبَاحُ فِي زُجَاجَةٍ ۖ الزُّجَاجَةُ كَأَنَّهَا كَوْكَبٌ دُرِّيٌّ يُوقَدُ مِن شَجَرَةٍ مُّبَارَكَةٍ زَيْتُونَةٍ لَّا شَرْقِيَّةٍ وَلَا غَرْبِيَّةٍ يَكَادُ زَيْتُهَا يُضِيءُ وَلَوْ لَمْ تَمْسَسْهُ نَارٌ ۚ نُّورٌ عَلَىٰ نُورٍ ۗ يَهْدِي اللَّهُ لِنُورِهِ مَن يَشَاءُ}$$
*"Allah is the Light of the heavens and the earth. The example of His light is like a niche within which is a lamp, the lamp is within glass, the glass as if it were a pearly [white] star lit from [the oil of] a blessed olive tree, neither of the east nor of the west, whose oil would almost glow even if untouched by fire. Light upon light. Allah guides to His light whom He wills."*

### Lowering the Gaze and Preserving Purity (24:30–31)
$$\text{قُل لِّلْمُؤْمِنِينَ يَغُضُّوا مِنْ أَبْصَارِهِمْ وَيَحْفَظُوا فُرُوجَهُمْ ۚ ذَٰلِكَ أَزْكَىٰ لَهُمْ ۗ إِنَّ اللَّهَ خَبِيرٌ بِمَا يَصْنَعُونَ ۝ وَقُل لِّلْمُؤْمِنَاتِ يَغْضُضْنَ مِنْ أَبْصَارِهِنَّ وَيَحْفَظْنَ فُرُوجَهُنَّ}$$
*"Tell the believing men to lower their gaze and guard their private parts. That is purer for them. Indeed, Allah is Acquainted with what they do. And tell the believing women to lower their gaze and guard their private parts..."*

### The Absolute Condemnation of Slander (24:19)
$$\text{إِنَّ الَّذِينَ يُحِبُّونَ أَن تَشِيعَ الْفَاحِشَةُ فِي الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا لَهُمْ عَذَابٌ أَلِيمٌ فِي الدُّنْيَا وَالْآخِرَةِ ۚ وَاللَّهُ يَعْلَمُ وَأَنتُمْ لَا تَعْلَمُونَ}$$
*"Indeed, those who like that immorality should be spread [or publicized] among those who believe will have a painful punishment in this world and the Hereafter. And Allah knows and you do not know."*

* *24:37:* '[Are] men whom neither commerce nor sale distracts from the remembrance of Allah and performance of prayer...'

## 6. What Classical Tafsir Explains
* **The Explanation of Ayat an-Nur (Verse 35):** Ubayy ibn Ka'b (RA) and Ibn Abbas (RA) explained that the 'niche' (*Mishkat*) represents the ribcage of the believer, the 'glass' (*Zujajah*) is the pure heart, and the 'lamp' (*Misbah*) is the divine revelation and faith ignited within. When the natural pure fitrah encounters divine revelation, it produces *Nur 'ala Nur* (Light upon Light), radiating moral clarity and peace.
* **The Forgiveness of Abu Bakr (Verse 22):** When Mistah ibn Uthathah (a relative whom Abu Bakr financially supported) participated in circulating the slander against Aisha, Abu Bakr swore never to give him charity again. Allah revealed verse 22: *'Would you not like that Allah should forgive you?'* Hearing this, Abu Bakr wept and said, 'By Allah, yes, I wish that Allah forgive me!' and restored the stipend immediately.

## 7. Important Supporting Hadiths
1. **The Guarantees of Paradise:**  
   *Sahih al-Bukhari (Hadith 6474) from Sahl ibn Sa'd (RA):*  
   "The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: 'Whoever guarantees for me what is between his two jawbones (his tongue) and what is between his legs (his private parts), I guarantee for him Paradise.'"
2. **The Golden Rule of Preserving Honor:**  
   *Sahih Muslim (Hadith 2586) from Abu Hurayrah (RA):*  
   "The Prophet ﷺ said: 'The Muslim is the brother of a Muslim: he does not betray him, lie to him, or humiliate him. All of a Muslim is sacred to another Muslim: his honor, his property, and his blood.'"
---

## 8. Key Concepts
1. **Nur 'ala Nur (Light upon Light):** The harmony between pure human intuition (*Fitrah*) and divine revelation (*Wahy*).
2. **Ghad al-Basar (Lowering the Gaze):** The conscious spiritual discipline of diverting eyes from prohibited and lustful imagery to protect the soul's light.
3. **Hadith al-Ifk (The Great Slander):** The historic trial of slander against Aisha (RA) that established universal legal protections for reputation.
4. **Tahsin al-Mujtama' (Societal Fortification):** Preventative ethical guidelines including home visitation permissions, privacy hours, and modesty laws.
---

## 9. Tadabbur (Guided Contemplation)
* **The Step-by-Step Trap of Shaytan (24:21):** Notice that Allah warns: *Do not follow the footsteps of Satan (Khutuwat ash-Shaytan).* Shaytan does not ask someone to commit a major sin immediately. He starts with a glance, a casual text, an unfiltered conversation, an excuse to meet, until one step leads naturally to the next. Modesty is protecting the perimeter before the castle is breached.
* **Would You Not Love That Allah Forgives You? (24:22):** When someone harms your reputation or betrays your kindness, your ego screams for revenge. But look at Abu Bakr: the slanderers targeted his beloved daughter and his Prophet ﷺ, yet when Allah asked *'Ala tuhibbuna an yaghfirallahu lakum?'*, he dissolved his grudge in an instant. Forgive people not because they deserve it, but because you desperately need Allah's forgiveness.
