# CHAPTER 15: SURAH AL-HIJR (سُورَةُ الحجر)
**Classification:** Makki  
**Order in Mushaf:** 15  
**Total Verses:** 99  
**Famous Names:** Surat al-Hijr, Surat al-Hifz (The Chapter of Preservation)  

---

## 1. Why This Surah Matters
Surah Al-Hijr is the divine citadel of preservation and reassurance. In an era when cynics and mockers attacked the message of Islam, Allah issued the timeless guarantee that has guarded the Holy Qur'an letter-by-letter across fifteen centuries: 'Indeed, it is We who sent down the Quran and indeed, We will be its guardian' (15:9). It explores the creation of Adam from clay, the breathing of the divine Ruh, the arrogance of Iblis, and ends with the ultimate psychological cure for an aching, constricted chest: prostration, prayer, and certainty until death.

---

## 2. Surah Context
Revealed in Makkah during a period of relentless mockery and public slander against the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ. The Makkan aristocrats openly called him insane (15:6) and demanded to see descending angels. Surah Al-Hijr descended to console his wounded heart, assuring him that previous messengers faced the exact same mockery, and that the ultimate victory belongs to truth.

---

## 3. Central Theme
**The Eternal Preservation of Divine Revelation (*Hifz al-Qur'an*), the Dignity of the Believer, and the Spiritual Cure for Grief and Mockery.**

---

## 4. Key Passages
1. **The Absolute Divine Guarantee of Preservation (15:9):**  
   $$\text{إِنَّا نَحْنُ نَزَّلْنَا الذِّكْرَ وَإِنَّا لَهُ لَحَافِظُونَ}$$  
   *"Indeed, it is We who sent down the Quran and indeed, We will be its guardian."*
2. **The Creation of Adam and the Divine Breath (15:28–29):**  
   $$\text{وَإِذْ قَالَ رَبُّكَ لِلْمَلَائِكَةِ إِنِّي خَالِقٌ بَشَرًا مِّن صَلْصَالٍ مِّنْ حَمَإٍ مَّسْنُونٍ ۝ فَإِذَا سَوَّيْتُهُ وَنَفَخْتُ فِيهِ مِن رُّوحِي فَقَعُوا لَهُ سَاجِدِينَ}$$  
   *"And [mention] when your Lord said to the angels, 'I will create a human being out of clay from an altered black mud. So when I have proportioned him and breathed into him of My [created] soul, then fall down in prostration to him.'"*
3. **The Divine Cure for a Constricted Heart (15:97–99):**  
   $$\text{وَلَقَدْ نَعْلَمُ أَنَّكَ يَضِيقُ صَدْرُكَ بِمَا يَقُولُونَ ۝ فَسَبِّحْ بِحَمْدِ رَبِّكَ وَكُن مِّنَ السَّاجِدِينَ ۝ وَاعْبُدْ رَبَّكَ حَتَّىٰ يَأْتِيَكَ الْيَقِينُ}$$  
   *"And We already know that your chest is constricted by what they say. So glorify the praises of your Lord and be of those who prostrate. And worship your Lord until there comes to you the certainty [death]."*

## 5. Translation Highlights (The Clear Quran)
* *15:21:* 'And there is not a thing but that with Us are its depositories, and We do not send it down except according to a known measure.'
* *15:49:* '[O Muhammad], inform My servants that I am the Forgiving, the Merciful.'
* *15:85:* '...So overlook [their faults] with gracious forgiveness.'

---

## 6. What Classical Tafsir Explains
* **The Immutability of the Qur'an (Verse 9):** Ibn Kathir notes that while previous scriptures (the Torah and Injeel) were entrusted to human custodians who altered and lost parts of them (per Surah 5:44), Allah Himself took personal, divine responsibility for preserving the Qur'an. Not a single vowel, letter, or word has been altered or lost across human history.
* **The Therapy of Prostration (Verses 97–99):** Imam al-Qurtubi and Ibn Kathir highlight that Allah acknowledges the physical and psychological toll that hurtful, mocking words inflict on the human chest (*yadiqu sadruk*). The divine prescription given to the Prophet ﷺ is not counter-insults, social withdrawal, or anxiety; it is falling in prostration (*Sujud*) and exalting Allah (*Tasbih*), because connecting the forehead to the dust restores spiritual equilibrium.

---

## 7. Important Supporting Hadiths
1. **The Miracle of the Qur'an:**  
   *Sahih al-Bukhari (Hadith 4981) from Abu Hurayrah (RA):*  
   "The Prophet ﷺ said: 'There was no prophet among the prophets except that he was given signs because of which people believed. But that which I have been given is divine inspiration (the Qur'an) which Allah has revealed to me. So I hope that I will have the most followers on the Day of Resurrection.'"
2. **Prostration in Moments of Grief:**  
   *Sunan Abi Dawud (Hadith 1319), Hasan:*  
   "Hudhayfah (RA) narrated: 'Whenever a matter grieved or distressed the Prophet ﷺ, he would immediately hasten to prayer.'"

---

## 8. Key Concepts
1. **Hifz al-Qur'an (Divine Preservation):** The supernatural protection ensuring that the Qur'anic text remains preserved identically in memory and manuscript until the end of time.
2. **Nafkh ar-Ruh (The Divine Breath):** The breathing of the soul into the clay form of Adam, giving humanity sacred spiritual dignity.
3. **As-Safh al-Jamil (Gracious Pardon):** Overlooking insults and mistreatment without lingering bitterness, vindictiveness, or public drama.
4. **Al-Yaqin (The Certainty):** A classical Quranic term denoting death, the ultimate threshold into eternal reality.

---

## 9. Tadabbur (Guided Contemplation)
* **When Words Hurt Your Chest (15:97):** Notice the tender compassion of Allah: He does not tell the Prophet ﷺ, 'Stop feeling sensitive.' He says: *We know that your chest is constricted by what they say.* Allah validates the human pain of cruel words, betrayal, and slander. But then He hands you the medicine: Drop to your knees. Put your forehead on the floor. In prostration, your heart is closer to Allah than anywhere else on earth. Let the praise of Allah absorb the toxicity of human speech.
* **The Treasury of All Things (15:21):** Allah tells you: Everything you desire—peace, wealth, health, clarity, companionship—exists in infinite, inexhaustible storehouses (*Khaza'in*) with Him. He withholds or releases it with precision (*bi-qadarin ma'lum*). If a door hasn't opened yet, it is not because the storehouse is empty; it is because the divine wisdom knows the exact hour when its arrival will nourish your soul rather than ruin it.

---

## 10. What This Means For Me
* When I face hurtful criticism, online hostility, or betrayal, I immediately perform two rak'ahs of prayer and prostrate before Allah.
* I recite and memorize the Qur'an with profound confidence, knowing I am holding the only book on earth personally guaranteed by the Creator.
* I practice *As-Safh al-Jamil* by letting go of petty grudges and choosing graceful forgiveness over constant argument.

---

## 11. One Practical Action
**Engage in a 5-Minute 'Sajdah of Relief' Today:**  
When your day feels heavy or exhausting, make fresh wudu, turn to the Qiblah, and place your forehead on the floor in a long, unhurried prostration outside of obligatory prayers. Recite *Subhana Rabbiyal A'la* slowly, pour out your worries, and let your chest expand.

---

## 12. Reflection Questions
1. When people speak ill of me or misunderstand my intentions, is my instinctive reaction angry defense or falling in prayer before Allah (15:97–98)?
2. How does realizing that the repositories of all blessings belong to Allah (15:21) change the way I ask Him for my worldly and spiritual needs?
3. Am I treating the Holy Qur'an with the awe deserved by a text personally guarded by the Lord of the worlds (15:9)?

$$\text{اللَّهُمَّ اجْعَلْنَا مِنْ أَهْلِ الْقُرْآنِ الَّذِينَ هُمْ أَهْلُكَ وَخَاصَّتُكَ ، اللَّهُمَّ اشْرَحْ صُدُورَنَا بِذِكْرِكَ وَاجْعَلْنَا مِنَ السَّاجِدِينَ الْمُخْبِتِينَ}$$  
*"O Allah, make us of the people of the Qur'an who are Your special people and chosen servants. O Allah, expand our chests with Your remembrance and make us among the humble who prostrate to You until certainty reaches us. Amin."*
