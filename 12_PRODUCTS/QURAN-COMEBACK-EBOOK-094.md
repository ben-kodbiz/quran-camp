### *Understand. Reflect. Live.*

# CHAPTER 94: SURAH ASH-SHARH (سُورَةُ الشرح)
**Classification:** Makki  
**Order in Mushaf:** 94  
**Total Verses:** 8  
**Famous Names:** Surat ash-Sharh, Al-Inshirah, Alam Nashrah  

---

## 1. Why This Surah Matters
Surah Ash-Sharh is the divine breath of fresh air that lifts crushing weights off the human chest. Following immediately upon Surah Ad-Duha, it opens with the tender rhetorical question: 'Did We not expand for you, [O Muhammad], your breast? And We removed from you your burden which had weighed heavily upon your back' (94:1–3). It establishes the glorious elevation of the Prophet's name across the cosmos (*Wa rafa'na laka dhikrak*), forever paired with Allah's name in the Adhan, Tashahhud, and Iqamah. It reveals the golden mathematical law of divine relief, repeated twice for absolute emphasis: 'Indeed, with hardship comes ease; indeed, with hardship comes ease' (*Fa-inna ma'al-'usri yusra*). It closes with the rhythm of purposeful life: when you finish one duty, rise to the next, and direct all yearning to your Lord.

---

## 2. Surah Context
Revealed in Makkah immediately following Surah Ad-Duha to console the Prophet ﷺ amidst crushing societal hostility and the immense spiritual weight of the prophetic mission.

---

## 3. Central Theme
**The Expansion of the Chest (*Inshirah as-Sadr*), the Lifting of Crushing Burdens, the Infallible Formula of Relief, and Continuous Spiritual Dedication.**

---

## 4. Key Passages
### Expanding the Chest and Lifting the Burden (94:1–4)
$$\text{أَلَمْ نَشْرَحْ لَكَ صَدْرَكَ ۝ وَوَضَعْنَا عَنكَ وِزْرَكَ ۝ الَّذِي أَنقَضَ ظَهْرَكَ ۝ وَرَفَعْنَا لَكَ ذِكْرَكَ}$$
*"Did We not expand for you, [O Muhammad], your breast? And We removed from you your burden Which had weighed upon your back, And raised high for you your repute."*

### The Double Promise of Ease (94:5–6)
$$\text{فَإِنَّ مَعَ الْعُسْرِ يُسْرًا ۝ إِنَّ مَعَ الْعُسْرِ يُسْرًا}$$
*"For indeed, with hardship [will be] ease. Indeed, with hardship [will be] ease."*

### The Rhythm of Purposeful Life (94:7–8)
$$\text{فَإِذَا فَرَغْتَ فَانصَبْ ۝ وَإِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ فَارْغَب}$$
*"So when you have finished [your duties], then stand up [for worship]. And to your Lord direct [your] longing."*

---

## 5. Translation Highlights (The Clear Quran)
* *94:5:* 'For indeed, with hardship comes ease.'
* *94:6:* 'Indeed, with hardship comes ease.'
* *94:8:* 'And to your Lord direct your longing.'

---

## 6. What Classical Tafsir Explains
* **One Hardship Cannot Defeat Two Eases (Verses 5–6):** Ibn Abbas (RA) famously deduced the linguistic miracle of verses 5–6: In Arabic grammar, *Al-'Usr* (hardship) is definite (with *Al-*), repeated identically as one single hardship; while *Yusra* (ease) is indefinite, denoting two distinct, multiplied eases! Ibn Abbas said: 'One hardship will never defeat two eases!' Hardship is singular and temporary; divine ease is multiple and overwhelming.
* **The Elevation of His Repute (Verse 4):** Qatadah and Mujahid noted: 'Allah elevated his mention in this world and the next: No khutbah is delivered, no Shahadah is recited, no Adhan is called across the globe five times a day except that it proclaims: *Ash-hadu anna Muhammadan Rasulullah*.'
---

## 7. Important Supporting Hadiths
1. **One Hardship Cannot Defeat Two Eases:**  
   *Al-Muwatta of Imam Malik (Hasan):*  
   "Umar ibn al-Khattab (RA) wrote to Abu Ubaydah during a severe crisis: 'Whatever hardship befalls a believer, Allah will bring about relief after it, for indeed one hardship cannot defeat two eases (*Lan yaghliba 'usrun yusrayn*)!'"
2. **The Cleansing of the Prophet's Chest:**  
   *Sahih Muslim (Hadith 162) from Anas ibn Malik (RA):*  
   "Anas described the physical miracle corresponding to verse 94:1: Gabriel came to the Prophet ﷺ while he was playing with boys, split his chest, removed a black clot, washed his heart with Zamzam water in a golden basin, and sealed it back together."
---

## 8. Key Concepts
1. **Inshirah as-Sadr (The Expansion of the Chest):** The divine bestowing of vast emotional capacity, clarity, tranquility, and peace in the face of pressure.
2. **Ma'al-'Usri Yusra (Ease Accompanying Hardship):** The profound reality that ease does not merely follow hardship chronologically; it is embedded *within* the hardship itself.
3. **Raf'udh-Dhikr (The Cosmic Elevation of Repute):** The divine honoring of the Prophet Muhammad's name alongside God across the entire cosmos.
4. **Fa-idha Faraghta Fansab (The Cycle of Continuous Renewal):** The principle of active living: transitioning immediately from worldly duties into devotional worship without idle decay.
---

## 9. Tadabbur (Guided Contemplation)
* **Ease is IN the Hardship, Not Just After (94:5–6):** Notice the preposition Allah chose: He did not say *Inna BA'DA al-'usri yusra* ('Ease comes *after* hardship'). He said *Inna MA'A al-'usri yusra* ('Indeed, WITH hardship is ease!'). Ease is not waiting at the end of the tunnel; ease is walking beside you inside the tunnel! Inside every cancer battle, there is spiritual purification; inside every job loss, there is redirection; inside every heartbreak, there is nearness to God. Look for the ease that is already present.
* **Direct Your Yearning to Your Lord (94:7–8):** When you finish your work shift, your exams, or your domestic chores: *Fa-idha faraghta fansab.* Do not collapse into mindless scrolling and vegetative numbness. Rise, wash your face, stand before Allah in prayer, and pour out your heart: *Wa ila Rabbika farghab* ('And to your Lord direct all your longing'). Your soul rests not in idleness, but in connection with its Creator.
---

## 10. What This Means For Me
* Whenever my chest feels constricted by stress or anxiety, I recite Surah Ash-Sharh and ask Allah for *Inshirah* (94:1).
* I hold absolute conviction during hardships that multiple avenues of divine ease are already accompanying me (94:5–6).
* I avoid idle, wasted time: transitioning from productive daily work directly into prayer and remembrance (94:7–8).

---

## 11. One Practical Action
**Identify the 'Ease' Inside Your Current Hardship:**  
Think of the single hardest trial in your life right now. Write down two positive blessings, lessons, or spiritual eases that have accompanied this trial, claiming the promise of *Inna ma'al-'usri yusra*.

---

## 12. Reflection Questions
1. Does my chest feel constricted by worldly stress, and have I asked Allah to expand it (*Alam nashrah*) (94:1)?
2. Am I looking for the ease that is already present *within* my hardships (94:5–6)?
3. When I finish my daily work, do I direct my longing to Allah or do I waste time in heedlessness (94:7–8)?

## 13. Closing Supplication
$$\text{اللَّهُمَّ اشْرَحْ صُدُورَنَا بِالْإِيمَانِ ، وَضَعْ عَنَّا أَوْزَارَنَا الَّتِي أَنْقَضَتْ ظُهُورَنَا ، اللَّهُمَّ اجْعَلْ مَعَ عُسْرِنَا يُسْرًا ، وَإِلَيْكَ يَا رَبَّنَا رَغْبَتُنَا وَرَجَاؤُنَا}$$  
*"O Allah, expand our chests with faith, and remove from us our burdens which weigh upon our backs. O Allah, grant with our hardship ease, and to You, our Lord, is our longing and our hope. Amin."*
