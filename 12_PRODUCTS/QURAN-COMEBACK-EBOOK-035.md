# CHAPTER 35: SURAH FATIR (سُورَةُ فاطر)
**Classification:** Makki  
**Order in Mushaf:** 35  
**Total Verses:** 45  
**Famous Names:** Surat Fatir, Surat al-Mala'ikah (The Angels)  

---

## 1. Why This Surah Matters
Surah Fatir is the breathtaking hymn of divine origination and human dependency. It begins by unveiling the Creator as *Fatir as-Samawati wal-Ard* (The Originator of the heavens and the earth), who crafts angels as messengers with wings of two, three, and four. It delivers the ultimate existential truth of our existence in verse 15: 'O mankind, you are the ones in absolute need of Allah, while Allah is the Free of need, the Praiseworthy.' It reveals that among all human beings, those who truly fear and revere Allah with profound awe are those who possess knowledge (*Innama yakhsha Allaha min 'ibadihi al-'ulama'*).

---

## 2. Surah Context
Revealed in Makkah to free the human mind from the delusions of self-sufficiency. The pagans believed their deities shared power with Allah. Surah Fatir demolished these notions by showing that false gods do not even own the paper-thin membrane of a date-seed (*Qitmir*).

---

## 3. Central Theme
**The Absolute Self-Sufficiency of Allah (*Al-Ghina*), Human Dependency (*Al-Faqr*), and Reverential Awe Through Knowledge (*Al-Khashyah*).**

---

## 4. Key Passages
### The Absolute Neediness of Humanity (35:15)
$$\text{يَا أَيُّهَا النَّاسُ أَنتُمُ الْفُقَرَاءُ إِلَى اللَّهِ ۖ وَاللَّهُ هُوَ الْغَنِيُّ الْحَمِيدُ}$$
*"O mankind, you are those in need of Allah, while Allah is the Free of need, the Praiseworthy."*

### The Fear of Allah Possessed by Scholars (35:28)
$$\text{إِنَّمَا يَخْشَى اللَّهَ مِنْ عِبَادِهِ الْعُلَمَاءُ ۗ إِنَّ اللَّهَ عَزِيزٌ غَفُورٌ}$$
*"Only those fear Allah, from among His servants, who have knowledge. Indeed, Allah is Exalted in Might and Forgiving."*

### The Three Tiers of Believers (35:32)
$$\text{ثُمَّ أَوْرَثْنَا الْكِتَابَ الَّذِينَ اصْطَفَيْنَا مِنْ عِبَادِنَا ۖ فَمِنْهُمْ ظَالِمٌ لِّنَفْسِهِ وَمِنْهُم مُّقْتَصِدٌ وَمِنْهُمْ سَابِقٌ بِالْخَيْرَاتِ بِإِذْنِ اللَّهِ ۚ ذَٰلِكَ هُوَ الْفَضْلُ الْكَبِيرُ}$$
*"Then We caused to inherit the Book those We have chosen of Our servants; and among them is he who wrongs himself, and among them is he who is moderate, and among them is he who is foremost in good deeds by permission of Allah. That [inheritance] is what is the great bounty."*

---

## 5. Translation Highlights (The Clear Quran)
* *35:2:* 'Whatever Allah grants to people of mercy—none can withhold it; and whatever He withholds—none can release it thereafter. And He is the Exalted in Might, the Wise.'
* *35:13:* '...And those you invoke other than Him do not possess as much as the membrane of a date-seed.'
* *35:29–30:* 'Indeed, those who recite the Book of Allah and establish prayer and spend out of what We have provided them... hope for a transaction that will never perish.'

---

## 6. What Classical Tafsir Explains
* **Who Are the 'Ulama'? (Verse 28):** Ibn Abbas (RA) stated: 'The knowledgeable person (*Al-'Alim*) is he who knows that Allah is capable of all things, and fears Him accordingly.' Imam Ahmad noted: 'The foundation of knowledge is the reverential fear of Allah (*Khashyatullah*).' Knowledge that produces arrogance is not Quranic knowledge; true knowledge produces deep humility.
* **The Good News of the Three Tiers (Verse 32):** Umar ibn al-Khattab (RA) wept upon reciting verse 32 and said: 'The foremost among us (*Sabiquna*) has preceded to Paradise; the moderate among us (*Muqtasiduna*) has been saved; and the one who wronged himself (*Zalimun li-nafsih*) has been forgiven!' All three categories belong to the chosen heirs of the Qur'an who will ultimately enter Jannah.
---

## 7. Important Supporting Hadiths
1. **The Business That Never Fails:**  
   *Sunan at-Tirmidhi (Hadith 2910), Sahih:*  
   "The Prophet ﷺ said regarding verse 35:29: 'Whoever recites a letter from the Book of Allah will have a good deed for it, and the good deed is multiplied by ten. I do not say *Alif-Lam-Meem* is a letter; rather *Alif* is a letter, *Lam* is a letter, and *Meem* is a letter.'"
2. **Human Neediness and Divine Riches:**  
   *Sahih Muslim (Hadith 2577) - Hadith Qudsi:*  
   "Allah Almighty says: 'O My servants, if the first of you and the last of you, human and jinn, were to rise up on one single plain and ask of Me, and I gave each person everything he asked, that would not diminish what is with Me any more than a needle takes away when dipped into the sea.'"
---

## 8. Key Concepts
1. **Al-Faqr ilallah (Ontological Neediness):** The intrinsic reality that every created being depends on God for every breath, heartbeat, and provision.
2. **Al-Khashyah (Reverential Awe):** Fear mixed with deep love, awe, and sublime knowledge of Allah's majesty.
3. **Tawrith al-Kitab (Inheritance of Scripture):** The sacred entrustment of the Holy Qur'an to the Ummah of Muhammad ﷺ across three spiritual tiers.
4. **Qitmir (The Date-Seed Membrane):** The Quranic metaphor illustrating the utter impotence and worthlessness of false idols.
---

## 9. Tadabbur (Guided Contemplation)
* **The Gate of Mercy (35:2):** Meditate on this absolute declaration: *Ma yaftahillahu lin-nasi mir-rahmatin fala mumsika laha* ('Whatever Allah opens of mercy, none can withhold'). If every government, corporation, and rival plots to block your sustenance, your promotion, or your healing, they cannot close a single door that Allah has ordained to open. And if He withholds it, none can force it open. Rest your anxious heart in this verse.
* **Which Category Are You in? (35:32):** Look at the three categories of believers: (1) The *Zalim li-nafsih* (does obligations but slips into minor sins), (2) The *Muqtasid* (does obligations and avoids sins, but neglects voluntary deeds), and (3) The *Sabiq bil-Khayrat* (hastens to voluntary deeds, avoids doubtful matters). Strive every single day to graduate from being merely moderate to being foremost in good deeds.
---

## 10. What This Means For Me
* I eradicate all feelings of self-sufficiency (*Istighna'*), starting every morning with the admission of my utter poverty and need before Allah (35:15).
* I pursue Islamic and worldly knowledge not for titles or prestige, but to deepen my reverence and awe (*Khashyah*) of the Creator.
* I seek to be among the *Sabiqun bil-Khayrat* by engaging in voluntary charity, fasting, and extra prayers beyond the bare minimum.

---

## 11. One Practical Action
**Invest in the 'Trade That Never Perishes' Today:**  
Honor verse 29 (*Tijaratan lan tabur*) by spending 15 minutes reciting the Qur'an with reflection, establishing your five prayers on time, and sending an unannounced financial gift to a person in need.

## 12. Reflection Questions
1. Do I live with the realization that I am in absolute need (*Faqir*) of Allah for every breath and heartbeat (35:15)?
2. Has my knowledge—secular or religious—made me more arrogant or more humble and God-fearing (35:28)?
3. Am I content with being a *Zalim li-nafsih* or *Muqtasid*, or am I striving to be a *Sabiq bil-Khayrat* (35:32)?

## 13. Closing Supplication
$$\text{اللَّهُمَّ إِنَّا فُقَرَاءُ إِلَىٰ رَحْمَتِكَ وَأَنْتَ الْغَنِيُّ الْحَمِيدُ ، اللَّهُمَّ ارْزُقْنَا خَشْيَتَكَ فِي الْغَيْبِ وَالشَّهَادَةِ ، وَاجْعَلْنَا مِنَ السَّابِقِينَ بِالْخَيْرَاتِ بِإِذْنِكَ}$$  
*"O Allah, we are desperately in need of Your mercy while You are the Free of need, the Praiseworthy. O Allah, grant us reverential awe of You in secret and in public, and make us among those foremost in good deeds by Your permission. Amin."*
