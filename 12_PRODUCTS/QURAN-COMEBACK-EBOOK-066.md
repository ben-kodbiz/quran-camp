# CHAPTER 66: SURAH AT-TAHRIM (سُورَةُ التحريم)
**Classification:** Madani  
**Order in Mushaf:** 66  
**Total Verses:** 12  
**Famous Names:** Surat at-Tahrim, Surat Ya Ayyuhan-Nabiyyu Lima Tuharrim  

---

## 1. Why This Surah Matters
Surah At-Tahrim provides deep, intimate lessons on domestic leadership, marital reconciliation, and personal spiritual responsibility. It commands believers to protect themselves and their families from an inferno whose fuel is people and stones (*Qu anfusakum wa ahlikum nara*). It presents the golden definition of true, sincere repentance (*Tawbah Nasuh*), and sets forth four unforgettable historical women as archetypes: two tragic examples of disbelieving wives of righteous prophets (the wives of Nuh and Lut), and two radiant female exemplars of supreme faith for all eternity: Asiyah (the wife of Pharaoh who prayed for a home near Allah in Paradise while being tortured) and Maryam bint 'Imran (who guarded her chastity and believed in the words of her Lord).

---

## 2. Surah Context
Revealed in Madinah regarding domestic strains in the Prophet's ﷺ household concerning honey and marital confidences. It showed that even the most blessed household is not immune to domestic challenges, and that salvation is strictly an individual choice.

---

## 3. Central Theme
**Domestic Ethics, Guarding the Household from Hellfire, Sincere Repentance (*At-Tawbah an-Nasuh*), and Female Exemplars of Faith.**

---

## 4. Key Passages
### Guarding Families from the Fire (66:6)
$$\text{يَا أَيُّهَا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا قُوا أَنفُسَكُمْ وَأَهْلِيكُمْ نَارًا وَقُودُهَا النَّاسُ وَالْحِجَارَةُ عَلَيْهَا مَلَائِكَةٌ غِلَاظٌ شِدَادٌ لَّا يَعْصُونَ اللَّهَ مَا أَمَرَهُمْ وَيَفْعَلُونَ مَا يُؤْمَرُونَ}$$
*"O you who have believed, protect yourselves and your families from a Fire whose fuel is people and stones, over which are [appointed] angels, harsh and severe; they do not disobey Allah in what He commands them but do what they are commanded."*

### The Call to Sincere Repentance (66:8)
$$\text{يَا أَيُّهَا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا تُوبُوا إِلَى اللَّهِ تَوْبَةً نَّصُوحًا عَسَىٰ رَبُّكُمْ أَن يُكَفِّرَ عَنكُمْ سَيِّئَاتِكُمْ وَيُدْخِلَكُمْ جَنَّاتٍ تَجْرِي مِن تَحْتِهَا الْأَنْهَارُ}$$
*"O you who have believed, repent to Allah with sincere repentance. Perhaps your Lord will remove from you your misdeeds and admit you to gardens beneath which rivers flow..."*

### Asiyah's Prayer for a Home in Jannah (66:11)
$$\text{وَضَرَبَ اللَّهُ مَثَلًا لِّلَّذِينَ آمَنُوا امْرَأَتَ فِرْعَوْنَ إِذْ قَالَتْ رَبِّ ابْنِ لِي عِندَكَ بَيْتًا فِي الْجَنَّةِ وَنَجِّنِي مِن فِرْعَوْنَ وَعَمَلِهِ وَنَجِّنِي مِنَ الْقَوْمِ الظَّالِمِينَ}$$
*"And Allah presents an example of those who believed: the wife of Pharaoh, when she said, 'My Lord, build for me near You a house in Paradise and save me from Pharaoh and his deeds and save me from the wrongdoing people.'"*

---

## 5. Translation Highlights (The Clear Quran)
* *66:1:* 'O Prophet, why do you prohibit [yourself from] what Allah has made lawful for you, seeking the approval of your wives? And Allah is Forgiving and Merciful.'
* *66:4:* 'If you two [wives] repent to Allah, [it is best], for your hearts have deviated...'
* *66:12:* 'And [the example of] Mary, the daughter of 'Imran, who guarded her chastity, so We blew into [her garment] through Our angel, and she believed in the words of her Lord and His scriptures and was of the devoutly obedient.'

---

## 6. What Classical Tafsir Explains
* **How to Protect Your Family (Verse 6):** Ali ibn Abi Talib (RA) explained *Qu anfusakum wa ahlikum nara*: 'Teach yourselves and teach your families good manners and knowledge (*'Allimuhum wa addibuhum*).' Protection from Hell is not accomplished merely by locking the front door, but by cultivating prayer, modesty, and Islamic values in the home.
* **The Three Pillars of Tawbah Nasuh (Verse 8):** Umar ibn al-Khattab and Al-Hasan al-Basri defined *Tawbah Nasuh* as: (1) Sincere remorse in the heart for the past sin, (2) Immediate cessation of the sin, and (3) A firm determination never to return to it, just as milk cannot return to the udder.
---

## 7. Important Supporting Hadiths
1. **The Four Greatest Women of Creation:**  
   *Sahih al-Bukhari (Hadith 3411) from Abu Musa al-Ash'ari (RA):*  
   "The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: 'Many men attained perfection, but among women none attained perfection except Maryam bint 'Imran and Asiyah the wife of Pharaoh; and the superiority of Aisha over other women is like the superiority of Tharid over all other food.'"
2. **Asiyah Gazing at Her Palace in Jannah:**  
   *Al-Mustadrak 'ala al-Sahihayn (Hadith 3841), Sahih:*  
   "Ibn Abbas narrated: 'When Pharaoh was torturing Asiyah with stakes under the scorching sun, whenever the guards walked away, angels shaded her with their wings, and she saw her palace being constructed in Paradise, smiling through the torture.'"
---

## 8. Key Concepts
1. **Tawbah Nasuh (Sincere Repentance):** The total, irreversible spiritual return to God characterized by genuine remorse and reformed habits.
2. **Mas'uliyyat al-Ahl (Family Stewardship):** The non-negotiable spiritual obligation of parents to mentor and protect their household from moral destruction.
3. **Baytun 'Indaka (A Home Near You):** Asiyah's profound spiritual priority: seeking nearness to God (*'Indaka*) before seeking the physical amenities of Paradise (*Fil-Jannah*).
4. **Al-Istiqlal ar-Ruhi (Spiritual Independence):** The principle that faith is an individual choice: righteous lineage cannot save a disbeliever, and a tyrant husband cannot ruin a believer's soul.
---

## 9. Tadabbur (Guided Contemplation)
* **The Priority in Asiyah's Du'a (66:11):** Look closely at the grammar of Asiyah's prayer: *Rabbi-bni li 'indaka baytan fil-Jannah.* Notice that she placed *'Indaka* ('Near You') before *Baytan fil-Jannah* ('A house in Paradise'). She wanted the Neighbor before the house! For Asiyah, Paradise was not merely rivers and gold; Paradise was proximity to Allah. In your du'as, do you seek God Himself, or only His gifts?
* **Your Salvation is an Individual Choice (66:10–12):** Surah At-Tahrim provides the ultimate lesson in spiritual autonomy: The wife of Nuh and the wife of Lut slept under the roof of two mighty prophets, yet entered Hellfire because faith cannot be borrowed. Asiyah lived in the palace of history's greatest tyrant, yet attained the highest station of Paradise. No matter your family background or environment, your relationship with Allah is entirely in your own hands.
---

## 10. What This Means For Me
* I take active responsibility for the spiritual education and character of my household (*Qu anfusakum wa ahlikum nara*).
* I practice *Tawbah Nasuh* by genuinely abandoning harmful habits and determining never to return to them (66:8).
* I prioritize nearness to Allah (*'Indaka*) above all worldly comforts, drawing inspiration from the courage of Asiyah (66:11).

## 12. Reflection Questions
1. Am I actively protecting my family from spiritual destruction, or am I only focused on their material comfort (66:6)?
2. Is my repentance truly *Nasuh* (sincere and lasting), or do I repeatedly return to the same sins without remorse (66:8)?
3. Am I using my family background as an excuse, or am I taking personal ownership of my faith like Asiyah (66:11)?

## 13. Closing Supplication
$$\text{رَبِّ ابْنِ لَنَا عِندَكَ بَيْتًا فِي الْجَنَّةِ ، اللَّهُمَّ قِنَا وَأَهْلِينَا نَارًا وَقُودُهَا النَّاسُ وَالْحِجَارَةُ ، وَتُبْ عَلَيْنَا تَوْبَةً نَصُوحًا تُطَهِّرُنَا بِهَا يَا غَفُورُ يَا رَحِيمُ}$$  
*"My Lord, build for us near You a house in Paradise. O Allah, protect us and our families from a Fire whose fuel is people and stones, and grant us sincere repentance through which You purify us, O Forgiving, O Merciful. Amin."*
