# CHAPTER 10: SURAH YUNUS (سُورَةُ يُونُسَ)
**Classification:** Makki  
**Order in Mushaf:** 10  
**Total Verses:** 109  
**Famous Names:** *Surat Yunus* (The Chapter of Jonah)  

---

## 1. Why This Surah Matters
Surah Yunus is the sanctuary of hope for souls enveloped in suffocating darkness. Named after Prophet Yunus (Jonah, peace be upon him), whose famous cry from the triple darkness of night, ocean, and the belly of the whale brought immediate deliverance, this surah addresses the human experience of distress, doubt, and transience. It contains one of the most vivid warnings against consumerist materialism—describing worldly life as a lush garden wiped clean overnight—while offering the divine alternative: the Qur'an as an intimate heart-medicine (*shifā’un limā fiṣ-ṣudūr*) and an open invitation from Allah to the Eternal Home of Peace (*Dār as-Salām*). Completing Chapter 10 marks the foundational milestone of our 114-surah journey.

---

## 2. Surah Context
Revealed in Makkah during the intense middle period of the Prophetic mission, shortly before the Hijrah. The Makkan leaders were demanding visible cosmic miracles, mocking the promise of resurrection, and refusing to listen to the recitation. Surah Yunus directs their attention away from spectacular fireworks toward the quiet, ongoing miracles that surround them daily: the steady cycles of the sun and moon, the ships gliding across stormy seas, and the psychology of human desperation when facing shipwreck.

---

## 3. Central Theme
**The Truth of Divine Wisdom (*Al-Hikmah*), Deliverance from Despair, and the Qur'an as Healing for the Fractured Soul.**

---

## 4. Key Passages
1. **The Cosmic Balance of Sun and Moon (10:5):**  
   $$\text{هُوَ الَّذِي جَعَلَ الشَّمْسَ ضِيَاءً وَالْقَمَرَ نُورًا وَقَدَّرَهُ مَنَازِلَ لِتَعْلَمُوا عَدَدَ السِّنِينَ وَالْحِسَابَ ۚ مَا خَلَقَ اللَّهُ ذَٰلِكَ إِلَّا بِالْحَقِّ}$$  
   *"It is He who made the sun a shining light and the moon a derived light and determined for it phases—that you may know the number of years and account [of time]. Allah has not created this except in truth."*
2. **The Parable of the Fleeting World (10:24):**  
   $$\text{إِنَّمَا مَثَلُ الْحَيَاةِ الدُّنْيَا كَمَاءٍ أَنزَلْنَاهُ مِنَ السَّمَاءِ فَاخْتَلَطَ بِهِ نَبَاتُ الْأَرْضِ مِمَّا يَأْكُلُ النَّاسُ وَالْأَنْعَامُ... أَتَاهَا أَمْرُنَا لَيْلًا أَوْ نَهَارًا فَجَعَلْنَاهَا حَصِيدًا كَأَن لَّمْ تَغْنَ بِالْأَمْسِ}$$  
   *"The example of [this] worldly life is like rain which We have sent down from the sky that the plants of the earth absorb—[those] from which men and livestock eat—until, when the earth has taken on its adornment and is beautified and its people think they have capability over it, there comes to it Our command by night or by day, and We make it as a harvest, as if it had not flourished yesterday."*
3. **The Medicine for the Chests (10:57–58):**  
   $$\text{يَا أَيُّهَا النَّاسُ قَدْ جَاءَتْكُم مَّوْعِظَةٌ مِّن رَّبِّكُمْ وَشِفَاءٌ لِّمَا فِي الصُّدُورِ وَهُدًى وَرَحْمَةٌ لِّلْمُؤْمِنِينَ (57) قُلْ بِفَضْلِ اللَّهِ وَبِرَحْمَتِهِ فَبِذَٰلِكَ فَلْيَفْرَحُوا هُوَ خَيْرٌ مِّمَّا يَجْمَعُونَ (58)}$$  
   *"O mankind, there has come to you instruction from your Lord and healing for what is in the breasts and guidance and mercy for the believers. Say, 'In the bounty of Allah and in His mercy—in that let them rejoice; it is better than whatever they accumulate.'"*
4. **The Exception of the People of Yunus (10:98):**  
   $$\text{فَلَوْلَا كَانَتْ قَرْيَةٌ آمَنَتْ فَنَفَعَهَا إِيمَانُهَا إِلَّا قَوْمَ يُونُسَ لَمَّا آمَنُوا كَشَفْنَا عَنْهُمْ عَذَابَ الْخِزْيِ فِي الْحَيَاةِ الدُّنْيَا وَمَتَّعْنَاهُمْ إِلَىٰ حِينٍ}$$  
   *"Why was there not a single city that believed so its faith would have benefited it, except the people of Yunus? When they believed, We removed from them the punishment of disgrace in worldly life and gave them enjoyment for a time."*

## 5. Translation Highlights (The Clear Quran)
* *10:12:* "When trouble touches man, he cries unto Us [in all postures]—lying on his side, or sitting, or standing. But when We have removed his trouble, he walks on as if he had never cried unto Us for a trouble that touched him!"
* *10:25:* "And Allah invites to the Home of Peace (Dar as-Salam) and guides whom He wills to a straight path."
* *10:62:* "Unquestionably, [for] the allies of Allah there will be no fear concerning them, nor will they grieve."

---

## 6. What Classical Tafsir Explains
* **Healing for What Is in the Chests (*Shifa'un lima fis-sudur*):** Ibn Kathir and classical commentators note that this verse is universal (*Ya ayyuhan-nas*—addressed to all humanity). The sicknesses of the chest are of two kinds: spiritual doubts and intellectual skepticism (*shubuhat*), and destructive desires and emotional turbulence (*shahawat* and grief). The Qur'an cures both: its rational evidence clears doubts, while its spiritual reminders calm anxiety and instill inner peace (*sakinah*).
* **The Exceptional Repentance of Nineveh (Verse 98):** Classical historians record that when the people of Yunus (the city of Nineveh) realized that their prophet had departed and divine clouds of punishment were gathering, they did not harden their hearts. The entire population—men, women, children, and even their cattle—walked out into the open fields, wept in collective remorse, and pleaded for mercy. Because their repentance was sincere before the final blow struck, Allah made them the only nation in history from whom collective destruction was lifted.

## 7. Important Supporting Hadiths
1. **The Infallible Supplication of Dhun-Nun (Prophet Yunus):**  
   *Jami' at-Tirmidhi* (Hadith 3505), *Musnad Ahmad* (Hadith 1462) from Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas (رضي الله عنه), graded **Sahih**:  
   The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said:  
   *"The supplication of Dhun-Nun (Yunus) when he called upon Allah while in the belly of the whale was:  
   'Lā ilāha illā anta subḥānaka innī kuntu minaẓ-ẓālimīn'  
   (There is no deity except You; exalted are You. Indeed, I have been of the wrongdoers).  
   No Muslim ever supplicates with this in any matter, but that Allah answers him."*
2. **Rejoicing in the Bounty of Allah:**  
   *Tafsir Ibn Kathir* (commentary on 10:58) records from Hilal ibn Yasaf: When the war tax of Iraq reached Umar ibn al-Khattab, his servant exclaimed: *"O Commander of the Believers, this is the bounty of Allah and His mercy!"* Umar said: *"You have spoken falsely. This is what people accumulate. The bounty of Allah and His mercy is the Book of Allah."*
3. **The Allies of Allah (Awliya' Allah):**  
   *Sunan Abi Dawud* (Hadith 3527), *Musnad Ahmad* (Hadith 22941), graded **Sahih**:  
   The Prophet ﷺ said: *"Indeed, among the servants of Allah are people who are neither prophets nor martyrs, but prophets and martyrs will envy them on the Day of Resurrection for their station before Allah."* The companions asked who they were, and he replied: *"They are people who love one another for the sake of Allah without any blood ties or financial interests between them... By Allah, their faces are light and they are upon light. They do not fear when people fear, nor do they grieve when people grieve."* Then he recited verse 10:62.

## 8. Key Concepts
1. **Shifā' al-Qulub (Cardiovascular Spiritual Healing):** The unique therapeutic quality of the Qur'an that directly repairs emotional wounds, anxiety, and moral confusion.
2. **Dār as-Salām (The Abode of Peace):** Paradise; named so because it is completely devoid of exhaustion, illness, grief, mortality, and conflict.
3. **Al-Farah bil-Qur'an (Spiritual Celebration):** True joy is not rooted in accumulated bank accounts or material possessions, but in having access to divine guidance (10:58).
4. **Al-Iftiqār ilā Allah (Utter Neediness Before Allah):** The posture of Prophet Yunus in the whale's belly: acknowledging total helplessness, declaring Allah's perfection, and admitting one's own shortcomings.

---

## 9. Tadabbur (Guided Contemplation)
* **The Belly of Your Own Whale:** Prophet Yunus found himself in three layers of suffocating darkness: the darkness of night, the depths of the black sea, and the enclosed stomach of a sea creature. In that moment, panic and despair were humanly natural. But instead of despairing, he whispered words of pure Tawhid and humility: *La ilaha illa Anta, subhanaka inni kuntu minaz-zalimin*.  
  What is the darkness trapping you today? Chronic pain? Financial collapse? A broken relationship? The medicine has not changed in three thousand years. Turn your heart to Allah, acknowledge your human weakness, and watch the waves recede.
* **What Are You Truly Rejoicing Over? (10:58):** We celebrate job promotions, new cars, and vacations. But when was the last time your heart felt genuine joy because you opened the Qur'an and understood a verse that healed your soul? Allah says: *In that let them rejoice; it is better than all the worldly goods they accumulate.*

## 10. What This Means For Me
* When facing crises where all human avenues are blocked, I turn immediately to the supplication of Prophet Yunus, relying on Allah’s infinite power.
* When worldly success or material accumulation starts making me feel arrogant, I remember verse 10:24 and remind myself that everything can vanish overnight.
* I treat the Qur'an not merely as an academic book of laws, but as my personal spiritual prescription (*shifa'*) whenever anxiety threatens to overcome my chest.

---

## 11. One Practical Action
**Anchor the Dua of Yunus in Your Distress Today:**  
Whenever you feel overwhelmed or trapped by worry today, pause, take a slow breath, and recite three times with complete presence:  
$$\text{لَّا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا أَنتَ سُبْحَانَكَ إِنِّي كُنتُ مِنَ الظَّالِمِينَ}$$  
Feel the weight of self-reliance lift from your shoulders.

## 12. Reflection Questions
1. What "darkness" in my life am I currently trying to escape using only worldly means, instead of crying out to Allah like Yunus?
2. How can I shift my daily definition of success away from "accumulating wealth" toward "rejoicing in the Qur'an" (10:58)?
3. Am I living as one of the *Awliya' Allah* (allies of Allah) who love others purely for His sake without transactional expectations?

## 13. Closing Supplication (The Prophetic Supplication for Healing and Relief from Grief)
$$\text{اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي عَبْدُكَ ، ابْنُ عَبْدِكَ ، ابْنُ أَمَتِكَ ، نَاصِيَتِي بِيَدِكَ ، مَاضٍ فِيَّ حُكْمُكَ ، عَدْلٌ فِيَّ قَضَاؤُكَ ، أَسْأَلُكَ بِكُلِّ اسْمٍ هُوَ لَكَ سَمَّيْتَ بِهِ نَفْسَكَ ، أَوْ أَنْزَلْتَهُ فِي كِتَابِكَ ، أَوْ عَلَّمْتَهُ أَحَدًا مِنْ خَلْقِكَ ، أَوِ اسْتَأْثَرْتَ بِهِ فِي عِلْمِ الْغَيْبِ عِنْدَكَ ، أَنْ تَجْعَلَ الْقُرْآنَ رَبِيعَ قَلْبِي ، وَنُورَ صَدْرِي ، وَجَلَاءَ حُزْنِي ، وَذَهَابَ هَمِّي}$$  
*"O Allah, I am Your servant, son of Your servant, son of Your maidservant. My forelock is in Your hand, Your command over me is forever executed, and Your decree over me is just. I ask You by every name belonging to You which You have named Yourself with, or revealed in Your Book, or taught to any of Your creation, or preserved in the knowledge of the unseen with You: that You make the Qur'an the spring of my heart, the light of my chest, the banisher of my grief, and the reliever of my anxiety. Amin."*
