# CHAPTER 34: SURAH SABA (سُورَةُ سبأ)
**Classification:** Makki  
**Order in Mushaf:** 34  
**Total Verses:** 54  
**Famous Names:** Surat Saba  

---

## 1. Why This Surah Matters
Surah Saba explores the economic, civilizational, and technological rise and fall of empires. It opens with universal praise (*Al-Hamd*) for the Sovereign who owns all things in heaven and earth, and contrasts two distinct civilizational models: First, the prophetic kingdom of Dawud and Sulayman, who used miraculous metallurgy and control over jinn and wind strictly as instruments of intense, active gratitude (*I'malu ala Dawuda shukra*); second, the hyper-prosperous agrarian civilization of Sheba (*Saba*), who turned ungrateful, treated luxury as an entitlement, and were shattered into scattered folklore by the catastrophic rupture of the Great Dam of Ma'rib (*Sayl al-'Arim*). It reminds humanity: 'Few of My servants are truly grateful' (34:13).

---

## 2. Surah Context
Revealed in Makkah to confront the mercantile elite of Quraysh who took pride in their commercial monopolies and trade caravans. Surah Saba demonstrated that wealth without gratitude breeds civilizational ruin.

---

## 3. Central Theme
**The Philosophy of Civilizational Gratitude (*Shukr*), the Fall of the Empire of Sheba, and the Absolute Accountability of Wealth.**

---

## 4. Key Passages
### The Command of Active Gratitude to Dawud (34:10–13)
$$\text{وَلَقَدْ آتَيْنَا دَاوُودَ مِنَّا فَضْلًا ۖ يَا جِبَالُ أَوِّبِي مَعَهُ وَالطَّيْرَ ۖ وَأَلَنَّا لَهُ الْحَدِيدَ ۝ أَنِ اعْمَلْ سَابِغَاتٍ وَقَدِّرْ فِي السَّرْدِ ۖ وَاعْمَلُوا صَالِحًا... اعْمَلُوا آلَ دَاوُودَ شُكْرًا ۚ وَقَلِيلٌ مِّنْ عِبَادِيَ الشَّكُورُ}$$
*"And We certainly gave David from Us bounty. [We said], 'O mountains, repeat [Our] praises with him, and the birds [as well].' And We made pliable for him iron, [Commanding him], 'Make full coats of mail and calculate [precisely] the links, and work [all of you] righteousness...' 'Work, O family of David, in gratitude.' And few of My servants are grateful."*

### The Ruin of Saba's Civilization (34:15–16)
$$\text{لَقَدْ كَانَ لِسَبَإٍ فِي مَسْكَنِهِمْ آيَةٌ ۖ جَنَّتَانِ عَن يَمِينٍ وَشِمَالٍ ۖ كُلُوا مِن رِّزْقِ رَبِّكُمْ وَاشْكُرُوا لَهُ ۚ بَلْدَةٌ طَيِّبَةٌ وَرَبٌّ غَفُورٌ ۝ فَأَعْرَضُوا فَأَرْسَلْنَا عَلَيْهِمْ سَيْلَ الْعَرِمِ وَبَدَّلْنَاهُم بِجَنَّتَيْهِمْ جَنَّتَيْنِ ذَوَاتَيْ أُكُلٍ خَمْطٍ وَأَثْلٍ وَشَيْءٍ مِّن سِدْرٍ قَلِيلٍ}$$
*"There was for [the tribe of] Sheba in their dwelling place a sign: two [fields of] gardens on the right and on the left. [They were told], 'Eat from the provisions of your Lord and be grateful to Him. A good land [have you], and a forgiving Lord.' But they turned away [refusing], so We sent upon them the flood of the dam, and We replaced their two [fields of] gardens with gardens of bitter fruit, tamarisks and something of sparse lote trees."*

### The Standing for Truth in Solitude and Pairs (34:46)
$$\text{قُلْ إِنَّمَا أَعِظُكُم بِوَاحِدَةٍ ۖ أَن تَقُومُوا لِلَّهِ مَثْنَىٰ وَفُرَادَىٰ ثُمَّ تَتَفَكَّرُوا ۚ مَا بِصَاحِبِكُم مِّن جِنَّةٍ}$$
*"Say, 'I only advise you of one [thing]—that you stand for Allah, [seeking truth] in pairs and individually, and then reflect: there is not in your companion any madness...'"*

## 5. Translation Highlights (The Clear Quran)
* *34:3:* '...Not absent from Him is an atom's weight within the heavens or within the earth or [what is] smaller than that or greater, except that it is in a clear register.'
* *34:28:* 'And We have not sent you except comprehensively to mankind as a bringer of good tidings and a warner...'
* *34:39:* 'Say, "Indeed, my Lord extends provision for whom He wills of His servants and restricts [it] for him. But whatever you spend in good—He will compensate it; and He is the best of providers."'

---

## 6. What Classical Tafsir Explains
* **Gratitude is an Action, Not Merely Words (Verse 13):** Ibn Kathir and Al-Qurtubi explain that Allah commanded the family of Dawud: *I'malu... Shukra* ('Act in gratitude'). Gratitude is not simply whispering *Alhamdulillah*; it is deploying the blessing (skills, wealth, strength) in the service of Allah and the upliftment of humanity.
* **The Psychology of Herd Thinking (Verse 46):** Imam al-Biqa'i highlights that mobs, echo chambers, and crowds paralyze rational reflection. Allah commands people to step away from the crowd—standing *Mathna wa Furada* (in pairs or alone)—to independently contemplate truth without peer pressure.
---

## 7. Important Supporting Hadiths
1. **The Reality of the People of Saba:**  
   *Jami' at-Tirmidhi (Hadith 3222), Sahih:*  
   "Farwah ibn Musayk (RA) asked the Prophet ﷺ: 'O Messenger of Allah, tell me about Saba: was it a man, a woman, or a land?' The Prophet ﷺ replied: 'He was a man among the Arabs who had ten sons; six settled in Yemen and four settled in the Levant (*Ash-Sham*).'"
2. **The Compensation of Charity:**  
   *Sahih Muslim (Hadith 2588) from Abu Hurayrah (RA):*  
   "The Prophet ﷺ said regarding verse 34:39: 'Charity does not decrease wealth, and Allah increases the honor of the servant who pardons, and no one humbles himself for Allah except that Allah elevates him.'"
---

## 8. Key Concepts
1. **Shukr al-'Amal (Active Gratitude):** Demonstrating thanks through righteous labor, service, and ethical stewardship.
2. **Sayl al-'Arim (The Dam Flood):** The historical collapse of the Marib Dam in ancient Yemen as divine retribution for arrogance and ingratitude.
3. **Mathna wa Furada (Independent Critical Thinking):** Stepping outside mob mentalities and societal groupthink to impartially evaluate truth.
4. **Ikhlaf an-Nafaqah (Divine Compensation):** The infallible promise that whatever wealth is spent for Allah's cause will be reimbursed and multiplied.
---

## 9. Tadabbur (Guided Contemplation)
* **Are You Among the 'Few'? (34:13):** Look at the sobering reality check from your Creator: *Wa qalilun min 'ibadiyash-shakur* ('And few of My servants are grateful'). Most people are professional complainers: fixating on the one delayed promotion while ignoring a billion heartbeats, functioning kidneys, running water, and loved ones. Will you strive to be among that elite, radiant minority (*Al-Qalil*) who wake up every morning intentionally thanking Allah?
* **The Replacement for What You Spend (34:39):** When you transfer money for charity, your bank account shows a deduction, but Allah's cosmic ledger registers a deposit with guaranteed returns: *Wa ma anfaqtum min shay'in fa-huwa yukhlifuh* ('Whatever you spend, He will replace it'). You can never out-give the One who created the universe.
---

## 10. What This Means For Me
* I transform my gratitude from mere words into daily actions (*I'malu Shukra*) by utilizing my professional skills and resources to serve others.
* I step away from toxic social media mobs and echo chambers to engage in calm, independent reflection (34:46).
* I give charity generously and fearlessly, holding absolute conviction in Allah's promise to compensate every penny spent in His cause.

## 11. One Practical Action
**Perform an Act of 'Active Gratitude' Today:**  
Identify one specific blessing you enjoy (e.g., your vehicle, your salary, or your health). Use it today to directly assist someone: give a ride, buy groceries for someone in need, or donate without hesitating.

## 12. Reflection Questions
1. Am I among the ungrateful majority or the grateful few (*Al-Qalil*) described in verse 13?
2. How does the ruin of the proud civilization of Saba warn modern affluent societies against self-absorption?
3. Do I hold back from charity out of fear of scarcity, or do I trust Allah's promise in verse 39?

## 13. Closing Supplication
$$\text{اللَّهُمَّ أَعِنَّا عَلَىٰ ذِكْرِكَ وَشُكْرِكَ وَحُسْنِ عِبَادَتِكَ ، اللَّهُمَّ اجْعَلْنَا مِنْ عِبَادِكَ الشَّكُورِينَ الْقَلِيلِينَ ، وَأَخْلِفْ عَلَيْنَا مَا أَنْفَقْنَا فِي سَبِيلِكَ}$$  
*"O Allah, assist us in remembering You, thanking You, and worshipping You with excellence. O Allah, make us among Your grateful few servants, and compensate for us whatever we spend in Your cause. Amin."*
