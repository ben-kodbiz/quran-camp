# CHAPTER 87: SURAH AL-A'LA (سُورَةُ الأعلى)
**Classification:** Makki  
**Order in Mushaf:** 87  
**Total Verses:** 19  
**Famous Names:** Surat al-A'la, Sabbihisma Rabbikal-A'la  

---

## 1. Why This Surah Matters
Surah Al-A'la is one of the most beloved and frequently recited chapters in the Sunnah of the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ, recited consistently in Friday prayers, Eid prayers, and the Witr prayer of every night. It opens with the grand command of celestial exultation: 'Exalt the name of your Lord, the Most High' (*Sabbihisma Rabbikal-A'la*), which instituted the sacred words spoken in every single prostration (*Sujud*): *Subhana Rabbiyal-A'la*. It promises the Prophet ﷺ effortless memorization of revelation: 'We will make you recite, and you will not forget' (87:6). It articulates the golden rule of spiritual psychology in verses 14–17: 'He has certainly succeeded who purifies himself... But you prefer the worldly life, while the Hereafter is better and more enduring.'

---

## 2. Surah Context
Revealed in early Makkah as one of the earliest revelations. It comforted the Messenger of Allah ﷺ regarding the retention of scripture and established the bedrock connection between this revelation and the ancient scrolls of Abraham and Moses (*Suhufi Ibrahima wa Musa*).

---

## 3. Central Theme
**Exalting the Transcendent Name of Allah, the Facilitation of Revelation, the Success of Self-Purification (*Tazkiyah*), and the Primacy of the Hereafter.**

---

## 4. Key Passages
### Exalting the Most High (87:1–5)
$$\text{سَبِّحِ اسْمَ رَبِّكَ الْأَعْلَى ۝ الَّذِي خَلَقَ فَسَوَّىٰ ۝ وَالَّذِي قَدَّرَ فَهَدَىٰ ۝ وَالَّذِي أَخْرَجَ الْمَرْعَىٰ ۝ فَجَعَلَهُ غُثَاءً أَحْوَىٰ}$$
*"Exalt the name of your Lord, the Most High, Who created and proportioned, And who destined and [then] guided, And who brings out the pasture, And [then] makes it black stubble."*

### The Promise of Infallible Memory (87:6–7)
$$\text{سَنُقْرِئُكَ فَلَا تَنسَىٰ ۝ إِلَّا مَا شَاءَ اللَّهُ ۚ إِنَّهُ يَعْلَمُ الْجَهْرَ وَمَا يَخْفَىٰ}$$
*"We will make you recite, [O Muhammad], and you will not forget, Except what Allah wills. Indeed, He knows what is declared and what is hidden."*

### The True Success and the Worldly Preference (87:14–17)
$$\text{قَدْ أَفْلَحَ مَن تَزَكَّىٰ ۝ وَذَكَرَ اسْمَ رَبِّهِ فَصَلَّىٰ ۝ بَلْ تُؤْثِرُونَ الْحَيَاةَ الدُّنْيَا ۝ وَالْآخِرَةُ خَيْرٌ وَأَبْقَىٰ}$$
*"He has certainly succeeded who purifies himself, And mentions the name of his Lord and prays. But you prefer the worldly life, While the Hereafter is better and more enduring."*

---

## 5. Translation Highlights (The Clear Quran)
* *87:8:* 'And We will ease you toward ease.'
* *87:9:* 'So remind, if the reminder should benefit.'
* *87:18–19:* 'Indeed, this is in the former scriptures, The scriptures of Abraham and Moses.'

---

## 6. What Classical Tafsir Explains
* **Institution of 'Subhana Rabbiyal-A'la' (Verse 1):** Uqbah ibn 'Amir (RA) narrated: When verse 56:96 (*Fa-sabbih bi-ismi Rabbikal-'Adhim*) was revealed, the Prophet ﷺ said: 'Place it in your bowing (*Ruku'*).' And when verse 87:1 (*Sabbihisma Rabbikal-A'la*) was revealed, the Prophet ﷺ said: 'Place it in your prostration (*Sujud*)!' Thus every forehead on earth proclaims this exact verse multiple times a day.
* **The Meaning of 'Qad Aflaha Man Tazakka' (Verse 14):** Ibn Abbas and Umar ibn Abdul-Aziz explained that *Tazakka* encompasses two interlinked purifications: purifying the soul from polytheism and sins, and paying *Zakat al-Fitr* at the end of Ramadan before going out to the Eid prayer (*Dhakarasma Rabbihi fa-salla*).
---

## 7. Important Supporting Hadiths
1. **The Prophet's Love for Surah Al-A'la:**  
   *Sahih Muslim (Hadith 878) from An-Nu'man ibn Bashir (RA):*  
   "The Messenger of Allah ﷺ used to recite in the two Eid prayers and in the Friday prayer: *Sabbihisma Rabbikal-A'la* (87) and *Hal ataka hadithul-Ghashiyah* (88); and when Eid and Friday coincided on the same day, he recited them in both prayers."
2. **The Witr Prayer Recitation:**  
   *Sunan an-Nasa'i (Hadith 1732), Sahih:*  
   "Ubayy ibn Ka'b (RA) narrated: 'The Messenger of Allah ﷺ used to recite in Witr: *Sabbihisma Rabbikal-A'la*, *Qul ya ayyuhal-kafirun*, and *Qul Huwallahu Ahad*.'"
---

## 8. Key Concepts
1. **Tasbih al-A'la (Exalting the Most High):** The foundational statement of divine transcendence proclaimed in every physical prostration.
2. **Sanuqri'uka Fala Tansa (The Divine Inscription):** The divine guarantee ensuring that the Holy Qur'an remains permanently imprinted upon the Prophet's heart.
3. **Nu-yassiruka lil-Yusra (Facilitating Toward Ease):** The blessed spiritual state where obedience, charity, and patience become natural, effortless joys.
4. **Al-Akhiratu Khayrun wa Abqa (The Supremacy of Eternity):** The core mathematical and spiritual calculus: the Hereafter is superior in quality (*Khayr*) and infinite in duration (*Abqa*).
---

## 9. Tadabbur (Guided Contemplation)
* **Better in Quality, Infinite in Duration (87:17):** Contemplate the two words describing Paradise: *Khayrun wa Abqa.* If someone offered you a mansion made of glass that shatters tomorrow, or a modest cottage made of solid gold that lasts forever, which would you choose? Dunya is temporary glass; Akhirah is eternal gold. Yet humanity chooses the glass: *Bal tu'thiruna al-hayatad-dunya.* Choose what is *Khayr* and *Abqa*.
* **We Will Ease You to Ease (87:8):** Look at the miraculous promise: *Wa nuyassiruka lil-yusra.* When you align your life with Allah, He does not just make religion easy; He eases *you* into a state of ease! Prayer stops feeling like a chore and becomes your oxygen. Charity stops feeling like a loss and becomes your joy. Ask Allah to grant you *Al-Yusra*.
---

## 10. What This Means For Me
* Whenever my forehead touches the dust in Sujud, I proclaim *Subhana Rabbiyal-A'la* with living presence and awe (87:1).
* I prioritize the Hereafter over worldly materialism, remembering that eternity is better and more enduring (*Khayrun wa Abqa*) (87:17).
* I purify my heart and give Zakat with joy, claiming the success of *Qad aflaha man tazakka* (87:14).

## 13. Closing Supplication
$$\text{سُبْحَانَ رَبِّنَا الْأَعْلَى ، اللَّهُمَّ يَسِّرْنَا لِلْيُسْرَىٰ وَجَنِّبْنَا الْعُسْرَىٰ ، اللَّهُمَّ زَكِّ نُفُوسَنَا أَنْتَ خَيْرُ مَنْ زَكَّاهَا ، وَاجْعَلِ الْآخِرَةَ خَيْرًا لَنَا مِنَ الْأُولَىٰ}$$  
*"Exalted is our Lord, the Most High. O Allah, ease us toward ease and preserve us from hardship. O Allah, purify our souls; You are the best to purify them. And make the Hereafter better and more enduring for us than this first life. Amin."*
