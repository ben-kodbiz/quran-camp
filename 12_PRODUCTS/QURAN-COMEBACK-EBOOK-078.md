# CHAPTER 78: SURAH AN-NABA' (سُورَةُ النبأ)
**Classification:** Makki  
**Order in Mushaf:** 78  
**Total Verses:** 40  
**Famous Names:** Surat an-Naba', Amma Yatasa'alun, Juz 'Amma  

---

## 1. Why This Surah Matters
Surah An-Naba' is the grand, majestic gateway to the final thirtieth part of the Qur'an (*Juz 'Amma*). It opens with the piercing question that echoes across the streets of Makkah: 'About what are they asking one another? About the great news—that over which they are in disagreement' (*'Anin-naba'il-'adhim*). It guides human reason through nine breathtaking exhibits of divine engineering: the earth as a bed, the mountains as pegs, sleep for rest, the night as a cloak, the sun as a blazing lamp, and rainclouds pouring torrential water. It contrasts the horrifying ambush of Hell (*Mirsada*) with the triumphant success of the righteous (*Inna lil-muttaqina mafaza*), ending with the disbeliever's agonizing cry of regret: 'Oh, I wish that I were dust!' (*Ya laytani kuntu turaba*).

---

## 2. Surah Context
Revealed in early Makkah when the Prophet ﷺ first proclaimed physical resurrection and final judgment. The Quraysh clustered in mocking debate: 'Will we really be brought back to life after becoming dust?' Surah An-Naba' descended to silence their dispute.

---

## 3. Central Theme
**The Great Tiding of Resurrection (*An-Naba' al-'Adhim*), Nine Cosmic Proofs of Divine Power, the Ambush of Hell, and the Cry of Regret.**

---

## 4. Key Passages
### The Great News and Nine Cosmic Proofs (78:1–7)
$$\text{عَمَّ يَتَسَاءَلُونَ ۝ عَنِ النَّبَإِ الْعَظِيمِ ۝ الَّذِي هُمْ فِيهِ مُخْتَلِفُونَ ۝ كَلَّا سَيَعْلَمُونَ... أَلَمْ نَجْعَلِ الْأَرْضَ مِهَادًا ۝ وَالْجِبَالَ أَوْتَادًا}$$
*"About what are they asking one another? About the great tiding—That over which they are in disagreement. No! They are going to know... Have We not made the earth a resting place? And the mountains as stakes?"*

### The Day of the Trumpet and the Parting Sky (78:18–19)
$$\text{يَوْمَ يُنفَخُ فِي الصُّورِ فَتَأْتُونَ أَفْوَاجًا ۝ وَفُتِحَتِ السَّمَاءُ فَكَانَتْ أَبْوَابًا ۝ وَسُيِّرَتِ الْجِبَالُ فَكَانَتْ سَرَابًا}$$
*"The Day the Horn is blown and you will come forth in crowds, And the heaven is opened and will become gateways, And the mountains are moved and will be but a mirage."*

### The Ultimate Cry of Regret (78:40)
$$\text{إِنَّا أَنذَرْنَاكُمْ عَذَابًا قَرِيبًا يَوْمَ يَنظُرُ الْمَرْءُ مَا قَدَّمَتْ يَدَاهُ وَيَقُولُ الْكَافِرُ يَا لَيْتَنِي كُنتُ تُرَابًا}$$
*"Indeed, We have warned you of a near punishment on the Day when a man will observe what his hands have put forth and the disbeliever will say, 'Oh, I wish that I were dust!'"*

---

## 5. Translation Highlights (The Clear Quran)
* *78:9–11:* 'And made your sleep [a means for] rest, And made the night as clothing, And made the day for livelihood.'
* *78:31:* 'Indeed, for the righteous is attainment [success]...'
* *78:38:* 'The Day that the Spirit and the angels will stand in rows, they will not speak except one whom the Most Merciful permits, and he will say what is correct.'

---

## 6. What Classical Tafsir Explains
* **The Mountains as Pegs (*Awtada*) (Verse 7):** Geological science has confirmed that mountains have deep roots extending deep into the earth's lithosphere, anchoring the tectonic plates exactly like tent pegs (*Awtad*). The Qur'an used this precise geophysical metaphor fifteen centuries before modern geology.
* **Why the Sinner Wishes He Were Dust (Verse 40):** Ibn Umar and Abu Hurayrah (RA) explained: On Judgment Day, Allah will assemble all animals (even the hornless sheep to settle scores with the horned sheep). Once justice between animals is settled, Allah says to them: *Kuni turaba* ('Become dust!'), and they dissolve into soil. Seeing the animals dissolve into dust and escaping Hellfire, the human disbeliever will cry in desperate envy: *Ya laytani kuntu turaba!* ('Oh, if only I were an animal that became dust!').
---

## 7. Important Supporting Hadiths
1. **Settling Scores Even for the Hornless Sheep:**  
   *Sahih Muslim (Hadith 2582) from Abu Hurayrah (RA):*  
   "The Prophet ﷺ said regarding verse 78:40: 'All rights will be restored on the Day of Resurrection, even the hornless sheep will settle its score with the horned sheep; then Allah will command the animals: *Become dust!* And the disbeliever will cry: *Ya laytani kuntu turaba!*'"
2. **The Surahs That Whitened the Prophet's Hair:**  
   *Jami' at-Tirmidhi (Hadith 3297), Sahih:*  
   "Abu Bakr (RA) said: 'O Messenger of Allah, your hair has turned white!' The Prophet ﷺ replied: 'Surah Hud, Al-Waqi'ah, Al-Mursalat, 'Amma Yatasa'alun (An-Naba'), and Idha ash-Shamsu Kuwwirat have whitened my hair.'"
---

## 8. Key Concepts
1. **An-Naba' al-'Adhim (The Great Tiding):** The supreme news of physical resurrection, cosmic judgment, and the afterlife.
2. **Al-Jibal Awtada (Mountains as Pegs):** The profound geological truth that mountains stabilize the earth's crust like tent pegs.
3. **Nawm Subata (Sleep as Biological Renewal):** The divine engineering of sleep shutting down motor functions to restore cellular and emotional equilibrium.
4. **Ya Laytani Kuntu Turaba (The Cry of Eternal Envy):** The ultimate tragic regret of the condemned wishing they were lifeless dust rather than facing judgment.
---

## 9. Tadabbur (Guided Contemplation)
* **The Divine Architecture of Your Day (78:9–11):** Notice the perfect rhythm Allah built for human biology: Sleep as a restorative pause (*Subata*), the night as a dark protective cloak (*Libasa*), and the day for economic pursuit (*Ma'asha*). Modern humanity reverses this: staying awake until 3 AM under blue-screen lights and sleeping during the daytime. Align your lifestyle with the divine rhythm of creation to find physical and mental health.
* **A Punishment That is 'Near' (78:40):** Allah warns: *Inna andharnakum 'adhaban QARIBA* ('We have warned you of a NEAR punishment'). You might think the Day of Judgment is millions of years away. But the moment your soul leaves your body, your personal Akhirah begins! Your death is nearer to you than your next breath. Prepare today for the account your hands have sent ahead.
---

## 10. What This Means For Me
* I organize my sleep and work around the natural divine rhythm: sleeping early at night and working with energy in the day (78:9–11).
* I audit what my hands are putting forth every day, knowing I will face it all on Judgment Day (78:40).
* I live with unshakeable certainty in the 'Great News' (*An-Naba' al-'Adhim*), letting it calibrate my worldly ambitions.

## 13. Closing Supplication
$$\text{اللَّهُمَّ آمِنَّا يَوْمَ يُنْفَخُ فِي الصُّورِ ، وَاجْعَلْنَا مِنَ الْمُتَّقِينَ الَّذِينَ لَهُمْ مَفَازًا ، وَلَا تَجْعَلْنَا مِمَّنْ يَقُولُونَ يَا لَيْتَنِي كُنْتُ تُرَابًا ، اللَّهُمَّ اخْتِمْ بِالصَّالِحَاتِ أَعْمَالَنَا}$$  
*"O Allah, grant us security on the Day the Horn is blown, make us among the righteous for whom is supreme success, and do not make us among those who cry: 'Oh, I wish I were dust!' O Allah, conclude our lives with righteous deeds. Amin."*
