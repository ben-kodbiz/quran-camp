# CHAPTER 64: SURAH AT-TAGHABUN (سُورَةُ التغابن)
**Classification:** Madani  
**Order in Mushaf:** 64  
**Total Verses:** 18  
**Famous Names:** Surat at-Taghabun, Yawm at-Taghabun  

---

## 1. Why This Surah Matters
Surah At-Taghabun explores the ultimate economics of eternity. Named after the Day of Resurrection as *Yawm at-Taghabun* (The Day of Mutual Loss and Mutual Gain), it exposes the cosmic reality where the disbeliever realizes he traded away eternal paradise for eighty years of cheap worldly amusement, while the patient believer realizes his small sacrifices have yielded infinite palaces in Jannah. It warns that even within one's own home, spouses and children can become subtle spiritual adversaries (*Inna min azwajikum wa awladikum 'aduwwan lakum fahdharuhum*), yet commands that family members be treated with pardon, overlooking, and forgiveness. It balances human effort with divine capacity: 'So fear Allah as much as you are able' (64:16).

---

## 2. Surah Context
Revealed in Madinah regarding Muslim men in Makkah who wished to make the Hijrah to join the Prophet ﷺ, but whose wives and children wept, pleaded, and emotionally blackmailed them: 'Do not abandon us to poverty!' Some men stayed behind, losing immense spiritual rewards. When they later migrated, they were tempted to punish their families, but Allah commanded forgiveness (64:14).

---

## 3. Central Theme
**The Day of Absolute Mutual Valuation (*At-Taghabun*), the Spiritual Crucible of Family and Wealth, and Fearing Allah to the Best of One's Ability (*Fattaqullaha mastata'tum*).**

---

## 4. Key Passages
### The Day of Mutual Loss and Gain (64:9)
$$\text{يَوْمَ يَجْمَعُكُمْ لِيَوْمِ الْجَمْعِ ۖ ذَٰلِكَ يَوْمُ التَّغَابُنِ ۗ وَمَن يُؤْمِن بِاللَّهِ وَيَعْمَلْ صَالِحًا يُكَفِّرْ عَنْهُ سَيِّئَاتِهِ وَيُدْخِلْهُ جَنَّاتٍ تَجْرِي مِن تَحْتِهَا الْأَنْهَارُ خَالِدِينَ فِيهَا أَبَدًا ۚ ذَٰلِكَ الْفَوْزُ الْعَظِيمُ}$$
*"The Day He will assemble you for the Day of Assembly—that is the Day of Mutual Loss and Gain. And whoever believes in Allah and does righteousness—He will remove from him his misdeeds and admit him to gardens beneath which rivers flow, wherein they will abide forever. That is the great attainment."*

### Navigating Family Trials with Pardon (64:14)
$$\text{يَا أَيُّهَا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا إِنَّ مِنْ أَزْوَاجِكُمْ وَأَوْلَادِكُمْ عَدُوًّا لَّكُمْ فَاحْذَرُوهُمْ ۚ وَإِن تَعْفُوا وَتَصْفَحُوا وَتَغْفِرُوا فَإِنَّ اللَّهَ غَفُورٌ رَّحِيمٌ}$$
*"O you who have believed, indeed, among your spouses and your children are enemies to you, so beware of them. But if you pardon and overlook and forgive—then indeed, Allah is Forgiving and Merciful."*

### Fear Allah as Much as You Are Able (64:16)
$$\text{فَاتَّقُوا اللَّهَ مَا اسْتَطَعْتُمْ وَاسْمَعُوا وَأَطِيعُوا وَأَنفِقُوا خَيْرًا لِّأَنفُسِكُمْ ۗ وَمَن يُوقَ شُحَّ نَفْسِهِ فَأُولَٰئِكَ هُمُ الْمُفْلِحُونَ}$$
*"So fear Allah as much as you are able and listen and obey and spend [in the way of Allah]; it is better for your selves. And whoever is protected from the stinginess of his soul—it is those who will be the successful."*

---

## 5. Translation Highlights (The Clear Quran)
* *64:11:* 'No disaster strikes except by permission of Allah. And whoever believes in Allah—He will guide his heart. And Allah is Knowing of all things.'
* *64:15:* 'Your wealth and your children are but a trial, and Allah has with Him a great reward.'
* *64:17:* 'If you loan Allah a goodly loan, He will multiply it for you and forgive you. And Allah is Most Appreciative and Forbearing.'

---

## 6. What Classical Tafsir Explains
* **The Meaning of 'At-Taghabun' (Verse 9):** Ibn Abbas (RA) and Imam Ibn Kathir explain that *At-Taghabun* is derived from *Ghabn* (a catastrophic commercial transaction where one party is utterly defrauded or cheated). On Judgment Day, the people of Hell realize they traded eternity for a fleeting illusion; while the believers take possession of the heavenly dwellings that the disbelievers forfeited through their rejection of faith.
* **Guiding the Broken Heart in Calamity (Verse 11):** Al-Qurtubi and Alqamah explain *Wa man yu'min billahi yahdi qalbah*: 'It refers to the person who, when struck by a sudden tragedy, knows with absolute conviction that it is from Allah, accepts His decree, and surrenders in peace.' Allah grants his heart serene guidance and comfort amidst tears.
---

## 7. Important Supporting Hadiths
1. **Children as a Cause for Greed and Fear:**  
   *Jami' at-Tirmidhi (Hadith 3278), Sahih:*  
   "The Prophet ﷺ picked up his grandsons Al-Hasan and Al-Husayn, hugged them, and said: 'Indeed, children are a cause for stinginess, cowardice, and grief (*Jubn, Bukhl, Mahzanah*), yet they are from the fragrant flowers of Allah!'"
2. **Fearing Allah to One's Full Capacity:**  
   *Sahih al-Bukhari (Hadith 7288) from Abu Hurayrah (RA):*  
   "Regarding verse 64:16, the Prophet ﷺ said: 'Whatever I command you to do, do of it as much as you are able (*Mastata'tum*); and whatever I forbid you from, avoid it entirely.'"
---

## 8. Key Concepts
1. **Yawm at-Taghabun (The Day of Mutual Evaluation):** The ultimate cosmic exchange where eternal gains and eternal losses are finalized.
2. **Hidayat al-Qalb 'ind al-Musibah (Heart Guidance in Calamity):** The special divine peace granted to the believer who accepts God's decree during tragedy.
3. **Fitnat al-Amwal wal-Awlad (The Test of Wealth and Family):** The reality that love for spouses and children must never eclipse loyalty to divine commandments.
4. **Fattaqullaha Mastata'tum (Do What You Are Able):** The mercy of the Shari'ah matching obligations to human physical and mental capacity.
---

## 9. Tadabbur (Guided Contemplation)
* **He Will Guide His Heart (64:11):** Read this verse whenever grief or loss strikes your home: *Wa man yu'min billahi yahdi qalbah.* When tragedy hits—a sudden death, a lost job, a broken relationship—the human heart shatters. But if you whisper from the depths of your pain: 'This was permitted by Allah, and I trust my Lord,' Allah places His hand over your heart. He pours in a supernatural tranquility that medicine cannot provide.
* **The Family as a Spiritual Test (64:14–15):** How can a beloved spouse or child be an 'enemy' (*'Aduwwan lakum*)? Not because they hate you, but because their worldly demands, desires, and tears can pressure you into earning unlawful income, skipping prayers, compromising ethics, or neglecting charity. Love your family fiercely, but love Allah more. And when family members let you down, meet them with *'Afw* (pardon) and *Safh* (grace).
---

## 10. What This Means For Me
* In every sudden loss or hardship, I surrender to Allah's decree, asking Him to guide and anchor my heart (64:11).
* I lead my family with love and firm principles, never compromising halal earnings or religious duties for worldly pressure (64:14).
* I respond to domestic friction with gracious forgiveness (*'Afw wa Safh*), mirroring Allah's mercy.
* I fear Allah and obey His commands to the absolute best of my daily capacity (64:16).

---

## 11. One Practical Action
**Practice 'Safh' (Overlooking) in Your Home Today:**  
Act on verse 64:14 (*Wa in ta'fu wa tasfahu wa taghfiru*): Forgive a spouse, child, or family member today for an annoying mistake or grievance without bringing it up or holding it over their head.

## 12. Reflection Questions
1. When personal tragedy strikes, do I panic or do I experience the heart-guidance of *Yahdi Qalbah* (64:11)?
2. Am I compromising my moral or religious principles to satisfy the worldly demands of my family (64:14)?
3. Are my daily investments of time securing 'Mutual Gain' (*Taghabun*) in Paradise, or am I trading eternity for illusion (64:9)?

## 13. Closing Supplication
$$\text{اللَّهُمَّ يَا مَنْ بِيَدِهِ مَلَكُوتُ كُلِّ شَيْءٍ ، اهْدِ قُلُوبَنَا عِنْدَ الْمَصَائِبِ ، وَأَصْلِحْ لَنَا أَزْوَاجَنَا وَذُرِّيَّاتِنَا ، وَارْزُقْنَا أَنْ نَتَّقِيَكَ مَا اسْتَطَعْنَا ، وَلَا تَجْعَلْنَا مِنَ الْخَاسِرِينَ يَوْمَ التَّغَابُنِ}$$  
*"O Allah, O One in whose hand is the realm of all things: Guide our hearts during calamities, rectify for us our spouses and our offspring, bless us to fear You to the best of our ability, and do not make us among the losers on the Day of Mutual Loss and Gain. Amin."*
