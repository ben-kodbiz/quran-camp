The Recitation to the Jinn Council & The 31 Refrains

In Jami' at-Tirmidhi (3291) and Al-Mustadrak lil-Hakim (2/473, Sahih), Jabir ibn Abdillah (RA) narrated: 'The Messenger of Allah ﷺ went out to his companions and recited Surah Ar-Rahman from beginning to end, but they remained quiet in solemn reflection. The Prophet ﷺ said: *'I recited it to the jinn on the Night of the Jinn, and they were better in response than you! Every time I recited: 'So which of the favors of your Lord will you both deny?', they replied: 'There is nothing of Your favors, our Lord, that we deny; and unto You belongs all praise!'*'

The refrain occurs exactly 31 times: 8 following worldly creation and origins, 7 following punishment and cosmic reckoning, and 16 following the twin tiers of Paradise.

Authorities: Jami' at-Tirmidhi 3291 • Al-Mustadrak lil-Hakim 2/473 • Tafsir Ibn Kathir (7/490) • Al-Baghawi

Kullu Man 'Alayha Fan: The Universal Dissolution of Creation (55:26–27)

This pair of verses forms the sublime fulcrum of Surah Ar-Rahman: 'Everyone upon the earth will perish, and there will remain the Face of your Lord, Owner of Majesty and Honor (*Dhu'l-Jalali wal-Ikram*).'

The Prophet ﷺ frequently supplicated with this name, saying in Jami' at-Tirmidhi (3525): 'Cling persistently to *Ya Dhal-Jalali wal-Ikram*!' It balances holy reverence (Jalal) with intimate divine generosity (Ikram), reminding finite mortals that true permanence is found only in devotion to the Living God.

Authorities: Jami' at-Tirmidhi 3525 • Sahih Muslim • Tafsir al-Tabari (22/306) • Tafsir Ibn Kathir (7/497)

The Two Tiers of Paradise: The Upper and Lower Jannatan (55:46, 62)

Surah Ar-Rahman provides an architectural breakdown of Paradise into two distinct tiers: The first pair of Gardens (Ayah 46) is for the foremost believers who lived in deep awe (*Al-Muqarraboon*), featuring spreading branches (Afnan), flowing springs, and couches lined with heavy silk brocade (Istabraq).

The second pair of Gardens (Ayah 62: *Wa min doonihima jannatan*) is for the general righteous believers (*Ashab al-Yameen*), featuring dark green foliage (Mudhammatan), gushing springs, and green cushions (Rafraf). As the Prophet explained in Sahih al-Bukhari (4878): 'Two gardens of silver, their vessels and all within them; and two gardens of gold, their vessels and all within them.'

Authorities: Sahih al-Bukhari 4878 • Sahih Muslim 180 • Tafsir al-Qurtubi (17/175) • Madarij as-Salikin

Hal Jaza'u al-Ihsani Illa al-Ihsan? (55:60)

Surah Ar-Rahman articulates the universal golden rule of divine-human reciprocity: 'Is the reward for excellence (*Ihsan*) anything other than excellence?!'

Ibn Abbas explained: 'Whoever says *La ilaha illallah* in this world and acts with righteousness, his recompense from Allah is nothing less than entry into the Gardens of Paradise.' It affirms that every tear of sincere worship, every act of charitable kindness, and every breath of patience is met with infinite, multiplying divine generosity.

Authorities: Tafsir al-Tabari (22/318) • Tafsir Ibn Kathir (7/505) • Al-Bahr al-Muheet
