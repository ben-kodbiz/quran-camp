Cosmic Curvature & Spherical Wrapping (39:5)

Surah Az-Zumar employs the precise lexical root *K-W-R* in *Yukawwiru al-layla 'alan-nahar* (He wraps the night over the day and wraps the day over the night). In Arabic lexicography, *Takweer* means winding a turban around a spherical head.

Classical scholars like Ibn Hazm and Al-Razi noted that this phrasing confirms the celestial sphere of the earth, as night wraps over day continuously without seam or pause, governing planetary time with flawless precision.

Authorities: Tafsir al-Kabir (Al-Razi) • Al-Fasl fil-Milal wal-Ahwa' (Ibn Hazm) • Tafsir Ibn Kathir

Shivering Skins & Ahsan al-Hadeeth (39:23)

Allah describes the Qur'an as *Ahsana al-Hadeeth* (The Best of Narratives), a mutually corroborating scripture (*Kitaban Mutashabihan*) repeating its truths (*Mathaniya*).

When recited, those who revere their Lord experience an involuntary somatic shivering of their skins (*Taqsha'irru minhu julood*), followed by deep relaxation where their flesh, skin, and hearts soften into remembrance. It bridges holy awe (*Khashyah*) with transcendent tranquility (*Sakeenah*).

Authorities: Sahih al-Bukhari • Tafsir al-Qurtubi (15/248) • Madarij as-Salikin (Ibn al-Qayyim)

The Ocean of Unbounded Mercy (39:53)

Sahih al-Bukhari (4810) and Sahih Muslim (122) relate from Ibn Abbas that pagan polytheists who had committed extensive murder and adultery came to the Prophet ﷺ asking if there was any hope for redemption.

Allah revealed this supreme verse of hope: 'Say: O My servants who have transgressed against their own souls! Do not despair of the mercy of Allah. Indeed, Allah forgives all sins entirely. Indeed, He is the Forgiving, the Merciful.'

Notice the tender divine address: *Ya 'Ibadi* (O My servants!), welcoming back the most desperate sinners into the sanctuary of His mercy.

Authorities: Sahih al-Bukhari 4810 • Sahih Muslim 122 • Asbab an-Nuzul (Al-Wahidi)

The Earth in His Grip & Heavens Folded (39:67)

In Sahih al-Bukhari (4811) and Sahih Muslim (2786), Ibn Mas'ud (RA) narrates that a Jewish rabbi approached the Prophet ﷺ saying: 'We find in our scriptures that Allah puts the heavens on one Finger, the earth on one Finger, the trees on one Finger, the waters on one Finger, and the rest of creation on one Finger, and says: I am the Sovereign!'

The Prophet ﷺ laughed with delight, confirming the truth of the statement, and recited Ayah 67: 'And they have not appraised Allah with true appraisal, while the earth entirely will be within His grip on the Day of Resurrection, and the heavens will be folded in His right hand.'

Authorities: Sahih al-Bukhari 4811 • Sahih Muslim 2786 • Tafsir Ibn Kathir (7/116)
