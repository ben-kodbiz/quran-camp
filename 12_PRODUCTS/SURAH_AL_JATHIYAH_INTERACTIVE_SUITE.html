Refuting Dahriyyah Atheism: Cursing Time (45:24)

Surah Al-Jathiyah records the timeless motto of philosophical materialism: 'There is nothing but our worldly life; we die and we live, and nothing destroys us except time (*Ad-Dahr*).' They deny the Creator, the soul, and resurrection, reducing human existence to biological mechanics.

In Sahih al-Bukhari (4826) and Sahih Muslim (2246), the Prophet ﷺ conveyed the divine Hadith Qudsi: 'The son of Adam hurts Me: he curses Time, whereas I am Time; in My hand is the night and day, and I alternate them as I will.' Attributing sovereign causality to blind time is an insult to the Living Sovereign.

Authorities: Sahih al-Bukhari 4826 • Sahih Muslim 2246 • Tafsir Ibn Kathir (7/276)

The Kneeling Nations on Judgment Day (45:28)

Surah Al-Jathiyah derives its name from this terrifying visual: 'And you will see every nation kneeling [on its knees]. Every nation will be called to its record.'

Kings, emperors, generals, and their subjects will kneel in absolute equality and humility before the Almighty Judge, stripped of all earthly pride, waiting for the celestial book that pronounces truth without bias or omission.

Authorities: Tafsir al-Tabari (22/86) • Tafsir al-Qurtubi (16/168) • Tafsir Ibn Kathir

Today We Forget You as You Forgot This Meeting (45:34)

Allah addresses the obstinate deniers who mocked revelation and indulged in worldly arrogance: 'Today We will forget you just as you forgot the meeting of this Day of yours!'

Sunni theologians explain that divine 'forgetting' (*Nisyan*) here signifies abandonment in retribution, leaving the criminal in the consequences of his own choices without intercession, relief, or second chances.

Authorities: Tafsir al-Baghawi • Tafsir Ibn Kathir (7/282) • Al-Mufradat

To Him Belongs All Majesty (Al-Kibriya') (45:36–37)

Surah Al-Jathiyah closes the entire magnificent seven-surah Hawameem cycle with a breathtaking doxology: 'So to Allah belongs all praise—Lord of the heavens and Lord of the earth, Lord of the worlds. And to Him belongs all majesty in the heavens and the earth, and He is the Almighty, the All-Wise.'

In Sahih Muslim, the Prophet ﷺ quoted the Hadith Qudsi: 'Majesty is My cloak, and Pride is My garment; whoever disputes with Me regarding either of them, I will cast him into the Fire.' True faith begins when mortal ego collapses before divine Kibriya'.

Authorities: Sahih Muslim 2620 • Tafsir al-Qurtubi (16/176) • Tafsir Ibn Kathir (7/285)
