# CHAPTER 113: SURAH AL-FALAQ (سُورَةُ الفلق)
**Classification:** Makki  
**Order in Mushaf:** 113  
**Total Verses:** 5  
**Famous Names:** Surat al-Falaq, Qul A'udhu bi-Rabbil Falaq, Al-Mu'awwidhatayn  

---

## 1. Why This Surah Matters
Surah Al-Falaq is the divine fortress against external cosmic and human evils—the first half of the two peerless sanctuaries of protection (*Al-Mu'awwidhatayn*). The Prophet ﷺ said: 'There have not been revealed the like of these two Surahs.' It commands humanity to seek refuge in the Lord of the Splitting Daybreak (*Rabb al-Falaq*)—the One whose light shatters the blackest midnight and splits the seed to produce life—against four specific, terrifying external dangers: the evil of all created things (*Sharri ma khalaq*); the sinister perils of the pitch-black night when it settles (*Ghasiqin idha waqab*); the dark occult malice of sorcerers who blow on knots (*An-Naffathati fil 'uqad*); and the venomous, toxic eye of the envious person when he envies (*Hasidin idha hasad*).

---

## 2. Surah Context
Revealed alongside Surah An-Nas (according to primary narrations) when the Jewish sorcerer Labid ibn al-A'sam cast a severe binding spell upon the Prophet ﷺ using hair and eleven knots buried in a well. Jibril descended with the two Surahs (totalling 11 verses); with the recitation of each verse, a knot untied and the Prophet was completely liberated.

---

## 3. Central Theme
**Seeking Inviolable Divine Refuge with the Lord of the Daybreak from All External Horrors: Darkness, Occult Sorcery, and the Destructive Eye of Envy.**

---

## 4. Key Passages
### The Lord of Daybreak and General Evil (113:1–2)
$$\text{قُلْ أَعُوذُ بِرَبِّ الْفَلَقِ ۝ مِن شَرِّ مَا خَلَقَ}$$
*"Say, 'I seek refuge in the Lord of daybreak, From the evil of that which He created.'"*

### The Three Specific Perils (113:3–5)
$$\text{وَمِن شَرِّ غَاسِقٍ إِذَا وَقَبَ ۝ وَمِن شَرِّ النَّفَّاثَاتِ فِي الْعُقَدِ ۝ وَمِن شَرِّ حَاسِدٍ إِذَا حَسَدَ}$$
*"And from the evil of darkness when it settles, And from the evil of the blowers in knots, And from the evil of an envier when he envies.'"*

---

## 5. Translation Highlights (The Clear Quran)
* *113:1:* 'Say: I seek refuge with the Lord of the Daybreak (*Rabbil-Falaq*).'
* *113:2:* 'From the evil of whatever He has created (*Sharri ma khalaq*).'
* *113:3:* 'And from the evil of the night when darkness settles (*Ghasiqin idha waqab*).'
* *113:4:* 'And from the evil of those who blow on knots (*Naffathati fil 'uqad*).'
* *113:5:* 'And from the evil of an envier when he envies (*Hasidin idha hasad*).'

---

## 6. What Classical Tafsir Explains
* **Why Allah is 'Lord of the Daybreak' (*Al-Falaq*) (Verse 1):** Ibn Kathir and As-Sa'di explain that *Falaq* is the splitting of dawn through the suffocating darkness of night. The One who has the power to split the darkest cosmic night and flood the world with warm morning light has the power to dispel the darkest grief, magical affliction, and psychic harm from your life.
* **The Evil of the Envier (*Hasid*) (Verse 5):** Al-Qurtubi explains that *Hasad* (destructive envy) is the burning desire that a blessing bestowed by Allah upon another person be stripped away and destroyed. The phrase *Idha hasad* ('when he envies') indicates that envy becomes active and toxic when the envier acts upon his jealousy through evil eyes, malicious gossip, or sabotage.
* **Blowing on Knots (*An-Naffathat fil 'Uqad*) (Verse 4):** Classical exegetes note that practitioners of dark occult magic tie cords into knots and blow toxic incantations and spit upon them to bind psychological harm upon their victims. Divine revelation explicitly validates the reality of these spiritual hazards and provides the ultimate supernatural cure.
---

## 7. Important Supporting Hadiths
1. **No Protection Like the Two Surahs:**  
   *Sunan an-Nasa'i (Hadith 5430) & Sunan Abi Dawud (Hadith 1463), Sahih:*  
   "The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said to 'Uqbah ibn 'Amir: 'Shall I not teach you the best two Surahs recited for refuge?' He taught him: *Qul a'udhu bi-Rabbil Falaq* and *Qul a'udhu bi-Rabbin Nas*, then said: 'No seeker of refuge has ever sought refuge with anything like them!'"
2. **The Reality of the Evil Eye:**  
   *Sahih Muslim (Hadith 2188) from Ibn 'Abbas (RA):*  
   "The Prophet ﷺ said: 'The evil eye (*Al-'Ayn*) is real; and if anything were to overtake the divine decree, it would be the evil eye.'"
3. **Blowing into Hands Before Sleeping:**  
   *Sahih al-Bukhari (Hadith 5017) from 'A'ishah (RA):*  
   "Whenever the Prophet ﷺ went to bed each night, he cupped his hands together, blew into them, and recited: *Qul huwallahu ahad*, *Qul a'udhu bi-Rabbil Falaq*, and *Qul a'udhu bi-Rabbin Nas*, then wiped his hands over as much of his body as he could reach, starting with his head, face, and the front of his body, doing that three times."
---

## 8. Key Concepts
1. **Al-Falaq (The Cleaving Daybreak):** The divine power to split darkness and birth light and relief.
2. **Isti'adhah (Seeking Divine Sanctuary):** Fleeing from terrifying cosmic forces to the shelter of the Almighty Creator.
3. **Naffathat (The Blowers on Knots):** The dark reality of occult sorcery countered by prophetic recitation.
4. **Hasad (Destructive Envy):** The spiritual poison that resents divine distribution of blessings, cured by Isti'adhah.
---

## 9. Tadabbur (Guided Contemplation)
* **Watching the Dawn Break:** Look at the horizon during Fajr: Watch how effortlessly Allah rips apart the black shroud of night with morning rays. Whatever despair or darkness you feel right now, the Lord of *Al-Falaq* can tear it open in seconds.
* **Purging Your Own Envy First:** Before seeking protection from other people's envy, audit your own heart: When someone buys a nicer house or gets married, do you feel a pang of jealousy? Immediately say: *Allahumma barik lahu* (O Allah, bless them in it) to kill the snake of envy.
* **The Shield of the Nightly Routine:** Do not go to sleep spiritually naked without your armor. Recite the Mu'awwidhatayn every single night without exception.
---

## 10. What This Means For Me
* Evil is real, occult harm is real, and envy is real; but Allah's words are infinitely more powerful than all of them combined.
* I do not need talismans, blue beads, amulets, or superstitious charms; the authentic Qur'anic *Isti'adhah* is my complete and sufficient protection.
* Whenever I see a blessing upon another believer, I must pray for Allah to increase them, protecting myself from becoming a *Hasid*.

## 11. One Practical Action
**The Complete Bedtime Ruqyah:**  
Perform the full prophetic bedtime protection tonight: Cup your hands, blow softly into your palms, recite Surah Al-Ikhlas, Al-Falaq, and An-Nas, and wipe your hands over your entire head, face, and front of your torso. Repeat this three complete times before sleeping.
