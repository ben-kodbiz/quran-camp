# CHAPTER 52: SURAH AT-TUR (سُورَةُ الطور)
**Classification:** Makki  
**Order in Mushaf:** 52  
**Total Verses:** 49  
**Famous Names:** Surat at-Tur  

---

## 1. Why This Surah Matters
Surah At-Tur is the earth-shaking declaration of divine reality. It begins with five solemn oaths: by Mount Sinai (*At-Tur*), by an Inscribed Book on unfolded parchment, by the Frequented House in heaven (*Al-Bayt al-Ma'mur*), by the elevated celestial roof, and by the swollen, fiery sea. It contains the verse that caused the pagan chief Jubayr ibn Mut'im's heart to almost fly out of his chest when he heard the Prophet ﷺ reciting it in Maghrib: 'Or were they created by nothing, or were they the creators? Or did they create the heavens and the earth? Rather, they are not certain' (52:35–36). It reveals the breathtaking family reunion of Paradise: righteous children reunited with their parents without deducting an ounce of anyone's reward (52:21).

---

## 2. Surah Context
Revealed in Makkah when the Quraysh plotted to wait for the Prophet's ﷺ death, dismissing him as a poet struck by poetic fate. Surah At-Tur demolished their skepticism with four devastating philosophical questions.

---

## 3. Central Theme
**The Inevitability of Judgment, the Intellectual Bankruptcy of Atheism, Eternal Family Reunions in Jannah, and Night Glorification.**

---

## 4. Key Passages
### The Great Four Philosophical Questions (52:35–37)
$$\text{أَمْ خُلِقُوا مِنْ غَيْرِ شَيْءٍ أَمْ هُمُ الْخَالِقُونَ ۝ أَمْ خَلَقُوا السَّمَاوَاتِ وَالْأَرْضَ ۚ بَل لَّا يُوقِنُونَ ۝ أَمْ عِندَهُمْ خَزَائِنُ رَبِّكَ أَمْ هُمُ الْمُصَيْطِرُونَ}$$
*"Or were they created by nothing, or were they the creators [of themselves]? Or did they create the heavens and the earth? Rather, they are not certain. Or have they the depositories [of the provisions] of your Lord, or are they the controllers [of them]?"*

### The Eternal Family Reunion in Paradise (52:21)
$$\text{وَالَّذِينَ آمَنُوا وَاتَّبَعَتْهُمْ ذُرِّيَّتُهُم بِإِيمَانٍ أَلْحَقْنَا بِهِمْ ذُرِّيَّتَهُمْ وَمَا أَلَتْنَاهُم مِّنْ عَمَلِهِم مِّن شَيْءٍ ۚ كُلُّ امْرِئٍ بِمَا كَسَبَ رَهِينٌ}$$
*"And those who believed and whose descendants followed them in faith—We will join with them their descendants, and We will not deprive them of anything of their deeds. Every person, for what he earned, is retained."*

### Praising Allah When Rising and at Night (52:48–49)
$$\text{وَاصْبِرْ لِحُكْمِ رَبِّكَ فَإِنَّكَ بِأَعْيُنِنَا ۖ وَسَبِّحْ بِحَمْدِ رَبِّكَ حِينَ تَقُومُ ۝ وَمِنَ اللَّيْلِ فَسَبِّحْهُ وَإِدْبَارَ النُّجُومِ}$$
*"And be patient, [O Muhammad], for the decision of your Lord, for indeed, you are in Our eyes [under Our care]. And exalt [Allah] with praise of your Lord when you arise. And in a part of the night exalt Him and after [the setting of] the stars."*

---

## 5. Translation Highlights (The Clear Quran)
* *52:7–8:* 'Indeed, the punishment of your Lord will occur. Of it there is no preventer.'
* *52:26–28:* 'They will say, "Indeed, we were before among our people fearful [of displeasure]. But Allah conferred favor upon us and protected us from the punishment of the Scorching Fire. Indeed, we used to supplicate Him before. Indeed, it is He who is the Beneficent, the Merciful."'
* *52:30:* 'Or do they say, "[He is] a poet for whom we anticipate the misfortune of time"?'

---

## 6. What Classical Tafsir Explains
* **The Conversion of Jubayr ibn Mut'im (Verses 35–36):** Imam al-Bukhari records that Jubayr ibn Mut'im said: 'I heard the Prophet ﷺ reciting Surah At-Tur in Maghrib prayer while I was still a polytheist. When he reached verses 35–36 (*Am khuliqu min ghayri shay'in am humul-khaliqun*), my heart felt as if it was about to fly out of my chest, and that was the first day faith settled in my heart!' These verses pose an airtight logical disjunction: You cannot come from nothing, and you cannot create yourself; therefore, you are created by the One True God.
* **The Mercy of Elevating Descendants (Verse 21):** Ibn Abbas (RA) explained: 'Allah will elevate the rank of the believer's children in Paradise to match their parent's higher station, even if their own deeds were lesser, so that the parent's eyes may be delighted—without diminishing the parent's reward in the least!'

## 7. Important Supporting Hadiths
1. **Recitation of Surah At-Tur in Maghrib:**  
   *Sahih al-Bukhari (Hadith 4854) from Jubayr ibn Mut'im (RA):*  
   "Jubayr ibn Mut'im narrated: 'I heard the Messenger of Allah ﷺ recite in Maghrib prayer Surah At-Tur...'"
2. **You Are Under Our Watchful Care:**  
   *Sunan Abi Dawud (Hadith 5085), Hasan:*  
   "Regarding verse 52:48 (*Fa-innaka bi-a'yunina*), the Prophet ﷺ taught: 'Whenever you rise from your sleep, say: *Subhanakallahumma wa bihamdika, ash-hadu an la ilaha illa Anta, astaghfiruka wa atubu ilayk*.'"
---

## 8. Key Concepts
1. **Burhan al-Khalq (The Proof of Creation):** The irrefutable philosophical argument proving divine creation through logical elimination of non-causes.
2. **Ilhaq adh-Dhurriyyah (Family Elevation in Jannah):** The divine grace reuniting righteous family members in the highest degrees of Paradise.
3. **Fa-innaka Bi-A'yunina (Under Divine Watchful Care):** The profound reassurance that the believer is under the protective, loving gaze of Allah during persecution.
4. **Al-Bayt al-Ma'mur (The Frequented House):** The heavenly sanctuary directly above the Ka'bah where 70,000 angels pray daily, never returning.
---

## 9. Tadabbur (Guided Contemplation)
* **You Are In Our Eyes (52:48):** Whenever you feel abandoned, slandered, or lonely, let these words wash over your heart: *Fa-innaka bi-a'yunina* ('For indeed, you are in Our eyes'). Allah sees every teardrop you swallow, every silent sacrifice you make, and every hardship you endure for His sake. You are not forgotten; you are under the personal watch of the Lord of the worlds.
* **The Secret of Jannah: Fear in Dunya (52:26–28):** Listen to the conversation of the people of Paradise: *Qalu inna kunna qablu fi ahlina mushfiqin* ('We used to be fearful of Allah's displeasure when we were among our families'). If you live with reverent caution and Taqwa in this world, Allah will grant you absolute peace in the next world. Fear Him today, and be secure forever.
---

## 10. What This Means For Me
* I rest my heart in the certainty of verse 52:48, knowing that in trials I am under Allah's watchful care (*Bi-a'yunina*).
* I strive to raise my family upon faith, yearning for the eternal family reunion promised in Paradise (52:21).
* I glorify Allah whenever I rise from sleep and during the stillness of the night (52:48–49).
