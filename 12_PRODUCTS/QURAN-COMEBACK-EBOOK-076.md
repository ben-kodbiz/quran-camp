# CHAPTER 76: SURAH AL-INSAN (سُورَةُ الإنسان)
**Classification:** Madani  
**Order in Mushaf:** 76  
**Total Verses:** 31  
**Famous Names:** Surat al-Insan, Surat ad-Dahr, Hal Ata  

---

## 1. Why This Surah Matters
Surah Al-Insan is the tender, radiant symphony of human creation and pure spiritual altruism. Beloved to the Sunnah, the Prophet ﷺ recited it every Friday morning in Fajr prayer alongside Surah As-Sajdah. It begins with human humility: 'Has there [not] come upon man a period of time when he was not a thing [even] mentioned?' (76:1). It immortalizes the highest plateau of charitable sincerity in religious history: feeding the poor, the orphan, and the prisoner purely for the Face of Allah: 'We feed you only for the Face of Allah; we wish not from you reward or gratitude' (*Innama nut'imukum li-wajhillahi la nuridu minkum jaza'an wa la shukura*). It rewards this selfless purity with the most luxuriant, detailed descriptions of Paradise in all of scripture.

---

## 2. Surah Context
Revealed regarding Ali ibn Abi Talib, Fatimah (RA), and their children (or believers in Madinah) who fasted three consecutive days to fulfill a vow; each evening at the moment of breaking fast, a beggar, an orphan, and a captive knocked at their door, and they gave away their only barley loaves, breaking their fast on plain water.

---

## 3. Central Theme
**Human Origins from Humble Beginnings, Sincere Altruism for the Face of Allah (*Ikhlas*), and the Breathtaking Luxuries of Paradise.**

---

## 4. Key Passages
### Human Non-Existence and Creation (76:1–2)
$$\text{هَلْ أَتَىٰ عَلَى الْإِنسَانِ حِينٌ مِّنَ الدَّهْرِ لَمْ يَكُن شَيْئًا مَّذْكُورًا ۝ إِنَّا خَلَقْنَا الْإِنسَانَ مِن نُّطْفَةٍ أَمْشَاجٍ نَّبْتَلِيهِ فَجَعَلْنَاهُ سَمِيعًا بَصِيرًا}$$
*"Has there [not] come upon man a period of time when he was not a thing [even] mentioned? Indeed, We created man from a sperm-drop mixture that We may try him; and We made him hearing and seeing."*

### Feeding for the Face of Allah Alone (76:8–9)
$$\text{وَيُطْعِمُونَ الطَّعَامَ عَلَىٰ حُبِّهِ مِسْكِينًا وَيَتِيمًا وَأَسِيرًا ۝ إِنَّمَا نُطْعِمُكُمْ لِوَجْهِ اللَّهِ لَا نُرِيدُ مِنكُمْ جَزَاءً وَلَا شُكُورًا}$$
*"And they give food in spite of love for it to the needy, the orphan, and the captive, [Saying], 'We feed you only for the Face of Allah. We wish not from you reward or gratitude.'"*

### The Rewards of Paradise (76:21–22)
$$\text{عَالِيَهُمْ ثِيَابُ سُندُسٍ خُضْرٌ وَإِسْتَبْرَقٌ ۖ وَحُلُّوا أَسَاوِرَ مِن فِضَّةٍ وَسَقَاهُمْ رَبُّهُمْ شَرَابًا طَهُورًا ۝ إِنَّ هَٰذَا كَانَ لَكُمْ جَزَاءً وَكَانَ سَعْيُكُم مَّشْكُورًا}$$
*"Upon them will be green garments of fine silk and brocade. And they will be adorned with bracelets of silver, and their Lord will give them a purifying drink. [And it will be said], 'Indeed, this is for you a reward, and your effort has been appreciated.'"*

## 5. Translation Highlights (The Clear Quran)
* *76:3:* 'Indeed, We guided him to the way, be he grateful or be he ungrateful.'
* *76:24:* 'So be patient for the decision of your Lord and do not obey from among them a sinner or an ungrateful [disbeliever].'
* *76:25–26:* 'And remember the name of your Lord [in prayer] morning and evening, And during the night prostrate to Him and exalt Him a long [part of the] night.'

---

## 6. What Classical Tafsir Explains
* **A Period When You Were Nothing (Verse 1):** Imam Ibn Kathir writes: 'Allah reminds humanity of their humble beginnings: For billions of years before your birth, the universe existed and your name was never mentioned; you were absolute nothingness!' Remembering that you came from non-existence completely shatters human arrogance.
* **Not Even Expecting a 'Thank You' (Verse 9):** Mujahid and Ibn Abbas explain: *La nuridu minkum jaza'an wa la shukura* ('We want from you neither compensation nor thanks'). They did not even utter these words with their tongues to make the poor feel indebted; Allah knew this pure state inside their hearts! Sincerity means not even needing a 'thank you' from the people you help.
---

## 7. Important Supporting Hadiths
1. **The Friday Fajr Recitation:**  
   *Sahih al-Bukhari (Hadith 891) from Abu Hurayrah (RA):*  
   "The Messenger of Allah ﷺ used to recite in the Fajr prayer on Fridays: *Alif-Lam-Meem. Tanzil* (Surah As-Sajdah) and *Hal ata 'alal-insan* (Surah Al-Insan)."
2. **Sponsoring the Orphan:**  
   *Sahih al-Bukhari (Hadith 5304) from Sahl ibn Sa'd (RA):*  
   "Regarding feeding the orphan (76:8), the Prophet ﷺ said: 'I and the sponsor of an orphan will be in Paradise like this,' and he joined his index and middle fingers together."
---

## 8. Key Concepts
1. **Lam Yakun Shay'an Madhkura (The Epoch of Non-Existence):** The humbling realization that humanity was non-existent before God's creative will brought us forth.
2. **Ikhlas al-It'am (Altruistic Feeding):** Giving food and charity purely for the pleasure of God without seeking social media recognition or verbal thanks.
3. **Sharabun Tahur (The Purifying Drink):** The celestial beverage served by the Lord Himself in Paradise that purifies the hearts of all malice.
4. **Sa'yun Mashkur (Appreciated Striving):** The ultimate divine validation where God Himself honors and appreciates the human servant's sacrifices.
---

## 9. Tadabbur (Guided Contemplation)
* **Feeding Despite Your Own Love for It (76:8):** Look at the phrasing: *Wa yut'imunat-ta'ama 'ala hubbih.* Anyone can donate leftover pizza, expired canned goods, or clothes they don't want anymore. The elite believers described in Surah Al-Insan gave away food *despite their intense love and need for it*. Real charity begins when you sacrifice something you actually desire for the sake of Allah.
* **Your Effort Has Been Appreciated (76:22):** Imagine standing in Paradise, and the Lord of the worlds says to you: *Wa kana sa'yukum mashkura* ('Indeed, your striving has been appreciated'). In this world, your spouse might not appreciate you, your boss might overlook your overtime, and people might forget your kindness. But Allah never forgets a single tear or sacrifice. The Creator of the universe will thank *you*.
---

## 10. What This Means For Me
* I eradicate arrogance by remembering that I was once nothing mentioned (*Lam yakun shay'an madhkura*) (76:1).
* I give charity and serve others purely for the Face of Allah, not even expecting a 'thank you' in return (76:9).
* I support orphans and the needy, aiming for nearness to the Prophet ﷺ in Paradise (76:8).
* I prostrate and glorify Allah during the night (*Wa minal-layli fasjud lahu*) (76:26).

---

## 11. One Practical Action
**Perform an Anonymous Act of Kindness:**  
Act on verse 76:9 (*La nuridu minkum jaza'an wa la shukura*): Buy a meal for a homeless person, leave groceries for a neighbor, or donate anonymously today, ensuring the recipient has no way of knowing your identity or thanking you.

## 12. Reflection Questions
1. Do I get offended when people I help fail to thank or appreciate me (76:9)?
2. Am I donating things I actually love, or only leftovers that cost me nothing (76:8)?
3. How does realizing that Allah will say *'Your effort has been appreciated'* (76:22) heal my feelings of being undervalued in worldly life?

## 13. Closing Supplication
$$\text{اللَّهُمَّ إِنَّا نَسْأَلُكَ إِخْلَاصًا كَإِخْلَاصِ الَّذِينَ يُطْعِمُونَ الطَّعَامَ لِوَجْهِكَ لَا يُرِيدُونَ جَزَاءً وَلَا شُكُورًا ، اللَّهُمَّ اسْقِنَا شَرَابًا طَهُورًا ، وَاجْعَلْ سَعْيَنَا مَشْكُورًا فِي جَنَّاتِ النَّعِيمِ}$$  
*"O Allah, we ask You for sincerity like the sincerity of those who feed food for Your Face, seeking neither reward nor gratitude. O Allah, grant us a purifying drink, and make our striving appreciated in the Gardens of Bliss. Amin."*
