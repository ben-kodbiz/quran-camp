# CHAPTER 82: SURAH AL-INFITAR (سُورَةُ الانفطار)
**Classification:** Makki  
**Order in Mushaf:** 82  
**Total Verses:** 19  
**Famous Names:** Surat al-Infitar, Idha as-Sama'un-Fatarat  

---

## 1. Why This Surah Matters
Surah Al-Infitar delivers the tender, heartbreaking divine appeal to human ingratitude. Following the catastrophic cosmic cleaving of the sky, the scattering of stars, and the overturning of graves, Allah asks humanity the single most penetrating moral question in revelation: 'O mankind, what has deceived you concerning your Lord, the Generous? Who created you, proportioned you, and balanced you? In whatever form He willed has He assembled you' (82:6–8). It reveals the noble recording angels (*Kiramun Katibin*) who know and document every single human action, and establishes that on the Day of Judgment, no soul will possess an ounce of power for another soul: 'And the command, that Day, is entirely with Allah' (82:19).

---

## 2. Surah Context
Revealed in early Makkah to confront the arrogant complacency of human beings who took God's kindness, patience, and gifts of biology as an excuse to live in disobedience.

---

## 3. Central Theme
**The Cleaving of the Sky, the Divine Appeal Against Ingratitude, the Noble Recording Angels (*Kiramun Katibin*), and Absolute Divine Sovereignty.**

---

## 4. Key Passages
### The Cleaving and the Graves Overturned (82:1–5)
$$\text{إِذَا السَّمَاءُ انفَطَرَتْ ۝ وَإِذَا الْكَوَاكِبُ انتَثَرَتْ ۝ وَإِذَا الْبِحَارُ فُجِّرَتْ ۝ وَإِذَا الْقُبُورُ بُعْثِرَتْ ۝ عَلِمَتْ نَفْسٌ مَّا قَدَّمَتْ وَأَخَّرَتْ}$$
*"When the sky breaks apart, And when the stars fall, scattering, And when the seas are erupted, And when the [contents of] graves are overturned, A soul will know what it has put forth and kept back."*

### The Tender Divine Appeal (82:6–8)
$$\text{يَا أَيُّهَا الْإِنسَانُ مَا غَرَّكَ بِرَبِّكَ الْكَرِيمِ ۝ الَّذِي خَلَقَكَ فَسَوَّاكَ فَعَدَلَكَ ۝ فِي أَيِّ صُورَةٍ مَّا شَاءَ رَكَّبَكَ}$$
*"O mankind, what has deceived you concerning your Lord, the Generous, Who created you, proportioned you, and balanced you? In whatever form He willed has He assembled you."*

### The Noble Recording Scribes (82:10–12)
$$\text{وَإِنَّ عَلَيْكُمْ لَحَافِظِينَ ۝ كِرَامًا كَاتِبِينَ ۝ يَعْلَمُونَ مَا تَفْعَلُونَ}$$
*"And indeed, [appointed] over you are keepers, Noble and recording; They know whatever you do."*

---

## 5. Translation Highlights (The Clear Quran)
* *82:13–14:* 'Indeed, the righteous will be in pleasure, And indeed, the wicked will be in Hellfire.'
* *82:17–18:* 'And what can make you know what is the Day of Recompense? Then, what can make you know what is the Day of Recompense?'
* *82:19:* 'It is the Day when a soul will not possess for another soul [power to do] a thing; and the command, that Day, is [entirely] with Allah.'

---

## 6. What Classical Tafsir Explains
* **What Deceived You Concerning Your Generous Lord? (Verse 6):** Al-Fudayl ibn 'Iyad wept upon reading this verse and whispered: 'What deceived me? Your generous forbearance, O Lord!' Imam Ibn Kathir comments: 'Allah asks: O human, how did you dare to disobey the Lord who showered you with blessings, who crafted your body with perfection, who hid your sins, and gave you respite? Did you mistake His gentleness for weakness?'
* **The Modesty Before the Noble Angels (Verses 10–12):** Ibn Abbas (RA) stated: 'Honor the noble angels accompanying you! They do not separate from you except in two states: in the bathroom and during marital intimacy. Feel shame before them as you would feel shame before two righteous men watching your conduct!'
---

## 7. Important Supporting Hadiths
1. **The Three Surahs of Direct Vision:**  
   *Jami' at-Tirmidhi (Hadith 3333), Hasan:*  
   "The Prophet ﷺ said: 'Whoever wishes to see the Day of Resurrection as if with his own eyes, let him recite *Idha ash-shamsu kuwwirat*, *Idha as-sama'un-fatarat* (82), and *Idha as-sama'un-shaqqat*.'"
2. **Sovereignty on the Day of Judgment:**  
   *Sahih al-Bukhari (Hadith 4812) from Abu Hurayrah (RA):*  
   "The Prophet ﷺ said regarding verse 82:19: 'Allah will hold the earth and fold the heavens in His Right Hand, then say: *I am the King! Where are the kings of the earth?*'"
---

## 8. Key Concepts
1. **Ma Gharraka bi-Rabbika (The Delusion of Presumption):** The fatal mistake of presuming upon God's patience and generosity to persist in sin.
2. **Kiramun Katibin (The Noble Scribes):** The dignified angelic recorders documenting every human deed with absolute fidelity.
3. **Taswiyat al-Jasad (Biological Proportionality):** The miracle of human anatomy: balanced bipedal posture, symmetrical sensory organs, and unique physical design.
4. **Al-Amru Yawma'idhin Lillah (Absolute Final Sovereignty):** The reality that all human mediation, titles, and influence are void on Judgment Day.
---

## 9. Tadabbur (Guided Contemplation)
* **Don't Take His Generosity for Granted (82:6):** When Allah addresses you as *Ma gharraka bi-Rabbikal-Karim?*, feel the loving sting of that reproach. If someone was cruel and tyrannical to you, you might rebel. But Allah has given you air, eyes, a beating heart, food, and endless second chances. When you use the eyes He engineered to look at what He forbade, what has deceived you? Answer: 'O Allah, Your generous patience deceived me, but now I repent.'
* **You Are Under Constant Angelic Escort (82:10–12):** Notice that Allah calls the recording angels *Kiraman* (Noble, honorable). If two dignified, noble scholars were sitting in your room watching you work on your laptop, would you browse filth or write venomous slander? No. The noble angels are far more honorable than any human guest. Live so that the *Kiramun Katibin* smile at the beauty of what they record.
---

## 10. What This Means For Me
* I never abuse Allah's generosity and patience as an excuse to delay repentance (82:6).
* I practice acute spiritual modesty in private, knowing that the *Kiramun Katibin* (noble angels) are recording my deeds (82:10–12).
* I place my ultimate hope only in Allah, knowing that no soul can rescue another on that Day (82:19).

## 11. One Practical Action
**Reflect on Allah's Personal Gifts to Your Body:**  
Stand in front of a mirror today. Look at your eyes, ears, facial symmetry, and hands. Recite verses 82:7–8 (*Alladhi khalaqaka fasawwaka fa-'adalak*), and thank Allah for assembling your body in health.

## 12. Reflection Questions
1. What has deceived me into taking my Generous Lord's patience for granted (82:6)?
2. Are the noble angels (*Kiramun Katibin*) recording good deeds or heedless waste from my daily screen time (82:10–12)?
3. Am I living with the acute realization that on Judgment Day, no human connection can save me (82:19)?

## 13. Closing Supplication
$$\text{اللَّهُمَّ يَا رَبَّنَا الْكَرِيمَ ، غَرَّنَا حِلْمُكَ وَسِتْرُكَ عَلَيْنَا فَاغْفِرْ لَنَا ، اللَّهُمَّ اسْتُرْ عَوْرَاتِنَا وَآمِنْ رَوْعَاتِنَا ، وَاجْعَلْ كِتَابَنَا فِي عِلِّيِّينَ مَعَ الْأَبْرَارِ}$$  
*"O Allah, our Generous Lord: Your forbearance and concealment over our sins deceived us, so forgive us. O Allah, conceal our faults, calm our fears, and place our record in 'Illiyyun with the righteous. Amin."*
