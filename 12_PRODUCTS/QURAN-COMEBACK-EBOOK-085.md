# CHAPTER 85: SURAH AL-BURUJ (سُورَةُ البروج)
**Classification:** Makki  
**Order in Mushaf:** 85  
**Total Verses:** 22  
**Famous Names:** Surat al-Buruj, Was-Sama'i Dhatil-Buruj  

---

## 1. Why This Surah Matters
Surah Al-Buruj is the heroic fortress of faith under persecution. Opening with oaths by the sky adorned with great stellar constellations (*Dhat al-Buruj*), the Promised Day, and the witness and witnessed, it documents history's most harrowing persecution: The Companions of the Trench (*Ashab al-Ukhdud*), where thousands of early monotheistic believers in ancient Yemen were thrown into fiery trenches simply because: 'They resented them not except that they believed in Allah, the Exalted in Might, the Praiseworthy' (85:8). It comforts the persecuted with the divine warning: 'Indeed, the vengeance of your Lord is severe' (*Inna batsha Rabbika lashadid*), reveals the sublime pair of divine names: *Al-Ghafur al-Wadud* (The All-Forgiving, the Full of Loving Affection), and declares the eternal majesty of the Qur'an preserved in the Inscribed Tablet (*Lawh Mahfuz*).

---

## 2. Surah Context
Revealed in Makkah during the brutal torture of vulnerable early Muslims (Bilal, Khabbab, Sumayyah, and Yasir). Surah Al-Buruj descended to reassure the bleeding believers that their martyrdom was the supreme triumph (*Al-Fawz al-Kabir*).

---

## 3. Central Theme
**Uncompromising Faith under Tyranny (*Ashab al-Ukhdud*), the Severity of Divine Retribution, the Loving Forgiveness of Allah (*Al-Wadud*), and the Infallible Preserved Tablet.**

---

## 4. Key Passages
### The Crime of Believing in God (85:4–8)
$$\text{قُتِلَ أَصْحَابُ الْأُخْدُودِ ۝ النَّارِ ذَاتِ الْوَقُودِ ۝ إِذْ هُمْ عَلَيْهَا قُعُودٌ ۝ وَهُمْ عَلَىٰ مَا يَفْعَلُونَ بِالْمُؤْمِنِينَ شُهُودٌ ۝ وَمَا نَقَمُوا مِنْهُمْ إِلَّا أَن يُؤْمِنُوا بِاللَّهِ الْعَزِيزِ الْحَمِيدِ}$$
*"Cursed were the companions of the trench—[Containing] the fire full of fuel, When they were sitting near it, And they, to what they were doing against the believers, were witnesses. And they resented them not except that they believed in Allah, the Exalted in Might, the Praiseworthy."*

### The Great Supreme Triumph (85:11)
$$\text{إِنَّ الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا وَعَمِلُوا الصَّالِحَاتِ لَهُمْ جَنَّاتٌ تَجْرِي مِن تَحْتِهَا الْأَنْهَارُ ۚ ذَٰلِكَ الْفَوْزُ الْكَبِيرُ}$$
*"Indeed, those who have believed and done righteous deeds will have gardens beneath which rivers flow. That is the great attainment."*

### The Attributes of Power and Love (85:12–16)
$$\text{إِنَّ بَطْشَ رَبِّكَ لَشَدِيدٌ ۝ إِنَّهُ هُوَ يُبْدِئُ وَيُعِيدُ ۝ وَهُوَ الْغَفُورُ الْوَدُودُ ۝ ذُو الْعَرْشِ الْمَجِيدُ ۝ فَعَّالٌ لِّمَا يُرِيدُ}$$
*"Indeed, the vengeance of your Lord is severe. Indeed, it is He who originates [creation] and repeats. And He is the Forgiving, the Affectionate, Honorable Owner of the Throne, Effecter of what He intends."*

---

## 5. Translation Highlights (The Clear Quran)
* *85:10:* 'Indeed, those who have tortured the believing men and believing women and then have not repented will have the punishment of Hell...'
* *85:20:* 'And Allah encompasses them from behind.'
* *85:21–22:* 'But this is an honored Quran [Inscribed] in a Preserved Slate [Lawh Mahfuz].'

---

## 6. What Classical Tafsir Explains
* **The Definition of True Triumph (Verse 11):** In worldly terms, the believers in the trench were burned alive without establishing a political state or escaping the fire. Yet Allah calls their steadfast death: *Dhalikal-fawzul-kabir* ('That is the SUPREME TRIUMPH!'). True victory is dying upon uncompromised Tawhid; earthly survival at the cost of your faith is eternal defeat.
* **The Pairing of 'Al-Ghafur' and 'Al-Wadud' (Verse 14):** Imam Ibn al-Qayyim notes the breathtaking tenderness of *Al-Wadud*: Allah is not merely *Ghafur* (One who forgives your sin and cancels punishment); He is *Al-Wadud* (One who loves you with deep, warm affection after you repent). A human judge might pardon a criminal but avoid looking at him; Allah pardons you and embraces your soul with divine love.
---

## 7. Important Supporting Hadiths
1. **The Story of the Boy and the King:**  
   *Sahih Muslim (Hadith 3005) from Suhayb ar-Rumi (RA):*  
   "The Prophet ﷺ narrated the long, magnificent epic of the Boy, the Monk, and the Sorcerer that led directly to the story of *Ashab al-Ukhdud* (85:4), where the boy sacrificed his life to guide an entire nation to Tawhid."
2. **Repentance Even for the Torturers:**  
   *Al-Hasan al-Basri in Tafsir Ibn Kathir:*  
   "Regarding verse 85:10 (*Thumma lam yatubu*), Al-Hasan said: 'Look at this boundless divine generosity! They burned alive His beloved saints, yet He still calls them: *"And then they do not repent"*—leaving the door of repentance open even for their torturers!'"
---

## 8. Key Concepts
1. **Ashab al-Ukhdud (The Martyrs of the Trench):** The ultimate historical benchmark of steadfast faith enduring lethal persecution without compromise.
2. **Al-Fawz al-Kabir (The Supreme Triumph):** Redefining success: victory is dying with uncorrupted faith, regardless of worldly outcome.
3. **Al-Wadud (The Infinitely Loving):** The divine attribute of tender, loving intimacy bestowed upon the repentant believers.
4. **Al-Lawh al-Mahfuz (The Preserved Slate):** The celestial, immutable tablet in which all cosmic decrees and the Holy Qur'an are preserved.
---

## 9. Tadabbur (Guided Contemplation)
* **What Was Their Only 'Crime'? (85:8):** Look at why tyrants throughout history hate sincere believers: *Wa ma naqamu minhum illa an yu'minu billah.* They did not steal, they did not commit treason, they did not murder. Their only 'crime' was refusing to bow to human idols, affirming that supreme authority belongs solely to Allah. When you face hostility for your faith, wear it as a badge of honor.
* **He is Al-Wadud (85:14):** After describing burning trenches and the severe vengeance of God (*Inna batsha Rabbika lashadid*), Allah introduces Himself as: *Wa Huwal-Ghafurul-Wadud.* Never think that God is a harsh, distant deity. He loves the sincere believer with a depth beyond human comprehension. Even when you slip and return in tears, He welcomes you with *Wudd* (overflowing love).
---

## 10. What This Means For Me
* I stand firm upon my faith regardless of social pressure, mockery, or worldly persecution (85:8).
* I reframe my definition of success: true triumph (*Al-Fawz al-Kabir*) is meeting Allah with a sound, faithful heart (85:11).
* I seek the loving affection (*Wudd*) of Allah by turning to Him in sincere repentance (85:14).

## 11. One Practical Action
**Pray for the Oppressed Believers Worldwide:**  
Honor the legacy of the martyrs of the trench: In your prayers today, make a heartfelt du'a for all Muslims currently suffering persecution, war, or unjust imprisonment around the globe.

## 12. Reflection Questions
1. Am I willing to compromise my principles for worldly comfort, or do I embody the steadfastness of *Ashab al-Ukhdud* (85:8)?
2. Do I understand true success as worldly victory, or as *Al-Fawz al-Kabir* (dying on faith) (85:11)?
3. How does knowing Allah is *Al-Wadud* (the Loving) draw my heart closer to Him after I repent (85:14)?

## 13. Closing Supplication
$$\text{اللَّهُمَّ يَا غَفُورُ يَا وَدُودُ يَا ذَا الْعَرْشِ الْمَجِيدِ يَا فَعَّالًا لِمَا يُرِيدُ ، انْصُرِ الْمُسْتَضْعَفِينَ مِنَ الْمُؤْمِنِينَ فِي كُلِّ مَكَانٍ ، وَارْزُقْنَا ثَبَاتًا كَثَبَاتِ أَهْلِ الْأُخْدُودِ ، وَاجْعَلْ فَوْزَنَا الْفَوْزَ الْكَبِيرَ}$$  
*"O Allah, O Forgiving, O Affectionate, Owner of the Glorious Throne, Effecter of what He intends: Grant victory to the oppressed believers everywhere, bless us with steadfastness like the people of the trench, and make our victory the Supreme Triumph. Amin."*
