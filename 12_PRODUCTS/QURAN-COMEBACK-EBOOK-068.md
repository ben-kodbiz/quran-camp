# CHAPTER 68: SURAH AL-QALAM (سُورَةُ القلم)
**Classification:** Makki  
**Order in Mushaf:** 68  
**Total Verses:** 52  
**Famous Names:** Surat al-Qalam, Nun wal-Qalam  

---

## 1. Why This Surah Matters
Surah Al-Qalam is the eternal coronation of prophetic character and intellectual integrity. It begins with the mystical letter *Nun* and an oath by the Pen and what is inscribed (*Wal-Qalami wa ma yasturun*), establishing the sacredness of writing, knowledge, and historical records. It issues the ultimate divine testimonial of the Prophet's ﷺ sublime character in verse 4: 'And indeed, you are of a great moral character' (*Wa innaka la'ala khuluqin 'adhim*). It relates the cautionary tale of the Greedy Garden Owners (*Ashab al-Jannah*) who conspired to harvest their fruit at dawn to deprive the poor, only to find their entire estate transformed into charred black ash overnight. It ends with the warning of Prophet Yunus: 'So be patient for the decision of your Lord and be not like the companion of the fish' (*Sahib al-Hut*).

---

## 2. Surah Context
Revealed in early Makkah as the second or third revelation (following Surah Al-'Alaq). The Quraysh mocked the Messenger of Allah ﷺ, claiming he was insane (*Majnun*). Surah Al-Qalam descended directly from Allah to vindicate his sanity and crown his character.

---

## 3. Central Theme
**The Sacredness of the Pen and Knowledge, the Incomparable Character of the Prophet ﷺ (*Khuluq 'Adhim*), the Peril of Greed (The Garden Owners), and Sabr.**

---

## 4. Key Passages
### The Oath by the Pen and the Sublime Character (68:1–4)
$$\text{ن ۚ وَالْقَلَمِ وَمَا يَسْطُرُونَ ۝ مَا أَنتَ بِنِعْمَةِ رَبِّكَ بِمَجْنُونٍ ۝ وَإِنَّ لَكَ لَأَجْرًا غَيْرَ مَمْنُونٍ ۝ وَإِنَّكَ لَعَلَىٰ خُلُقٍ عَظِيمٍ}$$
*"Nun. By the pen and what they inscribe, You are not, [O Muhammad], by the favor of your Lord, a madman. And indeed, for you is a reward uninterrupted. And indeed, you are of a great moral character."*

### The Ruin of the Greedy Garden Owners (68:17–20)
$$\text{إِنَّا بَلَوْنَاهُمْ كَمَا بَلَوْنَا أَصْحَابَ الْجَنَّةِ إِذْ أَقْسَمُوا لَيَصْرِمُنَّهَا مُصْبِحِينَ ۝ وَلَا يَسْتَثْنُونَ ۝ فَطَافَ عَلَيْهَا طَائِفٌ مِّن رَّبِّكَ وَهُمْ نَائِمُونَ ۝ فَأَصْبَحَتْ كَالصَّرِيمِ}$$
*"Indeed, We have tested them as We tested the companions of the garden, when they swore to cut its fruit in the [early] morning, Without making exception [i.e., saying 'In sha Allah']. So there came upon the garden an affliction from your Lord while they were asleep. And it became as though reaped [charred black]."*

### Do Not Be Like the Companion of the Fish (68:48–49)
$$\text{فَاصْبِرْ لِحُكْمِ رَبِّكَ وَلَا تَكُن كَصَاحِبِ الْحُوتِ إِذْ نَادَىٰ وَهُوَ مَكْظُومٌ ۝ لَّوْلَا أَن تَدَارَكَهُ نِعْمَةٌ مِّن رَّبِّهِ لَنُبِذَ بِالْعَرَاءِ وَهُوَ مَذْمُومٌ}$$
*"Then be patient for the decision of your Lord, [O Muhammad], and be not like the companion of the fish when he called out while he was distressed. If not that a favor from his Lord overtook him, he would have been thrown onto the naked shore while he was blamed."*

---

## 5. Translation Highlights (The Clear Quran)
* *68:10–11:* 'And do not obey every worthless habitual swearer, [And] scorner, going about with malicious gossip...'
* *68:34:* 'Indeed, for the righteous with their Lord are the Gardens of Pleasure.'
* *68:51–52:* 'And indeed, those who disbelieve would almost make you slip with their eyes when they hear the message... But it is not except a reminder to the worlds.'

---

## 6. What Classical Tafsir Explains
* **The Character of the Prophet ﷺ (Verse 4):** When Aisha (RA) was asked about the character of the Messenger of Allah ﷺ, she replied: 'His character was the Qur'an.' Imam Ibn Kathir writes: 'Whatever the Qur'an commanded, he did; whatever it prohibited, he avoided. The natural noble disposition (*Khuluq*) in him became an embodiment of revelation.'
* **The Lesson of the Garden Owners (Verses 17–32):** Their righteous father used to distribute a third of the harvest to the poor, keep a third for the farm, and use a third for his family. When he died, the sons said: 'Our father was foolish; let us cut out the poor!' They crept out at dawn without saying *In sha Allah*, only to find their orchard turned to ashes. Greed destroys wealth; charity protects it.
---

## 7. Important Supporting Hadiths
1. **The Creation of the Pen:**  
   *Jami' at-Tirmidhi (Hadith 2155), Sahih:*  
   "The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: 'The first thing that Allah created was the Pen. He said to it: *Write!* It said: *My Lord, what shall I write?* He said: *Write the decrees of everything until the Hour begins.*'"
2. **The Evil Eye (Al-'Ayn):**  
   *Sahih Muslim (Hadith 2188) from Ibn Abbas (RA):*  
   "Regarding verse 68:51 (*Wa in yakadulladhina kafaru layuzliqunaka bi-absarihim*), the Prophet ﷺ said: 'The evil eye is real; and if anything could precede the divine decree, the evil eye would precede it.'"
---

## 8. Key Concepts
1. **Khuluq 'Adhim (Sublime Character):** The peak moral and spiritual perfection embodied by the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ.
2. **Al-Qalam (The Sacred Pen):** The primary instrument of divine pre-destination, knowledge recording, and intellectual transmission.
3. **Ashab al-Jannah (The Garden Conspirators):** The archetype of generational wealth destroyed by refusing to share with the poor and destitute.
4. **Isti'jal Yunus (Impatience in Da'wah):** The warning against giving up on people or abandoning one's post before receiving divine permission.
---

## 9. Tadabbur (Guided Contemplation)
* **His Character Was the Qur'an (68:4):** If you want to know what the Qur'an looks like when it walks, talks, eats, smiles, forgives, and negotiates—look at the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ. He was a living Mushaf. Christianity has the Word made flesh; Islam has the Word made lived practice through the character of the Messenger ﷺ. Strive to make your character a reflection of the Book you read.
* **Depriving the Poor Destroys Your Business (68:17–20):** The garden owners thought they were being smart financial managers: 'If we cut out the charitable allocation, our profit margin jumps by 30%!' While they slept, a fire from heaven reduced their assets to zero. Whatever wealth you hoard from the poor becomes the spark that burns down your storehouse. Give generously to preserve your blessing.
---

## 10. What This Means For Me
* I strive to emulate the sublime character (*Khuluq 'Adhim*) of the Prophet ﷺ in patience, honesty, and gentleness (68:4).
* I honor the Pen and knowledge, using writing and communication to spread truth and beneficial knowledge (68:1).
* I always say *In sha Allah* when planning future business or personal projects, avoiding the arrogance of the garden owners (68:18).
* I give charity consistently from my income, knowing that hoarding from the poor invites ruin (68:27).

---

## 11. One Practical Action
**Say 'In Sha Allah' and Give to the Needy Today:**  
Review your upcoming plans and deliberately say *In sha Allah* for each one. Then, honor the lesson of the garden owners by giving food or financial help to someone in need today.

## 12. Reflection Questions
1. Does my daily behavior reflect the sublime moral character (*Khuluq 'Adhim*) praised in verse 4?
2. Am I hoarding my resources from the poor, risking the ruin that struck the garden owners (68:17–20)?
3. Do I say *In sha Allah* sincerely with my heart, or just as a casual phrase?

## 13. Closing Supplication
$$\text{اللَّهُمَّ كَمَا حَسَّنْتَ خَلْقَنَا فَحَسِّنْ أَخْلَاقَنَا ، اللَّهُمَّ اهْدِنَا لِأَحْسَنِ الْأَخْلَاقِ لَا يَهْدِي لِأَحْسَنِهَا إِلَّا أَنْتَ ، وَاصْرِفْ عَنَّا سَيِّئَهَا ، وَلَا تَجْعَلْنَا مِثْلَ أَصْحَابِ الْجَنَّةِ}$$  
*"O Allah, just as You have made our physical form beautiful, beautify our character. O Allah, guide us to the best of manners; none guides to the best of them except You. Turn away from us evil character, and do not make us like the companions of the garden. Amin."*
