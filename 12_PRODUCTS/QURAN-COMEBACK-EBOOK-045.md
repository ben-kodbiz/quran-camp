# CHAPTER 45: SURAH AL-JATHIYAH (سُورَةُ الجاثية)
**Classification:** Makki  
**Order in Mushaf:** 45  
**Total Verses:** 37  
**Famous Names:** Surat al-Jathiyah, Surat ash-Shari'ah  

---

## 1. Why This Surah Matters
Surah Al-Jathiyah is the dramatic courtroom finale of the seven *Hawamim* chapters. Named after the terrifying spectacle in verse 28 where every empire, superpower, and nation is seen collapsed upon its knees (*Kullu ummatin jathiyah*) before the divine tribunal, trembling as the Book of Deeds is opened to speak with absolute truth: 'This, Our record, speaks about you in truth. Indeed, We were having transcribed whatever you used to do' (45:29). It dismantles the secular nihilism of those who claim: 'There is nothing but our worldly life; we die and we live, and nothing destroys us except time' (*Ad-Dahr*). It concludes with the supreme celestial declaration: *Wa lahul-kibriya'u fis-samawati wal-ard* ('And to Him belongs all majesty in the heavens and the earth').

---

## 2. Surah Context
Revealed in Makkah to confront the arrogant atheistic materialists (*Ad-Dahriyyah*) who denied the afterlife, claiming humanity is governed purely by biological cycles and blind time. Surah Al-Jathiyah exposed their intellectual pride and set forth the divine Shari'ah as the only true path of life (45:18).

---

## 3. Central Theme
**The Inevitable Kneeling of Nations on Judgment Day, the Refutation of Secular Materialism (*Ad-Dahr*), and Absolute Divine Majesty (*Al-Kibriya'*).**

---

## 4. Key Passages
### The Divine Blueprint of the Shari'ah (45:18)
$$\text{ثُمَّ جَعَلْنَاكَ عَلَىٰ شَرِيعَةٍ مِّنَ الْأَمْرِ فَاتَّبِعْهَا وَلَا تَتَّبِعْ أَهْوَاءَ الَّذِينَ لَا يَعْلَمُونَ}$$
*"Then We put you, [O Muhammad], on an ordained way (Shari'ah) by Our command; so follow it and do not follow the inclinations of those who do not know."*

### Every Nation Kneeling in Awe (45:28–29)
$$\text{وَتَرَىٰ كُلَّ أُمَّةٍ جَاثِيَةً ۚ كُلُّ أُمَّةٍ تُدْعَىٰ إِلَىٰ كِتَابِهَا الْيَوْمَ تُجْزَوْنَ مَا كُنتُمْ تَعْمَلُونَ ۝ هَٰذَا كِتَابُنَا يَنطِقُ عَلَيْكُم بِالْحَقِّ ۚ إِنَّا كُنَّا نَسْتَنسِخُ مَا كُنتُمْ تَعْمَلُونَ}$$
*"And you will see every nation kneeling [from fear]. Every nation will be called to its record [and told], 'Today you will be recompensed for what you used to do. This, Our record, speaks about you in truth. Indeed, We were having transcribed whatever you used to do.'"*

### To Him Belongs All Majesty (45:36–37)
$$\text{فَلِلَّهِ الْحَمْدُ رَبِّ السَّمَاوَاتِ وَرَبِّ الْأَرْضِ رَبِّ الْعَالَمِينَ ۝ وَلَهُ الْكِبْرِيَاءُ فِي السَّمَاوَاتِ وَالْأَرْضِ ۖ وَهُوَ الْعَزِيزُ الْحَكِيمُ}$$
*"Then to Allah belongs [all] praise—Lord of the heavens and Lord of the earth, Lord of the worlds. And to Him belongs [all] majesty within the heavens and the earth, and He is the Exalted in Might, the Wise."*

---

## 5. Translation Highlights (The Clear Quran)
* *45:21:* 'Or do those who commit evils think We will make them like those who have believed and done righteous deeds—[make them] equal in their life and their death? Evil is that which they judge.'
* *45:23:* 'Have you seen he who has taken as his god his [own] desire, and Allah has sent him astray due to knowledge and has set a seal upon his hearing and his heart...'
* *45:24:* 'And they say, "There is not but our worldly life; we die and we live, and nothing destroys us except time." And they have of that no knowledge; they are only assuming.'

---

## 6. What Classical Tafsir Explains
* **The Meaning of 'Jathiyah' (Verse 28):** Imam Ibn Kathir and Mujahid explain that *Jathiyah* describes falling upon the knees—a posture of terror, helplessness, and absolute submission. Kings, presidents, dictators, and billionaires who swaggered arrogantly across the earth will be dropped to their knees in the dust, unable to move without divine permission.
* **We Were Transcribing (*Nastansikh*) (Verse 29):** Al-Hasan al-Basri noted: 'The angels record your deeds, and on the Day of Judgment, the original master copy is produced, matching every microsecond of your life.' *Nastansikh* means creating faithful replicas of human actions, leaving no room for denial.
---

## 7. Important Supporting Hadiths
1. **Majesty Belongs Exclusively to Allah:**  
   *Sahih Muslim (Hadith 2620) - Hadith Qudsi:*  
   "The Messenger of Allah ﷺ reported that Allah Almighty said: 'Pride (*Al-Kibriya'*) is My cloak, and Majesty (*Al-'Azamah*) is My garment. Whoever contends with Me regarding either of them, I will cast him into the Fire.'"
2. **Do Not Curse Time:**  
   *Sahih al-Bukhari (Hadith 4826) from Abu Hurayrah (RA):*  
   "Regarding verse 45:24, the Prophet ﷺ said: 'Allah said: The son of Adam hurts Me by cursing Time (*Ad-Dahr*), whereas I am Time—in My Hand is the night and the day; I alternate them.'"
---

## 8. Key Concepts
1. **Al-Jathiyah (The Kneeling Posture):** The universal eschatological subjugation of all humanity kneeling in trembling awe before the divine judge.
2. **Al-Kibriya' (Supreme Majesty):** The exclusive divine prerogative of ultimate greatness and pride, forbidden to any created entity.
3. **Ad-Dahriyyah (Philosophical Materialism):** The cynical worldview reducing existence to biological mortality and accidental time.
4. **Ash-Shari'ah (The Clear Path):** The divine legal and moral highway established by Allah to guide humanity away from erratic human passions.
---

## 9. Tadabbur (Guided Contemplation)
* **The Kneeling of Superpowers (45:28):** Picture the greatest imperial armies, tech titans, and political dynasties in history. On that Day, there will be no podiums, no motorcades, and no veto powers: *Wa tara kulla ummatin jathiyah* ('And you will see every nation on its knees'). If the entire human race will collapse on its knees before Allah tomorrow, bend your knees willingly in humble prayer today.
* **Our Record Speaks with Truth (45:29):** Think of the word *Yantiqu* ('speaks'). The record is not a silent paper scroll; it is a living transcription of your life that testifies with undeniable clarity. Every keystroke, every private whisper, every transaction is transcribed (*Nastansikh*). Live your private life today so that your record will be a source of joy when it speaks tomorrow.
---

## 10. What This Means For Me
* I commit to following the divine Shari'ah over cultural fads and personal whims (45:18).
* I bow my knees willingly in Sujud and prayer every day, anticipating the Great Kneeling of Judgment Day (45:28).
* I eradicate all pride (*Kibr*) from my demeanor, remembering that Majesty belongs exclusively to Allah (45:37).

---

## 11. One Practical Action
**Audit Your Intentions for 'Hawa' (Desire):**  
Reflect on verse 23: *'Have you seen he who takes his desire as his god?'* Identify one area where your personal desires conflict with Islamic ethics (e.g., finance, relationships, or temper). Subjugate your desire to the command of Allah today.

## 12. Reflection Questions
1. Am I living by the divine Shari'ah or following the shifting inclinations of popular culture (45:18)?
2. How will I feel when my personal book of deeds is opened and speaks with absolute truth (45:29)?
3. Do I exhibit pride or arrogance toward anyone, forgetting that *Al-Kibriya'* belongs only to Allah (45:37)?

## 13. Closing Supplication
$$\text{فَلِلَّهِ الْحَمْدُ رَبِّ السَّمَاوَاتِ وَرَبِّ الْأَرْضِ رَبِّ الْعَالَمِينَ ، وَلَهُ الْكِبْرِيَاءُ فِي السَّمَاوَاتِ وَالْأَرْضِ وَهُوَ الْعَزِيزُ الْحَكِيمُ ، اللَّهُمَّ آمِنَّا يَوْمَ تَجْثُو الْأُمَمُ ، وَبَيِّضْ وُجُوهَنَا عِنْدَ لِقَائِكَ}$$  
*"Then to Allah belongs all praise—Lord of the heavens and Lord of the earth, Lord of the worlds. And to Him belongs all majesty within the heavens and the earth, and He is the Exalted in Might, the Wise. O Allah, grant us security on the Day all nations kneel, and brighten our faces upon meeting You. Amin."*
