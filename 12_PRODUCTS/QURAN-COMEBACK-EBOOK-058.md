# CHAPTER 58: SURAH AL-MUJADILA (سُورَةُ المجادلة)
**Classification:** Madani  
**Order in Mushaf:** 58  
**Total Verses:** 22  
**Famous Names:** Surat al-Mujadilah, Surat Qad Sami'a  

---

## 1. Why This Surah Matters
Surah Al-Mujadila is the divine vindication of vulnerable human voices. It is uniquely famous across the entire Mushaf because every single verse contains the majestic name 'Allah' (appearing over 40 times in 22 verses). It opens with the historic plea of Khawlah bint Tha'labah, an ordinary Muslim woman whose husband subjected her to pre-Islamic verbal divorce (*Zihar*). When she pleaded with the Prophet ﷺ in Aisha's room, Allah heard her whispers from above the seven heavens, descending verse 1: 'Allah has heard the speech of the one who argues with you concerning her husband and directs her complaint to Allah' (*Qad sami'allahu qawlal-lati tujadiluka*). It exposes the evil of toxic conspiracies and secret whispers (*An-Najwa*), and ends with the unbreakable alliance of *Hizbullah* (The Party of Allah).

---

## 2. Surah Context
Revealed in Madinah around 5 AH when Aws ibn as-Samit uttered the archaic pagan formula to his wife Khawlah: 'You are to me like the back of my mother.' Khawlah sprinted to the Prophet ﷺ, pleading that she had dedicated her youth to him and had children who would starve if separated. Revelation descended immediately, legally outlawing *Zihar* and instituting expiation.

---

## 3. Central Theme
**Divine Omnipresence in Listening to the Oppressed, the Ethics of Secret Meetings (*An-Najwa*), and the Triumph of the Party of Allah (*Hizbullah*).**

---

## 4. Key Passages
### Allah Hears the Complaint of the Broken-Hearted (58:1)
$$\text{قَدْ سَمِعَ اللَّهُ قَوْلَ الَّتِي تُجَادِلُكَ فِي زَوْجِهَا وَتَشْتَكِي إِلَى اللَّهِ وَاللَّهُ يَسْمَعُ تَحَاوُرَكُمَا ۚ إِنَّ اللَّهَ سَمِيعٌ بَصِيرٌ}$$
*"Certainly has Allah heard the speech of the one who argues with you, [O Muhammad], concerning her husband and directs her complaint to Allah. And Allah hears your dialogue; indeed, Allah is Hearing and Seeing."*

### The Cosmic Third in Every Secret (58:7)
$$\text{أَلَمْ تَرَ أَنَّ اللَّهَ يَعْلَمُ مَا فِي السَّمَاوَاتِ وَمَا فِي الْأَرْضِ ۖ مَا يَكُونُ مِن نَّجْوَىٰ ثَلَاثَةٍ إِلَّا هُوَ رَابِعُهُمْ وَلَا خَمْسَةٍ إِلَّا هُوَ سَادِسُهُمْ وَلَا أَدْنَىٰ مِن ذَٰلِكَ وَلَا أَكْثَرَ إِلَّا هُوَ مَعَهُمْ أَيْنَ مَا كَانُوا}$$
*"Have you not considered that Allah knows what is in the heavens and what is on the earth? There is in no private conversation three but that He is the fourth of them, nor are there five but that He is the sixth of them—and no less than that and no more except that He is with them wherever they are."*

### The Triumph of the Party of Allah (58:22)
$$\text{أُولَٰئِكَ حِزْبُ اللَّهِ ۚ أَلَا إِنَّ حِزْبَ اللَّهِ هُمُ الْمُفْلِحُونَ}$$
*"Those are the party of Allah. Unquestionably, the party of Allah—they are the successful."*

## 5. Translation Highlights (The Clear Quran)
* *58:9:* 'O you who have believed, when you converse privately, do not converse for sin and aggression and disobedience to the Messenger but converse for righteousness and piety...'
* *58:10:* 'Private conversation is only from Satan that he may grieve those who have believed, but he will not harm them at all except by permission of Allah...'
* *58:11:* 'O you who have believed, when you are told, "Space yourselves" in assemblies, then make space; Allah will make space for you...'

---

## 6. What Classical Tafsir Explains
* **Aisha's Awe at Allah's Hearing (Verse 1):** Aisha (RA) famously exclaimed: 'Praise be to Allah whose hearing encompasses all voices! I was in the very same room with the woman (Khawlah) and the Messenger of Allah ﷺ, yet some of her whispers were muffled to me, while Allah heard her from above seven heavens and revealed: *"Certainly has Allah heard the speech..."*'
* **The Fourth in Every Secret Chamber (Verse 7):** Scholars of Tafsir emphasize that *Huwa rabi'uhum* denotes divine omniscience, hearing, and presence (*Ma'iyyah bi-ilmihi*). No backroom corporate conspiracy, no private slander group, and no secret political clique operates in isolation; the Almighty is auditing every syllable spoken.
---

## 7. Important Supporting Hadiths
1. **Umar Honoring Khawlah:**  
   *Tafsir Ibn Kathir / Bayhaqi in Dala'il an-Nubuwwah (Sahih):*  
   "Umar ibn al-Khattab (RA) was riding his donkey through Madinah when an elderly woman stopped him. Umar dismounted, bowed his head, and listened patiently to her for a long time. A man said: 'O Amir al-Mu'minin, you detained the elite leaders of Quraysh for this old woman?' Umar rebuked him: 'Woe to you! Do you know who this is? This is Khawlah, whose complaint Allah listened to from above seven heavens! By Allah, if she held me here until nightfall, I would not leave except for mandatory prayer!'"
2. **The Prohibition of Excluding Someone in Conversation:**  
   *Sahih al-Bukhari (Hadith 6290) from Abdullah ibn Mas'ud (RA):*  
   "The Prophet ﷺ said: 'When you are three people, two must not converse secretly excluding the third until you mix with people, for that will grieve him.'"
---

## 8. Key Concepts
1. **Qad Sami'allah (Divine Attentiveness):** The reality that the Creator personally hears and validates the tears and complaints of ordinary, broken human beings.
2. **An-Najwa (Private Whispering):** Secret consultations, which must strictly serve virtue and charity, never toxic exclusion or gossip.
3. **Adab al-Majalis (Gathering Etiquette):** The social courtesy of physically and emotionally making room for newcomers in gatherings.
4. **Hizbullah (The Party of Allah):** The global fellowship of sincere believers whose supreme loyalty is to God and His Messenger.
---

## 9. Tadabbur (Guided Contemplation)
* **Your Tears Reach Above Seven Heavens (58:1):** Khawlah had no army, no legal team, and no wealth. She was an aging woman facing domestic heartbreak. Yet her whispered complaint bypassed kings and emperors, triggering revelation that changed Islamic law forever. Never believe your prayers are insignificant. When you complain to Allah (*Tashtaki ilallah*), the Lord of the universe is listening.
* **The Toxicity of Secret Whispers (58:10):** Notice how Shaytan weaponizes exclusion: Two people whispering in a corner, glancing at a third person, or creating private side-chats to exclude a colleague or family member. Allah calls this out: *Innaman-najwa min ash-shaytani li-yahzunal-ladhina amanu* ('Private whispering is only from Satan to cause grief'). Maintain transparent, inclusive, and kind communication in all your spaces.
---

## 10. What This Means For Me
* Whenever I am mistreated or heartbroken, I direct my raw complaint directly to Allah (*Ashtaki ilallah*), knowing He hears (58:1).
* I refrain from excluding people in conversations, side-chats, or private whispers, protecting my brothers and sisters from emotional pain (58:10).
* I make physical and emotional room for others in gatherings, claiming the promise: 'Make room, and Allah will make room for you' (58:11).

---

## 11. One Practical Action
**Practice the Etiquette of 'Making Space' Today:**  
Honor verse 58:11 (*Tafassahu fil-majalis*): When in a meeting, gathering, or family meal today, intentionally welcome a quiet or isolated person, invite them to sit beside you, and give them space to speak.

---

## 12. Reflection Questions
1. Do I take my emotional heartbreak to people on social media or do I direct my complaint to Allah like Khawlah (58:1)?
2. Am I mindful that in every private conversation or digital chat, Allah is the silent auditor (58:7)?
3. Do my private discussions revolve around righteousness and piety, or gossip and division (58:9)?

## 13. Closing Supplication

*"O Allah, O All-Hearing, O All-Seeing: O One who heard the complaint of Khawlah from above seven heavens, heal our chests, relieve our distress, and make us among Your successful party. Amin."*
