## 1. Grant of License

When you purchase any digital product in the *Come Back to the Qur'an* collection, Huurs Studio grants you a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, non-transferable personal license to download, read, print, and study the materials.

### Permitted Uses:
* **Personal Devices:** You may install the EPUB, PDF, and HTML files on all personal devices owned by you and immediate household family members (e.g. your phone, iPad, Kindle, laptop).
* **Personal Printing:** You may print physical copies of the 7-day guide, 30-day workbook, or master book for your personal journaling and handwriting.
* **Family Sharing:** Sharing with your spouse, children, or parents within the same household is fully permitted and encouraged.

### Non-Permitted Uses:
* **Commercial Resale:** You may not resell, sub-license, rent, or distribute these files for commercial gain.
* **Public File Sharing:** You may not upload the raw `.epub`, `.md`, or `.pdf` files to public torrent sites, file-sharing platforms, or open WhatsApp/Telegram groups.
* **Derivative Works for Resale:** You may not repackage our text, layout, or branding into competing commercial products.

We recognize that sacred knowledge is meant to be shared in community:

* For institutional use exceeding 25 participants, please contact `education@huurs.com` for our complimentary institutional grant.
