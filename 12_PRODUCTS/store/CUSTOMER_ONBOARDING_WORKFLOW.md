## 2. Device-Specific Installation Instructions

### A. How to Read on Amazon Kindle (Paperwhite / Oasis / Scribe / App)
* **Recommended Format:** `COME_BACK_TO_QURAN_114_SURAHS.epub`
* **Step 1:** Go to Amazon's official portal: [amazon.com/sendtokindle](https://www.amazon.com/sendtokindle).
* **Step 2:** Sign in with your Amazon account.
* **Step 3:** Drag and drop the `.epub` file into the browser window.
* **Step 4:** Tap "Send". Within 2 minutes, the book will sync wirelessly to your Kindle device and Kindle mobile app with full cover art and active table of contents.

### B. How to Read on Apple Devices (iPad / iPhone / Mac)
* **Recommended Format:** `COME_BACK_TO_QURAN_114_SURAHS.epub`
* **Step 1:** Download the `.epub` file on your device.
* **Step 2:** Tap the downloaded file. It will automatically open in the native **Apple Books** app.
* **Tip:** For the best reading experience, set the font to *Iowan Old Style* or *Palatino*, and select the *Warm Sepia* or *Quiet Quiet* theme.

### C. How to Use the Offline Web Reader (`reader.html`)
* **Recommended Format:** `reader.html` (Included in Bundle & Tier 2)
* **Step 1:** Download `reader.html` to your computer, tablet, or phone.
* **Step 2:** Double-click to open in any web browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge).
* **Step 3 (No Internet Needed):** You can completely turn off Wi-Fi or go into Airplane mode. The entire 114-Surah text, search engine, and ambient rain audio generator run 100% locally from the single file.
* **Tip:** Bookmark the file in your browser's favorites bar for instant daily access.

### D. How to Print the Guided Journals (A4 / US Letter)
* **Recommended Format:** `7day_guided_journal_print.html` or `30day_journal_workbook_print.html`
* **Step 1:** Open the file in Chrome, Safari, or Edge.
* **Step 2:** Press `Ctrl + P` (Windows) or `Cmd + P` (Mac).
* **Step 3:** In the print dialog, select:
  - Destination: Save as PDF (or choose your physical printer).
  - Paper Size: A4 or US Letter.
  - Margins: Default.
  - Options: Ensure **"Background graphics"** is checked.
* **Step 4:** Print double-sided (flip on long edge) and bind in a simple binder or spiral notebook.
