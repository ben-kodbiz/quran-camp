# CHAPTER 59: SURAH AL-HASHR (سُورَةُ الحشر)
**Classification:** Madani  
**Order in Mushaf:** 59  
**Total Verses:** 24  
**Famous Names:** Surat al-Hashr, Surat Bani an-Nadir  

---

## 1. Why This Surah Matters
Surah Al-Hashr is the masterclass in communal solidarity, tactical psychology, and divine majesty. Named after the historic exile (*Al-Hashr*) of the treasonous tribe of Banu Nadir in 4 AH, it documents how their fortified fortresses and towering palm groves could not protect them when Allah's decree struck from angles they never anticipated (*Min haythu lam yahtasibu*). It immortalizes the sublime psychological virtue of the Ansar in verse 9—sacrificing for others even when in desperate personal need (*Wa yu'thiruna 'ala anfusihim wa law kana bihim khasasah*). The surah culminates in one of the most celebrated and majestic passages in all of scripture: the parable of the mountain shattering in awe if the Qur'an were revealed upon it (59:21), and the final cascade of Asma' Allah al-Husna (59:22–24).

---

## 2. Surah Context
Revealed in Madinah around 4 AH following the siege and bloodless surrender of the Banu Nadir, who plotted to drop a millstone from a rooftop to assassinate the Prophet ﷺ. Surah Al-Hashr legislated the distribution of the spoils of peace (*Al-Fay'*).

---

## 3. Central Theme
**The Sovereignty of Divine Strategy over Human Fortresses, the Altruism of the Ansar (*Al-Ithar*), and the Majestic Names of Allah (*Asma'ullah al-Husna*).**

---

## 4. Key Passages
### The Golden Standard of Altruism (59:9)
$$\text{وَالَّذِينَ تَبَوَّءُوا الدَّارَ وَالْإِيمَانَ مِن قَبْلِهِمْ يُحِبُّونَ مَنْ هَاجَرَ إِلَيْهِمْ وَلَا يَجِدُونَ فِي صُدُورِهِمْ حَاجَةً مِّمَّا أُوتُوا وَيُؤْثِرُونَ عَلَىٰ أَنفُسِهِمْ وَلَوْ كَانَ بِهِمْ خَصَاصَةٌ ۚ وَمَن يُوقَ شُحَّ نَفْسِهِ فَأُولَٰئِكَ هُمُ الْمُفْلِحُونَ}$$
*"And [also for] those who were settled in al-Madinah and [adopted] the faith before them. They love those who emigrated to them and find not any want in their breasts of what the emigrants were given but give [them] preference over themselves, even though they are in privation. And whoever is protected from the stinginess of his soul—it is those who will be the successful."*

### The Mountain Shattering Under the Qur'an (59:21)
$$\text{لَوْ أَنزَلْنَا هَٰذَا الْقُرْآنَ عَلَىٰ جَبَلٍ لَّرَأَيْتَهُ خَاشِعًا مُّتَصَدِّعًا مِّنْ خَشْيَةِ اللَّهِ ۚ وَتِلْكَ الْأَمْثَالُ نَضْرِبُهَا لِلنَّاسِ لَعَلَّهُمْ يَتَفَكَّرُونَ}$$
*"If We had sent down this Quran upon a mountain, you would have seen it humbled and splitting apart from fear of Allah. And these examples We present to the people that perhaps they will give thought."*

### The Sublime Finale of Divine Names (59:22–24)
$$\text{هُوَ اللَّهُ الَّذِي لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ ۖ عَالِمُ الْغَيْبِ وَالشَّهَادَةِ ۖ هُوَ الرَّحْمَٰنُ الرَّحِيمُ ۝ هُوَ اللَّهُ الَّذِي لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ الْمَلِكُ الْقُدُّوسُ السَّلَامُ الْمُؤْمِنُ الْمُهَيْمِنُ الْعَزِيزُ الْجَبَّارُ الْمُتَكَبِّرُ ۚ سُبْحَانَ اللَّهِ عَمَّا يُشْرِكُونَ ۝ هُوَ اللَّهُ الْخَالِقُ الْبَارِئُ الْمُصَوِّرُ ۖ لَهُ الْأَسْمَاءُ الْحُسْنَىٰ ۚ يُسَبِّحُ لَهُ مَا فِي السَّمَاوَاتِ وَالْأَرْضِ ۖ وَهُوَ الْعَزِيزُ الْحَكِيمُ}$$
*"He is Allah, other than whom there is no deity, Knower of the unseen and the witnessed. He is the Entirely Merciful, the Especially Merciful. He is Allah, other than whom there is no deity, the Sovereign, the Pure, the Perfection, the Bestower of Faith, the Overseer, the Exalted in Might, the Compeller, the Superior. Exalted is Allah above whatever they associate with Him. He is Allah, the Creator, the Inventor, the Fashioner; to Him belong the best names. Whatever is in the heavens and earth is exalting Him. And He is the Exalted in Might, the Wise."*

## 5. Translation Highlights (The Clear Quran)
* *59:2:* '...He cast terror into their hearts [so] they destroyed their houses with their own hands and the hands of the believers. So take warning, O people of insight.'
* *59:7:* '...So that it will not be a perpetual circulation among the rich from among you. And whatever the Messenger has given you—take; and what he has forbidden you—refrain from.'
* *59:18:* 'O you who have believed, fear Allah. And let every soul look to what it has put forth for tomorrow—and fear Allah. Indeed, Allah is Acquainted with what you do.'

---

## 6. What Classical Tafsir Explains
* **The Selflessness of the Ansar (Verse 9):** Imam al-Bukhari relates that a starving guest came to the Prophet ﷺ, who had nothing in his homes. An Ansari companion (Abu Talhah) took him home, told his wife to put the children to sleep, served their only meal to the guest, and pretended to eat by turning off the lamp so the guest could eat his fill. The next morning the Prophet ﷺ smiled and said: 'Allah was amazed and laughed tonight at what you two did!' and Allah revealed verse 59:9.
* **The Softness of Rock vs. The Hardness of Hearts (Verse 21):** Ibn Kathir comments: 'If an inanimate, solid, towering mountain of stone would be humbled and crack wide open out of awe of Allah upon hearing His words, how can human hearts—which possess reason, conscience, and eternal stakes—remain dry, hardened, and unmoved?'
---

## 7. Important Supporting Hadiths
1. **The Guest of Abu Talhah:**  
   *Sahih al-Bukhari (Hadith 3798) from Abu Hurayrah (RA):*  
   "A man came to the Prophet ﷺ, and the Prophet sent him to an Ansari companion. The companion preferred the guest over himself and his hungry family, regarding which verse 59:9 was revealed: *'Wa yu'thiruna 'ala anfusihim wa law kana bihim khasasah.'*"
2. **The Virtues of the Ending of Surah Al-Hashr:**  
   *Jami' at-Tirmidhi (Hadith 2922), Hasan:*  
   "The Prophet ﷺ said: 'Whoever says in the morning three times: *A'udhu billahis-Sami'il-'Alimi minash-shaytanir-rajim* and recites the last three verses of Surah Al-Hashr (59:22–24), Allah will appoint seventy thousand angels to send blessings upon him until evening; and if he dies that day, he dies a martyr.'"
---

## 8. Key Concepts
1. **Al-Ithar (Sacrificial Altruism):** The supreme moral quality of giving priority to the needs of others even when suffering personal scarcity.
2. **Shuhh an-Nafs (Innate Greed):** The petty, toxic spiritual stinginess that hoards resources and envies the success of fellow believers.
3. **Al-Asma' al-Husna (The Most Beautiful Names):** The fourteen transcendent divine names cataloged in verses 22–24 expressing divine perfection.
4. **Fa'tabiru Ya Ulil-Absar (Taking Admonition):** The command for people of insight to extract eternal lessons from the collapse of arrogant historical regimes.
---

## 9. Tadabbur (Guided Contemplation)
* **If a Mountain Had Received This Qur'an (59:21):** Picture Mount Everest or the granite cliffs of Mount Uhud. Allah says: If this revelation had been recited to that colossal mountain of granite, it would tremble, crack, and shatter into dust from *Khashyatullah*. Yet you hold this exact Qur'an in your hands, you recite it, and your heart barely flinches. Pray to Allah: 'O Lord, soften the stone in my chest so it feels the awe of Your speech.'
* **Look to What You Sent Ahead for Tomorrow (59:18):** Contemplate the word *Li-ghadin* ('for tomorrow'). Allah calls the Day of Judgment 'tomorrow' because our entire biological life is merely a blink of an eye. What have you sent ahead for your tomorrow? Did you send charity, prayers, tears of repentance, and kindness? Or are your hands empty?
---

## 10. What This Means For Me
* I recite the final three verses of Surah Al-Hashr every morning and evening to gain the prayers of 70,000 angels (59:22–24).
* I practice *Ithar* (altruism) by sharing my resources with those in need, overcoming the stinginess of my ego (59:9).
* I audit my life every night, asking: 'What did I send ahead today for my eternal tomorrow?' (59:18).

## 11. One Practical Action
**Memorize and Recite Verses 59:22–24 Daily:**  
Read verses 22 to 24 of Surah Al-Hashr today with mindful contemplation of all fourteen Divine Names (Al-Malik, Al-Quddus, As-Salam, Al-Mu'min, Al-Muhaymin, Al-'Aziz, Al-Jabbar, Al-Mutakabbir, Al-Khaliq, Al-Bari', Al-Musawwir).

## 12. Reflection Questions
1. If a massive mountain would shatter in awe of the Qur'an (59:21), why is my heart still dry and distracted?
2. Do I practice *Ithar* (giving preference to others) or am I controlled by *Shuhh an-Nafs* (stinginess) (59:9)?
3. What concrete good deeds did I send forth today for my eternal 'tomorrow' (59:18)?

## 13. Closing Supplication
$$\text{هُوَ اللَّهُ الَّذِي لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ الْمَلِكُ الْقُدُّوسُ السَّلَامُ الْمُؤْمِنُ الْمُهَيْمِنُ الْعَزِيزُ الْجَبَّارُ الْمُتَكَبِّرُ ، اللَّهُمَّ نَقِّ قُلُوبَنَا مِنْ شُحِّ أَنْفُسِنَا ، وَارْزُقْنَا خَشْيَتَكَ كَمَا يَخْشَاكَ الْجَبَلُ ، وَبَيِّضْ صَحَائِفَنَا لِغَدٍ}$$  
*"He is Allah, other than whom there is no deity, the Sovereign, the Pure, the Perfection, the Bestower of Faith, the Overseer, the Exalted in Might, the Compeller, the Superior. O Allah, purify our hearts from the stinginess of our souls, grant us awe of You as the mountain would fear You, and brighten our record for tomorrow. Amin."*
