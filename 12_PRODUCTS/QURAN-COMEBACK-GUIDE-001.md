```text
                                    DAY 01
                          THE DIVINE INVITATION
                   "Why did you stop listening to Him?"
================================================================================
```

### 1. The Ayah of the Day
Turn in your Qur'an to **Surah Muhammad (47:24)**:
$$\text{أَفَلَا يَتَدَبَّرُونَ الْقُرْآنَ أَمْ عَلَىٰ قُلُوبٍ أَقْفَالُهَا}$$
> *"Do they not then reflect upon the Quran? Or are there locks upon their hearts?"*

### 2. The 2-Minute Understanding (Tafsir Summary)
Classical mufassirun explain that *tadabbur* is derived from *dubur*—looking past surface speech to consider its ultimate consequences. Imam Ibn Kathir writes that when the heart is consumed by heedlessness and worldly noise, it gathers locks. But when Umar ibn al-Khattab (RA) heard a Yemeni youth say: *"Upon them are their locks until Allah unlocks them,"* he held him in deep esteem. You do not wait for a pure heart to approach the Qur'an; you bring your locked heart to Allah so He may open it.

### 3. Reflection Prompts
1. *What is the single biggest distraction locking your heart today?*  
   `[ Write here: ____________________________________________________________________ ]`
2. *When did you last spend five quiet minutes listening to Allah without checking your phone?*  
   `[ Write here: ____________________________________________________________________ ]`

### 4. Tonight's Dua & Action
* **Protocol:** Put your phone away. Recite 47:24 twice.
* **Dua:** *"Ya Allah, remover of locks, open my understanding to Your Book, and make the Qur'an the spring of my heart."*

```text
================================================================================
                                    DAY 02
                          THE LIVING COMPASS
                   "What is the Qur'an actually for?"
================================================================================
```

### 1. The Ayah of the Day
Turn in your Qur'an to **Surah Al-Baqarah (2:2)**:
$$\text{ذَٰلِكَ الْكِتَابُ لَا رَيْبَ ۛ فِيهِ ۛ هُدًى لِّلْمُتَّقِينَ}$$
> *"This is the Book! There is no doubt about it—a guide for those mindful of Allah."*

### 2. The 2-Minute Understanding (Tafsir Summary)
The Qur'an was not sent as a shelf ornament or academic riddle. Allah defines it as **Hudan**: *Guidance*. Ibn Kathir explains that it is specified for the *Muttaqin* because only those who acknowledge their need for direction will follow the map. When Ubayy ibn Ka'b (RA) asked Umar (RA) what *Taqwa* is, Umar described walking with gathered clothes on a path full of sharp thorns. *Taqwa* is living in this noisy world while treading with care so your soul remains untorn.

### 3. Reflection Prompts
1. *In what daily decision (work, family, speech) do you most urgently need divine guidance right now?*  
   `[ Write here: ____________________________________________________________________ ]`
2. *How would your day change if you consulted the Qur'an as a compass rather than a passive obligation?*  
   `[ Write here: ____________________________________________________________________ ]`

### 4. Tonight's Dua & Action
* **Protocol:** Open Surah Al-Baqarah verses 1–5. Read them slowly in English.
* **Dua:** *"Ya Hadi (O Guide), guide my steps along the Straight Path, and protect my heart from wandering into doubt."*

```text
================================================================================
                                    DAY 03
                          HE IS CLOSER THAN YOU THINK
                     "The conversation without barriers"
================================================================================
```

### 1. The Ayah of the Day
Turn in your Qur'an to **Surah Al-Baqarah (2:186)**:
$$\text{وَإِذَا سَأَلَكَ عِبَادِي عَنِّي فَإِنِّي قَرِيبٌ ۖ أُجِيبُ دَعْوَةَ الدَّاعِ إِذَا دَعَانِ}$$
> *"When My servants ask you ˹O Prophet˺ concerning Me: I am indeed near. I respond to the call of the caller when he calls upon Me."*

### 2. The 2-Minute Understanding (Tafsir Summary)
In Ma'arif-ul-Qur'an, Mufti Shafi notes that across the Qur'an, when questions are asked, Allah tells the Prophet ﷺ: *"Say [qul] to them..."* But in this verse, Allah omits *"Say"*, speaking directly to the servant: *"I am near."* There is no middleman, no application process, and no prerequisite of spiritual perfection. The moment you call, Allah responds.

### 3. Reflection Prompts
1. *What burden or worry have you been carrying entirely alone instead of placing it before Allah in dua?*  
   `[ Write here: ____________________________________________________________________ ]`
2. *Why do you feel distant from Allah when He has explicitly affirmed that He is near?*  
   `[ Write here: ____________________________________________________________________ ]`

### 4. Tonight's Dua & Action
* **Protocol:** Make wudu. Sit in private. Raise your hands for 3 full minutes without a script. Talk to Allah honestly.
* **Dua:** *"Ya Qarib (O Near One), You know my secret struggles and my open sins. Draw me near to You and never leave me to myself."*

```text
                          WHEN THE HEART MELTS
                    "Why the early believers wept"
================================================================================
```

### 1. The Ayah of the Day
Turn in your Qur'an to **Surah Az-Zumar (39:23)**:
$$\text{اللَّهُ نَزَّلَ أَحْسَنَ الْحَدِيثِ كِتَابًا مُتَشَابِهًا مَثَانِيَ تَقْشَعِرُّ مِنْهُ جُلُودُ الَّذِينَ يَخْشَوْنَ رَبَّهُمْ ثُمَّ تَلِينُ جُلُودُهُمْ وَقُلُوبُهُمْ إِلَىٰ ذِكْرِ اللَّهِ}$$
> *"Allah has sent down the best message—a Book of perfect consistency and repeated lessons—which causes the skin of those who fear their Lord to shiver, then their skin and hearts soften at the remembrance of Allah."*

### 2. The 2-Minute Understanding (Tafsir Summary)
Ibn Kathir records that when the Companions heard the Qur'an, they were not like hypocrites who yell or pretend; they stood in awe, their skin shivered with reverence, and then their hearts softened into peace and tears. When Abdullah ibn Mas'ud recited Surah An-Nisa to the Prophet ﷺ, tears overflowed from the blessed eyes of the Messenger of Allah (Sahih al-Bukhari 5050). The Qur'an was revealed to melt stone, not to leave hearts numb.

   `[ Write here: ____________________________________________________________________ ]`

* **Protocol:** Listen to a slow, measured recitation of Surah Az-Zumar verses 21–24 with the translation in your hands.
* **Dua:** *"O Allah, softener of hearts, soften my hardened chest toward Your Book and protect my eyes from spiritual dryness."*

```text
                                    DAY 05
                          DO NOT DESPAIR
                   "The door that never shuts"
================================================================================
```

### 1. The Ayah of the Day
Turn in your Qur'an to **Surah Az-Zumar (39:53)**:
$$\text{قُلْ يَا عِبَادِيَ الَّذِينَ أَسْرَفُوا عَلَىٰ أَنفُسِهِمْ لَا تَقْنَطُوا مِن رَّحْمَةِ اللَّهِ ۚ إِنَّ اللَّهَ يَغْفِرُ الذُّنُوبَ جَمِيعًا ۚ إِنَّهُ هُوَ الْغَفُورُ الرَّحِيمُ}$$
> *"Say, ˹O Prophet, that Allah says,˺ 'O My servants who have transgressed against themselves! Do not despair of the mercy of Allah. Indeed, Allah forgives all sins. Truly He is the All-Forgiving, Most Merciful.'"*

### 2. The 2-Minute Understanding (Tafsir Summary)
Ibn Kathir calls this verse the most hopeful ayah in the entire Qur'an. Notice how Allah addresses those who have committed excessive sins: He still calls them **"My servants"** (*'Ibādī*). He does not cast them out. He commands them: *Do not despair.* Despair is a trap of Shaytan designed to convince you that you are too broken to return to the Qur'an.

### 3. Reflection Prompts
1. *What past mistake or period of negligence makes you feel unworthy of reading the Qur'an?*  
   `[ Write here: ____________________________________________________________________ ]`
2. *If Allah forgives all sins without exception upon repentance, why are you holding yourself back?*  
   `[ Write here: ____________________________________________________________________ ]`

### 4. Tonight's Dua & Action
* **Protocol:** Read Surah Az-Zumar 39:53 three times. Believe every single word of it.
* **Dua:** *"Ya Ghafur (O Ever-Forgiving), I have wronged my soul immensely. I return to You today in surrender. Forgive my past and purify my future."*

```text
================================================================================
                                    DAY 06
                      THE SUSTAINABLE ARCHITECTURE
                 "How to build a 5-minute daily habit"
================================================================================
```

### 1. The Ayah of the Day
Turn in your Qur'an to **Surah Al-Muzzammil (73:4)**:
$$\text{أَوْ زِدْ عَلَيْهِ وَرَتِّلِ الْقُرْآنَ تَرْتِيلًا}$$
> *"Or a little more; and recite the Quran with measured, slow recitation."*

### 2. The 2-Minute Understanding (Tafsir Summary)
*Tartil* means reciting slowly, letter by letter, allowing the words to settle into the soul. The Prophet ﷺ said: *"The most beloved deeds to Allah are those that are consistent, even if they are small"* (Sahih al-Bukhari 6464). A permanent 5-minute habit of reading one ayah with understanding is vastly superior to reading thirty pages once a year and then abandoning the Book.

### 3. The 5-Minute Routine Card (Clip and Save)
* **Minute 1:** Quiet presence. Put your phone away.
* **Minute 2:** Recite one short passage slowly.
* **Minute 3:** Read the English translation.
* **Minute 4:** Ask: *What is Allah teaching me in this ayah today?*
* **Minute 5:** Make one private, heartfelt dua.

```text
                                    DAY 07
                          THE LIFELONG COMPANION
                     "The journey begins today"
================================================================================
```

### 1. The Ayah of the Day
Turn in your Qur'an to **Surah Al-Furqan (25:30)**:
$$\text{وَقَالَ الرَّسُولُ يَا رَبِّ إِنَّ قَوْمِي اتَّخَذُوا هَٰذَا الْقُرْآنَ مَهْجُورًا}$$
> *"The Messenger will say: 'O my Lord, indeed my people have taken this Quran as something to be abandoned.'"*

### 2. The Final Reflection
Ibn al-Qayyim explained that abandonment of the Qur'an (*hajr*) takes many forms: abandoning listening to it, abandoning reading it, abandoning reflecting on it, abandoning acting upon its guidance, and abandoning seeking healing from it. 

You have completed 7 days of quiet return. You have proven that you can find five minutes in your busy day for your Creator. 

Do not let today be the end of your journey. Let it be the day you made an unbreakable promise to yourself: **The Qur'an will never sit abandoned on my shelf again.**
