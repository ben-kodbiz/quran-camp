# CHAPTER 83: SURAH AL-MUTAFFIFIN (سُورَةُ المطففين)
**Classification:** Makki  
**Order in Mushaf:** 83  
**Total Verses:** 36  
**Famous Names:** Surat al-Mutaffifin, Waylun lil-Mutaffifin  

---

## 1. Why This Surah Matters
Surah Al-Mutaffifin is the divine manifesto of economic ethics and marketplace justice. It opens with an explosive curse of destruction (*Wayl*) directed against those who cheat in commercial scales: demanding full measure when they buy, but shortchanging others when they sell (83:1–3). It connects marketplace corruption directly to atheism: 'Do they not think that they will be resurrected for a tremendous Day?' (83:4–5). It contrasts the subterranean prison register of the wicked (*Sijjin*) with the soaring celestial register of the righteous (*'Illiyyun*), who drink from the purest, aged heavenly nectar sealed with musk (*Khitamuhu misk*). It closes with the ultimate cosmic vindication: on that Day, the believers will sit upon thrones, laughing at the mockers who once sneered at them in worldly life.

---

## 2. Surah Context
Revealed in transitional Makkah/Madinah. Ibn Abbas narrated that when the Prophet ﷺ arrived in Madinah, the people were notorious for cheating in weights and measures; when Surah Al-Mutaffifin was revealed, they became the most scrupulously honest merchants in Arabia.

---

## 3. Central Theme
**Commercial Integrity and Marketplace Ethics, the Reality of Sijjin and 'Illiyyun, the Sealed Musk-Nectar, and the Vindication of Believers.**

---

## 4. Key Passages
### The Curse on Commercial Cheaters (83:1–3)
$$\text{وَيْلٌ لِّلْمُطَفِّفِينَ ۝ الَّذِينَ إِذَا اكْتَالُوا عَلَى النَّاسِ يَسْتَوْفُونَ ۝ وَإِذَا كَالُوهُمْ أَو وَّزَنُوهُمْ يُخْسِرُونَ}$$
*"Woe to those who give short measure, Those who, when they take a measure from people, take in full. But if they give by measure or by weight to them, they cause loss."*

### The Day Humanity Stands Before the Lord (83:4–6)
$$\text{أَلَا يَظُنُّ أُولَٰئِكَ أَنَّهُم مَّبْعُوثُونَ ۝ لِيَوْمٍ عَظِيمٍ ۝ يَوْمَ يَقُومُ النَّاسُ لِرَبِّ الْعَالَمِينَ}$$
*"Do they not think that they will be resurrected For a tremendous Day—The Day when mankind will stand before the Lord of the worlds?"*

### The Competing for Pure Nectar Sealed with Musk (83:25–26)
$$\text{يُسْقَوْنَ مِن رَّحِيقٍ مَّخْتُومٍ ۝ خِتَامُهُ مِسْكٌ ۚ وَفِي ذَٰلِكَ فَلْيَتَنَافَسِ الْمُتَنَافِسُونَ}$$
*"They will be given to drink of pure wine [which was] sealed. The last of it [i.e., its residue] is musk. So for this let the competitors compete."*

### The Final Laugh (83:34–35)
$$\text{فَالْيَوْمَ الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا مِنَ الْكُفَّارِ يَضْحَكُونَ ۝ عَلَى الْأَرَائِكِ يَنظُرُونَ}$$
*"So Today those who believed are laughing at the disbelievers, On adorned couches, observing."*

## 5. Translation Highlights (The Clear Quran)
* *83:14:* 'No! Rather, the stain has covered their hearts of that which they were earning.'
* *83:15:* 'No! Indeed, from their Lord, that Day, they will be partitioned [veiled].'
* *83:22:* 'Indeed, the righteous will be in pleasure.'

---

## 6. What Classical Tafsir Explains
* **The Rusty Stain on the Heart (*Ar-Ran*) (Verse 14):** The Prophet ﷺ explained *Kalla bal rana 'ala qulubihim*: When a servant commits a sin, a black dot is etched onto his heart. If he repents and seeks forgiveness, his heart is polished clean. But if he persists and repeats the sin, the black spots multiply until his entire heart is encrusted in a dark, rust-like armor (*Ar-Ran*), blinding him to truth.
* **Veiled from Seeing Their Lord (Verse 15):** Imam ash-Shafi'i famously derived from verse 15 (*Kalla innahum 'an Rabbihim yawma'idhin lamahjubun*): 'Since Allah has veiled the wicked from seeing Him in wrath, this proves beyond all doubt that the righteous will gaze upon Him in love and joy!' Being denied the vision of God is the ultimate punishment of Hell.
---

## 7. Important Supporting Hadiths
1. **The Reformation of Madinah's Merchants:**  
   *Sunan Ibn Majah (Hadith 2223), Sahih:*  
   "Ibn Abbas (RA) said: 'When the Prophet ﷺ arrived in Madinah, the people were the worst in weighing and measuring. Then Allah revealed: *Waylun lil-Mutaffifin*, and after that, they gave the most meticulous, honest measure.'"
2. **The Black Spot on the Heart:**  
   *Jami' at-Tirmidhi (Hadith 3334), Sahih:*  
   "Abu Hurayrah (RA) reported that the Messenger of Allah ﷺ explained verse 83:14 regarding the progressive blackening of the heart caused by unrepented sins."
---

## 8. Key Concepts
1. **Al-Tatfif (Economic Cheating):** Shortchanging clients, underpaying employees, padding billable hours, or demanding full rights while withholding others' rights.
2. **Ar-Ran (The Heart's Rust):** The progressive spiritual calcification of the conscience caused by unrepented repeated sins.
3. **Sijjin vs. 'Illiyyun (The Subterranean vs. Celestial Archives):** The cosmic registers of human destiny: the lowest dungeon for the corrupt, and the highest heaven for the righteous.
4. **Khitamuhu Misk (Sealed with Musk):** The ultimate seal of excellence: let high-achievers compete (*Fa-yatafanas*) for the pure joys of Paradise rather than worldly trivia.
---

## 9. Tadabbur (Guided Contemplation)
* **Double Standards in Daily Life (83:2–3):** Notice the psychology of *Al-Tatfif*: When *you* buy a service, you demand 100% perfection, on-time delivery, and full measure (*Yastawfun*). But when *you* are on the clock, you arrive late, take long breaks, cut corners, and deliver 80% (*Yukhsirun*). *Tatfif* is not just market scales; it is hypocrisy in marriage, work, and friendship. Treat others with the same fairness you demand for yourself.
* **Compete for the Real Prize (83:26):** Allah looks at human ambition and says: *Wa fi dhalika falyatanafasil-mutanafisun* ('For this let the competitors compete!'). Humanity exhausts its energy competing for corporate vice presidencies, luxury cars, and follower counts—all of which turn to dust. If you have ambition, redirect your competitive fire into the eternal race: compete in Tahajjud, in charity, in Qur'an, and in humility.
---

## 10. What This Means For Me
* I practice scrupulous honesty in my professional and commercial work, giving full measure in hours, effort, and quality (83:1–3).
* I cleanse my heart daily with *Tawbah* and *Istighfar* to prevent the rusty stain of *Ar-Ran* from blinding my conscience (83:14).
* I compete in the eternal race (*Falyatanafas*) for Jannah rather than petty worldly status games (83:26).

---

## 11. One Practical Action
**Conduct an 'Economic Tatfif' Audit Today:**  
Examine your work today: Are you delivering the exact hours and quality your employer or clients are paying for? If you cut corners, rectify it today by giving extra focused effort, banishing *Tatfif* from your income.

---

## 12. Reflection Questions
1. Do I hold others to strict standards while giving myself generous passes when I fall short (83:2–3)?
2. Is my heart accumulating the dark rust of *Ar-Ran* due to repeated, normalized sins (83:14)?
3. What am I competing for in life: fleeting worldly prestige or the sealed nectar of Paradise (83:26)?

## 13. Closing Supplication
$$\text{اللَّهُمَّ طَهِّرْ قُلُوبَنَا مِنَ الرَّانِ ، وَنَجِّنَا مِنْ سِجِّينَ ، وَاجْعَلْ كِتَابَنَا فِي عِلِّيِّينَ ، اللَّهُمَّ اسْقِنَا مِنْ رَحِيقٍ مَخْتُومٍ خِتَامُهُ مِسْكٌ ، وَلَا تَحْجُبْنَا عَنْ رُؤْيَةِ وَجْهِكَ الْكَرِيمِ}$$  
*"O Allah, purify our hearts from rust, save us from Sijjin, and place our record in 'Illiyyun. O Allah, grant us to drink of pure nectar sealed with musk, and do not veil us from gazing upon Your Noble Face. Amin."*
