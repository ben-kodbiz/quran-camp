# CHAPTER 106: SURAH QURAYSH (سُورَةُ قريش)
**Classification:** Makki  
**Order in Mushaf:** 106  
**Total Verses:** 4  
**Famous Names:** Surat Quraysh, Li-Ilafi Quraysh  

---

## 1. Why This Surah Matters
Surah Quraysh is the twin theological sister to Surah Al-Fil. While Al-Fil described the destruction of the external enemy who sought to ruin Makkah, Surah Quraysh describes the positive blessings bestowed upon its inhabitants: commercial security, economic prosperity, and freedom from terror. Through the sacred covenant of the winter caravan to Yemen and the summer caravan to the Levant (*Rihlatash-shita'i was-sayf*), Quraysh traveled safely across bandit-infested deserts solely because they were the custodians of the Ka'bah. In return for these two fundamental human rights—food security (*At'amahum min joo'*) and physical safety (*Amanahum min khawf*)—Allah demands one singular duty: 'Let them worship the Lord of this House' (106:3).

---

## 2. Surah Context
Revealed in Makkah to remind the tribe of Quraysh of their immense geopolitical and economic privileges, warning them that their prosperity is a gift of the Ka'bah's Lord, not inherent tribal superiority.

---

## 3. Central Theme
**The Twin Foundations of Human Well-Being (Food and Safety) as Divine Blessings Calling for Undivided Worship.**

---

## 4. Key Passages
### The Familiarity and the Caravans (106:1–2)
$$\text{لِإِيلَافِ قُرَيْشٍ ۝ إِيلَافِهِمْ رِحْلَةَ الشِّتَاءِ وَالصَّيْفِ}$$
*"For the accustomed security of the Quraysh—Their accustomed security [in] the caravan of winter and summer."*

### The Core Obligation and the Twin Blessings (106:3–4)
$$\text{فَلْيَعْبُدُوا رَبَّ هَٰذَا الْبَيْتِ ۝ الَّذِي أَطْعَمَهُم مِّن جُوعٍ وَآمَنَهُم مِّنْ خَوْفٍ}$$
*"Let them worship the Lord of this House, Who has fed them, [saving them] from hunger and made them safe, [saving them] from fear."*

---

## 5. Translation Highlights (The Clear Quran)
* *106:1:* 'For the customary security of Quraysh (*Li-ilafi Quraysh*).'
* *106:2:* 'Their custom of trade caravans in winter and summer.'
* *106:3:* 'So let them worship the Lord of this Sacred House (*Rabba hadhal bayt*).'
* *106:4:* 'Who provided them with food against hunger, and secured them against fear (*Amanahum min khawf*).'

---

## 6. What Classical Tafsir Explains
* **The Economic Miracle of the Desert (Verses 1–2):** Ibn Kathir notes that Makkah was an uncultivated barren valley with no agriculture, rivers, or natural resources. Yet, because of Prophet Ibrahim's supplication, luxury fruits, wheat, and spices flowed to them from Syria, Yemen, and Abyssinia, and no desert tribes dared raid their caravans out of reverence for the Sanctuary.
* **The Twin Foundations of Society: Food and Safety (Verse 4):** As-Sa'di observes that all human happiness in this world rests upon two pillars: sustenance (*Amn al-Ghiza'i*) and security (*Amn as-Siyasi*). Without food, starvation kills; without safety, constant dread paralyzes. Allah guaranteed both to Makkah so they could dedicate themselves to worship.
* **The Connection to Surah Al-Fil:** In ancient manuscripts of Ubayy ibn Ka'b, Surah Al-Fil and Surah Quraysh were written as a single continuum: 'Did We not destroy the companions of the elephant... in order to secure Quraysh in their caravans? Therefore, worship the Lord of this House!'
---

## 7. Important Supporting Hadiths
1. **The Seven Virtues Given to Quraysh:**  
   *Al-Mu'jam al-Kabir of At-Tabarani, Sahih per Al-Albani:*  
   "The Prophet ﷺ said: 'Allah favored Quraysh with seven characteristics: ... among them, He revealed a Surah of the Qur'an regarding them in which He mentioned none other than them: *Li-ilafi Quraysh*.'"
2. **Whoever Wakes Up Safe and Fed:**  
   *Jami' at-Tirmidhi (Hadith 2346), Hasan:*  
   "The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: 'Whoever among you wakes up secure in his property, healthy in his body, and possessing his food for the day, it is as if the entire world has been gathered for him.'"
---

## 8. Key Concepts
1. **Ilaf (Accustomed Harmony & Security):** The divine stabilization of economic trade and social peace.
2. **Rabb Hadhal Bayt (Lord of this House):** The Creator who owns the Ka'bah and dispenses protection to those near it.
3. **Kifayat al-Joo' (Freedom from Hunger):** Food security as a direct spiritual blessing demanding gratitude.
4. **Amn min al-Khawf (Freedom from Fear):** Peace and civil safety as the foundation of religious devotion and civil life.
---

## 9. Tadabbur (Guided Contemplation)
* **The Hadith of the Three Blessings:** Look around you right now: Are you physically safe from bombing and assault? Do you have food for the next 24 hours? Are you free from debilitating illness? Realize you possess the equivalent of the entire globe.
* **Economic Success is Not Inherent Brilliance:** Quraysh thought their trade caravans were due to their sharp business acumen. Allah reminded them: You survive because of the Ka'bah. Audit your own career: Do you attribute success to your IQ, or to Allah's grace?
* **The True Price of Safety:** When you enjoy peace in your city and food in your pantry, the only legitimate payment is *Falya'budu*: dedicated, sincere worship of the Lord of the House.
---

## 10. What This Means For Me
* My daily grocery run and peaceful sleep at night are massive miracles that millions of refugees pray for daily.
* I must never use the economic stability Allah gives me to rebel against His commandments.
* True thanksgiving for food and safety is using my strength to feed the hungry and protect the vulnerable.

---

## 11. One Practical Action
**The Table of Gratitude and Action:**  
Tonight at dinner, before you take your first bite, recite Surah Quraysh aloud to yourself or your family. Reflect on the words *At'amahum min joo'in wa amanahum min khawf*, thank Allah for the warm meal, and make a financial transfer to a hunger relief kitchen.

---

## 12. Reflection Questions
1. How would your worship change if you experienced real hunger or constant physical fear for just three days?
2. What business privileges or economic conveniences do you routinely take for granted?
3. How can your community turn its material blessings into active centers of service and worship as commanded in verse 3?

## 13. Closing Supplication
$$\text{اللَّهُمَّ أَدِمْ عَلَيْنَا نِعْمَةَ الْأَمْنِ وَالْإِيمَانِ وَأَطْعِمْنَا مِنْ جُوعٍ وَآمِنَّا مِنْ خَوْفٍ وَاجْعَلْنَا لَكَ مِنَ الشَّاكِرِينَ}$$  
*"O Allah, perpetuate upon us the blessings of safety and faith, feed us against hunger, secure us against fear, and make us among Your grateful servants."*
