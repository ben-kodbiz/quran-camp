# CHAPTER 44: SURAH AD-DUKHAN (سُورَةُ الدخان)
**Classification:** Makki  
**Order in Mushaf:** 44  
**Total Verses:** 59  
**Famous Names:** Surat ad-Dukhan  

---

## 1. Why This Surah Matters
Surah Ad-Dukhan is the dramatic warning of cosmic upheaval, divine mercy, and the sobering fate of arrogant tyrants. It opens with the solemn proclamation of the Qur'an's descent on the Blessed Night (*Laylatin Mubarakah*—Laylatul Qadr), where every decree of wisdom is apportioned. It foretells the sign of the visible Smoke (*Dukhan Mubin*) that enshrouds the heavens. It recounts the drowning of Pharaoh and his armies, noting with haunting cosmic indifference: 'And the heaven and earth wept not for them, nor were they given respite' (44:29). It contrasts the toxic boiling Tree of Zaqqum in Hell with the tranquil gardens and silken robes of the righteous in Paradise.

---

## 2. Surah Context
Revealed in Makkah when the Prophet ﷺ prayed for a drought against the stubborn Quraysh who boycotted the believers. A famine struck Makkah so severely that people saw a haze resembling smoke in the sky from sheer starvation and exhaustion. The Quraysh pleaded for relief, but when relief came, they returned to arrogance.

---

## 3. Central Theme
**The Blessed Night of Decree, the Cosmic Sign of Smoke, the Indifference of the Universe to Tyrants, and the Tree of Zaqqum.**

---

## 4. Key Passages
### The Revelation on the Blessed Night (44:1–4)
$$\text{حم ۝ وَالْكِتَابِ الْمُبِينِ ۝ إِنَّا أَنزَلْنَاهُ فِي لَيْلَةٍ مُّبَارَكَةٍ ۚ إِنَّا كُنَّا مُنذِرِينَ ۝ فِيهَا يُفْرَقُ كُلُّ أَمْرٍ حَكِيمٍ}$$
*"Ha, Meem. By the clear Book, Indeed, We sent it down during a blessed night. Indeed, We were to warn. Therein is made distinct every matter of wisdom."*

### The Universe Did Not Weep for Them (44:25–29)
$$\text{كَمْ تَرَكُوا مِن جَنَّاتٍ وَعُيُونٍ ۝ وَزُرُوعٍ وَمَقَامٍ كَرِيمٍ ۝ وَنَعْمَةٍ كَانُوا فِيهَا فَاكِهِينَ ۝ كَذَٰلِكَ ۖ وَأَوْرَثْنَاهَا قَوْمًا آخَرِينَ ۝ فَمَا بَكَتْ عَلَيْهِمُ السَّمَاءُ وَالْأَرْضُ وَمَا كَانُوا مُنظَرِينَ}$$
*"How much they left behind of gardens and springs, And crops and noble sites, And comfort wherein they used to amuse themselves! Thus. And We caused to inherit it another people. And the heaven and earth wept not for them, nor were they reprieved."*

### The Tree of Zaqqum (44:43–46)
$$\text{إِنَّ شَجَرَتَ الزَّقُّومِ ۝ طَعَامُ الْأَثِيمِ ۝ كَالْمُهْلِ يَغْلِي فِي الْبُطُونِ ۝ كَغَلْيِ الْحَمِيمِ}$$
*"Indeed, the tree of deadly fruit (Zaqqum) Is food for the sinful, Like molten brass; it boils within bellies Like the boiling of scalding water."*

## 5. Translation Highlights (The Clear Quran)
* *44:10–11:* 'Then watch for the day when the sky will bring a visible smoke covering the people; this is a painful torment.'
* *44:38:* 'And We did not create the heavens and earth and that between them in play.'
* *44:51–52:* 'Indeed, the righteous will be in a secure place; Within gardens and springs.'

---

## 6. What Classical Tafsir Explains
* **The Weeping of Heaven and Earth (Verse 29):** Ibn Abbas (RA) was asked: 'Do the heavens and earth really weep for anyone?' He replied: 'Yes! Every believer has a gate in heaven through which his good deeds ascend and his provision descends; when he dies, that gate weeps for him. And the spot of earth upon which he used to pray and remember Allah weeps for him. But Pharaoh's people had no good deeds on earth and no righteous words ascending to heaven, so neither heaven nor earth wept for them!'
* **The Blessed Night (Verse 3):** The vast majority of scholars, including Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, and Ibn Kathir, confirm that *Laylatun Mubarakah* is Laylat al-Qadr in Ramadan (as confirmed in Surah 97 and Surah 2:185), during which the annual cosmic decrees of life, death, provision, and health are dispatched to the angels.
---

## 7. Important Supporting Hadiths
1. **The Sign of the Smoke (Ad-Dukhan):**  
   *Sahih Muslim (Hadith 2901) from Hudhayfah ibn Usayd (RA):*  
   "The Prophet ﷺ mentioned ten major signs of the Hour, beginning with: 'The Smoke (*Ad-Dukhan*), the Dajjal, the Beast, the rising of the sun from its west, the descent of Isa ibn Maryam...'"
2. **The Earth Weeping for the Believer:**  
   *Jami' at-Tirmidhi (Hadith 3255), Hasan:*  
   "The Prophet ﷺ said: 'There is no believer except that he has two gates: a gate from which his deed ascends and a gate from which his provision descends. When he dies, they both weep for him.' Then he recited: *'Fama bakat 'alayhimus-sama'u wal-ard'* (44:29)."
---

## 8. Key Concepts
1. **Laylatun Mubarakah (The Blessed Night):** The majestic night of destiny in Ramadan where annual decrees are handed to the angels.
2. **Ad-Dukhan al-Mubin (The Visible Smoke):** The cataclysmic eschatological smoke signaling the end of time, and the historical drought in Makkah.
3. **Buka' as-Sama' wal-Ard (The Mourning of Creation):** The profound reality that physical creation forms an emotional spiritual bond with the sincere worshipper.
4. **Shajarat az-Zaqqum (The Tree of Bitter Torment):** The hellish botanical creation boiling within the stomachs of persistent transgressors.
---

## 9. Tadabbur (Guided Contemplation)
* **Will the Earth Weep for You When You Die? (44:29):** Contemplate the passing of Pharaoh: He left behind palaces, golden gardens, private springs, and vast armies—and the cosmos did not shed a single tear. The earth did not care, and heaven remained silent. But when a quiet, sincere believer dies, the very spot of carpet where they made Sujud weeps! Fill your home, your workplace, and your prayer spaces with prostrations and Dhikr so that creation mourns your departure.
* **The Illusion of Leaving It All Behind (44:25–27):** Read the chilling inventory: *Kam taraku min jannatin wa 'uyun* ('How much they left behind of gardens and springs!'). You spend fifty years decorating your house, accumulating savings, and landscaping your lawn—and in one second, your soul is pulled out, and strangers inherit your property. Live for the estate that you will never leave: Jannah.
---

## 10. What This Means For Me
* I pray on diverse spots of earth in my home and workplace so that multiple patches of ground bear witness and weep for me (44:29).
* I honor and seek out Laylat al-Qadr with maximum devotion, knowing that all wise decrees are decided therein (44:3–4).
* I never let material prosperity delude me into thinking I am secure from Allah's decree.

---

## 11. One Practical Action
**Establish a New 'Sujud Spot' Today:**  
Pick a new, quiet corner in your home or office where you have never prayed before. Perform two voluntary rak'ahs there today, marking that spot of earth as a witness of your worship before Allah.

---

## 12. Reflection Questions
1. When I leave this world, will heaven and earth mourn my absence or remain indifferent like they were to Pharaoh (44:29)?
2. How am I preparing for the Blessed Night (*Laylatun Mubarakah*) where the affairs of my year are decreed (44:3–4)?
3. Am I building worldly estates that I will leave behind, or an eternal home in Paradise that will never perish?
