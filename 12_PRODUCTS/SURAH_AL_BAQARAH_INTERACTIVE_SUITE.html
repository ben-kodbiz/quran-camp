👑 SHOWCASE I: AYAT AL-KURSI — THE THRONE VERSE (PART 17)

The Supreme Ayah of the Holy Qur'an

When the Prophet (peace be upon him) asked Ubayy ibn Ka'b: "Which ayah in the Book of Allah with you is the greatest?" Ubayy answered: "Allahu la ilaha illa Huwa al-Hayy al-Qayyum." The Prophet struck him upon the chest and said: "May knowledge be joyful and sweet for you, O Abu al-Mundhir!" (Sahih Muslim).

Concentric Ring Composition (Chiasmus)

Ayat al-Kursi is structured across 10 perfect clauses organized in symmetrical mirror pairs:

  • A: Monotheism & Self-Subsistence (Allahu la ilaha illa Huwa al-Hayy al-Qayyum)
  • B: Eternal Wakefulness (Neither slumber nor sleep overtakes Him)
  • C: Absolute Dominion (To Him belongs whatever is in the heavens and earth)
  • D: Sovereign Authorization (Who can intercede except by His permission?)
  • E (Center Pivot): Omniscient Knowledge of past and future
  • D': Human Epistemological Limitation (They encompass none of His knowledge except what He wills)
  • C': Cosmic Throne Expansiveness (His Kursi encompasses the heavens and the earth)
  • B': Effortless Preservation (Their preservation tires Him not)
  • A': Supreme Transcendence (He is the Most High, the Most Great)
Classical Sunni Exegetical Anchors
  • Ibn Kathir: Confirms that Al-Hayy is the Ever-Living who never dies, and Al-Qayyum is the Self-Sustaining Maintainer of all existing things, without whom nothing could subsist for a fraction of a second.
  • Al-Qurtubi: Details that the Kursi is not a seat of physical resting, but a colossal celestial creation of magnificent grandeur demonstrating divine omnipotence, as Ibn Abbas explained: "The seven heavens compared to the Kursi are like a small coin tossed into a vast desert."
  • Sahih al-Bukhari: Abu Hurairah's encounter with the intruder at the charity storehouse, where the Prophet (peace be upon him) confirmed: "When you go to bed, recite Ayat al-Kursi, for Allah will appoint a guardian angel over you, and no devil will approach you until morning."
🛡️ SHOWCASE II: THE LAST TWO VERSES — AMANAR-RASOOL (PART 19)

The Crowning Seal & Nocturnal Ascension Gift

Abdullah ibn Mas'ud narrated that during the Night of Ascension (Al-Isra' wal-Mi'raj), the Messenger of Allah (peace be upon him) was granted three supreme gifts: the five daily prayers, the seal of Surah Al-Baqarah, and forgiveness of major sins for anyone in his nation who does not associate partners with Allah (Sahih Muslim).

The Magna Carta of Human Moral Capacity

When "Whether you reveal what is within yourselves or conceal it, Allah will bring you to account for it" descended, the Companions collapsed to their knees in weeping terror, fearing unintentional thoughts.

The revelation then descended with the ultimate universal relief:
'La yukallifullahu nafsan illa wus'aha' — Allah burdens no soul beyond its capacity. Involuntary whispers (*waswasah*) carry zero sin. Sincere striving is recognized, and divine mercy permanently supersedes crushing despair.

Answered Petitions: 'Qad Fa'alt' (I Have Done So)
  • Rabbana la tu'akhidhna in naseena aw akhta'na: "Our Lord, do not take us to task if we forget or err!" • Allah replies: "Qad fa'alt" (I have done so!)
  • Rabbana wa la tahmil 'alayna isran: "Our Lord, lay not upon us a burden like that laid upon nations before us!" • Allah replies: "Qad fa'alt" (I have done so!)
  • Rabbana wa la tuhammilna ma la taqata lana bih: "Our Lord, burden us not with that which we have no strength to bear!" • Allah replies: "Qad fa'alt" (I have done so!)
  • Anta Mawlana fansurna 'alal-qawmil-kafirin: "You are our Protector, so grant us victory over the disbelieving people!"
