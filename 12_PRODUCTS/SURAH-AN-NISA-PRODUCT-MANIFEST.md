The **Surah An-Nisa Master Cartography & Interactive Digital Suite** establishes the authoritative pedagogical, structural, and legal cartography for the fourth chapter of the Holy Qur'an. Drawing from over 10 hours of systematic exegesis across 29 foundation audio lectures, this publication packages the social, judicial, familial, martial, and theological dimensions of Surah An-Nisa into an elegant **16:9 landscape widescreen format** (792 &times; 480 pt).

While Surah Ali-Imran fortifies the ideological and military resolve of the community under crisis, **Surah An-Nisa constructs the internal institutional and ethical fabric of the civilization**:
1. **The Primordial Root & Vulnerable Protection:** Grounding all ethics in *Nafsin Wahidah* (a single ontological human root) and demanding fierce sanctuary for the vulnerable—orphans, widows, dependent relatives, and women disinherited under pagan custom.
2. **The Transcendent Standard of Justice (*Qist*):** Decoupling judicial impartiality from tribal kinship, emotional sentiment, wealth, and communal affiliation—demonstrated decisively in the Trial of Tu'mah ibn Ubayriq where divine revelation vindicated an innocent Jewish resident against a Muslim wrongdoer.
