# CHAPTER 108: SURAH AL-KAWTHAR (سُورَةُ الكوثر)
**Classification:** Makki  
**Order in Mushaf:** 108  
**Total Verses:** 3  
**Famous Names:** Surat al-Kawthar, Inna A'taynaka  

---

## 1. Why This Surah Matters
Surah Al-Kawthar is the shortest Surah in the Noble Qur'an—consisting of merely three verses and ten words—yet it stands as an ocean of divine consolation and prophetic vindication. When the infant sons of the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ (Al-Qasim and 'Abdullah) passed away, pagan aristocrats like Al-'As ibn Wa'il gloated with cruel taunts, declaring Muhammad to be *Abtar*—a severed man with no male lineage, whose legacy would vanish into historical obscurity. Allah descended this celestial rebuttal: 'Indeed, We have granted you, [O Muhammad], Al-Kawthar' (108:1)—infinite abundance in this life, eternal fame, millions of spiritual followers, and the majestic celestial River and Pool in Paradise. In response, the Prophet is commanded to anchor his devotion in two towering acts: sincere Prayer and Sacrifice for Allah alone, while his mocking enemy is the one forever severed.

---

## 2. Surah Context
Revealed in Makkah during the intense grief of the Prophet ﷺ following the death of his infant boys, when polytheist elites celebrated his personal tragedies.

---

## 3. Central Theme
**The Bestowal of Transcendent Abundance (*Al-Kawthar*) upon the Prophet ﷺ, Sincere Prayer and Sacrifice for Allah Alone, and the Extinction of His Mockers.**

---

## 4. Key Passages
### The Transcendent Gift (108:1)
$$\text{إِنَّا أَعْطَيْنَاكَ الْكَوْثَرَ}$$
*"Indeed, We have granted you, [O Muhammad], al-Kawthar."*

### The Twin Pillars of Gratitude (108:2)
$$\text{فَصَلِّ لِرَبِّكَ وَانْحَرْ}$$
*"So pray to your Lord and sacrifice [to Him alone]."*

### The Eternal Cutting Off of the Enemy (108:3)
$$\text{إِنَّ شَانِئَكَ هُوَ الْأَبْتَرُ}$$
*"Indeed, your enemy is the one cut off."*

---

## 5. Translation Highlights (The Clear Quran)
* *108:1:* 'Indeed, We have given you the Abundance (*Al-Kawthar*).'
* *108:2:* 'So pray to your Lord and sacrifice (*Fasalli li-Rabbika wanhar*).'
* *108:3:* 'Indeed, the one who hates you is the one severed (*Huwal Abtar*).'

---

## 6. What Classical Tafsir Explains
* **What is *Al-Kawthar*? (Verse 1):** Ibn 'Abbas explained: 'It is the immense goodness (*Al-Khayr al-Katheer*) that Allah granted him.' When his student Sa'id ibn Jubayr asked about the river in Paradise, Ibn 'Abbas replied: 'The heavenly river is only one part of the immense abundance Allah gave him.' It encompasses the Qur'an, Prophethood, the intercession (*Shafa'ah*), billions of believers sending blessings upon him, and the celestial Basin (*Al-Hawd*).
* **Pray and Sacrifice (*Fasalli li-Rabbika wanhar*) (Verse 2):** Imam Ibn Kathir highlights that while polytheists prayed to idols and slaughtered their animals before false gods, the Prophet ﷺ was commanded: Make your prayer exclusively for your Lord, and slaughter your sacrifices only in His name. This is the bedrock of *Tawheed* in physical and financial devotion.
* **The Fulfillment of Prophecy (*Huwal Abtar*) (Verse 3):** Centuries later, the name of Muhammad ﷺ is proclaimed five times every single day from every minaret across all continents, while his mocking enemies like Al-'As ibn Wa'il are completely forgotten except as footnotes of ignominy.
---

## 7. Important Supporting Hadiths
1. **The Description of the River Al-Kawthar:**  
   *Sahih Muslim (Hadith 400) from Anas ibn Malik (RA):*  
   "While the Prophet ﷺ was among us, he took a brief nap, then raised his head smiling. We asked: 'What made you smile, O Messenger of Allah?' He said: 'A Surah was just revealed to me: *Inna a'taynaka al-kawthar...*' Then he said: 'Do you know what Al-Kawthar is? It is a river my Lord has promised me in Paradise, containing abundant goodness. Over it is a cistern to which my Ummah will come on the Day of Resurrection; its drinking vessels are as numerous as the stars.'"
2. **Drink from the Prophet's Hand:**  
   *Sahih al-Bukhari (Hadith 6579) from Sahl ibn Sa'd (RA):*  
   "The Prophet ﷺ said: 'I am your predecessor at the Cistern (*Al-Hawd*). Whoever reaches it will drink, and whoever drinks from it will never feel thirst again.'"
---

## 8. Key Concepts
1. **Al-Kawthar (Abundance):** The limitless spiritual and celestial gifts bestowed upon the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ.
2. **Al-Hawd (The Prophetic Cistern):** The celestial pool on Judgment Day where believers drink sweet, cool water from the Prophet's hand.
3. **An-Nahr (Sacrifice for Allah Alone):** Slaughtering livestock solely in Allah's name, rejecting all polytheistic and commercial compromises.
4. **Al-Abtar (The Severed):** The tragic reality that enemies of divine truth will leave behind no honorable legacy, cut off from all good.
---

## 9. Tadabbur (Guided Contemplation)
* **Divine Comfort in the Face of Grief:** Contemplate the Prophet's immense sorrow losing his baby sons, followed by brutal taunts. Reflect on how Allah heals broken hearts with eternal promises instead of temporary worldly compensations.
* **Yearning for the Cistern (*Al-Hawd*):** Imagine the scorching heat of Judgment Day, fifty thousand years long, and coming face to face with Muhammad ﷺ beside a pool whiter than milk and sweeter than honey. What Sunnah will you follow today to earn that drink?
* **The Power of *Nahr*:** Sacrifice means parting with what you love for the One you love most. What ego, wealth, or comfort are you willing to sacrifice for Allah today?
---

## 10. What This Means For Me
* Whenever people mock my faith or demean my values, I will remember Surah Al-Kawthar: truth always outlives the cynic.
* I must send abundant Salawat (blessings) upon the Prophet ﷺ daily to express my gratitude for his sacrifice.
* My worship must remain pure: every prayer (*Salah*) and every sacrifice (*Nahr*) must be dedicated strictly to Allah.
