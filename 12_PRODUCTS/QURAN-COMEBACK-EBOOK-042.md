# CHAPTER 42: SURAH ASH-SHURA (سُورَةُ الشورى)
**Classification:** Makki  
**Order in Mushaf:** 42  
**Total Verses:** 53  
**Famous Names:** Surat ash-Shura, Hamim 'Ain Sin Qaf  

---

## 1. Why This Surah Matters
Surah Ash-Shura is the constitutional sanctuary of divine revelation and collective wisdom. It opens with the unique five-letter combination *Ha-Meem-'Ain-Seen-Qaf*, establishing that all prophetic revelation across human history originates from one singular divine source. It contains the bedrock theological statement of absolute monotheism in verse 11: *Laysa kamithlihi shay'un wa Huwas-Sami'ul-Basir* ('There is nothing like unto Him, and He is the Hearing, the Seeing'). It articulates the governance philosophy of the believers in verse 38: *Wa amruhum shura baynahum* ('And their affair is conducted by mutual consultation'), establishing transparency and communal consultation over authoritarian tyranny.

---

## 2. Surah Context
Revealed in Makkah to unite the hearts of the early believers. It showed that Islam is not a novel novelty, but the identical covenant given to Nuh, Ibrahim, Musa, and Isa (42:13). It warned against sectarian divisions and emphasized mutual consultation as an essential community virtue.

---

## 3. Central Theme
**The Transcendence of Allah (*Tanzih*), the Unity of Prophetic Revelation, Governance Through Consultation (*Ash-Shura*), and Divine Distribution of Provision.**

---

## 4. Key Passages
### The Absolute Transcendence of Allah (42:11)
$$\text{فَاطِرُ السَّمَاوَاتِ وَالْأَرْضِ ۚ جَعَلَ لَكُم مِّنْ أَنفُسِكُمْ أَزْوَاجًا وَمِنَ الْأَنْعَامِ أَزْوَاجًا ۖ يَذْرَؤُكُمْ فِيهِ ۚ لَيْسَ كَمِثْلِهِ شَيْءٌ ۖ وَهُوَ السَّمِيعُ الْبَصِيرُ}$$
*"[He is] Originator of the heavens and the earth. He has made for you from yourselves, mates, and among the cattle, mates; He multiplies you thereby. There is nothing like unto Him, and He is the Hearing, the Seeing."*

### The Common Thread of All Religions (42:13)
$$\text{شَرَعَ لَكُم مِّنَ الدِّينِ مَا وَصَّىٰ بِهِ نُوحًا وَالَّذِي أَوْحَيْنَا إِلَيْكَ وَمَا وَصَّيْنَا بِهِ إِبْرَاهِيمَ وَمُوسَىٰ وَعِيسَىٰ ۖ أَنْ أَقِيمُوا الدِّينَ وَلَا تَتَفَرَّقُوا فِيهِ}$$
*"He has ordained for you of religion what He enjoined upon Noah and that which We have revealed to you, [O Muhammad], and what We enjoined upon Abraham and Moses and Jesus—to establish the religion and not be divided therein."*

### The Culture of Mutual Consultation (42:38)
$$\text{وَالَّذِينَ اسْتَجَابُوا لِرَبِّهِمْ وَأَقَامُوا الصَّلَاةَ وَأَمْرُهُمْ شُورَىٰ بَيْنَهُمْ وَمِمَّا رَزَقْنَاهُمْ يُنفِقُونَ}$$
*"And those who have responded to their Lord and established prayer and whose affair is [determined by] consultation among themselves, and from what We have provided them, they spend."*

---

## 5. Translation Highlights (The Clear Quran)
* *42:19:* 'Allah is Subtle with His servants; He gives provision to whom He wills. And He is the Powerful, the Exalted in Might.'
* *42:20:* 'Whoever desires the harvest of the Hereafter—We increase for him in his harvest. And whoever desires the harvest of this world—We give him thereof, but there is not for him in the Hereafter any share.'
* *42:40:* 'And the retribution for an evil act is an evil one like it, but whoever pardons and makes reconciliation—his reward is [due] from Allah. Indeed, He does not like wrongdoers.'

---

## 6. What Classical Tafsir Explains
* **The Theological Golden Rule: 'Laysa Kamithlihi Shay'' (Verse 11):** Ahl as-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah scholars, including Imam Ibn Kathir and Al-Qurtubi, consider verse 11 the cornerstone of Islamic creed (*'Aqidah*): It negates any likeness, form, or resemblance between Allah and His creation (*Tanzih*), while simultaneously affirming His divine attributes of Hearing and Seeing (*Ithbat*) without distortion, anthropomorphism, or denial.
* **Shura as a Defining Spiritual Quality (Verse 38):** Notice how Allah places *Shura* (consultation) side by side between two pillars of worship: *Aqamu as-Salah* (establishing prayer) and *Yunfiqun* (spending charity). This proves that seeking advice from family, spouses, and team members is not merely a secular business tool; it is a sacred spiritual quality of the believers.
---

## 7. Important Supporting Hadiths
1. **Seeking Advice and Consultation:**  
   *Sunan at-Tirmidhi (Hadith 2822), Sahih:*  
   "Abu Hurayrah (RA) said: 'I never saw anyone who consulted his companions more frequently than the Messenger of Allah ﷺ.'"
2. **The Harvest of the Hereafter:**  
   *Sahih al-Bukhari (Hadith 6491) from Sahl ibn Sa'd (RA):*  
   "Regarding verse 42:20, the Prophet ﷺ said: 'If the world were worth to Allah the wing of a mosquito, He would not have given a disbeliever a drink of water from it.'"
---

## 8. Key Concepts
1. **Laysa Kamithlihi Shay' (Divine Incomparability):** The foundational principle of Tawhid that Allah shares zero likeness or physical resemblance with creation.
2. **Ash-Shura (Mutual Consultation):** The cooperative ethical practice of participatory decision-making in family, community, and governance.
3. **Harth al-Akhirah (The Eternal Harvest):** Investing one's life capital for eternal yields in Paradise rather than fleeting earthly returns.
4. **Iqamat ad-Din (Establishing the Faith):** The unified mandate of all prophets to implement pure monotheism and justice without sectarian fracture.
---

## 9. Tadabbur (Guided Contemplation)
* **What Harvest Are You Planting For? (42:20):** Allah uses the agrarian metaphor of *Harth* (harvest). If you plow, plant, and water exclusively for worldly fame, salary, and square footage, Allah will give you some of it—but your eternal account will be zero. But if your daily work, parenting, and worship are planted for the Hereafter, Allah multiplies your eternal harvest *and* provides for your worldly needs. Re-align your intention today.
* **The Power of Reconciliation (42:40):** Islam grants you the legal right to seek exact justice (*Jaza'u sayyi'atin sayyi'atun mithluha*). But it immediately offers the divine upgrade: *Faman 'afa wa aslaha fa-ajruhu 'alallah* ('Whoever pardons and reconciles, his reward is upon Allah Himself'). When Allah personally guarantees to reward your forgiveness, what could be greater? Pardon those who hurt you, and let the King of kings compensate you.
---

## 10. What This Means For Me
* I practice *Shura* in my home and professional life, consulting my spouse and colleagues rather than acting with autocratic pride.
* I plant for the harvest of the Hereafter (*Harth al-Akhirah*) by consecrating my career and talents for the sake of Allah (42:20).
* I choose forgiveness and reconciliation over endless grudge-bearing, trusting Allah for my reward (42:40).

---

## 11. One Practical Action
**Consult Your Family/Team on a Decision Today:**  
Revive the sunnah of *Shura* (42:38): Gather your family or team today, openly solicit their opinions on an upcoming decision without imposing your initial preference, and make them feel genuinely valued.

---

## 12. Reflection Questions
1. Am I running my household or organization with autocratic arrogance or through mutual *Shura* (42:38)?
2. Is the majority of my mental energy focused on *Harth ad-Dunya* (worldly gain) or *Harth al-Akhirah* (eternal harvest)?
3. Can I find it in my heart to pardon a personal offense today so that my reward is guaranteed by Allah (42:40)?

## 13. Closing Supplication
$$\text{اللَّهُمَّ يَا مَنْ لَيْسَ كَمِثْلِهِ شَيْءٌ وَهُوَ السَّمِيعُ الْبَصِيرُ ، اجْعَلْ حَرْثَنَا لِلْآخِرَةِ وَلَا تَجْعَلِ الدُّنْيَا أَكْبَرَ هَمِّنَا ، وَوَفِّقْنَا لِلشُّورَىٰ وَالْعَدْلِ وَالصَّفْحِ الْجَمِيلِ}$$  
*"O Allah, O One like Whom there is nothing, and He is the Hearing, the Seeing: Make our harvest for the Hereafter, and do not make the world our greatest concern. Grant us success in consultation, justice, and beautiful pardon. Amin."*
