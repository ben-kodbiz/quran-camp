# CHAPTER 104: SURAH AL-HUMAZAH (سُورَةُ الهمزة)
**Classification:** Makki  
**Order in Mushaf:** 104  
**Total Verses:** 9  
**Famous Names:** Surat al-Humazah, Waylun Likulli Humazah  

---

## 1. Why This Surah Matters
Surah Al-Humazah issues a catastrophic divine indictment against the toxic social habits of mockery, character assassination, and the psychological delusion of wealth. It begins with a declaration of ruin: 'Woe to every scorner and slanderer' (104:1)—those who assassinate reputations behind backs and sneer to people's faces while hoarding wealth and obsessively counting it (*'Addadah*), deluded into thinking that their bank balances make them immortal (*Akhladah*). The Surah shatters this vanity with *Al-Hutamah*—the Crushing Fire of Allah, kindled to penetrate the physical chest and incinerate the very spiritual seats of arrogance in vaulting, locked columns.

---

## 2. Surah Context
Revealed in early Makkah regarding wealthy pagan oligarchs like Al-Walid ibn al-Mughirah, Umayyah ibn Khalaf, and Al-Akhnas ibn Shariq, who mocked the Prophet ﷺ and impoverished early believers with sarcastic gestures and arrogance.

---

## 3. Central Theme
**The Condemnation of Backbiting, Slander, and Wealth-Obsession, and the Fiery Retribution of Al-Hutamah.**

---

## 4. Key Passages
### The Slanderer and the Hoarder (104:1–3)
$$\text{وَيْلٌ لِّكُلِّ هُمَزَةٍ لُّمَزَةٍ ۝ الَّذِي جَمَعَ مَالًا وَعَدَّدَهُ ۝ يَحْسَبُ أَنَّ مَالَهُ أَخْلَدَهُ}$$
*"Woe to every scorner and slanderer, Who collects wealth and [continuously] counts it. He thinks that his wealth will make him immortal."*

### The Crushing Fire Vaulting over Hearts (104:4–9)
$$\text{كَلَّا ۖ لَيُنبَذَنَّ فِي الْحُطَمَةِ ۝ وَمَا أَدْرَاكَ مَا الْحُطَمَةُ ۝ نَارُ اللَّهِ الْمُوقَدَةُ ۝ الَّتِي تَطَّلِعُ عَلَى الْأَفْئِدَةِ ۝ إِنَّهَا عَلَيْهِم مُّؤْصَدَةٌ ۝ فِي عَمَدٍ مُّمَدَّدَةٍ}$$
*"No! He will surely be thrown into the Crusher. And what can make you know what is the Crusher? It is the fire of Allah, [eternally] fueled, Which mounts directed at the hearts. Indeed, Hellfire will be closed over them, In columns extended."*

---

## 5. Translation Highlights (The Clear Quran)
* *104:1:* 'Woe to every backbiter and slanderer (*Humazatin Lumazah*).'
* *104:3:* 'Thinking that his wealth will make him live forever (*Akhladah*).'
* *104:6:* 'It is the kindled Fire of Allah (*Narullahil Muqadah*).'
* *104:7:* 'Which mounts up over the hearts (*Tattali'u 'alal af'idah*).'

---

## 6. What Classical Tafsir Explains
* **The Difference Between *Humazah* and *Lumazah* (Verse 1):** Ibn 'Abbas and Qatadah explain: *Humazah* refers to the one who slanders and bites the honor of people in their absence (backbiting), while *Lumazah* refers to the one who insults, mimics, and defames people to their face with mocking winks, sneers, and cynical gestures.
* **Why the Fire Attacks the Hearts (*Al-Af'idah*) (Verse 7):** Imam Al-Qurtubi and As-Sa'di explain that unlike physical fire that burns the outer flesh, *Al-Hutamah* reaches the delicate spiritual core—the heart—because the heart was the source of malice, arrogance, greed, and contempt for fellow human beings.
* **The Vaulted Columns (*'Amadin Mumaddadah*) (Verse 9):** Ibn Kathir notes that the gates of Hell will be locked and barred with massive, extended iron pillars so that no escape or breath of relief is possible, sealing the arrogant in their eternal suffocating prison.
---

## 7. Important Supporting Hadiths
1. **The Punishment for Backbiting:**  
   *Sunan Abi Dawud (Hadith 4878), Sahih:*  
   "The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: 'During my Ascension (*Mi'raj*), I passed by people who had copper claws with which they were scratching their faces and chests. I asked: Who are these, O Jibril? He said: These are the ones who consumed the flesh of people and slandered their honor.'"
2. **Defining Backbiting:**  
   *Sahih Muslim (Hadith 2589) from Abu Hurayrah (RA):*  
   "The Prophet ﷺ said: 'Do you know what backbiting is?' They said: 'Allah and His Messenger know best.' He said: 'Mentioning your brother with what he dislikes.' It was said: 'What if it is true?' He said: 'If it is true, you have backbitten him; and if it is not true, you have slandered him.'"
---

## 8. Key Concepts
1. **Humazah & Lumazah (Mockers & Slanderers):** Those who destroy reputations through gossip, sarcastic memes, and covert gestures.
2. **Ghurur al-Mal (Delusion of Wealth):** The subconscious belief that wealth protects against death and moral accountability.
3. **Al-Hutamah (The Crusher):** The specialized Hellfire that smashes arrogant bones and reaches the spiritual heart.
4. **Tattali'u 'alal Af'idah (Mounting over Hearts):** Divine poetic justice punishing the heart where pride and malice festered.
---

## 9. Tadabbur (Guided Contemplation)
* **The Tongue in the Digital Era:** Contemplate: How easy is it today to be a *Humazah* or *Lumazah* online—leaving snarky comments, retweeting gossip, mocking looks, and celebrating people's failures?
* **The Illusion of Immortality:** Examine your relationship with money: Are you hoarding savings while neglecting charity because you subconsciously believe wealth will insulate you from vulnerability and death?
* **Cleansing the Heart Before the Fire Does:** If the fire targets the heart because of arrogance and envy, start purifying your heart now through sincere *Istighfar* and wishing good for those you envied.
---

## 10. What This Means For Me
* My speech is a life-or-death spiritual issue; mocking another human being can drag me into *Al-Hutamah*.
* My bank balance is a temporary utility, not my savior; it cannot postpone my death by a single microsecond.
* Whenever someone's name is slandered in my presence, I must defend their honor or immediately walk away.

---

## 11. One Practical Action
**The Speech and Honor Shield:**  
For the next 7 days, impose a total freeze on any derogatory comments about absent individuals. If you catch yourself slipping into gossip or mocking someone, immediately perform two rak'ahs of repentance, make du'a for that person's forgiveness, and give a hidden charity.

1. What circles of friends or group chats tempt you to engage in sarcasm and slander (*Humazah/Lumazah*)?
2. How does the phrase 'he thinks his wealth will make him live forever' describe modern consumerist attitudes?
3. What steps will you take today to ensure your heart is pure of contempt toward others?

## 13. Closing Supplication
$$\text{اللَّهُمَّ طَهِّرْ أَلْسِنَتَنَا مِنَ الْغِيبَةِ وَالنَّمِيمَةِ وَطَهِّرْ قُلُوبَنَا مِنَ الْكِبْرِ وَالْغِلِّ وَالْحَسَدِ}$$  
*"O Allah, purify our tongues from backbiting and slander, and purify our hearts from arrogance, malice, and envy."*
