# HOW TO USE THIS 30-DAY JOURNAL

Welcome to your thirty-day journey back to the Book of Allah.

This journal is designed to be your private spiritual sanctuary. It requires only **10 to 15 quiet minutes each day**:
1. **Read the Daily Passage:** Recite the Arabic text slowly, then read the English translation with full mental presence.
2. **Internalize the Daily Reflection:** Read the commentary, letting its diagnostic insights examine your current life situation.
3. **Ponder the Tadabbur Prompt:** Write your honest, unedited answers in your physical or digital notebook. Do not write what you think you 'should' say; write the truth of your heart.
4. **Execute the Practical Action:** Every day concludes with one specific, measurable behavioral step. Faith is proven through action.
5. **Seal with the Closing Du'a:** Raise your hands and make the supplication with deep certainty (*Yaqeen*).
6. **Mark Your Habit Tracker:** Check off your morning/evening boxes to build momentum.

The seeds of your relief are being planted inside the very soil of your trial right now, even before the hardship concludes.
