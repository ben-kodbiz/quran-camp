# CHAPTER 100: SURAH AL-'ADIYAT (سُورَةُ العاديات)
**Classification:** Makki  
**Order in Mushaf:** 100  
**Total Verses:** 11  
**Famous Names:** Surat al-'Adiyat, Wal-'Adiyat  

---

## 1. Why This Surah Matters
Surah Al-'Adiyat is a dramatic poetic masterpiece that jolts human conscience through the metaphor of war horses. By the panting steeds charging through the dawn, striking sparks with their hooves, kicking up clouds of dust, and penetrating the heart of enemy lines—all in absolute, selfless obedience to their human master who merely feeds them grass—Allah exposes the tragic irony of human ingratitude: 'Indeed, mankind, to his Lord, is ungrateful' (*Lakanud*) (100:6). The animal charges into deadly spears for a mouthful of oats, while the human rebel, surrounded by oceans of divine blessings, denies and forgets his Creator. The Surah concludes with the unearthing of graves and the laying bare of all hidden secrets of the chest.

---

## 2. Surah Context
Revealed in early Makkah to shatter the romanticization of Bedouin cavalry warfare and redirect the warrior's admiration of loyalty toward supreme devotion to Allah.

---

## 3. Central Theme
**The Selfless Loyalty of War Horses Shaming Human Ingratitude, the Compulsive Love of Wealth, and the Exposure of Secrets of the Heart.**

---

## 4. Key Passages
### The Oath of the Charging Steeds (100:1–5)
$$\text{وَالْعَادِيَاتِ ضَبْحًا ۝ فَالْمُورِيَاتِ قَدْحًا ۝ فَالْمُغِيرَاتِ صُبْحًا ۝ فَأَثَرْنَ بِهِ نَقْعًا ۝ فَوَسَطْنَ بِهِ جَمْعًا}$$
*"By the racers, panting, And the producers of sparks [when] striking, And the chargers at dawn, Stirring up thereby [clouds of] dust, Arriving thereby in the center collectively..."*

### The Diagnosis of Human Ingratitude (100:6–8)
$$\text{إِنَّ الْإِنسَانَ لِرَبِّهِ لَكَنُودٌ ۝ وَإِنَّهُ عَلَىٰ ذَٰلِكَ لَشَهِيدٌ ۝ وَإِنَّهُ لِحُبِّ الْخَيْرِ لَشَدِيدٌ}$$
*"Indeed, mankind, to his Lord, is ungrateful. And indeed, he to that is a witness. And indeed, in love of wealth, he is intense."*

### The Exposure of the Graveyard and Chests (100:9–11)
$$\text{أَفَلَا يَعْلَمُ إِذَا بُعْثِرَ مَا فِي الْقُبُورِ ۝ وَحُصِّلَ مَا فِي الصُّدُورِ ۝ إِنَّ رَبَّهُم بِهِمْ يَوْمَئِذٍ لَّخَبِيرٌ}$$
*"Does he not know that when the contents of the graves are scattered, And that within the breasts is obtained, Indeed, their Lord with them, that Day, is [fully] Acquainted."*

---

## 5. Translation Highlights (The Clear Quran)
* *100:1:* 'By the charging steeds that pant (*Dabha*).'
* *100:6:* 'Indeed, human beings are ungrateful to their Lord (*Kanud*).'
* *100:8:* 'And he is fierce in his love of wealth (*Shadeed*).'
* *100:10:* 'And whatever is hidden in the chests is brought out (*Hussila ma fis-sudur*).'

---

## 6. What Classical Tafsir Explains
* **The Linguistic Meaning of *Kanud* (Verse 6):** Al-Hasan al-Basri explained: 'The *Kanud* is the one who counts his calamities and miseries, and forgets his blessings.' When 99 things go right and 1 thing goes wrong, the *Kanud* complains bitterly about the 1 and ignores the 99 gifts of Allah.
* **The Animal Shaming the Man (Verses 1–5):** Ibn Kathir notes that Allah swears by these war horses because they demonstrate total loyalty to their mortal masters, risking their lives in battle without hesitation. How shameful, then, that human beings, blessed with reason and sustained by the Creator, rebel against Him for the sake of petty worldly desires.
* **Collecting the Secrets of the Chests (Verse 10):** As-Sa'di writes that *Hussila* means to extract, separate, and lay open what was concealed in the heart—hypocrisy, envy, greed, faith, and love. On that day, outer pretenses disappear and inner spiritual states become visible reality.
---

## 7. Important Supporting Hadiths
1. **The Insatiable Craving for Wealth:**  
   *Sahih al-Bukhari (Hadith 6439) & Sahih Muslim (Hadith 1048) from Anas ibn Malik (RA):*  
   "The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: 'If the son of Adam had a valley of gold, he would desire to have two valleys. Nothing can fill his mouth except dust, and Allah turns in forgiveness to whoever repents.'"
2. **The Blessings of Horses:**  
   *Sahih al-Bukhari (Hadith 2849) from 'Urwah al-Bariqi (RA):*  
   "The Prophet ﷺ said: 'Goodness is tied to the forelocks of horses until the Day of Resurrection: reward and spoils of war.'"
---

## 8. Key Concepts
1. **Al-'Adiyat (The Charging Steeds):** Metaphor for supreme discipline, loyalty, and courage in devotion.
2. **Al-Kanud (The Grumbling Ingrate):** The person who fixates on grief and counts hardships while ignoring oceans of grace.
3. **Hubb al-Khayr (Intense Love of Wealth):** The compulsive attachment to material accumulation that blinds the heart.
4. **Tahsil as-Sudur (The Unveiling of Chests):** The extraction and public exposure of all inner motives on Judgment Day.
---

## 9. Tadabbur (Guided Contemplation)
* **Are You a 'Kanud'?:** Reflect on your speech over the past week: Did you talk more about your aches, traffic, financial stress, and complaints, or about Allah's endless gifts of breath, sight, safety, and guidance?
* **The Horse's Loyalty vs. Your Loyalty:** Consider how devoted a trained animal is to a rider who feeds it fodder. How devoted are you to the King of Kings who gave you your beating heart?
* **Examining What's in Your Chest:** Imagine everything inside your heart right now projected onto a massive screen for everyone to see. What jealousies, resentments, or greed must you purge before Allah extracts it on the Last Day?
---

## 10. What This Means For Me
* Gratitude is not a passive feeling; it is active loyalty to Allah when worldly desires urge me to compromise.
* I must consciously train myself to stop counting problems and start thanking Allah for unseen protections.
* My bank balance will stay behind in the dust; only the purity of my heart (*Salim*) will benefit me when graves are upturned.

---

## 11. One Practical Action
**The 24-Hour No-Complaint Challenge:**  
Commit to a strict 24-hour fast from all grumbling, complaining, or venting about your life circumstances. Every time you are tempted to complain about traffic, weather, food, or work, immediately replace it with: 'Alhamdulillah 'ala kulli hal' (Praise be to Allah in all conditions).

---

## 12. Reflection Questions
1. In what areas of your life are you acting like a *Kanud*, focusing on the few things you lack rather than the thousands you have?
2. How does your love for wealth and financial security affect your willingness to give charity and sacrifice your time for Allah?
3. What hidden intention inside your heart needs deep cleansing before the Day when all secrets are made public?

## 13. Closing Supplication
$$\text{اللَّهُمَّ أَعِنِّي عَلَى ذِكْرِكَ وَشُكْرِكَ وَحُسْنِ عِبَادَتِكَ وَطَهِّرْ قَلْبِي مِنَ الْكُنُودِ}$$  
*"O Allah, help me to remember You, thank You, and worship You in the best manner, and purify my heart from ingratitude."*
