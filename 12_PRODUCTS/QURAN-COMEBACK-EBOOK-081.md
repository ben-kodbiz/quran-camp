# CHAPTER 81: SURAH AT-TAKWIR (سُورَةُ التكوير)
**Classification:** Makki  
**Order in Mushaf:** 81  
**Total Verses:** 29  
**Famous Names:** Surat at-Takwir, Idha ash-Shamsu Kuwwirat  

---

## 1. Why This Surah Matters
Surah At-Takwir is the ultimate cinematic experience of cosmic dismantling. The Prophet ﷺ said: 'Whoever wishes to look at the Day of Resurrection as if seeing it with their own eyes, let them recite *Idha ash-shamsu kuwwirat*.' Across fourteen vivid, rhythmic clauses, the universe unravels: the sun is folded up, stars fall like dim sparks, mountains are set in motion, ten-month pregnant camels are abandoned, wild beasts are herded together, and the seas are ignited into fire. In the center of this cataclysm sits the divine trial for the victim of female infanticide: 'And when the female infant buried alive is asked: For what sin was she killed?' (81:8–9). It concludes with the cosmic question: 'So where are you going?' (*Fa-ayna tadh-habun*).

---

## 2. Surah Context
Revealed in early Makkah to shake polytheist society out of its tribal complacency and banish the barbaric pre-Islamic practice of female infanticide (*Wa'd al-Banat*).

---

## 3. Central Theme
**The Cosmic Cataclysm of Judgment Day, the Vindication of the Oppressed Infant Girl, and the Divine Direction of Revelation.**

---

## 4. Key Passages
### The Twelve Cosmic Convulsions (81:1–6)
$$\text{إِذَا الشَّمْسُ كُوِّرَتْ ۝ وَإِذَا النُّجُومُ انكَدَرَتْ ۝ وَإِذَا الْجِبَالُ سُيِّرَتْ ۝ وَإِذَا الْعِشَارُ عُطِّلَتْ ۝ وَإِذَا الْوُحُوشُ حُشِرَتْ ۝ وَإِذَا الْبِحَارُ سُجِّرَتْ}$$
*"When the sun is wrapped up [in darkness], And when the stars fall, dispersing, And when the mountains are removed, And when full-term she-camels are neglected, And when the wild beasts are gathered, And when the seas are set on fire..."*

### The Trial for the Murdered Baby Girl (81:8–9)
$$\text{وَإِذَا الْمَوْءُودَةُ سُئِلَتْ ۝ بِأَيِّ ذَنبٍ قُتِلَتْ}$$
*"And when the girl [who was] buried alive is asked, For what sin she was killed."*

### Where Are You Going? (81:26–28)
$$\text{فَأَيْنَ تَذْهَبُونَ ۝ إِنْ هُوَ إِلَّا ذِكْرٌ لِّلْعَالَمِينَ ۝ لِمَن شَاءَ مِنكُمْ أَن يَسْتَقِيمَ}$$
*"So where are you going? It is not except a reminder to the worlds, For whoever wills among you to take a right course."*

---

## 5. Translation Highlights (The Clear Quran)
* *81:14:* 'A soul will know what it has brought [with it].'
* *81:19–21:* 'Indeed, it is a word of a noble messenger, Possessor of power with the Owner of the Throne, secure [there], Obeyed there and trustworthy.'
* *81:29:* 'And you do not will except that Allah wills—Lord of the worlds.'

---

## 6. What Classical Tafsir Explains
* **Why the Infant is Questioned Instead of the Killer (Verse 8):** Imam Ibn Kathir and Al-Qurtubi highlight a terrifying legal subtlety: Allah does not even address the barbaric father who buried his daughter alive in the sand; Allah directly questions the innocent infant girl: *Bi-ayyi dhanbin qutilat?* This is the ultimate judicial humiliation for the oppressor: the judge ignores the criminal and confers supreme standing upon the murdered victim, terrifying the killer before eternal damnation.
* **The Sun 'Wrapped Up' (*Kuwwirat*) (Verse 1):** Classical philologists note that *Takwir* means wrapping a turban around a head. At the end of time, the nuclear radiance and light of the sun will be wound up and stripped away, plunging the solar system into darkness.
---

## 7. Important Supporting Hadiths
1. **Seeing the Resurrection with Your Own Eyes:**  
   *Jami' at-Tirmidhi (Hadith 3333), Hasan:*  
   "The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: 'Whoever wishes to look upon the Day of Resurrection as if seeing it with his own eyes, let him recite: *Idha ash-shamsu kuwwirat* (81), *Idha as-sama'un-fatarat* (82), and *Idha as-sama'un-shaqqat* (84).'"
2. **The Prohibition of Burying Daughters Alive:**  
   *Sahih al-Bukhari (Hadith 5977) from Al-Mughirah ibn Shu'bah (RA):*  
   "The Prophet ﷺ said: 'Indeed, Allah has forbidden you from disobedience to mothers, burying daughters alive (*Wa'd al-Banat*), withholding charity, and demanding what is not yours.'"
---

## 8. Key Concepts
1. **At-Takwir (The Cosmic Wrapping):** The terminal collapsing and extinguishing of the sun's solar radiation.
2. **Al-Maw'udah (The Buried Female Infant):** The innocent victim of patriarchal misogyny elevated as the primary plaintiff in God's supreme court.
3. **'Attalat al-'Ishar (Neglect of Prized Assets):** The total abandonment of human wealth and prestige in the sheer panic of the final hour.
4. **Fa-Ayna Tadh-habun (Where Are You Wandering?):** The divine rhetorical wake-up call confronting human escapism and aimless digital distraction.
---

## 9. Tadabbur (Guided Contemplation)
* **Where Are You Going? (81:26):** Pause and listen to this three-word question: *Fa-ayna tadh-habun?* Where are you running to? What path are you walking? You rush from meeting to meeting, scroll from app to app, chase promotion after promotion—but what is your final destination? If your path does not lead to the pleasure of Allah, you are running toward a cliff. Pause, turn around, and return to the straight path.
* **Every Soul Will Know What It Brought (81:14):** When the sun goes dark, the mountains dissolve, and the oceans burn—in that absolute stillness, *'Alimat nafsun ma ahdarat* ('A soul will know what it has presented'). All the corporate achievements, diplomas, and compliments will evaporate. The only luggage you will have is what you packed in your heart. Pack light on sins, and pack heavy on prayers and mercy.
---

## 10. What This Means For Me
* I pause regularly to ask myself: *Fa-ayna tadh-habun?* (Where am I going with my life and habits?) (81:26).
* I stand up fiercely for the rights of vulnerable girls and children, remembering Allah's vindication of *Al-Maw'udah* (81:8–9).
* I recite Surah At-Takwir to visualize the reality of Judgment Day as taught by the Prophet ﷺ.

**Recite Surah At-Takwir with Presence Today:**  
Read Surah At-Takwir in its entirety today, closing your eyes to visualize the twelve cosmic convulsions and let the question of verse 26 recalibrate your daily compass.

3. How does knowing that Allah will champion the oppressed victim (81:8–9) shape my commitment to justice?

## 13. Closing Supplication

*"O Allah, grant us safety on the Day the sun is wrapped up and the stars disperse. O Allah, brighten our records when every soul knows what it has presented, and guide us to Your straight path. Amin."*
