# CHAPTER 91: SURAH ASH-SHAMS (سُورَةُ الشمس)
**Classification:** Makki  
**Order in Mushaf:** 91  
**Total Verses:** 15  
**Famous Names:** Surat ash-Shams, Wash-Shamsi wa Duhaha  

---

## 1. Why This Surah Matters
Surah Ash-Shams is renowned as the surah containing the longest consecutive series of divine oaths in the entire Holy Qur'an: eleven breathtaking cosmic oaths sworn by the Sun and its morning brightness, by the Moon as it reflects its light, by the Day as it displays it, by the Night as it covers it, by the Sky and its construction, by the Earth and its spreading, and by the human soul and the One who proportioned it! Following this immense celestial buildup, Allah delivers the single most critical verdict on human destiny: 'He has certainly succeeded who purifies it, and he has failed who corrupts it' (*Qad aflaha man zakkaha, wa qad khaba man dassaha*). It illustrates the fatal consequence of unchecked corruption through the ancient tribe of Thamud who slaughtered God's sacred She-Camel.

---

## 2. Surah Context
Revealed in early Makkah to establish the supreme, universal law of human psychological and spiritual evolution: ultimate success (*Falah*) is strictly tied to purifying the soul (*Tazkiyah*), while destruction is guaranteed for burying it in sin.

---

## 3. Central Theme
**Eleven Cosmic Oaths, the Supreme Law of Soul Purification (*Tazkiyah* vs. *Tadsiya*), and the Annihilation of Thamud.**

---

## 4. Key Passages
### The Eleven Cosmic Oaths (91:1–7)
$$\text{وَالشَّمْسِ وَضُحَاهَا ۝ وَالْقَمَرِ إِذَا تَلَاهَا ۝ وَالنَّهَارِ إِذَا جَلَّاهَا ۝ وَاللَّيْلِ إِذَا يَغْشَاهَا ۝ وَالسَّمَاءِ وَمَا بَنَاهَا ۝ وَالْأَرْضِ وَمَا طَحَاهَا ۝ وَنَفْسٍ وَمَا سَوَّاهَا}$$
*"By the sun and its brightness, And [by] the moon when it follows it, And [by] the day when it displays it, And [by] the night when it covers it, And [by] the sky and He who constructed it, And [by] the earth and He who spread it, And [by] the soul and He who proportioned it."*

### The Core Verdict of Human Destiny (91:8–10)
$$\text{فَأَلْهَمَهَا فُجُورَهَا وَتَقْوَاهَا ۝ قَدْ أَفْلَحَ مَن زَكَّاهَا ۝ وَقَدْ خَابَ مَن دَسَّاهَا}$$
*"And inspired it [with discernment of] its wickedness and its righteousness, He has succeeded who purifies it, And he has failed who instills it [with corruption]."*

### The Slaughter of the She-Camel (91:11–14)
$$\text{كَذَّبَتْ ثَمُودُ بِطَغْوَاهَا ۝ إِذِ انبَعَثَ أَشْقَاهَا ۝ فَقَالَ لَهُمْ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ نَاقَةَ اللَّهِ وَسُقْيَاهَا ۝ فَكَذَّبُوهُ فَعَقَرُوهَا فَدَمْدَمَ عَلَيْهِمْ رَبُّهُم بِذَنبِهِمْ فَسَوَّاهَا}$$
*"Thamud denied [their prophet] by reason of their transgression, When the most wretched of them was sent forth. And the messenger of Allah said to them, '[Do not harm] the she-camel of Allah or [prevent her from] her drink.' But they denied him and hamstrung her. So their Lord brought down upon them destruction for their sin and leveled them."*

## 5. Translation Highlights (The Clear Quran)
* *91:15:* 'And He does not fear the consequence thereof.'

---

## 6. What Classical Tafsir Explains
* **Why Eleven Oaths for One Verdict? (Verses 1–10):** Imam Ibn Kathir and Al-Qurtubi highlight that never in the entire Qur'an did Allah assemble eleven continuous cosmic oaths consecutively except here! The Sun, Moon, Day, Night, Heaven, Earth, and Soul are called as cosmic witnesses to one monumental truth: Your eternal success depends entirely on whether you purify (*Zakkaha*) or bury and corrupt (*Dassaha*) your soul.
* **The Meaning of 'Zakkaha' and 'Dassaha' (Verses 9–10):** Ibn Abbas (RA) stated: *Zakkaha* means cultivating, elevating, and purifying the soul through obedience to Allah and moral virtue; while *Dassaha* comes from *Dassa* (to bury beneath the dirt)—symbolizing the person who buries his conscience under lusts, sins, and moral corruption until it suffocates.
---

## 7. Important Supporting Hadiths
1. **The Prophetic Du'a for the Purification of the Soul:**  
   *Sahih Muslim (Hadith 2722) from Zayd ibn Arqam (RA):*  
   "The Prophet ﷺ used to supplicate: 'O Allah, grant my soul its piety and purify it; You are the best to purify it. You are its Guardian and its Master (*Allahumma ati nafsi taqwaha, wa zakkiha Anta khayru man zakkaha, Anta Waliyyuha wa Mawlaha*).'"
2. **Recitation of Surah Ash-Shams in 'Isha:**  
   *Sahih al-Bukhari (Hadith 705) from Jabir (RA):*  
   "When Mu'adh led his people and prolonged the prayer, the Prophet ﷺ advised him: 'Why did you not recite *Sabbihisma Rabbikal-A'la*, *Wash-Shamsi wa Duhaha*, and *Wal-Layli idha yaghsha*?'"
---

## 8. Key Concepts
1. **Tazkiyat an-Nafs (Soul Purification):** The intentional, ongoing spiritual discipline of weeding out pride, envy, and greed while planting faith and virtue.
2. **Tadsiya (Moral Suffocation):** The tragic self-sabotage of burying one's conscience and fitrah beneath accumulated sins.
3. **Naqatullah (The She-Camel of God):** The miraculous living sign given to Thamud, testing their reverence for sacred symbols.
4. **Ilham al-Fujur wat-Taqwa (Moral Discernment):** The innate intuitive compass hardwired into every human soul distinguishing good from evil.
---

## 9. Tadabbur (Guided Contemplation)
* **Are You Purifying or Burying Your Soul? (91:9–10):** Every morning when you wake up, you make a choice: Will you *Zakkaha* (nourish and elevate your soul with Fajr, truth, and kindness), or will you *Dassaha* (bury your soul under mindless scrolling, lustful glances, and angry words)? The universe has sworn eleven times: There is no third option. You are either elevating your soul to Paradise or burying it in ruin.
* **The Complicity of Silence (91:14):** Notice that only one wretched man (Qudar ibn Salif) physically struck the She-Camel: *Idh-inba'atha ashqaha*. But Allah destroyed the *entire* nation of Thamud: *Fa-damdama 'alayhim Rabbuhum bi-dhanbihim fa-sawwaha!* Why? Because the public cheered him on, stayed silent, and consented to the oppression. When evil occurs, remaining silent makes you a partner in the crime. Stand up for truth.
---

## 10. What This Means For Me
* I dedicate conscious daily effort to purifying my soul (*Tazkiyah*) through prayer, repentance, and good character (91:9).
* I memorize and recite the prophetic du'a: *Allahumma ati nafsi taqwaha wa zakkiha* every morning.
* I refuse to remain a silent, consenting spectator to wrongdoing in my family or community (91:14).

---

## 11. One Practical Action
**Memorize the Prophetic 'Tazkiyah' Du'a Today:**  
Recite: *Allahumma ati nafsi taqwaha, wa zakkiha Anta khayru man zakkaha, Anta Waliyyuha wa Mawlaha* three times today, begging Allah for the purification of your soul.

## 12. Reflection Questions
1. What specific habits in my daily life are purifying my soul (*Zakkaha*), and what habits are burying it (*Dassaha*) (91:9–10)?
2. How does the buildup of eleven cosmic oaths impress upon me the seriousness of soul purification?
3. Am I silently tolerating wrongdoing like the people of Thamud, risking communal blame (91:14)?

## 13. Closing Supplication
$$\text{اللَّهُمَّ آتِ نُفُوسَنَا تَقْوَاهَا ، وَزَكِّهَا أَنْتَ خَيْرُ مَنْ زَكَّاهَا ، أَنْتَ وَلِيُّهَا وَمَوْلَاهَا ، اللَّهُمَّ نَجِّنَا مِنْ خَيْبَةِ مَنْ دَسَّاهَا ، وَاجْعَلْنَا مِنَ الْمُفْلِحِينَ الَّذِينَ زَكَّوْهَا}$$  
*"O Allah, grant our souls their piety and purify them; You are the best to purify them. You are their Guardian and Master. O Allah, save us from the failure of those who bury their souls, and make us among the successful who purify them. Amin."*
