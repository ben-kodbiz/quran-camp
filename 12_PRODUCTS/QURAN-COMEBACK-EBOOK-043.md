# CHAPTER 43: SURAH AZ-ZUKHRUF (سُورَةُ الزخرف)
**Classification:** Makki  
**Order in Mushaf:** 43  
**Total Verses:** 89  
**Famous Names:** Surat az-Zukhruf  

---

## 1. Why This Surah Matters
Surah Az-Zukhruf takes direct aim at the dazzling superficiality of materialism, named after the glittering gold decorations (*Zukhruf*) that humanity obsesses over. It reveals that if worldly wealth possessed any true spiritual weight with Allah, He would have made the roofs and stairways of the disbelievers' mansions out of pure silver and solid gold—yet all of it is merely fleeting worldly ornamentation (*Wa in kullu dhalika lamma mata'ul-hayatid-dunya*). It teaches the beautiful travel du'a when mounting vehicles (43:13–14), exposes the fatal trap of blind tradition ('We found our forefathers upon a path'), and warns that all worldly friendships built on sin will transform into venomous enmity on Judgment Day—except the friendships forged in Taqwa (43:67).

---

## 2. Surah Context
Revealed in Makkah when pagan aristocrats scoffed at the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ: 'Why was this Qur'an not revealed to a wealthy, influential tycoon from Makkah or Ta'if?' (43:31). Surah Az-Zukhruf demolished their metric of human value, showing that Allah distributes material wealth to the righteous and wicked alike, but reserves true honor for faith.

---

## 3. Central Theme
**The Worthlessness of Material Glamour (*Az-Zukhruf*), the Peril of Blind Conformity, and Eternal Friendships Grounded in Piety.**

---

## 4. Key Passages
### The Travel Supplication (43:13–14)
$$\text{لِتَسْتَوُوا عَلَىٰ ظُهُورِهِ ثُمَّ تَذْكُرُوا نِعْمَةَ رَبِّكُمْ إِذَا اسْتَوَيْتُمْ عَلَيْهِ وَتَقُولُوا سُبْحَانَ الَّذِي سَخَّرَ لَنَا هَٰذَا وَمَا كُنَّا لَهُ مُقْرِنِينَ ۝ وَإِنَّا إِلَىٰ رَبِّنَا لَمُنقَلِبُونَ}$$
*"That you may settle yourselves upon their backs and then remember the favor of your Lord when you have settled upon them and say, 'Exalted is He who has subjected this to us, and we could not have [otherwise] subdued it. And indeed, to our Lord we will return.'"*

### The Illusion of Gold and Silver (43:33–35)
$$\text{وَلَوْلَا أَن يَكُونَ النَّاسُ أُمَّةً وَاحِدَةً لَّجَعَلْنَا لِمَن يَكْفُرُ بِالرَّحْمَٰنِ لِبُيُوتِهِمْ سُقُفًا مِّن فِضَّةٍ وَمَعَارِجَ عَلَيْهَا يَظْهَرُونَ... وَزُخْرُفًا ۚ وَإِن كُلُّ ذَٰلِكَ لَمَّا مَتَاعُ الْحَيَاةِ الدُّنْيَا ۚ وَالْآخِرَةُ عِندَ رَبِّكَ لِلْمُتَّقِينَ}$$
*"And if it were not that the people would become one [united] community [of disbelievers], We would have made for those who disbelieve in the Most Merciful—for their houses roofs of silver and stairways upon which they mount... And gold ornament. But all that is nothing but the enjoyment of worldly life. And the Hereafter with your Lord is for the righteous."*

### Eternal Friendship in Taqwa (43:67)
$$\text{الْأَخِلَّاءُ يَوْمَئِذٍ بَعْضُهُمْ لِبَعْضٍ عَدُوٌّ إِلَّا الْمُتَّقِينَ}$$
*"Close friends, that Day, will be enemies to each other, except for the righteous."*

---

## 5. Translation Highlights (The Clear Quran)
* *43:32:* 'Do they distribute the mercy of your Lord? It is We who have apportioned among them their livelihood in the life of this world and have raised some of them above others in degrees [of rank] that they may make use of one another for service. But the mercy of your Lord is better than whatever they accumulate.'
* *43:36:* 'And whoever turns away from the remembrance of the Most Merciful—We appoint for him a devil, and he is to him a companion.'
* *43:89:* 'So turn away from them and say, "Peace." But they are going to know.'

---

## 6. What Classical Tafsir Explains
* **The Apportionment of Mercy (Verse 32):** Imam Ibn Kathir highlights that human beings do not get to dictate who receives spiritual blessings or prophetic missions. Allah distributes brains, looks, and wealth unequally in this world purely to create an interdependent social economy where humans serve each other; but eternal salvation is accessible to every humble servant equally.
* **Friendships Becoming Enmities (Verse 67):** Al-Qurtubi and Ali ibn Abi Talib (RA) explained: Any friendship founded on worldly fun, shared sins, mutual gossiping, or toxic vanity will curdle into violent hatred on Judgment Day, where each friend curses the other for leading them to Hell. Only friendships rooted in Taqwa—reminding each other of prayer, charity, and truth—will endure as eternal brotherhood in Jannah.
---

## 7. Important Supporting Hadiths
1. **The Sunnah of the Travel Du'a:**  
   *Sahih Muslim (Hadith 1342) from Ibn Umar (RA):*  
   "When the Messenger of Allah ﷺ mounted his camel to set out on a journey, he would say *Allahu Akbar* three times and then recite: *'Subhana alladhi sakh-khara lana hadha wa ma kunna lahu muqrinin, wa inna ila Rabbina lamunqalibun'* (43:13–14)."
2. **The Parable of the Good Companion:**  
   *Sahih al-Bukhari (Hadith 5534) from Abu Musa al-Ash'ari (RA):*  
   "Regarding the friends of Taqwa (43:67), the Prophet ﷺ said: 'The example of a righteous companion and an evil companion is like that of a seller of musk and a blacksmith's bellows...'"
---

## 8. Key Concepts
1. **Az-Zukhruf (Superficial Ornaments):** The dazzling material glitter that blinds human hearts to spiritual and moral reality.
2. **Du'a as-Safar (The Travel Litany):** The transformative prayer connecting modern transportation (cars, planes, trains) directly to ultimate return to God.
3. **Akhilla' at-Taqwa (Companions of Piety):** The only friendships that survive the Day of Judgment and blossom into eternal paradise.
4. **Qarin ash-Shaytan (The Demonic Companion):** The spiritual blindness that occurs when a person habitually turns away from divine remembrance.
---

## 9. Tadabbur (Guided Contemplation)
* **Every Car Ride is a Journey to the Grave (43:13–14):** Look at the profound pairing in the travel du'a: You step into your luxury vehicle or airplane, and you say: *Subhanalladhi sakh-khara lana hadha* (Exalted is He who subjected this to us). But you don't stop there. You immediately recite: *Wa inna ila Rabbina lamunqalibun* ('And indeed, to our Lord we will return'). Every physical journey on earth is a subconscious rehearsal of your final journey to the grave. Never start your engine without remembering your destination.
* **Audit Your Friend Circle (43:67):** Look at your best friends right now: the group chats, the weekend hangouts, the business partners. On Judgment Day, will they curse you for pulling them into gossip and missed prayers, or will they take your hand and walk with you into Paradise? *Al-Akhilla'u yawma'idhin ba'duhum li-ba'din 'aduwwun illa al-muttaqin.* Keep friends who love your Akhirah.
---

## 10. What This Means For Me
* I recite the travel supplication (43:13–14) every time I mount my vehicle, board an airplane, or travel.
* I refuse to measure human dignity by jewelry, car models, or zip codes, remembering that all gold is mere *Mata'* (43:35).
* I actively cultivate and cherish friendships rooted in Taqwa, knowing that only holy friendships endure into eternity (43:67).

---

## 11. One Practical Action
**Recite the Travel Du'a on Your Next Commute:**  
Next time you step into your car or public transit today, pause before driving and recite: *Subhana alladhi sakh-khara lana hadha wa ma kunna lahu muqrinin, wa inna ila Rabbina lamunqalibun* (43:13–14) with conscious reflection.

---

## 12. Reflection Questions
1. Do I judge people's value by their material 'Zukhruf' (wealth, fashion, status) or by their character (43:33–35)?
2. Will my current closest friends be my enemies or my companions on the Day of Judgment (43:67)?
3. Am I living with the awareness that my life is a journey steadily returning to my Lord (43:14)?

## 13. Closing Supplication
$$\text{سُبْحَانَ الَّذِي سَخَّرَ لَنَا هَٰذَا وَمَا كُنَّا لَهُ مُقْرِنِينَ وَإِنَّا إِلَىٰ رَبِّنَا لَمُنْقَلِبُونَ ، اللَّهُمَّ ارْزُقْنَا صُحْبَةَ الْمُتَّقِينَ ، وَلَا تَجْعَلْنَا مِمَّنْ يَغْفُلُ عَنْ ذِكْرِ الرَّحْمَٰنِ}$$  
*"Exalted is He who has subjected this to us, and we could not have subdued it; and indeed, to our Lord we will return. O Allah, bless us with the companionship of the righteous, and do not make us among those who neglect the remembrance of the Most Merciful. Amin."*
