# CHAPTER 47: SURAH MUHAMMAD (سُورَةُ محمد)
**Classification:** Madani  
**Order in Mushaf:** 47  
**Total Verses:** 38  
**Famous Names:** Surat Muhammad, Surat al-Qital (The Chapter of Fighting)  

---

## 1. Why This Surah Matters
Surah Muhammad is named after the greatest human being to walk the earth, Muhammad ﷺ, establishing that true faith is inextricably bound to following what was revealed to him (*Wa amanu bima nuzzila 'ala Muhammadin wa huwal-haqqu mir-Rabbihim*). It reveals the decisive rule of spiritual physics: for those who disbelieve and oppose truth, Allah renders all their good deeds completely null and void (*Adalla a'malahum*); while for those who believe and do good, Allah expiates their misdeeds and rectifies their entire state and mind (*Wa aslaha balahum*). It offers an intoxicating panoramic vision of the four rivers of Paradise (pure water, fresh milk, exquisite wine, and clarified honey) and ends with the piercing warning: 'Here you are—those invited to spend in the cause of Allah... and if you turn away, He will replace you with another people; then they will not be the likes of you' (47:38).

---

## 2. Surah Context
Revealed in Madinah around 1 AH shortly after the Hijrah, when the newly established Muslim community faced existential military threats from the Quraysh. It legislated rules of warfare, prisoner management, and warned against hypocrites whose hearts were diseased.

---

## 3. Central Theme
**The Absolute Authority of Prophetic Revelation, the Contrast Between Valid and Wasted Deeds, the Rivers of Paradise, and the Peril of Being Divinely Replaced.**

---

## 4. Key Passages
### Rectifying the State of Mind (47:2)
$$\text{وَالَّذِينَ آمَنُوا وَعَمِلُوا الصَّالِحَاتِ وَآمَنُوا بِمَا نُزِّلَ عَلَىٰ مُحَمَّدٍ وَهُوَ الْحَقُّ مِن رَّبِّهِمْ ۙ كَفَّرَ عَنْهُمْ سَيِّئَاتِهِمْ وَأَصْلَحَ بَالَهُمْ}$$
*"And those who believe and do righteous deeds and believe in what has been sent down upon Muhammad—and it is the truth from their Lord—He will remove from them their misdeeds and rectify their condition [and state of mind]."*

### The Four Rivers of Jannah (47:15)
$$\text{مَّثَلُ الْجَنَّةِ الَّتِي وُعِدَ الْمُتَّقُونَ ۖ فِيهَا أَنْهَارٌ مِّن مَّاءٍ غَيْرِ آسِنٍ وَأَنْهَارٌ مِّن لَّبَنٍ لَّمْ يَتَغَيَّرْ طَعْمُهُ وَأَنْهَارٌ مِّنْ خَمْرٍ لَّذَّةٍ لِّلشَّارِبِينَ وَأَنْهَارٌ مِّنْ عَسَلٍ مُّصَفًّى}$$
*"The description of Paradise promised to the righteous: wherein are rivers of water unaltered, rivers of milk the taste of which never changes, rivers of wine delicious to those who drink, and rivers of clarified honey..."*

### The Locks Upon the Hearts (47:24)
$$\text{أَفَلَا يَتَدَبَّرُونَ الْقُرْآنَ أَمْ عَلَىٰ قُلُوبٍ أَقْفَالُهَا}$$
*"Then do they not reflect upon the Quran, or are there locks upon [their] hearts?"*

### The Warning of Divine Replacement (47:38)
$$\text{هَا أَنتُمْ هَٰؤُلَاءِ تُدْعَوْنَ لِتُنفِقُوا فِي سَبِيلِ اللَّهِ فَمِنكُم مَّن يَبْخَلُ ۖ وَمَن يَبْخَلْ فَإِنَّمَا يَبْخَلُ عَن نَّفْسِهِ ۚ وَاللَّهُ الْغَنِيُّ وَأَنتُمُ الْفُقَرَاءُ ۚ وَإِن تَتَوَلَّوْا يَسْتَبْدِلْ قَوْمًا غَيْرَكُمْ ثُمَّ لَا يَكُونُوا أَمْثَالَكُم}$$
*"Here you are—those invited to spend in the cause of Allah—but among you are those who withhold [out of greed]. And whoever withholds only withholds from himself; and Allah is the Free of need, while you are the needy. And if you turn away, He will replace you with another people; then they will not be the likes of you."*

## 5. Translation Highlights (The Clear Quran)
* *47:7:* 'O you who have believed, if you support Allah, He will support you and plant firmly your feet.'
* *47:19:* 'So know, [O Muhammad], that there is no deity except Allah and ask forgiveness for your sin and for the believing men and believing women...'
* *47:33:* 'O you who have believed, obey Allah and obey the Messenger and do not render your deeds void.'

---

## 6. What Classical Tafsir Explains
* **What Does 'Wa Aslaha Balahum' Mean? (Verse 2):** Ibn Abbas (RA) and Imam Ibn Kathir explain that *Aslaha Balahum* encompasses rectifying their inner psyche, calming their mental anxieties, purifying their worldly affairs, granting them peace of mind, and perfecting their spiritual state. True peace of mind is an exclusive gift of living by revelation.
* **The Danger of Divine Replacement (Verse 38):** When verse 38 was revealed, the Companions asked: 'O Messenger of Allah, who are those who will replace us if we turn away?' The Prophet ﷺ tapped Salman al-Farsi on the shoulder and said: 'This man and his people (the Persians); by the One in whose Hand is my soul, if faith were suspended at the Pleiades (*Ath-Thurayya*), men from Persia would attain it.'
---

## 7. Important Supporting Hadiths
1. **Knowledge Precedes Speech and Action:**  
   *Sahih al-Bukhari (Chapter: Knowledge Before Word and Deed):*  
   "Imam al-Bukhari established an entire famous chapter based on verse 47:19 (*Fa'lam annahu la ilaha illallah*), stating: 'Knowledge must precede both speech and action, for Allah began with: *"So know that there is no deity except Allah"* before commanding *"and ask forgiveness"*.'"
2. **The Four Rivers of Jannah:**  
   *Jami' at-Tirmidhi (Hadith 2571), Sahih:*  
   "Mu'awiyah ibn Haydah reported that the Prophet ﷺ said: 'In Paradise there is an ocean of water, an ocean of honey, an ocean of milk, and an ocean of wine; then the rivers branch off from them.'"
---

## 8. Key Concepts
1. **Islah al-Bal (Psychological Rectification):** The profound state of inner peace, mental calm, and spiritual tranquility bestowed upon sincere believers.
2. **In Tansurullaha Yansurkum (The Mutual Support Pact):** The divine rule that standing up for God's religion guarantees divine empowerment and steadfastness.
3. **Aqfal al-Qulub (The Locked Hearts):** The spiritual disease where stubborn sin and hypocrisy seal the heart against the healing light of the Qur'an.
4. **Sunan al-Istibdal (The Law of Replacement):** The warning that Allah's religion does not depend on any specific ethnic group; if people turn away, Allah replaces them with passionate believers.
---

## 9. Tadabbur (Guided Contemplation)
* **Are There Locks on Your Heart? (47:24):** Look at the haunting question: *Afala yatadabbaruna al-Qur'ana am 'ala qulubin aqfaluha?* Notice the plural: *Aqfaluha* (its locks). A heart does not get locked by one sin; it gets locked by a combination of arrogance, greed, envy, neglect of prayer, and consuming unlawful earnings. When the locks click shut, the words of the Qur'an bounce off without penetrating. Ask Allah to smash the locks on your heart through sincere repentance and tearful Tadabbur.
* **Islam Does Not Need You; You Need Islam (47:38):** Never think you are doing Allah a favor by praying, donating, or defending His faith: *Wallahul-Ghaniyyu wa antumul-fuqara'* ('Allah is the Rich, and you are the poor'). If you grow arrogant, stingy, or negligent, Allah will effortlessly replace you with a generation that loves Him and whom He loves. Serve His cause with humble gratitude that He chose you.

## 10. What This Means For Me
* I support the religion of Allah through my time, wealth, and voice, knowing He will firmly plant my feet (47:7).
* I consistently pray for mental clarity and peace of mind (*Aslaha Balahum*) by aligning my life with the teachings of Muhammad ﷺ.
* I recite *La ilaha illallah* and ask forgiveness daily for myself and for all believing men and women (47:19).

## 13. Closing Supplication
$$\text{اللَّهُمَّ صَلِّ عَلَىٰ مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَىٰ آلِ مُحَمَّدٍ ، اللَّهُمَّ كَفِّرْ عَنَّا سَيِّئَاتِنَا وَأَصْلِحْ بَالَنَا ، اللَّهُمَّ انْصُرْنَا إِذَا نَصَرْنَا دِينَكَ وَثَبِّتْ أَقْدَامَنَا ، وَاغْفِرْ لَنَا وَلِلْمُؤْمِنِينَ وَالْمُؤْمِنَاتِ}$$  
*"O Allah, confer blessings upon Muhammad and the family of Muhammad. O Allah, expiate our sins and rectify our condition and state of mind. O Allah, grant us victory as we support Your religion, plant our feet firmly, and forgive us and all the believing men and believing women. Amin."*
