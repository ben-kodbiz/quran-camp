# CHAPTER 65: SURAH AT-TALAQ (سُورَةُ الطلاق)
**Classification:** Madani  
**Order in Mushaf:** 65  
**Total Verses:** 12  
**Famous Names:** Surat at-Talaq, Surat an-Nisa' as-Sughra (The Minor Chapter of Women)  

---

## 1. Why This Surah Matters
Surah At-Talaq is the divine fortress of legal dignity and emotional reassurance during life's most painful family transitions. Known classically as *Surat an-Nisa' as-Sughra*, it legislates the ethical conduct of divorce—commanding waiting periods (*'Iddah*), housing support, financial maintenance for nursing mothers, and absolute prohibition against vindictiveness or cruelty. Embedded within these precise family rulings sits one of the most famous and hopeful promises in the entire Holy Qur'an: 'And whoever fears Allah—He will make for him a way out and will provide for him from where he does not expect. And whoever relies upon Allah—then He is sufficient for him' (65:2–3). It reminds broken-hearted souls: 'Perhaps Allah will bring about after that a [new] matter' (*La tadri la'allallaha yuhdithu ba'da dhalika amra*).

---

## 2. Surah Context
Revealed in Madinah to reform pre-Islamic divorce practices where men manipulated waiting periods to trap and torment women. Surah At-Talaq established strict timelines, financial responsibilities, and infused family law with God-consciousness (*Taqwa*).

---

## 3. Central Theme
**Ethical and Dignified Family Transitions, God-Consciousness as the Key to Deliverance (*Al-Makhraj*), and Divine Sufficiency (*Hasbunallah*).**

---

## 4. Key Passages
### The Golden Promise of Deliverance and Provision (65:2–3)
$$\text{وَمَن يَتَّقِ اللَّهَ يَجْعَل لَّهُ مَخْرَجًا ۝ وَيَرْزُقْهُ مِنْ حَيْثُ لَا يَحْتَسِبُ ۚ وَمَن يَتَوَكَّلْ عَلَى اللَّهِ فَهُوَ حَسْبُهُ ۚ إِنَّ اللَّهَ بَالِغُ أَمْرِهِ ۚ قَدْ جَعَلَ اللَّهُ لِكُلِّ شَيْءٍ قَدْرًا}$$
*"And whoever fears Allah—He will make for him a way out And will provide for him from where he does not expect. And whoever relies upon Allah—then He is sufficient for him. Indeed, Allah will accomplish His purpose. Allah has already set for everything a [decreed] extent."*

### The Hope for Unexpected New Beginnings (65:1)
$$\text{لَا تَدْرِي لَعَلَّ اللَّهَ يُحْدِثُ بَعْدَ ذَٰلِكَ أَمْرًا}$$
*"You know not; perhaps Allah will bring about after that a [new] matter."*

### Ease After Hardship (65:7)
$$\text{لِيُنفِقْ ذُو سَعَةٍ مِّن سَعَتِهِ ۖ وَمَن قُدِرَ عَلَيْهِ رِزْقُهُ فَلْيُنفِقْ مِمَّا آتَاهُ اللَّهُ ۚ لَا يُكَلِّفُ اللَّهُ نَفْسًا إِلَّا مَا آتَاهَا ۚ سَيَجْعَلُ اللَّهُ بَعْدَ عُسْرٍ يُسْرًا}$$
*"Let a man of wealth spend from his wealth, and he whose provision is restricted—let him spend from what Allah has given him. Allah does not charge a soul except [according to] what He has given it. Allah will bring about, after hardship, ease."*

## 5. Translation Highlights (The Clear Quran)
* *65:4:* '...And whoever fears Allah—He will make for him of his matter ease.'
* *65:5:* '...And whoever fears Allah—He will remove for him his misdeeds and make great for him his reward.'
* *65:12:* 'It is Allah who has created seven heavens and of the earth, the like of them... that you may know that Allah is over all things competent...'

---

## 6. What Classical Tafsir Explains
* **Why is the Promise of 'Makhraj' Embedded in Divorce? (Verses 2–3):** Imam Ibn Kathir and Al-Qurtubi highlight a profound psychological wisdom: Divorce is often accompanied by financial panic ('How will I afford two households?'), emotional devastation, and social fear. In that exact moment of claustrophobic terror, Allah weaves the promise: *Whoever has Taqwa, He will make for him an exit (*Makhraj*) and provide from unexpected angles.* When worldly doors slam shut, Taqwa opens divine escape hatches.
* **The Meaning of 'Yuhdithu Ba'da Dhalika Amra' (Verse 1):** Scholars of Tafsir explain that this verse anchors human hope in times of heartbreak. The 'new matter' may be the miraculous rekindling of love between the estranged spouses during the waiting period, or it may be Allah granting both partners better, happier, and more peaceful marriages in the future.
---

## 7. Important Supporting Hadiths
1. **The Verse of Universal Sufficiency:**  
   *Musnad Ahmad (Hadith 21545) from Abu Dharr (RA):*  
   "The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: 'O Abu Dharr, indeed I know a verse which, if all mankind were to hold fast to it, it would suffice them entirely!' Then he recited: *'And whoever fears Allah—He will make for him a way out and provide for him from where he does not expect'* (65:2–3)."
2. **Ease Inevitably Follows Hardship:**  
   *Musnad Ahmad (Hadith 2803) from Ibn Abbas (RA):*  
   "The Prophet ﷺ said in his famous advice: 'And know that victory comes with patience, relief comes with distress, and with hardship comes ease (*Wa anna ma'al-'usri yusra*).'"
---

## 8. Key Concepts
1. **Al-Makhraj (The Divine Exit):** The miraculous way out provided by Allah to the God-fearing when all human pathways seem blocked.
2. **Rizqun la Yahtasib (Unexpected Sustenance):** Wealth, emotional peace, and resources arriving from avenues never calculated by human intellect.
3. **Yuhdithu Ba'da Dhalika Amra (The Unfolding Decree):** The comforting reality that Allah is constantly initiating new chapters of healing and relief.
4. **Sayaj'alullahu Ba'da 'Usrin Yusra (Guaranteed Relief):** The infallible spiritual law that hardship is always temporary and pregnant with imminent ease.
---

## 9. Tadabbur (Guided Contemplation)
* **When Every Exit is Blocked (65:2–3):** Have you ever felt so cornered by a crisis—a legal deadlock, a financial collapse, a marriage breakdown—that you could not see a single way forward? Surah At-Talaq hands you the master key: *Taqwa*. You do not need to figure out the solution; your only job is to obey Allah, avoid unlawful reactions, and maintain your integrity. Allah guarantees: *Yaj'al lahu makhraja* (He Himself will engineer the exit). And He will feed your soul from directions you never dreamed possible.
* **You Do Not Know What Tomorrow Brings (65:1):** When you are crying over an ending, repeat this phrase slowly: *La tadri la'allallaha yuhdithu ba'da dhalika amra* ('You do not know; perhaps Allah will bring about after this a brand new matter'). What feels like the end of your world is often the fertile soil where Allah plants your greatest rebirth. Trust His pen.
---

## 10. What This Means For Me
* Whenever I feel trapped in a financial, career, or emotional crisis, I anchor my heart in the promise of *Al-Makhraj* (65:2).
* I manage personal conflicts and separations with dignity, justice, and kindness, never stooping to vindictiveness.
* I hold absolute conviction that ease will inevitably follow my current hardship: *Sayaj'alullahu ba'da 'usrin yusra* (65:7).

---

## 11. One Practical Action
**Adopt the 'Tawakkul' Litany of 65:3:**  
Whenever anxiety about your future, career, or family strikes today, recite *Hasbiyallahu wa ni'mal-Wakil* and meditate on: *Wa man yatawakkal 'alallahi fahuwa hasbuh* ('And whoever relies upon Allah, He is sufficient for him').

---

## 12. Reflection Questions
1. Am I trying to solve my problems through unethical shortcuts, or am I maintaining *Taqwa* so Allah can grant me a *Makhraj* (65:2)?
2. How does the phrase *'Perhaps Allah will bring about after this a new matter'* (65:1) soothe my grief over recent endings?
3. Do I truly believe that Allah is sufficient (*Hasbuh*) for me when I place my trust in Him (65:3)?

## 13. Closing Supplication
$$\text{اللَّهُمَّ يَا مَنْ يَجْعَلُ لِمَنْ يَتَّقِيهِ مَخْرَجًا وَيَرْزُقُهُ مِنْ حَيْثُ لَا يَحْتَسِبُ ، اجْعَلْ لَنَا مِنْ كُلِّ هَمٍّ فَرَجًا ، وَمِنْ كُلِّ ضِيقٍ مَخْرَجًا ، وَاجْعَلْ بَعْدَ عُسْرِنَا يُسْرًا يَا رَبَّ الْعَالَمِينَ}$$  
*"O Allah, O One who makes for the God-fearing a way out and provides for them from where they do not expect: Make for us from every anxiety relief, and from every constriction an exit. Bring about after our hardship ease, O Lord of the worlds. Amin."*
