# CHAPTER 107: SURAH AL-MA'UN (سُورَةُ الماعون)
**Classification:** Makki  
**Order in Mushaf:** 107  
**Total Verses:** 7  
**Famous Names:** Surat al-Ma'un, Ara'aytal Ladhi  

---

## 1. Why This Surah Matters
Surah Al-Ma'un unmasks the ultimate pathology of religious hypocrisy: the fatal disconnect between ritual worship and human compassion. It opens with an arresting challenge: 'Have you seen the one who denies the Final Judgment?' (107:1). Astonishingly, the Qur'an does not define this denier by philosophical atheism, but by social cruelty: violently pushing away the orphan (*Yadu''ul yateem*) and failing to encourage the feeding of the impoverished (*La yahuddu 'ala ta'amil miskeen*). It issues a terrifying condemnation against praying people whose hearts are heedless, who perform ostentatious prayers to be seen by men (*Yura'un*), while refusing to lend even the smallest household utensil or basic kindness (*Al-Ma'un*).

---

## 2. Surah Context
Revealed in Makkah regarding tribal leaders like Abu Jahl or Al-'As ibn Wa'il, who claimed elite moral standing yet physically struck orphan children and refused charity, with later verses addressing hypocrites (*Munafiqoon*) in Madinah who prayed purely for social display.

---

## 3. Central Theme
**The Inseparable Bond between True Faith and Social Compassion, the Vanity of Ostentatious Prayer, and the Sin of Withholding Basic Kindnesses.**

---

## 4. Key Passages
### The Social Profile of the Religious Denier (107:1–3)
$$\text{أَرَأَيْتَ الَّذِي يُكَذِّبُ بِالدِّينِ ۝ فَذَٰلِكَ الَّذِي يَدُعُّ الْيَتِيمَ ۝ وَلَا يَحُضُّ عَلَىٰ طَعَامِ الْمِسْكِينِ}$$
*"Have you seen the one who denies the Recompense? For that is the one who drives away the orphan, And does not encourage the feeding of the poor."*

### The Woe of the Heedless and Ostentatious Worshiper (107:4–7)
$$\text{فَوَيْلٌ لِّلْمُصَلِّينَ ۝ الَّذِينَ هُمْ عَن صَلَاتِهِمْ سَاهُونَ ۝ الَّذِينَ هُمْ يُرَاءُونَ ۝ وَيَمْنَعُونَ الْمَاعُونَ}$$
*"So woe to those who pray, [But] who are heedless of their prayer—Those who make show [of their deeds], And withhold [simple] assistance."*

---

## 5. Translation Highlights (The Clear Quran)
* *107:1:* 'Have you seen the one who denies the Judgment (*Yukadh-dhibu bid-Deen*)?'
* *107:2:* 'That is the one who harshly pushes the orphan away (*Yadu''ul yateem*).'
* *107:4:* 'So woe to those who pray (*Fawaylun lil-musalleen*)!'
* *107:5:* 'Those who are heedless of their prayer (*'An salatihim sahun*).'
* *107:7:* 'And withhold basic small kindnesses (*Yamna'unal Ma'un*).'

---

## 6. What Classical Tafsir Explains
* **Heedless of Their Prayer (*'An Salatihim Sahun*) (Verse 5):** Imam Ibn Kathir notes that Allah said *'An Salatihim* (heedless *of* their prayer), not *Fee Salatihim* (in their prayer). Forgetting or getting distracted inside prayer is human; but being *heedless of* prayer means delaying it habitually until its time expires, abandoning its inner spirit, and praying mechanically without fear or contemplation.
* **What is *Al-Ma'un* (Verse 7):** The Companions, including 'Ali, Ibn Mas'ud, and Ibn 'Abbas (RA), explained that *Al-Ma'un* refers to everyday household necessities that people routinely share: a bucket of water, an axe, salt, a cooking pot, a needle, or a loan of small change. Refusing to lend a neighbor a cooking pot or salt reflects a spiritually frozen, stingy soul.
* **The Synthesis of Rights:** As-Sa'di writes that religion consists of fulfilling the rights of the Creator (sincere prayer devoid of *Riya'*) and fulfilling the rights of creation (compassion for orphans, feeding the poor, lending small utilities). The hypocrite fails on both fronts.
---

## 7. Important Supporting Hadiths
1. **Caretaker of the Orphan in Paradise:**  
   *Sahih al-Bukhari (Hadith 5304) from Sahl ibn Sa'd (RA):*  
   "The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said, holding his index and middle finger close together: 'I and the sponsor of an orphan will be in Paradise like this.'"
2. **Helping the Widow and the Needy:**  
   *Sahih al-Bukhari (Hadith 5353) & Sahih Muslim (Hadith 2982) from Abu Hurayrah (RA):*  
   "The Prophet ﷺ said: 'The one who looks after a widow or a poor person is like a mujahid striving in the cause of Allah, or like one who stands in prayer all night without tiring and fasts all day without breaking.'"
3. **The Danger of Showing Off (*Riya'*):**  
   *Musnad Ahmad (Hadith 23630), Sahih:*  
   "The Prophet ﷺ said: 'What I fear most for you is minor shirk.' They asked: 'What is minor shirk, O Messenger of Allah?' He said: 'Showing off (*Ar-Riya'*).'"
---

## 8. Key Concepts
1. **Takdhib bid-Deen (Denying Faith):** Exposed not just through disbelief in doctrine, but through cold cruelty toward the vulnerable.
2. **Riya' (Spiritual Ostentation):** Performing religious acts to win human applause, which voids divine reward.
3. **Sahw 'an as-Salah (Prayer Heedlessness):** Treating the sacred appointment of prayer with apathy, tardiness, and coldness.
4. **Al-Ma'un (Everyday Small Kindnesses):** Lending basic tools, cooking ingredients, and minor help to neighbors without resentment.
---

## 9. Tadabbur (Guided Contemplation)
* **Checking Your Prayer for Riya':** Do you prolong your recitation and bow lower when people are watching you in the mosque, but rush through your prayer in three minutes when alone in your room?
* **The Test of the Orphan and Beggar:** When a desperate beggar or poor person approaches you, what is your initial reflex: annoyance and irritation, or remembering that Allah is testing your claim to faith?
* **Lending Without Hesitation:** If a neighbor knocks on your door asking to borrow a ladder, a drill, or sugar, do you give it gladly with a smile, or do you invent excuses?
---

## 10. What This Means For Me
* My prayer is empty if it does not soften my heart toward the poor and make me generous with small kindnesses.
* I must protect my Salah from casual procrastination; delaying prayer out of laziness is a step toward the heedlessness warned in verse 5.
* Holiness is not aloof spirituality; true holiness is rolling up my sleeves to serve orphans and feed hungry people.

---

## 11. One Practical Action
**The Small Kindness Initiative:**  
Perform two concrete acts of *Ma'un* today: First, check on an elderly neighbor or friend and offer them practical help (e.g., carrying groceries, lending a tool, sharing food). Second, sponsor an orphan through a credible humanitarian agency or set up a recurring monthly donation for poor relief.

---

## 12. Reflection Questions
1. Why do you think Allah connects denying the Day of Judgment directly to mistreating orphans and ignoring the poor?
2. What small household items or tools do you hesitate to share with others out of selfishness?
3. How can you transform your daily prayers from mechanical rituals into transformative experiences that generate active mercy?

## 13. Closing Supplication
$$\text{اللَّهُمَّ إِنَّا نَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنَ الرِّيَاءِ وَالسُّمْعَةِ وَنَعُوذُ بِكَ أَنْ نَسْهُوَ عَنْ صَلَاتِنَا وَاجْعَلْنَا رُحَمَاءَ بِالْيَتَامَى وَالْمَسَاكِينِ}$$  
*"O Allah, we seek refuge in You from showing off and seeking fame, and we seek refuge in You from being heedless of our prayers, and make us merciful caretakers of orphans and the destitute."*
