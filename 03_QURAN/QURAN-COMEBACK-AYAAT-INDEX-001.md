## 2. Thematic Clustering Matrix

1. **The Foundations of Faith & Law (Juz 1–6):** Surahs 1–5 establishing Tawhid, divine covenant, moral responsibility, social justice, and communal ethics.
2. **Prophetic Narratives & Spiritual Fortitude (Juz 7–14):** Surahs 6–16 exploring the lineage of prophets, patience through hardship, reliance on divine decree, and cosmic signs in nature.
3. **Spiritual Purification & Moral Wisdom (Juz 15–20):** Surahs 17–27 covering the Night Journey, the cave sanctuary, maternal devotion (Maryam), patient worship (Ta-Ha), and ultimate devotion.
4. **Divine Majesty & The Cosmos (Juz 21–27):** Surahs 28–56 investigating the signs of Allah in the heavens, the wind, the sea, the stars, the trees, and the human heart.
5. **The Short Surahs of Awakening & Eternity (Juz 28–30):** Surahs 57–114 delivering rhythmic, piercing reminders of the brevity of earthly life, the inevitability of the Day of Judgment, and pure monotheism.
