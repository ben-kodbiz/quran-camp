## 56. Surah Al-Waqi'ah (الواقعة) — The Inevitable Event

- **Classification:** Makki (96 Verses)
- **Asbab Status:** Ayah-Level Incidents
- **Status Label:** Parity of the Foremost & Multitudes of Latter Believers
- **Reporting Sahabah:** Abu Hurairah, Abdullah ibn Abbas, Urwah ibn az-Zubayr

### Historical & Theological Context
Revealed in Mecca to deconstruct materialist nihilism by unveiling the tripartite division of humanity upon the Cataclysm: As-Sabiqun (The Foremost), As-hab al-Yameen (Companions of the Right), and As-hab ash-Shimal (Companions of the Left).

### Key Verified Historical Triggers & Canonical Sources

#### A. The Grieving Companions & The Parity of Believers (Verses 13–14 & 39–40)
- **Primary Source:** **Musnad Ahmad; Ibn Abi Hatim; Tafsir Ibn Kathir (8/3); Al-Wahidi, Asbab an-Nuzul, p. 280**
- **Narrator:** Abu Hurairah
- **Trigger:** When Ayah 13–14 descended: *"A multitude from the early generations, and a few from the latter,"* the companions wept bitterly, fearing that later generations would have virtually zero representation among the saved elite. Thereupon Allah revealed Ayahs 39–40 for the Companions of the Right: *"A multitude from the early generations, and a multitude from the latter generations,"* comforting the Ummah that vast hosts across history will inherit Paradise.

#### B. The Grand Oath by Astral Positions (Verses 75–77)
- **Primary Source:** **Sahih Muslim 73; Tafsir at-Tabari (23/135)**
- **Narrator:** Abdullah ibn Abbas
- **Trigger:** When shooting stars struck the sky and pagan Quraysh claimed rain was caused by lunar mansions (*Anwa'*), Allah swore by the cosmic descents of stars (*Mawaqi' an-Nujoom*) to affirm the noble transcendence of the preserved Quran against poetic hallucinations.

## 57. Surah Al-Hadid (الحديد) — The Iron

- **Classification:** Madani (29 Verses)
- **Asbab Status:** Ayah-Level Incidents
- **Status Label:** Heart-Humbling Admonition & The Goodly Loan
- **Reporting Sahabah:** Abdullah ibn Mas'ud, Anas ibn Malik, Abu ad-Darda

### Historical & Theological Context
The majestic opening of the Musabbihat (Surahs opening with divine glorification), integrating profound theology (Al-Awwal, Al-Akhir, Az-Zahir, Al-Batin) with the societal demand for financial sacrifice and industrial strength (Iron).

### Key Verified Historical Triggers & Canonical Sources

#### A. The Gentle Admonition of the Companions' Hearts (Verse 16)
- **Primary Source:** **Sahih Muslim 3027; Sunan an-Nasa'i; Tafsir Ibn Kathir (8/35)**
- **Narrator:** Abdullah ibn Mas'ud
- **Trigger:** Ibn Mas'ud testified: *"Between our acceptance of Islam and the revelation of this verse, with which Allah admonished us, there were only four years: 'Has the time not come for those who have believed that their hearts should become humbly submissive at the remembrance of Allah?'"* Allah noticed a slight relaxation in their devotional intensity and gently recalled them to awe.

#### B. The Goodly Loan (Verse 11)
- **Primary Source:** **Tafsir at-Tabari (23/180); Al-Wahidi, Asbab an-Nuzul, p. 281**
- **Narrator:** Abdullah ibn Mas'ud
- **Trigger:** When *"Who is it that will loan Allah a goodly loan?"* was revealed, Abu ad-Dahdah al-Ansari rushed to the Prophet ﷺ and asked: *"Does Allah really desire a loan from us?"* The Prophet said: *"Yes, O Abu ad-Dahdah."* Abu ad-Dahdah took the Prophet's hand and donated his entire prized orchard of 600 date palms in Medina for the cause of Allah.

## 58. Surah Al-Mujadilah (المجادلة) — The Pleading Woman

- **Classification:** Madani (22 Verses)
- **Asbab Status:** Whole-Surah Occasion
- **Status Label:** The Divine Hearing of Khawlah's Domestic Plea & Abolishing Zihar
- **Reporting Sahabah:** 'A'ishah bint Abi Bakr, Khawlah bint Tha'labah, Aws ibn as-Samit

### Historical & Theological Context
Revealed in Medina following an agonizing domestic crisis where an elderly woman was cast into legal and matrimonial limbo through the abusive pre-Islamic pagan formula of Zihar.

### Key Verified Historical Triggers & Canonical Sources

#### A. Khawlah's Dispute Over Zihar (Verses 1–4)
- **Primary Source:** **Sahih al-Bukhari 7386; Sunan Abi Dawud 2214; Musnad Ahmad 27319; Sunan an-Nasa'i 3460**
- **Narrator:** 'A'ishah bint Abi Bakr
- **Trigger:** Aws ibn as-Samit, in a fit of elderly rage, pronounced Zihar against his devoted wife Khawlah bint Tha'labah (*"You are to me like the back of my mother"*), which in Jahiliyyah meant permanent marital estrangement without the freedom of divorce. Khawlah wept before the Prophet ﷺ, pleading that she had young children who would starve if given to him or perish if kept by her. 'A'ishah sat in the corner of the apartment and could barely hear her whispers, yet Allah revealed from above seven heavens: *"Allah has heard the speech of she who disputes with you concerning her husband!"*—abolishing the practice and instituting the three-tiered Kaffarah.

#### B. Making Room in Assemblies (Verse 11)
- **Primary Source:** **Jami' at-Tirmidhi; Tafsir Ibn Kathir (8/57); Al-Wahidi p. 280**
- **Narrator:** Muqatil ibn Hayyan
- **Trigger:** On a Friday in the crowded mosque of Medina, veterans of the Battle of Badr arrived late and found no room to sit. The Prophet ﷺ told specific younger companions to rise to honour the elders of Badr. Sensing slight embarrassment, Allah revealed: *"When you are told to make room in assemblies, make room; Allah will make room for you!"*

## 59. Surah Al-Hashr (الحشر) — The Gathering

- **Classification:** Madani (24 Verses)
- **Asbab Status:** Whole-Surah Occasion
- **Status Label:** Siege & Banishment of Banu an-Nadir (Surat an-Nadir)
- **Reporting Sahabah:** Abdullah ibn Abbas, Sa'id ibn Jubayr, Umar ibn al-Khattab

### Historical & Theological Context
Revealed in the 4th year of the Hijrah chronicling the siege, surrender, and expulsion of the Jewish tribe Banu an-Nadir following their conspiracy to assassinate the Prophet by dropping a millstone from their rooftop.

### Key Verified Historical Triggers & Canonical Sources

#### A. The Surah of Banu an-Nadir (Verses 2–5)
- **Primary Source:** **Sahih al-Bukhari 4882; Sahih Muslim 1757**
- **Narrator:** Sa'id ibn Jubayr from Abdullah ibn Abbas
- **Trigger:** Sa'id ibn Jubayr asked Ibn Abbas about Surat al-Hashr. Ibn Abbas said: *"Say: Surat an-Nadir!"* It documents how their colossal stone fortresses failed against divine awe (*Ru'b*), forcing them to demolish their own mansions to salvage wood as they rode out toward Khaybar and Syria.

#### B. The Cutting of the Date Palms (Verse 5)
- **Primary Source:** **Sahih al-Bukhari 4884; Sahih Muslim 1740**
- **Narrator:** Abdullah ibn Umar
- **Trigger:** When the Prophet ordered the burning and felling of the Buwayrah date palms to exert psychological pressure on the entrenched conspirators, the hypocrites accused him of ecological vandalism. Allah revealed: *"Whatever palm trees you cut down or left standing on their roots—it was by permission of Allah and to disgrace the defiant."*

#### C. The Altruism of the Ansar (Verse 9)
- **Primary Source:** **Sahih al-Bukhari 3798; Sahih Muslim 2054**
- **Narrator:** Abu Hurairah
- **Trigger:** An Ansari companion took a destitute guest home when the Prophet had nothing to offer. He told his wife to dim the lamp and pretend to eat so the guest could eat to his fill. The next morning, the Prophet smiled: *"Allah approved of your act last night!"*—and Verse 9 was revealed.

## 60. Surah Al-Mumtahanah (الممتحنة) — The Examined Woman

- **Classification:** Madani (13 Verses)
- **Asbab Status:** Ayah-Level Incidents
- **Status Label:** Interception of Hatib's Epistle & The Women's Constitutional Oath
- **Reporting Sahabah:** Ali ibn Abi Talib, Umar ibn al-Khattab, Asma' bint Abi Bakr

### Historical & Theological Context
Revealed in late 8 AH preceding the Conquest of Mecca, regulating the ethics of political allegiance, sovereign disavowal, foreign relations with peaceful non-combatants, and the legal status of refugee women.

### Key Verified Historical Triggers & Canonical Sources

#### A. Hatib's Intercepted Letter to Quraysh (Verses 1–3)
- **Primary Source:** **Sahih al-Bukhari 4890; Sahih Muslim 2494**
- **Narrator:** Ali ibn Abi Talib
- **Trigger:** Before the secret mobilization for the Conquest of Mecca, Hatib ibn Abi Balta'ah sent a clandestine letter via a female rider (Sarah) warning Quraysh. Divine revelation alerted the Prophet; Ali and Az-Zubayr intercepted her at Rawdat Khakh and extracted the letter from her hair. When Umar asked to execute Hatib for treason, the Prophet stayed his hand, reminding him that Hatib had fought at Badr.

#### B. Benevolence Toward Non-Combatants (Verse 8)
- **Primary Source:** **Sahih al-Bukhari 2620; Sahih Muslim 1003**
- **Narrator:** Asma' bint Abi Bakr
- **Trigger:** Asma's polytheist mother Qutaylah came to Medina bearing gifts. Asma' hesitated to receive her until the Prophet instructed her to maintain filial kindness, prompting Verse 8: *"Allah does not forbid you from those who do not fight you in religion... that you treat them with kindness and justice."*

#### C. The Constitutional Oath of Women (Verse 12)
- **Primary Source:** **Sahih al-Bukhari 4891; Sahih Muslim 1866**
- **Narrator:** 'A'ishah bint Abi Bakr & Hind bint 'Utbah
- **Trigger:** Upon the Conquest of Mecca, Meccan women gathered to swear allegiance. The Prophet took their verbal pledge upon six moral commandments without touching any woman's hand.

## 61. Surah As-Saff (الصف) — The Solid Ranks

- **Classification:** Madani (14 Verses)
- **Asbab Status:** Ayah-Level Incidents
- **Status Label:** Rebuking Verbal Bravado & The Cemented Battle Array
- **Reporting Sahabah:** Abdullah ibn Salam, Abu Hurairah

### Historical & Theological Context
Revealed in Medina to eradicate the gap between verbal professions of piety and physical courage, setting the metaphor of the community as an immovable lead-cemented structure (*Bunyan Marsoos*).

### Key Verified Historical Triggers & Canonical Sources

#### A. Rebuking Unfulfilled Declarations (Verses 2–3)
- **Primary Source:** **Jami' at-Tirmidhi 3309; Musnad Ahmad; Al-Wahidi p. 284**
- **Narrator:** Abdullah ibn Salam
- **Trigger:** Companions sat conversing: *"If we only knew which deed is most beloved to Allah, we would spend our wealth and lives in it!"* When Allah revealed that striving in His cause with discipline was that deed, some wavered, prompting the piercing rebuke: *"O you who have believed, why do you say what you do not do? It is most hateful in the sight of Allah that you say what you do not do."*

#### B. The Herald of Ahmad by Jesus (Verse 6)
- **Primary Source:** **Sahih al-Bukhari 3532; Sahih Muslim 2354**
- **Narrator:** Jubayr ibn Mut'im & Abu Hurairah
- **Trigger:** Affirming the prophetic lineage where Jesus son of Mary confirms the Torah and heralds the arrival of the universal Comforter named Ahmad (*The Most Praised*).

## 62. Surah Al-Jumu'ah (الجمعة) — The Friday Congregation

- **Classification:** Madani (11 Verses)
- **Asbab Status:** Ayah-Level Incidents
- **Status Label:** The Friday Congregation & The Syrian Caravan Stampede
- **Reporting Sahabah:** Jabir ibn Abdillah, Salman al-Farsi, Abu Hurairah

### Historical & Theological Context
Codifies the weekly congregational liturgy of Jumu'ah, balancing mandatory cessation of commercial transactions during the Khutbah with vigorous resumption of trade and production thereafter.

### Key Verified Historical Triggers & Canonical Sources

#### A. The Arrival of Dihyah's Trade Caravan (Verses 9–11)
- **Primary Source:** **Sahih al-Bukhari 4899; Sahih Muslim 863; Jami' at-Tirmidhi 3311**
- **Narrator:** Jabir ibn Abdillah
- **Trigger:** During a severe famine in Medina, a commercial caravan led by Dihyah al-Kalbi arrived from Syria with wheat and oil, beating drums to announce market sales. While the Prophet ﷺ was delivering the Friday Khutbah on the minbar, the hungry congregation broke ranks and rushed out, leaving only twelve companions standing before him. Allah revealed: *"And when they see a transaction or a diversion, they rush to it and leave you standing. Say: What is with Allah is better than diversion and trade, and Allah is the best of providers!"*

#### B. Reaching the Latter Non-Arab Nations (Verse 3)
- **Primary Source:** **Sahih al-Bukhari 4897; Sahih Muslim 2546**
- **Narrator:** Abu Hurairah
- **Trigger:** When asked about *"and others of them who have not yet joined them,"* the Prophet placed his hand on Salman al-Farsi and said: *"Even if faith were at the Pleiades, men from these people (Persians) would attain it."*

## 63. Surah Al-Munafiqun (المنافقون) — The Hypocrites

- **Classification:** Madani (11 Verses)
- **Asbab Status:** Whole-Surah Occasion
- **Status Label:** The Treason of Ibn Ubayy at Muraysi' & Zayd's Divine Vindication
- **Reporting Sahabah:** Zayd ibn Arqam, Abdullah ibn Abbas, Jabir ibn Abdillah

### Historical & Theological Context
Revealed in 5/6 AH during the return from the Expedition of Banu al-Mustaliq (Al-Muraysi'), delivering an unsparing autopsy of covert treason, hollow physical charm (*Khushubun Musannadah*), and economic boycotts against the poor.

### Key Verified Historical Triggers & Canonical Sources

#### A. The Treasonous Slogan of Ibn Ubayy (Verses 7–8)
- **Primary Source:** **Sahih al-Bukhari 4900; Sahih Muslim 2584; Jami' at-Tirmidhi 3312**
- **Narrator:** Zayd ibn Arqam
- **Trigger:** At the well of Al-Muraysi', an argument flared between an emigrant and an ally of the Khazraj. Abdullah ibn Ubayy flared up, saying: *"Do not spend on those with the Messenger until they disperse!"* and boasted: *"When we return to Medina, the more honorable will surely expel the humiliated!"* Young Zayd ibn Arqam heard this and reported it to the Prophet. Ibn Ubayy swore false oaths denying it, causing some to doubt Zayd until this Surah descended, vindicating Zayd and declaring: *"To Allah belongs all honour, and to His Messenger, and to the believers."*

#### B. The Deathbed Yearning for Charity (Verse 10)
- **Primary Source:** **Tafsir Ibn Kathir (8/135); Tafsir at-Tabari (23/395)**
- **Narrator:** Abdullah ibn Abbas
- **Trigger:** Ibn Abbas explains that the dying soul yearns exclusively to give charity (*Sadaqah*) because of the monumental forgiveness witnessed at the moment the veil of mortality is torn.

## 64. Surah At-Taghabun (التغابن) — Mutual Disillusion

- **Classification:** Madani (18 Verses)
- **Asbab Status:** Ayah-Level Incidents
- **Status Label:** Meccan Families Hindering Migration & The Day of Mutual Disillusion
- **Reporting Sahabah:** Abdullah ibn Abbas, 'Alqamah ibn Qays

### Historical & Theological Context
A Musabbihat Surah revealing the true cosmic exchange rate between Dunya and Akhirah (Yawm at-Taghabun), and providing pastoral solace for believers whose families hindered their spiritual growth.

### Key Verified Historical Triggers & Canonical Sources

#### A. Spouses and Children as Domestic Obstacles (Verses 14–15)
- **Primary Source:** **Jami' at-Tirmidhi 3317; Sunan an-Nasa'i; Al-Wahidi p. 287**
- **Narrator:** Abdullah ibn Abbas
- **Trigger:** Men in Mecca embraced Islam and wished to migrate to Medina, but their wives and children wept and clung to them, preventing their departure. When they finally migrated later and saw the advanced spiritual rank of early companions, they intended to punish their families. Allah revealed: *"Indeed, among your spouses and your children are enemies to you, so beware of them. But if you pardon and overlook and forgive—then indeed, Allah is Forgiving and Merciful."*

#### B. Serenity in the Crucible of Calamity (Verse 11)
- **Primary Source:** **Sahih al-Bukhari; Tafsir Ibn Kathir (8/142)**
- **Narrator:** 'Alqamah ibn Qays
- **Trigger:** 'Alqamah explained the guided heart: *"It is the person who, when struck by a catastrophe, knows with certainty that it is from Allah, so he surrenders with serene contentment."*

## 65. Surah At-Talaq (الطلاق) — The Divorce

- **Classification:** Madani (12 Verses)
- **Asbab Status:** Ayah-Level Incidents
- **Status Label:** Minor An-Nisa: The Taxonomy of Waiting Periods & Sanctuary Housing
- **Reporting Sahabah:** Abdullah ibn Umar, Ubayy ibn Ka'b, Abu Dharr al-Ghifari

### Historical & Theological Context
Known classically as *Surat an-Nisa' as-Sughra* (Minor Surah An-Nisa), standardizing the procedural ethics of marital dissolution, the inviolability of the marital shelter during 'Iddah, and the divine guarantee of deliverance for the God-conscious.

### Key Verified Historical Triggers & Canonical Sources

#### A. Divorcing at the Prescribed Term (Verse 1)
- **Primary Source:** **Sahih al-Bukhari 5251; Sahih Muslim 1471**
- **Narrator:** Abdullah ibn Umar
- **Trigger:** Ibn Umar divorced his wife while she was menstruating. Umar asked the Prophet ﷺ, who was deeply angered and commanded: *"Order him to take her back until she purifies, then menstruates, then purifies again. Then if he wishes, he may retain her, or divorce her before intimacy. That is the prescribed term ('Iddah) which Allah commanded."*

#### B. Clarifying Waiting Periods for Non-Menstruating Women (Verse 4)
- **Primary Source:** **Mustadrak al-Hakim 2/492; Sunan Abi Dawud; Al-Wahidi p. 288**
- **Narrator:** Ubayy ibn Ka'b
- **Trigger:** Ubayy asked the Prophet: *"Some women of Medina do not menstruate—the young, post-menopausal, and pregnant. What is their term?"* Verse 4 descended, establishing three months for post-menopausal women and those with amenorrhea, and delivery of child for pregnant women.

#### C. The Way Out from Calamity (Verses 2–3)
- **Primary Source:** **Musnad Ahmad 21532; Sunan Ibn Majah**
- **Narrator:** Abu Dharr al-Ghifari
- **Trigger:** The Prophet recited: *"And whoever fears Allah—He will make for him a way out,"* and said: *"O Abu Dharr, if all people held fast to this verse, it would suffice them all!"*

## 66. Surah At-Tahrim (التحريم) — The Prohibition

- **Classification:** Madani (12 Verses)
- **Asbab Status:** Whole-Surah Occasion
- **Status Label:** The Domestic Honey Pledge, The Disclosed Secret & The Four Archetypes
- **Reporting Sahabah:** 'A'ishah bint Abi Bakr, Hafsah bint Umar, Umar ibn al-Khattab

### Historical & Theological Context
Revealed in Medina resolving a domestic crisis in the prophetic household, establishing the invalidity of oaths prohibiting lawful blessings, calling to sincere repentance (*Tawbah Nasoohah*), and contrasting two treacherous wives of prophets with two saintly women who conquered tyranny.

### Key Verified Historical Triggers & Canonical Sources

#### A. The Honey Incident & The Secret Vow (Verses 1–4)
- **Primary Source:** **Sahih al-Bukhari 4912; Sahih Muslim 1474**
- **Narrator:** 'A'ishah bint Abi Bakr
- **Trigger:** The Prophet ﷺ used to drink honey at the apartment of Zaynab bint Jahsh. 'A'ishah and Hafsah conspired to ask him: *"Have you eaten Maghafeer? I smell its pungent odor on you."* The Prophet, sensitive to scents, swore an oath never to drink honey again. When Hafsah disclosed this private conversation to 'A'ishah, Allah revealed the opening of At-Tahrim, releasing the Prophet from the self-imposed ban, commanding expiation, and calling the two wives to repentance.

#### B. The Celestial Coalition for the Messenger (Verse 4)
- **Primary Source:** **Sahih al-Bukhari 4913; Sahih Muslim 1479**
- **Narrator:** Umar ibn al-Khattab
- **Trigger:** When rumors spread that the Prophet had divorced all his wives over domestic friction, Umar rushed to his daughter Hafsah, finding her weeping. Verse 4 warned that if they conspired against him, Allah, Gabriel, the righteous believers, and the mobilized angelic hosts were his guardians.

#### C. The Four Archetypal Women of History (Verses 10–12)
- **Primary Source:** **Sahih al-Bukhari 3411; Sahih Muslim 2431**
- **Narrator:** Abu Musa al-Ash'ari
- **Trigger:** Setting the definitive historical precedent: the wives of Noah and Lot perished despite being married to righteous prophets, proving holy marriage grants no exemption from personal faith; while Asiya wife of Pharaoh and Maryam bint 'Imran achieved the highest stations of Paradise despite living in tyrannical or solitary conditions.
