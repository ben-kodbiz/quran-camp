## Surahs 36 to 45 (Ya-Sin to Al-Jathiyah)

## 36. Surah Ya-Sin (يس) — Ya-Sin

- **Classification:** Makki (83 Verses)
- **Asbab Status:** Ayah-Level Incidents
- **Status Label:** Footprints to the Mosque & The Crumbling Bone Dispute
- **Reporting Sahabah:** Abu Sa'id al-Khudri, Jundub ibn Abdillah, Abdullah ibn Abbas

### Historical & Theological Context
Regarded as the beating heart of the Qur'an, Surah Ya-Sin addresses the existential foundations of faith: monotheism, apostolic legitimacy, bodily resurrection, and cosmic balance. Specific verses were triggered by concrete historical disputes between the Prophet ﷺ and pagan chieftains who denied somatic resurrection, as well as the pastoral guidance given to Companions seeking closeness to the Prophet's Mosque.

### Key Verified Historical Triggers & Canonical Sources

#### A. The Footsteps of Banu Salimah Recorded (Verse 12)
- **Primary Source:** **Sahih Muslim 665; Jami' at-Tirmidhi 3226; Sunan an-Nasa'i 700**
- **Narrator:** Abu Sa'id al-Khudri and Jabir ibn Abdillah
- **Trigger:** The clan of Banu Salimah resided at the outer perimeter of Madinah and desired to sell their homes and relocate close to the Prophet's Mosque. The Prophet ﷺ disapproved of leaving the outskirts of Madinah unpopulated and addressed them:  
  *«O Banu Salimah! Stay in your dwellings; your footprints are being recorded! Stay in your dwellings; your footprints are being recorded!»*  
  Thereupon Allah confirmed verse 12:  
  *«Indeed, it is We who bring the dead to life and record what they have put forth and what they left behind (atharahum), and all things We have enumerated in a clear register.»*

#### B. Ubayy ibn Khalaf and the Crumbling Bone (Verses 77–79)
- **Primary Source:** **Al-Wahidi, Asbab an-Nuzul, p. 254; Al-Mustadrak lil-Hakim (2/429, Sahih); Tafsir Ibn Kathir (6/592)**
- **Narrator:** Abdullah ibn Abbas
- **Trigger:** Ubayy ibn Khalaf came to the Prophet ﷺ clutching a decayed, rotten human bone from a graveyard. Crushing it in his fingers and blowing the powdery bone dust into the Prophet's face, he sneered: *"O Muhammad, do you claim that Allah can revive this after it has rotted into powder?"*  
  The Prophet ﷺ replied: *"Yes, He will cause you to die, then He will resurrect you, and then He will cast you into the Fire!"*  
  Allah revealed verses 77–79:  
  *«Does man not consider that We created him from a [mere] sperm-drop - then at once he is an open disputant? And he presents for Us an example and forgets his [own] creation. He says, "Who will give life to bones while they are disintegrated?" Say: "He will give them life Who produced them the first time; and He is, of all creation, Knowing."»*

## 37. Surah As-Saffat (الصافات) — Those Arrayed in Ranks

- **Classification:** Makki (182 Verses)
- **Asbab Status:** Ayah-Level Incidents & Liturgical Closure
- **Status Label:** Meteoric Celestial Defense & The Liturgical Triad
- **Reporting Sahabah:** Abu Hurairah, Abdullah ibn Abbas, Ali ibn Abi Talib

### Historical & Theological Context
Revealed in Mecca to establish angelic discipline, the unassailable celestial barrier protecting revelation from demonic infiltration, the great trials of earlier prophets, and the sacrifice of Ismail.

### Key Verified Historical Triggers & Canonical Sources

#### A. Shooting Stars Guarding Against Eavesdropping Devils (Verses 6–10)
- **Primary Source:** **Sahih al-Bukhari 4701; Sahih Muslim 2229**
- **Narrator:** Abu Hurairah and Abdullah ibn Abbas
- **Trigger:** When the pagan soothsayers (*Kuhhan*) claimed they received heavenly secrets from jinn, the Prophet ﷺ clarified that with the advent of the Qur'an, celestial security was sealed. Devils climbing on each other's shoulders to overhear angelic decrees are struck by a piercing meteoric projectile (*Shihabun Thaqib*), wiping out their transmission before they can mix ninety-nine lies with one stolen truth.

#### B. The Prophet's Liturgical Closing Formula (Verses 180–182)
- **Primary Source:** **Sunan Abi Dawud; Musnad Ahmad 3/250; Al-Mu'jam al-Awsat lil-Tabarani**
- **Narrator:** Ali ibn Abi Talib
- **Trigger:** The Prophet ﷺ stated: *"Whoever desires to be measured with the fullest measure of reward when he concludes his gathering or prayer, let him say: Subhana Rabbika Rabbil-'Izzati 'amma yasifoon, wa salamun 'alal-mursaleen, wal-hamdu lillahi Rabbil-'alameen."*

## 38. Surah Sad (ص) — Sad

- **Classification:** Makki (88 Verses)
- **Asbab Status:** Ayah-Level Incidents
- **Status Label:** The Chieftains' Delegation at Abu Talib's Deathbed
- **Reporting Sahabah:** Abdullah ibn Abbas, Ibn Mas'ud

### Historical & Theological Context
A dramatic Meccan chapter contrasting the elite arrogance of Quraysh with the humility of prophet-kings (Dawud and Sulayman) and the endurance of Ayyub. The entire opening was directly occasioned by an emergency political delegation attempting to negotiate a truce.

### Key Verified Historical Triggers & Canonical Sources

#### A. The Council of Pagan Chiefs at Abu Talib's Deathbed (Verses 1–5)
- **Primary Source:** **Jami' at-Tirmidhi 3232 (Sahih); Musnad Ahmad 1/362; Al-Mustadrak lil-Hakim 2/432; Al-Wahidi p. 256**
- **Narrator:** Abdullah ibn Abbas
- **Trigger:** When Abu Talib became terminally ill, the chieftains of Quraysh (Abu Jahl, Umayyah ibn Khalaf, Utbah ibn Rabi'ah) feared that if he died and they attacked Muhammad afterwards, Arabia would say they lacked chivalry. They met with Abu Talib, demanding he broker a compromise. The Prophet ﷺ entered and said: *"O my uncle! I ask of them only one word, by which all Arabs will submit to them and all non-Arabs will pay tribute."* Abu Jahl eagerly exclaimed: *"By your father, we would give you ten words!"* The Prophet ﷺ replied: *"Say: La ilaha illallah, and cast off what you worship besides Him."*  
  The chieftains clapped their hands in dismay and walked away saying: *"Has he made all the gods into one God? This is truly an astonishing thing!"*  
  Thereupon verses 1–5 were revealed.

#### B. The Debate of the High Council / Al-Mala' al-A'la (Verse 69)
- **Primary Source:** **Jami' at-Tirmidhi 3233, 3235 (Sahih)**
- **Narrator:** Mu'adh ibn Jabal and Ibn Abbas
- **Trigger:** The Prophet ﷺ was asked about the unseen High Council, and he explained that the archangels debate the great expiations (*Kaffarat*): walking to congregational prayers, remaining in the mosque after prayer, performing ablution thoroughly in hardship, feeding the hungry, and praying at night while people sleep.

## 39. Surah Az-Zumar (الزمر) — The Crowds

- **Classification:** Makki (75 Verses)
- **Asbab Status:** Ayah-Level Incidents
- **Status Label:** Universal Redemption for Sinners & The Jewish Rabbi Hadith
- **Reporting Sahabah:** Abdullah ibn Abbas, Abdullah ibn Mas'ud, Abu Hurairah

### Historical & Theological Context
Centered upon *Ikhlas* (uncompromised sincerity), the wrapping of night and day, and the dual eschatological throngs. It houses the most universally hopeful verse in Islamic scripture.

### Key Verified Historical Triggers & Canonical Sources

#### A. The Supreme Proclamation of Infinite Mercy (Verse 53)
- **Primary Source:** **Sahih al-Bukhari 4810; Sahih Muslim 122**
- **Narrator:** Abdullah ibn Abbas
- **Trigger:** A group of polytheists who had committed extensive murder, adultery, and idolatry came to the Prophet ﷺ and said: *"Indeed, what you say and call to is beautiful, if only you could inform us whether there is any expiation for what we have committed in the past."* Thereupon Allah revealed verse 53:  
  *«Say: "O My servants who have transgressed against themselves! Do not despair of the mercy of Allah. Indeed, Allah forgives all sins. Indeed, it is He who is the Forgiving, the Merciful."»*

#### B. The Jewish Rabbi and the Earth in His Grip (Verse 67)
- **Primary Source:** **Sahih al-Bukhari 4811; Sahih Muslim 2786**
- **Narrator:** Abdullah ibn Mas'ud
- **Trigger:** A Jewish scholar came to the Prophet ﷺ and said: *"O Muhammad, we learn that Allah will place the heavens on one Finger, the earth on one Finger, the trees on one Finger, the water and dust on one Finger, and the rest of creation on one Finger, and say: 'I am the Sovereign!'"* The Prophet ﷺ laughed until his molars were visible in affirmation of the rabbi's words, then recited verse 67:  
  *«And they have not appraised Allah with true appraisal, while the earth entirely will be [within] His grip on the Day of Resurrection, and the heavens will be folded in His right hand...»*

## 40. Surah Ghafir / Al-Mu'min (غافر) — The Forgiver

- **Classification:** Makki (85 Verses)
- **Asbab Status:** Ayah-Level Incidents
- **Status Label:** Abu Bakr's Defense at the Ka'bah & Supplication as Worship
- **Reporting Sahabah:** Abdullah ibn Amr ibn al-'As, Nu'man ibn Bashir, Anas ibn Malik

### Historical & Theological Context
The first of the seven Hawameem, introducing the dramatic court case of the Secret Believer from the house of Pharaoh. Its historical resonance in Mecca was immediate when early Muslims faced violent mob persecution.

### Key Verified Historical Triggers & Canonical Sources

#### A. Abu Bakr Defending the Prophet with Verse 28
- **Primary Source:** **Sahih al-Bukhari 3856**
- **Narrator:** Abdullah ibn Amr ibn al-'As
- **Trigger:** Urwah ibn al-Zubayr asked Abdullah ibn Amr: *"Tell me of the worst thing the polytheists did to the Prophet."* He replied: *"I saw Uqbah ibn Abi Mu'ayt come to the Prophet while he was praying at the Ka'bah. Uqbah wrapped his garment tightly around the Prophet's neck and throttled him violently. Abu Bakr rushed in, grabbed Uqbah by the shoulder, pushed him away, and cried out with tears streaming: 'Will you kill a man simply because he says: My Lord is Allah, while he has brought you clear proofs from your Lord?!'"*—reciting the exact words of the Believer of Pharaoh's family (Verse 28).

#### B. Supplication is the Core of Worship (Verse 60)
- **Primary Source:** **Jami' at-Tirmidhi 2969, 3247 (Sahih); Sunan Abi Dawud 1479; Sunan Ibn Majah 3828**
- **Narrator:** Nu'man ibn Bashir
- **Trigger:** Companions asked whether Allah was near so they could whisper to Him, or far so they must call out. The Prophet ﷺ taught: *"Supplication is the very essence of worship (*Ad-Du'a'u huwa al-'ibadah*),"* then recited verse 60:  
  *«And your Lord says: "Call upon Me; I will respond to you. Indeed, those who disdain My worship will enter Hell rendered contemptible."»*

## 41. Surah Fussilat (فصلت) — Explained in Detail

- **Classification:** Makki (54 Verses)
- **Asbab Status:** Ayah-Level Incidents
- **Status Label:** Utbah's Diplomatic Bribe & The Ka'bah Whisperers
- **Reporting Sahabah:** Abdullah ibn Mas'ud, Jabir ibn Abdillah

### Historical & Theological Context
Celebrated for its majestic Arabic clarity, cosmological six-era creation, and the somatic witness of human skin.

### Key Verified Historical Triggers & Canonical Sources

#### A. The Aristocratic Bribe of Utbah ibn Rabi'ah (Verses 1–13)
- **Primary Source:** **Dala'il an-Nubuwwah lil-Bayhaqi; Sirah Ibn Hisham 1/293; Tafsir Ibn Kathir 7/162**
- **Narrator:** Jabir ibn Abdillah and Ibn Abbas
- **Trigger:** Utbah ibn Rabi'ah offered the Prophet ﷺ supreme political kingship, boundless wealth, or medical treatment if he abandoned his monotheistic call. The Prophet listened calmly, then recited Surah Fussilat. When he reached verse 13 (*Fa-in a'radoo faqul andhartukum sa'iqatan mithla sa'iqati 'Adin wa Thamood*), Utbah was so terrified of immediate divine annihilation that he placed his hand over the Prophet's mouth, begging him to stop for the sake of kinship. Utbah returned to Quraysh advising them to leave the Prophet entirely alone.

#### B. Three Men Whispering at the Ka'bah (Verse 22)
- **Primary Source:** **Sahih al-Bukhari 4817; Sahih Muslim 2775; Jami' at-Tirmidhi 3248**
- **Narrator:** Abdullah ibn Mas'ud
- **Trigger:** Three men (two Thaqafis and one Qurayshi) hid near the curtain of the Ka'bah. One asked: *"Do you think Allah hears what we say?"* The second said: *"He hears if we speak aloud, but not if we whisper."* The third said: *"If He hears when we speak aloud, He surely hears when we whisper!"*  
  Thereupon Allah revealed verse 22:  
  *«And you did not conceal yourselves lest your hearing, your eyes, and your skins should testify against you, but you thought that Allah does not know much of what you do.»*

## 42. Surah Ash-Shura (الشورى) — Consultation

- **Classification:** Makki (53 Verses)
- **Asbab Status:** Ayah-Level Incidents
- **Status Label:** Affection for Kinfolk (Al-Mawaddah) & Deliberative Governance
- **Reporting Sahabah:** Abdullah ibn Abbas, Abu Hurairah

### Historical & Theological Context
Establishes the common theological thread uniting Nuh, Ibrahim, Musa, 'Isa, and Muhammad ﷺ, while codifying collaborative consultation (*Shura*) as a fundamental pillar of Islamic social architecture.

### Key Verified Historical Triggers & Canonical Sources

#### A. Affection for Kinfolk / Al-Mawaddata fil-Qurba (Verse 23)
- **Primary Source:** **Sahih al-Bukhari 4818; Jami' at-Tirmidhi 3251**
- **Narrator:** Abdullah ibn Abbas
- **Trigger:** Sa'id ibn Jubayr asked Ibn Abbas about verse 23: *«Say: I do not ask of you any payment for this except affection toward kinsfolk.»* Ibn Abbas explained: *"There was not a single clan of Quraysh except that the Prophet ﷺ had kinship with them. So he said: 'I ask no wealth from you; I only ask that you maintain the ties of kinship and affection that join us, and do not persecute me.'"*

## 43. Surah Az-Zukhruf (الزخرف) — The Gold Adornments

- **Classification:** Makki (89 Verses)
- **Asbab Status:** Ayah-Level Incidents
- **Status Label:** The Billionaire Fallacy & The Riding Doxology
- **Reporting Sahabah:** Abdullah ibn Abbas, Ali ibn Abi Talib, Abdullah ibn Umar

### Historical & Theological Context
Deconstructs materialistic delusions, exposing that earthly gold and silver (*Zukhruf*) are trivial before Allah, and establishes the timeless travel prayer.

### Key Verified Historical Triggers & Canonical Sources

#### A. The Oligarchs of Makkah and Ta'if (Verse 31)
- **Primary Source:** **Al-Wahidi, Asbab an-Nuzul, p. 273; Tafsir at-Tabari 21/603**
- **Narrator:** Abdullah ibn Abbas and Qatadah
- **Trigger:** Al-Walid ibn al-Mughirah (the wealthiest magnate of Makkah) and Urwah ibn Mas'ud ath-Thaqafi (the chieftain of Ta'if) claimed: *"Why was this Qur'an not revealed to a great, wealthy man of the two cities?!"* Allah replied in verse 32: *«Do they distribute the mercy of your Lord? We have apportioned among them their livelihood in the life of this world...»*

#### B. The Riding Supplication (Verses 13–14)
- **Primary Source:** **Sahih Muslim 1342; Jami' at-Tirmidhi 3446; Sunan Abi Dawud 2599**
- **Narrator:** Abdullah ibn Umar and Ali ibn Abi Talib
- **Trigger:** The Prophet ﷺ established the Sunnah that whenever mounting a camel, horse, or vehicle, one says *Bismillah* (thrice), *Allahu Akbar* (thrice), and then recites verses 13–14:  
  *«Glory to Him Who has subjected this to us, and we could not have otherwise subdued it. And indeed, to our Lord we will return.»*

## 44. Surah Ad-Dukhan (الدخان) — The Smoke

- **Classification:** Makki (59 Verses)
- **Asbab Status:** Ayah-Level Incidents
- **Status Label:** The Great Famine of Makkah & The Appointed Smoke
- **Reporting Sahabah:** Abdullah ibn Mas'ud, Abdullah ibn Abbas

### Historical & Theological Context
Anchored in the revelation of the Blessed Night (*Laylatun Mubarakah*) and the terrifying atmospheric famine that struck pagan Makkah.

### Key Verified Historical Triggers & Canonical Sources

#### A. The Drought and Visible Smoke (Verses 10–16)
- **Primary Source:** **Sahih al-Bukhari 4809, 4821; Sahih Muslim 2798**
- **Narrator:** Abdullah ibn Mas'ud
- **Trigger:** When Quraysh persistently rejected the message, the Prophet ﷺ supplicated: *"O Allah, assist me against them with seven years [of famine] like the seven years of Yusuf."* Drought struck so severely that people starved, eating carrion, hides, and bones. When a man looked up at the sky, he saw what appeared to be smoke due to extreme hunger and delirium. Abu Sufyan pleaded with the Prophet to pray for relief. When rain arrived, they reverted to polytheism. Allah then promised the Supreme Strike (*Al-Batshata al-Kubra*), which struck them at the Battle of Badr.

## 45. Surah Al-Jathiyah (الجاثية) — The Kneeling

- **Classification:** Makki (37 Verses)
- **Asbab Status:** Ayah-Level Incidents
- **Status Label:** Refutation of Dahriyyah Materialism & The Command to Forgive
- **Reporting Sahabah:** Abu Hurairah, Abdullah ibn Abbas

### Historical & Theological Context
The final Hawameem, concluding with the terrifying visual of every single nation kneeling before the Divine Judgment, and refuting materialistic atheism.

### Key Verified Historical Triggers & Canonical Sources

#### A. The Hadith Qudsi Refuting the Dahriyyah (Verse 24)
- **Primary Source:** **Sahih al-Bukhari 4826; Sahih Muslim 2246**
- **Narrator:** Abu Hurairah
- **Trigger:** The materialists claimed: *«There is nothing but our worldly life; we die and we live, and nothing destroys us except time (ad-dahr).»*  
  The Prophet ﷺ revealed the Hadith Qudsi:  
  *«Allah the Almighty says: "The son of Adam offends Me: he curses Time, whereas I am Time; in My hand is the night and day, and I alternate them as I will."»*

#### B. Command to Forgive During Meccan Vulnerability (Verse 14)
- **Primary Source:** **Al-Wahidi, Asbab an-Nuzul, p. 277; Tafsir Ibn Kathir 7/269**
- **Narrator:** Abdullah ibn Abbas
- **Trigger:** When a pagan polytheist severely insulted Umar ibn al-Khattab in Makkah, Umar wanted to execute him on the spot. Allah revealed verse 14:  
  *«Tell those who believe to forgive those who do not look forward to the days of Allah...»*—commanding patience and strategic restraint during the early Meccan period.
