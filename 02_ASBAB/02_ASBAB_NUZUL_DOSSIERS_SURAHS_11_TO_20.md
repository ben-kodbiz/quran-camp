## Surahs 11 to 20 (Hud to Ta-Ha)

## 11. Surah Hud (هود) — Hud

- **Classification:** Makki (Late Meccan Epoch)
- **Asbab Status:** Ayah-Level Incidents
- **Status Label:** Prophetic Steadfastness & The Expiation of Good Deeds
- **Reporting Sahabah:** Abdullah ibn Mas'ud, Abu Bakr as-Siddiq, Abu Juhaifah

### Historical & Theological Context
Revealed during the grueling late Meccan period. The Prophet ﷺ remarked regarding this chapter and its sisters: *"Surah Hud and its sisters have turned my hair white"* (Tirmidhi 3297), owing to the terrifying accounts of destroyed nations and the uncompromising divine command: *«So remain on a right course as you have been commanded...»* (Verse 112). Specific verses also descended addressing personal moral expiation.

### Key Verified Historical Triggers & Canonical Sources

#### A. Good Deeds Wiping Out Transgressions (Verse 114)
- **Primary Source:** **Sahih al-Bukhari 4687 & Sahih Muslim 2763**
- **Narrator:** Abdullah ibn Mas'ud
- **Trigger:** A man from the Ansar (Abu al-Yusr Ka'b ibn Amr) approached a woman buying dates in his shop, was overtaken by temptation, embraced and kissed her, but went no further. Stricken with intense remorse, he confessed to Abu Bakr, then Umar, and finally came weeping to the Prophet ﷺ asking for the prescribed punishment. The Prophet remained silent until Allah revealed:  
  *«And establish prayer at the two ends of the day and at the approach of the night. Indeed, good deeds do away with misdeeds. That is a reminder for those who remember.»*  
  The man asked: *"O Messenger of Allah, is this specifically for me?"* The Prophet replied: *"It is for anyone of my Ummah who encounters a similar situation."*

#### B. Turning the Prophet's Hair White
- **Primary Source:** **Jami' at-Tirmidhi 3297 (Sahih by Al-Albani)**
- **Narrator:** Abu Bakr as-Siddiq
- **Trigger:** Abu Bakr remarked to the Prophet ﷺ: *"O Messenger of Allah, you have aged (gray hair has appeared)!"* The Prophet replied: *"Hud, Al-Waqi'ah, Al-Mursalat, 'Amma Yatasa'aloon, and Idha ash-Shamsu Kuwwirat have made me gray."*

## 12. Surah Yusuf (يوسف) — Joseph

- **Classification:** Makki (The Year of Sorrow / 'Am al-Huzn)
- **Asbab Status:** Ibtida'an / Purely Spontaneous
- **Status Label:** Purely Spontaneous (Consolation of the Sorrowful Heart)
- **Reporting Sahabah:** Abdullah ibn Abbas, Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas

### Historical & Theological Context
Revealed in its entirety as a continuous, unified narrative in Mecca during the *'Am al-Huzn* (Year of Sorrow), immediately following the deaths of Khadijah and Abu Talib and the expulsion from Ta'if. It possesses **no worldly grievance or situational legal trigger**; rather, it was revealed spontaneously by divine compassion to soothe the Prophet's broken heart through the mirror of Yusuf's betrayal, patience, and eventual triumph.

### Canonical Hadith Citations
1. **Mustadrak al-Hakim (2/345) & Sunan Sa'id ibn Mansur:**
   > Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas narrated:  
   > *The Qur'an was being revealed to the Prophet ﷺ, and the companions said: "O Messenger of Allah, if only you would narrate stories to us!" Thereupon Allah revealed: «We relate to you, [O Muhammad], the best of stories in what We have revealed to you of this Qur'an...» (Yusuf: 3).*
2. **Exegetical Consensus (Ibn Kathir & Al-Qurtubi):**
   Classical scholars confirm that unlike episodic surahs, Surah Yusuf descended in one single revelation unit to demonstrate how divine *Lutf* (subtle benevolence) guides the believer through abandonment, false accusation, imprisonment, and elevation.

## 13. Surah Ar-Ra'd (الرعد) — The Thunder

- **Classification:** Madani (with Meccan thematic intersections)
- **Asbab Status:** Ayah-Level Incidents
- **Status Label:** Cosmic Doxology & The Struck Blasphemers
- **Reporting Sahabah:** Anas ibn Malik, Abdullah ibn Abbas

### Historical & Theological Context
Revealed to illustrate the cosmic obedience of physical creation—where the thunder itself glorifies Allah with praise and the angels tremble in awe of Him. Specific verses descended regarding defiant tribal arrogance.

### Key Verified Historical Triggers & Canonical Sources

#### A. The Thunderbolt Striking the Arrogant Chieftain (Verse 13)
- **Primary Source:** **Musnad Ahmad 12558, Sunan an-Nasa'i in Al-Kubra 11091, Al-Mu'jam al-Awsat of Tabarani**
- **Narrator:** Anas ibn Malik
- **Trigger:** The Prophet ﷺ sent an emissary to an arrogant pagan chieftain (Arbad ibn Qays or Amir ibn al-Tufayl) calling him to Allah. The chieftain scoffed: *"Who is this Lord of yours? Is He of gold? Is He of silver? Or is He of brass?"* While he was uttering this sacrilege, a lightning bolt split from a clear sky and struck him dead on the spot. Verse 13 descended:  
  *«And the thunder exalts [Allah] with praise of Him - and the angels [as well] from fear of Him - and He sends thunderbolts and strikes therewith whom He wills while they dispute about Allah; and He is severe in assault.»*

#### B. Inner Psychological Transformation (Verse 11)
- **Primary Source:** Classical Usul at-Tafsir (Al-Tabari & Ibn Kathir)
- **Context:** Establishes the divine sociopolitical law: *«Indeed, Allah will not change the condition of a people until they change what is in themselves.»*

## 14. Surah Ibrahim (إبراهيم) — Abraham

- **Classification:** Makki
- **Asbab Status:** Ibtida'an / Primarily Spontaneous
- **Status Label:** Primarily Spontaneous (Parables of Truth & Falsehood)
- **Reporting Sahabah:** Abdullah ibn Umar, Anas ibn Malik

### Historical & Theological Context
Spontaneous Meccan discourse on the timeless nature of faith: moving mankind from darkness into light (*Li-tukhrija an-nasa mina az-zulumati ila an-noor*). It contains no legal disputes or mundane situational sabab.

### Key Context & Canonical Hadith Citations
1. **The Parable of the Good Tree (Verses 24–25):**
   - **Primary Source:** **Sahih al-Bukhari 61 & Sahih Muslim 2811**
   - **Narrator:** Abdullah ibn Umar
   - **Trigger:** The Prophet ﷺ asked his companions: *"There is a tree whose leaves do not fall, and it is like the believer. Tell me what it is?"* The people suggested various desert trees. Ibn Umar recalled: *"It occurred to me that it was the date-palm (*An-Nakhlah*), but being the youngest, I was too shy to speak."* The Prophet ﷺ then said: *"It is the date-palm."* Verse 24 states: *«A good word like a good tree, whose root is firmly fixed and its branches high in the sky...»*
2. **Ibrahim's Supplication at the Ka'bah (Verses 35–41):**
   Spontaneous prophetic litany establishing Mecca as a secure sanctuary and begging for monotheism across generational lineages.

## 15. Surah Al-Hijr (الحجر) — The Rocky Tract

- **Classification:** Makki
- **Asbab Status:** Ayah-Level Incidents
- **Status Label:** Commencing Public Proclamation & Protection from Mockers
- **Reporting Sahabah:** Abdullah ibn Abbas, Anas ibn Malik

### Historical & Theological Context
Revealed during the pivotal transition when the Prophet ﷺ was commanded to transition from private propagation (*Dawah Sirriyyah*) to open public proclamation (*Dawah Jahriyyah*), facing severe derision from the elite Qurayshi mockers (*Al-Mustahzi'oon*).

### Key Verified Historical Triggers & Canonical Sources

#### A. Proclaim Openly What You Are Commanded (Verse 94)
- **Primary Source:** **Sahih al-Bukhari 4770 & Al-Wahidi, p. 195**
- **Narrator:** Abdullah ibn Abbas
- **Trigger:** For the first three years of revelation, the Prophet taught faith discreetly in the House of Al-Arqam. Allah revealed:  
  *«Then declare what you are commanded and turn away from the polytheists.»*  
  Upon this, the Prophet ascended the mount of As-Safa and summoned the clans of Quraysh publicly for the first time.

#### B. Protection from the Five Mockers (Verse 95)
- **Primary Source:** **Mustadrak al-Hakim (2/358) & Al-Suyuti, Lubab an-Nuqul**
- **Narrator:** Abdullah ibn Abbas
- **Trigger:** Five prominent chieftains of Quraysh (Al-Walid ibn al-Mughirah, Al-'As ibn Wa'il, Al-Aswad ibn al-Muttalib, Al-Aswad ibn Abd Yaghuth, and Al-Harith ibn al-Tulaytilah) relentlessly mocked and harassed the Prophet. Verse 95 descended:  
  *«Indeed, We are sufficient for you against the mockers.»*  
  Shortly thereafter, the Angel Gabriel pointed towards each of them, and within days, each perished through mysterious ailments or fatal accidents before the Battle of Badr.

## 16. Surah An-Nahl (النحل) — The Bee

- **Classification:** Makki (with closing verses intersecting Uhud)
- **Asbab Status:** Ayah-Level Incidents
- **Status Label:** The Surah of Blessings & The Coerced Confession
- **Reporting Sahabah:** Abdullah ibn Abbas, Ammar ibn Yasir, Ubayy ibn Ka'b

### Historical & Theological Context
Celebrated as *Surat an-Ni'am* (The Surah of Divine Favors), detailing cosmic ecology, milk production, and bee architecture. Critical historical incidents are verified regarding the early Meccan martyrs and the trauma of Hamzah's mutilation at Uhud.

### Key Verified Historical Triggers & Canonical Sources

#### A. The Coerced Confession of Ammar ibn Yasir (Verse 106)
- **Primary Source:** **Mustadrak al-Hakim (2/357), Sunan al-Bayhaqi (8/208), Al-Tabari (14/181)**
- **Narrator:** Abdullah ibn Abbas
- **Trigger:** The polytheists tortured Ammar ibn Yasir after martyring his parents, Sumayyah and Yasir. They held him under water and burned him until, under extreme agony, he verbally uttered praise for their idols and reviled the Prophet. Released, Ammar came running to the Prophet weeping in profound anguish: *"I am ruined, O Messenger of Allah! I did not cease until I spoke ill of you!"* The Prophet asked: *"How do you find your heart?"* Ammar replied: *"Firmly tranquil with faith."* The Prophet gently wiped his tears and said: *"If they return to torture you, repeat what you said."* Thereupon Allah revealed:  
  *«Whoever disbelieves in Allah after his belief... except for one who is forced while his heart is content in faith...»* (Verse 106).

#### B. Forbidding Retaliatory Mutilation at Uhud (Verses 125–128)
- **Primary Source:** **Jami' at-Tirmidhi 3129 & Musnad Ahmad 21229**
- **Narrator:** Ubayy ibn Ka'b
- **Trigger:** On the day of Uhud, seeing the body of his beloved uncle Hamzah with his abdomen sliced open and liver removed, the Prophet was overcome with grief and said: *"If Allah grants me victory over Quraysh, I will mutilate thirty of their men in retaliation!"* The companions echoed this oath. Thereupon, verses 126–127 descended:  
  *«And if you punish [an enemy, O believers], punish with an equivalent of that with which you were harmed. But if you are patient - it is better for those who are patient. And be patient, [O Muhammad], and your patience is not but through Allah...»*  
  The Prophet immediately expiated his oath, forgave, and forbade mutilation (*Muthlah*) forever.

## 17. Surah Al-Isra (الإسراء) — The Night Journey

- **Classification:** Makki
- **Asbab Status:** Ayah-Level Inquiries
- **Status Label:** The Celestial Journey & Rabbinic Inquiries on the Soul
- **Reporting Sahabah:** Abdullah ibn Mas'ud, Abdullah ibn Abbas, Jabir ibn Abdillah

### Historical & Theological Context
Framed around the miraculous nocturnal ascent from the Sacred Mosque in Mecca to the Distant Mosque (*Al-Aqsa*) in Jerusalem, and up through the seven celestial realms. In Mecca, it also answered rabbinical challenges formulated by Jewish authorities to test the Prophet's authenticity.

### Key Verified Historical Triggers & Canonical Sources

#### A. Rabbinic Question Regarding the Soul / Ar-Ruh (Verse 85)
- **Primary Source:** **Sahih al-Bukhari 4721 & Sahih Muslim 2794**
- **Narrator:** Abdullah ibn Mas'ud
- **Trigger:** Ibn Mas'ud narrates: *"While I was walking with the Prophet ﷺ in a field in Medina (or Mecca), he was leaning on a palm-stalk cane. A group of Jewish scholars passed by and said to one another: 'Ask him about the soul.' Some said: 'Do not ask him, lest he says something you hate.' They said: 'O Abu al-Qasim, what is the soul (Ar-Ruh)?' The Prophet remained silent, leaning on his staff. I knew that revelation was descending upon him. When the state cleared, he recited:  
  *«And they ask you, [O Muhammad], about the soul. Say, "The soul is of the affair of my Lord. And mankind have not been given of knowledge except a little."»* (Verse 85).

#### B. The Night Journey & Qurayshi Mockery (Verse 1)
- **Primary Source:** **Sahih al-Bukhari 3886 & Sahih Muslim 170**
- **Narrator:** Jabir ibn Abdillah
- **Trigger:** When the Prophet announced his journey to Jerusalem and back in a single night, Quraysh mocked him and demanded physical descriptions of Bayt al-Maqdis. Allah projected the visual image of the sanctuary before the Prophet's eyes, allowing him to describe every gate and pillar accurately.

## 18. Surah Al-Kahf (الكهف) — The Cave

- **Classification:** Makki
- **Asbab Status:** Major Rabbinic Test Questions
- **Status Label:** The Three Rabbinic Enigmas (Cave Youths, Spirit, Two-Horned King)
- **Reporting Sahabah:** Abdullah ibn Abbas

### Historical & Theological Context
Revealed when Quraysh sent An-Nadr ibn al-Harith and 'Uqbah ibn Abi Mu'ayt to the Jewish rabbis of Yathrib (Medina) seeking litmus tests to determine if Muhammad ﷺ was a genuine prophet. The rabbis replied: *"Ask him three questions which only a true prophet can answer: ask about the young men who disappeared in ancient times, ask about the mighty traveler who reached the ends of the earth, and ask about the Spirit."*

### Key Verified Historical Triggers & Canonical Sources

#### A. The Rabbinic Inquiry & The Omission of In Sha' Allah (Verses 9–26, 83–98)
- **Primary Source:** **Tafsir at-Tabari (15/195), Ibn Kathir (5/133), Al-Wahidi, p. 204**
- **Narrator:** Abdullah ibn Abbas
- **Trigger:** When the envoys returned to Mecca and posed the three questions, the Prophet ﷺ confidently replied: *"I will tell you tomorrow,"* forgetting to say *"In Sha' Allah"* (If Allah wills). Revelation ceased entirely for fifteen nights, causing severe anxiety to the Prophet and jubilation to Quraysh. Then Angel Gabriel descended with Surah Al-Kahf, containing:
  1. The detailed narrative of the Cave Companions (Verses 9–26).
  2. The admonition regarding human speech: *«And never say of anything, "Indeed, I will do that tomorrow," except [when adding], "If Allah wills"...»* (Verses 23–24).
  3. The story of Dhul-Qarnayn traversing to the rising and setting places of the sun (Verses 83–98).

## 19. Surah Maryam (مريم) — Mary

- **Classification:** Makki (Recited before the Negus of Abyssinia)
- **Asbab Status:** Ayah-Level Incidents
- **Status Label:** Prophetic Gentleness & The Insolent Debtor
- **Reporting Sahabah:** Khabbab ibn al-Aratt, Abdullah ibn Abbas

### Historical & Theological Context
Celebrated historically as the chapter recited by Ja'far ibn Abi Talib before the Christian Negus (Ashamah) of Abyssinia, causing the king and his bishops to weep until their beards were soaked. Specific verses also addressed the insolent arrogance of Meccan financiers.

### Key Verified Historical Triggers & Canonical Sources

#### A. The Arrogant Claim of Al-'As ibn Wa'il (Verses 77–80)
- **Primary Source:** **Sahih al-Bukhari 4732 & Sahih Muslim 2795**
- **Narrator:** Khabbab ibn al-Aratt (Direct Personal Eyewitness Testimony)
- **Trigger:** Khabbab narrated: *"I was a blacksmith during the Jahiliyyah, and Al-'As ibn Wa'il owed me a debt for swords I crafted. I went to him demanding payment. He said: 'I will never pay you until you disbelieve in Muhammad!' I said: 'I will never disbelieve in Muhammad until you die and are resurrected.' Al-'As mocked: 'Will I die and then be resurrected? When I am raised, I will have wealth and children, and I will pay you then!' Thereupon Allah revealed:  
  *«Have you seen the one who disbelieved in Our verses and said, "I will surely be given wealth and children [in the Next Life]?" Has he looked into the unseen, or has he taken from the Most Merciful a promise? No! We will record what he says and extend for him from the punishment extensively...»* (Verses 77–80).

#### B. The Descent of Divine Affection / Wudd (Verse 96)
- **Primary Source:** **Sahih al-Bukhari 7485 & Sahih Muslim 2637**
- **Narrator:** Abu Hurairah
- **Trigger:** The Prophet ﷺ explained: *"When Allah loves a servant, He calls Gabriel saying: 'I love so-and-so, so love him.' Then Gabriel loves him and calls out in the heavens... then acceptance is placed for him upon the earth."* Verse 96 states: *«Indeed, those who have believed and done righteous deeds - the Most Merciful will appoint for them affection.»*

## 20. Surah Ta-Ha (طه) — Ta-Ha

- **Classification:** Makki
- **Asbab Status:** Ibtida'an / Primarily Spontaneous
- **Status Label:** Primarily Spontaneous (Solace of Revelation & Umar's Conversion)
- **Reporting Sahabah:** Abdullah ibn Abbas, Anas ibn Malik

### Historical & Theological Context
Revealed in early Mecca to comfort the Messenger ﷺ against pagan taunts that his religion had brought him nothing but hardship. It famously served as the emotional and spiritual catalyst that shattered the stubborn heart of Umar ibn al-Khattab, leading directly to his conversion to Islam.

### Key Context & Canonical Hadith Citations
1. **Revelation Sent Not to Cause Distress (Verses 1–3):**
   - **Primary Source:** Classical Tafsir (Al-Tabari & Ibn Kathir)
   - **Occasion:** The Prophet ﷺ would pray at night until his feet became swollen. The Quraysh mocked: *"This revelation was only sent upon Muhammad to cause him misery and exhaustion!"* Allah revealed:  
     *«Ta-Ha. We have not sent down to you the Qur'an that you be distressed, but only as a reminder for those who fear [Allah]...»*
2. **The Conversion of Umar ibn al-Khattab:**
   - **Primary Source:** **Sirah Ibn Hisham & Sunan al-Bayhaqi in Dala'il an-Nubuwwah (2/216)**
   - **Narrator:** Anas ibn Malik
   - **Trigger:** Umar set out sword in hand intending to assassinate the Prophet. Diverted by a clansman who revealed that his own sister Fatimah and her husband Sa'id ibn Zayd had accepted Islam, Umar stormed into their home, struck Sa'id and wounded his sister. Ashamed of the blood, he asked to read the parchment they were hiding. Upon reciting the opening verses of Ta-Ha: *«Indeed, I am Allah. There is no deity except Me, so worship Me and establish prayer for My remembrance»* (Verse 14), Umar trembled, wept, and proclaimed: *"Direct me to Muhammad so that I may submit!"*
