## 21. Surah Al-Anbiya (الأنبياء) — The Prophets

- **Classification:** Makki
- **Asbab Status:** Ayah-Level Debates
- **Status Label:** The Litany of Prophetic Deliverance & Pagan Dialectics
- **Reporting Sahabah:** Abdullah ibn Abbas, Abu Hurairah

### Historical & Theological Context
Revealed in Mecca as an existential warning of the approaching cosmic accounting (*Iqtaraba lin-nasi hisabuhum*). While predominantly an awe-inspiring catalog of prophetic litanies (Ibrahim's cool furnace, Yunus in the whale, Ayyub's restoration), a specific philosophical challenge was addressed regarding objects of worship.

### Key Verified Historical Triggers & Canonical Sources

#### A. The Polemic of Ibn al-Ziba'ra (Verses 98–101)
- **Primary Source:** **Musnad Ahmad 2455, Mustadrak al-Hakim (2/384), Al-Wahidi p. 220**
- **Narrator:** Abdullah ibn Abbas
- **Trigger:** When the Prophet ﷺ recited verse 98: *«Indeed, you [disbelievers] and what you worship other than Allah are the firewood of Hell...»*, the pagan polemicist Abdullah ibn al-Ziba'ra entered and challenged: *"O Muhammad! Are you not claiming that Jesus son of Mary was a righteous prophet? Yet the Christians worship Jesus, and the people of Banu Malih worship angels, and Uzayr is worshipped! Are they all in Hellfire with our idols?"* The Quraysh laughed in triumph. Thereupon, Allah revealed verses 101–102:  
  *«Indeed, those for whom the best [reward] has preceded from Us - they are from it far removed. They will not hear the slightest sound of it, while they are, in that which their souls desire, abiding eternally.»*  
  The word *Ma* (what) in Arabic grammatically denotes inanimate stones and carved idols, not righteous servants worshipped against their will.

## 22. Surah Al-Hajj (الحج) — The Pilgrimage

- **Classification:** Madani (with Meccan apocalyptic inception)
- **Asbab Status:** Ayah-Level Compound
- **Status Label:** Badr Duelists & The Constitutional Charter of Self-Defense
- **Reporting Sahabah:** Ali ibn Abi Talib, Abu Dharr al-Ghifari, Abdullah ibn Abbas

### Historical & Theological Context
Possessing a unique structural duality—commencing with the cataclysmic tremor of the Hour (*Zalzalat as-Sa'ah*), and detailing the ancient Abrahamic pilgrimage. Critical verses directly record the first military confrontation of Islam and the legal authorization of armed resistance.

### Key Verified Historical Triggers & Canonical Sources

#### A. The Duelists of the Battle of Badr (Verses 19–24)
- **Primary Source:** **Sahih al-Bukhari 4743 & Sahih Muslim 3033**
- **Narrators:** Ali ibn Abi Talib and Abu Dharr al-Ghifari
- **Trigger:** Abu Dharr swore by Allah that verse 19 descended specifically regarding the six combatants who opened the Battle of Badr:  
  *«These are two adversaries who have disputed over their Lord...»*  
  The three Muslim champions—Ali ibn Abi Talib, Hamzah ibn Abd al-Muttalib, and Ubaydah ibn al-Harith—faced the three Qurayshi stalwarts—'Utbah ibn Rabi'ah, Shaybah ibn Rabi'ah, and Al-Walid ibn 'Utbah. Ali stated: *"I will be the first person to kneel on my knees before the Most Merciful for judgment on the Day of Resurrection!"*

#### B. The Charter of Armed Defense (Verse 39)
- **Primary Source:** **Jami' at-Tirmidhi 3171 & Sunan an-Nasa'i 3085**
- **Narrator:** Abdullah ibn Abbas
- **Trigger:** When the Prophet ﷺ was driven out of Mecca, Abu Bakr wept and said: *"They have driven out their Prophet; indeed to Allah we belong and to Him we return, they will surely be destroyed!"* Thereupon, the very first verse permitting military defense was revealed:  
  *«Permission [to fight] has been given to those who are being fought, because they were wronged. And indeed, Allah is competent to give them victory.»*

## 23. Surah Al-Mu'minun (المؤمنون) — The Believers

- **Classification:** Makki
- **Asbab Status:** Ibtida'an / Purely Spontaneous
- **Status Label:** Purely Spontaneous (The Morphogenetic Design & The Barzakh)
- **Reporting Sahabah:** Umar ibn al-Khattab, 'A'ishah bint Abi Bakr

### Historical & Theological Context
Revealed spontaneously in Mecca as the timeless constitutional definition of true spiritual success (*Qad aflaha al-mu'minoon*). It contains no situational legal grievance; rather, it delineates the psychological stations of Khushu' in prayer, marital purity, the seven embryological stages of human creation, and the impassable cosmic barrier (*Barzakh*).

### Canonical Hadith Citations
1. **Jami' at-Tirmidhi 3173 & Musnad Ahmad 223:**
   > Umar ibn al-Khattab narrated:  
   > *«Whenever revelation descended upon the Messenger of Allah ﷺ, a sound like the humming of bees could be heard near his face. Once, after revelation paused, he faced the Qiblah, raised his hands and supplicated: 'O Allah, increase us and do not diminish us; honor us and do not humiliate us; give us and do not deprive us; favor us and do not let others be favored over us; and make us pleased and be pleased with us!' Then he said: 'Ten verses have just descended upon me; whoever establishes them shall enter Paradise,' and he recited: «Certainly will the believers have succeeded...» until he completed ten verses.»*
2. **Sahih Muslim 746:**
   'A'ishah was asked about the character of the Prophet ﷺ; she replied: *"His character was the Qur'an,"* and recited the opening ten verses of Surah Al-Mu'minun.

## 24. Surah An-Nur (النور) — The Light

- **Classification:** Madani (Year 5–6 AH)
- **Asbab Status:** Ayah-Level Compound
- **Status Label:** Hadith al-Ifk (Slander of 'A'ishah) & The Law of Li'an
- **Reporting Sahabah:** 'A'ishah bint Abi Bakr, Abdullah ibn Abbas, Sahl ibn Sa'd

### Historical & Theological Context
Revealed to sanctify the social, domestic, and sexual purity of the Muslim community. It resolved the greatest crisis of communal slander in early Islamic history, established the penal sanctions for false accusation (*Qadhf*), instituted domestic privacy (*Isti'dhan*), and unveiled the supreme cosmic parable of Divine Light (*Ayat an-Nur*).

### Key Verified Historical Triggers & Canonical Sources

#### A. Hadith al-Ifk: Divine Exoneration of 'A'ishah (Verses 11–26)
- **Primary Source:** **Sahih al-Bukhari 4750 & Sahih Muslim 2770**
- **Narrator:** 'A'ishah bint Abi Bakr (Comprehensive First-Person Testimony)
- **Trigger:** Returning from the expedition of Banu al-Mustaliq, 'A'ishah was inadvertently left behind while searching for her lost necklace. Safwan ibn al-Mu'attal discovered her sleeping, lowered his camel, and led her back to Medina without speaking a single word. Abdullah ibn Ubayy ibn Salul fabricated a vile accusation of infidelity, swaying Hassan ibn Thabit, Hamnah bint Jahsh, and Mistah ibn Uthathah. Medina was gripped with agony for a full month while revelation ceased. Finally, verses 11–22 descended, vindicating 'A'ishah's pristine purity from above seven heavens and denouncing the slanderers.

#### B. Abu Bakr Restoring Charity to Mistah (Verse 22)
- **Primary Source:** **Sahih al-Bukhari 4757 & Sahih Muslim 2770**
- **Narrator:** 'A'ishah bint Abi Bakr
- **Trigger:** Mistah was a poor relative whom Abu Bakr supported financially. Angered by Mistah's involvement in the slander, Abu Bakr swore never to spend a dirham on him again. Verse 22 descended:  
  *«And let not those of virtue among you and ample means swear not to give [aid] to their relatives... Would you not like that Allah should forgive you?»*  
  Hearing this, Abu Bakr wept and said: *"By Allah, yes! We desire that You forgive us, O our Lord!"* and immediately doubled Mistah's allowance.

#### C. The Legislation of Li'an / Spousal Imprecation (Verses 6–9)
- **Primary Source:** **Sahih al-Bukhari 4747 & Sahih Muslim 1492**
- **Narrators:** Sahl ibn Sa'd and Abdullah ibn Abbas
- **Trigger:** Hilal ibn Umayyah caught his wife in adultery and reported it to the Prophet ﷺ. The Prophet said: *"Bring proof (four eyewitnesses), or else the prescribed stripes of slander (*Hadd al-Qadhf*) shall fall upon your back!"* Hilal exclaimed: *"By the One who sent you with truth, I am telling the truth, and Allah will reveal that which clears my back!"* Gabriel descended with verses 6–9 instituting the five oaths of *Li'an*.

## 25. Surah Al-Furqan (الفرقان) — The Criterion

- **Classification:** Makki
- **Asbab Status:** Ayah-Level Incidents
- **Status Label:** The Ultimate Remorse of Toxic Companionship
- **Reporting Sahabah:** Abdullah ibn Abbas, Ibn Jurayj

### Historical & Theological Context
Revealed as the Sovereign Criterion distinguishing truth from falsehood, cosmic signs in the celestial spheres, and crowning with the sublime portrait of the Servants of the Most Merciful (*'Ibad ar-Rahman*).

### Key Verified Historical Triggers & Canonical Sources

#### A. Biting Hands in Eternal Remorse (Verses 27–29)
- **Primary Source:** **Al-Wahidi, Asbab an-Nuzul, p. 235; Tafsir at-Tabari (19/265); Ibn Kathir (6/107)**
- **Narrator:** Abdullah ibn Abbas
- **Trigger:** 'Uqbah ibn Abi Mu'ayt was accustomed to hosting banquets for the Quraysh elite. He invited the Prophet ﷺ, who refused to touch his food until 'Uqbah testified to the Shahadah. 'Uqbah complied. When his intimate friend Ubayy ibn Khalaf learned of this, he said: *"Has your face become blackened? I will never look at you unless you go to Muhammad, spit in his face, and renounce his faith!"* The wretched 'Uqbah went and did so. Verses 27–29 were revealed capturing his eternal agony on the Day of Judgment:  
  *«And the Day the wrongdoer will bite on his hands [in regret] he will say, "Oh, I wish I had taken with the Messenger a way. Oh, woe to me! I wish I had not taken that one as a friend. He led me astray from the message after it had come to me..."»*  
  Both 'Uqbah and Ubayy were slain as unrepentant belligerents at Badr and Uhud.

## 26. Surah Ash-Shu'ara (الشعراء) — The Poets

- **Classification:** Makki (with concluding verses on Islamic poetry)
- **Asbab Status:** Ayah-Level Vindication
- **Status Label:** Prophetic Consolation & Exoneration of the Believing Poets
- **Reporting Sahabah:** Ka'b ibn Malik, Abdullah ibn Rawahah, Hassan ibn Thabit

### Historical & Theological Context
Contains the sweeping prophetic cycle (Musa, Ibrahim, Nuh, Hud, Salih, Lut, Shu'ayb) refuting the accusation that the Prophet was a poet or madman. Specific concluding verses addressed the distinction between pagan satire and principled Islamic defense.

### Key Verified Historical Triggers & Canonical Sources

#### A. The Exemption of the Believing Poets (Verses 224–227)
- **Primary Source:** **Jami' at-Tirmidhi 3221, Mustadrak al-Hakim (2/399), Sunan al-Bayhaqi (10/238)**
- **Narrators:** Ka'b ibn Malik and Abdullah ibn Abbas
- **Trigger:** When verses 224–226 descended: *«And the poets - [only] the deviants follow them. Do you not see that in every valley they roam, and that they say what they do not do?»*, the Muslim poets—Hassan ibn Thabit, Ka'b ibn Malik, and Abdullah ibn Rawahah—came weeping to the Prophet saying: *"O Messenger of Allah, Allah knows that we compose poetry!"* Thereupon, the exception in verse 227 descended:  
  *«Except those [poets] who believe and do righteous deeds and remember Allah often and defend themselves after they were wronged...»*  
  The Prophet told Hassan: *"Recite poetry satirizing Quraysh, for Gabriel is with you as long as you defend Allah and His Messenger!"*

## 27. Surah An-Naml (النمل) — The Ant

- **Classification:** Makki
- **Asbab Status:** Ibtida'an / Purely Spontaneous
- **Status Label:** Purely Spontaneous (The Dominion of Wisdom & Cosmic Wonder)
- **Reporting Sahabah:** Abdullah ibn Abbas, Anas ibn Malik

### Historical & Theological Context
Revealed spontaneously in Mecca as a breathtaking narrative of wisdom, knowledge, and humility before divine majesty. It chronicles Dawud and Sulayman, the compassionate Queen Ant warning her colony, the strategic Hoopoe bird, Bilqis Queen of Sheba, and the mountain clouds passing away. It contains **no situational crisis or mundane litigation trigger**.

### Exegetical Synthesis
An-Naml stands as an absolute exemplar of **Category 3 (Ibtida'an)**: an ontological revelation establishing the supremacy of divine knowledge over worldly monarchies and showcasing how even the smallest creature (the ant) recognizes the sovereignty of Allah.

## 28. Surah Al-Qasas (القصص) — The Stories

- **Classification:** Makki (with comforting verse at Juhfah during Hijrah)
- **Asbab Status:** Ayah-Level Incidents
- **Status Label:** Deathbed of Abu Talib & Solace of Return at Juhfah
- **Reporting Sahabah:** Abu Hurairah, Al-Musayyib ibn Hazn, Abdullah ibn Abbas

### Historical & Theological Context
Focuses upon the subversion of Pharaonic tyranny through the fragile Nile basket and Musa's upbringing in Pharaoh's palace. Key verses capture two of the most poignant emotional moments in the Sirah: the agonizing deathbed of Abu Talib, and the Prophet's tearful exile from Mecca.

### Key Verified Historical Triggers & Canonical Sources

#### A. The Deathbed of Abu Talib (Verse 56)
- **Primary Source:** **Sahih al-Bukhari 4772 & Sahih Muslim 24**
- **Narrators:** Al-Musayyib ibn Hazn and Abu Hurairah
- **Trigger:** When Abu Talib was on his deathbed, the Prophet ﷺ entered while Abu Jahl and Abdullah ibn Abi Umayyah were sitting beside him. The Prophet pleaded: *"O my uncle! Say 'La ilaha illa Allah,' a word by which I may plead for you before Allah!"* Abu Jahl interrupted: *"O Abu Talib! Will you abandon the religion of Abd al-Muttalib?"* They continued pressing him until his final words were: *"Upon the religion of Abd al-Muttalib."* The Prophet wept: *"By Allah, I will continue seeking forgiveness for you until I am forbidden."* Thereupon Allah revealed:  
  *«Indeed, [O Muhammad], you do not guide whom you love, but Allah guides whom He wills. And He is most knowing of the [rightly] guided.»* (Verse 56).

#### B. The Promise of Return to Mecca at Juhfah (Verse 85)
- **Primary Source:** **Sahih al-Bukhari 4773 & Sunan an-Nasa'i in Al-Kubra**
- **Narrator:** Abdullah ibn Abbas
- **Trigger:** As the Prophet departed Mecca during the Hijrah, he reached the crossroads of Juhfah, stopped, looked back toward Mecca, and wept longing for his birthplace. Gabriel descended with verse 85:  
  *«Indeed, He who ordained for you the Qur'an will surely bring you back to a place of return (Mecca in triumph)...»*

## 29. Surah Al-Ankabut (العنكبوت) — The Spider

- **Classification:** Makki
- **Asbab Status:** Ayah-Level Incidents
- **Status Label:** The Smelting of Faith & Filial Dilemmas of Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas
- **Reporting Sahabah:** Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas, Abdullah ibn Abbas

### Historical & Theological Context
Revealed to prepare early Muslims for the crucible of persecution: *«Do the people think that they will be left to say, "We believe" and they will not be tested?»* (Verse 2). It anchors the fragility of polytheism in the spider's web (*Bayt al-Ankabut*).

### Key Verified Historical Triggers & Canonical Sources

#### A. Sa'd's Mother's Hunger Strike (Verse 8)
- **Primary Source:** **Sahih Muslim 1748, Jami' at-Tirmidhi 3189, Sunan Abi Dawud 2891**
- **Narrator:** Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas (Direct Personal Eyewitness Testimony)
- **Trigger:** Sa'd was exceptionally dutiful to his mother, Hamnah bint Sufyan. When he embraced Islam, she vowed: *"By Allah, I will neither eat, drink, nor sit in the shade until you disbelieve in Muhammad, so that you are shamed across Arabia as a mother-killer!"* She persisted until she fell unconscious from dehydration, and her mouth had to be pried open with sticks to pour broth. Sa'd came to her and said gently but firmly: *"O mother, by Allah, if you had one hundred souls and they exited one by one, I would never leave my religion. So eat if you wish, or starve if you wish!"* Seeing his unshakable resolve, she broke her fast. Allah revealed:  
  *«And We have enjoined upon man goodness to parents. But if they endeavor to make you associate with Me that of which you have no knowledge, do not obey them...»* (Verse 8).

## 30. Surah Ar-Rum (الروم) — The Romans

- **Classification:** Makki (Around 615 CE / 7 Years Before Hijrah)
- **Asbab Status:** Major Geopolitical Prophecy
- **Status Label:** The Byzantine Defeat & Abu Bakr's Prophetic Wager
- **Reporting Sahabah:** Abdullah ibn Abbas, Abu Sa'id al-Khudri, Ibn Mas'ud

### Historical & Theological Context
Revealed when the Christian Byzantine Empire suffered catastrophic defeats against the Zoroastrian Persian Empire (the fall of Jerusalem in 614 CE). The Meccan pagans rejoiced, claiming: *"The Zoroastrians, who are unlettered idolaters like us, have crushed the Byzantines who possess scriptures like you; soon we will crush you!"*

### Key Verified Historical Triggers & Canonical Sources

#### A. The Prophecy & Abu Bakr's Wager (Verses 1–5)
- **Primary Source:** **Jami' at-Tirmidhi 3193 (Hasan Sahih) & Musnad Ahmad 2495**
- **Narrators:** Abdullah ibn Abbas and Abu Sa'id al-Khudri
- **Trigger:** Allah revealed: *«Alif, Lam, Meem. The Romans have been defeated in the nearest land. But they, after their defeat, will overcome within several (3 to 9) years. To Allah belongs the command before and after. And that day the believers will rejoice in the victory of Allah...»*  
  Hearing this, Abu Bakr made a wager with Ubayy ibn Khalaf before wagering was prohibited: six years for 100 camels. The Prophet advised him: *"Bid' (several) in Arabic means between three and nine years; extend the term and increase the bet."* Within seven years, Heraclius counterattacked, miraculously routing the Persian army at Nineveh (627 CE) precisely synchronizing with the believers' joy over the Battle of Badr!
