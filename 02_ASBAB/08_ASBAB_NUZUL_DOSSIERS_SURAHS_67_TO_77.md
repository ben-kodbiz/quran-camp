# Project Asbab Nuzul — Scholarly Dossier Volume 8
## Surahs 67 to 77 (Al-Mulk to Al-Mursalat — Juz Tabarak)

---

## 67. Surah Al-Mulk (الملك) — The Dominion

- **Classification:** Makki (30 Verses)
- **Asbab Status:** Ayah-Level Incidents & Salvific Merit
- **Status Label:** The Subdued Whispers & The Salvific Shield
- **Reporting Sahabah:** Abu Hurairah, Abdullah ibn Abbas, Jabir ibn Abdillah

### Historical & Theological Context
Early Makki revelation opening Juz 29, delivering cosmic and ontological proof of divine sovereignty, serving as an interceding safeguard against punishment of the grave.

### Key Verified Historical Triggers & Canonical Sources

#### A. The Subdued Whispers of the Idolaters (Verse 13)
- **Primary Source:** **Al-Wahidi, Asbab an-Nuzul, p. 289; Tafsir Ibn Kathir (8/176); Tafsir at-Tabari (23/508)**
- **Narrator:** Abdullah ibn Abbas
- **Trigger:** When pagan Meccans plotted secretly against the Prophet ﷺ, whispering among themselves: *"Lower your voices so the God of Muhammad cannot hear,"* Jibril descended with Ayah 13: *"And conceal your speech or disclose it; indeed, He is Knowing of that within the breasts."*

#### B. The Interceding Shield of Thirty Verses
- **Primary Source:** **Jami' at-Tirmidhi 2891 (Hasan); Sunan Abi Dawud 1400; Musnad Ahmad 7915**
- **Narrator:** Abu Hurairah
- **Trigger:** The Prophet ﷺ designated this Surah as a continuous intercessor: *"There is a surah of thirty verses in the Qur'an which intercedes for its companion until he is forgiven: Tabarak alladhi bi-yadihi al-Mulk."*

## 68. Surah Al-Qalam (القلم) — The Pen

- **Classification:** Makki (52 Verses)
- **Asbab Status:** Whole-Surah & Ayah-Level Incidents
- **Status Label:** Exoneration from Madness & The Slanderer's Snout
- **Reporting Sahabah:** Abdullah ibn Abbas, 'A'ishah bint Abi Bakr, Mujahid

### Historical & Theological Context
The second chronological revelation following Surah Al-'Alaq, defending the mental and moral integrity of the Prophet against Meccan charges of insanity and sorcery.

### Key Verified Historical Triggers & Canonical Sources

#### A. Exoneration from the Slander of Madness (Verses 1–4)
- **Primary Source:** **Sahih al-Bukhari 4917; Tafsir Ibn Kathir (8/187); Al-Wahidi, Asbab an-Nuzul, p. 290**
- **Narrator:** 'A'ishah bint Abi Bakr & Abdullah ibn Abbas
- **Trigger:** When Quraysh elders maliciously circulated the rumor that Muhammad had become mentally deranged (*majnun*), Allah swore by the Pen and the lines of revelation: *"You are not, by the favor of your Lord, a madman. And indeed, for you is a reward uninterrupted. And indeed, you are of a magnificent character."*

#### B. Branding the Arrogant Slanderer (Verses 10–16)
- **Primary Source:** **Tafsir at-Tabari (23/532); Tafsir Ibn Kathir (8/191); Zad al-Masir**
- **Narrator:** Abdullah ibn Abbas
- **Trigger:** Revealed regarding Al-Walid ibn al-Mughirah or Al-Aswad ibn Abd Yaghuth, who spearheaded the slander campaign: *"Hammazin mashsha'in bi-nameem... Sanasimuhu 'alal-khartoom"* (We will brand him on the snout), a permanent mark of ignominy realized when he was injured in the nose at Badr.

## 69. Surah Al-Haqqah (الحاقة) — The Inevitable Reality

- **Classification:** Makki (52 Verses)
- **Asbab Status:** Ayah-Level Incidents & Conversion Trigger
- **Status Label:** The Inevitable Reality & Umar's Heart-Opening at the Ka'bah
- **Reporting Sahabah:** Umar ibn al-Khattab, Abdullah ibn Abbas

### Historical & Theological Context
Uncompromising early Makki disclosure of the absolute Reality (Al-Haqqah), detailing the catastrophic downfall of historical deniers and the stark contrast of the Book in the Right vs. Left hand.

### Key Verified Historical Triggers & Canonical Sources

#### A. Umar's Encounter with the Recitation at the Ka'bah (Verses 40–43)
- **Primary Source:** **Musnad Ahmad 115 (Isnad Hasan); Tafsir Ibn Kathir (8/208); Sirat Ibn Hisham**
- **Narrator:** Umar ibn al-Khattab
- **Trigger:** Umar secretly shadowed the Prophet ﷺ as he prayed alone inside the Ka'bah. Umar thought: *"By Allah, he is a poet!"* The Prophet recited: *"Indeed, it is the word of a noble Messenger, and not the word of a poet; little do you believe!"* Umar then thought: *"A soothsayer then!"* The Prophet recited: *"Nor the word of a soothsayer; little do you remember. It is a revelation from the Lord of the worlds."* Umar confessed: *"Islam entered deeply into my heart at that moment."*

#### B. The Severing of the Life-Artery (Verses 44–47)
- **Primary Source:** **Tafsir at-Tabari (23/580); Tafsir Ibn Kathir (8/212)**
- **Narrator:** Abdullah ibn Abbas
- **Trigger:** Defending prophetic veracity against pagan claims of fabrication: *"And if he had fabricated against Us some sayings, We would have seized him by the right hand; then We would have severed from him his life-artery (al-wateen)."*

## 70. Surah Al-Ma'arij (المعارج) — The Ascending Steps

- **Classification:** Makki (44 Verses)
- **Asbab Status:** Occasion of Descent (Verse 1)
- **Status Label:** An-Nadr's Cynical Prayer for Torment
- **Reporting Sahabah:** Abdullah ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Al-Hasan al-Basri

### Historical & Theological Context
Revealed when Meccan oligarchs cynically challenged the Prophet to summon immediate physical doom upon them if his message was true.

### Key Verified Historical Triggers & Canonical Sources

#### A. The Cynical Invocation of Impending Doom (Verse 1)
- **Primary Source:** **Al-Wahidi, Asbab an-Nuzul, p. 292; Tafsir Ibn Kathir (8/221); Tafsir at-Tabari (23/593)**
- **Narrator:** Abdullah ibn Abbas
- **Trigger:** An-Nadr ibn al-Harith insolently proclaimed: *"O Allah, if this should be the truth from You, then rain down upon us stones from the sky or bring us a painful punishment"* (Al-Anfal 8:32). Allah immediately revealed: *"A questioner asked about a penalty bound to happen (Sa'ala sa'ilun bi-'adhabin waqi'), to the disbelievers, of it there is no preventer."*

## 71. Surah Nuh (نوح) — Noah

- **Classification:** Makki (28 Verses)
- **Asbab Status:** Historical Parable & Prophetic Reassurance
- **Status Label:** Nine Centuries of Patient Da'wah & The Rejection of Idolatry
- **Reporting Sahabah:** Abdullah ibn Abbas, Urwah ibn az-Zubayr

### Historical & Theological Context
Consoling the Prophet during intense Meccan boycott and intransigence through the 950-year struggle of Noah against ancestral idolatry.

### Key Verified Historical Triggers & Canonical Sources

#### A. Genealogy of the Pre-Islamic Idols (Verse 23)
- **Primary Source:** **Sahih al-Bukhari 4920; Tafsir at-Tabari (23/631); Tafsir Ibn Kathir (8/233)**
- **Narrator:** Abdullah ibn Abbas
- **Trigger:** The names mentioned in Ayah 23 (*Wadd, Suwa', Yaghuth, Ya'uq, Nasr*) were originally righteous men from the people of Noah. After their demise, Shaytan inspired their people to erect statues at their gathering places. With the passage of generations and the loss of knowledge, those statues were transfigured into worshipped idols, later adopted by pagan Arab tribes.

## 72. Surah Al-Jinn (الجن) — The Jinn

- **Classification:** Makki (28 Verses)
- **Asbab Status:** Whole-Surah Occasion
- **Status Label:** The Valley of Nakhlah Listening & The Barred Heavens
- **Reporting Sahabah:** Abdullah ibn Abbas, Alqamah, Ibn Mas'ud

### Historical & Theological Context
Revealed following the Prophet's return from the harrowing journey to At-Ta'if, camping at the valley of Nakhlah, where a company of Jinn overheard the morning prayer.

### Key Verified Historical Triggers & Canonical Sources

#### A. The Night of the Jinn at Nakhlah (Verses 1–2)
- **Primary Source:** **Sahih al-Bukhari 4921; Sahih Muslim 449; Jami' at-Tirmidhi 3323**
- **Narrator:** Abdullah ibn Abbas
- **Trigger:** When shooting meteors barred the Jinn from eavesdropping on heavenly decrees, groups of Jinn traversed the earth searching for the cause. A delegation reached the Prophet ﷺ in the valley of Nakhlah during Fajr prayer; upon hearing the Qur'an, they embraced faith and returned to their people as warners: *"Indeed, we have heard an astonishing Qur'an!"*

## 73. Surah Al-Muzzammil (المزمل) — The Enshrouded One

- **Classification:** Makki (20 Verses; Verse 20 Madani)
- **Asbab Status:** Whole-Surah & Abrogation Occasion
- **Status Label:** The Night Vigil Crucible & The Gracious Abrogation
- **Reporting Sahabah:** 'A'ishah bint Abi Bakr, Sa'd ibn Hisham, Abdullah ibn Abbas

### Historical & Theological Context
The early nocturnal training regimen of the nascent Muslim community, requiring mandatory night prayer for an entire year before being eased into a voluntary act of devotion.

### Key Verified Historical Triggers & Canonical Sources

#### A. The Mandatory Year-Long Night Vigil & Its Abrogation (Verses 1–4 & 20)
- **Primary Source:** **Sahih Muslim 746; Sunan Abi Dawud 1342; Sunan an-Nasa'i 1601; Tafsir Ibn Kathir (8/252)**
- **Narrator:** 'A'ishah bint Abi Bakr
- **Trigger:** The initial verses mandated night prayer for half the night or more. The companions stood until their feet were swollen for twelve full months. Thereafter, Allah revealed verse 20: *"He knows that you cannot keep count of it, so He has turned to you in forgiveness, so recite what is easy of the Qur'an,"* transforming the vigil into an intensely meritorious voluntary practice.

## 74. Surah Al-Muddaththir (المدثر) — The Cloaked One

- **Classification:** Makki (56 Verses)
- **Asbab Status:** Whole-Surah Occasion & Ayah-Level Incidents
- **Status Label:** The Resumption of Revelation & The Arrogant Deliberation of Al-Walid
- **Reporting Sahabah:** Jabir ibn Abdillah, Abdullah ibn Abbas

### Historical & Theological Context
The pivotal moment marking the end of the pause in revelation (Fatrat al-Wahy), commanding the Prophet to rise from cloistered contemplation to active public warning (*Qum fa-andhir*).

### Key Verified Historical Triggers & Canonical Sources

#### A. The Resumption of Revelation after the Pause (Verses 1–5)
- **Primary Source:** **Sahih al-Bukhari 4922; Sahih Muslim 161; Musnad Ahmad**
- **Narrator:** Jabir ibn Abdillah
- **Trigger:** While walking, the Prophet ﷺ heard a voice from heaven and saw the angel who had visited him in Hira sitting on a throne between sky and earth. Overcome with awe, he retreated to his house saying: *"Zammilooni! Dath-thirooni!"* (Wrap me up!), whereupon Jibril revealed: *"O you who covers himself! Arise and warn!"*

#### B. The Deliberation of Al-Walid ibn al-Mughirah (Verses 11–26)
- **Primary Source:** **Mustadrak al-Hakim 2/507; Al-Wahidi, Asbab an-Nuzul, p. 296; Tafsir Ibn Kathir (8/262)**
- **Narrator:** Abdullah ibn Abbas
- **Trigger:** Al-Walid gathered Quraysh to agree upon a single slander against the Prophet before the pilgrimage. After rejecting charges of madness, poetry, and soothsaying, he devised the claim of 'inherited magic' (*sihr yu'thar*). Allah denounced him in verses 11–26: *"Leave Me with the one I created alone... I will drive him into Saqar."*

## 75. Surah Al-Qiyamah (القيامة) — The Resurrection

- **Classification:** Makki (40 Verses)
- **Asbab Status:** Ayah-Level Revelation Protocol & Denier's Boast
- **Status Label:** The Revelation Tongue Haste Protocol & The Rebuilt Fingertips
- **Reporting Sahabah:** Abdullah ibn Abbas, Sa'id ibn Jubayr

### Historical & Theological Context
Revealed during the height of Meccan skepticism regarding bodily resurrection, while establishing the divine protocol for receiving and memorizing scripture.

### Key Verified Historical Triggers & Canonical Sources

#### A. The Protocol of Quiet Listening During Revelation (Verses 16–19)
- **Primary Source:** **Sahih al-Bukhari 4927; Sahih Muslim 448; Sunan an-Nasa'i; Tafsir Ibn Kathir (8/275)**
- **Narrator:** Abdullah ibn Abbas
- **Trigger:** The Prophet ﷺ used to endure great strain, rapidly moving his tongue and lips while Jibril was reciting, eager to memorize every word. Allah instructed him: *"Do not move your tongue with it to make haste with it. Indeed, upon Us is its collection and its recitation... Then upon Us is its clarification."*

#### B. The Reconstructed Fingertips (Verses 3–4)
- **Primary Source:** **Tafsir at-Tabari (24/55); Al-Wahidi, Asbab an-Nuzul, p. 297**
- **Narrator:** Abdullah ibn Abbas
- **Trigger:** Adi ibn Rabi'ah scoffingly asked: *"If Allah collects rotten bones, will He recreate them?"* Allah revealed: *"Does man think that We will not assemble his bones? Yes, We are able even to restore his fingertips (bananah)!"*

## 76. Surah Al-Insan (الإنسان) — Man

- **Classification:** Madani (31 Verses)
- **Asbab Status:** Ayah-Level Incidents & Altruistic Vow
- **Status Label:** Feeding the Captive for Allah's Face & The Offer of Corrupt Compromise
- **Reporting Sahabah:** Abdullah ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah

### Historical & Theological Context
Honoring extraordinary self-sacrificing altruism in Medina while fortifying the Prophet against Meccan and hypocrite pressures to dilute divine mandates.

### Key Verified Historical Triggers & Canonical Sources

#### A. Feeding the Destitute, Orphan, and Captive for Allah's Face (Verses 8–9)
- **Primary Source:** **Tafsir Ibn Kathir (8/286); Tafsir at-Tabari (24/92); Al-Wahidi, Asbab an-Nuzul, p. 298**
- **Narrator:** Abdullah ibn Abbas & Qatadah
- **Trigger:** The righteous household fasted and surrendered their evening sustenance three consecutive nights to a destitute person (*miskeen*), an orphan (*yateem*), and a prisoner of war (*aseer*), seeking zero earthly reward or verbal thanks (*Innama nut'imukum li-wajhillah*).

#### B. Rejecting the Seductions of Wealth and Compromise (Verse 24)
- **Primary Source:** **Zad al-Masir (Ibn al-Jawzi); Tafsir Ibn Kathir (8/292)**
- **Narrator:** Abdullah ibn Abbas
- **Trigger:** Meccan elites offered wealth and women to entice the Prophet into muting his critique of idolatry; Allah commanded: *"So be patient for the decision of your Lord and do not obey from among them a sinner (athim) or an ungrateful one (kafoor)."*

---

## 77. Surah Al-Mursalat (المرسلات) — The Emissaries

- **Classification:** Makki (50 Verses)
- **Asbab Status:** Whole-Surah Descent
- **Status Label:** Fresh Revelation in the Cave of Mina & The Serpent Escape
- **Reporting Sahabah:** Abdullah ibn Mas'ud, Al-Aswad

### Historical & Theological Context
Revealed in an intimate mountainous cave in Mina during the Makki pilgrimage season, sealing the prophetic warnings of Juz Tabarak.

### Key Verified Historical Triggers & Canonical Sources

#### A. Revelation in the Cave of Mina (Whole Surah)
- **Primary Source:** **Sahih al-Bukhari 4930; Sahih Muslim 2234; Sunan an-Nasa'i; Musnad Ahmad**
- **Narrator:** Abdullah ibn Mas'ud
- **Trigger:** Ibn Mas'ud narrated: *"While we were with the Prophet ﷺ in a cave at Mina, Surah Al-Mursalat was revealed to him, and we were taking it fresh from his lips. Suddenly a snake leapt out at us. The Prophet said: 'Kill it!' We hurried to kill it, but it escaped into its hole. The Prophet said: 'It has been protected from your harm just as you were protected from its harm.'"*
