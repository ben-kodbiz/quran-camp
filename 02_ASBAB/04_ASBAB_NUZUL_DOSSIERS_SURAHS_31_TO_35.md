## Surahs 31 to 35 (Luqman to Fatir)

## 31. Surah Luqman (لقمان) — Luqman

- **Classification:** Makki
- **Asbab Status:** Ayah-Level Incidents
- **Status Label:** Commercialized Diversion (*Lahw al-Hadith*) & The Five Keys of the Unseen
- **Reporting Sahabah:** Abdullah ibn Abbas, Abdullah ibn Mas'ud, Abu Hurairah

### Historical & Theological Context
Synthesizes parental mentorship, filial gratitude, and moral decorum. Specific verses directly countered aggressive commercialized propaganda deployed by the Quraysh elite to distract the populace from listening to the Qur'an.

### Key Verified Historical Triggers & Canonical Sources

#### A. The Purchasing of Idle Tales / Lahw al-Hadith (Verse 6)
- **Primary Source:** **Al-Wahidi, Asbab an-Nuzul, p. 248; Tafsir at-Tabari (20/126); Sunan al-Bayhaqi (10/221)**
- **Narrators:** Abdullah ibn Abbas and Abdullah ibn Mas'ud
- **Trigger:** An-Nadr ibn al-Harith, a wealthy merchant of Mecca, traveled to Persia and purchased the folklore and musical romances of Persian kings (Rustam and Isfandiyar) along with singing slave-girls. Whenever he saw someone drawn to the Prophet's recitation, he placed a singing girl with him or read Persian fables, claiming: *"Muhammad tells you tales of 'Ad and Thamud, but I give you music and the tales of the Persian emperors!"* Thereupon Allah revealed:  
  *«And of the people is he who buys the amusement of speech (lahw al-hadith) to mislead [others] from the way of Allah without knowledge and who takes it in ridicule. Those will have a humiliating punishment.»* (Verse 6).  
  Ibn Mas'ud was asked about this verse and swore three times by Allah: *"By Allah, besides Whom there is no other deity, it refers to musical distraction (*Al-Ghina'*)."*

#### B. The Five Keys of the Unseen / Mafatih al-Ghayb (Verse 34)
- **Primary Source:** **Sahih al-Bukhari 4778 & Sahih Muslim 9**
- **Narrator:** Abdullah ibn Umar and Abu Hurairah
- **Trigger:** In the famous Hadith of Jibril, when Gabriel asked: *"When is the Hour?"*, the Prophet replied: *"The one questioned knows no more than the questioner, but I will tell you of its signs... It is among five things which none knows except Allah,"* then recited verse 34:  
  *«Indeed, Allah [alone] has knowledge of the Hour and sends down the rain and knows what is in the wombs. And no soul perceives what it will earn tomorrow, and no soul perceives in what land it will die...»*

## 32. Surah As-Sajdah (السجدة) — The Prostration

- **Classification:** Makki
- **Asbab Status:** Ibtida'an / Purely Spontaneous
- **Status Label:** Purely Spontaneous (The Canonical Friday Dawn Recitation)
- **Reporting Sahabah:** Abu Hurairah, Abdullah ibn Abbas, Jabir ibn Abdillah

### Historical & Theological Context
Revealed spontaneously in Mecca as an existential meditation on creation from clay (*Teen*), the thousand-year administrative cosmic cycle, the secret night vigils (*Tatajafa junoobuhum 'ani al-madaji'*), and eternal eye-delights. It has **no situational worldly grievance**; its canonical occasion is liturgical: established as the Sunnah of every Friday Fajr prayer.

### Canonical Hadith Citations
1. **Sahih al-Bukhari 891 & Sahih Muslim 880:**
   > Narrated Abu Hurairah:  
   > *«The Prophet ﷺ used to recite in the Fajr prayer on Fridays: Alif, Lam, Meem, Tanzil (Surah As-Sajdah) in the first rak'ah, and Hal Ata 'ala al-Insani (Surah Al-Insan) in the second rak'ah.»*
2. **Jami' at-Tirmidhi 2921 (Sahih):**
   Jabir ibn Abdillah reported: *"The Prophet ﷺ would never go to sleep at night until he had recited Alif, Lam, Meem Tanzil (As-Sajdah) and Tabarak alladhi bi-yadihi al-Mulk (Al-Mulk)."*
3. **Sahih al-Bukhari 4779 & Sahih Muslim 2824:**
   Abu Hurairah transmitted the Hadith Qudsi explaining verse 17 (*Fala ta'lamu nafsun ma ukhfiya lahum min qurrati a'yun*):  
   *«Allah says: "I have prepared for My righteous servants that which no eye has seen, no ear has heard, and has never crossed the human imagination."»*

## 33. Surah Al-Ahzab (الأحزاب) — The Confederates

- **Classification:** Madani (Shawwal 5 AH to Dhu al-Qa'dah 5 AH)
- **Asbab Status:** Ayah-Level Compound (Five Major Crises)
- **Status Label:** Compound (The Trench, Banu Qurayzah, Adoption Abolition, Hijab)
- **Reporting Sahabah:** 'A'ishah bint Abi Bakr, Anas ibn Malik, Abdullah ibn Umar, Zayd ibn Thabit

### Historical & Theological Context
Al-Ahzab encompasses five pivotal crises of Year 5 AH: the existential siege of 10,000 allied confederates (*Ahzab*), the treachery of Banu Qurayzah, the revolutionary social reform abolishing pre-Islamic adoption, the marriage to Zaynab bint Jahsh, and the descent of the universal charters of Hijab and Jilbab.

### Key Verified Historical Triggers & Canonical Sources

#### A. Abolition of Pre-Islamic Adoption (Verses 4–5, 37)
- **Primary Source:** **Sahih al-Bukhari 4782 & Sahih Muslim 1772**
- **Narrator:** Abdullah ibn Umar
- **Trigger:** In pre-Islamic Arabia, an adopted child took the name, bloodline, and inheritance rights of his adopter. Zayd ibn Harithah was known throughout Arabia as *Zayd ibn Muhammad*. Verse 5 descended abolishing this fiction:  
  *«Call them by [the names of] their fathers; it is more just in the sight of Allah...»*  
  Later, when Zayd divorced his wife Zaynab bint Jahsh due to irreconcilable temperamental incompatibility, Allah commanded the Prophet to marry Zaynab (Verse 37) to permanently break the taboo that an adopted son's ex-wife was forbidden to the adoptive father.

#### B. The Charter of Domestic Hijab (Verse 53)
- **Primary Source:** **Sahih al-Bukhari 4790 & Sahih Muslim 1428**
- **Narrator:** Anas ibn Malik (Direct Personal Eyewitness Testimony)
- **Trigger:** On the night of the Prophet's marriage banquet with Zaynab, dinner guests lingered late conversing in the house while the Prophet wished to retire. Out of sublime shyness (*Haya'*), he hesitated to ask them to leave. Verse 53 was revealed:  
  *«O you who have believed, do not enter the houses of the Prophet except when you are permitted for a meal... And when you ask [his wives] for something, ask them from behind a screen (*Hijab*). That is purer for your hearts and their hearts...»*  
  Anas testified: *"The Prophet stepped back, drew the curtain between myself and his room, and recited this verse."*

#### C. The Outer Mantle / Charter of the Jilbab (Verse 59)
- **Primary Source:** **Sahih al-Bukhari 4795 & Sunan Abi Dawud 4101**
- **Narrator:** 'A'ishah bint Abi Bakr
- **Trigger:** In Medina, women would go out at night to relieve themselves in open fields. Hypocrites and rakes would loiter near roadways harassing women, claiming if confronted: *"We only thought they were slave women!"* Verse 59 descended ordering free believing women to cast their outer cloaks (*Jilbab*) over themselves so they would be recognized as dignified believers and not molested (*Dhalika adna an yu'rafna fala yu'dhayn*).

## 34. Surah Saba (سبأ) — Sheba

- **Classification:** Makki
- **Asbab Status:** Ibtida'an / Purely Spontaneous
- **Status Label:** Purely Spontaneous (Cosmic Ownership & The Cataclysm of Arim)
- **Reporting Sahabah:** Abdullah ibn Abbas, Farwah ibn Musayk

### Historical & Theological Context
Revealed spontaneously in Mecca detailing cosmic gratitude vs civilizational collapse. It sets Dawud's singing mountains and pliable iron, and Sulayman's control of wind and molten copper against the arrogant, ingrate kingdom of Sheba whose catastrophic dam breach (*Sayl al-'Arim*) obliterated their civilization. It contains **no situational worldly litigation trigger**.

### Key Context & Canonical Hadith Citations
1. **The Nature of Saba (Nation vs Person):**
   - **Primary Source:** **Jami' at-Tirmidhi 3225 (Hasan Sahih) & Musnad Ahmad 21303**
   - **Narrator:** Farwah ibn Musayk
   - **Trigger:** A man asked the Prophet ﷺ: *"O Messenger of Allah, what is Saba? Is it a land or a woman?"* The Prophet replied: *"It is neither a land nor a woman; rather, it was a man of the Arabs who fathered ten tribes: six settled in Yemen (including Himyar, Kindah, Madhhij, Ash'ar) and four settled in the Levant (including Ghassan, Lakhm, Judham)."*
2. **Solitary Reflection (Verse 46):**
   *«Say, "I only advise you of one [thing] - that you stand for Allah, [seeking truth] in pairs and individually, and then reflect...»* The ultimate intellectual antidote to herd mentality.

## 35. Surah Fatir (فاطر) — The Originator

- **Classification:** Makki
- **Asbab Status:** Ibtida'an / Purely Spontaneous
- **Status Label:** Purely Spontaneous (Multi-Winged Angels & The Heirs of the Book)
- **Reporting Sahabah:** Abdullah ibn Mas'ud, Abu Sa'id al-Khudri, 'A'ishah bint Abi Bakr

### Historical & Theological Context
Revealed spontaneously in Mecca as a breathtaking meditation on Allah as *Fatir* (The Originator of the heavens and earth out of absolute non-existence). It details the multi-winged celestial hierarchy of angels, the absolute ontological poverty of mankind (*Antumu al-fuqara'u ila Allah*), the multi-colored geological mountain strata (*Judad*), and the inheritance of the Qur'an by three classes of believers.

### Canonical Hadith Citations
1. **Gabriel with Six Hundred Wings (Verse 1):**
   - **Primary Source:** **Sahih al-Bukhari 3232 & Sahih Muslim 174**
   - **Narrator:** Abdullah ibn Mas'ud
   - **Trigger:** Regarding verse 1: *«[All] praise is [due] to Allah, Originator of the heavens and the earth, who made the angels messengers having wings, two or three or four...»*, Ibn Mas'ud narrated: *"The Messenger of Allah ﷺ saw Gabriel in his true celestial form having six hundred wings, with multicolored pearls and rubies falling from his plumage, blocking the entire horizon between the earth and the heavens."*
2. **The Three Heirs of the Book (Verse 32):**
   - **Primary Source:** **Musnad Ahmad 11463 & Jami' at-Tirmidhi 3231**
   - **Narrator:** Abu Sa'id al-Khudri
   - **Trigger:** Regarding verse 32: *«Then We caused to inherit the Book those We have chosen of Our servants; and among them is he who wrongs himself (*Zalimun li-nafsih*), and among them is he who is moderate (*Muqtasid*), and among them is he who is foremost in good deeds (*Sabiqun bil-khayrat*)...»* The Prophet ﷺ explained: *"All three classes are from this Ummah, and all of them are destined for Paradise through Allah's overarching mercy."*
