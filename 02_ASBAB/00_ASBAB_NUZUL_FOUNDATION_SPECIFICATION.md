# Project Asbab Nuzul: Orthodox Sunni Foundation Specification
### The Epistemological Principles, Transmission Criteria & Classification of the Occasions of Revelation

---

## 1. Executive Mandate & Purpose

The **Asbab Nuzul** project operates as a specialized knowledge vertical within the Huurs Studio ecosystem. Its sole objective is to establish an unshakeable, Tier-1 verified inventory of the historical occasions, real-world catalysts, questions, and crises that coincided with the descent of Qur'anic revelation to the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ.

### Core Axiom: Knowledge of the Cause Bequeaths Knowledge of the Effect
As formulated by Sheikh al-Islam Ibn Taymiyyah in *Muqaddimah fi Usul al-Tafsir*:
> مَعْرِفَةُ السَّبَبِ تُورِثُ مَعْرِفَةَ الْمُسَبَّبِ  
> *"Knowledge of the cause (Sabab) bequeaths knowledge of that which was caused (Musabbab)."*

Understanding the historical occasion provides the vital historical, legal, and linguistic context required to:
1. Identify whether a legal ruling was general or specific (*'Amm aw Khass*).
2. Establish the primary legal axiom: *Al-'Ibratu bi-'Umum al-Lafdh la bi-Khusus as-Sabab* (The governing principle is the generality of the wording, not the particularity of the specific occasion).
3. Distinguish divine legislative progression (*Tadrij*) and historical context from theological dogma.
4. Protect against misinterpreting verses out of their intended historical scope.

## 2. Epistemological Criteria for Acceptance (*Qawa'id at-Tawtheeq*)

Not every historical tale narrated in late books of history qualifies as a genuine *Sabab an-Nuzul*. In strict adherence to Sunni Hadith methodology, the following five criteria are mandatory:

### Rule 1: Direct Transmission from Eye-Witness Companions
Imam al-Wahidi writes in the introduction to *Asbab an-Nuzul*:
> *"It is not permissible to speak on the occasions of revelation of the Book except through narration and hearing from those who witnessed the revelation, stood at the occasions, and investigated their causes."*

A statement of a Companion (*Sahabi*) saying: *"This verse was revealed concerning such an incident"* carries the legal ruling of a raised, marfu' tradition (*Hukmuhu al-Raf'*) because the Sahabah would not speak on the reasons for revelation based on mere personal conjecture.

### Rule 2: Explicit Formula vs Associative Exegesis
Classical Sunni scholars (Al-Zarkashi, Al-Suyuti, Ibn Taymiyyah) distinguish between two linguistic forms:
- **Explicit Declaration (*Sareeh*):**  
  Phrases such as: *"The reason for the revelation of this verse was..."* (سَبَبُ نُزُولِ هَٰذِهِ الْآيَةِ كَذَا) or *"Such an event occurred, so Allah revealed..."* (حَدَثَ كَذَا فَنَزَلَتْ). This is a definitive *Sabab an-Nuzul*.
- **Interpretive Association (*Ghayr Sareeh*):**  
  Phrases such as: *"I hold this verse was revealed concerning..."* (نَزَلَتْ هَٰذِهِ الْآيَةُ فِي كَذَا) or *"This verse applies to..."* This often denotes the Companion using the verse to illustrate a contemporary ruling, rather than identifying the original historical trigger of its descent.

### Rule 3: Strict Source Hierarchy
Every claim in Project Asbab Nuzul must be sourced directly from the classical Sunni hierarchy:
1. **Pinnacle (Muttafaq 'Alayh):** *Sahih al-Bukhari* and *Sahih Muslim*.
2. **The Four Sunan & Musnads:** *Sunan Abi Dawud, Jami' at-Tirmidhi, Sunan an-Nasa'i, Sunan Ibn Majah, Musnad Ahmad, Muwatta Malik*.
3. **Classical Verified Compilations:** *Mustadrak al-Hakim* (conditioned upon authentication by Imam adh-Dhahabi), *Ibn Hibban*, and *Ibn Khuzaymah*.
4. **Primary Classical Exegetical Ledgers:** *Jami' al-Bayan* of Imam al-Tabari, *Tafsir Ibn Abi Hatim*, and *Tafsir Ibn Kathir*.
5. **Specialized Asbab Monographs:** *Asbab an-Nuzul* by Al-Wahidi and *Lubab an-Nuqul* by Al-Suyuti.

Weak (*Da'if*), disconnected (*Munqati'*), or fabricated (*Mawdu'*) narrations are strictly prohibited.

## 3. The Tripartite Categorization of Surahs

All 114 Surahs of the Qur'an are classified into one of three distinct categories:

### Category 1: Surah-Level Asbab (Whole-Chapter Revelations)
Chapters that descended all at once in direct response to a single, specific historical catalyst, challenge, or question. Examples include:
- *Surah Al-Masad (111):* The Mount Safa gathering and Abu Lahab's curse (*Sahih al-Bukhari 4770*).
- *Surah Al-Ikhlas (112):* Polytheists demanding the lineage of Allah (*Jami' at-Tirmidhi 3364*).
- *Surah Al-Kawthar (108):* Pagan elites mocking the Prophet as *Abtar* after his infant son's death (*Sahih Muslim 400*).
- *Surah Al-Kafirun (109):* Quraysh offering a syncretic annual rotation of worship (*Al-Wahidi; Ibn Kathir*).
- *Surah Al-Falaq & An-Nas (113–114):* The sorcery of Labid ibn al-A'sam (*Sahih al-Bukhari 5763*).
- *Surah Al-Munafiqun (63):* Exposing Abdullah ibn Ubayy's conspiracy at Muraysi' (*Sahih al-Bukhari 4900*).
- *Surah Al-Mujadilah (58):* Khawlah bint Tha'labah's complaint concerning Zihar (*Sunan an-Nasa'i 3460*).
- *Surah Al-Mumtahanah (60):* Hatib ibn Abi Balta'ah's intercepted letter (*Sahih al-Bukhari 4890*).

### Category 2: Ayah-Level Asbab (Compound Revelations)
Large, comprehensive chapters revealed over months or years, where major sections or individual passages were triggered by specific real-world incidents, while the rest was revealed *Ibtida'an*. Examples include:
- *Surah Al-Baqarah (02):* Over 40 verified distinct asbab (Qiblah shift, Ramadan fasting, usury, debt).
- *Surah Ali-Imran (03):* The delegation of Najran (Mubahalah) and the Battle of Uhud (*Bukhari 4380*).
- *Surah An-Nisa (04):* Post-Uhud orphan crises, inheritance, and marital laws (*Bukhari 4574*).
- *Surah Al-Anfal (08):* The Battle of Badr and the division of spoils (*Bukhari 4882*).
- *Surah At-Tawbah (09):* The Tabuk expedition and the three who stayed behind (*Bukhari 4679*).
- *Surah An-Nur (24):* The Slander of 'A'ishah (*Hadith al-Ifk*, *Bukhari 4750*) and *Li'an*.
- *Surah Al-Ahzab (33):* The 10,000 Confederate Siege, adoption reform, and *Ayat al-Jilbab*.

### Category 3: Purely Ibtidā'an (Spontaneous Revelations)
Chapters revealed without any specific preceding worldly event or inquiry, descending directly as primordial guidance, cosmic contemplation, descriptions of the afterlife, and histories of earlier prophets. Examples include:
- *Surah Yusuf (12):* The complete, coherent life of Prophet Joseph (*Ahsan al-Qasas*).
- *Surah Ta-Ha (20):* Spontaneous divine solace and the chronicle of Moses.
- *Surah Al-Mu'minun (23):* Universal spiritual virtues and embryonic morphogenesis.
- *Surah An-Naml (27):* The prophetic dominion of Solomon and the Queen of Sheba.
- *Surah As-Sajdah (32):* Creation from clay, night vigils, and Friday dawn liturgy.
- *Surah Saba (34):* Cosmic ownership, Dawud's iron, and the flood of Ma'rib.
- *Surah Fatir (35):* Multi-winged angels, human Faqr, and the three heirs of the Book.
